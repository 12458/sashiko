# TTY LAYER AND SERIAL DRIVERS — drivers/tty/serial (part 5)

Serial Core (`serial_core.c`, `serial_ctrl.c`, `serial_port.c`), GPIO modem-control (`serial_mctrl_gpio.*`), and TXx9 (`serial_txx9.c`), Renesas SCI (`sh-sci*`), SiFive (`sifive.c`), Spreadtrum (`sprd_serial.c`), and STM32 (`stm32-usart.*`) UART drivers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- After `ops->set_termios()`, `uart_change_line_settings()` must hold `uport->lock` to sync `UPSTAT_CTS_ENABLE`/`UPSTAT_DCD_ENABLE` with `c_cflag`, recompute `hw_stopped` (`uart_softcts_mode()`, `get_mctrl()`), and call `stop_tx()`/`__uart_start()`; `uart_set_termios()` must reload `cflag` from `tty->termios.c_cflag` afterward before testing `CBAUD == B0`. [F226, F227]
- `uart_port_startup()` on a console with `uport->cons->cflag` moves `cflag`/`ispeed`/`ospeed` to `tty->termios` and zeroes `cons->{cflag,ispeed,ospeed}`, and `uart_shutdown()` saves them back. `uart_shutdown()`/`uart_tty_port_shutdown()` must call `tty_port_set_suspended(port, false)`; `uart_suspend_port()` on an initialized port saves `uport->mctrl` before zeroing it under `uport->lock` across `ops->shutdown()`, then restores it. [F231, F232, F233]
- While `SER_RS485_ENABLED` is set, `uart_update_mctrl()`, `uart_suspend_port()`, `uart_resume_port()`, and `uart_configure_port()` skip `ops->set_mctrl()`; clearing `SER_RS485_ENABLED` in `uart_set_rs485_config()` calls `ops->set_mctrl(port, port->mctrl)` under `port->lock`. `uart_get_rs485_mode()` sets `SER_RS485_TERMINATE_BUS`/`RX_DURING_TX` in `rs485_supported.flags` when acquiring those GPIOs; both `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485()` and `uart_get_rs485_mode()` call `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485_delays()`; and `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485()` masks `rs485->flags` with `supported_flags` before resolving equal `SER_RS485_RTS_ON_SEND`/`AFTER_SEND` polarity. [F228, F230, F249, F265]
- In `uart_throttle()`/`unthrottle()`, mask `mask` with `~port->status` after `ops->throttle()`/`unthrottle()` before `UPSTAT_AUTORTS`/`AUTOXOFF`. `uart_insert_char()` tests `(status & ignore_status_mask & ~overrun) == 0` for char insertion and `status & ~ignore_status_mask & overrun` for `TTY_OVERRUN`. In `receive_chars()` (`serial_txx9.c`) and `handle_lsr_errors()` (`sprd_serial.c`), break (`UBRK`/`SPRD_LSR_BI`) precedes parity/framing checks and clears `UFER | UPER` (`FE | PE`) before `read_status_mask`. [F266, F268, F270]
- `struct uart_port port` must stay at offset 0 of `struct sci_port` and `struct sprd_uart_port` because IRQ, sysfs, and platform remove/PM callbacks cast `void *` drvdata/`dev_id` directly between `uart_port *` and the outer struct. [F294, F298]
- In `sh-sci`, private `SCI_PORT_TYPE` (`SCI_PORT_RSCI`, `BIT(7)`) stays in `sci_port->type` while `uart_port->type` uses `SCI_PUBLIC_PORT_ID(type)`. On `SCIx_RZV2H_SCIF_REGTYPE`, `sci_init_pins()`/`sci_set_mctrl()` skip unimplemented `SCFCR_MCE` and `SCSPTR` RTS/CTS bits. `SCI_SR(x)` requires `x` in `[1, 32]`, `sampling_rate_mask` must be non-zero, and `min_sr()`/`max_sr()`/`for_each_sr()` yield 1-based `sr` while `*srr = sr - 1` is 0-based. [F258, F262, F286, F317]
- In `sh-sci.c`, `sci_startup()` sets `tx_occurred = false`, and `sci_shutdown()` sets `autorts = false` and preserves `hscif_tot` in `SCSCR` (`sci_shutdown_complete()`). In `sci_suspend()`/`resume()`, when `!console_suspend_enabled && uart_console(&sport->port)`, use `ops->console_save()`/`restore()` instead of `sport->rstc`. `sci_clear_SCxSR()` on `SCIFA_ORER` writes `sci_serial_in(port, SCxSR) & mask`, whereas SCIF writes `mask & ~(SCIF_FERC | SCIF_PERC)`; if `sci_receive_chars()` exits with `copied == 0` (`tty_buffer_request_room() == 0`), it still reads `SCxRDR`/`SCxSR` and clears `SCxSR_RDxF_CLEAR(port)`. `sci_flush_buffer()` resets `tx_dma_len = 0` and terminates TX DMA (`cookie_tx = -EINVAL`); `sci_dma_rx_chan_invalidate()` under `uart_port_lock` clears `chan_rx = NULL`, `cookie_rx[] = -EINVAL`, and `active_rx = 0`. [F240, F242, F269, F275, F322]
- In `stm32-usart`, optional offsets in `stm32_usart_offsets` (`rtor`, `rqr`, `icr`, `presc`, `hwcfgr1`) use `UNDEF_REG` (`0xffff`) and require `!= UNDEF_REG`. `stm32_usart_startup()` sets `throttled = false` before RX DMA/IRQ enable; `stm32_usart_rx_dma_start_or_resume()` sets `last_res = RX_BUF_L` when setting `rx_dma_busy = true`, and `stm32_usart_push_buffer_dma()` decrements `last_res` by `dma_count` and wraps `0` to `RX_BUF_L`. [F243, F244, F263]
- In `sprd_serial.c`, `dma_request_chan()` leaves `ERR_PTR` in `tx_dma.chn`/`rx_dma.chn`, so test `tx_dma.enable`/`rx_dma.enable` (never `.chn`). `sprd_start_dma_rx()` resets `pos = rx_dma.phys_addr` and `rx_buf_tail = rx_dma.virt`, and `sprd_uart_dma_irq()`/`sprd_complete_rx_dma()` advance `rx_buf_tail` by the `trans_len` delta of `pos`. In `serial_txx9.c`, `receive_chars()` clears `TXX9_SIDISR_RFDN_MASK` in `next_ignore_status_mask` each loop, sets it on `UOER`, and updates `up->ignore_status_mask` at `ignore_char:`; `serial_txx9_interrupt()` clears `TXX9_SIDISR_TDIS` from `status` when `!(sio_in(up, TXX9_SIDICR) & TXX9_SIDICR_TIE)`. [F213, F273, F277, F299]

## Synchronization

- Dereferencing `state->uart_port` without `state->port.mutex` requires `uart_port_ref()`/`ref_lock()` and `uart_port_deref()`/`unlock_deref()` (`uart_port_check()` requires `state->port.mutex`). `uart_port_unlock_deref()` unlocks `uport->lock` before `uart_port_deref(uport)`. `uart_alloc/free_xmit_buf()` must never call `free_page()` under `uport->lock`, and `uart_free_xmit_buf()` clears `xmit_buf = NULL` and resets `xmit_fifo` under the lock first. [F173, F174, F193]
- `uart_set_options()` requires `console_list_lock`, and `serial_core_add_one_port()` skips `uart_port_spin_lock_init(uport)` if `uart_console_registered(uport)`. `uart_update_timeout()` requires `port->lock` to write `port->frame_time`, and lockless readers (`uart_fifo_timeout()`) use `READ_ONCE()`. [F198, F201]
- Use `uart_port_lock*()`/`unlock*()` (not `spin_lock*()`), except `CON_NBCON` `device_lock`/`unlock` (`sifive_serial_device_lock()`/`unlock()`) and `uart_port_set_cons()` which use `__uart_port_lock_irqsave()`/`unlock_irqrestore()`. Legacy console `.write` (`serial_console_write()`, `sprd_console_write()`, `stm32_usart_console_write()`) uses `uart_port_trylock_irqsave()` when `oops_in_progress` is set (and skips locking when `port->sysrq` is set in `sh-sci.c`/`sprd_serial.c`). [F207, F341, F343]
- `serial_base_port_startup()`/`shutdown()` update `port_dev->tx_enabled` under `uart_port_lock_irqsave()`; `uart_shutdown()`/`uart_tty_port_shutdown()` call `serial_base_port_shutdown()` before `uart_port_shutdown()` and `uart_free_xmit_buf()`; and `serial_port_runtime_resume()`/`suspend()` only call `ops->start_tx(port)` if `!(port->flags & UPF_DEAD)` and `port_dev->tx_enabled` is true under `uart_port_lock_irqsave()`. [F202, F306]
- `uart_rs485_config()`/`uart_set_rs485_config()` call `gpiod_set_value_cansleep()` outside `scoped_guard(uart_port_lock_irqsave, port)` (`port->rs485_config` must not sleep); RS485/ISO7816 ioctl handlers call `copy_from/to_user()` outside `scoped_guard(uart_port_lock_irqsave, port)`. [F325, F327]
- In `sh-sci.c`, `sci_dma_tx_release()` calls `cancel_work_sync(&s->work_tx)` and `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` outside `uart_port_lock`, and `sci_dma_rx_release()` drops `uart_port_lock_irqsave` after invalidating `chan_rx` before `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` and `dma_free_coherent()`. In `stm32-usart.c`, call `tty_flip_buffer_push()` only after `uart_unlock_and_check_sysrq*()`; `stm32_usart_rx/tx_dma_terminate()` use `dmaengine_terminate_async()`, while `stm32_usart_shutdown()` calls `dmaengine_synchronize(rx_ch)` outside `port->lock`. In `sifive.c`, `__ssp_enable/disable_txwm/rxwm()` update `ssp->ier` and `SIFIVE_SERIAL_IE_OFFS` under `ssp->port.lock`, and `sifive_serial_clk_notifier()` updates `ssp->port.uartclk` before `__ssp_update_div()`. [F206, F246, F335, F338, F339]

## Object Lifetimes

- `serial_core_register_port()` sets `UPF_DEAD` before `serial_core_port_device_add()` until `serial_core_add_one_port()`. In `__uart_start()`, `pm_runtime_get(&port_dev->dev)` pairs with `pm_runtime_put_noidle(&port_dev->dev)` on error (`err < 0 && err != -EINPROGRESS`) and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend(&port_dev->dev)` otherwise (`pm_runtime_enabled()` checks `port->dev`). [F175, F192, F279]
- In `uart_change_port()`, if `ops->request_port(uport)` fails, restore and re-request old `iobase`, `type`, `hub6`, `iotype`, `regshift`, and `mapbase` (or set `uport->type = PORT_UNKNOWN`). `uart_rs485_config()`/`uart_set_rs485_config()` roll back `rs485_term_gpio` and `rs485_rx_during_tx_gpio` if `port->rs485_config()` fails. `uart_suspend_port()`/`resume_port()` call `put_device(tty_dev)` on `device_find_child()` on all paths; `uart_line_info()` and failing `uart_poll_init()` restore saved `state->pm_state` via `uart_change_pm()`. [F179, F181, F182, F183]
- In `sh-sci.c`, PIO fallback clears `chan_tx`/`chan_rx` while preserving `chan_tx_saved`/`chan_rx_saved` for `sci_free_dma()`. `sci_dma_rx_release()` frees the single `buf_len_rx * 2` coherent allocation via `rx_buf[0]` and `sg_dma_address(&sg_rx[0])`. `sci_startup()` calls `sci_free_dma()` if `sci_request_irq()` fails; `sci_shutdown()` cancels `rx_timer`/`rx_fifo_timer` and calls `sci_free_irq()` before `sci_free_dma()`; and `sci_release_port()`/`serial_txx9_release_resource()` set `port->membase = NULL` after `iounmap()` while `sci_request_port()`/`serial_txx9_request_resource()` release the mem region on `ioremap()` failure. [F185, F186, F187, F296]
- In `sh-sci.c`, `scix_early_console_setup()` uses `sci_ports[0]` (`port_cfg` must not be `__initdata`) and sets `sci_uart_earlycon = true`: `sci_probe()` returns `-EBUSY` if `mapbase != res->start` probes into `sci_ports[0]` while `sci_uart_earlycon` is true; `sci_probe_single()` on the earlycon port calls `pm_runtime_get_noresume(&dev->dev)` and sets `sci_uart_earlycon_dev_probing = true` before `uart_add_one_port()`; and `early_console_exit()` zeroes `sci_ports[0]` unless `sci_uart_earlycon_dev_probing` is true. [F239, F320, F321]
- `stm32_usart_startup()` calls `free_irq()` if `stm32_usart_rx_dma_start_or_resume()` fails, and `stm32_usart_rx_dma_start_or_resume()` clears `rx_dma_busy = false` (and calls `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` if `dmaengine_submit()` failed) on error. `stm32_usart_serial_en_wakeup()` with `rx_ch` pauses/drains/terminates RX DMA on wakeup enable and restarts it on disable. `sprd_probe()` failure and `sprd_remove()` clear `sprd_port[index] = NULL`, decrement `sprd_ports_num` (unregistering `sprd_uart_driver` at 0), and call `sprd_rx_free_buf()`. `sifive_serial_probe()` error paths and `sifive_serial_remove()` unwind `uart_add_one_port()`, `__ssp_add_console_port()`, `request_irq()`, and `clk_notifier_register()` in reverse. [F189, F190, F191, F315]

## API Contracts

- `uart_handle_dcd_change()`, `uart_handle_cts_change()`, and `uart_write_wakeup()` require `uport->lock`; `uart_xchar_out()` requires `uport->serial_out`; `uart_insert_char()` callers must call `tty_flip_buffer_push()`; and `uart_port_startup()`/`uart_startup()` return `1` on `PORT_UNKNOWN` or `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` startup failure (only set initialized on `0` and map `> 0` to `0`). `uart_update_timeout()`/`uart_get_divisor()` require non-zero `baud`, and `uart_update_timeout()` uses `(u64)tty_get_frame_size(cflag) * NSEC_PER_SEC` with `DIV64_U64_ROUND_UP()`. [F200, F210, F280, F281, F344, F345]
- `__uart_read_properties()` always sets `port->iotype` (`UPIO_PORT` if `iobase != 0` else `UPIO_MEM` by default, or `UPIO_UNKNOWN` before `-EINVAL` on invalid `reg-io-width`); with `use_defaults == false` (`uart_read_and_validate_port_properties()`), it allows `irq = 0` and checks `port->mapsize < value` before applying `reg-offset`, whereas `uart_read_port_properties()` requires an IRQ. [F214, F236, F352]
- `mctrl_gpio_init*()` returns `ERR_PTR` on failure and `NULL` when `!CONFIG_GPIOLIB` (check `IS_ERR()`, keep `enum mctrl_gpio_idx`, `mctrl_gpios_desc[]`, and `!CONFIG_GPIOLIB` stubs synced). `mctrl_gpio_set()`/`get()`/`get_outputs()` use non-sleeping `gpiod_*`; `mctrl_gpio_set()` compacts output GPIOs into `0..count - 1` of `desc_array` and writes every bit `0..count - 1` of `values` via `__assign_bit()`; and atomic/`port->lock` callers use `mctrl_gpio_disable_ms_no_sync()`. [F211, F238, F257, F293, F328, F329]
- In `sh-sci.c`, `offset` in `sci_serial_in()`/`out()`/`sci_getreg()` is a register enum index and optional registers require checking `sci_getreg(port, REG)->size`; DMA calls use `chan->device->dev` with `dma_sync_single_for_device()` in `sci_dma_tx_work_fn()`; and `sci_port_ops` with `suspend_regs_size() == 0` (`rsci_port_ops`) get `ZERO_SIZE_PTR` in `suspend_regs` and must leave `console_save`/`restore` `NULL`. [F222, F297, F336]

## Error Handling

- `stm32_usart_serial_probe()` resets `rx_ch`/`tx_ch` to `NULL` on non-deferral DMA probe failure; multi-condition ISRs (`stm32_usart_interrupt()`, `sci_mpxed_interrupt()`, `serial_txx9_interrupt()`) start with `IRQ_NONE`; and `serial_port_runtime_suspend()` returns `0` if `!pm_runtime_enabled(dev)`. In `uart_set_info()`, `!UPF_FIXED_PORT` rejects `uartclk = baud_base * 16 == 0`, and `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` rejects `irq < 0`, `irq >= irq_get_nr_irqs()`, or `baud_base < 9600`. [F212, F217, F255, F307]

## External Contracts

- `uart_get_info()`, `uart_set_info()`, and `port_show()` convert `close_delay`/`closing_wait` between jiffies and centiseconds (preserving `ASYNC_CLOSING_WAIT_NONE`), split/combine `iobase` and `port`/`port_high` via `HIGH_BITS_OFFSET`, and zero `*retinfo` at `uart_get_info()` entry before `if (!uport)`. In `uart_set_info()`, non-`CAP_SYS_ADMIN` may only change `UPF_USR_MASK` and `custom_divisor`, and `change_irq || change_port` requires `security_locked_down(LOCKDOWN_TIOCSSERIAL)`. `serial_rs485` fields come from `padding0`/`padding1` (zeroed in `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485()`), and `uart_set_iso7816_config()` rejects non-zero `iso7816.reserved` words. [F224, F251, F253, F254, F347, F350]

## Known Pitfalls

- `uart_line_info()` `INFOBIT`/`STATBIT` strings in `char stat_buf[32]` start with `'|'` and fit in `sizeof(stat_buf) - 2`. In `stm32-usart.c` and `sh-sci.c`, DT `uart-has-rtscts`/`st,hw-flow-ctrl` fails probe with `-EINVAL` if `UART_GPIO_CTS` or `UART_GPIO_RTS` is in `gpios`. [F292, F311]
- In `sh-sci.c`, `PORT_SCI` must not set `SCSCR_TE` before `SCSCR_TIE` (set with/after `SCSCR_TIE` in `sci_start_tx()`); `SCFCR` writes after `sci_reset()` mask out `~(SCFCR_RFRST | SCFCR_TFRST)`; `sci_tx_empty()` returns `TIOCSER_TEMT` if `!s->tx_occurred` (`sci_dma_check_tx_occurred()`); `sci_txfill()`/`rxfill()` mask with `(fifosize << 1) - 1`; `sci_receive_chars()` decrements both `count` and `i` on `uart_handle_sysrq_char()`; and in `sci_request_irq()`, when `i = SCIx_MUX_IRQ` (`== SCIx_NR_IRQS`), index `port->irqstr` by `j` and take `irq` from `up->irq`. [F241, F260, F261, F274, F319]
- In `sci_set_termios()`, `sci_sck_calc()`/`brg_calc()`/`scbrr_calc()` write temporaries (`brr1`, `srr1`, `cks1`, `dl1`), check `bps > UINT_MAX / prediv`, and clamp `br`/`dl` to `>= 1U`; `HSSRR` casts `srr + 1` and `baud` to `int` in `DIV_ROUND_CLOSEST()` and masks `shift << HSCIF_SRHP_SHIFT` with `HSCIF_SRHP_MASK`; and `sifive.c`/`sh-sci.c` divisor and delay calculations use `DIV_ROUND_UP`. [F278, F284, F285, F288]
- In `stm32_usart_receive_chars_pio()`, clear `sr & USART_SR_ERR_MASK` in `ofs->icr` before `stm32_usart_get_char_pio()`, and set `sr |= USART_SR_DUMMY_RX` on every byte for `!CREAD`; with software RS485 RTS, `stm32_usart_start_tx()`/`transmit_chars()` enable `USART_CR1_TCIE` instead of deasserting RTS when `xmit_fifo` empties. In `serial_txx9.c`, `serial_txx9_initialize()` waits `udelay(1)` after `TXX9_SIFCR_SWRST`; `serial_txx9_pm()` skips init when `oldstate == -1`; and console ports skip init in `serial_txx9_config_port()` and FIFO reset/RX-TX disable in `serial_txx9_shutdown()`. [F245, F247, F267, F313, F323]
