# BLUETOOTH DRIVERS — drivers/bluetooth (part 2)

Implements Bluetooth host controller transport drivers for USB (`btusb`), SDIO (`btsdio`, `btmtksdio`), PCMCIA (`dtl1_cs`), RSI (`btrsi`), and UART/Serdev (`hci_bcm`, `hci_ag6xx`, `hci_ath`), plus shared vendor helpers (`btmtk`, `btqca`, `btrtl`) for firmware loading, vendor HCI commands, PM, coredumps, and packet framing.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In USB packet reassembly (`btusb_recv_intr`, `btusb_recv_bulk`, `btusb_recv_isoc`, `btmtk_recv_isopkt`), once the HCI header is complete, check `skb_tailroom(skb) < hci_skb_expect(skb)` and free `skb` with `-EILSEQ` on overflow; in `btusb_recv_isoc`, when `skb->len == HCI_SCO_HDR_SIZE`, also validate the handle via `btusb_validate_sco_handle()` (unless `HCI_USER_CHANNEL` is set). [F94, F180]
- In `btusb.c`, `data->isoc` and `data->diag` are optional and can be `NULL` (`BTUSB_AMP`, `BTUSB_BROKEN_ISOC`, or failed claim), so check for `NULL` before dereferencing. When `btusb_switch_alt_setting()` changes `data->isoc_altsetting`, kill `data->isoc_anchor`, free/NULL `data->sco_skb` under `data->rxlock`, and call `__set_isoc_interface()` to re-resolve `isoc_tx_ep`/`isoc_rx_ep`. When `data->sco_num == 0` in `btusb_work()`, kill `isoc_anchor`, clear `BTUSB_ISOC_RUNNING`, call `__set_isoc_interface(hdev, 0)`, and clear `BTUSB_DID_ISO_RESUME` with `usb_autopm_put_interface()`. [F50, F55, F56]
- In `btusb.c`, TX URBs using `btusb_tx_complete` or `btusb_isoc_tx_complete` must set `urb->context = skb` and `skb->dev = (void *)hdev`. Opcodes in `btusb_hci_drv_supported_commands[]` must stay in lockstep with `btusb_hci_drv_common_handlers[]` and `btusb_hci_drv_specific_handlers[]`. [F62, F128]
- Disable runtime autosuspend during coredumps (`0xfc6f` in `btmtk_usb_recv_acl`/`btmtksdio_recv_acl`, or `seqno == 0` in `handle_dump_pkt_qca`). `handle_dump_pkt_qca()` requires prior validation (`acl_pkt_is_dump_qca`/`evt_pkt_is_dump_qca`), pulls `offsetof(struct qca_dump_hdr, data0)` on `seqno == 0` vs `data` on `seqno > 0`, sets `BTUSB_HW_SSR_ACTIVE`, and at `out:` must re-enable autosuspend, zero `ram_dump_size`/`ram_dump_seqno`, and clear `BTUSB_HW_SSR_ACTIVE`. [F61, F95, F156]
- In `btmtk_process_coredump()`, verify `skb->len > MTK_COREDUMP_END_LEN` before indexing `skb->data[skb->len - MTK_COREDUMP_END_LEN]`; `HCI_DEVCOREDUMP_IDLE` must `fallthrough;` to `HCI_DEVCOREDUMP_ACTIVE` after `hci_devcd_init()` so the initial `skb` is appended. [F85, F107]
- In `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtksdio_open()` sets `BTMTKSDIO_FUNC_ENABLED`, and `btmtksdio_close()` (which clears it), `remove`, and PM callbacks must check it before touching SDIO. `btmtksdio_reset()` sets `BTMTKSDIO_HW_RESET_ACTIVE`, so `btmtksdio_shutdown()` must skip WMT commands when set, and `btmtksdio_setup()` must clear `BT_DL_STATE` in `MT7921_DLSTATUS` and `BTMTKSDIO_HW_RESET_ACTIVE`. [F64, F65]
- In `btmtksdio_rx_packet()`, after validating `rx_size == le16_to_cpu(sdio_hdr->len)` and pulling `sdio_hdr`, verify `pad_size = skb->len - pkts[i].hlen - dlen >= 0` and `skb_trim()` before `pkts[i].recv()`. In `btsdio_rx_packet()`, verify `4 <= len <= 65543` before subtracting 4. [F89, F97]
- In `btrtl.c`, `btrtl_initialize()` returns `btrtl_dev->ic_info == NULL` when unmatched (retrying `check_version:` at most once via `drop_fw`), so `btrtl_download_firmware()`, `btrtl_set_quirks()`, and `btrtl_setup_realtek()` must NULL-check `ic_info`. Match firmware 1-based chip IDs against `btrtl_dev->rom_version + 1`; pull both `sizeof(*common_subsec)` and `subsec_len` via `rtl_iov_pull_data()` before any `continue` in `btrtl_parse_section()`; reject `length == 0` in `rtlbt_parse_firmware()`; and validate `cfg_len` and `entry->len` (`>= 4` and `>= 13`) in `btrtl_get_uart_settings()`. [F42, F90, F93, F112, F113]
- In `btqca.c`, `qca_download_firmware()` must copy `fw->data` into a writable `vmalloc()` buffer before `qca_tlv_check_data()`, which mutates `TLV_TYPE_NVM` bytes in place and sets both `config->dnld_mode` and `config->dnld_type` (since `dnld_mode` is overwritten on the last segment while `dnld_type` gates `qca_inject_cmd_complete_event()`). [F69, F70]
- In `qca_read_fw_board_id()`, check `skb->len >= 3` after pulling `edl`. In `btusb_generate_qca_nvm_name()`, select `"_gf"` from `ver->ram_version` independently of `qca_extract_board_id(ver)` (which maps `0xffff` to `0`). In `qca_uart_setup()`, `QCA_WCN6855`/`QCA_QCA2066`/`QCA_WCN7850` must use `qca_read_fw_board_id()` and `qca_get_nvm_name_by_board()`, and `QCA_WCN3950` maps `SOC_ID_T`/`SOC_ID_S` to `"t"`/`"s"`. [F80, F142, F143]
- In `bcm_suspend_device()`, if `set_device_wakeup()` fails after setting `bdev->is_suspended = true` and disabling flow control, clear `is_suspended` and re-enable flow control (`hci_uart_set_flow_control(bdev->hu, false)`). In `ath_enqueue()`, extract `ath->cur_sleep` from `HCI_OP_ATH_SLEEP` before `skb_push(skb, 1)` and set `HCI_UART_SENDING` before scheduling `ctxtsw`. In `ag6xx_setup()`, check `fw->data + fw->size > pbn->data + plen` (rejecting `<=`) before `intel_mem_write()` to preserve room for the `0xffffffff` sentinel. [F75, F76, F96]

## Synchronization
- In `btusb.c` and `btmtk.c`, hold `data->rxlock` (or `isorxlock`) with `spin_lock_irqsave()` whenever accessing or freeing `evt_skb`, `acl_skb`, `sco_skb`, or `isopkt_skb`, setting pointers to `NULL` when freed or handed off; inside `rxlock` in `btusb_free_frags()` and `btusb_switch_alt_setting()`, use `dev_kfree_skb_irq()`, not `kfree_skb()`. [F8, F31, F157]
- In `btusb.c`, acquire `data->txlock` with `spin_lock_irqsave()`/`spin_lock_irq()` and update `BTUSB_SUSPENDING` and `data->tx_in_flight` together under it. Gate suspend/resume on `data->suspend_count` (`++` from 0, `--` to 0), decrementing it if `btusb_suspend()` aborts on `tx_in_flight`. Guard disabling `data->oob_wake_irq` in `btusb_oob_wake_handler()` (using `disable_irq_nosync()`) and `btusb_resume()` with `test_and_clear_bit(BTUSB_OOB_WAKE_ENABLED, &data->flags)`. [F57, F58, F159, F162]
- In `btmtk.c` and `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()` must wait on `BTMTK_TX_WAIT_VND_EVT` with `TASK_UNINTERRUPTIBLE`; WMT event receivers (`btmtk_usb_wmt_recv`, `btmtksdio_recv_event`) must assign `evt_skb` before `test_and_clear_bit()` and call `smp_mb__after_atomic()` before `wake_up_bit()`. [F22, F166]
- In `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtksdio_open()` must clear `C_INT_EN_CLR` in `MTK_REG_CHLPCR` and mask `MTK_REG_CHIER` before `sdio_claim_irq()`, setting `C_INT_EN_SET` only after configuring `CHCR`/`CHIER`. `btmtksdio_close()` must write `C_INT_EN_CLR`, `sdio_release_irq()`, and `cancel_work_sync(&bdev->txrx_work)` before `btmtksdio_fw_pmctrl()` and `sdio_disable_func()`. Serialize `btmtksdio_fw_pmctrl()`/`btmtksdio_drv_pmctrl()` with `sdio_claim_host()` and poll `MTK_REG_CHLPCR` (and `MTK_REG_PD2HRM0R` when applicable). [F29, F133, F150]
- In `hci_bcm.c`, hold `bcm_device_lock` whenever accessing `bcm_device_list`, calling `bcm_device_exists()`, or dereferencing a platform `bcm->dev` or `bdev->hu`. [F38]

## Object Lifetimes
- `btmtk_process_coredump()` unconditionally consumes `skb` (via `kfree_skb()` or `hci_devcd_append()`, which consumes `skb` even on error); callers (`btmtk_process_coredump`, `handle_dump_pkt_qca`) must read all `skb->data`/`skb->len` fields before calling `hci_devcd_append()` and never free or inspect `skb` after. [F1, F2]
- `hdev->send` callbacks must not free `skb` on error because the caller frees it. In `btusb.c`, `submit_tx_urb()`, `submit_or_queue_tx_urb()`, and `play_deferred()` consume the URB reference, and `kfree(urb->setup_packet)` must run on every failure or completion. In `btsdio_tx_packet()` and `btmtksdio_tx_packet()`, on `sdio_writesb()` failure after `skb_push()`, strip the header with `skb_pull()` and do not free `skb` (the worker requeues it). [F10, F11, F174]
- In `btmtk.c` and `btmtksdio.c`, whenever `evt_skb` is freed via `kfree_skb()`, immediately set `evt_skb = NULL`. In `btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()` and `btmtk_usb_shutdown()`, balance `usb_autopm_get_interface(data->intf)` with `usb_autopm_put_interface()` on all paths. [F7, F152]
- In `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtksdio_probe()` must call `pm_runtime_put_noidle()` (paired with `pm_runtime_get_noresume()` in `remove`) and restore `bdev->dev->of_node` with `of_node_put()` after `of_find_compatible_node()`. If `btmtksdio_reset_setting()` fails in `setup`, call `devm_gpiod_put()` and set `bdev->reset = NULL`. In `btmtksdio_remove()`, call `btmtksdio_close()` if `BTMTKSDIO_FUNC_ENABLED` is set before unregistering `hdev`. [F16, F17, F18, F134]
- Release `btrtl_initialize()` results via `btrtl_free()`, not `kfree()`. `struct rtl_subsection::data` borrows from `btrtl_dev->fw_data`, so `fw_data` must outlive `rtlbt_parse_firmware_v2()` and `btrtl_free()` must only `kfree(entry)` via `list_for_each_entry_safe()` (while `btrtl_insert_ordered_subsec()` uses raw `struct list_head *pos`). In `qca_download_firmware()`, every error path after `vmalloc()` must `goto out` to `vfree(data)`. [F4, F5, F6, F109]
- In `hci_bcm.c`, if `clk_is_match(dev->lpo_clk, dev->txco_clk)`, `devm_clk_put(dev->txco_clk)` and set it to `NULL`. Gate regulator/clock toggles in `bcm_gpio_set_power()` on `dev->res_enabled` and non-NULL `dev->supplies[0].supply`. Only clear `bdev->hu = NULL` in `bcm_open()`/`bcm_close()` when `!hu->serdev`, and in `bcm_close()`, when `bdev->irq_acquired` is set, call `devm_free_irq()`, `device_init_wakeup(false)`, and `pm_runtime_disable()` under `bcm_device_lock`. [F21, F74, F139, F155]

## API Contracts
- Every declaration under `#if IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_BT_MTK/QCA/RTL)` in `btmtk.h`, `btqca.h`, and `btrtl.h` must have a matching `#else` stub returning `-EOPNOTSUPP` or `ERR_PTR(-EOPNOTSUPP)`. `hdev` passed to `btrtl_*`/`btrealtek_*` requires `sizeof(struct btrealtek_data)` via `hci_alloc_dev_priv()`, and `btmtk_*`/`alloc_mtk_intr_urb()` requires `sizeof(struct btmtk_data)` (so `btmtksdio` must not call them). Access `hdev->quirk_flags` and `realtek->flags` only via bitmap helpers (`hci_set_quirk`, `btrealtek_set_flag`, etc.). [F78, F126, F127, F132]
- TX URB allocators (`alloc_ctrl_urb`, `alloc_bulk_urb`, `alloc_isoc_urb`, `alloc_diag_urb`, `alloc_mtk_intr_urb`) return `ERR_PTR(-errno)`, never `NULL`. RX URB submissions in `btusb_resume()` and `btmtk_usb_resume()` must use `GFP_NOIO`. In `alloc_mtk_intr_urb()`, call `btmtk_isopkt_pad()` (`skb_pad()` + `__skb_put()` to `MTK_ISO_THRESHOLD`) before `usb_fill_int_urb()` captures `skb->data`/`skb->len`. [F49, F129, F160, F181]
- In `btusb.c`, bypass SCO connection checks when `HCI_USER_CHANNEL` is set. In `btusb_hci_drv_read_info()`, size `rp` using `num_supported_commands * sizeof(__le16)`, set `rp->num_supported_commands` before indexing `rp->supported_commands[]` (`__counted_by_le`), and fill `rp->driver_name` with `strscpy_pad()`. In `btmtksdio_get_codec_config_data()`, NULL-check and zero `*ven_data`/`*ven_len` on entry and `kfree(*ven_data)` + NULL it on error. In `struct bcm_device`, `serdev_hu` must remain the first member. [F53, F73, F104, F124, F125]
- Coredump triggers (`btusb_coredump_qca`, `btmtk_coredump`, `btrtl_coredump`) must use `__hci_cmd_send()`, not `__hci_cmd_sync()`. Inject synthetic `HCI_EV_CMD_COMPLETE` via `hci_recv_frame()` when suppressed by firmware (`qca_inject_cmd_complete_event`, `inject_cmd_complete`). Pass `0` (not `HCI_EV_VENDOR`) as `event` to `__hci_cmd_sync_ev()` for QCA commands returning `HCI_EV_CMD_COMPLETE` (including `soc_type >= QCA_WCN3991`). [F44, F172, F173]

## Error Handling
- Do not propagate `-EOPNOTSUPP` from `hci_devcd_register()` or `btmtk_register_coredump()` to abort probe or setup. Call `hci_cmd_sync_cancel()` in `btusb` when interrupt URB submission fails (`err != -EPERM`) or a command URB completes with non-zero status. [F45, F183]
- In `btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()` and `mtk_hci_wmt_sync()`, a positive return from `wait_on_bit_timeout()` must clear the wait bit and set `err = -ETIMEDOUT`; when polling `btmtk_usb_func_query()`/`btmtksdio_func_query()`, check both `err < 0` and signed `status < 0`. In `btqca.c` helpers initializing `err = 0`, set a negative errno before every `goto out`. [F43, F47, F48]

## External Contracts & Known Pitfalls
- In `btqca.c`, `soc_type >= QCA_WCN3991` responses have a 1-byte status prefix at `edl->data[0]` (`qca_btsoc_version` at `edl->data + 1`) and no `tlv_seg_resp`. `qca_set_bdaddr()` must `baswap()` its `bdaddr_t` argument (unlike `qca_set_bdaddr_rome()`/`btusb_set_bdaddr_wcn6855()`). `qca_read_fw_build_info()` must `kstrndup()` the un-terminated label at `&edl->data[1]` after bounds-checking `skb->len`. In `qca_tlv_check_data()` (`TLV_TYPE_NVM`), use `length + (tlv->data - fw_data)` for the `fw_size` check after advancing `tlv++`. [F83, F84, F130, F131]
- In MediaTek WMT commands, verify `hlen <= 255` and set `hdr->dlen = cpu_to_le16(wmt_params->dlen + 1)`; convert `btmtk_hci_wmt_evt_funcc::status` (`__be16`) with `be16_to_cpu()`; and in `btmtk_usb_recv_acl()`/`btmtksdio_recv_acl()`, `case 0xfc6f:` must pass `skb_clone()` (in USB) to `btmtk_process_coredump()` and fall through to `hci_recv_diag()`. [F86, F87, F108]
- Never call `hdev->reset(hdev)` inside `btusb_reset()` or vendor `hdev->reset` callbacks. For CSR `0a12:0001`, use `wMaxPacketSize` in `btusb_submit_intr_urb()` and apply fake-CSR quirks in `btusb_setup_csr()`. In `rsi_hci_send_pkt()`, preserve `bt_cb(new_skb)->pkt_type` on realloc and use `memmove()` (not `memcpy()`) after sampling `skb->data`/`skb->len` for `RSI_DMA_ALIGN`. In `hci_bcm.c`, match `hu->tty->dev->parent == dev->dev->parent` in `bcm_open()`, drive `dev->reset` with `!powered` in `bcm_gpio_set_shutdown()`, and select `acpi_bcm_int_last_gpios` when `dev->irq != 0` in `bcm_acpi_probe()`. [F72, F105, F115, F123, F141, F145, F146]
