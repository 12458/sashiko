# NETWORKING [IPv4/IPv6] — net/ipv6 (part 2)

Implements IPv6 input/output pipelines (`ip6_input.c`, `ip6_output.c`, `ip6_offload.c`, `ip6_checksum.c`), the IPv6 FIB radix tree (`ip6_fib.c`), ICMPv6 (`icmp.c`, `ip6_icmp.c`), IPv6 Flow Labels (`ip6_flowlabel.c`), IPv6 GRE/ERSPAN tunnels (`ip6_gre.c`), IOAM (`ioam6.c`, `ioam6_iptunnel.c`), and ILA (`ila/*`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `struct fib6_info`, `fib6_nh[]` is allocated only when `f6i->nh == NULL` (`with_fib6_nh`), and `fib6_siblings` shares a union with `nh_list`: check `f6i->nh` before touching `f6i->fib6_nh` or `f6i->fib6_siblings`. [F594]
- In `ip6_fib.c`, `fib6_add_1()`, `fib6_node_lookup_1()`, and `fib6_locate_1()` must cast `leaf` (`struct fib6_info *`) to `(u8 *)` before adding byte `offset` (`fib6_dst`/`fib6_src`) to form `struct rt6key *`; `fib6_add_1()` and `fib6_locate_1()` must check `plen < fn->fn_bit` and `plen == fn->fn_bit` (and `plen > bit` when splitting) before `addr_bit_set()` so the bit index is in `0..127`; and the `struct lookup_args` array passed to `fib6_node_lookup_1()` must end with `{ .offset = 0 }`. [F631, F662, F677]
- Removing a route in `fib6_del_route()` or a node in `fib6_repair_tree()` must adjust active `fib6_walker` cursors in `net->ipv6.fib6_walkers` under `net->ipv6.fib6_walker_lock` and reset `fn->rr_ptr` if it points to the removed route; in `fib6_clean_node()`, when `c->func()` returns `-1`, set `w->leaf = rt` before `fib6_del(rt, &info)` and return `0` immediately on success without continuing `for_each_fib6_walker_rt(w)` or overwriting `w->leaf`. [F607, F646]
- `fib6_new_sernum()` must wrap `net->ipv6.fib6_sernum` from `INT_MAX` to `1` and never return `0` (`FIB6_NO_SERNUM_CHANGE`/unset `rt->sernum`), and adding/deleting routes with `fib6_requires_src(rt)` (`rt->fib6_src.plen > 0`) in `fib6_add()`/`fib6_del()` must call `fib6_routes_require_src_inc()`/`dec()` in lockstep. [F611, F667]
- In `__ip6_append_data()`, check `mtu <= fragheaderlen`, `((mtu - fragheaderlen) & ~7) + fragheaderlen <= sizeof(struct frag_hdr)`, and `headersize + transhdrlen > mtu` before computing `maxfraglen`; zero `transhdrlen`, `exthdrlen`, `dst_exthdrlen`, `cork->tx_flags`, and `tskey` after queueing the first fragment; and enable `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` only for unfragmented `IPPROTO_UDP` with `headersize == sizeof(struct ipv6hdr)` and `NETIF_F_IPV6_CSUM | NETIF_F_HW_CSUM`, and `zc = true` only with `NETIF_F_SG` and `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL`. [F618, F641, F709]
- Non-final fragment payloads after `hlen + sizeof(struct frag_hdr)` in `ip6_fragment()`, `ip6_frag_next()`, `ip6_fraglist_prepare()`, and `ipv6_gso_segment()` must be a non-zero multiple of 8 bytes (`& ~7`); in `ip6_finish_output_gso_slowpath_drop()`, call `ip6_fragment()` only when `segs->len > mtu` and pass `segs->len <= mtu` directly to `ip6_finish_output2()`. [F640, F711]
- In `ip6_forward()`, do not decrement `hdr->hop_limit` before `ip6_forward_proxy_check(skb) > 0` passes packets to `ip6_input(skb)` or before `skb_cow(skb, dev->hard_header_len)` and reloading `hdr = ipv6_hdr(skb)`; in `ip6_sk_dst_check()`, verify `dst->ops->family == AF_INET6` before `dst_rt6_info(dst)`. [F679, F710]
- In `ip6_rcv_core()`, set `IP6CB(skb)->iif` from `ip6_dst_idev(skb_dst(skb))->dev->ifindex` when `skb_valid_dst(skb)` and `dev->ifindex` otherwise; in `ip6_protocol_deliver_rcu()` and `ip6_mc_input()`, when `inet6_sdif(skb)` is non-zero (VRF), pass `dev_get_by_index_rcu(net, sdif)` rather than `skb->dev` to `ipv6_chk_mcast_addr()`. [F703, F705]
- In `ipv6_gso_segment()`, after `ipv6_gso_pull_exthdrs()`, abort if `!skb_reset_transport_header_careful(skb)`; in `sit_ip6ip6_gro_receive()` and `ip4ip6_gro_receive()`, if `NAPI_GRO_CB(skb)->encap_mark` is already set, set `NAPI_GRO_CB(skb)->flush = 1` and return `NULL`. [F624, F708]
- In `icmpv6_mask_allow()`, check `type > ICMPV6_MSG_MAX` before `test_bit(type, net->ipv6.sysctl.icmpv6_ratemask)`; in `ip6_err_gen_icmpv6_unreach()`, reset RFC 4884 `data_len = 0` unless `data_len >= 128`, `(data_len & 7) == 0`, and `skb->len >= data_len` after `pskb_may_pull(skb, nhs + sizeof(struct ipv6hdr) + 8)`. [F634, F681]
- In `ip6_gre.c`: reconfiguring a tunnel (`ip6gre_changelink()`, `ip6erspan_changelink()`, `SIOCCHGTUNNEL`) must unlink from `ign->tunnels`, update `t->parms`, reset `t->dst_cache` via `ip6gre_tnl_copy_tnl_parm()`, and relink; `ign->fb_tunnel_dev` (`ip6gre0`) must have `netns_immutable = true` and be rejected/ignored in `changelink` (`-EINVAL`), `dellink`, and `SIOCDELTUNNEL` (`-EPERM`); in `ip6gre_exit_rtnl_net()`, guard the `ign->tunnels` pass with `if (!net_eq(dev_net(t->dev), net))`; and in `ip6erspan_tunnel_xmit()` with `collect_md`, verify `IP_TUNNEL_ERSPAN_OPT_BIT` and `tun_info->options_len >= sizeof(*md)` before reading `ip_tunnel_info_opts(tun_info)`. [F613, F636, F658, F713]
- In `ioam6`: keep `tuninfo` last in `struct ioam6_lwt` and `traceh` last in `struct ioam6_lwt_encap`; gate `ilwt->tundst` on `mode != IOAM6_IPTUNNEL_MODE_INLINE` and `ilwt->tunsrc` also on `ilwt->has_tunsrc`; ensure `1 <= ilwt->freq.k <= ilwt->freq.n <= IOAM6_IPTUNNEL_FREQ_MAX` with `ilwt->pkt_cnt` read as `u32` before `% ilwt->freq.n`; multiply 4-octet word fields (`remlen`, `nodelen`, `sclen`) by 4 for byte lengths/offsets; and in `ioam6_genl_addsc()`, keep `sc->len = ALIGN(len, 4) <= IOAM6_MAX_SCHEMA_DATA_LEN` (`255 * 4`) in zero-allocated `sizeof(*sc) + len_aligned`. [F592, F626, F628, F663, F671]
- In `ila`: in `ila_update_ipv6_locator()`, run checksum adjustment before `iaddr->loc = p->locator`, and in `ila_csum_adjust_transport()` call `pskb_may_pull(skb, sizeof(struct ipv6hdr) + sizeof(struct <l4hdr>))` before reading the L4 header; in `ila_add_mapping()` and `ila_del_mapping()`, replacing/deleting the head `ila_map` when `ila->next` is non-NULL must use `rhashtable_replace_fast()`; and in `ila_xlat_nl_dump()`, use `rhashtable_walk_peek(rhiter)` to resume `ila->next` with `iter->skip` and reset `skip = 0` before `rhashtable_walk_next(rhiter)`. [F621, F623, F639, F656]
- In `struct ip6_flowlabel`, `fl->owner.pid` is valid only when `fl->share == IPV6_FL_S_PROCESS` and `fl->owner.uid` only when `fl->share == IPV6_FL_S_USER`. [F600]

## Synchronization
- FIB locking: FIB mutations (`fib6_add()`, `fib6_del()`) and walks (`fib6_walk()`, `fib6_walk_continue()`) must hold `spin_lock_bh(&table->tb6_lock)` and use `GFP_ATOMIC`; lock nesting is `fib6_gc_lock` -> `table->tb6_lock` -> `fib6_walker_lock` (and `rt->nh->lock` inside `table->tb6_lock` in `fib6_add_rt2node_nh()` and `fib6_purge_rt()` for `rt->nh->dead` and `rt->nh_list`); and `fib6_net_exit()` must call `timer_delete_sync(&net->ipv6.ip6_fib_timer)` before freeing `fib6_table` entries. [F574, F575, F689, F690]
- FIB RCU & sernum: `__fib6_update_sernum_upto_root()` must run `smp_wmb()` before `WRITE_ONCE(fn->fn_sernum, ...)`, pairing with `smp_rmb()` after `READ_ONCE(fn->fn_sernum)` in `fib6_get_cookie_safe()`; resuming `fib6_walk_continue()` after dropping `tb6_lock` must check `READ_ONCE(w->root->fn_sernum)` and reset `w->state = FWS_INIT`, `w->node = w->root`, and `w->skip = w->count` if changed; detaching a multipath sibling in `fib6_del_route()` or `fib6_add_rt2node()` must use `WRITE_ONCE(rt->fib6_nsiblings, 0)` and `list_del_rcu(&rt->fib6_siblings)`; and published `fib6_node`s removed in `fib6_repair_tree()` must be freed via `node_free()` (`kfree_rcu()`), not `node_free_immediate()`. [F570, F572, F643, F691]
- `ip6_output()`, `ip6_xmit()`, and the `skb_has_frag_list(skb)` fastpath of `ip6_fragment()` must hold `rcu_read_lock()` across the entire output/fragment loop and `ip6_dst_idev()` stats updates, obtaining `dev` via `dst_dev_rcu(dst)`; `ip6_xmit()` and `NF_INET_LOCAL_OUT` hooks must not assume `lock_sock(sk)` is held. [F682, F684, F685]
- `icmpv6_xmit_lock()` callers (`icmp6_send()`, `icmpv6_echo_reply()`) must disable BH first, take per-CPU `ipv6_icmp_sk` via `spin_trylock(&sk->sk_lock.slock)`, and unlock via `icmpv6_xmit_unlock()` which restores `sock_net_set(sk, &init_net)` before unlocking; lockless RCU lookups in `fl_lookup()`, `__fl6_sock_lookup()`, and `ipv6_flowlabel_get()` must pin `fl` with `atomic_inc_not_zero(&fl->users)` under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F577, F579]
- In `ioam6.c`, mutations to `nsdata->namespaces`, `nsdata->schemas`, `ns->schema`, and `sc->ns` require `nsdata->lock`, and `ioam6_genl_delns()`/`delsc()` must clear the peer's RCU pointer before `kfree_rcu()`; in `ila_xlat.c`, `ila_lookup_wildcards()`/`by_params()` require `rcu_read_lock()`, while bucket mutations in `ila_add_mapping()`, `ila_del_mapping()`, and `ila_xlat_nl_cmd_flush()` must hold `ila_get_lock()` and free `ila_map` via `kfree_rcu()`. [F583, F698]
- In `ioam6_output()` and `ila_output()`, bracket per-CPU `dst_cache_get()` and `dst_cache_set_ip6()` with `local_bh_disable()`/`enable()`, do not cache `dst` directly when `orig_dst->lwtstate == dst->lwtstate`, and in `ila_output()` call `skb_dst_drop(skb)` before `skb_dst_set(skb, dst)`. [F553, F584]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `ip6_fib.c`: in `fib6_purge_rt()`, when `refcount_read(&rt->fib6_ref) != 1`, replace `fn->leaf == rt` on ancestor intermediate nodes (`!(fn->fn_flags & RTN_RTINFO)`) with `fib6_find_prefix()`; before `fib6_info_destroy_rcu()`, `rt->fib6_node` must be `NULL` and `fib6_drop_pcpu_from()` must set `f6i->fib6_destroying = 1` with a memory barrier before `xchg(&pcpu_rt->from, NULL)`; and callers that suspend `fib6_walk()`/`fib6_walk_continue()` (`res > 0`) must call `fib6_walker_unlink()` before freeing `w`. [F559, F560, F566]
- When coalescing `tmp_skb` onto `frag_list` in `__ip6_make_skb()` (and `ip6_fragment()`), add `tmp_skb->truesize` to `skb->truesize` and clear `tmp_skb->destructor` and `tmp_skb->sk` to `NULL`; once `fl_create()` succeeds, free `fl` via `fl_free(fl)` (not `kfree(fl)`); in `ip6_gre.c`, `ndo_uninit` unlinks the tunnel and calls `netdev_put(dev, &t->dev_tracker)` while `ip6gre_dev_free()` (`priv_destructor`) destroys `t->gro_cells` and `t->dst_cache`; and in `ila_add_mapping()`, serialize lazy `ila_nf_hook_ops` registration under `ila_mutex` and unregister in `ila_xlat_pre_exit_net()` before `ila_xlat_exit_net()` destroys `rhash_table`. [F557, F563, F565, F582]

## API Contracts
- `ip6_dst_lookup()` and `ip6_dst_lookup_tail()` release `*dst` and set `*dst = NULL` on error, and `xfrm_lookup()` consumes the input `dst` even on `IS_ERR(dst)`: call `dst_release(dst)` only when `ip6_dst_lookup()` returns 0 and `xfrm_lookup()` did not return `IS_ERR(dst)`. [F567]
- In `ip6_protocol_deliver_rcu()`, `ret > 0` from `ipprot->handler(skb)` resubmits (`ret` is `nexthdr` if `INET6_PROTO_FINAL`, else update `IP6CB(skb)->nhoff` and `skb->transport_header`); in `ip6_rcv_core()`, call `skb_orphan(skb)` when `!skb_sk_is_prefetched(skb)` before `NF_INET_PRE_ROUTING`; in `ip6_fragment()`'s `frag_list` fastpath, call `ip6_fraglist_prepare(skb, &iter)` before `output()` and `ip6_fraglist_next(&iter)` only after `output()` succeeds; and in `ipv6_gro_pull_exthdrs()`, call `skb_gro_header(skb, off + sizeof(*opth), off)` for `ipv6_optlen(opth)` and then `skb_gro_header(skb, off + len, off)` before dereferencing `opth` or advancing `off += len`. [F601, F625, F653, F704]
- `icmpv6_err_convert()` returns `1` if fatal and `0` otherwise while writing a positive errno to `*err`; `icmpv6_notify()` pulls only `inner_offset + 8` bytes before `ipprot->err_handler`, so handlers reading past 8 bytes at `skb->data + offset` must call `pskb_may_pull()`; any `fib6_walker` callback returning `0` after finishing `w->node` must set `w->leaf = NULL`; in `ila_csum_adjust_transport()`, pass `pseudohdr = true` and `ipv6 = true` to `inet_proto_csum_replace_by_diff()`; and in `ip6_flowlabel.c`, `ipv6_flowlabel_renew()` uses `ns_capable(net->user_ns, CAP_NET_ADMIN)` while `check_linger()` and `mem_check()` require `capable(CAP_NET_ADMIN)`, and `ip6fl_seq_show()` must translate `pid` via `pid_nr_ns(fl->owner.pid, state->pid_ns)` and `uid` via `from_kuid_munged(seq_user_ns(seq), fl->owner.uid)`. [F586, F589, F633, F645, F717, F718]

## Error Handling
- In `fib6_add()`, if `fib6_add_1()` returns `ERR_PTR(fn)`, reset `fn = NULL` before `goto out`/`failure`, and if a later step fails after setting `pn->leaf = rt` (`pn != fn`), restore `pn->leaf` before `fib6_repair_tree()`; in `fib6_add_rt2node()`, if `call_fib6_entry_notifiers()` fails after linking `rt->fib6_siblings`, unlink via `list_del_rcu(&rt->fib6_siblings)`, decrement each sibling's `fib6_nsiblings`, reset `rt->fib6_nsiblings = 0`, and call `rt6_multipath_rebalance()`. [F561, F598]
- In `ip6_finish_output2()`, check `__neigh_create()` with `IS_ERR(neigh)`; in `ip6_fragment()`, fast-path failures inside `skb_walk_frags(skb, frag)` must jump to `slow_path_clean` (not `slow_path`) with `frag` unchanged to revert prior fragments' `sk`, `destructor`, and `skb->truesize`; and in `icmpv6_route_lookup()`, if initial `xfrm_lookup()` returns `ERR_PTR(-EPERM)`, reset `dst = NULL` before the reverse-decoded relookup. [F588, F603, F652]

## External Contracts
- In `is_ineligible()` (`IPPROTO_ICMPV6`), check `!tp && frag_off != 0` (`false`) before `!tp || !(*tp & ICMPV6_INFOMSG_MASK)` (`true`); in `fl_create()`, reject `fl->opt->opt_flen != 0`, and in `fl6_merge_options()`, take `hopopt`/`dst0opt`/`srcrt` from `fl->opt` and `dst1opt` from `fopt`; in `ip6erspan_tap_validate()`, check `ntohl(nla_get_be32(...)) & ~ID_MASK` for `IFLA_GRE_IKEY`/`OKEY` and mask v1/v2 ERSPAN fields; and in `ila_build_state()` (`ILA_ATYPE_USE_FORMAT`), require `cfg6->fc_dst_len >= 67`, reject `ILA_ATYPE_IID`, and reject `ILA_CSUM_NEUTRAL_MAP` if `ila_csum_neutral_set(iaddr->ident)` is set. [F637, F659, F715, F716]

## Known Pitfalls
- Refresh pointers into `skb->head` (`ipv6_hdr(skb)`, `pkt_md`, `prevhdr`) after `__iptunnel_pull_header()`, `ip6_tnl_parse_tlv_enc_lim()`, `skb_checksum_help()`, or `skb_cow()`; and use `memmove()` (not `memcpy()`) when shifting headers/payload in-place in `ioam6_do_inline()`, `ipv6_gro_complete()`, and `ip6_err_gen_icmpv6_unreach()`. [F614, F620, F673, F676]
- In `fib6_del_route()` and `fib6_add_rt2node()`, use `list_for_each_entry_safe(sibling, next_sibling, &rt->fib6_siblings, fib6_siblings)` and pass `next_sibling`/`temp_sibling` (not `sibling`/`rt`) to `rt6_multipath_rebalance()`; inside `for_each_fib6_walker_rt(w)` in `fib6_dump_node()` and `fib6_clean_node()`, skip siblings via `rt = list_last_entry(&rt->fib6_siblings, struct fib6_info, fib6_siblings)`; and when unlinking via `*ins = iter->fib6_next` or `*flp = fl->next`, advance `ins`/`flp` only when retaining the entry. [F644, F647, F649]
- In `csum_ipv6_magic()`, fold unsigned 32-bit overflow carry immediately after every addition into `sum`; in `ip6_flowlabel.c`, compare `jiffies` timestamps with `time_after`/`time_before` or `(long)(a - b) > 0`; and in `icmpv6_xrlim_allow()`, pass `NULL` as `idev` to `__ICMP6_INC_STATS(net, NULL, ICMP6_MIB_RATELIMITHOST)`. [F660, F668, F702]
