# SOUND - SOC LAYER / DYNAMIC AUDIO POWER MANAGEMENT (ASoC) — sound/soc/tegra

Implements NVIDIA Tegra30 and Tegra210/186/234/264 Audio Hub (AHUB) crossbar and audio processing module drivers, including I2S, DMIC, AMX, MIXER, MVC, SFC, and OPE (PEQ and MBDRC). These drivers expose DAPM crossbar routing muxes, hardware coefficient RAM programming, and DAI interfaces within the Tegra AHUB pipeline.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Sequential RAM helpers (`tegra210_peq_read_ram`, `tegra210_peq_write_ram`, `tegra210_mbdrc_write_ram`, `tegra210_amx_write_map_ram`, `tegra210_sfc_write_ram`) write `*_ADDR_INIT_EN | *_SEQ_ACCESS_EN` to `*_RAM_CTRL` once and loop over the fixed `*_RAM_DATA` register without incrementing the MMIO address. All `*_RAM_DATA` registers must be marked `.volatile_reg`, and readable FIFO/RAM data registers (`CHANNEL_TXFIFO`, `CHANNEL_RXFIFO`, `TEGRA210_MIXER_*_RAM_DATA`, `TEGRA210_PEQ_CFG_RAM_*DATA`, `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_RAM_DATA`, `TEGRA210_SFC_CFG_RAM_DATA`) must also be marked `.precious_reg`. [F330, F361]
- Volatile RAM and control state is not restored by `regcache_sync()` alone: `tegra210_amx_runtime_resume()` must call `tegra210_amx_write_map_ram(amx)` to reprogram `TEGRA210_AMX_CFG_RAM_DATA` and `TEGRA210_AMX_OUT_BYTE_EN0`; `tegra210_mvc_runtime_suspend()` must save `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL` into `mvc->ctrl_value` before `regcache_cache_only()`, and `tegra210_mvc_runtime_resume()` must rewrite `mvc->ctrl_value` and trigger `TEGRA210_MVC_VOLUME_SWITCH_TRIGGER`; `tegra210_ope_runtime_suspend()` and `tegra210_ope_runtime_resume()` must transition `ope->regmap`, `ope->peq_regmap`, and `ope->mbdrc_regmap` together, calling `tegra210_peq_save()` before enabling cache-only on suspend and `tegra210_peq_restore()` after `regcache_sync()` on resume. [F297, F305, F357]
- `tegra210_amx_startup()` and `tegra210_i2s_init()` must poll `TEGRA210_AMX_STATUS` or `TEGRA210_I2S_RX_STATUS`/`TEGRA210_I2S_TX_STATUS` via `regmap_read_poll_timeout()` to verify the module is disabled before asserting soft reset. In `tegra210_i2s_sw_reset()`, before setting `I2S_SOFT_RESET_EN` in `reset_reg`, the driver must read and save `cif_reg`, `stream_reg`, and `TEGRA210_I2S_CTRL` (`+ i2s_ctrl_offset`), and restore all three after the reset bit self-clears. [F306, F356]
- In `tegra210_i2s.c` and `tegra210_amx.c`, accesses to registers that shifted on Tegra264 (`TEGRA210_I2S_TX_*`, `TEGRA210_I2S_CTRL`, `TEGRA210_I2S_TIMING`, `TEGRA210_I2S_SLOT_CTRL`, `TEGRA210_AMX_CFG_RAM_*`) must add the per-SoC offset (`soc_data->tx_offset`, `soc_data->i2s_ctrl_offset`, or `soc_data->reg_offset`) and use `tegra264_set_cif()` when `soc_data->max_ch` is 32. [F362]
- In `tegra210_i2s`, `client_sample_format` must remain a signed `int` initialized to `-EINVAL` in `tegra210_i2s_probe()` and checked with `i2s->client_sample_format >= 0` in `tegra210_i2s_hw_params()`, because `0` is `SNDRV_PCM_FORMAT_S8`. Also, `tegra210_i2s_set_fmt()` caches `i2s->dai_fmt` and sets `data_offset` immediately except for `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_RIGHT_J`, which must be deferred to `tegra210_i2s_set_timing_params()` (`num_bclk - sample_size`). Finally, `clk_sync_input` is optional: `tegra210_i2s_probe()` must not fail on `IS_ERR(i2s->clk_sync_input)`, and `tegra210_i2s_set_clock_rate()` must guard `clk_set_rate()` with `!IS_ERR(i2s->clk_sync_input)`. [F286, F307, F354]
- In `tegra210_ahub.c` and `tegra210_ahub.h`: `MUX_VALUE(npart, nbit)` encodes crossbar MUX inputs as 1-based global bit positions (`1 + nbit + npart * 32`) while `0` encodes `"None"`, matching 1-based `ffs(reg_val)` in `tegra_ahub_get_value_enum` and `value - 1` in `tegra_ahub_put_value_enum`; each `tegra*_ahub_mux_values` array must match its paired `tegra*_ahub_mux_texts` array in length and element order; and `struct tegra_ahub_soc_data.reg_count` must never exceed `TEGRA_XBAR_UPDATE_MAX_REG` (`update[TEGRA_XBAR_UPDATE_MAX_REG]` stack bound). [F312, F313, F314]
- In `tegra30_ahub.h` and `tegra30_ahub.c`, `enum tegra30_ahub_rxcif` values must equal the 0-based register index (`0..TEGRA30_AHUB_AUDIO_RX_COUNT - 1`) of `TEGRA30_AHUB_AUDIO_RX`, and `enum tegra30_ahub_txcif` values must equal the 0-based bit shift (`1 << txcif`) written by `tegra30_ahub_set_rx_cif_source`. [F323]
- DAI array ordering invariants: in `tegra210_amx.c`, `tegra210_amx_dais` must place the 4 `IN_DAI` entries at indices `0..3` (`dai->id` indexes `AMX_CH_REG`) and `OUT_DAI` at `TEGRA_AMX_OUT_DAI_ID` (`4`); in `tegra210_mixer.c`, `tegra210_mixer_dais` must list all `TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_MAX` (`10`) `IN_DAI` entries at indices `0..9` (`dai->id` indexes `mixer->gain_value[]`) before `OUT_DAI` entries (TX index is `dai->id - TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_MAX`). [F317, F318]
- In `tegra210_sfc.c`: `tegra210_sfc_rates[]` and both dimensions of `coef_addr_table[][]` must have `TEGRA210_SFC_NUM_RATES` (`13`) entries in exact hardware `TEGRA210_SFC_RX_FREQ`/`TEGRA210_SFC_TX_FREQ` index order, with every valid `coef_addr_table` entry pointing to at least `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_DEPTH` (`64`) `s32` values; and `tegra210_sfc_startup()` clears `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_EN` before soft reset while `tegra210_sfc_write_coeff_ram()` must only re-enable `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_EN` when `sfc->srate_in != sfc->srate_out`. [F298, F299, F321]
- In `tegra210_mvc.c`: changing `mvc->curve_type` in `tegra210_mvc_put_curve_type()` requires verifying `TEGRA210_MVC_EN` is not set in `TEGRA210_MVC_ENABLE` and calling `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings()` to reset `mvc->volume[]` and reprogram `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL`, `TEGRA210_MVC_INIT_VOL`, `TEGRA210_MVC_TARGET_VOL`, and `TEGRA210_MVC_SWITCH`; in `tegra210_mvc_update_vol()`, when `per_ch_enable` is false (channel 0 master volume), the driver must clear `TEGRA210_MVC_PER_CHAN_CTRL_EN` in `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL` and propagate `mvc->volume[0]` across `mvc->volume[1..TEGRA210_MVC_MAX_CHAN_COUNT-1]`. [F300, F302]
- Regmap register-offset normalization: in `tegra210_mixer_wr_reg`, `tegra210_mixer_rd_reg`, and `tegra210_mixer_volatile_reg`, `reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_LIMIT` must be checked before `else if (reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_TX_LIMIT)` so RX offsets normalize to `MIXER_REG_BASE(reg)` and TX offsets normalize to `MIXER_REG_BASE(reg) + TEGRA210_MIXER_TX1_ENABLE`; in `tegra210_mbdrc_wr_reg`, `tegra210_mbdrc_rd_reg`, `tegra210_mbdrc_volatile_reg`, and `tegra210_mbdrc_precious_reg`, offsets with `reg >= TEGRA210_MBDRC_IIR_CFG` must be reduced modulo `TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_PARAM_STRIDE * TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_COUNT` before `switch (reg)`. [F327, F328]
- In `tegra210_peq.c`, every control using `tegra210_peq_ram_get` or `tegra210_peq_ram_put` must have `params->soc.num_regs <= TEGRA210_PEQ_GAIN_PARAM_SIZE_PER_CH` (`62`), matching the static `biquad_coeff_buffer[TEGRA210_PEQ_GAIN_PARAM_SIZE_PER_CH]` array. Also, in `struct tegra_soc_bytes`, `struct soc_bytes soc` must remain the first field at offset 0 because `tegra210_peq_param_info` and `tegra210_mbdrc_param_info` cast `kcontrol->private_value` directly to `struct soc_bytes *`. [F319, F338]
- All AHUB and child module `dev_pm_ops` (`tegra_ahub_pm_ops`, `tegra210_amx_pm_ops`, `tegra210_dmic_pm_ops`, `tegra210_i2s_pm_ops`, `tegra210_mixer_pm_ops`, `tegra210_mvc_pm_ops`, `tegra210_ope_pm_ops`, `tegra210_sfc_pm_ops`, `tegra30_ahub_pm_ops`, `tegra30_i2s_pm_ops`) must use normal `SYSTEM_SLEEP_PM_OPS` rather than late system sleep ops. [F344]

## Synchronization

- `tegra210_i2s_sw_reset`, `tegra210_i2s_init`, `tegra210_mixer_write_ram`, `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain`, `tegra210_mvc_volume_switch_timeout`, and `tegra210_sfc_soft_reset` sleep via `regmap_read_poll_timeout` and must only be called from process-context callbacks (such as `.startup`, `.hw_params`, DAPM widget events, or kcontrol handlers), never from atomic PCM `.trigger` callbacks. [F364]
- In `tegra30_ahub_probe`, all global `ahub` singleton fields (`soc_data`, `dev`, `clocks`, `resets`, `apbif_addr`, `regmap_apbif`, `regmap_ahub`) must be initialized and `pm_runtime_enable` called before `of_platform_populate`, and `ahub` must be reset to `NULL` on probe failure or in `tegra30_ahub_remove`. [F277]
- In `tegra210_ope_probe`, `dev_set_drvdata(dev, ope)` must precede `tegra210_peq_regmap_init` and `tegra210_mbdrc_regmap_init`, and all three regmaps (`ope->regmap`, `ope->peq_regmap`, `ope->mbdrc_regmap`) must be initialized before `devm_snd_soc_register_component` and `pm_runtime_enable`. [F279]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `tegra30_i2s_platform_probe` and `tegra30_i2s_platform_remove`, resources must be torn down in strict reverse order of acquisition: `tegra_pcm_platform_unregister`, `snd_soc_unregister_component`, unrouting and freeing `capture_fifo_cif`, unrouting `playback_i2s_cif` and freeing `playback_fifo_cif`, and `pm_runtime_disable`. [F271]
- Device-tree node references acquired via `of_get_child_by_name` or `of_graph_get_endpoint_by_regs` in `tegra210_parse_client_convert`, `tegra210_peq_regmap_init`, and `tegra210_mbdrc_regmap_init` must be released with `of_node_put` before returning on both success and error paths. [F275]
- `tegra30_ahub_runtime_resume()` must assert `ahub->resets`, enable `ahub->clocks`, wait `usleep_range(10, 100)`, and then deassert `ahub->resets` before exiting cache-only mode and syncing `regmap_apbif` and `regmap_ahub`, calling `clk_bulk_disable_unprepare(ahub->nclocks, ahub->clocks)` if deassert or sync fails. [F352]

## API Contracts

- Callers of `tegra_set_cif()` and `tegra264_set_cif()` must zero-initialize `struct tegra_cif_conf` before populating it and pass 1-based channel counts (`>= 1`) in `audio_ch` and `client_ch`; similarly, callers of `tegra30_ahub_set_cif` and `tegra124_ahub_set_cif` must pass 1-based channel counts (`>= 1`) in `conf->audio_channels` and `conf->client_channels`, as all four helpers subtract 1 before shifting into bitfields. [F288, F371]
- In `tegra30_ahub.c`: `tegra30_ahub_set_rx_cif_source()` and `tegra30_ahub_unset_rx_cif_source()` take the destination `enum tegra30_ahub_rxcif` first (`i2s->playback_i2s_cif` for playback, `i2s->capture_fifo_cif` for capture); `tegra30_ahub_enable/disable/free_rx_fifo` (and `tx_fifo` counterparts) require an APBIF FIFO enum value in `[0, TEGRA30_AHUB_CHANNEL_CTRL_COUNT)` (`playback_fifo_cif` or `capture_fifo_cif`), never an I2S/DAM/SPDIF CIF; and `tegra30_ahub_allocate_rx_fifo`/`tx_fifo` must compute `*fiforeg` from physical `ahub->apbif_addr` (`res0->start`) rather than virtual `regs_apbif` for DMA slave configuration. [F294, F322, F363]
- In `tegra210_ope_component_probe`, `snd_soc_component_init_regmap` binds the ASoC component to `ope->regmap` for DAPM routing after `tegra210_peq_component_init` and `tegra210_mbdrc_component_init`, while PEQ and MBDRC callbacks must explicitly pass `ope->peq_regmap` and `ope->mbdrc_regmap` rather than using `snd_soc_component_read`/`write` or `ope->regmap`. [F329]
- `TEGRA_SOC_BYTES_EXT` callbacks paired with `tegra210_peq_param_info()` (`SNDRV_CTL_ELEM_TYPE_INTEGER`) must access `ucontrol->value.integer.value[]`, whereas callbacks paired with `tegra210_mbdrc_param_info()` (`SNDRV_CTL_ELEM_TYPE_BYTES`) must access `ucontrol->value.bytes.data`. [F284]
- In CPU DAI `.set_fmt` callbacks `tegra210_i2s_set_fmt` and `tegra30_i2s_set_fmt`, `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_BP_FP` must set the I2S master enable bit (`I2S_CTRL_MASTER_EN` / `TEGRA30_I2S_CTRL_MASTER_ENABLE`) and `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_BC_FC` must clear it because ASoC core flips the clock provider flags for the CPU DAI before calling `.set_fmt`. [F365]

## Error Handling

- In `tegra210_mvc_update_vol`, `tegra210_mvc_update_mute`, and `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain`, every exit path after `pm_runtime_get_sync(cmpnt->dev)`—including timeout errors and the unchanged-volume check `mvc->volume[chan] == old_volume`—must jump to the `pm_runtime_put(cmpnt->dev)` cleanup label (`end` or `rpm_put`). [F276]
- In `coef_addr_table`, off-diagonal unsupported sample-rate conversions must be initialized to `UNSUPP_CONV` (`(void *)(-EOPNOTSUPP)`) rather than `BYPASS_CONV` (`NULL`), because `tegra210_sfc_write_coeff_ram()` checks `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(coeff_ram)` and returns `PTR_ERR_OR_ZERO(coeff_ram)`. [F287]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `tegra210_mvc_platform_probe`, `pm_runtime_enable(dev)` must be called before `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings(mvc, &pdev->dev)` because `mvc->regmap` starts in cache-only mode and `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings` relies on `pm_runtime_get_sync(dev)` to exit cache-only mode before writing volatile `TEGRA210_MVC_SWITCH`. [F282]
- In `tegra210_mbdrc_hw_params`, multi-bit mode fields with a zero-valued variant such as `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_MBDRC_MODE_BYPASS` must be isolated with `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_MBDRC_MODE_MASK` and compared using `==` rather than tested with bitwise `&`. [F325]
- Fixed-point overflow and sign-extension rules: in `tegra210_dmic_hw_params()`, `DEFAULT_GAIN_Q23` must be held in a 64-bit integer (`gain_q23`) before multiplying by `dmic->boost_gain` (up to `MAX_BOOST_GAIN = 25599`) and divided using `div_u64()`; in `tegra210_mvc_conv_vol()` and `tegra210_mvc_get_vol()`, `CURVE_POLY` conversions must clamp `val` to `10000` and shift by 16 after dividing by 100 (on write) or before multiplying by 100 (on read), while `CURVE_LINEAR` must keep `val` signed (`s32`) across `>> 8`; in `tegra210_mbdrc_vol_get()`, the `TEGRA210_MBDRC_MASTER_VOL` value must be stored in a signed `int` before `>> mc->shift` (`23`) and subtracting `TEGRA210_MBDRC_MASTER_VOL_MIN` (`-256`). [F331, F333, F334]
