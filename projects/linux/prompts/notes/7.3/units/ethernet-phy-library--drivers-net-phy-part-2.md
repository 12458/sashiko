# ETHERNET PHY LIBRARY — drivers/net/phy (part 2)

Implements the core MDIO bus and device subsystem (`mdio_bus.c`, `mdio_bus_provider.c`, `mdio_device.c`, `mdio_devres.c`), Ethernet PHY drivers for Marvell (`marvell.c`), Micrel/Microchip (`micrel.c`, `microchip.c`), and Meson GXL (`meson-gxl.c`), and the Microchip RDS PTP timestamping library (`microchip_rds_ptp.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `marvell.c`, functions switching to `MII_MARVELL_FIBER_PAGE` (`m88e1111_config_aneg`, `m88e1510_config_aneg`, `marvell_suspend`, `marvell_resume`) must restore `MII_MARVELL_COPPER_PAGE` via `marvell_set_page()` on all normal and `error:` paths without overwriting `err`; `marvell_read_status()` restores copper on error/down but stays on `MII_MARVELL_FIBER_PAGE` if fiber `phydev->link` is up. When `phydev->interface == PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_SGMII`, never access `MII_MARVELL_FIBER_PAGE`. [F355, F384, F430]
- During `__mdiobus_register()`, `mdiobus_scan_bus_c22()` runs before `mdiobus_scan_bus_c45()`; C45 scanning must be skipped if `mdiobus_prevent_c45_scan()` finds any C22 PHY with `MICREL_OUI`, and must skip any address `i` where `bus->mdio_map[i]` is populated. `mdiobus_find_device()` (and `mdiobus_get_phy()`, `mdiobus_is_registered_device()`) must verify `0 <= addr < ARRAY_SIZE(bus->mdio_map)` before indexing `bus->mdio_map[addr]`. [F413, F425]
- `mdio_bus_match()` must reject `(dev, drv)` pairs where `!(mdiodrv->mdiodrv.flags & MDIO_DEVICE_IS_PHY)` differs from `!(mdio->flags & MDIO_DEVICE_FLAG_PHY)` before calling `of_driver_match_device()` or `mdio->bus_match()`, because `mdio_probe()` downcasts `drv` to `struct mdio_driver` whereas PHY drivers embed `mdio_driver_common` in `struct phy_driver`. [F449]
- `mdiobus_alloc_size()` aligns `sizeof(*bus)` to `NETDEV_ALIGN` when `size > 0` before setting `bus->priv = (void *)bus + aligned_size`, sets `bus->state = MDIOBUS_ALLOCATED`, and initializes `bus->irq[i]` to `PHY_POLL` and `bus->stats[i].syncp` via `u64_stats_init()`. Never allocate `struct mii_bus` with raw `kzalloc()`, and never `kfree(bus->priv)` separately in `mdiobus_release()` or `mdiobus_free()`. [F385, F446]
- `mdio_device_reset()` short-circuits when `mdiodev->reset_state == value` and updates `mdiodev->reset_state = value` after toggling `reset_gpio` and `reset_ctrl`, relying on `mdio_device_create()` initializing `mdiodev->reset_state` to `-1`. [F394]
- In `lan8842_probe()` and `lan8842_handle_interrupt()`, check `priv->rev` (`LAN8842_SKU_REG` on `LAN8814_PAGE_COMMON_REGS`) against `LAN8842_REV_8832`: skip PHY package join, PTP init, and PTP/GPIO IRQ polling on `LAN8842_REV_8832`. Otherwise, `lan8842_probe()` must call `devm_phy_package_join()` with `sizeof(struct lan8814_shared_priv)` and `lan8842_ptp_probe_once()` before `lan8814_ptp_init()`. [F406, F467]
- In `mchp_rds_ptp_txtstamp()` and `lan8814_txtstamp()`, `case HWTSTAMP_TX_ONESTEP_SYNC:` must call `kfree_skb(skb)` and return when `ptp_msg_is_sync(skb, type)` is true, and otherwise `fallthrough` to `case HWTSTAMP_TX_ON:` to set `SKBTX_IN_PROGRESS` and enqueue non-SYNC packets onto `tx_queue`. `clock->mchp_rds_ptp_event` is initialized to `-1` in `mchp_rds_ptp_probe()`, set to `pin` by `mchp_get_event()`, and reset to `-1` by `mchp_rds_ptp_perout_off()`. [F411, F434]
- On LAN8841, `lan8841_ptp_perout()` and `lan8841_ptp_extts()` validate `0 <= pin < LAN8841_PTP_GPIO_NUM` (10); `lan8841_ptp_enable_event()`/`remove_event()` route pins `0..4` to `LAN8841_GPIO_DATA_SEL1` (`3 * pin`) and `5..9` to `LAN8841_GPIO_DATA_SEL2` (`3 * (pin - 5)`). On LAN8814/LAN8842, `lan8814_ptpci_verify()`, `lan8814_ptp_perout()`, and `lan8814_ptp_extts()` restrict `PTP_PF_PEROUT` to pins `0..1` (`pin == chan`) and `PTP_PF_EXTTS` to pin `3` (`LAN8814_PTP_EXTTS_NUM`). [F421, F422]
- In `micrel.c`, every `struct kszphy_type` on a `phy_driver` using `ksz886x_cable_test_get_status()` must populate `.cable_diag_reg` and `.pair_mask`. `ksz9x31_cable_test_start()` caches `MII_CTRL1000` master/slave bits in `priv->vct_ctrl1000` and `ksz9x31_cable_test_get_status()` restores them when `*finished = true`. In both `*_cable_test_get_status()` functions, `-EAGAIN` on a pair must `continue` without clearing `pair` in `pair_mask`. [F401, F402, F435]
- In `ksz9131_mdix_update()` and `ksz9477_mdix_update()`, never overwrite `phydev->mdix_ctrl`; when `phydev->mdix_ctrl != ETH_TP_MDI_AUTO`, copy `phydev->mdix_ctrl` to `phydev->mdix`. Exiting `BMCR_PDOWN` resets KSZ PHYs, so `ksz8061_config_init()`, `ksz8061_resume()`, `ksz9477_resume()`, and `kszphy_resume()` must wait `usleep_range(1000, 2000)` after clearing `BMCR_PDOWN` before writing registers and calling `phydev->drv->config_intr()`. [F403, F475]
- `ksz8051_match_phy_device()` and `ksz8795_match_phy_device()` test `BMSR_ERCAP` in `MII_BMSR` to distinguish KSZ8051 PHY from KSZ87xx switches. `kszphy_get_sqi()` maps `avg_raw_sqi >= ARRAY_SIZE(ksz_sqi_mapping)` to `0` before indexing. `lan8842_config_init()` LED blinking requires configuring `LAN8814_LED_CTRL_1`/`2` on `LAN8814_PAGE_PORT_REGS` alongside `LAN8814_GPIO_EN2` on `LAN8814_PAGE_COMMON_REGS`. `lan88xx_link_change_notify()` gates hardware reinit and 100Mbps speed-toggling on `phydev->state == PHY_NOLINK`. [F419, F423, F468, F473]
- In `marvell.c`, when `m88e1111_config_init()` sets SGMII and resets, it must call `genphy_read_abilities(phydev)` and OR `phydev->supported` into `phydev->advertising`. In `marvell_config_aneg()` and `m88e1121_config_aneg()`, `changed` accumulates positive returns from polarity/RGMII helpers, and `genphy_soft_reset()` runs after `genphy_config_aneg()` if `phydev->autoneg != AUTONEG_ENABLE || changed`. `marvell_of_reg_init()` checks `"marvell,reg-init"` byte `len` against `4 * sizeof(*paddr)` and divides by `sizeof(*paddr)` before `i < len - 3; i += 4`. In `marvell_hw_stats`, fiber stats are last (`NB_FIBER_STATS`), and stat arrays must not exceed `NB_STAT_MAX`. [F398, F429, F450, F451]

## Synchronization

- Unlocked MDIO accessors (`__mdiobus_*`) require holding `bus->mdio_lock`. Locked MDIO accessors (`mdiobus_*`) acquire sleeping mutex `bus->mdio_lock`; neither set may run in atomic/IRQ context. In `mdiobus_stats_acct()`, 64-bit stats updates must disable preemption and use `u64_stats_update_begin`/`end(&stats->syncp)` under `bus->mdio_lock`, paired with `u64_stats_fetch_begin`/`retry` in `mdio_bus_get_stat()`. [F363, F481, F482]
- In `micrel.c`, `lanphy_*_page_reg()` must hold `phy_lock_mdio_bus()` across the multi-transfer sequence using unlocked `__phy_*` helpers. Shared PTP/GPIO operations on `LAN8814_PAGE_COMMON_REGS` (`lan8814_ptpci_*`, `lan8814_ptp_perout`, `lan8814_ptp_extts`, `lan8814_handle_gpio_interrupt`) must hold `shared->shared_lock` outside `mdio_lock`. [F367, F486]
- In `mchp_rds_ptp_ltc_adjtime()` and `lan8841_ptp_adjtime()`, unlock `ptp_lock` before calling `gettime64()`/`settime64()` and re-acquire it before updating registers. On LAN8841, all accesses to `ptp_priv->seconds` require `spin_lock_irqsave(&ptp_priv->seconds_lock, flags)`, and `ptp_priv->ptp_lock` must be released before acquiring `seconds_lock` or calling `ptp_cancel_worker_sync(ptp_priv->ptp_clock)` (`lan8841_ptp_enable_processing(false)`, `lan8841_suspend()`). [F368, F370, F492]
- In `microchip_rds_ptp.c` and `micrel.c`, `txtstamp`/`rxtstamp` hooks run in atomic network context without MDIO I/O and synchronize with threaded IRQ handlers via `spin_lock_irqsave` on `tx_queue.lock`, `rx_queue.lock`, and `rx_ts_lock`. In `lan8814_match_tx_skb()`, `lan8814_match_skb()`, `mchp_rds_ptp_match_tx_skb()`, and `mchp_rds_ptp_match_skb()`, hold the queue spinlock while walking and calling `__skb_unlink()`, and release it before `skb_complete_tx_timestamp()` or `netif_rx()`. [F369, F490]

## Object Lifetimes

- `__mdiobus_register()` calls `fwnode_handle_get()` on `dev_fwnode(&bus->dev)` only when `bus->state == MDIOBUS_ALLOCATED`, and `mdiobus_release()` calls `fwnode_handle_put()` only when `bus->state == MDIOBUS_RELEASED` (set by `mdiobus_free()`). [F348]
- In `__mdiobus_register()`, `mdio_lock`/`shared_lock` init and `bus->reset_gpiod` deassertion (`reset_delay_us` + `reset_post_delay_us`) must complete before `bus->reset()` and bus scanning. Both `__mdiobus_register()` rollback and `mdiobus_unregister()` must call `mdiodev->device_remove(mdiodev)` then `mdiodev->device_free(mdiodev)` on all `bus->mdio_map[]` entries, re-assert `bus->reset_gpiod` to 1, and call `device_del(&bus->dev)`. [F349, F365, F459]
- `mdio_device_remove()` must call `device_del(&mdiodev->dev)` before `mdiobus_unregister_device(mdiodev)` so `mdio_remove()` asserts reset before handles are released; callers must then call `mdio_device_free(mdiodev)` (never `kfree(mdiodev)`). In `of_mdiobus_find_phy()`, when `child` matches `mdiodev->addr`, `device_set_node(dev, of_fwnode_handle(child))` takes ownership of `child` (dropped in `mdio_device_release()`), so do not call `of_node_put(child)` on that path. [F350, F351, F364]
- `mchp_rds_ptp_txtstamp()` and `lan8814_txtstamp()` take ownership of `skb` and must either `kfree_skb(skb)` or enqueue it on `tx_queue`. Allocated RX timestamp nodes (`mchp_rds_ptp_rx_ts`, `lan8814_ptp_rx_ts`) must be freed via `kfree(rx_ts)` if matched immediately, or added to `rx_ts_list` under `rx_ts_lock` to be unlinked and freed on match or purged in `.hwtstamp`. `kszphy_probe()` must call `clk_disable_unprepare()` on `devm_clk_get_optional_enabled()` so `priv->clk` starts disabled (`priv->clk_enable == false`), and `kszphy_generic_suspend()`/`resume()` must balance `kszphy_disable_clk()`/`enable_clk()` only when `genphy_suspend()` succeeds. [F357, F358, F474]

## API Contracts

- `__mdiobus_register()` requires `bus` and `bus->name`, matching C22/C45 read/write pairs, and at least one read callback. `__devm_mdiobus_register()` and `__devm_of_mdiobus_register()` require `bus` allocated for the same `dev` via `devm_mdiobus_alloc_size()`. Unlocked `__mdiobus_*read/write` helpers must reject `addr >= PHY_MAX_ADDR` with `-ENXIO` before transfer callbacks or `bus->stats[addr]`. [F353, F379, F412]
- `__mdiobus_modify_changed()`, `__mdiobus_c45_modify_changed()`, and `lanphy_modify_page_reg()` return `< 0` on error, `0` if unchanged, and `1` if modified; callers returning `0` on success must check `< 0`, and callers shifting variables into `set` (e.g., `m88e1510_set_temp_critical()`) must clamp/mask to field width before shifting. [F374, F445]
- `mdio_bus.c` and `mdio_device.c` (`CONFIG_MDIO_BUS`) must not call `libphy.o` (`CONFIG_PHYLIB`) functions (`get_phy_device()`, `phy_device_register()`, `phy_device_free()`) without `CONFIG_PHYLIB` guards. `mdio_device_reset()` and `mchp_rds_ptp_handle_interrupt()` / PHY `handle_interrupt` callbacks sleep and must only run in process or threaded IRQ context. [F484, F491, F495]
- `mchp_rds_ptp_probe()` returns `ERR_PTR` on failure, `NULL` when PHC/`CONFIG_MICROCHIP_PHY_RDS_PTP` is disabled, or a valid pointer; callers must check both `IS_ERR(clock)` and `!clock`. `lanphy_*_page_reg()` must use `LAN8814_PAGE_PORT_REGS` (page 5) for per-port registers and `LAN8814_PAGE_COMMON_REGS` (page 4) for common registers; `mchp_rds_phy_*_mmd()` must pass the matching `MCHP_RDS_PTP_PORT` vs `MCHP_RDS_PTP_CLOCK` base selector. [F376, F388, F390]
- `lan8841_ptp_remove_event(ptp_priv, pin, event)` and `lan8841_ptp_enable_event(ptp_priv, pin, event, pulse_width)` take `pin` (`0..9`) second and `event` (`LAN8841_EVENT_A`/`B`) third. `lan8814_get_sig_rx()` must balance `skb_push(skb, ETH_HLEN)` before `ptp_classify_raw()` with `skb_pull_inline(skb, ETH_HLEN)` before queuing or `netif_rx()`. `phy_driver` entries relying on `phy_init_hw()` for reset (`lan88xx_link_change_notify()`, `ksz9031_read_status()`, `ksz8061_config_init()`) must populate `.soft_reset`. [F391, F497, F499]
- In `marvell.c`, modifying `MII_88E1510_GEN_CTRL_REG_1_MODE_MASK` (page 18) in `m88e1510_config_init()`/`sfp_insert()`/`sfp_remove()` requires setting `MII_88E1510_GEN_CTRL_REG_1_RESET` immediately after. `m88e1111_set_downshift()` and `m88e1011_set_downshift()` take 1-based `cnt` (`1..8` encoded as `cnt - 1`, `-E2BIG` if `> 8`), while `*_get_downshift()` adds `1`. For `marvell_drivers` using `marvell_probe()`, `driver_data` must be `const struct marvell_hwmon_ops *` (millidegrees Celsius) or `NULL`. [F392, F397, F452, F502]
- Cable test helpers in `marvell.c` and `micrel.c` pass distances/steps in cm and amplitudes in mV, reporting fault length only on faulted pairs; `marvell_vct5_cable_test_tdr_start()` converts cm via `marvell_vct5_cm2distance()` and rejects `> MII_VCT5_SAMPLE_POINT_DISTANCE_MAX` (511) with `-EINVAL`. `meson_gxl_read_reg()` and `meson_gxl_write_reg()` must bracket bank access with `meson_gxl_open_banks()` and `meson_gxl_close_banks()` on all exits. [F418, F469, F503]

## Error Handling

- `mdiobus_scan*()` returns `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)` when no PHY is at `addr`; `mdiobus_scan_bus_c22()` and `mdiobus_scan_bus_c45()` must ignore `-ENODEV` and only fail on other `IS_ERR(phydev)`. `mdiobus_register_reset()` and `__mdiobus_register()` must check `IS_ERR()` on local variables before assigning `mdiodev->reset_ctrl` or `bus->reset_gpiod`. [F375, F382]
- In `micrel.c`, check `ptp_priv->ptp_clock != NULL` before calling `ptp_cancel_worker_sync()` or `ptp_clock_index()`. In `__lan8814_ptp_probe_once()` and `mchp_rds_ptp_probe()`, `memset` each `struct ptp_pin_desc` from `devm_kmalloc_array()` to 0. `m88e1540_set_fld()` must reject enabling FLD with `-EBUSY` (`phydev_warn()`) if `eee_enabled` is set; `lan8814_ptp_perout()` and `lan8841_ptp_perout()` must use `pr_warn_ratelimited()` for invalid user timings. [F380, F387, F509, F510]

## External Contracts

- Every PHY ID in a module's `struct phy_driver` array (`marvell_drivers`, `ksphy_driver`, `microchip_phy_driver`, `meson_gxl_phy`) must also appear in its `MODULE_DEVICE_TABLE(mdio, ...)` array. `lan8841_rxtstamp()` must decode `ptp_header->reserved2` with `__be32_to_cpu()`, extract 2-bit seconds (`[31:30]`) and ns (`[29:0]`), and zero `header->reserved2` before `netif_rx()`. [F420, F464]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `mchp_rds_ptp_match_skb()`, `mchp_rds_ptp_match_tx_skb()`, `lan8814_match_skb()`, and `lan8814_match_tx_skb()`, only dereference `skb` after `skb_queue_walk_safe` if the boolean match flag (`rc`/`ret`) is `true`; in `mchp_rds_ptp_match_rx_skb()`, save the match into `rx_ts_var` rather than testing the `list_for_each_entry_safe` cursor `rx_ts`. [F432, F433]
- For 48-bit values across three 16-bit registers (`lan8814_ptp_clock_get`, `lan8841_ptp_gettime64`, `mchp_rds_ptp_ltc_gettime64`, `lan8842_get_stat`), accumulate into a 64-bit variable with `<<= 16` steps; when writing 48-bit `time64_t` (`lan8814_ptp_clock_set`, `lan8841_ptp_settime64`, `mchp_rds_ptp_ltc_settime64`), pass `upper_16_bits(sec)` to `*_SEC_MID` and `upper_32_bits(sec)` to `*_SEC_HI`; in `*_adjfine()`, split `scaled_ppm` into `upper_16_bits` and `lower_16_bits` to avoid 32-bit overflow. [F437, F438, F439]
- Fault-distance helpers (`m88e3082_vct_distrfln_2_cm`, `m88e1111_vct_distrfln_2_cm`, `ksz9x31_cable_test_fault_length`) must clamp/threshold raw values before subtracting offsets to prevent `u32` underflow. `marvell_vct7_cable_test_start()` and `marvell_vct5_cable_test_tdr_start()` must update `priv->cable_test_tdr` and reset the opposite VCT engine's control registers. `m88e1510_resume()` must toggle downshift off and back to `cnt` after `marvell_resume()`. `marvell_hwmon_name()` allocates `strlen(devname)` without `+ 1`, relying on `isalnum()` stripping at least one char. [F399, F400, F443, F453]
- `ksz8081_config_aneg()` and `ksz886x_config_aneg()` must configure MDI-X after `genphy_config_aneg()`. Never reassert the shared `coma-mode` GPIO in per-port suspend (`lan8814_release_coma_mode()`). `ksz8041_suspend()`/`resume()` must only toggle `priv->clk` without `genphy_suspend()`/`resume()` (errata DS80000700A). `"Microchip LAN88xx"` (`0x0007c132`) must omit `.config_intr`/`.handle_interrupt`, and `lan88xx_suspend()` must only call `genphy_suspend()` when `!priv->wolopts`. In `meson_gxl_read_status()`, return `genphy_restart_aneg()` immediately when corrupted `MII_LPA` is detected. [F404, F405, F428, F466, F477, F478]
