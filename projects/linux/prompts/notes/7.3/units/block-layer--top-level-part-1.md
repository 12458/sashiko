# BLOCK LAYER — (top level) (part 1)

This unit comprises TCG Opal self-encrypting drive management (`block/sed-opal.c`), T10 Protection Information (`block/t10-pi.c`), the loopback driver (`drivers/block/loop.c`), the Micron P320/P420 SSD driver (`drivers/block/mtip32xx/`), and m68k Amiga/Atari floppy drivers (`drivers/block/amiflop.c`, `drivers/block/ataflop.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `cmd_finalize()` must append `CMD_FINALIZE_BYTES_NEEDED` (7) token bytes before 4-byte padding (matching `generic_table_write_data()`'s reservation), setting `hdr->subpkt.length = cmd->pos - sizeof(*hdr)` before padding and `hdr->pkt.length` / `hdr->cp.length` after padding. [F502, F503]
- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `response_parse()` tests cumulative `<=` bounds on `pos[0]` and must check `TINY_ATOM_BYTE`, `SHORT_ATOM_BYTE`, `MEDIUM_ATOM_BYTE`, and `LONG_ATOM_BYTE` in ascending order; `locking_range_status()` must test `if (rlocked && wlocked)` before `wlocked` or `rlocked` alone; `set_lr_boolean_ace()` must call `add_boolean_object_ref(..., OPAL_BOOLEAN_OR)` after `add_authority_object_ref()` only when `u > 0`; `opal_discovery0_end()` must pass `body` (not `body->features`) to `check_geometry()`. [F505, F525, F526, F527]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `loop_init()` sets `part_shift = fls(max_part)` and `max_part = (1UL << part_shift) - 1` when `max_part > 0`, and must reject `(1UL << part_shift) > DISK_MAX_PARTS` or `max_loop > 1UL << (MINORBITS - part_shift)` with `-EINVAL`. [F535]
- In `mtip32xx`, `struct mtip_cmd` shares `sg` and `icmd` in a union discriminated by `blk_rq_is_passthrough(rq)` in `mtip_queue_rq()`: passthrough requests (`MTIP_TAG_INTERNAL`, tag 0) use `cmd->icmd` in `mtip_issue_reserved_cmd()`, whereas NCQ I/O uses `cmd->sg` in `mtip_hw_submit_io()`. NCQ completion, active-check, and reissue paths (`mtip_commands_active()`, `mtip_workq_sdbfx()`, `mtip_handle_tfe()`, `mtip_service_thread()`) must exclude tag 0 and start/wrap scans at slot 1. [F484, F513]
- In `mtip32xx`, an 8-bit tag splits into `MTIP_TAG_INDEX(tag)` (`tag >> 5`) and `MTIP_TAG_BIT(tag)` (`tag & 0x1F`), packed into `fis->sect_count` by `mtip_hw_submit_io()` as `((rq->tag << 3) | (rq->tag >> 5))`. On 64-bit platforms, per-group 32-bit reads of `port->cmds_to_issue` in `mtip_hw_read_registers()` must index `cmds_to_issue[n / 2]` shifted by `32 * (n & 1)`. [F514, F517]
- In `mtip32xx`, DMA buffers and `struct mtip_cmd_sg` entries require 8-byte-aligned addresses and lengths (`command_sg->info = (len - 1) & 0x3FFFFF`). Upper 32-bit DMA registers (`PORT_LST_ADDR_HI`, `PORT_FIS_ADDR_HI`, `hdr->ctbau`) may be written only when `HOST_CAP_64` (`MTIP_PF_HOST_CAP_64`) is set. `struct work_struct work` must stay at offset 0 of `struct mtip_work` for `DEFINE_HANDLER()` casts. [F515, F552, F568]
- In `mtip_detect_product()`, `dd->slot_groups` is `(HOST_HSORG & 7) + 1` only when `HSORG_STYLE` (`0x8`) is set, clamped to `MTIP_MAX_SLOT_GROUPS` (8). `hba_setup(dd)` must set `HSORG_DISABLE_SLOTGRP_INTR | HSORG_DISABLE_SLOTGRP_PXIS` in `HOST_HSORG` on init and resume. `mtip_init_port()` must call `mtip_deinit_port(port)`, program DMA addresses, and clear `PORT_SCR_ERR`, `port->completed[i]`, `PORT_IRQ_STAT`, and `HOST_IRQ_STAT` before writing `DEF_PORT_IRQ` to `PORT_IRQ_MASK`. [F563, F567, F569]
- In `mtip_hw_suspend()`, `mtip_standby_drive(dd)` must precede clearing `HOST_IRQ_EN` and `mtip_deinit_port(dd->port)`. On surprise removal (`dd->sr = true` on vendor ID `0xFFFF`), `mtip_pci_remove()`, `mtip_hw_exit()`, and `mtip_standby_drive()` must skip `mtip_quiesce_io()`, `mtip_standby_immediate()`, `mtip_deinit_port()`, and `HOST_CTL` MMIO. [F566, F573]
- In `ataflop.c`, non-zero format types (`MINOR(dev) >> 2`) must be decremented before checking `type < NUM_DISK_MINORS` and indexing `minor2disktype[type]`; invalidating `TrackBuffer` requires `BufferDrive = -1`, and `floppy_revalidate()` must restore `UDT = &default_params[drive]` when `blocks != 0`. `fd_select_drive()` must load `UD.track` into `FDCREG_TRACK` and `UDT->fdc_speed` into `dma_wd.fdc_speed`, `check_change()` must restore `sound_ym.wd_data = old_porta`, and `fd_motor_off_timer()` must wait for `FDCSTAT_MOTORON` (`0x80`) to clear while `!stdma_islocked()` before `fd_deselect()`. [F499, F500, F518, F576]
- In `amiflop.c`, CIA-A status (`DSKRDY`, `DSKTRACK0`, `DSKPROT`, `DSKCHANGE`) and CIA-B control (`DSKMOTOR`, `DSKSEL0`..`DSKSEL3`, `DSKSTEP`) signals are active-low. Before seeking in `get_track()`, closing in `floppy_release()`, or `FDFLUSH`, if `unit[drive].dirty == 1` (or unconditionally for `FDFLUSH`), `flush_track_timer + drive` must be deleted and `non_int_flush_track(drive)` called before replacing `track` or `trackbuf`. [F501, F574]

## Synchronization

- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `loop_global_lock_killable(lo, global)` locks `loop_validate_mutex` (if `global`) before `lo->lo_mutex` and requires the same `global` boolean in `loop_global_unlock()`. In `loop_change_fd()`, when `!is_loop`, `loop_validate_mutex` must be locked and unlocked after `loop_global_unlock()` and before `fput(old_file)`. [F430, F431]
- In `loop_configure()`, `loop_assign_backing_file()` must be ordered by `wmb()` before `lo->lo_state = Lo_bound`, pairing with `rmb()` in `loop_validate_file()` after checking `l->lo_state == Lo_bound`; each hop in `loop_validate_file()`'s `while (is_loop_device(f))` loop must check `f->f_mapping->host->i_rdev == bdev->bd_dev` and `l->lo_state != Lo_bound` on cursor `f` before advancing `f = l->lo_backing_file`. [F448, F520]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `__loop_clr_fd()` must clear `lo->lo_backing_file = NULL` under `spin_lock_irq(&lo->lo_lock)` before `fput(filp)`. Calling `fput()` on a backing file, `loop_reread_partitions()`, or `vfs_getattr()` in `loop_get_status()` (after pinning `f_path` with `path_get()` under `lo->lo_mutex` while `Lo_bound`) must happen outside `lo->lo_mutex`. [F452, F454, F603]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, when `Lo_bound`, `loop_update_dio()` requires `lo->lo_mutex` and `blk_mq_freeze_queue()`, and enabling `LO_FLAGS_DIRECT_IO` requires `vfs_fsync()` first. Without `BLK_OPEN_EXCL`, `loop_configure()` and `loop_set_block_size()` must wrap `lo->lo_mutex` with `bd_prepare_to_claim()` / `bd_abort_claiming()`. In `loop_add()`, `lo->idr_visible` becomes `true` only after `add_disk()`, and `loop_control_remove()` clears `idr_visible` under `loop_ctl_mutex` and drops it before locking `lo->lo_mutex`. [F458, F602, F604]
- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `dev->dev_lock` must be held across all command/session steps (`setup_opal_dev()`, `execute_step()`, `execute_steps()`) and `dev->unlk_lst` accesses (`add_suspend_info()`, `opal_lock_check_for_saved_key()`, `opal_unlock_from_suspend()`), whereas callers of `clean_opal_dev()` and `check_opal_support()` must not hold `dev->dev_lock`. [F460, F588]
- In `mtip_handle_irq()`, when `PORT_IRQ_SDB_FIS` queues `workers > 0`, only the last worker decrementing `dd->irq_workers_active` to zero in `mtip_workq_sdbfx()` writes `0xffffffff` to `HOST_IRQ_STAT` (`do_irq_enable = 0` in ISR). Because `mtip_issue_ncq_command()` uses `spin_lock(&port->cmd_issue_lock[group])`, that lock must never be taken in hardirq or timer context. [F572, F589]
- In `ataflop.c`, `ataflop_queue_rq()` takes `stdma_try_lock()` and sets `fd_request = bd->rq`; early error exits before `do_fd_action()` must call `fd_end_request_cur(BLK_STS_IOERR)` and `stdma_release()`. `do_format()` must freeze/quiesce the queue and call `stdma_lock()` before touching `BufferDrive`/`TrackBuffer`, and call `finish_fdc()` before unquiescing/unfreezing. Accesses to `sound_ym` require `local_irq_save()`, and timers in `ataflop.c` / `amiflop.c` must check `!stdma_islocked()` / `try_fdc()` before FDC access. [F446, F465, F466, F597]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `loop_assign_backing_file()` saves `f_mapping`'s GFP mask in `lo->old_gfp_mask` and clears `__GFP_IO | __GFP_FS`, which `loop_change_fd()` and `__loop_clr_fd()` must restore before replacing or clearing `lo->lo_backing_file`. Under `lo->lo_work_lock`, `loop_queue_work()` must call `list_del_init(&worker->idle_list)`, and `loop_process_work()` may add `worker` to `idle_worker_list` only when `!work_pending(&worker->work)`. In `lo_free_disk()`, `destroy_workqueue(lo->workqueue)` must precede `loop_free_idle_workers(lo, true)` and `timer_shutdown_sync(&lo->timer)`. [F432, F435, F459]
- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `gen_key()` and `start_SIDASP_opal_session()` must `kfree()` and null `dev->prev_data` allocated by `get_active_key_cont()` or `get_msid_cpin_pin()`, whereas `read_table_data()` nulls the borrowed `dev->resp` pointer from `read_table_data_cont()` without `kfree()`. `add_suspend_info()` must unlink and `kfree()` any existing `dev->unlk_lst` entry with `iter->lr == sus->lr` before appending `sus`, `clean_opal_dev()` must free all `unlk_lst` entries on `free_opal_dev()` or `opal_reverttper()`, and `opal_discovery0_end()` must mask `dev->flags &= OPAL_FL_SUPPORTED` before parsing. [F439, F440, F495]
- In `mtip32xx`, `mtip_exec_internal_command()` must call `blk_mq_free_request(rq)` and `clear_bit(MTIP_PF_IC_ACTIVE_BIT, &port->flags)` on both `mtip_quiesce_io()` failure and `exec_ic_exit`. Because `mtip_block_free_disk()` frees `dd->index` and `kfree(dd)`, `mtip_pci_remove()` and `mtip_block_shutdown()` must finish all `dd` accesses (`blk_mq_free_tag_set()`, `mtip_hw_exit()`, `destroy_workqueue(dd->isr_workq)`) before `put_disk(dd->disk)`. [F443, F444]

## API Contracts

- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `response_get_token()` returns `ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)` on error (use `IS_ERR()`), and `response_get_string()` sets `*store = NULL` on failure (check `!*store`). `opal_unlock_from_suspend()` returns `true` on failure and `false` on success, and for each entry must reset `dev->tsn = 0` and `dev->hsn = 0`, run `__opal_lock_unlock()`, and if `OPAL_FL_MBR_ENABLED`, run `__opal_set_mbr_done()`. [F468, F469, F470, F562]
- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `build_locking_user()` writes `buffer[7] = lr + 1`, so 0-based SUM ranges pass `lr` while 1-based `enum opal_user` callers (`who != OPAL_ADMIN1`) pass `who - 1`. `read_table_data()` callers (`opal_read_table()`) must reject `size == 0`, and table read/write helpers must check `(size > table_len || offset > table_len - size)`. In `sed_ioctl()`, `p` is `memdup_user()`ed only for `cmd & IOC_IN` and never auto-copied back; `_IOR` handlers use `arg` directly and `_IOW` handlers returning data (`opal_locking_range_status()`) must `copy_to_user()` into `arg`. [F504, F506, F546]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `lo_offset` and `lo_sizelimit` are bytes (`loff_t`), whereas `lo_calculate_size()` returns 512-byte sectors (`loopsize >> 9`) and must use `i_size_read(file->f_mapping->host)` when `S_ISBLK(file_inode(file)->i_mode)`. For block-backed loops, `loop_get_discard_config()` must use `bdev_write_zeroes_sectors(bdev)`. `loop_change_fd()` and `loop_configure()` must call `dev_set_uevent_suppress(..., 0)` before `loop_reread_partitions(lo)` and then emit `kobject_uevent(..., KOBJ_CHANGE)`. [F480, F557, F558, F598, F607]
- In `lo_rw_aio()`, `atomic_set(&cmd->ref, 2)` must precede direct `read_iter()`/`write_iter()` calls (`cmd->iocb.ki_flags = cmd->use_aio ? IOCB_DIRECT : 0`, paired with `kiocb_start_write()` before `write_iter()`); `loop_handle_cmd()` must save `cmd->blkcg_css` and `cmd->memcg_css` before `do_req_filebacked()` and not touch `cmd` on `-EIOCBQUEUED`; only when `atomic_dec_and_test(&cmd->ref)` succeeds in `lo_rw_aio_do_completion()` may `cmd->bvec` be freed/nulled, `kiocb_end_write()` called on writes, and `rq` completed. [F436, F457, F599, F600]
- In `mtip32xx`, `mtip_hw_get_identify()` and `mtip_block_initialize()` return positive `MTIP_FTL_REBUILD_MAGIC` (`0xED51`) on FTL rebuild, negative errno on error, and `0` when ready; `mtip_pci_probe()` must check `rv < 0` for errors and set `MTIP_DDF_INIT_DONE_BIT` only when `rv != MTIP_FTL_REBUILD_MAGIC`. Callers must check `mtip_hw_get_capacity()`'s boolean return (`port->identify_valid != 0`). `mtip_exec_internal_command()` requires an 8-byte-aligned `buffer`, `fis_len` in 32-bit DWords, `buf_len` in bytes, and `timeout` in ms. [F482, F483, F486]
- In `amiflop.c` and `ataflop.c`, `floppy_open()` must return `-EBUSY` if the drive is already open (`fd_ref[drive]` or `p->ref`) under a different format minor (`fd_device[drive]` or `p->type`). `amiflop.c` must allocate `raw_buf` with `amiga_chip_alloc()`; `ataflop.c` must allocate `DMABuffer`/`TrackBuffer` with `atari_stram_alloc()`, bounce `ReqData` when `!ATARIHW_PRESENT(EXTD_DMA)`, and call `dma_cache_maintenance()` around DMA. [F594, F596, F614]

## Error Handling

- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `execute_steps()` must call `end_opal_session_error(dev)` when a step at `state > 0` fails, never at `state == 0` or `opal_discovery0_step()`. `parse_and_check_status()`, `finalize_and_send()`, and `opal_step.fn` return negative errnos or positive `u8` Opal status codes on failure (check `if (err)`). Token builders (`add_token_*()`) require `err` initialized to `0` and checked before sending. [F438, F467, F471]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `loop_configure()` and `loop_change_fd()` must validate files via `loop_check_backing_file()` (requiring `read_iter`, and `write_iter` only if `file->f_mode & FMODE_WRITE`, while missing write support in `loop_configure()` sets `LO_FLAGS_READ_ONLY`) and call `fput(file)` on error. [F477, F519]
- In `mtip_hw_submit_io()`, `blk_rq_map_sg()`'s count must stay in `command->scatter_ents`, while `dma_map_sg()`'s return is checked for `0` in `nents` (`-ENOMEM`). In `exec_drive_taskfile()` and `mtip_block_initialize()`, `ERR_PTR` returns in `inbuf` or `dd->mtip_svc_handler` must be reset to `NULL` before cleanup, and `inbuf_dma`/`outbuf_dma` zeroed after `dma_unmap_single()`. In `mtip_quiesce_io()`, `active` must be initialized to `true` before the loop. In `ataflop.c`, `floppy_open()` error paths and `floppy_release()` must reset `p->ref = 0` when `p->ref < 0` (`BLK_OPEN_EXCL`) rather than decrementing it. [F481, F485, F487, F531]

## External Contracts

- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `opal_locking_range_status()` must `copy_to_user()` `struct opal_lr_status` starting at `offsetof(struct opal_lr_status, range_start)`, and `opal_get_status()` / `opal_get_geometry()` must zero-initialize their output structs. [F473]
- In `drivers/block/loop.c`, `loop_get_status()`, `loop_info64_to_old()`, and `loop_info64_to_compat()` must `memset()` output structs to zero and return `-EOVERFLOW` on field truncation; `loop_set_status_from_info()` must reject `lo_offset > LLONG_MAX` or `lo_sizelimit > LLONG_MAX` (`-EOVERFLOW`) and set `lo->lo_file_name[LO_NAME_SIZE - 1] = 0`. In `exec_drive_taskfile()`, `outtotal` must match the native/compat taskfile header size, with `out_size` and `in_size` capped at `130560` (`512 * 0xFF`). [F479, F511, F516]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `block/sed-opal.c`, `response_parse_short()` must reject unsigned integer atoms with `tok->len > 9` (`-EINVAL`) and cast `pos[i]` to `u64` before shifting (`(u64)pos[i] << (8 * b)`); `add_token_u64()` must compute non-tiny byte length as `DIV_ROUND_UP(msb, 8)`; `OPAL_HALF_UID_AUTHORITY_OBJ_REF` and `OPAL_HALF_UID_BOOLEAN_ACE` in `opaluid[]` must use length `OPAL_UID_LENGTH / 2`. [F536, F537, F545]
- In `loop_attr_backing_file_show()`, `file_path(lo->lo_backing_file, buf, PAGE_SIZE - 1)` returns a pointer `p` inside `buf` that must be moved with `memmove(buf, p, ret)` before appending `"\n\0"`. [F547]
- In `mtip32xx.c`, `mtip_hw_get_capacity()` must widen `u16` `port->identify[100..103]` to `u64` before shifting by 16/32/48, and `mtip_block_getgeo()` must use `sector_div()`. `port->identify` is a `u16 *` indexed by word offsets (`+ 10`, `+ 23`, `+ 27`), whereas `ata_swap_string()` takes byte lengths (`20`, `8`, `40`) and `strscpy()` into `cbuf` requires `byte_len + 1`. In `amiflop.c`, `encode_block()` and `dos_encode_block()` write `len * 2` bytes and read `raw[-1]` / `dest[-1]`, so callers must write gap words at `raw_buf[0]` first and advance `unsigned long *raw` by `len / 2`. [F540, F555, F556]
