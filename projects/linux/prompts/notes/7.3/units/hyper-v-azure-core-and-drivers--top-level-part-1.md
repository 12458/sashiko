# Hyper-V/Azure CORE AND DRIVERS — (top level) (part 1)

Core Hyper-V guest/root-partition infrastructure (`mshyperv`, `hyperv_timer`, VMBus `drivers/hv/`, MSHV `mshv_*`), integration services (`hv_balloon`, `hv_kvp`, `hv_snapshot`, `hv_util`), and synthetic HID (`hid-hyperv`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Every path in `vmbus_onoffer()` and `vmbus_process_offer()` must decrement `vmbus_connection.offer_in_progress` once (under `channel_mutex` when adding to `chn_list`, `sc_list`, or `channels[]`). On suspend (`vmbus_bus_suspend`), channels are unmapped via `vmbus_channel_unmap_relid()` with `offermsg.child_relid = INVALID_RELID` (`hv_process_channel_removal()` skips unmap and `vmbus_release_relid()` when `INVALID_RELID`); on resume (`oldchannel != NULL`), `vmbus_onoffer()` updates `child_relid`, calls `vmbus_setup_channel_state()` if other `offer` fields changed, and calls `vmbus_channel_map_relid()` under `channel_mutex`. [F8, F67, F68]
- Before calling `hv_clear_allocated_cpu()` or `hv_update_allocated_cpus()` under `channel_mutex`, first unlink the channel from `chn_list`/`sc_list` (`hv_process_channel_removal()`) or update `channel->target_cpu` (`vmbus_channel_set_cpu()`). [F66]
- `vmbus_devs[]` indices must match `enum vmbus_device_type`. When `hv_is_isolation_supported()`, `vmbus_connect()` rejects versions `< VERSION_WIN10_V5_2` and `vmbus_is_valid_offer()` rejects non-hvsock offers without `.allowed_in_isolated = true` in `vmbus_devs[]`. `channel_message_table[CHANNELMSG_COUNT]` indices must match `enum vmbus_channel_message_type`, with `min_payload_len` at least the struct size cast by `message_handler`. [F87, F88, F89, F166]
- `hv_identify_partition_type()` only sets `HV_PARTITION_TYPE_ROOT` or `HV_PARTITION_TYPE_L1VH` when `(ms_hyperv.priv_high & HV_CREATE_PARTITIONS)` and `!(ms_hyperv.priv_high & HV_ISOLATION)`. In `hv_post_message()`, a TDX VM with a paravisor (`hv_isolation_type_tdx() && ms_hyperv.paravisor_present`) stages messages in `this_cpu_ptr(hv_context.cpu_context)->post_msg_page` instead of `*this_cpu_ptr(hyperv_pcpu_input_arg)`. [F81, F111]
- `alloc_balloon_pages()` splits high-order pages, sets `PageOffline`, and updates `num_pages_ballooned`, `NR_BALLOON_PAGES`, and `adjust_managed_page_count()`; `balloon_up()` error paths and `balloon_down()` must free them via `free_balloon_pages()`. `dm_device.ha_lock` (dropped before `hv_mem_hot_add()` in `handle_pg_range()`) protects `ha_region_list`, `num_pages_onlined`, and `hv_hotadd_state`: `hv_mem_hot_add()` advances `ha_end_pfn`/`covered_end_pfn` under `ha_lock` before `add_memory()` and rolls both back on error; `pfn_covered()` records non-contiguous `[covered_end_pfn, start_pfn)` in `has->gap_list`; and `hv_page_online_one()` marks `!has_pfn_is_backed()` PFNs `__SetPageOffline()` without `generic_online_page()`. [F16, F42, F73, F74, F564]
- `hv_util` and `hv_snapshot` callbacks must verify `recvlen >= ICMSG_HDR` and `recvlen >= ICMSG_HDR + sizeof(<payload>)` before reading `recv_buffer`, and `hv_vss_init()` / `hv_vss_onchannelcallback()` must use `VSS_MAX_PKT_SIZE` (`HV_HYP_PAGE_SIZE * 2`) for `FREEZE` LUN data. [F97, F98]
- In `hid-hyperv`, `hid_set_drvdata()` stores `struct hv_device *` while `hv_set_drvdata()` stores `struct mousevsc_dev *`, so HID callbacks (`mousevsc_hid_parse()`) must call `hv_get_drvdata(hid_get_drvdata(hid_dev))`. `mousevsc_connect_to_vsp()` and `mousevsc_on_receive_device_info()` run on both probe and `mousevsc_resume()`, so they must call `reinit_completion(&input_dev->wait_event)` and `kfree()` existing `hid_desc` and `report_desc` before reallocating. [F86, F154]

## Synchronization

- Per-CPU hypercall page users (`hyperv_pcpu_input_arg`, `hyperv_pcpu_output_arg`, `post_msg_page`), such as `hv_post_message()`, `hv_call_deposit_pages()`, and `hv_call_get_vp_registers()`, must use `local_irq_save()` across buffer setup, hypercall, and output reads without sleeping allocations. [F178]
- `vmbus_connection.chn_msg_list` requires `spin_lock_irqsave(&vmbus_connection.channelmsg_lock, ...)`, and `msginfo` must be linked before `vmbus_post_msg()`. In `__vmbus_establish_gpadl()`, `__vmbus_open()`, and `vmbus_teardown_gpadl()`, check `channel->rescind` after linking `msginfo` under `channelmsg_lock` and before `wait_for_completion(&msginfo->waitevent)`. `send_modifychannel_with_ack()` (called under `channel_mutex`) must unlock `channel_mutex` around `wait_for_completion(&info->waitevent)` and re-lock it before returning. [F7, F21, F197]
- In `vmbus_process_offer()` and `hv_synic_cleanup()` / `hv_pick_new_cpu()`, acquire `cpus_read_lock()` outside `channel_mutex`. `vmbus_process_offer()` queues `vmbus_add_channel_work()` onto `handle_primary_chan_wq` or `handle_sub_chan_wq` after decrementing `offer_in_progress`. `vmbus_add_channel_work()` sets `newchannel->probe_done = true` on all paths, and `vmbus_onoffer_rescind()` waits for `READ_ONCE(channel->probe_done)` after `vmbus_rescind_cleanup()` before unregistering/removing. [F28, F184, F185]
- `vmbus_channel_map_relid()` uses `virt_store_mb()` into `vmbus_connection.channels[relid]`, paired with `WRITE_ONCE(..., NULL)` in `vmbus_channel_unmap_relid()` and `READ_ONCE()` in `relid2channel()`. In `vmbus_on_event()`, if `HV_CALL_BATCHED` and `hv_end_read(&channel->inbound)` is non-zero, call `hv_begin_read(&channel->inbound)` and `tasklet_schedule(&channel->callback_event)` instead of looping. `__vmbus_request_addr_match()` requires `lock_requestor(channel, flags)`; unlocked callers use `vmbus_request_addr_match()`. [F30, F113, F187]
- `mshv_update_routing_table()` checks `ue[i].gsi < MSHV_MAX_GUEST_IRQS`, sizes `mshv_girq_info_tbl` to `max(gsi) + 1` (`mshv_ret_girq_entry()` checks `!girq_tbl || irqnum >= num_rt_entries`), swaps `pt_girq_tbl` under `pt_irq_lock`, calls `mshv_irqfd_routing_update()` (under `pt_irqfds_lock` and `irqfd_irqe_sc` write seqcount), and calls `synchronize_srcu_expedited(&partition->pt_irq_srcu)` before freeing the old table. `pt->ioeventfds_list` edits require `pt->pt_mutex`, and removals in `mshv_deassign_ioeventfd()` / `mshv_eventfd_release()` require `synchronize_rcu()` before `ioeventfd_release()`. [F38, F107, F194]
- In `hv_kvp.c` and `hv_snapshot.c`, `kvp_respond_to_host()` / `vss_respond_to_host()` may only run if `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` on `kvp_timeout_work` / `vss_timeout_work` returns true. `hvt_reset()` frees `hvt->outmsg`, clears `outmsg_len`, and calls `hvt->on_reset()` (failing any in-flight host request if `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` succeeds and setting `.state = HVUTIL_DEVICE_INIT`). `hv_kvp_pre_suspend()` and `hv_vss_pre_suspend()` disable `callback_event`, cancel works, and force `.state = HVUTIL_READY` (sending `VSS_OP_THAW` to `hvt` for VSS) before `util_suspend()`. [F41, F76, F77]
- `hv_free_page_report()` increments `hv_hypercall_multi_failure` for `dm_thread_func()` instead of calling `disable_page_reporting()` in callback context. `mousevsc_probe()` sets `input_dev->init_complete = true` only after `hid_add_device()`, and `mousevsc_on_receive()` ignores `SYNTH_HID_INPUT_REPORT` while `!init_complete`. [F158, F562]

## Object Lifetimes

- `vmbus_device_register()` frees `newchannel->device_obj` via `vmbus_device_release()` on failure, so `vmbus_add_channel_work()` must not `kfree(newchannel->device_obj)`. Before `newchannel->kobj` is initialized in `vmbus_add_channel_work()`, early exits (`vmbus_process_offer()`, `vmbus_onoffer()`) must call `kfree(newchannel)`, not `free_channel()`. [F1, F2]
- `vmbus_close_internal()` (and `vmbus_onoffer_rescind()` before setting `rescind`) must first call `vmbus_reset_channel_cb()` (disabling `callback_event` and clearing `onchannel_callback` under `spin_lock_irqsave(&channel->sched_lock, ...)`), and only call `vmbus_free_requestor()` and allow `vmbus_free_ring()` if `CHANNELMSG_CLOSECHANNEL` and `vmbus_teardown_gpadl()` succeed. `vmbus_disconnect_ring()` closes/removes all sub-channels on `channel->sc_list` (waiting on `rescind_event` if `rescind` and calling `hv_process_channel_removal()` under `channel_mutex`) before closing the primary channel; `vmbus_disconnect_ring()` and `vmbus_free_channels()` require `list_for_each_entry_safe()`. In `vmbus_onoffer_rescind()`, evaluate `clean_up_chan_for_suspend = is_hvsock_channel(channel) || is_sub_channel(channel)` before unregistering/removing `channel`. [F5, F6, F116, F119, F186]
- `hyperv_pcpu_input_arg` and `hyperv_pcpu_output_arg` allocated in `hv_common_cpu_init()` are reused on CPU re-online and must not be freed in `hv_common_cpu_die()`. `__vmbus_establish_gpadl()` sets `gpadl->decrypted = true` before `set_memory_decrypted()` and only clears it if `set_memory_encrypted()` succeeds; if `set_memory_decrypted()` or `set_memory_encrypted()` fails in SynIC, VMBus connect/disconnect, or GPADL paths, leak the pages (`vmbus_free_ring()` only frees `ringbuffer_page` when `!ringbuffer_gpadlhandle.decrypted`). [F10, F69, F181]
- `mshv_irqfd_deassign()` and `mshv_irqfd_release()` must use `hlist_for_each_entry_safe()` on `pt_irqfds_list` with `mshv_irqfd_deactivate()` and then `flush_workqueue(irqfd_cleanup_wq)`. `mshv_irqfd_shutdown()` calls `remove_wait_queue()` before resampler teardown, eventfd release, and `kfree(irqfd)`. `mshv_irqfd_resampler_shutdown()` (under `irqfds_resampler_lock`) calls `hlist_del_rcu(&irqfd->irqfd_resampler_hnode)` and `synchronize_srcu(&pt->pt_irq_srcu)` before unregistering/freeing `rp`. [F35, F36, F122]
- `hvutil_transport_destroy()` sets `hvt->mode = HVUTIL_TRANSPORT_DESTROY` and wakes `outmsg_q` under `hvt->lock`, unlinks `hvt`, and if `mode_old == HVUTIL_TRANSPORT_CHARDEV`, waits for `hvt->release` before `hvt_transport_free()`. In `util_probe()`, `vmbus_open()` precedes `srv->util_init_transport()`, calling `vmbus_close()` before `srv->util_deinit()` on failure. In `hid-hyperv`, `mousevsc_init()` registers `mousevsc_hid_driver` before `mousevsc_drv`, and `hid_parse()`/`hid_hw_start()` run in `mousevsc_hid_probe()`. [F40, F156, F157]

## API Contracts

- `hyperv_pcpu_input_arg` and `hyperv_pcpu_output_arg` are `void * __percpu *`: pass `*this_cpu_ptr(...)` directly to `hv_do_hypercall()` (never `&output`), checking `hv_output_page_exists()` before using output. `hv_do_hypercall()` buffers use `virt_to_phys()` and must be 8-byte aligned (`HV_HYPERCALL_PARAM_ALIGN`) in direct-mapped memory (never stack/`vmalloc`); `hv_query_ext_cap()` uses a `static` 8-byte-aligned `hv_extended_cap` after checking `HV_ENABLE_EXTENDED_HYPERCALLS`. [F46, F47, F168]
- `hv_call_deposit_pages()` rejects `num_pages > HV_DEPOSIT_MAX`, calls `split_page(pages[i], order)`, and transfers ownership on `HVCALL_DEPOSIT_MEMORY` success; if allocation fails at `order == 0` (setting `num_allocations = i`) or the hypercall fails, free order-0 pages (`__free_page(pfn_to_page(base_pfn + j))`) for `0 .. num_allocations - 1`. [F18, F105, F123]
- `vmbus_alloc_ring()` / `vmbus_open()` sizes must be multiples of guest `PAGE_SIZE` (`VMBUS_RING_SIZE()`), `__vmbus_open()` rejects `userdatalen > MAX_USER_DEFINED_BYTES`, and `vmbus_connection.int_page` / `monitor_pages` must use `__get_free_page()` / `free_page()` (`PAGE_SIZE`) for UIO mapping. When `vmbus_initiate_unload(crash == true)` runs, pass `can_sleep = false` to `vmbus_post_msg()` and poll via `vmbus_wait_for_unload()`. [F93, F176, F189]

## Error Handling

- Hypercall helpers (`hv_do_hypercall`, `hv_do_rep_hypercall`, `hv_ghcb_hypercall`, `hv_tdx_hypercall`) put status in bits [15:0] (`hv_result`) and completed reps in bits [43:32] (`hv_repcomp`); check `hv_result_success(status)` and convert via `hv_result_to_errno(status)`. `hv_post_message()` returns `-EMSGSIZE` or positive 16-bit `hv_result(status)`; use `vmbus_post_msg()` for negative errnos and retries. [F44, F45]
- Pages decrypted with `set_memory_decrypted()` in `vmbus_connect()`, `hv_common_cpu_init()`, and `hv_synic_alloc()` must be zeroed with `memset()` after decryption. In `hv_common_cpu_init()`, assign `*inputarg` only after `set_memory_decrypted()` and `memset()`, and check `*this_cpu_ptr(hyperv_pcpu_input_arg) != NULL` in early callers (`hv_free_page_report()`). In `hv_call_get_vp_registers()` / `hv_call_set_vp_registers()`, zero `rsvd_z8` and `rsvd_z16` on reused `hyperv_pcpu_input_arg` and advance `registers` by `hv_repcomp(status)`. [F48, F52, F60]
- Guard `this_cpu_ptr(hv_synic_eventring_tail)` in `hv_common_cpu_init()` / `hv_common_cpu_die()` with `if (hv_parent_partition())`. `relid2channel()` checks `vmbus_connection.channels != NULL` and `relid < MAX_CHANNEL_RELIDS`, and callers must check for `NULL`. `cpuhp_setup_state()` returns `0` on success for `CPUHP_AP_HYPERV_ONLINE` / `CPUHP_AP_HYPERV_TIMER_STARTING`; check `ret < 0` and pass the enum constant to `cpuhp_remove_state()`. `hv_timesync_init()` checks `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(hv_ptp_clock)` after `ptp_clock_register()` and sets `hv_ptp_clock = NULL`. [F49, F50, F58, F62]

## External Contracts

- `create_gpadl_header()` splits PFNs across `CHANNELMSG_GPADL_HEADER` and `CHANNELMSG_GPADL_BODY` (`<= MAX_SIZE_CHANNEL_MESSAGE`, 240 bytes); `msgsize` includes `sizeof(struct vmbus_channel_msginfo)` (subtracted before `vmbus_post_msg()`) and `sizeof(struct gpa_range) + pfncount * sizeof(u64)`, while `range_buflen` is `pagecount * sizeof(u64)`, and `msgbody` links via its first field `msglistentry`. For `HV_GPADL_RING`, `hv_gpadl_size()`, `hv_ring_gpadl_send_hvpgoffset()`, and `hv_gpadl_hvpfn()` subtract `PAGE_SIZE - HV_HYP_PAGE_SIZE` per ring header before `>> HV_HYP_PAGE_SHIFT`, while GPADL `set_memory_decrypted()` / `set_memory_encrypted()` page counts use `PFN_UP(size)`. [F95, F131, F132, F142, F143, F144]

## Known Pitfalls

- `read_hv_sched_clock_tsc()` (`noinstr`, so `read_hv_clock_msr()` and `hv_setup_sched_clock()` must be `__always_inline` and use `hv_raw_get_msr()`) scales by `NSEC_PER_SEC / HV_CLOCK_HZ` and relies on unsigned 64-bit wraparound when subtracting `hv_sched_clock_offset`, which `hv_restore_sched_clock_state()` / `restore_hv_clock_tsc_state()` adjusts by `hv_ref_counter_at_suspend - hv_read_reference_counter()` via `hv_adj_sched_clock_offset()`. [F65, F136, F188, F568]
- `vmbus_prep_negotiate_resp()` checks `buflen >= ICMSG_HDR + offsetof(struct icmsg_negotiate, reserved)`, caps version counts to `IC_VERSION_NEGOTIATION_MAX_VER_COUNT`, checks `ICMSG_NEGOTIATE_PKT_SIZE(...) <= buflen`, resets `found_match = false` between framework (`0 .. icframe_vercnt - 1`) and service (`icframe_vercnt .. icframe_vercnt + icmsg_vercnt - 1`) loops, and finishes both loops before overwriting `negop` in place. [F84, F96, F117]
- `kvp_transaction.state` and `vss_transaction.state` must not be reset to `HVUTIL_READY` until after `kvp_respond_to_host()` / `vss_respond_to_host()` finish mutating `recv_buffer`. [F78]
