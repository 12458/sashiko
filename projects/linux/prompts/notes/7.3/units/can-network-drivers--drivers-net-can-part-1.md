# CAN NETWORK DRIVERS — drivers/net/can (part 1)

Core CAN device framework (`drivers/net/can/dev/*`) and memory-mapped controller drivers (`at91_can`, `c_can`/`d_can`, `cc770`, `flexcan`, `grcan`, `ifi_canfd`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `alloc_candev_mqs()` places `can_ml_priv` and `echo_skb` after `sizeof_priv`: drivers must embed `struct can_priv` at offset 0 of their private struct and pass `sizeof(*priv)`. Arbitrary `net_device *` to `can_priv *` conversion must use `safe_candev_priv()` (`dev->type == ARPHRD_CAN` and `dev->rtnl_link_ops == &can_link_ops`). [F71, F109, F112]
- RX paths must clamp 4-bit Classical CAN DLCs to `CAN_MAX_DLEN` via `can_cc_dlc2len()` (and map FD DLCs via `can_fd_dlc2len()`) before setting `cf->len` or copying data. Sub-32-bit hardware RX timestamps must be left-shifted to full `u32` (`<< 16` in `flexcan`/`at91_can`) so `can_rx_offload_compare()` wraps cleanly. [F70, F100]
- In error handlers (`c_can_handle_bus_err`, `ifi_canfd_handle_lec_err`, `at91_irq_err_frame`), `stats.tx_errors` (ACK/bit0/bit1), `stats.rx_errors` (stuff/form/CRC/overload), and `IFI_CANFD_ERROR_CTR` resets must run even if `alloc_can_err_skb()` returns `NULL`. [F51]
- In `dev/netlink.c`, `can_get_size()` (`can_data_bittiming_get_size`, `can_tdc_get_size`, `can_ctrlmode_ext_get_size`) must match `can_fill_info()`, and pointer-backed constant arrays (`termination_const`, `bitrate_const`, `fd.data_bitrate_const`) must use `sizeof(*ptr) * cnt`, not `sizeof(ptr)`. [F65, F114]
- `flexcan_rx_offload_setup()` must recompute `mb_size`, `mb_count`, `tx_mb_reserved`, `tx_mb_idx`, `tx_mb`, `tx_mask`, and `rx_mask` on every `flexcan_open()`. Address mailboxes only via `flexcan_get_mb(priv, mb_index)` after `flexcan_rx_offload_setup()` with `mb_index < priv->mb_count`. [F46, F53, F115]
- In `flexcan_chip_start()`, save `regs->ctrl` into `priv->reg_ctrl_default` before clearing `FLEXCAN_CTRL_ERR_ALL`; writing `regs->mecr` requires setting `FLEXCAN_CTRL2_ECRWRE`, clearing `FLEXCAN_MECR_ECRWRDIS` before modifying bits, and re-locking both afterward. In `flexcan_suspend()`/`resume()`, transition `priv->can.state` to `CAN_STATE_SLEEPING` and `CAN_STATE_ERROR_ACTIVE` only when `netif_running(dev)`. [F52, F54, F62]
- In `flexcan_start_xmit()` and `flexcan_mailbox_read()`, `mb->data[]` stores big-endian 32-bit words and `i` is a byte offset (`+= sizeof(u32)`): index `cfd->data[i]`, index `mb->data[i / sizeof(u32)]`, and convert with `be32_to_cpup()` / `cpu_to_be32()`. In `flexcan_set_bittiming_cbt()`, clamp `bt->phase_seg1` above `0x20` and `dbt->phase_seg1` above `0x8`, adding the excess to `prop_seg` before `FIELD_PREP()`. [F83, F103, F117]
- In `c_can`, hardware objects are 1-based (`1..msg_obj_num`), whereas TX indices `idx` (`0..msg_obj_tx_num - 1`) for `echo_skb` and `priv->tx_dir` are 0-based (`obj = idx + priv->msg_obj_tx_first`); `ring->obj_num` (`msg_obj_num / 2`) must be a power of two, and `c_can_get_tx_free()` only uses `head - tail` on `BOSCH_D_CAN`. In `c_can.h`, the 11 `C_CAN_IF1_*` and 11 `C_CAN_IF2_*` `enum reg` entries (`IF_ENUM_REG_LEN == 11`) must stay contiguous in identical order across `reg_map_c_can`/`d_can` with 32-bit pairs adjacent. [F66, F77, F105]
- In `c_can_setup_tx_object()`, if RTR differs from `priv->tx_dir` at `idx`, call `c_can_inval_msg_object()` and `change_bit(idx, &priv->tx_dir)`; `c_can_chip_config()` must reset `priv->tx_dir = 0`. In `c_can_poll()`, read `C_CAN_STS_REG` only when `atomic_xchg(&priv->sie_pending, 0)` is non-zero (writing `LEC_UNUSED` on `BOSCH_C_CAN`), and in `c_can_read_objects()`, clear `pend &= ~BIT(obj - 1)` before `IF_MCONT_MSGLST` and `!(ctrl & IF_MCONT_NEWDAT)` `continue` branches. [F58, F59, F94]
- In `cc770`, `cc770_msgobj` (16B with `u8 dontuse`) and `cc770_regs` must remain `__packed`; `msgobj[0]` aliases global registers, hardware objects are `1..15`, logical objects `0..CC770_OBJ_MAX - 1` map via `obj2msgobj(o)`, and `intid2obj(intid)` must be bounds-checked against `CC770_OBJ_MAX`. In `at91_irq_tx()`, `break` on the first mailbox with `!(reg_sr & (1 << mb))`, and only call `netif_wake_queue(dev)` when `(tx_head & get_head_mask(priv)) != 0` or `(tx_tail & get_head_mask(priv)) == 0`. [F80, F92, F119]
- In `grcan.c`, `txwr`, `txrd`, `rxwr`, `rxrd`, and `priv->eskbp` are byte offsets stepped by `GRCAN_MSG_SIZE` (16), whereas `echo_skb` takes `offset / GRCAN_MSG_SIZE`, and `grcan_txspace()` reserves one slot so `txwr != eskbp` when full. Both `dma->tx.handle` and `dma->rx.handle` must be aligned to `GRCAN_BUFFER_ALIGNMENT` (1024B) and sizes must satisfy `!GRCAN_INVALID_BUFFER_SIZE(s)`. [F84, F85]

## Synchronization

- In `c_can_start_xmit()`, `flexcan_start_xmit()`, `grcan_start_xmit()`, and `ifi_canfd_start_xmit()`, TX setup and `can_put_echo_skb()` must complete before writing the register/command that triggers transmission so TX-complete IRQs cannot race ahead of `priv->echo_skb[idx]`. [F2, F20]
- `close_candev()` and `can_restart_now()` hold `rtnl_lock` and call `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&priv->restart_work)` before `can_flush_echo_skb()` or `can_restart()`; therefore `can_restart_work()`, `can_restart()`, and `do_set_mode(..., CAN_MODE_START)` must never acquire `rtnl_lock`. In `can_restart()`, call `netif_carrier_on()` before `do_set_mode(dev, CAN_MODE_START)` (reverting with `netif_carrier_off()` on error). [F15, F16, F154]
- Configuration mutations (`can_change_mtu`, `can_ctrlmode_changelink`, `can_dbt_changelink`, `can_changelink`, `at91_can` `mb0_id_store`) must hold `rtnl_lock` and return `-EBUSY` when `dev->flags & IFF_UP`. `can_set_termination()` must use `gpiod_set_value_cansleep()`. [F29, F146, F161]
- `can_rx_offload_queue_timestamp()`/`tail()` enqueue only to `offload->skb_irq_queue`, spliced into `offload->skb_queue` under `offload->skb_queue.lock` in `can_rx_offload_irq_finish()` or `can_rx_offload_threaded_irq_finish()` (which brackets `napi_schedule()` with `local_bh_disable()`/`enable()`). In `can_rx_offload_napi_poll()`, after `napi_complete_done()` when `work_done < quota`, recheck `!skb_queue_empty(&offload->skb_queue)` and call `napi_schedule()`. [F17, F18, F147]
- Echo SKB and `can_rx_offload_queue_*()` helpers must use `dev_kfree_skb_any()` / `dev_consume_skb_any()`, never `kfree_skb()` / `consume_skb()`. Hardirq/NAPI paths (`c_can_handle_state_change()`, `flexcan_irq_state()`) must call `__c_can_get_berr_counter()` and `__flexcan_get_berr_counter()` instead of the PM-sleeping `c_can_get_berr_counter()` / `flexcan_get_berr_counter()`. [F149, F150]
- In `c_can_main.c`, `c_can_start_xmit()` must use `IF_TX` (`1`) while NAPI polling and `c_can_configure_msg_objects()` use `IF_NAPI` (`0`). `c_can_tx_busy()` must execute `smp_mb()` after `netif_stop_queue()` and recheck `c_can_get_tx_free()`, pairing with `c_can_do_tx()` executing `smp_mb()` between `tx_ring->tail += pkts` and `netif_wake_queue()`. `c_can_hw_raminit_syscon()` must hold `raminit_lock`, mask regmap updates/polls to `start | done`, and clear `done` in `ctrl` after pulsing `start`. [F21, F56, F57]
- In `flexcan_chip_interrupts_enable()`, bracket writes to `regs->ctrl`, `regs->imask2`, and `regs->imask1` with `disable_irq(dev->irq)` and `enable_irq(dev->irq)`. [F28]
- In `grcan.c`, always acquire `priv->lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave()` across all accesses to `eskbp`, `resetting`, `closing`, `catch_up_echo_skb()`, `grcan_start()`, `netif_wake_queue()`, `grcan_reset_timer()` (only when `!resetting && !closing`), and `regs->imr` re-enable in `grcan_poll()`. Resync `eskbp` to `regs->txrd` on `grcan_reset()`, and save/restore `eskbp` and ring/imr registers across `grcan_reset()` in `grcan_running_reset()`. [F23, F26, F60, F61, F151]
- In `grcan_close()`, set `priv->closing = true` under `priv->lock`, unlock before `timer_delete_sync(&hang_timer)` / `rr_timer`, re-lock for `grcan_stop_hardware(dev)`, and call `free_irq()` and `grcan_free_dma_buffers()` after unlocking. Execute `mb()` between reading `dma->rx.buf` and updating `&regs->rxrd` in `grcan_receive()`, and `wmb()` between writing `dma->tx.buf` and updating `&regs->txwr` in `grcan_start_xmit()`. [F141, F142]

## Object Lifetimes

- `__can_get_echo_skb()`, `can_free_echo_skb()`, and `can_flush_echo_skb()` must clear `priv->echo_skb[idx] = NULL`. `can_skb_set_owner()` must use `refcount_inc_not_zero(&sk->sk_refcnt)` before setting `skb->destructor = sock_efree`. [F6, F7]
- In `c_can_plat_probe()`, jump to `exit_free_device` before `pm_runtime_enable()` and `exit_pm_runtime` (`pm_runtime_disable()`) after. `register_c_can_dev()` and `c_can_stop()` must select sleep pinctrl state, and `c_can_start()` must select active/default pinctrl (putting the handle with `pinctrl_put()`). In `cc770` probes, call `free_cc770dev(dev)` on error after `alloc_cc770dev()` before `iounmap()` and region release. [F9, F11, F138]
- In `flexcan_probe()`, `register_flexcandev()` calls `pm_runtime_put()` only on success, so `flexcan_probe()` must call `pm_runtime_put_noidle()` on `register_flexcandev()` failure and `flexcan_remove()` must not. In `flexcan_open()`, keep interrupts masked in `regs->ctrl` until `can_rx_offload_enable()` and all `request_irq()` calls succeed; on close/unwind, call `flexcan_chip_interrupts_disable()` before `free_irq()`, `can_rx_offload_disable()` before stopping the chip, and `can_rx_offload_del()` before `close_candev()`. [F12, F13, F130]
- When `netif_running(dev)` and `!device_may_wakeup(device)`, `flexcan_suspend()` must disable the transceiver and `flexcan_resume()` must re-enable it before `flexcan_chip_start()` (disabling it again if `flexcan_chip_start()` fails). `flexcan_noirq_suspend()`/`resume()` must guard `pm_runtime_force_suspend()`/`resume()` with the identical `!(device_may_wakeup(device) && (quirks & FLEXCAN_QUIRK_SETUP_STOP_MODE_SCMI))` check when `netif_running(dev)`. [F132, F133]
- Devices allocated with `alloc_c_can_dev()` must be freed via `free_c_can_dev()` (calling `netif_napi_del(&priv->napi)` before `free_candev()`), and `grcan_remove()` must also call `netif_napi_del(&priv->napi)` before `free_candev()`. In `grcan_close()`, call `close_candev(dev)` before clearing `echo_skb_max`/`echo_skb` and freeing `priv->echo_skb`; `grcan_free_dma_buffers()` must pass `dma->base_size`, `dma->base_buf`, and `dma->base_handle` to `dma_free_coherent()`; and `grcan_probe()`/`grcan_remove()` must call `of_node_put(sysid_parent)` and `irq_dispose_mapping()`. [F8, F10, F120, F143]

## API Contracts

- `candev` drivers must validate TX frames with `can_dev_dropped_skb()` (not `can_dropped_invalid_skb()`), and SKB protocol checks must accept `ETH_P_CANXL` alongside `ETH_P_CAN`/`ETH_P_CANFD`. `can_put_echo_skb()`, `can_get_echo_skb()`, and `can_free_echo_skb()` take a 0-based `idx < priv->echo_skb_max` (subtract `get_mb_tx_first(priv)` in `at91_can` and `priv->msg_obj_tx_first` in `c_can`); `can_put_echo_skb()` always consumes/frees `skb` when `idx < echo_skb_max`, so finish reading `skb` and `NETDEV_TX_BUSY` checks first. [F1, F36, F75, F86, F111]
- `can_rx_offload_queue_timestamp()`/`tail()` always consume/free `skb` (even on `-ENOBUFS`), so callers must never free `skb` on error. `offload->mailbox_read` must return `NULL` when empty, an `sk_buff *` on read, and `ERR_PTR(-errno)` after acknowledging the hardware mailbox when `drop` is true or SKB allocation fails. `add_timestamp()`/`add_fifo()` require `mailbox_read != NULL` (`mb_first`, `mb_last < 64`, `mb_last > 0` if `mb_first >= mb_last`), while `add_manual()` requires `mailbox_read == NULL`. [F3, F30, F32, F74]
- `alloc_can*_skb()` and `alloc_can_err_skb()` set `*cf = NULL` on failure; `can_change_state()` accepts `cf == NULL` and must still be called when `alloc_can_err_skb()` fails. Before `register_candev()`, `termination_const`, `termination_const_cnt`, and `do_set_termination` must be all set or all unset, `bitrate_const` paired with `bitrate_const_cnt` and mutually exclusive with `bittiming_const`, and `sjw_max != 0`; call `can_set_static_ctrlmode(dev, static_mode)` after setting `priv->ctrlmode_supported` with disjoint bits; every `ndo_open`/`ndo_stop` must call `open_candev()`/`close_candev()`. [F33, F163, F164, F165]
- Do not add `cf->len` to `stats->rx_bytes` or `tx_bytes` when `CAN_RTR_FLAG` or `CAN_ERR_FLAG` is set. In NAPI poll callbacks, count every RX and error SKB passed to `netif_receive_skb()` toward `work_done`, and only call `napi_complete_done()`/`napi_complete()` and re-enable IRQs when `work_done < quota`. [F172, F173]

## Error Handling

- `can_put_echo_skb()`, `__can_get_echo_skb()`, and `can_free_echo_skb()` must check `idx < priv->echo_skb_max` and return `-EINVAL`/`NULL` via `netdev_err()` instead of `BUG_ON()`. `can_restart()` and `can_changelink()` must check `priv->do_set_mode != NULL`, and `can_restart_now()` must propagate `can_restart()`'s error. [F38, F180]
- Disabling `CAN_CTRLMODE_FD` must zero `priv->fd.data_bittiming` and `priv->fd.tdc` and clear `CAN_CTRLMODE_FD_TDC_MASK`; `can_tdc_changelink()` failure in `can_dbt_changelink()` must clear `tdc_mask` from `priv->ctrlmode`. In `can_tdc_fill_info()`, keep `err` initialized non-zero so `IFLA_CAN_TDC_TDCV` is emitted only when `tdc_manual` is true or `do_get_auto_tdcv` succeeds. [F43, F44]
- In `flexcan_probe()`, convert `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)` from `devm_regulator_get_optional()` to `NULL` in `priv->reg_xceiver`, and in `flexcan_setup_stop_mode()`, treat `-EINVAL` as `0` while propagating other negative errors. In `grcan_start_xmit()`, call `grcan_txbug_workaround()` before `can_put_echo_skb()` and return `*netdev_tx_status` on `-EBUSY`. [F41, F42, F45]

## External Contracts

- `IFLA_CAN_RESTART` (`can_restart_now()`) is only allowed when `priv->do_set_mode` is set, `dev->flags & IFF_UP`, `priv->restart_ms == 0`, and `priv->state == CAN_STATE_BUS_OFF`. [F176]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `can_dbt_changelink()` and `can_changelink()`, pass the local `dbt`/`bt` (not `dbt_params->data_bittiming` or `priv->bittiming`) to `bitrate_max` checks and `can_calc_tdco()` before `memcpy()`. In `can_calc_bittiming()` / `can_fixup_bittiming()`, cast 32-bit operands to `u64` before multiplying and use `do_div()` or `DIV_U64_ROUND_CLOSEST()`. Use non-strict `>=` and `<=` for directional error-state checks in `can_change_state()` and `flexcan_irq_state()`. [F87, F97, F101]
- In `__skb_queue_add_sort()`, use `insert` saved before `break` (not `pos`, which equals the list head sentinel on full walk). In `at91_irq()` and `flexcan_irq()`, `break` when `can_rx_offload_irq_offload_timestamp()` returns 0. Pass `&priv->regs->iflag1` or `&priv->regs->imask1` (never `iflag2`/`imask2` at `addr - 4`) to `flexcan_read64_mask()` / `flexcan_write64()`, and use `BIT_ULL()`, `GENMASK_ULL()`, and `(u64)` casts for 64-bit mailbox masks. [F82, F89, F90, F102]
- In `flexcan`: check `if (bitrate)` before dividing in `flexcan_chip_freeze()`; bracket `FLEXCAN_CTRL_CLK_SRC` writes with `flexcan_chip_disable()`/`enable()`; assert `FLEXCAN_CTRL2_WRMFRZ` in `flexcan_ram_init()` and run it before setting `FLEXCAN_MCR_FEN`; clear `FLEXCAN_MCR_SRX_DIS` and `FLEXCAN_FDCTRL_TDCEN` when `CAN_CTRLMODE_LOOPBACK` is set; write `FLEXCAN_MB_CODE_TX_INACTIVE` to `tx_mb_reserved->can_ctrl` in `flexcan_chip_start()` and twice after writing `tx_mb` in `flexcan_start_xmit()` (ERR005829); and clear `regs->iflag1` then read `&regs->timer` on all paths in `flexcan_mailbox_read()`. [F108, F125, F127, F128, F129, F131]
- In `at91_can`, disable the TX mailbox (`AT91_MB_MODE_DISABLED`) before writing `AT91_MID(mb)` in `at91_start_xmit()`, OR-accumulate every `AT91_SR` read into `*reg_sr_p` in `at91_get_reg_sr_rx()`, and do a dummy `at91_read(priv, AT91_SR)` after `AT91_MR_CANEN` in `at91_chip_start()`. In `cc770`, call `cc770_probe_chip()` before `register_candev()`; check `CC770_OBJ_FLAG_RTR` before `CC770_OBJ_FLAG_RX` in `cc770_interrupt()`; in `cc770_tx_interrupt()`, if `ctrl1 & NEWDAT_SET`, call `cc770_rx()` and re-trigger `cc770_tx()` without consuming `priv->tx_skb`. In `ifi_canfd`, verify `IFI_CANFD_IP_ID` and minimum revision in probe, and write `IFI_CANFD_ERROR_CTR_UNLOCK_MAGIC` before resetting/enabling `IFI_CANFD_ERROR_CTR`. [F64, F95, F139, F140, F144, F145]
