# INTEL ETHERNET DRIVERS — drivers/net/ethernet/intel (part 7)

This component comprises the Intel 82576/I350 Virtual Function driver (`igbvf`) and the hardware abstraction, diagnostics, register dump, ethtool, LED, and MAC/NVM/I225 layers of the Intel I225/I226 2.5GbE driver (`igc`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `struct igbvf_buffer`, `mapped_as_page` discriminates whether Tx `dma` was mapped with `skb_frag_dma_map()` or `dma_map_single()`, while on Rx rings `adapter->rx_ps_hdr_size` (non-zero vs 0) discriminates `dma_unmap_single()` length and `page_dma` usage. In `igbvf_change_mtu()`, `igbvf_down()` must run before mutating `rx_buffer_len`, and `igbvf_setup_srrctl()` must update `rx_ps_hdr_size` before `igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers()`. [F545, F563]
- In `igbvf_clean_rx_irq()`, split-header length `hlen` from `rx_desc->wb.lower.lo_dword.hs_rss.hdr_info` (`E1000_RXDADV_HDRBUFLEN_MASK`) must be clamped to `adapter->rx_ps_hdr_size` before `skb_put(skb, hlen)`. When `E1000_RXD_STAT_EOP` is clear, `next_buffer`'s unused `skb` and `dma` must move into the current `buffer_info` while the partially assembled `skb` goes into `next_buffer->skb` with `next_buffer->dma = 0` before jumping to `next_desc`. [F583, F591]
- In `igbvf_configure_tx()`, `E1000_DCA_TXCTRL_TX_WB_RO_EN` must be cleared in `DCA_TXCTRL(0)` to disable Tx write-back relaxed ordering, which corrupts `igbvf_clean_tx_irq()`. [F634]
- When `e1000_poll_for_msg()` or `e1000_poll_for_ack()` times out, it sets `hw->mbx.timeout = 0`, failing subsequent `e1000_write_posted_mbx()` calls and making `e1000_check_for_link_vf()` request a reset until `e1000_reset_hw_vf()` restores `E1000_VF_MBX_INIT_TIMEOUT`. In `e1000_update_mc_addr_list_vf()`, `hash_list` aliases `&msgbuf[1]` (`u16` in `u32 msgbuf[E1000_VFMAILBOX_SIZE]`), so multicast hashes must be capped at 30 (`(E1000_VFMAILBOX_SIZE - 1) * 2`). [F559, F611]
- Because `igbvf` hardware counters do not clear on read and wrap at 32 bits, `igbvf_initialize_last_counter_stats()` must init `last_<stat>` and `base_<stat>` at probe, `UPDATE_VF_COUNTER` must accumulate wraps, and `igbvf_get_ethtool_stats()` must subtract `base_stat_offset`. `igbvf_update_stats()` must not read VF counters when `adapter->link_speed == 0`, `test_bit(__IGBVF_RESETTING, &adapter->state)`, or `pci_channel_offline(pdev)`. [F561, F643]
- Fields in `igbvf_gstrings_stats[]`, `igc_gstrings_stats[]`, and `igc_gstrings_net_stats[]` must be 32- or 64-bit (with matching current/base widths in `IGBVF_STAT`). `IGC_STATS_LEN`, `igc_ethtool_get_strings()`, and `igc_ethtool_get_stats()` must stay synced: after `igc_gstrings_stats` and `igc_gstrings_net_stats`, they emit `IGC_TX_QUEUE_STATS_LEN` (3, summing `restart_queue` and `restart_queue2`) per Tx queue and `IGC_RX_QUEUE_STATS_LEN` (5) per Rx queue. [F572, F615]
- In `igc_ethtool_set_ringparam()`, changing `adapter->tx_ring[i]->count` or `rx_ring[i]->count` must update `adapter->tx_ring_count` and `rx_ring_count` in lockstep. [F557]
- In `igc_ethtool_set_coalesce()`, when `IGC_FLAG_QUEUE_PAIRS` is set, independent `tx_coalesce_usecs` changes return `-EINVAL` and `tx_itr_setting` must mirror `rx_itr_setting`; when `rx_coalesce_usecs == 0`, `IGC_FLAG_DMAC` must be cleared; and every `q_vector` must update `itr_val` and `set_itr = 1`. `igc_ethtool_set_channels()` must return `-EINVAL` when `adapter->strict_priority_enable` is set. [F569, F674]
- In `igc_rx_fifo_flush_base()`, when `IGC_MANC_RCV_TCO_EN` is set, save and restore `IGC_RXDCTL(0..3)`, `IGC_RLPML`, `IGC_RCTL`, and `IGC_RFCTL` around the flush, and read `IGC_ROC`, `IGC_RNBC`, and `IGC_MPC` afterward to clear error counts. [F568]
- In `igc_mac.c`, `igc_config_fc_after_link_up()` must only mutate `hw->fc.current_mode` (leaving user-configured `hw->fc.requested_mode` intact for `igc_setup_link()`). When decoding `IGC_STATUS` in `igc_get_speed_and_duplex_copper()` and `igc_ethtool_get_link_ksettings()`, `IGC_STATUS_SPEED_1000` is also set at 2.5 Gbps, so check `IGC_STATUS_SPEED_2500` first. [F571, F595]
- In `igc_led_hw_control_is_supported()`, the combined TX+RX check (`(flags & BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_TX)) && (flags & BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_RX)) && !(flags & ~IGC_ACTIVITY_MODES)`) must precede single-direction rejection (`flags & IGC_ACTIVITY_MODES`) and single-bit check (`flags & (flags - 1)`). [F596]
- `TXD_USE_COUNT(S)` in `igc.h` must use `DIV_ROUND_UP(S, IGC_MAX_DATA_PER_TXD)`. `IGC_PTM_SHORT_CYC_DEFAULT` in `igc_defines.h` must be `>= 4` us. `reg_test[]` in `igc_diag.c` and `igc_reg_info_tbl[]` in `igc_dump.c` must end with a zeroed sentinel. [F603, F630, F631]

## Synchronization
- Callers in `igc_ethtool.c` must hold `adapter->nfc_rule_lock` across `igc_get_nfc_rule()`, `igc_ethtool_check_nfc_rule()`, `igc_add_nfc_rule()`, `igc_del_nfc_rule()`, and any access to the borrowed `struct igc_nfc_rule` pointer from `igc_get_nfc_rule()`. [F508]
- `igc_get_hw_semaphore_i225()` must call `igc_put_hw_semaphore()` if `IGC_SWSM_SWESMBI` times out, and `igc_acquire_swfw_sync_i225()` / `igc_release_swfw_sync_i225()` must always call `igc_put_hw_semaphore(hw)` before sleeping or returning. [F516]
- While holding `hw->nvm.ops.acquire(hw)`, `igc_validate_nvm_checksum_i225()` and `igc_update_nvm_checksum_i225()` must use raw `igc_read_nvm_eerd()` and `igc_write_nvm_srwr()` (not `hw->nvm.ops.read`/`write`, which re-acquire the non-recursive semaphore) and release on all exits. `igc_read_nvm_srrd_i225()` and `igc_write_nvm_srwr_i225()` must acquire/release the NVM semaphore in bursts of at most `IGC_EERD_EEWR_MAX_COUNT` words. [F517, F535]
- In `igc_ethtool_get_stats()`, hold `adapter->stats64_lock` across `igc_update_stats()` and copying `adapter->stats`/`stats64`, and read queue `tx_stats`/`rx_stats` inside `u64_stats_fetch_begin()`/`retry()` loops on `tx_syncp`, `tx_syncp2`, and `rx_syncp`. In `igc_led_set()` and `igc_led_get()`, serialize `IGC_LEDCTL` with `adapter->led_mutex` wrapped in `pm_runtime_get_sync()` / `pm_runtime_put()`. [F529, F530]
- In `igbvf_tx_queue_adv()`, execute `wmb()` after populating `tx_desc->read.*` and before setting `buffer_info[first].next_to_watch = tx_desc` and writing `tx_ring->tail`, paired with `smp_rmb()` in `igbvf_clean_tx_irq()` after reading non-NULL `eop_desc = buffer_info->next_to_watch`. In `igbvf_maybe_stop_tx()`, after `netif_stop_queue()`, execute `smp_mb()` before rechecking `igbvf_desc_unused() < size`, paired with `smp_mb()` in `igbvf_clean_tx_irq()` between updating `next_to_clean` and checking `netif_queue_stopped()`. [F536, F538]
- All `igbvf` PF-VF mailbox callers (`e1000_write_mbx_vf`, `e1000_read_mbx_vf`, `hw->mac.ops` callbacks) must hold `spin_lock_bh(&hw->mbx_lock)` because `igbvf_set_rx_mode` runs in BH context. `igbvf_set_multi`, `igbvf_set_uni`, and `igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers` run in atomic context and must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and `udelay()`. Because `igbvf_intr_msix_tx` calls `igbvf_clean_tx_irq` in hardirq context, `igbvf_put_txbuf` must use `dev_kfree_skb_any()`. [F644, F645, F646]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `igbvf_down()` and `igbvf_remove()`, set `__IGBVF_DOWN` before `timer_delete_sync(&adapter->watchdog_timer)` and `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->watchdog_task)` so `watchdog_timer` is not re-armed. In `igbvf_remove()`, `timer_delete_sync(&adapter->watchdog_timer)`, `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->reset_task)`, `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->watchdog_task)`, and `unregister_netdev()` must precede `netif_napi_del(&adapter->rx_ring->napi)`, which must precede `kfree(adapter->rx_ring)`. [F510, F525]
- In `igbvf_open()`, `igbvf_setup_tx_resources()`, `igbvf_setup_rx_resources()`, and `igbvf_configure()` must complete before `igbvf_request_irq()`; on IRQ failure, free Rx then Tx resources and call `igbvf_reset()`. When resizing live rings in `igbvf_set_ringparam()` and `igc_ethtool_set_ringparam()`, do not free `adapter->tx_ring`/`rx_ring` structs; allocate into `temp_ring` first, then free old resources and copy `temp_ring` in place. [F511, F512]
- Every path unmapping/freeing `struct igbvf_buffer` (`igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers()`, `igbvf_clean_rx_irq()`, `igbvf_clean_rx_ring()`, `igbvf_put_txbuf()`) must clear `skb`, `page`, `dma`, and `page_dma` to `NULL`/`0`. On DMA failure in `igbvf_tx_map_adv()`, clear failed `buffer_info` fields and unmap prior fragments via `igbvf_put_txbuf()` without freeing `skb`; `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` then frees `skb`, clears `buffer_info[first].time_stamp`, and rewinds `next_to_use` to `first`. [F514, F515]
- After `skb_fill_page_desc()` in `igbvf_clean_rx_irq()`, either transfer page ownership via `buffer_info->page = NULL` (if `rx_buffer_len > PAGE_SIZE / 2` or `page_count(page) != 1`) or call `get_page(buffer_info->page)` for half-page reuse. [F520]
- In `igc_ethtool_set_mm()`, call `static_branch_inc`/`dec(&igc_fpe_enabled)` only when `fpe->mmsv.pmac_enabled != cmd->pmac_enabled`, checked before `ethtool_mmsv_set_mm()`. In `igc_ethtool_diag_test()`, offline testing must clear `__IGC_TESTING` before calling `igc_open(netdev)`. [F519, F521]
- In `igc_init_phy_params_base()`, call `hw->phy.ops.reset(hw)` before `igc_get_phy_id(hw)` to reset the PHY page register. In `igc_init_hw_base()`, call `igc_clear_hw_cntrs_base(hw)` after `igc_setup_link(hw)`. [F624, F625]

## API Contracts
- `e1000_check_for_bit_vf()`, `e1000_check_for_msg_vf()`, `e1000_check_for_ack_vf()`, and `e1000_check_for_rst_vf()` return `0` (`E1000_SUCCESS`) when the `V2PMAILBOX` bit is set and `-E1000_ERR_MBX` when clear; callers must negate the return. All `V2PMAILBOX(0)` reads must use `e1000_read_v2p_mailbox()` and `e1000_check_for_bit_vf()` to latch `E1000_V2PMAILBOX_R2C_BITS` into `hw->dev_spec.vf.v2p_mailbox`. [F539, F558]
- `e1000_check_for_link_vf()` returns `0` even when link is down and `-E1000_ERR_MAC_INIT` only when a PF NACK or mailbox timeout requires a reset; callers (`igbvf_has_link`) must check `!hw->mac.get_link_status` or `E1000_STATUS_LU` for link state. [F540]
- The `size` argument to `hw->mbx.ops.read`, `write`, `read_posted`, and `write_posted` is in `u32` words (max `E1000_VFMAILBOX_SIZE = 16`), not bytes. `e1000_update_mc_addr_list_vf()` and `igc_update_mc_addr_list()` take a packed `u8` array of `ETH_ALEN`-byte MACs and the address count (not byte size). [F553, F610]
- On flashless I225/I226 (`!igc_get_flash_presence_i225(hw)`), `hw->nvm.ops.write`, `validate`, and `update` are `NULL`; callers must check flash presence or non-NULL ops. `hw->nvm.ops.read()`/`write()` take `offset` and `count` in 16-bit LE words; byte-addressed callers (`igc_ethtool_get_eeprom`/`set_eeprom`) must shift byte offsets by 1, swap endianness, and read-modify-write odd boundary bytes. `igc_write_nvm_srwr()` must reject `offset >= nvm->word_size`, `words > (nvm->word_size - offset)`, or `words == 0` with `-IGC_ERR_NVM`. [F543, F550, F575]
- `igc_acquire_swfw_sync_i225()` and `igc_release_swfw_sync_i225()` expect unshifted 16-bit software masks (`IGC_SWFW_EEP_SM`, `IGC_SWFW_PHY0_SM`). `igc_power_down_phy_copper_base()` must only call `igc_power_down_phy_copper(hw)` when both `igc_enable_mng_pass_thru(hw)` and `igc_check_reset_block(hw)` are false. [F554, F642]
- In `igbvf_set_ringparam()` and `igc_ethtool_set_ringparam()`, clamp Tx/Rx counts to `[MIN, MAX]` and align to `REQ_TX_DESCRIPTOR_MULTIPLE` / `REQ_RX_DESCRIPTOR_MULTIPLE` (8). In `igbvf_poll()`, when `work_done < budget`, update ITR and re-enable `EIMS` only if `napi_complete_done(napi, work_done)` returns true and `__IGBVF_DOWN` is clear. [F573, F660]
- In `igbvf_tso()`, `skb_cow_head(skb, 0)` must succeed before modifying `skb_network_header(skb)` or `skb_checksum_start(skb)`; on negative return, `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` must drop `skb` via `dev_kfree_skb_any()` and return `NETDEV_TX_OK`. [F661]
- `igbvf_features_check()` clears csum/TSO when `skb_network_offset(skb) > IGBVF_MAX_MAC_HDR_LEN` (127) or network header length `> IGBVF_MAX_NETWORK_HDR_LEN` (511), and clears `NETIF_F_TSO` on encapsulated SKBs without `NETIF_F_TSO_MANGLEID`. In `igbvf_probe()`, add `NETIF_F_HW_VLAN_CTAG_FILTER`/`RX`/`TX` to `netdev->features` only after initializing `netdev->vlan_features` from `netdev->features`. [F662, F666]
- In `igbvf_tx_csum()`, verify `skb_csum_is_sctp(skb)` before setting `E1000_ADVTXD_TUCMD_L4T_SCTP` and fall back to `skb_checksum_help(skb)` on unrecognized requests; even on non-`CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` or fallback, jump to `no_csum` to emit a context descriptor if `IGBVF_TX_FLAGS_VLAN` is set, and recheck `skb->ip_summed == CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` in `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` before setting `IGBVF_TX_FLAGS_CSUM`. In `igbvf_rx_checksum_adv()`, set `CHECKSUM_UNNECESSARY` only when `IXSM`, `IGBVF_FLAG_RX_CSUM_DISABLED`, `TCPE`, and `IPE` are clear and `TCPCS` or `UDPCS` is set. [F593, F663, F664]

## Error Handling
- `igbvf_set_interrupt_capability()` resets `adapter->msix_entries` to `NULL` without returning an error on `kcalloc()` or `pci_enable_msix_range()` failure, so `igbvf_request_irq`, `igbvf_free_irq`, `igbvf_irq_disable`, and `igbvf_up` must guard `adapter->msix_entries` for `NULL`. [F542]
- Zero-initialize `struct igc_nfc_rule` (`kzalloc`) before `igc_ethtool_init_nfc_rule()` and `igc_ethtool_check_nfc_rule()` (which uses `memcmp()`). Zero the 3-dword stack `msgbuf[3]` in `e1000_rar_set_vf()` and `e1000_set_uc_addr_vf()` before copying the 6-byte MAC. In `igc_init_nvm_params_base()`, check `rd32(IGC_EECD)` for all-ones (`!(~eecd)`) and return `-ENXIO`. [F547, F548, F626]

## External Contracts
- In `igc_ethtool_get_nfc_rules()`, check `cnt == cmd->rule_cnt` before writing `rule_locs[cnt]` and return `-EMSGSIZE` if full. In `igc_ethtool_init_nfc_rule()`, set `IGC_FILTER_FLAG_DEFAULT_QUEUE` only when `match_flags == 0`, `fsp->flow_type == ETHER_FLOW` (no `FLOW_EXT`), and MAC/`h_proto` masks are all zero. [F577, F578]
- In `igc_ethtool_get_regs()` and `igbvf_get_regs()`, `memset` the full `get_regs_len` buffer first; `igc_ethtool_get_regs()` must read `IGC_EICS`/`IGC_ICS` (not clear-on-read `EICR`/`ICR`) and keep duplicate `IGC_RAL`/`IGC_RAH` entries at `172..203`. [F669]

## Known Pitfalls
- Because `ring->size` is rounded up to 4096 bytes, `igbvf_configure_tx()` and `igbvf_configure_rx()` must program `TDLEN(0)` and `RDLEN(0)` with `ring->count * sizeof(descriptor)`, not `ring->size`. In `igbvf_configure_tx()`, `igbvf_configure_rx()`, and `igbvf_down()`, clearing `E1000_TXDCTL_QUEUE_ENABLE` or `E1000_RXDCTL_QUEUE_ENABLE` must be followed by `e1e_flush()` and `msleep(10)`. [F602, F633]
- In `igc_init_nvm_params_base()`, cap `size` at 15 before `nvm->word_size = BIT(size)`. In `igc_rar_set()`, follow both `wr32(IGC_RAL(index), ...)` and `wr32(IGC_RAH(index), ...)` with `wrfl()`. [F604, F632]
- In `igbvf_receive_skb()`, on `e1000_vfadapt_i350` (`IGBVF_FLAG_RX_LB_VLAN_BSWAP`) with `E1000_RXD_STAT_VP` and `E1000_RXDEXT_STATERR_LB` set, decode the VLAN tag with `be16_to_cpu()` instead of `le16_to_cpu()`, and mask with `E1000_RXD_SPC_VLAN_MASK` (`0x0FFF`) before indexing `adapter->active_vlans` with `test_bit()`. [F617, F629]
