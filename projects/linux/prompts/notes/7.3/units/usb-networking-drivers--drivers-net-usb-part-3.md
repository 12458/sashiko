# USB NETWORKING DRIVERS — drivers/net/usb (part 3)

This unit comprises four `usbnet`-based USB Ethernet minidrivers: `smsc95xx` (Microchip/SMSC LAN95xx USB 2.0 10/100 controllers), `sr9700` (CoreChip SR9700 USB 1.1), `sr9800` (CoreChip SR9800 USB 2.0), and `zaurus` (Sharp Zaurus PDAs and pseudo-MDLM/BLAN devices), providing USB vendor register access, MDIO/phylib and IRQ domain integration (`smsc95xx`), Wake-on-LAN, runtime PM, and RX/TX SKB framing.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `smsc95xx_bind()` sets `EVENT_NO_IP_ALIGN` in `dev->flags` and `smsc95xx_reset()` sets `HW_CFG_RXDOFF_` to `NET_IP_ALIGN << 9`, which `smsc95xx_rx_fixup()` relies on when pulling `4 + NET_IP_ALIGN` header bytes and computing `align_count = (4 - ((size + NET_IP_ALIGN) % 4)) % 4`. Frame `size` (`RX_STS_FL_`) must be checked against `skb->len` and `ETH_FRAME_LEN + 12` before copying or pulling; on `RX_STS_ES_`, record error stats and still `skb_pull` `size` and `align_count` rather than returning `0`. When `NETIF_F_RXCSUM` is active, `smsc95xx_rx_csum_offload()` must run before `skb_trim(..., len - 4)` strips the FCS, reading `skb_tail_pointer(skb) - 2` via `get_unaligned()` before trimming 2 bytes. [F234, F255, F305, F309]
- In `sr_rx_fixup()` (`sr9800.c`), each subframe's 11-bit `size` (`header & 0x7ff`) must match `(~header >> 16) & 0x07ff` and satisfy `size <= dev->net->mtu + ETH_HLEN + VLAN_HLEN` and `size + offset <= skb->len`, advancing `offset` by `(size + 1) & 0xfffe` to reach exact `skb->len`. [F243]
- When `sr_tx_fixup()` shifts `skb->data` in place via `memmove(skb->head + 4, skb->data, skb->len)` (only when `!skb_cloned(skb)` and `headroom + tailroom >= 4 + padlen`), it must immediately call `skb_set_tail_pointer(skb, skb->len)` before pushing the header or appending `padbytes`. [F229]
- `struct sr_data` (`sr9800.h`) must not exceed `sizeof(unsigned long[5])` (20 bytes on 32-bit); async writes in `sr_set_multicast()` and `sr_set_mac_address()` must pass `data->multi_filter` and `data->mac_addr` in `dev->data`, never stack buffers, to `sr_write_cmd_async()`. [F230]
- Interrupt URB callbacks must validate `urb->actual_length` (`== 4` in `smsc95xx_status`, `>= 8` in `sr9700_status` and `sr_status`) before reading `urb->transfer_buffer`. [F246]
- `pdata->mac_cr` persists across calls: in `smsc95xx_set_multicast()`, each `IFF_PROMISC` / `IFF_ALLMULTI` / `!netdev_mc_empty` / `else` branch must clear the other two filter bits (`MAC_CR_PRMS_`, `MAC_CR_MCPAS_`, `MAC_CR_HPFILT_`), and the 6-bit index from `smsc95xx_hash()` must mask its shift with `0x1F` (`0x01 << (bitnum & 0x1F)`) while `bitnum & 0x20` selects `pdata->hash_hi` vs `pdata->hash_lo`. In `smsc95xx_mac_update_fullduplex()`, `MAC_CR_FDPX_` and `MAC_CR_RCVOWN_` must be toggled with opposite polarities. [F247, F248, F261]
- In `sr9700_set_multicast()` and `sr_set_multicast()`, `IFF_PROMISC` must precede `IFF_ALLMULTI || netdev_mc_count(...) > MAX`, which must precede `netdev_for_each_mc_addr`. Shifting unsigned 32-bit `ether_crc(ETH_ALEN, ha->addr)` right by 26 bounds the hash to `0..63` so `>> 3` indexes the 8-byte filter array and `& 7` shifts by `< 8`. [F249, F262]
- `pdata->features` is probed from the upper 16 bits of `ID_REV` in `smsc95xx_bind()`: `PHY_EDPD_CONFIG` and `SUSPEND1` in `smsc95xx_autosuspend()` require `FEATURE_PHY_NLP_CROSSOVER`, and `wuff_filter_count` (4 vs 8) is gated on `FEATURE_8_WAKEUP_FILTERS`. In `smsc95xx_suspend()`, `WUFF` write loops iterate over all `wuff_filter_count` (up to 8) slots, so `filter_mask` must be zero-allocated for 32 `u32` words (`LAN9500A_WUFF_NUM * 4`) and `command[2]`, `offset[2]`, and `crc[4]` sized for 8 filters and zero-initialized; when `pdata->wolopts & WAKE_PHY` is set, enter `SUSPEND1` and jump to `done` only when `!link_up`, falling through to configure `PM_CTL_ED_EN_` and `SUSPEND0` when `link_up` is true. [F253, F271, F280]
- `smsc95xx_resume()` must snapshot `pdata->suspend_flags` into a local variable and clear `pdata->suspend_flags = 0` before any `WUCSR` or `PM_CTRL` register accesses that can fail and jump to `done`. [F224]
- Both `smsc95xx_reset()` (via `smsc95xx_set_mac_address()`) and `sr9800_reset()` (via `SR_CMD_WRITE_NODE_ID`) must reprogram the hardware MAC address registers from `dev->net->dev_addr` after chip software reset. [F286]
- In `smsc95xx_set_pauseparam()`, store user-requested `pause` fields in `pdata->pause_rx`, `pdata->pause_tx`, and `pdata->pause_autoneg` unconditionally, while forcing `pause_autoneg_rx` and `pause_autoneg_tx` passed to `phy_set_asym_pause()` to `false` when `!pause->autoneg`. [F252]
- `smsc95xx_ethtool_getregs()` must step register byte offset `i` by `sizeof(u32)` from `ID_REV` through `COE_CR` while indexing `u32 *data` by `j++`, matching `COE_CR - ID_REV + sizeof(u32)` from `smsc95xx_ethtool_getregslen()`. [F272]
- Busy-wait loops in `smsc95xx_phy_wait_not_busy()`, `smsc95xx_wait_eeprom()`, and `smsc95xx_eeprom_confirm_not_busy()` must store `jiffies` in `unsigned long start_time` and check `time_after(jiffies, start_time + HZ)`. [F267]

## Synchronization
- In `smsc95xx_bind()`, `dev->driver_priv = pdata` and `spin_lock_init(&pdata->mac_cr_lock)` must precede `smsc95xx_reset(dev)` (which uses both). Likewise, `pdata->is_internal_phy`, `pdata->mdiobus->phy_mask`, `pdata->mdiobus->priv = dev`, and bus callbacks must be set before `mdiobus_register()`, and `pdata->phydev->irq` (with its `irq_domain` mapping and `irq_chip` handler) must be configured before `phy_connect_direct()`. [F202, F206]
- In `smsc95xx.c`, all updates to `pdata->mac_cr` outside `smsc95xx_reset()` must be serialized with `spin_lock_irqsave(&pdata->mac_cr_lock, flags)` / `spin_unlock_irqrestore()`, dropping the lock before calling `smsc95xx_write_reg()` or `smsc95xx_write_reg_async()`. [F295]
- In `smsc95xx_suspend()`, `smsc95xx_resume()`, and `smsc95xx_reset_resume()`, `pdata->pm_task` must be set to `current` before any register or PHY access and cleared to `NULL` on every exit path so `smsc95xx_read_reg()` and `smsc95xx_write_reg()` use `_nopm` USB helpers only for the active PM task. [F209]
- In `smsc95xx_status()`, `generic_handle_domain_irq(pdata->irqdomain, PHY_HWIRQ)` must run inside `local_irq_save(flags)` / `local_irq_restore(flags)`, and `pdata->irqchip` callbacks must never perform sleeping USB register accesses. [F297]
- Indirect PHY and EEPROM sequences (`smsc95xx_mdio_read`, `smsc95xx_mdio_write`, `smsc95xx_mdiobus_reset`, `smsc95xx_phy_wait_not_busy`, `sr_share_read_word`, `sr_share_write_word`, `sr_mdio_read`, `sr_mdio_write`) must hold `dev->phy_mutex`. In `sr9800.c`, `sr_mdio_read()` and `sr_mdio_write()` must also enable software MII via `sr_set_sw_mii(dev)` before `SR_CMD_READ_MII_REG` / `SR_CMD_WRITE_MII_REG` and restore `sr_set_hw_mii(dev)` before unlocking `dev->phy_mutex`. [F231, F298]

## Object Lifetimes
- Both `smsc95xx_bind()` error unwind and `smsc95xx_unbind()` must release resources in reverse acquisition order: `phy_disconnect`, `mdiobus_unregister`, `mdiobus_free`, `irq_dispose_mapping`, `irq_domain_remove`, `irq_domain_free_fwnode`, and `kfree(pdata)`. `smsc95xx_info` must set `.reset = smsc95xx_reset`, `.check_connect = smsc95xx_start_phy` (`phy_start()` after MAC reset on open), and `.stop = smsc95xx_stop` (`phy_stop()` on close before URBs stop or `phy_disconnect()` runs). [F192, F198, F274]
- When `FEATURE_REMOTE_WAKEUP` is absent in `pdata->features`, `smsc95xx_manage_power()` must pair `usb_autopm_get_interface_no_resume(dev->intf)` (`on == 1`) with `usb_autopm_put_interface(dev->intf)` (`on == 0`), and must not touch autopm when `FEATURE_REMOTE_WAKEUP` is set. In `smsc95xx_suspend()`, `filter_mask` from `kcalloc()` must be freed via `kfree(filter_mask)` both on `WUFF` write failure before `goto done` and right after the `WUFF` loop. [F195, F197]
- `smsc95xx_tx_fixup()`, `sr9700_tx_fixup()`, `sr_tx_fixup()`, and `zaurus_tx_fixup()` must unshare/expand `skb` (`skb_cow_head()` or `!skb_cloned(skb)` plus `skb_copy_expand()`) before mutating `skb->data` or pushing/putting framing bytes, and must call `dev_kfree_skb_any(skb)` whenever returning `NULL`. [F308]
- In `sr9800_bind()`, `sr_get_phyid()` must only be called after `SR_CMD_SW_PHY_SELECT` and `sr9800_phy_powerup()` have powered up and reset the PHY, and `sr_get_phyid()` must poll `MII_PHYSID1` until it is neither `0` nor `0xFFFF` (up to 100 ms). [F290]
- In `zaurus.c`, any `struct driver_info` with `.bind = zaurus_bind` (which claims the CDC slave data interface via `usbnet_generic_cdc_bind()`) must set `.unbind = usbnet_cdc_unbind`. [F193]

## API Contracts
- In `smsc95xx_rx_fixup()`, `sr9700_rx_fixup()`, and `sr_rx_fixup()`, aggregated sub-frames must be copied into new SKBs via `netdev_alloc_skb_ip_align()`; never use `skb_clone()` or overwrite `skb->truesize`. In `smsc95xx_rx_fixup()` and `sr9700_rx_fixup()` (no `FLAG_MULTI_PACKET`), pass non-final SKBs to `usbnet_skb_return()` and trim the final frame in-place in `skb`, returning non-zero without freeing `skb`. In `sr9800.c` (`FLAG_MULTI_PACKET` set), `sr_rx_fixup()` must allocate a new `sr_skb` and call `usbnet_skb_return()` for every sub-frame including the last (leaving `usbnet` to free `skb`), and `sr_tx_fixup()` must call `usbnet_set_skb_tx_stats(skb, 1, 0)` before returning `skb`. [F190, F191, F302, F304]
- All `.tx_fixup` and `.rx_fixup` SKB allocations/reallocations (`sr_tx_fixup`, `zaurus_tx_fixup`, `smsc95xx_rx_fixup`, `sr9700_rx_fixup`, `sr_rx_fixup`) must use caller-passed `gfp_t flags` or `GFP_ATOMIC`-safe helpers (`netdev_alloc_skb_ip_align`, `skb_cow_head`), never `GFP_KERNEL`. [F300]
- `smsc95xx_read_reg()`, `smsc95xx_write_reg()`, `sr_read()`, `sr_write()`, `sr_read_cmd()`, and `sr_write_cmd()` return positive byte counts on success rather than `0`; callers must test `ret < 0` for errors, and functions returning `0`/-errno must not `return ret` on timeout or completion. [F212]
- `sr9700_get_eeprom()` and `sr_get_eeprom()` cast `data` to `__le16 *` and read 2-byte words, so they must reject odd `eeprom->len` (and odd `eeprom->offset` in `sr9700_get_eeprom()`) with `-EINVAL`, and `sr9700_get_eeprom()` must pass word offsets (`eeprom->offset / 2 + i`) to `sr_read_eeprom_word()`. [F220]
- `smsc95xx_init_mac_address()` must query Device Tree via `platform_get_ethdev_address(&dev->udev->dev, dev->net)` using `&dev->udev->dev` (not `&dev->intf->dev`), validate with `is_valid_ether_addr()`, and fall back to EEPROM (`EEPROM_MAC_OFFSET`) then `eth_hw_addr_random()`. [F285]
- In `zaurus.c`, `blan_mdlm_bind()` must verify `intf->cur_altsetting->extra` has one `USB_CDC_MDLM_TYPE` descriptor (`blan_guid` or `safe_guid`) and one `USB_CDC_MDLM_DETAIL_TYPE` descriptor with `detail->bLength == sizeof(*detail) + 2` (type 0, SAFE) or `sizeof(*detail) + 3` (type 1, BLAN) and `(detail->bDetailData[1] & ~0x02) == 0x01`, returning `-ENODEV` otherwise. [F245, F293]

## Error Handling
- Synchronous USB control wrappers (`smsc95xx_read_reg`, `sr_read`, `sr_write`, `sr_read_cmd`, `sr_write_cmd`) must treat non-negative byte counts from `usbnet_read_cmd()` / `usbnet_write_cmd()` shorter than the requested transfer size as errors (`-ENODATA` or `-EINVAL`) before consuming the buffer. [F210]
- Every `driver_info.bind` callback (`smsc95xx_bind`, `sr9700_bind`, `sr9800_bind`, `blan_mdlm_bind`, `zaurus_bind`) must check `usbnet_get_endpoints(dev, intf)` and propagate any non-zero error. In `smsc95xx_bind()`, `ret` holds `0` or `4` after `smsc95xx_reset()`, `smsc95xx_read_reg()`, and `mdiobus_register()`, so any null/zero failure check (`irq_create_mapping()`, `phy_find_first()`) must set `ret` to a negative errno before jumping to a cleanup label. [F213, F214]
- In `smsc95xx_set_pauseparam()` and `smsc95xx_ioctl()`, `ndev->phydev` must be guarded (`!phydev` or `!netif_running(netdev)`) before passing it to `phylib` or reading `phydev->link`. [F217]
- In `smsc95xx_suspend()`, if any step after `usbnet_suspend(intf, message)` fails during runtime autosuspend (`PMSG_IS_AUTO(message)`), it must jump to `done:` and call `usbnet_resume(intf)` before returning the error. [F278]
- `smsc95xx_read_reg()`, `smsc95xx_write_reg()`, `smsc95xx_mdio_read()`, `smsc95xx_mdio_write()`, and `smsc95xx_mac_update_fullduplex()` must suppress `netdev_warn()` when register access returns `-ENODEV`, and `smsc95xx_phy_wait_not_busy()` and `smsc95xx_mdio_read()` must convert `-ENODEV` to `0`. [F313]

## External Contracts
- `smsc95xx_handle_link_change()` must both update MAC duplex/flow-control registers via `smsc95xx_mac_update_fullduplex(dev)` and notify `usbnet` via `usbnet_defer_kevent(dev, EVENT_LINK_CHANGE)`. [F222]
- `smsc95xx_bind()` must only advertise `NETIF_F_IP_CSUM` (IPv4) and never `NETIF_F_IPV6_CSUM` or `NETIF_F_HW_CSUM` in `dev->net->features` or `dev->net->hw_features` because LAN95xx hardware does not convert a `0` UDP checksum to `0xffff` for IPv6. [F306]
- Any device added to `products` in `drivers/net/usb/zaurus.c` matching `ZAURUS_MASTER_INTERFACE` (CDC Ethernet) or `ZAURUS_FAKE_INTERFACE` must have a matching blacklist entry (`.driver_info = 0`) in `drivers/net/usb/cdc_ether.c`. [F292]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `smsc95xx_tx_fixup()`, `smsc95xx_can_tx_checksum(skb)` must reject packets with `skb->len <= 45` or `skb->csum_offset >= (skb->len - skb_checksum_start_offset(skb)) - 5`; when false on a `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` SKB, fold `csum_partial()` in software after `skb_cow_head()` and omit the 4-byte preamble and `TX_CMD_B_CSUM_ENABLE`. Evaluate `tx_cmd_b`, `smsc95xx_can_tx_checksum(skb)`, and `smsc95xx_calc_csum_preamble(skb)` (truncating offsets to `u16 low_16`, `u16 high_16` before `(high_16 << 16) | low_16`) before `skb_push()`, and when hardware TX csum is used, push the 4-byte preamble before the 8-byte `tx_cmd_a`/`tx_cmd_b` header and add 4 to both `tx_cmd_a` and `tx_cmd_b`. [F226, F236, F237, F265, F307]
- In `sr9700_tx_fixup()`, sample unframed length `len = skb->len` before `__skb_push(skb, SR_TX_OVERHEAD)`, but evaluate the ZLP padding check `(skb->len % dev->maxpacket) == 0` after `__skb_push(skb, SR_TX_OVERHEAD)` to increment `len`. [F228]
- Multi-byte headers, checksum trailers, and padding words in SKB and URB buffers (`smsc95xx_rx_csum_offload`, `smsc95xx_rx_fixup`, `smsc95xx_tx_fixup`, `smsc95xx_status`, `sr_rx_fixup`, `sr_tx_fixup`) must use `get_unaligned_le32()`, `get_unaligned()`, or `put_unaligned_le32()`. [F301]
- In `smsc95xx_wait_eeprom()`, the loop breaks on `!(val & E2P_CMD_BUSY_)` or `val & E2P_CMD_TIMEOUT_`, so the post-loop check must test `val & (E2P_CMD_TIMEOUT_ | E2P_CMD_BUSY_)`. In `smsc95xx_reset()`, update `LED_GPIO_CFG` via read-modify-write rather than overwriting it with a constant. When modifying `PM_CTRL` in `smsc95xx_enter_suspend0/1/2/3`, mask out `PM_CTL_SUS_MODE_ | PM_CTL_WUPS_ | PM_CTL_PHY_RST_` before setting `PM_CTL_SUS_MODE_*`. [F250, F282, F283]
- In `sr_get_phyid()`, check the signed `int` return of `sr_mdio_read()` for errors (`<= 0` for `MII_PHYSID1`, `< 0` for `MII_PHYSID2`) and mask with `0xffff` before shifting (`(phy_reg & 0xffff) << 16`) and ORing into `u32 phy_id`. [F266]
