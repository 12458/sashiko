# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu (part 4)

Implements GMC and GTT TTM management (`amdgpu_gmc.c`, `amdgpu_gtt_mgr.c`), BO lifecycle (`amdgpu_object.c`), HMM notifiers (`amdgpu_hmm.c`), VMID/PASID allocators (`amdgpu_ids.c`), IB and scheduler jobs (`amdgpu_ib.c`, `amdgpu_job.c`), IH ring and IRQ dispatch (`amdgpu_ih.c`, `amdgpu_irq.c`), MES (`amdgpu_mes.c`), KMS ioctls (`amdgpu_kms.c`), and MCA/JPEG/ISP/I2C/HDP/LSDMA/MMHUB/NBIO helpers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `to_amdgpu_bo_user()`, `amdgpu_bo_set_tiling_flags()`, `amdgpu_bo_get_tiling_flags()`, `amdgpu_bo_set_metadata()`, and `amdgpu_bo_get_metadata()` are valid only on BOs allocated with `sizeof(struct amdgpu_bo_user)` via `amdgpu_bo_create_user()` and enforce `BUG_ON(bo->tbo.type == ttm_bo_type_kernel)`. [F595]
- `amdgpu_bo_pin()` and `amdgpu_bo_unpin()` must only call `dma_buf_pin()`/`dma_buf_unpin()` and update `adev->vram_pin_size`, `adev->visible_pin_size`, and `adev->gart_pin_size` when `bo->tbo.pin_count` transitions between 0 and 1. [F556]
- In `amdgpu_bo_placement_from_domain()`, `AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_VRAM` must precede `AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_GTT` in `abo->placements` (so `amdgpu_bo_fault_reserve_notify()` sets `TTM_PL_FLAG_DESIRED` on `abo->placements[0]` for VRAM) and `TTM_PL_FLAG_FALLBACK` is set on GTT only when `abo->tbo.resource`, `!(adev->flags & AMD_IS_APU)`, and `domain & abo->preferred_domains & AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_VRAM`; whereas `amdgpu_bo_validate_size()` must test `AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_GTT` before `AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_VRAM` so `(VRAM | GTT)` allocations validate against `TTM_PL_TT`. [F652, F653, F654]
- In `amdgpu_vmid_grab()`, `job->gds_switch_needed` must be computed via `amdgpu_vmid_gds_switch_needed(id, job)` before overwriting `id->gds_*`, `id->gws_*`, and `id->oa_*` with `job`'s values; `amdgpu_vmid_mgr_init()` and `amdgpu_vmid_reset_all()` must start at `j = 1` (never adding `ids[0]` to `ids_lru`) and cap `id_mgr->num_ids` at `adev->vm_manager.first_kfd_vmid` on GCHUB (or MMHUB when `GC_HWIP < 10.0.0`). [F616, F617]
- When a per-queue reset succeeds in `amdgpu_job_timedout()` without full GPU recovery, it must increment `ring->adev->gpu_reset_counter` so `amdgpu_vmid_had_gpu_reset()` detects stale VMID state against `id->current_gpu_reset_count`. [F618]
- In `amdgpu_job_run()`, if `job->generation != amdgpu_vm_generation(adev, job->vm)` or `job->job_run_counter && job->gang_submit`, `finished` must be marked `-ECANCELED` and `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` skipped while still incrementing `job->job_run_counter` and calling `amdgpu_job_free_resources(job)`; in `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, `status` starts with `AMDGPU_HAVE_CTX_SWITCH` before the IB loop and clears `status &= ~AMDGPU_HAVE_CTX_SWITCH` after each iteration so only the first IB gets it. [F619, F658]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_allocate_vm_inv_eng()`, per-hub `vm_inv_engs[i]` bitmasks reserve engine 5 when `adev->enable_mes` and engine 3 when `adev->enable_umsch_mm`, and SDMA shared rings (`amdgpu_sdma_is_shared_inv_eng()`) copy `vm_inv_eng` from `amdgpu_sdma_get_shared_ring()`; in `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb()` and `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb_pasid()`, both direct `gmc_funcs` MMIO and KIQ (`kiq->pmf->kiq_invalidate_tlbs`) must emit `flush_tlb_needs_extra_type_2` (`flush_type = 2`) and `flush_tlb_needs_extra_type_0` (`flush_type = 0`) in lockstep, sizing KIQ `ndw` to match. [F626, F627]
- `amdgpu_gmc_pdb0_alloc()` and `amdgpu_gmc_init_pdb0()` must size PDB0 for `npdes + 1` 8-byte entries (`PAGE_ALIGN((npdes + 1) * 8)`) so the `(npdes + 1)`-th entry maps the system GART PTB after the `npdes` XGMI VRAM entries; walking `gmc->fault_ring` via `fault->next` in `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults()` and `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults_remove()` must stop whenever the next `fault->timestamp >= tmp`. [F646, F660]
- In `struct amdgpu_ih_ring`, `rptr`, `wptr`, `ring_size`, and `ptr_mask` are byte offsets while `ih->ring` is a `uint32_t *` array of LE dwords (indexed via `>> 2` with `le32_to_cpu()`/`cpu_to_le32()`); when `ih->use_bus_addr` is true in `amdgpu_ih_ring_init()`/`amdgpu_ih_ring_fini()`, `dma_alloc_coherent()`/`dma_free_coherent()` must use `ih->ring_size + 8` for `wptr_cpu` and `rptr_cpu`; and in `amdgpu_ih_process()`, if `ih->overflow` is set, skip `amdgpu_ih_set_rptr()` and do not jump to `restart_ih`. [F639, F641, F659]
- In `amdgpu_irq_add_id()` and `amdgpu_irq_dispatch()`, `client_id < AMDGPU_IRQ_CLIENTID_MAX` and `src_id < AMDGPU_MAX_IRQ_SRC_ID` must be checked before indexing `adev->irq.client[client_id]`, `sources[src_id]`, or `adev->irq.virq[src_id]`. [F642]
- Under `mgpu_info.mutex`, `amdgpu_register_gpu_instance()` and `amdgpu_unregister_gpu_instance()` keep `mgpu_info.num_gpu` and `num_apu`/`num_dgpu` in lockstep and compact `mgpu_info.gpu_ins[i]` from `gpu_ins[num_gpu - 1]` on removal; `amdgpu_populate_ip_map()` must fill trailing unused `adev->ip_map.dev_inst[ip_block][l .. HWIP_MAX_INSTANCE - 1]` slots with `-1`. [F629, F649]
- In `amdgpu_mes_init_microcode()` and `isp_load_fw_by_psp()`, LE firmware headers (`mes_hdr->*`, `hdr->ucode_size_bytes`) and microcode dwords (`ucode_ptr[24]`) require `le32_to_cpu()`; in `amdgpu_jpeg_dump_ip_state()`, `adev->jpeg.reg_list[0]` (`UVD_JPEG_POWER_STATUS`) is read into `ip_dump[inst_off]` first and registers `1 .. reg_count - 1` are read only when `(ip_dump[inst_off] & 0x1) != 1`. [F650, F664]

## Synchronization
- In `amdgpu_bo_create()`, when `bp->resv` is non-NULL the caller holds its lock, so completion and `fail_unreserve` paths must only unlock `bo->tbo.base.resv` when `!bp->resv`; callers of `amdgpu_bo_pin()`, `amdgpu_bo_unpin()`, `amdgpu_bo_kmap()`, `amdgpu_bo_get_tiling_flags()`, and `amdgpu_bo_gpu_offset()` must hold `bo->tbo.base.resv` (except `amdgpu_bo_gpu_offset()` when `bo->tbo.pin_count > 0` or `bo->tbo.type == ttm_bo_type_kernel`). [F554, F587]
- `amdgpu_mes_lock()` and `amdgpu_mes_unlock()` must pair `memalloc_noreclaim_save()` and `memalloc_noreclaim_restore(mes->saved_flags)` inside `mes->mutex_hidden`, and code holding `amdgpu_mes_lock()` must not acquire BO reservation locks or allocate memory with reclaim enabled. [F568]
- `amdgpu_irq_get()` and `amdgpu_irq_put()` only call `amdgpu_irq_update()` when `src->enabled_types[type]` transitions between 0 and 1, and `amdgpu_irq_update()` must re-check `amdgpu_irq_enabled()` under `adev->irq.lock` before calling `src->funcs->set()`. [F570]
- In `amdgpu_ih_ring_write()`, IV dwords must be written to `ih->ring` before checking `wptr != READ_ONCE(ih->rptr)`, followed by `wmb()` before `WRITE_ONCE(*ih->wptr_cpu, cpu_to_le32(wptr))`; readers (`amdgpu_ih_process()`, `amdgpu_ih_wait_on_checkpoint_process_ts()`, `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults_remove()`) must execute `rmb()` after `amdgpu_ih_get_wptr(adev, ih)` before reading `ih->ring`. [F576, F577]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb_pasid()` and `amdgpu_gmc_fw_reg_write_reg_wait()`, `kiq->ring_lock` must be held across `amdgpu_ring_alloc()`, packet emission, `amdgpu_fence_emit_polling()`, and `amdgpu_ring_commit()` (or `amdgpu_ring_undo()` on failure), and released before `amdgpu_fence_wait_polling()` and `msleep()`; in `amdgpu_i2c_pre_xfer()`/`amdgpu_i2c_post_xfer()`, `i2c->mutex` must be balanced and `post_xfer` must do dummy `RREG32()` reads on `rec->mask_clk_reg` and `rec->mask_data_reg`. [F586, F700]

## Object Lifetimes
- `amdgpu_job_set_gang_leader()` must not increment `leader->base.s_fence->scheduled` refcount when `job == leader`, and `amdgpu_job_free()` must only call `dma_fence_put(job->gang_submit)` when `job->gang_submit != &job->base.s_fence->scheduled`; in `amdgpu_job_submit()`, `dma_fence_get(&job->base.s_fence->finished)` and `amdgpu_job_free_resources(job)` must occur after `drm_sched_job_arm()` and before `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`. [F559, F584]
- `amdgpu_job_prepare_job()` must not clear `job->vm` to NULL after `amdgpu_vmid_grab()` because `amdgpu_job_run()` and `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` read `job->vm`; in `amdgpu_vmid_grab()`, when `job->vm_needs_flush` is true, `id->last_flush` must be released with `dma_fence_put()` and set to NULL under `id_mgr->lock`. [F560, F567]
- In `amdgpu_hmm_register()`, if `mmu_interval_notifier_insert()` fails, `bo->notifier.mm` must be cleared to NULL so `amdgpu_hmm_unregister()` (`if (!bo->notifier.mm)`) does not pass an uninitialized notifier to `mmu_interval_notifier_remove()`. [F564]
- In `amdgpu_irq_fini_hw()`, if `adev->irq.installed` is true, `free_irq()` and (when `adev->irq.msi_enabled`) `pci_free_irq_vectors()` must run before `amdgpu_ih_ring_fini()`; in `amdgpu_gmc_init_pdb0()` and `amdgpu_job_timedout()`, MMIO access and reset must be bracketed by `drm_dev_enter()` / `drm_dev_exit()`. [F694, F705]

## API Contracts
- When `amdgpu_job_alloc()` or `amdgpu_job_alloc_with_ib()` is called with `entity == NULL`, `drm_sched_job_init()` is skipped so teardown must check `entity` / `job->base.entity` before `drm_sched_job_cleanup(&job->base)`; `amdgpu_job_alloc()` allocates `struct_size(*job, ibs, num_ibs)` with `(*job)->num_ibs = 0` so callers must set `job->num_ibs <= num_ibs`; and `amdgpu_job_submit_direct()` calls `amdgpu_job_free(job)` only when `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` succeeds, so callers must free `job` on error and not on success. [F558, F597, F679]
- `amdgpu_vmid_grab()` (`amdgpu_vmid_grab_idle()`, `amdgpu_vmid_grab_reserved()`) returns `0` both when a VMID is assigned (`job->vmid` set, `*fence` unchanged) and when all usable VMIDs are busy (`job->vmid == 0`, `*fence` set to a referenced `struct dma_fence *` to wait on), returning a negative errno only on failure. [F591]
- In `amdgpu_bo_create()`, callers must set `bp->bo_ptr_size >= sizeof(struct amdgpu_bo)` (or `sizeof(struct amdgpu_bo_vm)` plus `entries[]` bytes in `amdgpu_bo_create_vm()`), 1-based `bp->xcp_id_plus1` (`0` = any) converts to `bo->xcp_id = bp->xcp_id_plus1 - 1` when `adev->gmc.mem_partitions` is present, and `GDS`/`GWS`/`OA` domains shift `bp->size` left by `PAGE_SHIFT` so `amdgpu_job_set_resources()` must shift `amdgpu_bo_gpu_offset()` and `amdgpu_bo_size()` right by `PAGE_SHIFT`. [F605, F637, F682]
- `amdgpu_mem_partition_info.range.lpfn` is inclusive (`start + size - 1`) whereas `struct ttm_place.lpfn` is exclusive, so `amdgpu_bo_placement_from_domain()` must set `places[c].lpfn = adev->gmc.mem_partitions[mem_id].range.lpfn + 1`; in `amdgpu_bo_get_metadata()`, non-NULL `buffer` with `buffer_size < ubo->metadata_size` must fail with `-EINVAL`. [F606, F638]
- `amdgpu_bo_fault_reserve_notify()` returns a `vm_fault_t` bitmask (`0`, `VM_FAULT_NOPAGE` on `-EBUSY`/`-ERESTARTSYS`, or `VM_FAULT_SIGBUS` when pinned, on other `ttm_bo_validate()` errors, or if VRAM stays non-CPU-visible) and must never return a raw negative errno. [F607]
- `amdgpu_bo_fb_aper_addr()` and `amdgpu_bo_gpu_offset_no_check()` must pass the linear offset through `amdgpu_gmc_sign_extend()`, whereas callers passing `amdgpu_csa_vaddr(adev)` to `amdgpu_map_static_csa()`/`amdgpu_unmap_static_csa()` must mask with `& AMDGPU_GMC_HOLE_MASK`; `amdgpu_bo_init()`/`amdgpu_bo_fini()` must only reserve WC memtype/MTRR when `!adev->gmc.xgmi.connected_to_cpu && !adev->gmc.is_app_apu`, and `amdgpu_bo_support_uswc()` rejects USWC on `CONFIG_X86_32`, `CONFIG_X86 && !CONFIG_X86_PAT`, or `!drm_arch_can_wc_memory()`. [F670, F678, F717]
- `isp_user_buffer_alloc()` and `isp_kernel_buffer_alloc()` must check `is_valid_isp_device(ispdev->dev.parent, adev->dev)` before creating a GTT BO; callers of `amdgpu_bo_create_reserved()`, `amdgpu_bo_create_kernel()`, or `isp_kernel_buffer_alloc()` must initialize `*bo_ptr` to `NULL` for new BOs, and `isp_user_buffer_free()` takes `void *` whereas `isp_kernel_buffer_free()` takes `void **`. [F685, F704]
- Callers of `amdgpu_mes_detect_and_reset_hung_queues()` must pass `hung_db_array` with at least `adev->mes.hung_queue_db_array_size` `u32` elements, and callers of `amdgpu_mes_set_shader_debugger()` must pass a `tcp_watch_cntl` array of at least 4 `uint32_t` elements. [F689]

## Error Handling
- In `amdgpu_sched_ops`, `amdgpu_job_prepare_job()` must never return an `ERR_PTR` (on error it calls `dma_fence_set_error()` on `job->base.s_fence->finished` and returns `NULL`), whereas `amdgpu_job_run()` skips `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` when `finished->error < 0` and returns `ERR_PTR(r)` if `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` fails. [F592]
- `amdgpu_bo_create_reserved()` unreferences `*bo_ptr` and returns `0` when `size == 0`, sets `free = true` only when allocating a new BO (`*bo_ptr == NULL`), and on `error_free` calls `amdgpu_bo_unref(bo_ptr)` only when `free` is true; in `amdgpu_irq_dispatch()`, `entry.timestamp` and `entry.timestamp_src` must be zero-initialized before `amdgpu_ih_decode_iv()` for legacy SOCs (`cik`, `cz`, `iceland`, `si`, `tonga`). [F599, F603]

## External Contracts
- In `amdgpu_info_ioctl()`, every `copy_to_user()` must clamp to `min(size, sizeof(out_struct))`; `AMDGPU_INFO_DEV_INFO` must bound the `cu_bitmap` `memcpy()` by `sizeof(dev_info->cu_bitmap)` (`uint32_t [4][4]`) rather than `sizeof(adev->gfx.cu_info.bitmap)`; and `AMDGPU_INFO_VBIOS_IMAGE` must reject `bios_offset >= bios_size` before computing `adev->bios + bios_offset` and `bios_size - bios_offset`. [F630, F632, F690]
- In `amdgpu_hw_ip_info()`, `info->query_hw_ip.ip_instance >= AMDGPU_HW_IP_INSTANCE_MAX_COUNT` must return `-EINVAL`, `info->query_hw_ip.type` must be validated by the `switch` before indexing `amdgpu_ctx_num_entities[type]`, and each `AMDGPU_HW_IP_*` case must index its own IP block's ring array; in `amdgpu_firmware_info()`, `info->query_fw.ip_instance` must be `0` and `query_fw->index` bounds-checked against the IP's instance count before indexing. [F633, F634, F655]
- In `amdgpu_jpeg_dec_parse_cs()`, every 2-dword packet must have `CP_PACKETJ_GET_RES(ib->ptr[i]) == 0` and `PACKETJ_TYPE0`/`PACKETJ_TYPE3` registers must pass `amdgpu_jpeg_reg_valid(reg)` (`[JPEG_REG_RANGE_START, JPEG_REG_RANGE_END]` excluding `[JPEG_ATOMIC_RANGE_START, JPEG_ATOMIC_RANGE_END]`); `amdgpu_get_vblank_counter_kms()` must loop reading `amdgpu_display_vblank_get_counter()` and `amdgpu_display_get_crtc_scanoutpos()` until the counter is unchanged and increment `count` when `vpos >= 0`. [F647, F734]

## Known Pitfalls
- When `amdgpu_bo_create_kernel_at()` replaces `(*bo_ptr)->tbo.resource` via `ttm_resource_free()` and `ttm_bo_mem_space()`, it must call `amdgpu_bo_kunmap(*bo_ptr)` before freeing the old resource and `amdgpu_bo_kmap(*bo_ptr, cpu_addr)` afterward. [F612]
- 48-bit IH timestamps (`entry.timestamp`, `ih->processed_timestamp`, `fault->timestamp_expiry`) must be ordered via `amdgpu_ih_ts_after(t1, t2)` or `amdgpu_ih_ts_after_or_equal(t1, t2)` (which shift left by 16 bits before signed subtraction; `amdgpu_ih_ts_after(t1, t2)` is true when `t2` is after `t1`), and in `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults()`, `stamp` must be `max(timestamp, AMDGPU_GMC_FAULT_TIMEOUT + 1) - AMDGPU_GMC_FAULT_TIMEOUT` to avoid unsigned underflow. [F645, F665, F666]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_vram_location()` and `amdgpu_gmc_init_sw_mem_ranges()`, 32-bit MB limits (`amdgpu_vis_vram_limit`, `amdgpu_vram_limit`) and 32-bit PFNs (`size`, `mem_ranges[l].range.fpfn`) must be cast to `u64` before shifting left; in `amdgpu_gmc_need_nps_switch_req()`, hardware `supp_modes` is 0-based (`BIT(mode - 1)` when `mode != UNKNOWN_MEMORY_PARTITION_MODE`) whereas `adev->gmc.supported_nps_modes` and `AMDGPU_ALL_NPS_MASK` are 1-based (`BIT(mode)`). [F669, F672]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_fw_reg_write_reg_wait()`, if `amdgpu_fence_wait_polling()` fails (`r < 1`) and `in_interrupt()` is true, jump to `failed_kiq` without `msleep()`; in `amdgpu_hdp_generic_flush()`, direct MMIO `WREG32()` to `(adev->rmmio_remap.reg_offset + KFD_MMIO_REMAP_HDP_MEM_FLUSH_CNTL) >> 2` must be followed by `adev->nbio.funcs->get_memsize(adev)` when non-NULL. [F699, F707]
- In `amdgpu_jpeg_psp_update_sram()`, `dpg_sram_curr_addr` and `dpg_sram_cpu_addr` (`uint32_t *`) must be cast to `uintptr_t` to compute `ucode_size` in bytes and reset before each sequence; in `amdgpu_jpeg_dec_ring_test_ring()` and `amdgpu_jpeg_dec_ring_test_ib()`, MMIO on `external.jpeg_pitch` must be skipped when `amdgpu_sriov_vf(adev)` is true, and `test_ring` must follow the initial `WREG32()` with `RREG32()`. [F688, F698]
