# NFS, SUNRPC, AND LOCKD CLIENTS — fs/nfs (part 6)

Implements NFS buffered read/write/commit (`read.c`, `write.c`), pNFS client core, deviceid cache, and DS helpers (`pnfs*.c`), superblock/mount operations (`super.c`), async silly-delete/rename (`unlink.c`), NFSv2 procedures (`proc.c`), symlink reading (`symlink.c`), and sysfs/sysctl (`sysfs.c`, `sysctl.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()`, after locking `head`, `nfs_page_group_lock(head)`, and verifying `head == folio->private`, call `nfs_cancel_remove_inode(head, inode)` to clear `PG_REMOVE` and restore `PG_INODE_REF`. In `nfs_destroy_unlinked_subrequests()`, advance `destroy_list` (stopping at `subreq->wb_this_page == old_head`) before resetting `subreq->wb_this_page = subreq` and `wb_head = subreq` under `nfs_page_set_headlock(subreq)`. [F360, F458]
- `lo->plh_stateid.seqid` is `__be32`, whereas `plh_barrier`, `plh_return_seq`, and `pls_seq` are host `u32` compared via `pnfs_seqid_is_newer()`, requiring `be32_to_cpu()`/`cpu_to_be32()` at every boundary. In `pnfs_set_layout_stateid()`, if `!update_barrier && atomic_read(&lo->plh_outstanding) == 1`, call `pnfs_barrier_update(lo, be32_to_cpu(lo->plh_stateid.seqid))`. In `pnfs_layoutreturn_free_lsegs()`, call `pnfs_reset_return_info(lo)` after `pnfs_set_layout_stateid()` for remaining `plh_return_segs`. [F414, F442, F471]
- In `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()`, removing the last segment from `lo->plh_segs` sets `NFS_LAYOUT_INVALID_STID` when `plh_outstanding == 0` unless `NFS_LSEG_LAYOUTRETURN` is on `pls_flags` or `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN_REQUESTED`/`NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN` is on `plh_flags`. Broken by: calling `mark_lseg_invalid()` in `pnfs_roc()` before setting `NFS_LSEG_LAYOUTRETURN` or `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN_REQUESTED`. [F417]
- `pnfs_layout_set_fail_bit()` increments `lo->plh_refcount` when setting `NFS_LAYOUT_RO_FAILED`/`RW_FAILED`, so clearing them or `__pnfs_destroy_layout()` must use `pnfs_layout_clear_fail_bit()`. `pnfs_detach_layout_hdr()` must reset `nfsi->write_io = 0` and `read_io = 0` when setting `nfsi->layout = NULL`. [F415, F416]
- `set_pnfs_layoutdriver()` increments `cl_mds_count` only after `set_layoutdriver` succeeds; `unset_pnfs_layoutdriver()` decrements `cl_mds_count` and calls `nfs4_deviceid_purge_client()` on zero before clearing `pnfs_curr_ld`. Layout driver `id == 0` is rejected (`-EINVAL`) and `ld_prefs[]` must end with `0`. [F421, F443]
- `lgp->args.layout.pglen` (`lgp->res.layoutp->pglen`) must stay `max_pages * PAGE_SIZE` because `pnfs_layoutget_free()` frees `pglen / PAGE_SIZE` pages via `nfs4_free_pages()`. `plh_lwb` and `nfs_commit_data::lwb` store 1-based exclusive end offsets, which `pnfs_layoutcommit_inode()` converts to `end_pos - 1` (or `U64_MAX` if `0`) in `lastbytewritten`. [F435, F475]
- In `pnfs_generic_commit_pagelist()`, `ds_commit_index` is `-1` for MDS commits and `0 .. nbuckets - 1` for DS commits (`< 0` selects `nfs_initiate_commit()` vs `initiate_commit()`). In `nfs_pageio_reset_read_mds()` and `nfs_pageio_reset_write_mds()`, call `pgio->pg_ops->pg_cleanup(pgio)` before setting `pg_ops = &nfs_pgio_rw_ops`, call `nfs_pageio_stop_mirroring(pgio)` on writes, and reset `pg_mirrors[0].pg_bsize` to `rsize`/`wsize`. [F425, F440]
- When `nfs_can_extend_write()` is true in `nfs_update_folio()`, round `offset` and `end` only to `PAGE_SIZE` boundaries (capped at `pagelen`), not `folio_size(folio)`. In `nfs_pseudoflavour_to_name()`, keep `NFS_AUTH_INFO_MAX_FLAVORS` and `{ UINT_MAX, "unknown" }` in `sec_flavours` in sync. [F413, F503]

## Synchronization

- `nfs_inode_add_request()` must call `nfs_lock_request(req)` before holding `mapping->i_private_lock` to set `PG_MAPPED`, `folio_set_private(folio)`, and `folio->private = req`. Detaching `folio->private` in `nfs_inode_remove_request()` and checking `head == folio->private` in `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()` both require `nfs_page_group_lock`. [F378, F393]
- `nfs_page_group_lock_subreq()` requires `head` locked and `nfs_page_group_lock(head)` held; it drops `nfs_page_group_unlock(head)` before `nfs_wait_on_request(subreq)` and re-acquires it after. On negative error, it calls `nfs_unroll_locks(head, subreq)`, `nfs_release_request(subreq)`, and returns with `head`'s page group lock dropped. [F361, F486]
- `nfs_request_add_commit_list_locked()`, `nfs_request_remove_commit_list()`, and `pnfs_generic_clear_request_commit()` require `commit_mutex` and `PG_BUSY` on `req`. Under those locks, `PG_CLEAN`, commit-list membership, and `cinfo->mds->ncommit` must stay in lockstep (`nfs_clear_request_commit()` also calls `nfs_folio_clear_commit()`), and `cinfo->ds->nwritten`/`ncommitting` must match `PG_COMMIT_TO_DS` and bucket list moves in `pnfs_layout_mark_request_commit()`, `pnfs_generic_clear_request_commit()`, `pnfs_bucket_scan_ds_commit_list()`, and `pnfs_bucket_get_committing()`. [F383, F424, F430]
- `pnfs_add_commit_array()`, `pnfs_generic_ds_cinfo_release_lseg()`, and `pnfs_generic_ds_cinfo_destroy()` require `inode->i_lock`, and `pnfs_free_commit_array()` uses `kfree_rcu()`. In `pnfs_generic_scan_commit_lists()`, `pnfs_generic_recover_commit_reqs()`, and `pnfs_alloc_ds_commits_list()`, `list_for_each_entry_rcu` over `fl_cinfo->commits` must pin `array` via `pnfs_get_commit_array()`, drop `rcu_read_unlock()` for bucket work, and re-take `rcu_read_lock()` before `pnfs_put_commit_array()`. `pnfs_free_lseg()` and `pnfs_free_lseg_list()` must only be called after dropping `inode->i_lock` because `ld->free_lseg` acquires `inode->i_lock`. [F489, F492, F493]
- In `nfs_layoutget_end()`, `pnfs_clear_layoutreturn_waitbit()`, `pnfs_clear_first_layoutget()`, and `pnfs_clear_layoutcommitting()`, clearing a wait bit on `lo->plh_flags` or `NFS_I(inode)->flags` requires `smp_mb__after_atomic()` before `wake_up_bit()`. `pnfs_wait_on_layoutreturn()` and `pnfs_clear_layoutreturn_waitbit()` must hold `inode->i_lock`. [F379, F491]
- Link `lo` onto `server->layouts` under `clp->cl_lock` via `_add_to_server_list()` before sending `LAYOUTGET`. `pnfs_prepare_layoutreturn()` only defers for `atomic_read(&lo->plh_outstanding) != 0` when `lo->plh_return_seq == 0`. `pnfs_layout_bulk_destroy_byserver_locked()` requires `clp->cl_lock` and `rcu_read_lock()`, dropping both before `iput()` or `nfs_sb_deactive()`, then reacquiring them and returning `-EAGAIN`. `pnfs_destroy_layout_final()` waits under `inode->i_lock` via `wait_var_event_spinlock()` until `nfsi->layout != lo` (woken by `wake_up_var_locked()` in `pnfs_put_layout_hdr()` when `I_FREEING | I_CLEAR` is set). [F386, F387, F388, F450]
- In `_nfs4_pnfs_v3_ds_connect()` and `_nfs4_pnfs_v4_ds_connect()`, initialize `clp` (including `nfs4_init_ds_session()`) and issue `smp_wmb()` before `WRITE_ONCE(ds->ds_clp, clp)` and `nfs4_clear_ds_conn_bit(ds)`. [F390]
- `__nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` uses `atomic_inc_not_zero(&d->ref)` under `rcu_read_lock()`. `nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` rechecks `__nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` under `nfs4_deviceid_lock` before incrementing `new->ref` a second time and calling `hlist_add_head_rcu()` (or `free_deviceid_node(new)` if lost, never `nfs4_put_deviceid_node(new)`). `nfs4_delete_deviceid()` and `_deviceid_purge_client()` unhash under `nfs4_deviceid_lock` and drop it before `nfs4_put_deviceid_node()` (which unhashes `NFS_DEVICEID_NOCACHE` nodes when `ref` hits 2). [F367, F391, F494]
- In `shutdown_store()` / `shutdown_nfs_client()`, only call `shutdown_client(clp->cl_rpcclient)` or `nfs_mark_client_ready(clp, -EIO)` when all servers in `clp->cl_superblocks` (under `rcu_read_lock()`) have `NFS_MOUNT_SHUTDOWN` set. `nfs_netns_client::identifier` is read under `rcu_read_lock()` in `nfs_netns_identifier_show()` and replaced via `xchg()` + `synchronize_rcu()` before `kfree(old)` in `nfs_netns_identifier_store()`. In `nfs_ctx_key_to_expire()`, drop `rcu_read_unlock()` before `lookup_cred()`, and update `ctx->ll_cred` via `xchg()` before `put_rpccred(old)`. [F380, F496, F497]
- In `nfs_get_link()`, when `dentry == NULL` (RCU walk), never sleep: call `nfs_revalidate_mapping_rcu(inode)` and `filemap_get_folio()`, returning `ERR_PTR(-ECHILD)` if absent/not uptodate. In `nfs_show_stats()`, do not sleep inside the `preempt_disable()` loop over `nfss->io_stats`. [F480, F484]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `nfs_return_empty_folio()`, `nfs_readpage_release()`, and `nfs_read_add_folio()`, call `folio_unlock(folio)` only if `nfs_netfs_folio_unlock(folio)` returns non-zero. `nfs_read_add_folio()` always consumes the folio lock, so callers (`nfs_do_read_folio()`, `nfs_readahead()`) must never unlock `folio` afterward. [F358, F403]
- `nfs_init_commit()` steals `@lseg` into `data->lseg`, takes `get_nfs_open_context(ctx)`, and calls `nfs_commit_begin(cinfo->mds)`. `pnfs_bucket_get_committing()` returns a referenced `lseg` (via `pnfs_free_bucket_lseg(bucket)` when `committing` and `written` are both empty, or `pnfs_get_lseg(bucket->lseg)`), which callers must pass to `nfs_init_commit()` or `pnfs_put_lseg()`. [F363, F364]
- Inserting an `lseg` into `lo->plh_segs` takes `pnfs_get_layout_hdr(lo)` (dropped in `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()`), so `pnfs_put_lseg()` holds an extra `pnfs_get_layout_hdr(lo)` across `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()` and `spin_unlock(&inode->i_lock)`, and skips `pnfs_free_lseg(lseg)` if `pnfs_cache_lseg_for_layoutreturn()` is true. In `pnfs_read_resend_pnfs()`, when `NFS_IOHDR_REDO` is set, call `pnfs_put_lseg(hdr->lseg)` and set `hdr->lseg = NULL` before `nfs_pageio_resend()`. [F368, F369]
- `nfs_sb_active()` multiplexes one `sb->s_active` ref across `server->active` until `nfs_sb_deactive()` sees `atomic_dec_and_test(&server->active)`; never call `deactivate_super(sb)` directly after `nfs_sb_active(sb)` or call `nfs_sb_deactive(sb)` if `nfs_sb_active(sb)` returned `false`. [F372]
- `nfs_sillyrename()` attaches `nfs_unlinkdata` via `nfs_async_unlink()` before `nfs_async_rename()` (cancelling with `nfs_cancel_async_unlink()` on error). Setting `dentry->d_fsdata` and `DCACHE_NFSFS_RENAMED` under `d_lock` must `kfree()` any displaced `d_fsdata` outside `d_lock`, and `nfs_complete_unlink()` / `nfs_cancel_async_unlink()` clear both under `d_lock`. In `nfs_call_unlink()`, once `d_in_lookup(alias)` is true, `rmdir_sem`, `alias`, `data`, and `nfs_sb_active()` must be released via `nfs_async_unlink_release()`. [F374, F375, F483]
- `nfs_netns_client_release()` only frees `clp->identifier` while `nfs_netns_object_release()` frees `struct nfs_netns_client`, so `nfs_netns_sysfs_destroy()` puts `clp->kobject` before `clp->nfs_net_kobj`. `nfs_sysfs_sb_release()` is a no-op and `nfs_sysfs_remove_server()` only calls `kobject_del(&server->kobj)` because `nfs_free_server()` calls `kobject_put(&server->kobj)` and frees `server` via RCU. [F376, F377]

## API Contracts

- `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()` and `nfs_try_to_update_request()` return a locked `struct nfs_page *`, `NULL`, or `ERR_PTR(-errno)`. `nfs_wb_folio_cancel()` calls `folio_cancel_dirty(folio)` after detaching a non-NULL head request. `nfs_migrate_folio()` returns `-EBUSY` if `folio_test_private(src)` or `folio_test_private_2(src)` remains set after flushing (`MIGRATE_SYNC` only) or waiting (`!MIGRATE_ASYNC`). [F402, F504, F506]
- `nfs_commitdata_alloc()` and `nfs_writehdr_alloc()` use `nfs_io_gfp_mask()`, fall back to `mempool_alloc(..., GFP_NOWAIT)` + `memset()`, and free via `mempool_free()`. `pnfs_add_commit_array()` does not consume `@new` if an existing array is returned (`if (array != new) pnfs_free_commit_array(new)`). `pnfs_commit_list()` returns `PNFS_NOT_ATTEMPTED` (`1`), `PNFS_ATTEMPTED` (`0`), or `< 0`, and `__nfs_commit_inode()` clamps non-negative returns from `nfs_generic_commit_list()` to `0`. [F365, F404, F481]
- `pnfs_register_layoutdriver()` requires non-zero `id` and non-NULL `alloc_lseg`/`free_lseg`. `pnfs_alloc_init_layoutget_args()` sets `lgp->res.status = -NFS4ERR_DELAY`. `pnfs_layout_process()` validates `res->range` with `pnfs_sanity_check_layout_range()` before `alloc_lseg()`, checks `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(lseg)`, and maps `NULL` to `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)`. [F397, F398, F433, F498]
- `nfs_request_mount()` passes `ctx->timeo`/`retrans` to `nfs_mount()` only when `(request.protocol == XPRT_TRANSPORT_UDP) == !(ctx->flags & NFS_MOUNT_TCP)`. Mount/DS `timeo` is in deciseconds (`HZ / 10`) and `ctx->ac*` in seconds (`HZ`), whereas `to_initval`, `connect_timeout`, and `nfss->ac*` are in `jiffies`. [F406, F407]
- For NFSv2: `nfs_proc_write_setup()` forces `NFS_FILE_SYNC` and `commit_setup`/`commit_rpc_prepare` call `BUG()`; creation procedures ensure `fh->size == 0` and `nfs_fattr_init(fattr)` before `nfs_instantiate()`/`nfs_add_or_obtain()` when omitted by the RPC; `nfs_proc_symlink()` rejects `len > NFS2_MAXPATHLEN` (`-ENAMETOOLONG`); and `nfs_lock_check_bounds()` rejects `file_lock` bounds that truncate in `__s32` or violate `0 <= start <= end`. [F409, F447, F499]

## Error Handling

- In `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()`, if `nfs_wait_on_request(head)` fails before `nfs_lock_request(head)`, call `nfs_release_request(head)`; after locking `head`, use `nfs_unlock_and_release_request(head)`. In `nfs_get_tree_common()`, `ctx->server` is taken at entry: call `nfs_free_server(server)` if `sget_fc()` fails or reuses a superblock (`s->s_fs_info != server`), but call `deactivate_locked_super(s)` on errors after `s->s_fs_info == server`. [F359, F373]
- When `pnfs_update_layout()` returns an `ERR_PTR` into `pgio->pg_lseg`, `pg_init` callbacks must set `pgio->pg_error = PTR_ERR(pgio->pg_lseg)` and `pgio->pg_lseg = NULL`. In `pnfs_layoutcommit_inode()`, `-ENOSPC` from `ld->prepare_layoutcommit()` must re-set `NFS_INO_LAYOUTCOMMIT` and `mark_as_dirty = true` and still send `nfs4_proc_layoutcommit(data, sync)`. [F395, F399]
- In `nfs_readpage_retry()` and `nfs_writeback_result()`, `resp->count == 0` on a short I/O is `-EIO`; otherwise `nfs_readpage_retry()` advances `hdr->mds_offset`, `argp->offset`, and `argp->pgbase` by `resp->count`, decrements `argp->count`, and zeroes `resp->count`/`resp->eof` before `rpc_restart_call_prepare(task)`. `nfs_writepages()` breaks on `err == -EAGAIN && (mntflags & NFS_MOUNT_SOFTERR)`. Write/commit RPC failures must call `nfs_mapping_set_error()`. [F426, F460, F461, F511]

## External Contracts

- `nfs_netns_identifier_store()` clamps length to `CONTAINER_ID_MAXLEN` (64), strips `'\n'` via `nfs_string_strip()`, ignores empty strings, and uses `kmemdup_nul()`. `param_set_portnr()` rejects `NULL` and values `> NFS_CALLBACK_MAXPORTNR` (`65535U`). `nfs_statfs()` sets `f_frsize = f_bsize = s_blocksize` and reports blocks in `s_blocksize_bits`. [F445, F446, F449]
- `struct nfs4_pnfs_ds` and `nfs_compare_super_address()` (`net_eq()`) are netns-scoped; `nfs_compare_super()` also checks `nfs_compare_userns()` and `security_sb_mnt_opts_compat()`; and `pnfs_roc()` requires `cred_fscmp(cred, lc_cred) == 0`. Child kobjects under `nfs_kset_type`/`nfs_sb_ktype` require `.namespace` and `sysfs_create_file_ns(..., nfs_netns_server_namespace(&server->kobj))`. Async RPCs in `nfs_do_call_unlink()`, `nfs_async_rename()`, and `nfs_commit_list()` pass `msg.rpc_cred = data->cred` with `RPC_TASK_CRED_NOREF` and hold the cred until `rpc_release`. [F501, F502, F509, F510]

## Known Pitfalls

- When `!task->tk_ops` in `nfs_readpage_retry()` and `nfs_writeback_result()`, set `hdr->pnfs_error = -EAGAIN` instead of `rpc_restart_call_prepare(task)`, and keep two sequential `if (!hdr->pnfs_error)` and `if (hdr->pnfs_error)` checks in `pnfs_ld_read_done()` / `pnfs_ld_write_done()`. [F401]
- Guard against overflow/underflow: check `req_start >= seg_end` before `seg_end - req_start` in `pnfs_generic_pg_test()`; check `bytes > hdr->good_bytes` before `hdr->good_bytes - bytes` in `nfs_read_completion()` (and keep tail zeroing in `nfs_read_add_folio()`); check `i_size > 0` before `i_size - 1` in `nfs_grow_file()`; check `arg.length != NFS4_MAX_UINT64` before `PAGE_ALIGN(arg.length)` in `pnfs_update_layout()`; use `nfs_size_to_loff_t(fattr->size)` in `nfs_writeback_check_extend()`; widen `nfs2_fsstat` block counts to `u64` in `nfs_proc_statfs()`; and bound `strncat()` to `sizeof(name) - strlen(name) - 1` in `nfs_sysfs_link_rpc_client()`. [F436, F437, F448, F464, F465, F467, F478]
