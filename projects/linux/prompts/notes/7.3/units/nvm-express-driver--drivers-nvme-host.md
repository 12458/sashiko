# NVM EXPRESS DRIVER — drivers/nvme/host

Implements NVMe host transport drivers (PCIe, TCP/TLS, RDMA), sysfs attributes, Persistent Reservations (`pr_ops`), Zoned Namespaces (ZNS), tracepoints, and public headers (`blk_mq_ops` and `nvme_ctrl_ops`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Async Event Requests (`nvme_is_aen_req` on QID 0 or `nvme_tcp_async_req` matching `&ctrl->async_req`) lack a `struct request`; check them before `nvme_find_rq` or `blk_mq_rq_from_pdu`. Reused blk-mq PDUs require `nvme_prep_rq` to zero `iod->flags`, `nr_descriptors`, `total_len`, and `meta_total_len`, and `nvme_tcp_setup_cmd_pdu` to reset `req->status = cpu_to_le16(NVME_SC_SUCCESS)` and send counters. [F240, F243]
- `queues[0]` is admin and `queues[1..n]` are I/O queues, whereas I/O `blk_mq_tag_set` indices (`hctx_idx`, `tag_set.tags[]`, `mq_map[]`) are 0-based (offset by 1). `nvme_tcp_set_queue_io_cpu()` picks the online CPU with `mq_map[cpu] == qid - 1` and lowest `nvme_tcp_cpu_queues[cpu]`, incrementing it and setting `NVME_TCP_Q_IO_CPU_SET`; `nvme_tcp_stop_queue_nowait()` decrements `nvme_tcp_cpu_queues[queue->io_cpu]` on `test_and_clear_bit(NVME_TCP_Q_IO_CPU_SET, &queue->flags)`. [F247, F248, F274]
- `nvme_tcp_init_recv_ctx()` sets `pdu_remaining = sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_rsp_pdu) + nvme_tcp_hdgst_len(queue)`, `pdu_offset = 0`, `data_remaining = -1`, and `ddgst_remaining = 0` on queue start or after RSP, R2T, C2H data, or DDGST phases; in `nvme_tcp_recv_pdu()`, call it before `nvme_tcp_rsp`/`nvme_tcp_r2t`, but not in `nvme_tcp_c2h_data` before `nvme_tcp_recv_data()`/`nvme_tcp_recv_ddgst()`. `nvme_tcp_recv_state()` must test `pdu_remaining` before `ddgst_remaining` and fall back to `NVME_TCP_RECV_DATA` last. [F258, F292, F296]
- In `nvme_tcp_recv_pdu()`, verify `hdr->hlen == sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_rsp_pdu)` before `nvme_tcp_verify_hdgst()` or `nvme_tcp_check_ddgst()`. `req->pdu` is `sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_cmd_pdu) + hdgst` bytes, and `nvme_tcp_req_data_pdu()` must place `nvme_tcp_data_pdu` at `req->pdu + sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_cmd_pdu) - sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_data_pdu)`. `nvme_tcp_fetch_request()` must call `list_del_init(&req->entry)` and `init_llist_node(&req->lentry)`, and `nvme_tcp_process_req_list()` must prepend via `list_add()` only when `send_list` is empty. [F266, F281, F299]
- When `nvme_pci_setup_data_prp()` fills a PRP page (`i == NVME_CTRL_PAGE_SIZE >> 3`) with payload left, move `old_prp_list[i - 1]` to `prp_list[0]` of the new `descriptor_pools.large` page, store `cpu_to_le64(prp_list_dma)` in `old_prp_list[i - 1]`, and reset `i = 1`. In `nvme_pci_setup_meta_sgls()`, when `entries > 1`, `sg_list[0]` holds the segment descriptor and data descriptors are at `sg_list[1..i]`, capping `max_integrity_segments` at `NVME_MAX_META_SEGS` and freeing from `&sge[1]` in `nvme_unmap_metadata()`. [F279, F300]
- `nvme_pci_alloc_dev()` must use `struct_size(dev, descriptor_pools, nr_node_ids)`; `nvme_dbbuf_dma_alloc()` must zero `dev->dbbuf_dbs` and `dev->dbbuf_eis` (`nvme_dbbuf_size(dev)` bytes) when reusing across resets; `nvme_setup_io_queues()` must sample `write_queues`/`poll_queues` into `dev->nr_write_queues`/`nr_poll_queues` once for `nvme_setup_irqs()`/`nvme_calc_irq_sets()`. Check `dma_merge_boundary != 0` before `!(PAGE_SIZE & dma_merge_boundary)` in `nvme_alloc_host_mem()`, `offset > bar_size` before `bar_size - offset` in `nvme_map_cmb()`, and `is_power_of_2(zi->zone_size)` in `nvme_query_zone_info()`. [F251, F255, F285, F286, F290, F315]
- `nvme_rdma_alloc_tag_set()`, `nvme_rdma_init_request()`, and `nvme_rdma_dma_map_req()` must keep trailing PDU offsets in lockstep (`data_sgl` SGs at `req + 1`, `metadata_sgl` after `NVME_RDMA_DATA_SGL_SIZE`, metadata SGs at `req->metadata_sgl + 1`). Verify `wc->byte_len >= sizeof(struct nvme_completion)` in `nvme_rdma_recv_done()` and `rej_data_len >= sizeof(u16)` in `nvme_rdma_conn_rejected()`. [F262, F273]

## Synchronization

- Non-IRQ callers of `nvme_poll_cq()` (`nvme_poll()`, `nvme_poll_irqdisable()`, `nvme_reap_pending_cqes()`) must hold `nvmeq->cq_poll_lock`, and `nvme_poll_irqdisable()` must bracket it with `disable_irq()`/`enable_irq()` on `nvmeq->cq_vector`. `nvme_reap_pending_cqes()` must run only after disabling the PCI device and freeing queue IRQs, and before `nvme_cancel_tagset()`. [F211, F330]
- In `nvme_poll_cq()`, after `nvme_cqe_pending()` sees a matching phase bit via `READ_ONCE(hcqe->status)`, issue `dma_rmb()` before reading CQE fields in `nvme_handle_cqe()`. In `nvme_dbbuf_update_and_check_event()`, issue `wmb()` before writing `*dbbuf_db`, and a full `mb()` between writing `*dbbuf_db` and reading `*dbbuf_ei`. [F212, F213]
- Serialize writes to `nvmeq->sq_cmds`, updates to `sq_tail`/`last_sq_tail`, and `nvme_sq_copy_cmd()`/`nvme_write_sq_db()` under `nvmeq->sq_lock`. In `nvme_create_queue()` and `nvme_pci_configure_admin_queue()`, `nvme_init_queue()` must finish before `queue_request_irq()`, and `NVMEQ_ENABLED` is set only after `queue_request_irq()` succeeds. [F214, F327]
- In `nvme_setup_io_queues()` and `nvme_create_queue()`, run synchronous admin commands (`nvme_set_queue_count()`, `adapter_alloc_cq/sq()`, `nvme_delete_io_queues()`) without `dev->shutdown_lock`, re-acquiring via `nvme_setup_io_queues_trylock()`. In `nvme_pci_update_nr_queues()`, take `mutex_trylock(&dev->shutdown_lock)` and verify `dev->online_queues > 0` before `blk_mq_update_nr_hw_queues()` and `nvme_free_queues()`. [F217, F218]
- On `nvme_reset_work` failure or `pci_dev_is_disconnected(pdev)` in `nvme_timeout`, transition `dev->ctrl` to `NVME_CTRL_DELETING` before `nvme_dev_disable` to unblock `nvme_wait_reset`/`nvme_error_resume`. In `nvme_dev_disable`, `nvme_tcp_teardown_admin/io_queues`, and `nvme_rdma_teardown_admin/io_queues`, call `nvme_unquiesce_io/admin_queue` after canceling requests and before removing tagsets. [F358, F380]
- In `tcp.c`, `nvme_tcp_queue_request()` and `nvme_tcp_handle_r2t()` use `llist_add()` on `queue->req_list`, while draining `req_list` to `send_list`, modifying `queue->request`, and calling `nvme_tcp_try_send()` require `queue->send_mutex`. In `nvme_tcp_try_send_data()`, snapshot `req->data_len`, `h2cdata_left`, and `data_sent` before `sock_sendmsg()`, calling `nvme_tcp_advance_req(req, ret)` only when `req_data_sent + ret < req_data_len`. [F221, F305]
- Modify `sk->sk_user_data` and `sk_data_ready`/`sk_write_space`/`sk_state_change` under `write_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock)` and read under `read_lock_bh`; in `__nvme_tcp_stop_queue()`, call `kernel_sock_shutdown(queue->sock, SHUT_RDWR)` and `nvme_tcp_restore_sock_ops(queue)` before `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)`. `nvme_rdma_stop_queue()` must hold `queue->queue_lock` across `test_and_clear_bit(NVME_RDMA_Q_LIVE, &queue->flags)` and `__nvme_rdma_stop_queue()`; `nvme_rdma_find_get_device()` must hold `device_list_mutex` and use `nvme_rdma_dev_get()`; and callers of `nvme_find_path(head)` must hold `srcu_read_lock(&head->srcu)` across path lookup and sync submission. [F226, F227, F228, F333, F355]

## Object Lifetimes

- `nvme_rdma/tcp_create_ctrl` only add `ctrl->list` after `setup_ctrl` succeeds, and `nvme_rdma/tcp_free_ctrl` must call `nvmf_free_options(nctrl->opts)` only when `!list_empty(&ctrl->list)`. `nvme_tcp_free_admin_queue` must cancel `async_event_work`, call `nvme_tcp_free_async_req`, and set `async_req.pdu = NULL` before `nvme_tcp_free_queue(ctrl, 0)`; `nvme_rdma_destroy_admin_queue` must clear `ctrl->async_event_sqe.data = NULL` after `nvme_rdma_free_qe`. [F195, F202]
- `nvme_tcp_stop_queue()` and `nvme_tcp_stop_io_queues()` must follow `nvme_tcp_stop_queue_nowait()` (which clears `queue->tls_enabled = false`) with `nvme_tcp_wait_queue()` until `sk_wmem_alloc_get()` drains. In `nvme_tcp_error_recovery_work` and `nvme_reset_ctrl_work`, call `nvme_tcp_key_revoke_needed(ctrl)` and `nvme_auth_revoke_tls_key(ctrl)` before `nvme_tcp_teardown_admin_queue` or `nvme_tcp_teardown_ctrl` (which zeroes `ctrl->tls_pskid`). [F206, F223, F260]
- In `nvme_tcp_alloc_queue`, `sock_alloc_file` consumes `queue->sock` on error (jump to `err_destroy_mutex` without releasing it); once it succeeds, release via `fput(queue->sock->file)` and set `queue->sock = NULL`. In `nvme_rdma_process_nvme_rsp`, when posting `nvme_rdma_inv_rkey` (no `IB_WC_WITH_INVALIDATE` on `req->mr`), return without calling `nvme_rdma_end_request(req)` so `nvme_rdma_inv_rkey_done` drops the second `req->ref`. [F204, F205]
- When `iod->nr_descriptors > 1`, `nvme_free_descriptors` must read `next_dma_addr` from `prp_list[last_prp]` before `dma_pool_free`. `nvme_free_host_mem()` must pass `dev->host_mem_descs_size` to `dma_free_coherent()`, while `nvme_free_host_mem_multi()` iterates `dev->nr_host_mem_descs`. When `nvme_timeout` decrements `dev->ctrl.abort_limit`, restore it via `atomic_inc` on negative return, `blk_mq_alloc_request` failure, or in `abort_endio` before `blk_mq_free_request`. [F199, F208, F314]

## API Contracts

- `nvme_try_complete_req` shifts its `__le16` wire `status` right by 1, so callers with unshifted status (`nvme_tcp_end_request`) must pass `cpu_to_le16(status << 1)`. Look up untrusted wire `command_id` values via `nvme_find_rq()`; use `nvme_cid_to_rq()` only in `nvme_tcp_recv_data()`/`nvme_tcp_recv_ddgst()` after `nvme_tcp_handle_c2h_data()` validates `command_id`. In `nvme_tcp/rdma_timeout`, if `nvme_ctrl_state(ctrl) != NVME_CTRL_LIVE`, stop the queue, call `nvmf_complete_timed_out_request(rq)`, and return `BLK_EH_DONE`. [F244, F271, F382]
- Configure `queue->sock->sk` in `nvme_tcp_alloc_queue` with `sk_allocation = GFP_ATOMIC`, `sk_use_task_frag = false`, `sk_set_memalloc`, and `nvme_tcp_reclassify_socket`, and run `nvme_tcp_try_send` under `memalloc_noreclaim_save`. Clear `MSG_SPLICE_PAGES` in `nvme_tcp_try_send_data` when `!sendpages_ok(page, len, offset)`, and return `false` from `nvme_tcp_queue_more` when `nvme_tcp_queue_tls(queue)`. On timeout/signal in `nvme_tcp_start_tls`, call `tls_handshake_cancel(queue->sock->sk)`, and verify `tls_get_record_type == TLS_RECORD_TYPE_DATA` in `nvme_tcp_init_connection` when TLS is active. [F375, F376, F377]
- In `nvme_rdma_queue_rq()`, call `ib_dma_sync_single_for_cpu()` on `req->sqe.dma` before `nvme_setup_cmd()`/`nvme_rdma_map_data()` and `ib_dma_sync_single_for_device()` before `nvme_rdma_post_send()`. Set `ctrl->async_event_sqe.cqe.done = nvme_rdma_async_done` (never `nvme_rdma_send_done`). In `nvme_rdma_destroy_queue_ib()`, destroy MR pools then `ib_destroy_qp(queue->qp)` (not `rdma_destroy_qp`), freeing `ib_cq` via `ib_free_cq()` for poll queues or `ib_cq_pool_put()` otherwise. [F264, F322, F338]
- Call `nvme_pci_avg_seg_size(req)` only when `blk_rq_nr_phys_segments(req) > 0`, and return `BLK_STS_AGAIN` from `nvme_pci_setup_data_simple` when `is_pci_p2pdma_page(bv.bv_page)`. Convert between 512B sectors and ZNS LBAs via `nvme_lba_to_sect`/`nvme_sect_to_lba`. In `pr.c`, pass negative errnos from `__nvme_send_pr_command` through and convert `>= 0` status via `nvme_status_to_pr_err`, retrying `nvme_pr_resv_report` with `cdw11 = 0` on `NVME_SC_HOST_ID_INCONSIST`. In `trace.c`, save `trace_seq_buffer_ptr(p)` and append `trace_seq_putc(p, 0)` in all `nvme_trace_*` helpers. [F245, F310, F326, F367, F385]

## Error Handling

- In `nvme_tcp/rdma_setup_ctrl`, any failure after initial admin queue setup (including the second `nvme_tcp_configure_admin_queue(ctrl, false)` for concat) must jump to `destroy_admin` and pass `new` to `teardown_admin_queue`; `nvme_tcp/rdma_create_ctrl` must clamp `ret > 0` to `-EIO` before `ERR_PTR(ret)`. Call `nvme_unquiesce_io_queues` after `nvme_dev_disable(dev, false)` only when `nvme_try_sched_reset` returns non-zero, and normalize `ret > 0` from `nvme_create_queue` to `0` in `nvme_create_io_queues`. [F200, F230, F233, F236]
- Populate `iod->cmd.common.dptr` or `sg_list[0]` (`nvme_pci_sgl_set_seg`) before `nvme_unmap_data`/`nvme_unmap_metadata` on `iter.status` failure in `nvme_pci_setup_data_prp/sgl` and `nvme_pci_setup_meta_sgls`. In `nvme_tcp_init_connection()`, receive `icresp` with `MSG_WAITALL`, check `ret < 0` before `ret < sizeof(*icresp)` (`-ECONNRESET`), and validate `hdr.plen == sizeof(*icresp)`, 4-byte alignment, and `maxh2cdata >= NVME_TCP_MIN_MAXH2CDATA`. In `nvme_rdma_map_sg_fr`, treat `ib_map_mr_sg` returning `nr < count` as failure (`nr < 0 ? nr : -EINVAL`), returning `req->mr` via `ib_mr_pool_put` and setting `req->mr = NULL`. In `sysfs.c`, gate `nvme_identify_ns` in `ns_head_update_nuse`/`ns_update_nuse` with `__ratelimit(&head->rs_nuse)` (returning 0 when rate-limited), and call `device_remove_file_self(dev, attr)` before `nvme_delete_ctrl_sync(ctrl)` in `nvme_sysfs_delete`. [F232, F238, F242, F268, F387]

## External Contracts

- Allocate PCIe I/O CQs before SQs (`adapter_alloc_cq/sq`) and delete all I/O SQs before any CQs (`nvme_delete_io_queues`). In `nvme_pci_configure_admin_queue()`, set `dev->subsystem` only if `NVME_REG_VS >= NVME_VS(1, 1, 0)` and `NVME_CAP_NSSRC(dev->ctrl.cap)`, clearing `NVME_CSTS_NSSRO` by writing it directly to `NVME_REG_CSTS` only when `dev->subsystem` is set. [F329, F340]
- In `nvme_tcp_recv_pdu()`, dispatch `nvme_tcp_c2h_term` to `nvme_tcp_handle_c2h_term()` and return before digest checks, bounding `hdr.plen` to `[NVME_TCP_MIN_C2HTERM_PLEN, NVME_TCP_MAX_C2HTERM_PLEN]` and `pdu->fes < ARRAY_SIZE(msg_table)` with `0x05` mapped to `NVME_TCP_FES_DATA_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. In `nvme_tcp_configure_admin_queue()`, when `ctrl->opts->concat && !ctrl->tls_pskid`, return `0` right after `nvme_tcp_start_queue(ctrl, 0)` without calling `nvme_enable_ctrl()` or `nvme_init_ctrl_finish()`. In `nvme_rdma_route_resolved`, `nvme_rdma_cm_req` requires 1-based `hrqsize`, 0-based `hsqsize`, and `cntlid` set only when `priv.qid != 0`. Mask `status` with `NVME_SCT_SC_MASK` in `nvme_status_to_pr_err()` without mapping `NVME_SC_CMD_SIZE_LIM_EXCEEDED` (`0x183`) to unsupported/invalid. [F246, F267, F293, F294, F332]

## Known Pitfalls

- Use `put_unaligned_le24/le32()` and `get_unaligned_le16()` for `length[3]`/`key[4]` in `nvme_keyed_sgl_desc` and `regctl[2]` in `nvme_reservation_status(_ext)`. Because `nvme_pci_setup_meta_sgls()` overwrites `cmd.common.flags` with `NVME_CMD_SGL_METASEG`, test data SGLs with `nvme_pci_cmd_use_sgl()` and meta SGLs with `nvme_pci_cmd_use_meta_sgl()`, and use `DIV_ROUND_UP(length, NVME_CTRL_PAGE_SIZE)` in `nvme_pci_setup_data_prp`. Keep the sequential `if (req->state == ...)` chain in `nvme_tcp_try_send()`, exiting via `goto out` after `NVME_TCP_SEND_CMD_PDU` when `!nvme_tcp_has_inline_data(req)`. Pass `nvme_rdma_get_max_fr_pages(...) + 1` to `ib_mr_pool_init` in `nvme_rdma_create_queue_ib`. [F283, F291, F297, F312, F369]
- In `pci.c`, use `1ULL << (12 + 4 * szu)` in `nvme_cmb_size_unit`, cast `max_host_mem_size_mb`/`hmpre`/`hmmin` to `u64` before multiplying in `nvme_setup_host_mem`, use `div_u64`/`do_div` in `nvme_cmb_qdepth` and `nvme_alloc_host_mem_multi`, and cast both `(new_idx - event_idx - 1)` and `(new_idx - old)` to `u16` in `nvme_dbbuf_need_event`. Use `SQ_SIZE(nvmeq)` and offset `sq_cmds` by `sq_tail << sqes` (copying `sizeof(*cmd)` bytes) in `nvme_sq_copy_cmd()`, and index `u32 *` doorbell/dbbuf pointers by `dev->db_stride` without multiplying by 4 (while `nvme_dbbuf_size`/`db_bar_size` multiply by `8 * dev->db_stride`). [F307, F308, F311, F313, F316, F317]
- Call `nvme_update_attrs(dev)` only after `nvme_pci_enable()`, `nvme_init_ctrl_finish()`, and `nvme_setup_host_mem()`; sample `was_suspend = !!(dev->ctrl.ctrl_config & NVME_CC_SHN_NORMAL)` in `nvme_reset_work()` before `nvme_dev_disable()`/`nvme_pci_enable()`; and hold `dev->shutdown_lock` with `adminq` IRQ disabled around `nvme_remap_bar()` in `nvme_setup_io_queues()`, updating `adminq->q_db = dev->dbs`. In `nvme_suspend()`'s host-managed power state path, freeze/sync queues, disable HMB (`nvme_set_host_mem(ndev, 0)`), save `ndev->last_ps`, and call `pci_save_state(pdev)` before `nvme_set_power_state(ctrl, ctrl->npss)` (`pci_load_saved_state(pdev, NULL)` if `ret > 0`), and always `nvme_unfreeze(ctrl)`. [F331, F348, F350, F351]
