# NETWORKING [GENERAL] — include

Core and protocol-level kernel headers (`include/linux/`, `include/net/`) defining packet buffers (`struct sk_buff`, `struct skb_shared_info`), sockets (`struct socket`), destination cache entries (`struct dst_entry`, `struct metadata_dst`), Netlink/RTNetlink (`rtnl_lock`), Ethtool interfaces, traffic control actions (`struct tc_action`), CoDel AQM, generic framer APIs, and IPv6/SCTP/ERSPAN/tunnel protocols.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct sk_buff` layout: `next` and `prev` must stay first in both `struct sk_buff_head` (`sk_buff_list`) and `struct sk_buff`; metadata copied on clone belongs inside `struct_group(headers, ...)`, while `tail`, `end`, `head`, `data`, `truesize`, `users`, and `extensions` must stay at the end; moving `cloned`, `pkt_type`, `tstamp_type`, or `tc_at_ingress` relative to `__cloned_offset`, `__pkt_type_offset`, or `__mono_tc_offset` requires updating `CLONED_*`, `PKT_TYPE_*`, `SKB_TSTAMP_TYPE_MASK`, `TC_AT_INGRESS_MASK`, and `SKB_BF_MONO_TC_OFFSET`. [F811, F812, F847]
- `struct skb_shared_info` & fclones: fields before `dataref` are bulk-cleared in `__alloc_skb()`, and `frags[MAX_SKB_FRAGS]` must stay last (`pskb_expand_head()` copies up to `frags[nr_frags]`). `dataref` packs total refs in low 16 bits (`SKB_DATAREF_MASK`) and `nohdr` refs in high 16 bits (`>> SKB_DATAREF_SHIFT`), so `skb_header_cloned()` checks `(dataref & SKB_DATAREF_MASK) - (dataref >> SKB_DATAREF_SHIFT) != 1` and `skb_data_unref()` subtracts `(1 << SKB_DATAREF_SHIFT) + 1` when `skb->nohdr` is set. `container_of(skb, struct sk_buff_fclones, skb1)` is only valid when `skb->fclone == SKB_FCLONE_ORIG`. [F813, F872, F885]
- `skb_dst(skb)` may be a routing `dst_entry` or `struct metadata_dst` (`dst->flags & DST_METADATA`): downcast via `skb_metadata_dst(skb)`, check `md_dst->type` before accessing `md_dst->u`, check `skb_valid_dst(skb)` in routing paths, and add a `case` in `skb_metadata_dst_cmp()` for every `enum metadata_type` variant (`default` returns `1`). [F795, F859]
- Tagged pointers & GRO state: `skb->_skb_refdst` and `skb_dstref_steal()` encode `SKB_DST_NOREF` (`1UL`) in bit 0 and must be masked with `SKB_DST_PTRMASK` (`skb_dst()`, `refdst_drop()`); attaching any dst or `nf_conntrack` (`skb_dst_set*()`, `__skb_dst_copy()`, `skb_dst_force()`, `skb_set_nfct()`) must set `skb->slow_gro |= !!val`. `dst->_metrics` encodes `DST_METRICS_FLAGS` (`DST_METRICS_READ_ONLY`, `DST_METRICS_REFCOUNTED`) in low 2 bits: arrays passed to `dst_init_metrics()` need 4-byte alignment, readers must use `DST_METRICS_PTR(dst)`, and writers must use `dst_metrics_write_ptr()` or `dst_metric_set()` to COW. [F796, F797, F810, F821]
- `skb->extensions` is only valid when `skb->active_extensions != 0` (check `skb_ext_exist(skb, id)` or `skb_ext_find(skb, id)`). In `struct skb_ext`, `offset[SKB_EXT_NUM]` and `chunks` use 8-byte units (`offset[id] == 0` is absent), and `SKB_EXT_NUM` must stay `<= 8` to fit `u8 active_extensions`. [F799, F853]
- Coupled `sk_buff` unions & hashes: set `inner_protocol`/`inner_ipproto` via `skb_set_inner_protocol()` or `skb_set_inner_ipproto()` (updates `inner_protocol_type`); update `hash`, `sw_hash`, and `l4_hash` together via `__skb_set_hash()`, `skb_clear_hash()`, or `skb_copy_hash()`; `napi_id` and `sender_cpu` share a union (`1..NR_CPUS` vs `>= MIN_NAPI_ID`), so `__skb_mark_napi_id()` must check `!napi_id_valid(skb->napi_id)` and `sk_mark_napi_id()` must update `sk->sk_napi_id` with `sk_rx_queue_update()`. [F815, F816, F826]
- `skb_mac_header_rebuild*()` must sample `old_mac = skb_mac_header(skb)` before `skb_set_mac_header()` and use `memmove()` (not `memcpy()`). [F824]
- `struct dst_entry` layout & expiry: `__rcuref` must stay at 64-byte aligned offset 64 on 32/64-bit (`BUILD_BUG_ON` in `dst_hold()`); `dst_set_expires()` must substitute `1` when `jiffies + timeout == 0` (`0` means unset) and compare non-zero `dst->expires` via `time_before(expires, old)`. [F832, F870]
- Ethtool & fixed-size layouts: new `ETH_RSS_HASH_*_BIT` values before `ETH_RSS_HASH_FUNCS_COUNT` require updating `rss_hash_func_strings[]`; `ethtool_rxfh_context.data[]` stores priv at 0, indir at `ALIGN(ctx->priv_size, 4)`, and key at `ctx->key_off` (use `ethtool_rxfh_context_priv/indir/key()`); `wait` must stay first in `struct socket_wq`; `skb->cb` (`NETLINK_CB()`) and `netlink_callback.ctx` (`NL_ASSERT_CTX_FITS()`) must not exceed 48 bytes (`NETLINK_CTX_SIZE`); `struct prefix_info` must stay 32 bytes. [F829, F838, F839, F840, F845]
- CoDel & busy-loop time: `codel_dequeue()`'s drop loop must `goto end;` after `INET_ECN_set_ce(skb)` and clear `vars->dropping = false` when `codel_should_drop()` returns `false`; `codel_time_t` comparisons must use `codel_time_after/before[_eq]()`; `busy_loop_timeout()` and `sk_busy_loop_timeout()` must use `time_after(now, end_time)`. [F862, F867, F871]

## Synchronization
- `skb_unref()` (when `refcount_read(&skb->users) == 1` and `!CONFIG_DEBUG_NET`) and `skb_data_unref()` (when `atomic_read(&shinfo->dataref) == bias`) elide the atomic RMW and must execute `smp_rmb()` before returning `true`. [F777]
- Non-atomic `sk_buff_head` mutators (`__skb_insert`, `__skb_unlink`, `__skb_dequeue`, `__skb_queue_*`) require holding `list->lock` (or exclusive ownership) and must use `WRITE_ONCE()` on `next`, `prev`, and `qlen` to pair with `READ_ONCE()` in `skb_queue_empty_lockless()` and `skb_peek_tail()`. [F781]
- Per-CPU `dst_cache_get*()` / `dst_cache_set*()` require local BH disabled; concurrent invalidation must use `dst_cache_reset()`, whereas `dst_cache_reset_now()` and `dst_cache_destroy()` are unsynchronized and require no concurrent users. [F778, F779]
- `rtnl_dereference(p)` and `dev_ingress_queue(dev)` require holding `rtnl_lock`; RCU-only readers must use `rcu_dereference_rtnl(p)` or `rcu_dereference(p)` (`dev_ingress_queue_rcu(dev)`). [F783]
- In `struct tc_action`, `tc_action_ops->act` runs under RCU-BH lock (must not sleep) and `tcf_register_action()` requires non-NULL `act`, `dump`, and `init`; updating `a->tcfa_bstats` when `a->cpu_bstats == NULL` requires holding `a->tcfa_lock` (`tcf_action_update_bstats()`). [F780, F929]
- With `CONFIG_NETPOLL` and non-NULL `dev->npinfo`, NAPI polling must serialize via `netpoll_poll_lock()` and `netpoll_poll_unlock()` (`smp_store_release(&napi->poll_owner, -1)`). [F776]
- `task_cls_classid()` must return `0` if `in_interrupt()` and read `task_css()` under `rcu_read_lock()`; `task_get_classid()` must check `softirq_count()` and read `sk->sk_cgrp_data` instead of `current` in softirq. [F912]

## Object Lifetimes
- `skb_dst_set()`, `skb_dst_set_noref()`, and `skb_dstref_restore()` require no refcounted dst (`skb_dst_check_unset()`); call `skb_dst_drop(skb)` first when replacing. When `SKB_DST_NOREF` is set, `skb_dst_set_noref()` and `skb_dst()` require RCU/RCU-BH read lock, and `skb_dst_force()` must be called under `rcu_read_lock()` before leaving RCU. [F753, F913]
- Use `skb_ext_reset(skb)` instead of `skb_ext_put(skb)` on a live `sk_buff` (`skb_ext_put()` leaves `active_extensions` non-zero). `__skb_ext_copy()` and `__nf_copy()` do not drop existing `dst` refs; use `skb_ext_copy()` and `nf_copy()` if `dst` may hold one. [F759, F760]
- `skb_peek()`, `skb_peek_next()`, and `skb_peek_tail()` return borrowed pointers without incrementing `skb->users`; hold the queue lock or call `skb_get()`. [F762]
- Shared/static `dst_entry` metrics (e.g., `dst_default_metrics`) must use `dst_init_metrics(dst, src, true)` (`read_only = true`) so `dst_destroy_metrics_generic()` does not free them. [F764]
- `__in6_dev_put()` and `__in6_ifa_put()` do not test for zero or destroy; use `in6_dev_put()` / `in6_ifa_put()` if it may be the last ref. Under RCU-only lookup, use `refcount_inc_not_zero()` (`in6_ifa_hold_safe()`, `__ipv4_neigh_lookup()`) rather than `in6_ifa_hold()`. [F767, F923]
- Use `netlink_skb_clone()` instead of `skb_clone()` on Netlink skbs so `skb->destructor` is propagated when `is_vmalloc_addr(skb->head)` is true. [F770]
- When `skb_zcopy_managed(skb)` (`SKBFL_MANAGED_FRAG_REFS`) is set, `skb_frag_unref()` is a no-op; call `skb_zcopy_downgrade_managed(skb)` before taking/dropping per-frag refs. `mm_account_pinned_pages(&mmp)` requires a zeroed `mmp.user` and pairs with `mm_unaccount_pinned_pages(&mmp)`. [F937, F944]
- `struct framer` consumers must call `framer_init()` before `framer_power_on/get_status/set_config/get_config()` and `framer_power_off()` before `framer_exit()`. When `ethtool_ops::begin` returns 0, the core always calls `ethtool_ops::complete` even on op failure (do not manually run `complete` cleanup on error). [F890, F896]

## API Contracts
- `skb_share_check()` and `skb_unshare()` always consume the input `skb` (even on `NULL` return), and along with `skb_unclone*()` and `skb_header_unclone()` unconditionally call `might_sleep_if(gfpflags_allow_blocking(pri))` (pass `GFP_ATOMIC` in atomic/RCU/softirq contexts). `skb_pad()`, `skb_padto()`, and `skb_put_padto()` free `skb` on error, whereas `__skb_pad()` and `__skb_put_padto(..., false)` do not. [F755, F756, F919]
- `sk_buff` offsets & queues: access `tail`/`end` via `skb_tail_pointer()`, `skb_end_pointer()`, `skb_end_offset()`, and `skb_reset/set_tail_pointer()`; check `skb_transport/mac_header_was_set()` (`~0U` sentinel) or `skb_inner_network_header_was_set()` (`> 0`), and use `skb_reset_transport_header_careful()` if `skb->data - skb->head` may overflow `u16`; check `skb_rx_queue_recorded(skb)` before `skb_get_rx_queue(skb)` (`queue_mapping - 1`) and never mix with `skb_set/get_queue_mapping()`; use `skb_queue_walk_safe()` (or `*_safe` variants) or `skb_rbtree_walk_from_safe()` when unlinking/freeing; guard `skb_queue_next/prev()` with `!skb_queue_is_last/first()` (or use `skb_peek_next()`). [F805, F850, F851, F854, F856, F857, F947]
- Checksums: for `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL`, `skb->csum_start` is from `skb->head` (`skb_checksum_start_offset(skb)` is from `skb->data`), and `NETIF_F_SCTP_CRC` drivers must check `skb->csum_not_inet`; never set both `NETIF_F_HW_CSUM` and `NETIF_F_IP[V6]_CSUM`; on RX, `CHECKSUM_UNNECESSARY` must not modify the header checksum, and whole-packet hardware checksums must use `CHECKSUM_COMPLETE`. [F809, F901, F902]
- Frags & zero-copy: `netdev/napi_alloc_frag_align()` take power-of-2 alignment while `__netdev/__napi_alloc_frag_align()` take `-align`; iterate compound/highmem frags with `skb_frag_foreach_page()` for `kmap_local_page()`, and use `skb_frag_address_safe()` if not known mapped; TX-to-RX paths must call `skb_orphan_frags_rx()` instead of `skb_orphan_frags()`; validate zero-copy send pages with `sendpage[s]_ok()`; `skb_irq_freeable()` requires no `destructor`, `secpath`, `nfct`, `_skb_refdst`, or `frag_list`. [F806, F916, F920, F936, F940]
- Dst, protocol & Netlink helpers: `dst_metric*()` take 1-based `RTAX_*` indices (`dst_metric()` warns on `RTAX_MTU`/`RTAX_ADVMSS`/`RTAX_HOPLIMIT`; `dst_metric_locked()` takes unshifted `RTAX_*`); `METADATA_IP_TUNNEL` `metadata_dst` ops must include `u.tun_info.options_len`; `ipv4/ipv6_change_dsfield()` take `mask` as bits to keep and a shifted 8-bit `value` (`ipv6_change_dsfield()` widens to `u16` before `<< 4`); access ERSPAN `vlan`, `session_id`, and `hwid` via `get/set_*()`; use `__in6_dev_stats_get(dev, skb)` for VRF-aware IPv6 stats; check Netlink caps via `netlink_[ns_|net_]capable()`; `NL_SET_ERR_MSG()` requires a string literal while formatted messages use `NL_SET_ERR_MSG_FMT()`; `codel_control_law()` must shift `vars->rec_inv_sqrt << REC_INV_SQRT_SHIFT`; `framer_create()` requires `.get_status` with `FRAMER_FLAG_POLL_STATUS`. [F804, F808, F836, F868, F875, F879, F887, F928, F939, F943]
- Ethtool RSS, coalesce & link ops: `ethtool_check_ops()` requires `supported_coalesce_params` with `set_coalesce`, `get_rxfh_fields` and `rxfh_per_ctx_fields == rxfh_per_ctx_key` with `supported_input_xfrm`, and `rxfh_max_num_contexts != 1` (exclusive upper bound when `> 0`); `create_rxfh_context` requires `modify/remove_rxfh_context` and populating `ctx` defaults when `indir`/`key` is `NULL` or `hfunc`/`input_xfrm` is `*_NO_CHANGE`; `ethtool_rxfh_indir_default()` requires `n_rx_rings != 0`; `get/set_per_queue_coalesce` must bounds-check queue index against RX/TX counts; `get/set_link_ksettings` must ignore `base.cmd`/`link_mode_masks_nwords`, size masks with `__ETHTOOL_LINK_MODE_MASK_NBITS`, and convert 32-bit masks via `bitmap_from/to_arr32()` or `ethtool_convert_*_u32*`. [F828, F841, F844, F873, F889, F899, F924]
- Ethtool stats, strings, PHC & MMSV: `get_rmon/fec_stats` ranges need a `{}` sentinel within `ETHTOOL_RMON_HIST_MAX` (11) or `ETHTOOL_FEC_HIST_MAX - 1` (16), and `get_fec_stats` must set either `total` or `lanes[]` (`lanes[0] == ETHTOOL_STAT_NOT_SET` when unused); `ethtool_sprintf/puts/cpy()` auto-advance `*data` by `ETH_GSTRING_LEN`; `ethtool_get_phc_vclocks() > 0` returns a caller-`kfree()`d `*vclock_index`; `ethtool_mmsv` commits preemptible TCs only when `tx_active` is true and requires `ethtool_mmsv_stop()` on stop/suspend. [F769, F843, F888, F897, F927]

## Error Handling
- `devm_framer_optional_get()` returns `NULL` when absent/disabled and `ERR_PTR` on error, whereas `framer_init/exit/power_on/power_off()` dereference `framer` unconditionally (check `IS_ERR()` and non-`NULL`). [F787]
- `tun_dst_unclone()` returns `ERR_PTR`, whereas `skb_tunnel_info_unclone()`, `tun_rx_dst()`, `__ip[v6]_tun_set_dst()`, `dst_neigh_lookup[_skb]()`, and `dst_metrics_write_ptr()` return `NULL` on failure. [F788, F789, F790]
- `NL_REQ_ATTR_CHECK()` returns `1` when missing and `0` when present (do not return directly as errno). `NL_SET_ERR_MSG*` accept `NULL` `extack`, and outer cleanup paths should use `NL_SET_ERR_MSG_WEAK[_MOD]()`. [F791, F803]
- `skb_checksum_complete()`, `[__]skb_checksum_validate*()`, `skb_checksum_init*()`, and `skb_checksum_simple_validate()` return `0` (`__sum16`) when valid/deferred and non-zero when invalid. [F792]
- When `skb_frag_is_net_iov(frag)` is true, `skb_frag_page()` and `skb_frag_address[_safe]()` return `NULL`; when page-backed, `skb_frag_net_iov(frag)` returns `NULL`. [F800]

## External Contracts
- 32-bit compat structs (`compat_ethtool_rx_flow_spec`, `compat_ethtool_rxnfc`, `compat_group_req`, `compat_group_source_req`, `compat_group_filter`) must use `compat_u64` or `__aligned(4) __packed`. [F837]
- In `struct sctphdr`, `source`, `dest`, and `vtag` are big-endian, while `checksum` is little-endian (`__le32`). Ethtool stats initialized by `ethtool_stats_init()` must use `u64` fields set to `ETHTOOL_STAT_NOT_SET` (`~0ULL`). `proto_ops::recvmsg` must set `msg->msg_namelen` iff `msg->msg_name != NULL`. [F846, F874, F941]

## Known Pitfalls
- Pointers into `skb->head` (`skb->data`, `skb_network_header(skb)`, `skb_shinfo(skb)`) become stale across reallocating calls (`pskb_expand_head()`, `pskb_[network_]may_pull()`, `skb_cow()`, `skb_try_make_writable()`, `pskb_trim_rcsum()`) and must be reloaded. [F814]
- After `skb_queue_walk*()` finishes without `break`, `skb` is `(struct sk_buff *)(queue)`, not `NULL`. In `skb_frag_foreach_page()`, when `!skb_frag_must_loop(p)`, `p_off + p_len` can exceed `PAGE_SIZE` on compound pages. [F855, F858]
- `skb_dst_set_noref(skb, NULL)` sets `SKB_DST_NOREF`, so check `skb_dst_is_noref(skb)` rather than `skb->_skb_refdst & SKB_DST_NOREF`. In `__skb_metadata_differs()`, the `fallthrough` chains mutate `a` and `b` via `__it_diff()`, whereas `default:`/`slow:` assumes unmodified `a` and `b`. [F860, F864]
- Overflow pitfalls: in `codel_Newton_step()`, cast `invsqrt` to `u64` before squaring and shift `val >>= 2` before `* invsqrt`; in `codel_time_to_us()`, cast `val` to `u64` before `<< CODEL_SHIFT` and use `do_div()`; in `addrconf_timeout_fixup(timeout, unit)`, return `~0UL` for `INFINITY_LIFE_TIME` and clamp to `LONG_MAX / unit`. [F865, F866, F869]
