# INTEL ETHERNET DRIVERS — drivers/net/ethernet/intel/ice (part 5)

This unit manages MSI-X/virtual interrupt tracking, Link Aggregation (LAG) offload/failover, LAN Tx/Rx descriptors, and core VSI lifecycle routines.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `ICE_VSI_CHNL` VSIs omit `tx_rings`, `rx_rings`, `txq_map`, `rxq_map`, `q_vectors`, and `pf->vsi_stats[vsi->idx]`, and `ICE_VSI_LB` VSIs omit `q_vectors`; guard accesses by VSI type or NULL checks. `vsi->vf` must be non-NULL for `ICE_VSI_VF`, and for `ICE_VSI_CTRL` is non-NULL only on VF control VSIs (NULL on PF control VSI). [F46, F47]
- New `enum ice_vsi_type` values require lockstep cases in `ice_vsi_type_str`, `ice_vsi_set_num_qs`, `ice_vsi_alloc_def`, `ice_vsi_init`, `ice_set_agg_vsi`, and `ice_vsi_cfg_def`. Because `pf->vsi` is sparse (`ice_vsi_free` NULLs slots), every `ice_for_each_vsi(pf, i)` loop must NULL-check `pf->vsi[i]`. [F78, F93]
- Index per-queue HW registers (`QRX_CTRL`, `QRXFLXP_CNTXT`, `QRX_CONTEXT`, `QINT_TQCTL`, `QINT_RQCTL`) and `ice_aqc_cfg_txqs_buf` handles via PF-space `vsi->rxq_map[q]` / `vsi->txq_map[q]`, while `rx_rings[q]` / `tx_rings[q]` use VSI-relative `q`. In `ice_vsi_alloc_arrays`, size `vsi->txq_map` as `vsi->alloc_txq + num_possible_cpus()` for XDP Tx rings mapped at `vsi->alloc_txq` before `num_xdp_txq` is set. [F84, F113]
- Partial VSI updates via `ice_update_vsi` (`ice_vsi_update_security`, `ice_vsi_update_local_lb`, `ice_vsi_cfg_tc`) must seed `ctx.info` from `vsi->info`, set `ICE_AQ_VSI_PROP_*_VALID` in `valid_sections`, and copy changes back to `vsi->info` on success. Preserve `ICE_AQ_VSI_SEC_FLAG_ALLOW_DEST_OVRD` on `ICE_VSI_PF` in `ice_vsi_update_security`. [F59, F60]
- `ice_vsi_setup_q_map` and `ice_vsi_setup_q_map_mqprio` must reject total TC queues exceeding `vsi->alloc_txq`/`alloc_rxq` before setting `num_txq`/`num_rxq`. In `ice_vsi_setup_q_map`, clamp `vsi->tc_cfg.numtc` to >= 1 before dividing `qcount_rx`/`qcount_tx`, and clamp `num_rxq_per_tc` to >= 1 before `order_base_2()`. [F87, F109]
- Reset `vsi->stat_offsets_loaded = false` in `ice_vsi_cfg_def` and when `ice_is_reset_in_progress(pf->state)` in `ice_update_eth_stats` before sampling `GLV_*` counters. `ice_vsi_set_num_desc` must only set `num_rx_desc`/`num_tx_desc` to defaults when 0. When `ice_vsi_cfg_rss_lut_key` restores `vsi->rss_size` from `orig_rss_size` after ADQ teardown, clear `vsi->orig_rss_size = 0`. [F65, F66, F67]
- In `ice_set_q_vector_intrl`, if `q_vector->tx` or `rx` has `ITR_IS_DYNAMIC`, call `ice_write_intrl(q_vector, 4)` without overwriting `q_vector->intrl`. In `ice_vsi_ctrl_all_rx_rings`, call `ice_vsi_ctrl_one_rx_ring(..., false)` on all Rx rings, call `ice_flush(&vsi->back->hw)` once, then poll via `ice_vsi_wait_one_rx_ring`. [F68, F103]
- In `ice_set_agg_vsi`, skip `ice_agg_node`s with `num_vsis >= ICE_MAX_VSIS_IN_AGG_NODE`, bound by `ICE_MAX_PF_AGG_NODES` / `ICE_MAX_VF_AGG_NODES`, and update `agg_node->num_vsis` and `vsi->agg_node` together; `ice_vsi_decfg` must decrement `vsi->agg_node->num_vsis` on `ICE_VSI_VF`. [F69, F91]
- When the active uplink port of a primary SR-IOV LAG PF changes or unlinks (`ice_lag_monitor_act_bkup`, `ice_lag_act_bkup_unlink`, `ice_lag_aa_move_vf_qs`), call `ice_lag_config_eswitch` with the active `net_device` to update `repr->dst->u.port_info.lower_dev` and call `netif_keep_dst(netdev)`. [F58, F152]
- In `ice_lag_changeupper_event` (link) and `ice_lag_chk_comp`, verify `ice_is_switchdev_running` on the primary PF before configuring shared SWIDs, prune lists, or LP filters. In `ice_lag_set_swid`, cache `local_lag->bond_swid = primary_swid` on link so unlink (`!link`) can free `bond_swid`, clear it to 0, and restore `port_info->sw_id`. [F74, F131]
- In Active-Active LAG, `sec_vf[ICE_MAX_SRIOV_VFS]` and `q_home[ICE_MAX_SRIOV_VFS][ICE_MAX_RSS_QS_PER_VF]` are indexed by `vf_id` and queue `i`. Secondary placeholder VF VSIs (`lag->sec_vf[vf->vf_id]`) in `ice_lag_monitor_act_act` require `ice_lag_aa_clear_spoof`, and on unlink in `ice_lag_changeupper_event` must be released via `ice_vsi_release` and nulled. [F75, F85]
- In `ice_lag_aa_move_vf_qs`, unlink moved `q_node`s from `parent->children` and `old_hw->port_info->sib_head[0][layer]`, link into `new_hw->port_info->sib_head[0][layer]` and `n_prt->children`, and set `lag->q_home[vf_id][i] = dest`. In `ice_lag_qbuf_recfg`, `i` indexes `ice_get_lan_q_ctx` while `count` indexes `qbuf->queue_info[count]`; callers must check returned `valq > 0` before `ice_aq_cfg_lan_txq`. [F71, F98]
- Flexible-array AdminQ buffers in `ice_lag.c` (`ice_sw_rule_lkup_rx_tx`, `ice_sw_rule_vsi_list`, `ice_aqc_cfg_txqs_buf`, `ice_aqc_alloc_free_res_elem`) must use `ICE_SW_RULE_*_SIZE()` or `struct_size()` for both `kzalloc` and AdminQ length, never `sizeof(*ptr)`. [F120]
- Enable `ICE_F_CGU`, `ICE_F_SMA_CTRL`, and `ICE_F_GNSS` in `ice_init_feature_support` only on E810 when `ice_pf_src_tmr_owned(pf)` and the netlist/module check pass. Enable `ICE_F_SRIOV_LAG` / `ICE_F_SRIOV_AA_LAG` only when both `dev_caps.common_cap` and `ice_pkg_has_lport_extract(&pf->hw)` are true. When `ice_is_safe_mode(pf)` is true, skip DDP-dependent RSS flow and switch rules in `ice_lib.c`. [F128, F129, F130]

## Synchronization
- `ice_ena_vsi` and `ice_dis_vsi` must acquire/release `rtnl_lock()` around `ice_open_internal` and `ice_vsi_close` on running PF/SF netdevs iff `locked == false`. In `ice_dis_vsi` (`locked == false`), re-check `test_bit(ICE_VSI_DOWN, vsi->state)` after acquiring `rtnl_lock()`. [F19, F33]
- Serialize `pf->vsi[]`, `pf->next_vsi`, and `vsi->xdp_state_lock` init/destroy under `pf->sw_mutex` in `ice_vsi_alloc` / `ice_vsi_free`, and `pf->avail_txqs` / `avail_rxqs` under `pf->avail_q_mutex` in `ice_vsi_get_qs` / `ice_vsi_put_qs`. [F13, F144]
- Hold `vsi->xdp_state_lock` in `ice_vsi_rebuild` across `ice_vsi_realloc_stat_arrays`, `ice_vsi_decfg`, `ice_vsi_cfg_def`, and `ice_vsi_cfg_tc_lan`. Update `tx_rings[]`, `rx_rings[]`, `tx_ring_stats[]`, and `rx_ring_stats[]` with `WRITE_ONCE` and free via `kfree_rcu`, clearing `q_vector` ring pointers in `ice_vsi_clear_rings` before freeing rings. [F28, F142]
- Initialize `lag->pf`, `netdev`, `role`, `active_port`, and `notif_block.notifier_call` before `ice_register_lag_handler(lag)` in `ice_init_lag`. In `ice_deinit_lag`, call `ice_unregister_lag_handler()` then `flush_workqueue(ice_lag_wq)` before freeing recipes/LAG and setting `pf->lag = NULL`. [F10, F23]
- Enclose `for_each_netdev_in_bond_rcu` and `netdev_master_upper_dev_get_rcu` in `rcu_read_lock()` / `rcu_read_unlock()`, using `GFP_ATOMIC` for any allocations inside. [F25]

## Object Lifetimes
- `ice_lag_process_event` owns `lag_work` and its `netdev_list`: on all exit paths it must unlink/kfree every entry, set `lag->netdev_head = NULL` under `pf->lag_mutex`, and `kfree(lag_work)`. Callers of `ice_lag_build_netdev_list(lag, &ndlist)` must call `ice_lag_destroy_netdev_list(lag, &ndlist)` before returning. In `ice_lag_cfg_dflt_fltr`, if TX `ice_lag_cfg_fltr` fails after RX succeeded, roll back RX with `!add`. [F4, F8, F119]
- `struct ice_vsi`, its 5 arrays (`tx_rings`, `rx_rings`, `txq_map`, `rxq_map`, `q_vectors`), `rss_hkey_user`, and `rss_lut_user` use `devm_kcalloc`/`devm_kzalloc` and must be freed via `devm_kfree()`. `ice_vsi_alloc_arrays` rolls back in reverse order, and `ice_vsi_free_arrays` NULLs all 5 pointers. [F11, F125]
- Call `ice_vsi_put_qs` before `ice_vsi_free_arrays` (`ice_vsi_decfg`, `ice_vsi_cfg_def`) while `txq_map`/`rxq_map` exist. Ring, queue-map, and stat allocation/teardown must iterate with `ice_for_each_alloc_txq`/`alloc_rxq`, not `ice_for_each_txq`/`rxq`. [F64, F94]
- `ice_vsi_decfg` preserves `pf->vsi_stats[vsi->idx]`. In `ice_vsi_rebuild`, `ice_vsi_realloc_stat_arrays` must run before `ice_vsi_decfg` and `ice_vsi_cfg_def` (while `alloc_txq`/`alloc_rxq` hold old counts), `kfree_rcu()` excess tail entries before shrinking with `krealloc_array(..., __GFP_ZERO)`, restore the old pointer on NULL return, and `ice_vsi_alloc_ring_stats` must rebind `ring->ring_stats`. [F16, F62, F115]
- In `ice_vsi_release`, call `ice_vsi_delete` (which calls `ice_vsi_free`) after `ice_vsi_close` and `ice_vsi_decfg` only when `!ice_is_reset_in_progress(pf->state)`. `ice_vsi_free_irq` must check `vsi->irqs_ready`, call `ice_vsi_release_msix`, set `irqs_ready = false`, and only free IRQs on non-VF `q_vectors` with `num_ring_tx > 0` or `num_ring_rx > 0`. [F17, F18]

## API Contracts
- `ice_alloc_irq` returns `struct msi_map` by value; on `pci_msix_alloc_irq_at` failure it must call `ice_free_irq_res` and return negative `.index`. Callers check `map.index < 0` and must not call `ice_free_irq` on failure. Call `pci_msix_alloc_irq_at` / `pci_msix_free_irq` only when `entry->dynamic` is true. `ice_virt_get_irqs` returns signed `int` (`-ENOENT` or PF-relative MSI-X index), which `ice_virt_free_irqs` requires. [F1, F35, F36, F164]
- Call `ice_lag_prepare_vf_reset` and `ice_lag_complete_vf_reset` in pairs under the same `pf->lag_mutex` hold, passing the returned `act_prt`. In `ice_lag_move_vf_node_tc`, `ice_lag_aa_move_vf_qs`, `ice_lag_reclaim_vf_tc`, and `ice_lag_move_vf_nodes_tc_sync`, suspend VF scheduler TEIDs via `ice_sched_suspend_resume_elems(..., true)` before moving/reconfiguring queues and resume with `false` on all exit paths. [F42, F136]
- `ice_vsi_setup` requires `ICE_VSI_FLAG_INIT` and non-NULL `params->port_info`, returning `NULL` (never `ERR_PTR`) on failure. `ice_get_free_slot` takes an array of pointer-sized elements and element count, returning `ICE_NO_VSI` (`0xffff`) when full. [F44, F45, F117]
- In `ice_vsi_init`, `ctxt->vf_num` for `ICE_VSI_VF` is `vsi->vf->vf_id + hw->func_caps.vf_base_id`, whereas `ice_vsi_stop_lan_tx_rings` takes PF-relative `rel_vmvf_num`. In VSI queue mapping, `ICE_AQ_VSI_TC_Q_NUM_M` in `tc_mapping[i]` takes `order_base_2(qcount)`, while `q_mapping[1]` takes raw Rx queue count. [F52, F159]
- `ice_set_min_bw_limit` / `ice_set_max_bw_limit` take Kbps and treat `0` as clearing via `ice_cfg_vsi_bw_dflt_lmt_per_tc`. `ice_write_itr` and `ice_write_intrl` take usec; `ice_intrl_usec_to_reg` requires non-zero `gran`. [F54, F110, F160]
- `ice_lag_set_swid` and `ice_wait_for_reset` sleep and require process context. Call `ice_napi_add` only during initial driver load after `vsi->netdev` creation. When `ice_lag_is_switchdev_running(vsi->back)` is true, `ice_set_dflt_vsi` must return 0 without calling `ice_cfg_dflt_vsi`. [F141, F148, F153, F156]

## Error Handling
- Store `pci_alloc_irq_vectors` return in signed `int` (`vectors < 0`) in `ice_init_interrupt_scheme`. Verify both `pf->lag` and `lag->upper_netdev` are non-NULL in `ice_lag_is_switchdev_running` and `ice_lag_prepare_vf_reset`. In `ice_register_lag_handler`, reset `notif_blk->notifier_call = NULL` if `register_netdevice_notifier` fails. [F34, F37, F40]
- In `ice_pull_qvec_from_rc`, switch on `rc->type` and NULL-check the ring pointer before accessing `rc->rx_ring`/`tx_ring`; NULL-check the returned `q_vector` in callers. Initialize `bool exists = false` before `ice_check_if_dflt_vsi` in `ice_is_dflt_vsi_in_use`. Ignore `-EEXIST` as success in `ice_vsi_add_vlan_zero` / `del_vlan_zero`, and do not fail `ice_vsi_cfg_def` on RSS LUT/key/flow errors. [F48, F51, F56, F163]

## External Contracts
- Mask `union ice_32b_rx_flex_desc` `pkt_len` with `ICE_RX_FLX_DESC_PKT_LEN_M` and `ptype_flex_flags0` with `ICE_RX_FLEX_DESC_PTYPE_M`. In `struct ice_tx_desc`, `MACLEN` is in 2-byte words while `IPLEN` and `L4LEN` are in 4-byte dwords. Use `ULL` / `BIT_ULL()` for 64-bit descriptor masks at bits 31..63 in `ice_lan_tx_rx.h`, and cast operands to `u64` before shifting by `ICE_TXD_QW1_TX_BUF_SZ_S` (34), `ICE_TXD_QW1_L2TAG1_S` (48), or `ICE_TXD_CTX_QW1_MSS_S` (50). [F89, F90, F106, F107]
- Verify `netif_is_ice(netdev)` before calling `netdev_priv(netdev)` in LAG paths. `ice_lag_event_handler` must ignore events unless `netif_is_ice(netdev)`, event is `NETDEV_CHANGEUPPER`/`BONDING_INFO`/`UNREGISTER`, `IFF_BONDING` is set, `lag->netdev` is non-NULL, and `net_eq(dev_net(netdev), &init_net)`. [F149, F150]

## Known Pitfalls
- `vsi->idx` indexes `pf->vsi[]`, `pf->vsi_stats[]`, and `hw->vsi_ctx[]`, whereas `vsi->vsi_num` is the HW VSI ID for `GLV_*` and switch rules; however, in `ice_lag.c`, the `vsi_num` parameter of VF node/queue movement helpers (`ice_lag_qbuf_recfg`, `ice_lag_aa_move_vf_qs`, etc.) is actually `vsi->idx`. [F82, F83]
- `lag->port_bitmap` uses bitmasks `ICE_LAGP_M`/`ICE_LAGS_M`, `lag->q_home` and `dest` use 0-based indices `ICE_LAGP_IDX`/`ICE_LAGS_IDX`, and `active_port`/`bond_lport_*` store HW `lport` numbers (`ICE_LAG_INVALID_PORT`). [F86]
- In `ice_lag_aa_move_vf_qs`, toggle `*odd = !(*odd)` at the start of each iteration before any `continue`, and target `pri_hw` and primary `vsi_num` for `ice_get_lan_q_ctx`, `ice_lag_aa_qbuf_recfg`, and `ice_aq_cfg_lan_txq`, while using `new_hw->port_info` and `dst_vsi_num` for `ice_sched_get_free_qparent`. In `ice_lag_add_prune_list` / `del_prune_list`, look up the VSI list on `&lag->pf->hw` (`lag->pf->vsi[0]->idx`), but issue `ice_aq_sw_rules` on `&event_pf->hw` (`event_pf->vsi[0]->vsi_num`). [F99, F100, F104]
- Loop index pitfalls in `ice_lib.c`: increment `agg_node_iter++` before every `continue` in `ice_set_agg_vsi`; initialize `txq`/`rxq` to 0 once before the outer `ice_for_each_q_vector` loop in `ice_vsi_cfg_msix` and `ice_vsi_release_msix`; do not reset `i = 0` in the second loop of `ice_vsi_rebuild_set_coalesce`. [F95, F96, F97]
