# NFS, SUNRPC, AND LOCKD CLIENTS — fs/nfs (part 2)

This unit implements NFS inode management (`inode.c`), mount/fs_context setup (`fs_context.c`, `getroot.c`, `mount_clnt.c`), automounts/referrals (`namespace.c`), fscache/netfs (`fscache.c`), localio (`localio.c`), and pNFS file (`filelayout/`) and flexfile (`flexfilelayout/`) layout drivers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- When `nfs_post_op_update_inode_force_wcc_locked()` cannot force WCC because `(fattr->valid & NFS_ATTR_FATTR) == 0` or `!attr_cmp`, it must call `nfs_ooo_record()` before clearing `NFS_ATTR_FATTR_PRECHANGE` and `PRE*` flags. When `nfs_inode_attach_open_context()` attaches the first context to an empty `nfsi->open_files` while `nfs_ooo_test(nfsi)` is true, it must call `nfs_set_cache_invalid(inode, NFS_INO_INVALID_DATA | NFS_INO_REVAL_FORCED)` under `inode->i_lock`. [F249, F250]
- In `nfs_fscache_open_file()`, when `inode_is_open_for_write(inode)` is true on a valid cookie, `fscache_use_cookie(cookie, true)` must be followed by `nfs_fscache_update_auxdata()` and `fscache_invalidate(..., FSCACHE_INVAL_DIO_WRITE)`. In `nfs_netfs_read_completion()`, `NETFS_SREQ_CLEAR_TAIL` must only be set when `NFS_IOHDR_EOF` is set and `sreq->rreq->origin` is neither `NETFS_UNBUFFERED_READ` nor `NETFS_DIO_READ`, and `hdr->netfs` must be set to `NULL` after `nfs_netfs_put(netfs)`. [F254, F255]
- In `filelayout_write_done_cb()` and `ff_layout_write_done_cb()`, `hdr->fattr.valid` must be cleared to `0` before `nfs_writeback_update_inode(hdr)`. In `ff_layout_read/write_done_cb()`, both `NFS_IOHDR_RESEND_PNFS` and `NFS_IOHDR_RESEND_MDS` must be cleared on `hdr->flags` before setting the new resend bit. In flexfile read/write/commit accounting, `test_and_set/clear_bit(NFS_IOHDR_STAT, ...)` on `hdr->flags` or `cdata->flags` must gate `nfs4_ff_layout_stat_io_start/end_*()` so `nfs4_ff_start_busy_timer()` pairs with at most one `nfs4_ff_end_busy_timer()`. [F203, F238, F262]
- In `nfs4_fl_alloc_deviceid_node()`, `dsaddr->ds_num` must be set to `num` immediately after `kzalloc()` before accessing `dsaddr->ds_list[]` (`__counted_by(ds_num)`) or calling `nfs4_fl_free_deviceid()`. Decoded `stripe_count` must be `<= NFS4_PNFS_MAX_STRIPE_CNT` (4096), `ds_num <= NFS4_PNFS_MAX_MULTI_CNT` (256), and every `stripe_indices` entry `< ds_num`. [F265, F308]
- `filelayout_check_layout()` must reject `fl->stripe_unit == 0`. `filelayout_check_deviceid()` must enforce `fl->first_stripe_index < dsaddr->stripe_count` and `fl->num_fh` in `{0, 1, dsaddr->ds_num}` (`STRIPE_SPARSE`) or `dsaddr->stripe_count` (`STRIPE_DENSE`). Commit bucket indices are `0..dsaddr->ds_num - 1` (`STRIPE_SPARSE`) or `0..dsaddr->stripe_count - 1` (`STRIPE_DENSE`), not raw `hdr->ds_commit_idx`. [F266, F267, F292]
- In `ff_layout_alloc_lseg()`, `fls->mirror_array_cnt` must be set immediately after `kzalloc()` before indexing `fls->mirror_array[]` (`__counted_by(mirror_array_cnt)`) or calling `_ff_layout_free_lseg()`. `mirror_array_cnt` must be in `1..NFS4_FLEXFILE_LAYOUT_MAX_MIRROR_CNT`, every mirror must have identical `dss_count` in `1..NFS4_FLEXFILE_LAYOUT_MAX_STRIPE_CNT`, and `stripe_unit` must be non-zero whenever `dss_count > 1`. [F269, F309]
- In `ff_layout_match_io()`, `task->tk_calldata` must only be passed to `ff_layout_match_rw()` or `ff_layout_match_commit()` after matching `task->tk_ops` against flexfile `rpc_call_ops`. In `ff_layout_encode_layoutstats()`, the XDR opaque length at `start` must be `cpu_to_be32((xdr->p - start - 1) * 4)`. [F278, F314]
- `filelayout_decode_layout()` and `decode_nfs_fh()` must check `fh->size <= NFS_MAXFHSIZE`, and `decode_fhandle3()` (and v3 `nfs23_parse_monolithic()`) must check `size > 0 && size <= NFS3_FHSIZE`. `decode_auth_flavors()` must clamp wire `entries` to `NFS_MAX_SECFLAVORS` before `xdr_inline_decode(xdr, 4 * entries)` and to `*res->auth_count` before writing `res->auth_flavors`. [F270, F271]

## Synchronization
- In `nfs_fhget()`, when `iget5_locked()` returns an `I_NEW` inode, all `nfs_inode` fields, `nfs_inode_init_regular/dir()`, `i_op`, timeouts, and `nfs_fscache_init_inode()` must be initialized before `unlock_new_inode()`. `nfs_set_inode_stale_locked()`, `nfs_zap_caches_locked()`, and (when `!I_NEW`) `nfs_set_cache_invalid()` require `inode->i_lock`; setting `NFS_INO_INVALID_DATA` in `nfs_set_cache_invalid()` must call `nfs_fscache_invalidate()` and (if `nrpages == 0` or `NFS_INO_INVALID_DATA` remains set) `nfs_ooo_clear()`. [F205, F206, F246, F328]
- In `nfs_clear_invalid_mapping()`, the lockless fast-path check after waiting on `NFS_INO_INVALIDATING` must pair `smp_rmb()` and `smp_load_acquire(&nfsi->cache_validity)` with `smp_wmb()` and `nfs_set_cache_invalid()`'s `smp_store_release()`, and re-check `NFS_INO_INVALIDATING` and `NFS_INO_INVALID_DATA` under `inode->i_lock`. `nfs_vmtruncate()` must enter and exit under `inode->i_lock`, dropping it across `truncate_pagecache()`. [F207, F211]
- `nfs_file_block_o_direct()` requires exclusive `inode->i_rwsem` and must be called before `nfs_sync_inode(inode)` in `nfs_setattr()` on regular files. When the `down_read_killable(&inode->i_rwsem)` check of `NFS_INO_ODIRECT` fails in `nfs_start_io_read/direct()`, the caller must drop `i_rwsem`, take `down_write_killable(&inode->i_rwsem)`, re-test `NFS_INO_ODIRECT` in `nfs_file_block_o_direct()` or `nfs_block_buffered()`, and call `downgrade_write(&inode->i_rwsem)`. [F209, F210]
- `nfsi->open_files` is RCU-protected: `nfs_find_open_context()` must use `list_for_each_entry_rcu()` under `rcu_read_lock()` and `get_nfs_open_context()`, while `nfs_inode_attach_open_context()` and `__put_nfs_open_context()` must mutate it under `inode->i_lock` with RCU list ops and `kfree_rcu()`. `nfs_clear_pnfs_ds_commit_verifiers()` must iterate `cinfo->commits` with `list_for_each_entry_rcu()` under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F337, F338]
- `nfs_io_gfp_mask()` must only add `__GFP_NORETRY | __GFP_NOWARN` for `PF_WQ_WORKER` when `current_gfp_context(GFP_KERNEL) == GFP_KERNEL`. `nfs_local_iocb_init()` must pass `GFP_NOIO` to `nfs_local_iocb_alloc()` when `!(hdr->rw_mode & FMODE_READ)`. [F319, F321]
- `clp->cl_nfssvc_boot` is protected by `clp->cl_boot_lock` (`read_seqbegin/retry()` in `nfs_copy_boot_verifier()`, `write_seqlock()` in `nfs_reset_boot_verifier()`), and any failure in `nfs_local_write/commit_done()` must call `nfs_reset_boot_verifier(inode)`. `nfs_local_probe_async_work()` must pin `clp` via `refcount_inc_not_zero(&clp->cl_count)`, and `nfs_local_probe()` must bracket `nfs_server_uuid_is_local(clp)` and `nfs_localio_enable_client(clp)` with `nfs_uuid_begin/end(&clp->cl_uuid)`. Localio I/O (`nfs_local_call_read/write`) queues on `nfslocaliod_workqueue` (no `WQ_MEM_RECLAIM`), while AIO completion defers `nfs_local_pgio_release()` and `nfs_local_vfs_getattr()` to `nfsiod_workqueue`. [F216, F217, F218, F259]
- `filelayout_check_deviceid()` and `ff_layout_init_mirror_ds()` must publish `fl->dsaddr` or `mirror->dss[dss_id].mirror_ds` via `cmpxchg(..., NULL, ...)`, dropping the ref on failure. In flexfilelayout, `dss_info->ro_cred/rw_cred` are read under `rcu_read_lock()` via `get_cred_rcu()`, and updating an existing mirror in `ff_layout_alloc_lseg()` must use `xchg()` and `put_cred()`. `flo->error_list` and `ff_layout->mirrors` take `lo->plh_inode->i_lock` outer to `mirror->lock` (`read_stat/write_stat`), and `pnfs_report_layoutstat()` must run after releasing `mirror->lock`. [F220, F221, F222]

## Object Lifetimes
- `__nfs_local_open_fh()` and `nfs_local_open_fh()` results must be released via `nfs_local_file_put()` (`nfs_to_nfsd_file_put_local()`) to drop both the `nfsd_file` and nfsd `net` refs. `nfs_file_set_open_context()` calls `get_nfs_open_context(ctx)`, so callers passing `alloc_nfs_open_context()` must still call `put_nfs_open_context(ctx)`. `nfs_destroy_inodecache()` must call `rcu_barrier()` before `kmem_cache_destroy(nfs_inode_cachep)`. [F185, F189, F224]
- In `nfs_get_root()`, once `fc->root = root` is set, error paths (`error_splat_root`) must `dput(fc->root)` and set `fc->root = NULL`, and `name` must be set to `NULL` when moved to `root->d_fsdata`. `nfs_fs_context_dup()` must allocate a fresh `ctx->mntfh`, call `get_nfs_version(ctx->nfs_mod)`, and NULL-clear all owned pointers freed by `nfs_fs_context_free()`; `nfs_fs_context_parse_param()` must `kfree()` any existing pointer in `ctx` before taking `param->string` and set `param->string = NULL`. [F194, F195, F196]
- `nfs_fs_context_validate()` must call `module_put()` on old `fc->fs_type->owner` and `__module_get()` on `ctx->nfs_mod->nfs_fs->owner` when updating `fc->fs_type`. Both `nfs_init_fs_context()` (when `fc->root` is non-NULL) and `nfs_d_automount()` must replace `fc->net_ns` with `client->cl_net` via `put_net()`/`get_net()` when `fc->net_ns != client->cl_net`. [F253, F352]
- `ff_layout_mirror_prepare_stats()` increments `mirror->ref` via `refcount_inc_not_zero()` and sets `layoutstat_ops` (requiring `opaque` to be `ld_private` in `struct nfs42_layoutstat_devinfo` and `opaque->data == &mirror->dss[dss_id]`); callers like `ff_layout_free_layoutreturn()` must call `ff_layout_free_iostats_array()` to drop each mirror ref. [F202, F316]

## API Contracts
- `nfs_netfs_init_request()` must check `!file` before `nfs_file_open_context(file)`, and `nfs_netfs_free_request()` must check `rreq->netfs_priv` before `put_nfs_open_context()`. `nfs_netfs_issue_read()` must call `nfs_netfs_put()` after `nfs_pageio_complete_read()` so `nfs_netfs_put()` clamps `sreq->transferred` to `sreq->len` and calls `netfs_read_subreq_terminated()` only when `refcount_dec_and_test(&netfs->refcount)` succeeds. [F223, F233, F346]
- When `netfs_inode(inode)->cache` is set, `nfs_fscache_init_inode()` must call `mapping_set_release_always(inode->i_mapping)` and `nfs_netfs_folio_unlock()` must return 0. In `nfs_fscache_release_folio()`, if `folio_test_private_2(folio)` is true, it must return `false` without waiting when `current_is_kswapd()` or `!(gfp & __GFP_FS)`, and otherwise call `folio_wait_private_2(folio)` before `fscache_note_page_release()`. [F347, F348]
- Pre-RPC errors in `filelayout_initiate_commit()` and `ff_layout_initiate_commit()` must call `pnfs_generic_prepare_to_resend_writes(data)` and `pnfs_generic_commit_release(data)`. In `nfs4_fl_select_ds_fh()` and `select_ds_fh_from_commit()`, `STRIPE_SPARSE` with `num_fh == 0` returns `NULL` to keep the MDS OPEN fh in `args.fh`. [F204, F237]
- In `filelayout_set_layoutcommit()` and `ff_layout_write_done_cb()`, `end_offs` must be `0` when `hdr->res.verf->committed == NFS_UNSTABLE`. In `nfs4_ff_layout_prepare_ds()`, `fail_return` must be `false` on read paths and `true` on write/commit paths. When `ff_layout_alloc_lseg()` calls `prepare_kernel_cred(&init_task)` with `!(gfp_flags & __GFP_FS)`, it must bracket it with `memalloc_nofs_save/restore()`. [F239, F240, F356]
- `nfs_path()` (and `nfs_devname()`) walks backwards from `buffer + buflen_in` under `read_seqbegin(&rename_lock)` and `rcu_read_lock()`, locking one `dentry->d_lock` at a time and holding the root `dentry->d_lock` across `read_seqretry(&rename_lock, seq)` and copying `base = dentry->d_fsdata`. It must set `*p = end` before prepending `base`, and callers must read from the returned pointer and free the original `buffer`. [F214, F310, F311]

## Error Handling
- In `nfs_net_init()`, failure of `nfs_fs_proc_net_init(net)` must unwind `rpc_proc_unregister(net, "nfs")` and `nfs_clients_exit(net)`. `nfs4_label_alloc()` returns `NULL` when unsupported and `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)` on failure, so callers (`nfs_alloc_fattr_with_label()`) must test `IS_ERR()`. `nfs_namespace_getattr/setattr()` must check `NFS_FH(d_inode(...))->size != 0` before `nfs_getattr/setattr()`. [F187, F235, F243]
- `mnt_xdr_dec_mountres/3()` return `0` on protocol/fh errors and set `res->errno`, so `nfs_mount()` must check both `status < 0` and `result.errno != 0`. In `nfs_local_pgio/commit_done()`, negative VFS errnos must be converted for `res.op_status` via `nfs_localio_errno_to_nfs4_stat(status)` while keeping the negative errno in `task.tk_status`. [F230, F244]
- `nfs4_ff_layout_prepare_ds()` and `ff_layout_choose/get_*_ds_for_read()` return `ERR_PTR` on failure; callers must check `IS_ERR(ds)`. In `ff_layout_choose_ds_for_read()`, if `check_device` rejects `ds`, `ds` must be reset to `ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)` before `continue`. In `ff_layout_read/write_pagelist()`, `IS_ERR(ds)` must set `ds_fatal_error = nfs_error_is_fatal(PTR_ERR(ds))` so `out_failed` returns `PNFS_NOT_ATTEMPTED` on fatal errors. [F225, F227, F282]
- `mirror->dss[dss_id].mirror_ds` can be `NULL`, `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)`, or valid: general accessors (`FF_LAYOUT_DEVID_NODE()`, `ff_layout_cancel_io()`, `ff_layout_track_ds_error()`, `ff_layout_mirror_prepare_stats()`) must use `IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`, whereas `ff_read/rw_layout_has_available_ds()` must treat `NULL` as available and only reject `IS_ERR(mirror_ds)`. In `ff_layout_async_handle_error_v4/v3()`, `op_status` must be switched on before `task->tk_status`. [F229, F280, F288]

## External Contracts
- `NFS_SB_MASK` must exclude `SB_RDONLY`. `nfs_fscache_update_auxdata()` must `memset(auxdata, 0, sizeof(*auxdata))` before setting fields. In `nfs23_parse_monolithic()`, legacy `nfs_mount_data` v1–5 must initialize missing fields, zero-pad `mntfh->data`, NUL-terminate `data->context`, and in `case 3` set `NFS_MOUNT_SECFLAVOUR` in `extra_flags` (not `data->flags`). [F274, F277, F287, F339]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `nfs_fhget()`, `nfs_attr_check_mountpoint()` must precede `nfs_attr_use_mounted_on_fileid()`, and `NFS_ATTR_FATTR_V4_REFERRAL` must be tested before `NFS_ATTR_FATTR_MOUNTPOINT`. In `nfs_ooo_merge()`, merging with `gap[i]` must replace `gap[i]` with `gap[--cnt]` and reset `i = -1`. [F285, F289]
- `nfs_inode_attrs_cmp_generic()` and `nfs_update_inode()` must compare `gencount` via `(long)(a - b) > 0` (plus wrap check against `nfs_read_attr_generation_counter()`); `nfs_inode_attrs_cmp_monotonic()` must use `s64 diff = fattr->change_attr - inode_peek_iversion_raw(inode)`; `nfs_attribute_timeout()` and `nfs_update_inode()` must use `time_in_range_open()`; `nfs_timespec_to_change_attr()` and `__nfsd4_change_attribute()` must cast `(u64)ts->tv_sec << 30`. [F298, F299, F300, F301]
- In `nfs_path()`, `buflen` must be `ssize_t` and checked `< 0` before adjusting `end`, `dentry_in`/`buflen_in` must not be modified, and `Elong_unlock` must check `read_seqretry(&rename_lock, seq)` before returning `-ENAMETOOLONG`. In `nfs_fscache_get_super_cookie()`, `key` needs `>= NFS_MAX_KEY_LEN + 18` bytes for `nfs_append_int()`. [F272, F283, F317]
- `nfs_page_array_len()` must widen `base` and `len` to `unsigned long` before `+ PAGE_SIZE - 1`. `nfs_local_run_commit()` must clamp `end < start` to `LLONG_MAX`. `param_set/get_nfs_timeout()` must clamp against `INT_MAX / HZ` and `INT_MAX - (HZ - 1)` and reschedule/cancel `nfs_automount_task`. [F264, F303, F305, F306]
- In `flexfilelayout`, `nfs4_ff_layout_calc_dss_id()` and `ff_layout_pg_test()` must guard against `stripe_unit == 0` when `dss_count == 1`. 64-bit stripe divisions must use `div_u64/div_u64_rem/do_div()`, and pNFS range ends must use `pnfs_calc_offset_end/pnfs_end_offset()`. `ff_lseg_match_mirrors()` must derive `fl1/fl2` from `l1/l2`; `nfs4_ff_layoutstat_start_io()` must cast to `s64` before `* 1000LL`; `decode_name()` must bound `p` by `len`. [F281, F293, F294, F295, F296, F318]
