# NETWORKING DRIVERS — drivers (part 2)

This component contains the Linux PTP (IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol) core framework and hardware/virtual clock drivers (`drivers/ptp/*`), alongside the IBM s390 Channel-to-Channel (CTC/MPC) mainframe network driver (`drivers/s390/net/ctcm_*`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct timestamp_event_queue` is a circular FIFO of `PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS` (128) slots with capacity `PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS - 1` (`queue_free(q)`) where `head`/`tail` wrap modulo `PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS` via `WRITE_ONCE` and `enqueue_external_timestamp()` advances `head` when full; it discriminates `src->type` (`src->timestamp` vs `src->offset`) and must overwrite `dst->flags = PTP_EXTTS_EVENT_VALID` (not `|=`) before setting `PTP_EXT_OFFSET`. [F905, F940]
- The initial `timestamp_event_queue` added to `ptp->tsevqs` in `ptp_clock_register()` must stay at the list head until `ptp_clock_release()`, while per-fd queues in `ptp_open()` use `list_add_tail()` and `pccontext->private_clkdata` (with `queue->dfs_bitmap.n_elements = DIV_ROUND_UP(PTP_MAX_CHANNELS, BITS_PER_BYTE * sizeof(u32))`). [F920, F981]
- In `ptp_read()`, `cnt` must be a multiple of `sizeof(struct ptp_extts_event)` (else `-EINVAL`) and capped to `EXTTS_BUFSIZE` before `kmalloc(EXTTS_BUFSIZE, GFP_KERNEL)`; in `ptp_mask_en_single()` and `ptp_clock_event()`, channel/index must satisfy `0 <= idx < PTP_MAX_CHANNELS` (2048) before `set_bit`/`test_bit` on `tsevq->mask`. [F941, F942]
- In `ptp_pin_getfunc()` and `ptp_pin_setfunc()`, `pd.index` must be checked `< ops->n_pins` and clamped with `array_index_nospec(pd.index, ops->n_pins)` before indexing `ops->pin_config`, `ptp_set_pinfunc()` must bound `chan` against `n_ext_ts`, `n_per_out`, or `0` (`PTP_PF_PHYSYNC`), and `ptp_populate_pin_groups()` must allocate `1 + n_pins` entries in `ptp->pin_attr` and keep `ptp->pin_attr_groups[1]` NULL. [F938, F944]
- Every child device under a physical `ptp->dev` must be a `ptp_vclock` (`unregister_vclock()` unconditionally calls `info_to_vclock(ptp->info)`); in `ptp_vclock_adjfine()`, `timecounter_read(&vclock->tc)` must run under `vclock->lock` before updating `vclock->cc.mult`; and in `ptp_clock_adjtime()` (`ADJ_SETOFFSET`), `ts.tv_nsec` must be cast to `unsigned long` and checked `< NSEC_PER_SEC` before `timespec64_add()`. [F921, F969, F986]
- In `ptp_dte.c`, `ptp_dte_settime()` must reset `ts_ovf_last` and `ts_wrap_cnt` to 0 under `ptp_dte->lock`, `dte_write_nco_delta()` / `dte_read_nco_with_ovf()` must update them in lockstep, and `ptp_dte_suspend()` must read all `DTE_NUM_REGS_TO_RESTORE` registers into `reg_val[]` before writing 0 to `DTE_NCO_INC_REG`. [F922, F997]
- In `ptp_fc3.c`, `idtfc3->ns` and `last_counter` must be updated together under `idtfc3->lock` in `idtfc3_timecounter_read()` (modulo `sub_sync_count`) and `idtfc3_timecounter_update()` (after `idtfc3_write_subcounter()` succeeds), and `ns2counters()` must compute negative `nsec` via `sync = -div_u64_rem(-nsec - 1, idtfc3->ns_per_sync, &rem) - 1` and `*sub_ns = idtfc3->ns_per_sync - rem - 1`. [F924, F971]
- While software phase pull-in (`channel->phase_pull_in` in `ptp_clockmatrix.c` or `channel->ddco` in `ptp_idt82p33.c`) is active, `current_freq_scaled_ppm` / `current_freq` holds the baseline frequency restored on stop, and `adjtime` must return `-EBUSY` while `adjfine` returns `0`. [F926]
- In `ptp_idt82p33.c`, `_idt82p33_gettime()` and `_idt82p33_settime()` must set `channel->discard_next_extts = true` and `_idt82p33_gettime()` must suspend/re-arm active `extts_mask` channels; ToD buffers are 10 bytes in `ptp_idt82p33.c` (`buf[0..3]` `tv_nsec`, `buf[4..9]` `tv_sec`) vs 11 bytes in `ptp_clockmatrix.c` (`buf[0]` sub-ns, `buf[1..4]` `tv_nsec`, `buf[5..10]` `tv_sec`). [F927, F947]
- In `idtcm_load_firmware()`, `idtfc3_load_firmware()`, and `idt82p33_load_firmware()`, iterate 4-byte records with signed `s32 len` (`len > 0; len -= sizeof(*rec)`), reject non-zero `rec->reserved` with `-EINVAL`, skip `regaddr < GPIO_USER_CONTROL || regaddr >= scratch` and `loaddr` in `0x7c..0x7f`/`0xfc..0xff` in ClockMatrix (or `loaddr > 0x7b` in 82P33), and resolve V520-relocated registers via `IDTCM_FW_REG(idtcm->fw_ver, V520, REG)`. [F945, F946, F1002]
- In `ptp_ines.c`, `ines_ptp_probe_channel()` must check `index <= INES_N_PORTS - 1` before `ines_find_port()`; `ines_find_txts()` and `ines_rxfifo_read()` must verify `DATA_READ_POS == 0` in `ts_stat_tx`/`ts_stat_rx` before reading 12-word frames; and `ines_rxts64()`/`ines_txts64()` must truncate each 32-bit read to `u16` before shifting left by 16. [F948, F949, F979]
- In `ptp_pch.c`, 64-bit timestamp registers (`systime_lo`, `rx_snap_lo`, `tx_snap_lo`, `asms_hi`) use 32-ns ticks and must be shifted by `TICKS_NS_SHIFT` (5) on read/write, and `pch_reset()` must follow `pch_block_reset()` with `pch_set_system_time_count()`. [F978, F1000]
- In `ctcm`, `ctcm_ch_alloc_buffer()` binds `ch->ccw[1]`'s CDA to `ch->trans_skb_data`, so reusing `ch->trans_skb` must reset `data = ch->trans_skb_data`, `skb_reset_tail_pointer()`, and `len = 0`; `chx_rx()` and `ctcm_unpack_skb()` must validate `8 <= len <= ch->max_bufsize`, `block_len <= len <= check_len`, `header->length > LL_HEADER_LENGTH`, `len > LL_HEADER_LENGTH`, and `header->length - LL_HEADER_LENGTH <= skb_tailroom(pskb)`. [F930, F951]
- In `ctcm`, non-MPC packets require `LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2` (8) bytes of framing overhead (`new_mtu <= max_bufsize - LL_HEADER_LENGTH - 2` and `ch->collect_len + l <= ch->max_bufsize - 2`) while MPC packets require `TH_HEADER_LENGTH + PDU_HEADER_LENGTH` headroom; in `enum ctc_ch_events` and `enum ctc_ch_states`, non-MPC entries must precede `CTC_NR_EVENTS`/`CTC_NR_STATES`, `CTC_EVENT_SEND_XID`/`CH_XID0_PENDING` must equal those sentinels, and `CTC_MPC_NR_*` must be last. [F952, F959]

## Synchronization
- `ptp_clock_event()` acquires `ptp->tsevqs_lock` and nests `queue->lock` from hardirq context, so process-context callers must use `spin_lock_irqsave()` and never acquire `tsevqs_lock` inside `queue->lock`. [F1008]
- Callers of `ptp_set_pinfunc()` and `ptp_find_pin()` must hold `ptp->pincfg_mux` (`.enable` and `.verify` callbacks must call `ptp_find_pin()`, not `ptp_find_pin_unlocked()`), and `ptp_set_pinfunc()` must return `0` on self-assignment (`pin1 && i == pin`) without calling `ptp_disable_pinfunc()` while disabling and resetting any other `pin1` holding `(func, chan)`. [F875, F1012]
- `ptp_clock_unregister()` must never be called while holding a driver mutex acquired by `.enable` or `.do_aux_work` (`idtcm->lock`, `idt82p33->lock`, `idtfc3->lock`); `ptp_vclock_in_use()` must return `false` without locking `n_vclocks_mux` when `ptp->is_virtual_clock` is true (and virtual clocks must use `PTP_LOCK_VIRTUAL` on `clock.rwsem`). [F890, F1011]
- `ptp_kvm_get_time_fn()` and `ptp_kvm_gettime()` must hold `kvm_ptp_lock` across `kvm_arch_ptp_get_crosststamp()` and `kvm_arch_ptp_get_clock()`, with `preempt_disable_notrace()` wrapping `kvm_arch_ptp_get_crosststamp()`. [F1014]
- In `ctcm_transmit_skb()`, `ch->collect_lock` must be held across `fsm_getstate(ch->fsm) != CTC_STATE_TXIDLE` and `collect_queue` enqueue, pairing with `chx_txdone()` checking `ch->collect_len > 0` under `ch->collect_lock` before setting `CTC_STATE_TXIDLE`. [F898]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `ptp_clock_register()`, once `device_initialize(&ptp->dev)` sets `ptp->dev.release = ptp_clock_release`, any subsequent failure must clean up via `put_device(&ptp->dev)` rather than pre-device error labels; `ptp_clock_unregister()` must pin `ptp->dev` with `get_device()`, set `ptp->defunct = 1`, wake `ptp->tsev_wq`, call `posix_clock_unregister()`, disable events, and stop `ptp->aux_work`/`ptp->kworker` before the final `put_device(&ptp->dev)`. [F871, F893]
- Under `ptp->n_vclocks_mux`, `n_vclocks_store()` must unregister excess virtual clocks via `device_for_each_child_reverse()` and reset `ptp->vclock_index` slots to `-1`, while `max_vclocks_store()` must verify `0 < max <= PTP_MAX_VCLOCKS_LIMIT` and `max >= ptp->n_vclocks` before reallocating; `ptp_clock_index_by_of_node()` and `ptp_clock_index_by_dev()` must match `dev->parent` and call `put_device(dev)`. [F876, F939, F995]
- `ines_ptp_ctrl_probe()` must finish `ines_clock_init()` before `register_mii_tstamp_controller()` and adding `clock->list` under `ines_clocks_lock`, and `ines_ptp_ctrl_remove()` must unregister and unlink `clock->list` under `ines_clocks_lock` before `ines_clock_cleanup()`; in `ptp_pch.c`, `ptp_clock_register()` must precede `request_irq()` in `pch_probe()`, and `free_irq()` must precede `ptp_clock_unregister()` on teardown. [F878, F896]
- In `ptp_kvm_x86.c` under `CC_ATTR_GUEST_MEM_ENCRYPT`, once `set_memory_decrypted()` succeeds on `clock_pair`, `kvm_arch_ptp_exit()` (and init rollback) must call `set_memory_encrypted()` before `free_page()` and clear `clock_pair = NULL`. [F879]
- In `ctcm`, SKBs on a write channel's `collect_queue` or `io_queue` have `skb->users` incremented, so `chx_txdone()`, `ctcmpc_chx_txdone()`, and `ctcm_purge_skb_queue()` must call `refcount_dec(&skb->users)` before freeing; callers of `ctcm_checkalloc_buffer(ch)` must reload any local `skb = ch->trans_skb` pointer afterward. [F882, F990]
- `struct ctcm_priv` is allocated in `ctcm_probe_device()` (with `get_device(&cgdev->dev)`) and freed only in `ctcm_remove_device()` (with `put_device(&cgdev->dev)`), so `ctcm_free_netdevice()` and `ctcm_shutdown_device()` must never `kfree(priv)` and must null out `mpcg`, `fsm`, `xid`, and `channel[]`; if `ctcm_register_dbf_views()` fails, it must call `ctcm_unregister_dbf_views()`, which unregisters handles and resets `ctcm_dbf[x].id = NULL`. [F885, F886]

## API Contracts
- Drivers must pass per-instance copies of `ptp_clock_info` and `pin_config` to `ptp_clock_register()` (which mutates callbacks and `pin_config[]` in place), implement `getmaxphase` when `adjphase` is set, and provide `pin_config` and `verify` when `n_pins > 0`. [F919, F1021]
- `ptp_schedule_worker()` and `ptp_cancel_worker_sync()` require `info->do_aux_work != NULL` at registration; `do_aux_work` returns a `long` delay `>= 0` to re-queue `ptp->aux_work` or `< 0` (`-1`) to stop; and `ptp_find_pin*()`, `ptp_clock_index_by_*()`, and `ptp_pin_name2index()` return `-1` on failure, requiring a signed `int` check `< 0`. [F903, F904, F1023]
- In `idtcm_enable()`, `idtfc3_enable()`, and `idt82p33_enable()`, `PTP_CLK_REQ_PEROUT` must handle `!on` before checking that `rq->perout.period` is 1 second; in `ptp_ines.c`, `ines_txtstamp()` always consumes `skb` (and `ines_txtstamp_work()` clears `port->tx_skb = NULL`), while `ines_rxtstamp()` consumes `skb` via `netif_rx()` only when returning `true`; and `pch_set_station_address()` returns positive `PCH_INVALIDPARAM` (`1`) on error, requiring `!= 0` checks. [F877, F911, F957]
- In `ctcm`, `ch->ccw`, `ch->trans_skb`, `sweep_skb`, and TX bounce buffers for SKBs reaching 2 GiB (`>> 31`) must use `GFP_DMA`; freeing `ch->trans_skb` or reprogramming a `ccw1` must call `clear_normalized_cda()` (and `ctcm_chx_start()` must clear `cda` and `CCW_FLAG_IDA` on `ch->ccw[4]`); and `fsm_event()`/`fsm_addtimer()` must pass `struct channel *` to `ch->fsm` and `struct net_device *` to `priv->fsm`. [F884, F991, F1016]

## Error Handling
- `idtfc3_read_subcounter()` and `get_output_base_addr()` return negative error codes in a signed `int`, so callers must check `< 0` (or `base > 0`) before casting to unsigned; `kvm_arch_ptp_init()` must return `-EOPNOTSUPP` when hypervisor PTP is unavailable so `ptp_kvm_init()` exits silently. [F909, F910, F1029]
- `ctcm_irq_handler()` must check `ctcm_check_irb_error(cdev, irb)` before dereferencing `irb` (which may be an `ERR_PTR`); if `ctcm_transmit_skb()` fails after `skb_push(LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2)` and `refcount_inc(&skb->users)`, it must `skb_pull(skb, LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2)`, undo the refcount/queueing, and call `ctcm_clear_busy(ch->netdev)`. [F912, F914]

## External Contracts
- In `ptp_ioctl()`, compat syscalls must apply `compat_ptr(arg)` to all commands except scalar `PTP_ENABLE_PPS` and `PTP_ENABLE_PPS2`; hardware-mutating ioctls require `FMODE_WRITE` (`-EACCES`) and PPS toggling requires `capable(CAP_SYS_TIME)` (`-EPERM`); and V2 ioctls (`PTP_*_REQUEST2`, `PTP_PIN_*FUNC2`) must reject unknown flags and non-zero `rsv` words with `-EINVAL` while V1 masks flags and zeroes `rsv`. [F943, F1026, F1028]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `ptp_dte.c`, `dte_read_nco()` must cast `sum3` and `sum2` to `s64` before shifting by `DTE_NCO_SUM3_SHIFT` (36) and `DTE_NCO_SUM2_SHIFT` (4), writing `sum3` to `DTE_NCO_OVERFLOW_REG` requires masking with `DTE_NCO_SUM3_MASK` and shifting left by `DTE_NCO_SUM3_WR_SHIFT` (8), and `DTE_PPB_ADJ(ppb)` must cast `abs(ppb)` to `u64` before `* BIT(28)` and `div64_u64(..., 125000000ULL)`. [F966, F968, F996]
- `idt82p33_set_tod_trigger()` must preserve the other 4-bit nibble (`READ_TRIGGER_MASK`/`WRITE_TRIGGER_MASK`) in `channel->dpll_tod_trigger`; `_idtcm_set_dpll_hw_tod()` must write `HW_DPLL_TOD_OVR__0` after clearing `0x08` in `HW_DPLL_TOD_CTRL_1` when `wr_trig == HW_TOD_WR_TRIG_SEL_MSB`, but before clearing `0x08` otherwise. [F928, F929]
- In `idtcm_load_firmware()` and `idtfc3_load_firmware()`, `check_and_set_masks()` / `idtfc3_set_hw_param()` must return `-EINVAL` for bad metadata and `-EFAULT` for ordinary registers, and the loop must advance `rec++` and reset `err = 0` before any `continue`; in `idtcm_extts_enable()` and `idt82p33_extts_enable()`, `ptp_find_pin()` and `event_channel[index]` use the requesting `channel`, whereas trigger arming targets `&idtcm->channel[index]` / `&idt82p33->channel[index]`. [F961, F964]
- In `idtcm_get_dco_delay()`, `mbuf` must be `u8 mbuf[8] = {0}` for `get_unaligned_le64(mbuf)`, `n` clamped to 1 when 0, and `fodFreq >= 500000000` checked before dividing `18 * (u64)NSEC_PER_SEC`; in `idtfc3_get_tdc_apll_freq()`, mask `tdc_ref_div` with `TDC_REF_DIV_CONFIG_MASK` before shifting and cast `tdc_fb_div_int` to `u64` before multiplying. [F974, F975, F987]
- In `ctcm_irq_handler()` and `ccw_unit_check()`, test composite status bits (`DEV_STAT_BUSY`+`DEV_STAT_ATTENTION`, `SNS0_EQUIPMENT_CHECK`+`SNS0_BUS_OUT_CHECK`) before individual bits, and parenthesize `CTCM_ID_SIZE` (`20+3`) in multiplicative/subtractive expressions. [F958, F992]
