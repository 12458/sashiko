# NFC SUBSYSTEM — net/nfc (part 1)

This component implements core protocol stacks and controller interfaces for the Linux NFC subsystem: the Logical Link Control Protocol (LLCP) and its AF_NFC socket layer, the NFC Digital Protocol stack (NFC-DEP and RF technology polling/anti-collision), the Host Controller Interface (HCI) with SHDLC/NOP link layers, and the NFC Controller Interface (NCI) with its SPI and UART (tty line discipline) transport helpers. It sits between low-level NFC hardware drivers and upper-layer NFC core/socket APIs, handling framing, state machines, flow control, fragmentation/reassembly, and command/response serialization.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `nfc_llcp_sock_alloc` initializes `llcp_sock->reserved_ssap` to `LLCP_SAP_MAX`, and `nfc_llcp_recv_connect` only sets `new_sock->reserved_ssap = sock->ssap` for SDP SAPs (`LLCP_WKS_NUM_SAP <= sock->ssap < LLCP_LOCAL_NUM_SAP`), so `llcp_sock_release` must check `llcp_sock->reserved_ssap < LLCP_SAP_MAX` before calling `nfc_llcp_put_ssap`. [F241]
- In struct llc_shdlc, arming or stopping t1_timer or t2_timer must update t1_active or t2_active in lockstep, and each sent I-frame queued on ack_pending_q must store its jiffies timestamp in skb->cb because llc_shdlc_sm_work detects timer expiration via t1_active/t2_active && !timer_pending() and llc_shdlc_reset_t2 re-arms t2_timer from skb->cb. [F246]
- When moving unacknowledged frames from shdlc->ack_pending_q back to shdlc->send_q for retransmission, llc_shdlc_requeue_ack_pending must dequeue from the tail of ack_pending_q, strip the 1-byte SHDLC control header pushed by llc_shdlc_handle_send_queue, queue at the head of send_q, and reset shdlc->ns to shdlc->dnr. [F247]
- In struct nfc_hci_dev, setting hdev->cmd_pending_msg must be accompanied by arming hdev->cmd_timer under hdev->msg_tx_mutex, and __nfc_hci_cmd_completion must stop cmd_timer, free and clear cmd_pending_msg, and schedule msg_tx_work because nfc_hci_msg_tx_work treats cmd_pending_msg != NULL with !timer_pending(&hdev->cmd_timer) as a command timeout. [F248]
- Both standalone HCI (struct nfc_hci_dev) and HCI-over-NCI (struct nci_hci_dev) maintain bidirectional gate-to-pipe mappings in gate2pipe[] and pipes[], which must be updated together when connecting, creating, or resetting pipes (nfc_hci_connect_gate, nfc_hci_cmd_received, nfc_hci_reset_pipes, and their nci_hci_* counterparts). [F249]
- Adding a child socket to a listener's accept_queue in nfc_llcp_accept_enqueue must call sock_hold(sk), set llcp_sock->parent, and call sk_acceptq_added(parent), while removing it in nfc_llcp_accept_dequeue or nfc_llcp_accept_unlink must unlink accept_queue, call sk_acceptq_removed(parent), and call sock_put(sk). [F252]
- Dynamic SDP SAPs (LLCP_WKS_NUM_SAP <= ssap < LLCP_LOCAL_NUM_SAP) are refcounted across a listener and its accepted sockets in local->local_sdp_cnt[ssap - LLCP_WKS_NUM_SAP], and nfc_llcp_put_ssap only clears local->local_sdp and resets the listener's ssap to LLCP_SDP_UNBOUND when atomic_dec_and_test(client_cnt) is true. [F253]
- When the LLCP MAC link goes down, nfc_llcp_mac_is_down must reset local->remote_miu to LLCP_DEFAULT_MIU and local->remote_lto to LLCP_DEFAULT_LTO and call nfc_llcp_socket_release(local, true, 0) so that active connection/bound sockets are closed while local->raw_sockets remain open across link cycles. [F255]

## Synchronization

- In `nci_uart_set_driver`, `nu->tty`, `nu->tx_q`, `nu->write_work`, and `nu->rx_lock` must be initialized, `nu->ops.open(nu)` must succeed, and `try_module_get(nu->owner)` must be acquired before `nu` is published to `tty->disc_data`. [F203]
- `nfc_llcp_register_device` must initialize `local->ref`, `sdp_lock`, `sdreq_lock`, socket list rwlocks (`sockets.lock`, `connecting_sockets.lock`, `raw_sockets.lock`), timers, and work structs before linking `local->list` into `llcp_devices` under `llcp_devices_lock`. [F205]
- `nci_open_device` (and the reset phase of `nci_close_device`) must set `NCI_INIT` in `ndev->flags` and initialize `ndev->cmd_cnt` to 1 before issuing any `__nci_request` or driver `init`/`setup` callbacks, because `nci_recv_frame` drops incoming frames unless `NCI_UP` or `NCI_INIT` is set. [F206]
- `nci_unregister_device` must set `NCI_UNREG` in `ndev->flags` and call `nci_close_device` before calling `destroy_workqueue` on `cmd_wq`, `rx_wq`, and `tx_wq`, and both `nci_open_device` (on error) and `nci_close_device` must preserve `BIT(NCI_UNREG)` when clearing `ndev->flags` under `ndev->req_lock`. [F208]
- `nfc_hci_unregister_device` must set `hdev->shutting_down = true` and complete any `hdev->cmd_pending_msg` under `hdev->msg_tx_mutex`, then call `timer_delete_sync(&hdev->cmd_timer)`, `cancel_work_sync(&hdev->msg_tx_work)`, and `cancel_work_sync(&hdev->msg_rx_work)` before calling `nfc_unregister_device` and freeing `msg_tx_queue`. [F210]
- `nfc_llcp_unregister_device` must remove `local` from `llcp_devices` under `llcp_devices_lock` via `nfc_llcp_remove_local` before calling `local_cleanup`, and `local_cleanup` must call `timer_delete_sync` on `link_timer` and `sdreq_timer` before calling `cancel_work_sync` on `tx_work`, `rx_work`, `timeout_work`, and `sdreq_timeout_work`. [F211]
- `nfc_llcp_find_local` must hold `llcp_devices_lock` continuously across traversing `llcp_devices` and acquiring the reference on the matched `struct nfc_llcp_local` via `nfc_llcp_local_get`, and callers must release that reference with `nfc_llcp_local_put`. [F212]
- `nci_request` must hold `ndev->req_lock` across checking `test_bit(NCI_UP, &ndev->flags)` and executing `__nci_request` so that only one NCI control request is pending on `ndev->req_completion` at a time and cannot start after `nci_close_device` clears `NCI_UP`. [F213]

## Object Lifetimes

- `struct nci_conn_info` (and its `dest_params`) in `ndev->conn_info_list` are allocated with `devm_kzalloc(&ndev->nfc_dev->dev, ...)`, and when closing a connection in `nci_core_conn_close_rsp_packet`, if `conn_info == ndev->rf_conn_info`, `ndev->rf_conn_info` must be cleared to `NULL` before `devm_kfree(&ndev->nfc_dev->dev, conn_info)`. [F194]
- Whenever `ndev->rx_data_reassembly` is freed (on `skb_cow_head` failure or reassembly completion in `nci_add_rx_data_frag`, or on RF deactivation in `nci_rf_deactivate_ntf_packet`), `ndev->rx_data_reassembly` must be set to `NULL`. [F195]
- `ddev->saved_skb` and `ddev->chaining_skb` hold active NFC-DEP retry and chaining buffers across async callbacks and must both be freed with `kfree_skb` and set to `NULL` on completion or error in `digital_in_recv_dep_res`, `digital_tg_recv_dep_req`, `digital_in_send_dep_req`, and `digital_tg_send_dep_res`. [F201]
- During NFC-A polling, the `struct nfc_target *target` allocated in `digital_in_recv_sens_res` is passed as `cb_context` across `digital_in_send_sdd_req`, `digital_in_send_sel_req`, and `digital_in_send_rats`, so `digital_in_recv_sdd_res` and `digital_in_recv_sel_res` must only free `target` on terminal completion or error. [F202]
- Because `nfc_hci_execute_cmd` allocates `ew_wq` and `struct hcp_exec_waiter hcp_ew` on the stack, it must wait with uninterruptible `wait_event(ew_wq, hcp_ew.exec_complete == true)`, and `nfc_hci_execute_cb` must set `hcp_ew->exec_complete = true` only after storing `exec_result` and `result_skb`. [F225]
- An nfc_llcp_sock in state LLCP_CONNECTED holds an active nfc_dev reference on llcp_sock->dev (acquired in llcp_sock_connect or nfc_llcp_recv_connect), so any transition out of LLCP_CONNECTED (in nfc_llcp_socket_release or nfc_llcp_recv_disc) or destruction while still LLCP_CONNECTED (in llcp_sock_destruct) must call nfc_put_device. [F251]
- In NFC-DEP retransmission paths (digital_in_send_saved_skb and digital_tg_send_saved_skb), skb_get(ddev->saved_skb) must be called before passing ddev->saved_skb to digital_in_send_cmd or digital_tg_send_cmd, and ddev->saved_skb must be freed and nulled once the PDU is acknowledged or the exchange aborts. [F265]
- `llc_shdlc_connect` stores the address of its stack-allocated `connect_wq` in `shdlc->connect_wq` without clearing it on return, so `llc_shdlc_connect` must wait uninterruptibly and `llc_shdlc_connect_complete` must only be called while in `SHDLC_CONNECTING` or `SHDLC_NEGOTIATING`. [F320]

## API Contracts

- `nfc_llcp_send_snl_sdres` and `nfc_llcp_send_snl_sdreq` only consume and unlink `struct nfc_llcp_sdp_tlv` entries from `tlv_list` when `nfc_llcp_allocate_snl` succeeds; if `nfc_llcp_allocate_snl` returns an `ERR_PTR`, `tlv_list` remains untouched. [F183]
- In `llcp_sock_recvmsg`, after `skb_recv_datagram` dequeues `skb`, if `skb_copy_datagram_msg` fails with `-EFAULT` or if a `SOCK_STREAM`, `SOCK_DGRAM`, or `SOCK_RAW` socket has unconsumed bytes (`skb->len > 0` after `skb_pull`) when `MSG_PEEK` is not set, `skb` must be re-queued at the head of `sk->sk_receive_queue` rather than freed. [F185]
- `nfc_llc_xmit_from_hci` has asymmetric SKB ownership across failure: in `nfc_hci_msg_tx_work`, when `nfc_llc_xmit_from_hci` returns `< 0`, the caller frees `skb`, so `xmit_from_hci` implementations (`llc_nop_xmit_from_hci` / `ops->xmit`, `llc_shdlc_xmit_from_hci`) must not free `skb` when returning a negative error. [F191]
- `nci_send_data` always consumes the `skb` passed to it—queuing it onto `ndev->tx_q`, freeing it after copying into fragments in `nci_queue_tx_data_frags`, or freeing it at `free_exit` on error—so callers (`nci_transceive`, `nci_tm_send`, `nci_hci_send_data`) must never free `skb` even when `nci_send_data` returns an error. [F196]
- In `nci_uart_default_recv_buf`, once a complete packet is assembled (`nu->rx_packet_len == nu->rx_skb->len`), `nu->ops.recv(nu, nu->rx_skb)` is called and `nu->rx_skb` is unconditionally set to `NULL`, transferring ownership of `rx_skb` to `nu->ops.recv` regardless of its return value. [F198]
- In `nfc_hci_event_received`, `hdev->ops->event_received` must return `<= 0` when it handles the event and consumes `skb` (where `< 0` triggers `nfc_hci_driver_failure(hdev, r)` at `exit_noskb:`), or `> 0` (`1`) when it leaves `skb` unconsumed for standard HCI event processing and `kfree_skb(skb)` at `exit:`. [F230]
- In `hci_transceive`, `hdev->ops->im_transceive` must return `<= 0` when it handles the transmission or `1` to request fallback to standard `NFC_HCI_WR_XCHG_DATA` command transmission (converted to `-ENOTSUPP` on non-Reader-A/B gates), and must never free `skb` because `hci_transceive` unconditionally calls `kfree_skb(skb)`. [F231]
- `nfc_hci_create_pipe` and `nci_hci_create_pipe` return `u8` pipe IDs with `NFC_HCI_INVALID_PIPE` / `NCI_HCI_INVALID_PIPE` (`0x80`) as the error sentinel while storing the command status in `*result`, and `nci_hci_create_pipe` also maps `pipe >= NCI_HCI_MAX_PIPES` to `NCI_HCI_INVALID_PIPE`. [F234]

## Error Handling

- In `nfc_hci_connect_gate` and `nci_hci_connect_gate`, if a dynamic pipe was created via `nfc_hci_create_pipe` or `nci_hci_create_pipe` (`pipe_created == true`) and opening the pipe subsequently fails, the newly created pipe must be deleted via `nfc_hci_delete_pipe` or `nci_hci_delete_pipe` before returning the error. [F187]
- In `net/nfc/digital_dep.c` and `net/nfc/digital_technology.c`, `nfc_digital_cmd_complete_t` callbacks receive `resp` as an `ERR_PTR` on failure and must check `IS_ERR(resp)` and set `resp = NULL` before jumping to cleanup labels that call `dev_kfree_skb(resp)` or `kfree_skb(resp)`. [F226]
- `digital_recv_dep_data_gather` returns `ERR_PTR(rc)` on failure (having freed `resp` and `ddev->chaining_skb`), `NULL` when `DIGITAL_NFC_DEP_MI_BIT_SET(pfb)` chaining is still in progress, or the complete `struct sk_buff *`, so `digital_in_recv_dep_res` and `digital_tg_recv_dep_req` must handle `IS_ERR(resp)` and `!resp` separately. [F227]
- `digital_send_dep_data_prep` returns `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)` on failure, a newly allocated fragment SKB when `skb->len > ddev->remote_payload_max` (storing `skb` in `ddev->chaining_skb`), or `skb` itself otherwise, so `digital_in_send_dep_req` and `digital_tg_send_dep_res` must only `kfree_skb(tmp_skb)` on send failure when `tmp_skb != skb`. [F228]
- In `digital_tg_recv_dep_req`, `nfc_tm_data_received` consumes `resp` on both success and error (via `nfc_llcp_data_received`), so `digital_tg_recv_dep_req` must clear `resp = NULL` when `nfc_tm_data_received` returns non-zero before falling through to `exit:` where `if (rc) kfree_skb(resp);` executes. [F229]
- In `nfc_hci_execute_cb` and `nfc_hci_execute_cmd`, `skb` is saved in `hcp_ew->result_skb` only when `err == 0` (and freed in `nfc_hci_execute_cb` when `err != 0`), and `nfc_hci_execute_cmd` only populates `*skb` when `hcp_ew.exec_result == 0` and `skb` is non-NULL (freeing `hcp_ew.result_skb` if `skb == NULL`). [F232]
- In `__nci_request`, `ndev->req_result` is mapped through `nci_to_errno(ndev->req_result)` when `ndev->req_status == NCI_REQ_DONE` (completed via `nci_req_complete`), but is negated as `-ndev->req_result` when `ndev->req_status == NCI_REQ_CANCELED` (canceled via `nci_req_cancel`, which requires a positive errno argument). [F236]
- In `net/nfc/llcp_commands.c`, `llcp_allocate_pdu` returns `NULL` on error whereas `nfc_llcp_allocate_snl` returns an `ERR_PTR` (`-ENODEV` or `-ENOMEM`), so callers of `llcp_allocate_pdu` must check `skb == NULL` while callers of `nfc_llcp_allocate_snl` (`nfc_llcp_send_snl_sdres`, `nfc_llcp_send_snl_sdreq`) must check `IS_ERR(skb)`. [F244]

## External Contracts

- llcp_sock_bind() and llcp_raw_sock_bind() accept any sockaddr length alen >= offsetofend(struct sockaddr, sa_family) by zero-initializing a local struct sockaddr_nfc_llcp and copying min(sizeof(llcp_addr), alen) bytes, whereas llcp_sock_connect() and SOCK_DGRAM llcp_sock_sendmsg() require at least sizeof(struct sockaddr_nfc_llcp). [F271]
- llcp_sock_bind() requires llcp_addr.dsap to be 0 (returning -EINVAL otherwise) because a bound socket acts as a local endpoint/listener, whereas llcp_sock_connect() requires full sizeof(struct sockaddr_nfc_llcp) and at least one of addr->service_name_len or addr->dsap to be non-zero. [F365]
- Creating an AF_NFC LLCP socket with type SOCK_RAW in llcp_sock_create() requires CAP_NET_RAW (returning -EPERM if !capable(CAP_NET_RAW)) and binds the socket to llcp_rawsock_ops, which rejects connect, accept, listen, sendmsg, setsockopt, and getsockopt. [F367]

## Known Pitfalls

- In nfc_hci_recv_from_llc and nci_hci_data_received_cb, the struct hcp_packet / nci_hcp_packet pointer must be refreshed from hcp_skb->data after fragment reassembly because reassembly purges rx_hcp_frags (freeing the incoming skb) and places the HCP message header at the start of the newly allocated hcp_skb. [F250]
- In `llc_shdlc_handle_rcv_queue`, switching on `control & SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_MASK` (`0xe0`) must handle both `SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_I` (`0x80`) and `SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_I2` (`0xa0`) for I-frames because bit 5 (`0x20`) is the most significant bit of the 3-bit `N(S)` sequence number (`SHDLC_CONTROL_NS_MASK`). [F289]
- In `nfc_hci_recv_from_llc` and `nci_hci_data_received_cb`, `NFC_HCI_FRAGMENT` and `NCI_HCI_FRAGMENT` (`0x7f`) mask the 7-bit pipe ID while `~NFC_HCI_FRAGMENT` (`0x80`) is the final-fragment chaining bit, so unfragmented packets must have `packet->header` masked with `NFC_HCI_FRAGMENT` (`NCI_HCI_FRAGMENT`) before dispatch. [F290]
- In `nfc_llcp_sdreq_timeout_work` and `nfc_llcp_send_snl_sdreq`, iterating over an `hlist_head` while moving `struct nfc_llcp_sdp_tlv` entries to another list via `hlist_del` and `hlist_add_head` must use `hlist_for_each_entry_safe`. [F299]
- In `nfc_llcp_connecting_sock_get`, because `llcp_sock = nfc_llcp_sock(sk)` is assigned on every iteration of `sk_for_each` and a match jumps to `out:`, `llcp_sock` must be explicitly reset to `NULL` after the loop when no socket matches `ssap`. [F300]
- In nci_activate_target, protocol (__u32) must be checked against NFC_PROTO_MAX (8) before evaluating (1 << protocol) against nci_target->supported_protocols. [F309]
- In net/nfc/digital_dep.c, arithmetic on the 2-bit NFC-DEP packet number ddev->curr_nfc_dep_pni or incoming pfb (such as curr_nfc_dep_pni + 1, curr_nfc_dep_pni - 1, or pfb + 1) must be masked with DIGITAL_NFC_DEP_PFB_PNI() (& 0x03) before storing or comparing. [F312]
