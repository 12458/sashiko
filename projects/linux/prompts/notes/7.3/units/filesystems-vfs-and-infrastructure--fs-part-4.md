# FILESYSTEMS (VFS and infrastructure) — fs (part 4)

Implements mount namespaces, mount trees, and shared-subtree propagation (`fs/namespace.c`, `fs/pnode.c`, `fs/pnode.h`, `fs/proc_namespace.c`), file opening/closing/truncation (`fs/open.c`), anonymous and FIFO pipes (`fs/pipe.c`), POSIX Access Control Lists (`fs/posix_acl.c`), and the `nsfs` and `pidfs` pseudo-filesystems (`fs/nsfs.c`, `fs/pidfs.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `struct mount::mnt_ns` can be `NULL`, `MNT_NS_INTERNAL` (`ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)`), or a `struct mnt_namespace *`; test `is_mounted()` (`!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(mnt->mnt_ns)`) rather than `if (mnt->mnt_ns)` before dereferencing `mnt->mnt_ns`. In `do_move_mount()` and `clone_private_mount()`, test `check_mnt()`: require `anon_ns_root()` only when `!check_mnt()`, and `mnt_has_parent()`/`!IS_MNT_LOCKED()` only when moving a mount in the caller's namespace. [F238, F292]
- In `attach_recursive_mnt()`, for each `child` in `tree_list`, look up any existing covering mount `q` via `__lookup_mnt()`, call `commit_tree(child)`, and (when propagating/copying across `user_namespace`s, as in `copy_mnt_ns()`) run `lock_mnt_tree(child)` to set `MNT_LOCK_*`/`MNT_LOCKED` before `mnt_change_mountpoint()` reparents `q` onto `topmost_overmount(child)`. [F252, F285, F349]
- In `propagate_umount()`, call `reparent(m->overmount)` only when `!will_be_unmounted(over)`. In the first pass (`trim_one()`), `trim_ancestors()` must only clear `T_UMOUNT_CANDIDATE` without unlinking from `candidates`; the second pass (`handle_locked()`) unlinks ancestor chains and must drain `candidates` with `while (!list_empty(&candidates))`. [F250, F287]
- When adding to a peer group whose member is `IS_MNT_SLAVE`, `clone_mnt()` and `do_set_group()` must insert `mnt_slave` immediately after the peer via `hlist_add_behind()`. `propagation_next()` and `next_group()` must skip `mnt_slave_list` only on `IS_MNT_NEW(m)`, while `is_anon_ns(m->mnt_ns)` mounts are skipped only as secondary copy targets in `need_secondary()`. [F253, F288]
- `count_mounts()` stages counts in `ns->pending_mounts` (subtracting `ns->nr_mounts` and `ns->pending_mounts` from `max = READ_ONCE(sysctl_mount_max)` step by step before `mounts > max`), which must be folded into `ns->nr_mounts` and zeroed in `commit_tree()` or cleared on `attach_recursive_mnt()` rollback. `propagate_mnt()`, `bulk_make_private()`, and `propagate_umount()` must clear `T_MARKED` and `T_UMOUNT_CANDIDATE` in `mnt->mnt_t_flags` before returning. [F254, F257, F310]
- Moving an attached mount in `attach_recursive_mnt()` or `pivot_root()` requires `list_del_init(&mnt->mnt_expire)`, and `pivot_root()` must transfer `MNT_LOCKED` from `root_mnt` to `new_mnt`. In `path_mount()`, `(flags & (MS_REMOUNT | MS_BIND)) == (MS_REMOUNT | MS_BIND)` (`do_reconfigure_mnt()`) must precede `MS_REMOUNT` (`do_remount()`) and `MS_BIND` (`do_loopback()`). `fs_opts` (`show_sb_opts`) and `mnt_opts` (`show_vfsmnt_opts`) must end with `{ 0, NULL }`. [F259, F284, F294]
- A pipe's `ring_size` must be a power of two `<= (pipe_index_t)-1u`: `round_pipe_size()` must reject `size > (1U << 31)` before `roundup_pow_of_two(size)` and clamp `< PAGE_SIZE` to `PAGE_SIZE`, and `pipe_resize_ring()` must reject `nr_slots > (pipe_index_t)-1u`. Because `pipe->head` and `pipe->tail` wrap unmasked, occupancy/empty/full checks must cast `(pipe_index_t)(head - tail)` via `pipe_occupancy()`, `pipe_empty()`, and `pipe_full()`. [F278, F304, F305, F306]
- Cached inode `struct posix_acl` objects are shared and store `kuid_t`/`kgid_t` in `inode->i_sb->s_user_ns` (applying `mnt_idmap` only in `vfs_set_acl_idmapped_mnt()`, `vfs_posix_acl_to_xattr()`, and `posix_acl_permission()`); `__posix_acl_create()`, `__posix_acl_chmod()`, and `posix_acl_create()` must `posix_acl_clone()` before mutating in place. `posix_acl_valid()` enforces strict tag order (`ACL_USER_OBJ`..`ACL_OTHER`), which `posix_acl_permission()` relies on when scanning forward to `ACL_MASK` and checking `if (found) return -EACCES;` at `ACL_OTHER`. [F266, F301, F360]
- `access_need_override_creds()` must match `access_override_creds()` on `fsuid`, `fsgid`, and `cap_effective`. In `pidfs_add_pid`, `pidfs_ino_nr` starts at 2 and must advance by 2 when `pidfs_ino(pidfs_ino_nr)` wraps to 0 on 32-bit, where 64-bit `pid->ino` splits into `i_ino` and `i_generation`. [F269, F276]

## Synchronization

- Lock order for mounting is `inode_lock(dentry->d_inode)` -> `namespace_lock()` (`namespace_sem`) -> `mount_lock`. In `do_lock_mount()`, evaluate `where_to_mount()` under `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)` with `m->mnt` and `dentry` pinned (`mntget()`/`dget()` when `&m->mnt != path->mnt`) before taking `inode_lock` and `namespace_lock`, and revalidate under `mount_locked_reader` after; `__unlock_mount()` must release `inode_unlock()` and `unpin_mountpoint()` (under `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)`) before `namespace_unlock()`. [F213, F214]
- Unmounting (`umount_tree()`, `umount_mnt()`) or setting `emptied_ns` under `namespace_sem` must use `namespace_lock()`/`namespace_unlock()` (`guard(namespace_excl)`), never raw `down_write()`/`up_write()`. Callers holding `namespace_sem` (`do_statmount()`, `do_listmount()`) must not call `path_put()` (including `__free(path_put)`) until after `namespace_sem` is dropped unless the path remains pinned. [F193, F325]
- Walking `mnt->mnt_mounts` or `mnt->mnt_parent` requires `mount_lock` (`read_seqlock_excl`/`write_seqlock`) or `namespace_sem` while mounted/pinned. `__lookup_mnt()` requires `mount_lock`'s spinlock, or `rcu_read_lock()` with `read_seqbegin(&mount_lock)` and retry/fallback to `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)` on `read_seqretry()`. `path_overmounted()` requires `namespace_sem` and must never be called under `lock_mount_hash()`. [F211, F216, F327]
- `mnt_get_write_access()` and `mnt_put_write_access()` must disable preemption around `mnt_writers` updates and `WRITE_HOLD` checks; `mnt_get_write_access()` requires `smp_mb()` after `mnt_inc_writers()` before spinning on `__test_write_hold(READ_ONCE(mnt->mnt_pprev_for_sb))` (which on `CONFIG_PREEMPT_RT` re-enables preemption and cycles `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)`), and `smp_rmb()` before `mnt_is_readonly()`. [F208, F328]
- Under `mount_lock`, `mnt_hold_writers()` requires `smp_mb()` after `set_write_hold(mnt)` before `mnt_get_writers(mnt)`, and `mnt_unhold_writers()` requires `smp_wmb()` before `clear_write_hold(mnt)`. `do_idmap_mount()` requires `is_anon_ns(mnt->mnt_ns)`, `mnt_hold_writers()`, `lock_mount_hash()`, `OPEN_TREE_CLONE` if `MOUNT_KATTR_IDMAP_REPLACE` is set, and `smp_store_release(&mnt->mnt_idmap, ...)`. [F209, F217]
- Mount propagation state (`mnt_share`, `mnt_slave_list`, `mnt_slave`, `mnt_master`, `mnt_group_id`, `mnt_t_flags`) requires `namespace_lock()` (acquired before `lock_mount_hash()`), and `do_change_type()`/`do_set_group()` must check `may_change_propagation()` (`!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(m->mnt_ns)` and `ns_capable(m->mnt_ns->user_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`) on every target mount. [F218, F343]
- Because `mntput_no_expire()` takes an RCU fastpath when `READ_ONCE(mnt->mnt_ns)` is non-NULL, clearing `mnt->mnt_ns` (`mnt_make_shortterm()`, `umount_tree()`) requires an RCU grace period before final `mntput()`. Non-`MNT_INTERNAL` `cleanup_mnt()` must be deferred to process context (`task_work_add(..., TWA_RESUME)` or `delayed_mntput_work`), `free_vfsmnt()` in `cleanup_mnt()` must use `call_rcu(&mnt->mnt_rcu, delayed_free_vfsmnt)`, and `mnt_ns_tree_remove()` must use `call_rcu(&ns->ns.ns_rcu, mnt_ns_release_rcu)`. [F215, F333, F334]
- Outside `pipe->mutex`, read `pipe->head` and `pipe->tail` atomically via `READ_ONCE(pipe->head_tail)`. Because `watch_queue` uses `pipe->rd_wait.lock` without `pipe->mutex`, `anon_pipe_read()` must use `smp_load_acquire(&pipe->head)`, and `pipe_update_tail()` (when `pipe_has_watch_queue(pipe)`) and `pipe_resize_ring()` (which linearizes slots to `0` and sets `tail = 0`, `head = n`) must hold `spin_lock_irq(&pipe->rd_wait.lock)`. [F220, F222, F263]
- `pipe_double_lock()` locks distinct pipes in address order (`pipe1 < pipe2`), and `anon_pipe_write()` must reject `watch_queue` pipes before locking `pipe->mutex`. `inode->i_lock` (outside `pipe->mutex`) protects `inode->i_pipe` and `pipe->files`: `fifo_open()` must call `alloc_pipe_info()` outside `inode->i_lock` and recheck `inode->i_pipe` after relocking, and `put_pipe_info()` must drop `inode->i_lock` before `free_pipe_info()`. [F223, F224, F338]
- In `__get_acl()`, install `uncached_acl_sentinel(current)` via `cmpxchg(p, ACL_NOT_CACHED, sentinel)` before `get_acl`/`get_inode_acl`, revert to `ACL_NOT_CACHED` on error, and call `posix_acl_dup(acl)` before `try_cmpxchg(p, &sentinel, acl)` (releasing on failure). `get_cached_acl()` uses `refcount_inc_not_zero()` under `rcu_read_lock()`, and `get_cached_acl_rcu()` passes `LOOKUP_RCU` to `get_inode_acl()` on `ACL_DONT_CACHE`. `pidfs_register_pid()` and `pidfs_exit()` synchronize `pid->attr` under `spin_lock_irq(&pid->wait_pidfd.lock)`, publishing `attr->exit_info` via `smp_store_release()`. [F207, F225, F226, F341]

## Object Lifetimes

- `vfs_truncate()` acquires `mnt_want_write(path->mnt)` then `get_write_access(inode)`, calls `break_lease()`, and drops them in reverse order. `finish_open()` does not consume the caller's `dentry` ref, whereas `finish_no_open()` consumes it when `!IS_ERR(dentry)`. `mount_subtree(m, name)` always consumes `@m` and returns a dentry with `s->s_active` elevated and `s->s_umount` write-locked. Stack `struct pinned_mountpoint` users (`lookup_mountpoint()`, `get_mountpoint()`, `do_lock_mount()`) must call `unpin_mountpoint()`. [F191, F194, F324, F355]
- In `statmount`'s `retry:` loop, every iteration must call `kvfree(ks->seq.buf)` (allocated by `prepare_kstatmount()`) and `path_put(&ks->root)` (grabbed by `do_statmount()`). In `pidfd_info()` and `pidfd_ioctl()`, `get_pid_task()` is freed via `__free(put_task)` and `get_task_cred(task)` requires `put_cred(c)`. [F200, F203]
- `alloc_pipe_info()`, `pipe_set_size()`, and `free_pipe_info()` account `user->pipe_bufs` against `pipe->user` (never `current_user()`) in `pipe->nr_accounted` (unless `pipe_has_watch_queue(pipe)`), reverting on error and uncharging before `free_uid(pipe->user)`. Callers dropping `pipe->mutex` must re-read `head`, `tail`, and `pipe_buf()` after relocking. In `create_pipe_files()` (`pipe->files = 2`), failure before `alloc_file_pseudo()` requires `free_pipe_info()` and `iput()`, failure of `alloc_file_clone()` requires `put_pipe_info()` and `fput(f)`, and both files need `file_set_fsnotify_mode(..., FMODE_NONOTIFY_PERM)`. [F204, F205, F321, F346, F358]

## API Contracts

- `mnt_want_write()`/`mnt_want_write_file()` include `sb_start_write()` freeze protection and pair with `mnt_drop_write()`/`mnt_drop_write_file()`; `mnt_get_write_access()`/`mnt_get_write_access_file()` do not and pair with `mnt_put_write_access()`/`mnt_put_write_access_file()`. Never cross-pair `*_file()` (which skip `mnt_writers` when `FMODE_WRITER` is set) with non-`_file` functions. [F197, F347]
- `do_lock_mount()` and `lock_mount_exact()` set `res->parent = ERR_PTR(err)` on failure, so check `IS_ERR(mp.parent)` before accessing `mp`. `skip_mnt_tree(p)` returns `p`'s last descendant: `for (...; p = next_mnt(p, root))` loops (`copy_tree()`) must do `p = skip_mnt_tree(p); continue;`, whereas `while` loops (`copy_mnt_ns()`) must call `next_mnt(skip_mnt_tree(p), root)`. [F240, F290]
- `build_mount_idmapped()`/`can_idmap_mount()` require `is_anon_ns(mnt->mnt_ns)`, `FS_ALLOW_IDMAP` without `SB_I_NOIDMAP`, `mnt_userns` neither `&init_user_ns` nor `s_user_ns`, and `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` in both. `path_mount` writes NUL at `data_page[PAGE_SIZE - 1]` and strips `MS_MGC_VAL`. `kernel_file_open()` uses `alloc_empty_file_noaccount()` and must never be installed via `fd_install()`. [F274, F350, F356]
- `get_pipe_info()` must verify `file->f_op` is `&pipefifo_fops` or `&pipeanon_fops` (and `!pipe_has_watch_queue(pipe)` if `for_splice`). In `nsfs` and `pidfs`, `inode->i_private` (not `dentry->d_fsdata`) holds `struct ns_common *` or `struct pid *`; verify `proc_ns_file(file)`, `dentry->d_op == &ns_dentry_operations` (and `ns->ops == &mntns_operations` or `ns->ns_type`), or `file->f_op == &pidfs_file_operations` (`pidfd_pid()`) before downcasting. [F246, F249, F262]
- `__posix_acl_create()` and `__posix_acl_chmod()` always consume `*acl` and replace it with a clone or `NULL`, whereas `set_cached_acl()` and `vfs_set_acl()` do not consume `acl`. `posix_acl_create()` must set `*acl = *default_acl = NULL` before any early return and (like `posix_acl_from_xattr()`) allocate with `GFP_NOFS`. `posix_acl_equiv_mode()` and `posix_acl_create_masq()` return `< 0` on error, `0` if mode-equivalent, and `1` otherwise; `->set_acl()` for `ACL_TYPE_ACCESS` must use `posix_acl_update_mode()` to strip `S_ISGID` when unauthorized. [F206, F233, F235, F342, F361]

## Error Handling

- Because `mnt_hold_writers(m)` leaves `WRITE_HOLD` set when returning `-EBUSY`, `mount_setattr_prepare()` must advance `m = next_mnt(m, mnt)` before breaking on failure so rollback calls `mnt_unhold_writers(m)`. In `anon_pipe_read()` and `anon_pipe_write()`, once `ret > 0`, subsequent failures (`-EFAULT`, `-ENOMEM`, `-EPIPE`, `-EAGAIN`, `-ERESTARTSYS`) must only set `ret` when `!ret`. [F241, F245]

## External Contracts

- `SYSCALL_DEFINE1(close)` must map `-ERESTART*` from `filp_flush()` to `-EINTR` after `file_close_fd(fd)`. `vfs_fallocate()` must reject `offset < 0 || len <= 0` (`-EINVAL`) and use `check_add_overflow(offset, len, &sum)` before `sum > inode->i_sb->s_maxbytes` (`-EFBIG`). `posix_acl_to_xattr()` and `vfs_posix_acl_to_xattr()` must set `ext_entry->e_id = cpu_to_le32(ACL_UNDEFINED_ID)` for all tags except `ACL_USER`/`ACL_GROUP`. [F236, F243, F313]
- `build_mount_kattr()` must reject partial `MOUNT_ATTR__ATIME` in `attr_clr` and require full `MOUNT_ATTR__ATIME` in `attr_clr` when setting atime in `attr_set`. `copy_mnt_id_req()` and `prepare_klistmount()` must reject non-zero mount IDs `<= MNT_UNIQUE_ID_OFFSET` (`1ULL << 31`). Every `do_statmount` flag must be in `STATMOUNT_SUPPORTED` (and `STATMOUNT_STRING_REQ` if string-producing); `statmount_mnt_opts()` and `show_sb_opts()` must call `security_sb_show_options()` unconditionally; foreign-ns or unreachable `statmount`/`listmount` queries require `ns_capable_noaudit(ns->user_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`. [F272, F295, F297, F309, F351]
- `copy_ns_info_to_user` must decrement `kinfo->nr_mounts` by 1 when `mnt_ns->nr_mounts` is non-zero. `nsfs_fh_to_dentry()` must verify trailing bytes beyond `NSFS_FID_SIZE_U32_LATEST` are zero via `memchr_inv()` and require `ns_capable(owning_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)` if `!current_in_namespace()`. `pidfs_dname` must use `"anon_inode:[pidfd]"`; `pidfd_info()` requires `pid_in_current_pidns(pid)` (`-ESRCH`) and munges creds into `current_user_ns()`; `pidfd_ioctl()` requires `ptrace_may_access(task, PTRACE_MODE_READ_FSCREDS)`. [F275, F277, F353, F354]

## Known Pitfalls

- `statmount_mnt_root()`, `statmount_sb_source()`, `statmount_mnt_opts()`, and `statmount_opt_process()` must check `seq_has_overflowed(seq)` (`-EAGAIN`) before modifying `seq->buf` in place; `statmount_string()` and `retry_statmount()` must use `check_add_overflow()`/`check_mul_overflow()` (`<= MAX_RW_COUNT`), and `statmount_opt_process()` must check `++count == INT_MAX` (`-EOVERFLOW`). [F260, F311, F312]
- In `propagate_mnt()`, check `if (!need_secondary(n, dest_mp)) continue;` before testing `type & CL_SLAVE` and resetting `type = CL_MAKE_SHARED`. In `mark_mounts_for_expiry()` and `shrink_submounts()`, move mounts to `graveyard` and unmount with `while (!list_empty(&graveyard))` calling `umount_tree()`, never `list_for_each_entry_safe()`. [F289, F298]
