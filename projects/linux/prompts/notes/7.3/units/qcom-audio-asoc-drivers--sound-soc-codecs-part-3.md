# QCOM AUDIO (ASoC) DRIVERS — sound/soc/codecs (part 3)

Qualcomm ASoC codec and smart speaker amplifier drivers covering the WCD9380/WCD9385 master codec, the WCD9390/WCD9395 master codec and SoundWire slave driver (with USB Type-C analog audio switch support), and the WSA881x, WSA883x, and WSA884x SoundWire Class-D smart speaker amplifiers (including thermal/hwmon sensing).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `wcd938x.c` and `wcd939x.c`, PDM watchdog IRQs (`hphr_pdm_wd_int`, `hphl_pdm_wd_int`, `aux_pdm_wd_int`/`ear_pdm_wd_int`) are disabled via `disable_irq_nosync` after `request_threaded_irq` in probe and paired with `enable_irq` in `SND_SOC_DAPM_POST_PMU` and `disable_irq_nosync` in `PRE_PMD`. In `wcd938x_codec_ear_dac_event` and `wcd938x_codec_enable_ear_pa`, `wcd938x->ear_rx_path` is latched from `WCD938X_DIGITAL_CDC_EAR_PATH_CTL` in `PRE_PMU` and reused across `POST_PMU`, `PRE_PMD`, and `POST_PMD` to toggle matching AUX/HPHL clocks, compander, and watchdog IRQ. [F248, F313]
- In `wcd938x.c`, `wcd938x->flyback_cur_det_disable` refcounts active EAR and AUX paths: `wcd938x_codec_ear_dac_event` and `wcd938x_codec_aux_dac_event` clear `WCD938X_EN_CUR_DET_MASK` in `WCD938X_FLYBACK_EN` when incrementing from 0 in `PRE_PMU`, and `wcd938x_codec_enable_ear_pa` and `wcd938x_codec_enable_aux_pa` restore it when decrementing to 0 in `POST_PMD`. [F287]
- In `wcd938x_codec_hphl_dac_event`, `wcd938x_codec_hphr_dac_event`, `wcd939x_codec_hphl_dac_event`, and `wcd939x_codec_hphr_dac_event`, `SND_SOC_DAPM_POST_PMU` skips the 5ms compander delay when the opposite channel's compander is enabled in software (`comp1_enable`/`comp2_enable`) but not yet set in `COMP_CTL_0`. Broken by: unconditionally sleeping 5ms or inverting the `COMP_CTL_0` check. [F312]
- In `wcd938x_set_swr_port` and `wcd939x_set_swr_port`, updating `wcd->port_enable[portidx]` must be paired with `wcd938x_connect_port`/`wcd939x_connect_port` to update `wcd->port_config[port_num - 1].ch_mask` (`port_config` in `wcd938x_sdw_priv`/`wcd939x_sdw_priv` is sized to the larger RX `WCD938X_MAX_SWR_PORTS`/`WCD939X_MAX_SWR_PORTS` since RX and TX share the struct). `wsa881x_hw_params`, `wsa883x_hw_params`, and `wcd939x_sdw_hw_params` must reset `active_ports = 0` (and `wcd->sconfig.ch_count = 1` in `wcd939x_sdw_hw_params`) before compacting ports for `sdw_stream_add_slave`. [F285, F300, F328]
- When initializing `pdev->prop.sink_ports` or `pdev->prop.source_ports` for an N-element `sdw_dpn_prop` array in `wcd9390_probe`, `wsa881x_probe`, or `wsa883x_probe`, the bitmask must be `GENMASK(N - 1, 0)` rather than `GENMASK(N, 0)` because `GENMASK` is inclusive. [F302]
- In `wsa881x.c`, `wsa881x_set_port` must call `wsa881x_boost_ctrl` whenever `port_enable[WSA881X_PORT_BOOST]` changes, while `wsa881x_spkr_pa_event` gates visense TXFE and ADC enable/disable in `POST_PMU` and `POST_PMD` on `wsa881x->port_prepared[WSA881X_PORT_VISENSE]` as updated by `wsa881x_port_prep`. [F297]
- In `wcd939x-sdw.c`, `wcd9390_probe` must start the TX regmap in `regcache_cache_only(wcd->regmap, true)` until SoundWire enumeration syncs it, and `wcd939x_sdw_runtime_suspend` must set `regcache_cache_only(true)` and `regcache_mark_dirty()` so `wcd939x_sdw_runtime_resume` syncs cached state back to hardware. [F295]
- In `wcd938x_wcd_mbhc_calc_impedance` and `wcd939x_wcd_mbhc_calc_impedance`, `ANA_MBHC_BTN5..7`, `MBHC_CTL_CLK`, `MBHC_NEW_ZDET_ANA_CTL`, and `ANA_MBHC_ELECT` `FSM_EN` must be saved before impedance ramps and restored with `ANA_MBHC_MECH` at `zdet_complete` on all exits; in `wcd938x`, if left `z1L` is `WCD938X_ZDET_FLOATING_IMPEDANCE` or `> WCD938X_ZDET_VAL_100K`, `zdet_param_ptr` and `d1` must reset to `&zdet_param[1]` and `d1_a[1]` before the right ramp. `wcd938x`/`wcd939x_mbhc_get_result_params` must bound both ramp-completion and `ramp_down` loops by `WCD938X`/`WCD939X_ZDET_NUM_MEASUREMENTS`. [F290, F316, F317]
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x_mbhc_moisture_config` and `wcd938x`/`wcd939x_mbhc_get_moisture_status`, moisture detection must be forced to `R_OFF` in `MBHC_NEW_CTL_2` whenever `mbhc_cfg.moist_rref == R_OFF`, `!mbhc_cfg.hphl_swh` (NC jack), or (on WCD939x) `typec_analog_mux` is true. In `wcd938x_wcd_mbhc_qfuse_cal` and `wcd939x_wcd_mbhc_qfuse_cal`, the 8-bit EFUSE byte `q1` is sign-magnitude where bit 7 (`0x80`/`BIT(7)`) indicates subtraction and must be masked off (`q1 & 0x7F`/`GENMASK(6, 0)`) before scaling. [F291, F310]
- In `wcd938x_micbias_control` and `wcd939x_micbias_control`, 1-based `micb_num` (`MIC_BIAS_1`..`MIC_BIAS_4`) must be validated by `switch (micb_num)` before `micb_index = micb_num - 1` indexes the 4-element `micb_ref` and `pullup_ref` arrays. On `ADC1`..`ADC4` DAPM widgets (`wcd938x_codec_enable_adc`), widget shift values (`0..3`) must match the `WCD_ADC1`..`WCD_ADC4` bit indices in `status_mask`. [F306, F334]
- In `wcd938x_soc_codec_probe` and `wcd939x_soc_codec_probe`, variant-specific ALSA controls (`wcd9380`/`wcd9385_snd_controls`, `wcd9390`/`wcd9395_snd_controls`) must be registered via `snd_soc_add_component_controls()` only after reading the chip ID from `WCD938X`/`WCD939X_DIGITAL_EFUSE_REG_0`. Similarly, in `wsa883x_init`, after setting `wsa883x->comp_offset = COMP_OFFSET2`, `WSA8830` and `WSA8832` variants (`WSA883X_OTP_REG_0 & WSA883X_ID_MASK`) must override `comp_offset` to `COMP_OFFSET3` and update `WSA883X_DRE_OFFSET_MASK` in `WSA883X_DRE_CTL_0`. [F343, F345]

## Synchronization

- Under `wsa883x->sp_lock`, `wsa883x_spkr_event` sets `pa_on = true` in `POST_PMU` and `false` in `PRE_PMD`. `wsa883x_get_temp` must hold `sp_lock` (`guard(mutex)`): if `pa_on`, return cached `wsa883x->temperature * 1000` without touching `WSA883X_PA_FSM_BYP` or `WSA883X_TADC_VALUE_CTL`; if `!pa_on`, balance `pm_runtime_resume_and_get` with `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend`, clear `WSA883X_PA_FSM_BYP` to `0x0` before any exit, guard `d1 == d2` before dividing by `d2 - d1`, verify `in_range(val, WSA883X_LOW_TEMP_THRESHOLD, range)` before caching `val` (°C) in `temperature`, and scale `*temp` by 1000 (m°C). [F251, F298, F308, F368]
- In `wcd938x_bind()` and `wcd939x_bind()`, `wcd938x_irq_init()`/`wcd939x_irq_init()` must initialize `virq` and `irq_chip` and assign `virq` to `sdw_priv[AIF1_PB]->slave_irq` and `sdw_priv[AIF1_CAP]->slave_irq` before `snd_soc_register_component()` is called. [F255]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `wcd938x_bind` and `wcd939x_bind`, after `component_bind_all`, `device_link_add` (`DL_FLAG_STATELESS | DL_FLAG_PM_RUNTIME`) must link `rxdev` to `txdev` and `dev` to both `txdev` and `rxdev` so `txdev` (the main CSR regmap) never suspends while `rxdev` or master `dev` is active; on any bind error and in `wcd938x_unbind`/`wcd939x_unbind`, device links, `rxdev`/`txdev` references from `of_sdw_find_device_by_node` (`put_device`), and `component_unbind_all` must be unwound in reverse order (via `err_remove_rx_link`). [F241, F337]
- In `wcd938x_soc_codec_probe` and `wcd939x_soc_codec_probe`, `wcd_clsh_ctrl_alloc`, the three `request_threaded_irq` watchdog IRQs, and `wcd938x_mbhc_init` / `wcd939x_mbhc_init` must be unwound in reverse order on probe failure (via `err_free_*` labels) and in `wcd938x_soc_codec_remove` / `wcd939x_soc_codec_remove`. [F242]
- When `wcd939x->typec_analog_mux` is true, `wcd939x_add_typec` registers the codec's `typec_mux` and `typec_switch` during `wcd939x_probe`, whereas remote USBSS `wcd939x->typec_switch` is acquired via `fwnode_typec_switch_get()` only in `wcd939x_bind()`. Both `wcd939x_swap_gnd_mic()` and error/unbind cleanup in `wcd939x_bind()` must check `wcd939x->typec_analog_mux` (and `wcd939x->typec_switch` in `wcd939x_swap_gnd_mic()`) before calling `typec_switch_set()` or `typec_switch_put()`. [F274, F339]
- In `wcd938x_populate_dt_data`, `devm_mux_control_get()` must only be called if `of_property_present(dev->of_node, "mux-controls")`, and errors from it or `mux_control_try_select()` must be returned rather than falling back to `us_euro_gpio`. `wcd938x->mux_setup_done` tracks active selection on `us_euro_mux`: `wcd938x_swap_gnd_mic` and `wcd938x_remove` must call `mux_control_deselect` only when `mux_setup_done` is true, and `wcd938x_swap_gnd_mic` must set `mux_setup_done = false` without toggling `mux_state` if `mux_control_try_select` fails. [F293, F342]
- In `wcd938x_remove` and `wcd939x_remove`, after `component_master_del()`, the driver must call `pm_runtime_disable(dev)`, `pm_runtime_set_suspended(dev)`, and `pm_runtime_dont_use_autosuspend(dev)` to balance the runtime PM and autosuspend configuration enabled in `wcd938x_probe` and `wcd939x_probe`. [F353]

## API Contracts

- SoundWire hardware port numbers (`ch_info->port_num` and `prepare_ch->num`) are 1-based, so driver arrays `port_config[]` and `port_prepared[]` (in `wcd938x_connect_port`, `wcd939x_connect_port`, `wsa881x_port_prep`, `wsa883x_port_prep`) must be indexed with `port_num - 1` or `prepare_ch->num - 1`, whereas `sdw_slave.m_port_map[]` must be populated starting at `&pdev->m_port_map[1]`. [F279]
- `snd_soc_component_write_field()` takes an unshifted field value that it shifts by the mask's lowest set bit (as in `wcd939x_set_swr_clk_rate()`), whereas `snd_soc_component_update_bits()` and `regmap_update_bits()` require the value argument to be pre-shifted to align with `mask` (as in `wcd938x_set_swr_clk_rate()` and `wsa881x_put_pa_gain()`). [F281]
- Master platform devices store `struct wcd938x_priv *`/`wcd939x_priv *` in drvdata, while child SoundWire slave devices (`rxdev`, `txdev`) store `struct wcd938x_sdw_priv *`/`wcd939x_sdw_priv *`. In `wsa883x_probe()`, `devm_hwmon_device_register_with_info()` must receive `struct wsa883x_priv *` as drvdata for `wsa883x_hwmon_read()` -> `wsa883x_get_temp()`. In `wcd939x_add_typec()`, `mux_desc.drvdata` and `sw_desc.drvdata` take `struct wcd939x_priv *`, whereas `devm_add_action_or_reset()` callbacks `wcd939x_typec_mux_unregister`/`wcd939x_typec_switch_unregister` must receive `typec_mux_dev *`/`typec_switch_dev *`. [F330, F331, F332]
- In `wcd938x_mbhc_zdet_ramp()` and `wcd939x_mbhc_zdet_ramp()`, out-parameters `zl` and `zr` are individually optional (`NULL` when measuring a single channel), so left and right channel measurements and pointer writes must be guarded with `NULL` checks. `wcd938x_mbhc_program_btn_thr` and `wcd939x_mbhc_program_btn_thr` must reject `num_btn > WCD_MBHC_DEF_BUTTONS` before iterating `0 .. num_btn - 1` so `btn_high[i]` and `ANA_MBHC_BTN0 + i` stay within the 8 button threshold registers. [F275, F307]
- In `wcd938x_probe()` and `wcd939x_probe()`, `common.dev` and `common.max_bias` (`<= WCD_MAX_MICBIAS`) must be initialized on `struct wcd_common` before calling `wcd938x_populate_dt_data()` or `wcd939x_populate_dt_data()`, which pass `&common` to `wcd_dt_parse_micbias_info()`. [F278]
- In `wcd938x.c` and `wcd939x.c`, `wcd_irq_chip_map()` (`wcd_domain_ops` in `wcd938x_irq_init`/`wcd939x_irq_init`) must call `irq_set_nested_thread(virq, 1)` on the root `wcd_irq_chip` mapping so that `wcd_interrupt_callback()` (invoked via `wcd9390_interrupt_callback()`) can dispatch `regmap_irq_thread()` synchronously via `handle_nested_irq()`. [F358]
- SoundWire `struct regmap_config` definitions (`wcd939x_regmap_config`, `wsa881x_regmap_config`, and `wsa883x_regmap_config`) passed to `regmap_init_sdw()` or `devm_regmap_init_sdw()` must not set `.can_multi_write = true`, because `regmap-sdw` does not support multi-register writes and misprograms the codec during `regmap_multi_reg_write()`. [F363]
- In `DECLARE_TLV_DB_SCALE(name, min, step, mute)` and `TLV_DB_SCALE_ITEM(min, step, mute)` (e.g., `line_gain`, `pa_gain`), `min` and `step` are in 0.01 dB units and `mute` is a boolean flag that must be `0` when raw value 0 does not mute. In `struct snd_soc_dai_driver` definitions (`wcd938x_dais`, `wcd939x_dais`, `wsa881x_dais`, `wsa883x_dais`), each playback/capture stream must set `.rate_min <= .rate_max` and `.channels_min <= .channels_max`. [F267, F364]
- In `wcd938x.h` and `wcd939x.h`, the `#if IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_SND_SOC_WCD938X_SDW)` and `#if IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_SND_SOC_WCD939X_SDW)` declarations for `*_sdw_free`, `*_sdw_set_sdw_stream`, and `*_sdw_hw_params` must be updated in lockstep with their `#else` `static inline` `-EOPNOTSUPP` stubs. [F301]

## Error Handling

- `wcd938x_mbhc_init()` and `wcd939x_mbhc_init()` must check the return value of `wcd_mbhc_init()` with `IS_ERR()` and propagate `PTR_ERR()` to abort component probe, preventing an error-encoded `wcd_mbhc` pointer from being dereferenced later in `wcd_mbhc_start()` or `wcd_mbhc_stop()`. [F265]
- `wcd938x_populate_dt_data()` and `wcd938x_probe()` must propagate the exact error code returned by GPIO, mux, regulator, and micbias acquisition helpers rather than overwriting it with a fixed errno such as `-EINVAL`, so that `-EPROBE_DEFER` is preserved. [F266]
- Callers of `wcd938x_get_adc_mode()` and `wcd939x_get_adc_mode()` (such as `wcd938x_adc_enable_req()` and `wcd939x_adc_enable_req()`) must store the result in a signed `int` and check for `mode < 0` before writing the mode to the `TX_ANA_MODE` register via `snd_soc_component_write_field()`. [F269]

## External Contracts

- In `wcd938x_snd_controls`, volume controls whose hardware registers map increasing raw values to decreasing dB gains (`HPHL Volume` and `HPHR Volume`) must set the `SOC_SINGLE_TLV` `invert` parameter to `1` and pair it with `DECLARE_TLV_DB_SCALE(line_gain, -3000, 150, 0)` starting at the minimum dB value. [F362]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `wcd938x_mbhc_get_result_params` and `wcd939x_mbhc_get_result_params`, `denom = (x1 * d1) - (1 << (14 - noff))` must remain a signed integer (`int32_t`/`s32`) and be checked with `denom > 0` before dividing, because floating impedances yield `x1 * d1 <= (1 << (14 - noff))` and rely on `denom <= 0` to reach the floating-impedance branch. Broken by: changing `denom` to an unsigned type or checking `denom != 0`. [F323]
