# DRM DRIVER FOR NVIDIA GEFORCE/QUADRO GPUS — drivers/gpu/drm/nouveau (part 3)

This unit implements DRM/GEM/TTM memory management, GPU VA space management (`nouveau_uvmm`), DRM GPU scheduler integration (`nouveau_sched`), Shared Virtual Memory (`nouveau_svm`), fence backends (`nv04`–`nvc0`), hwmon/LED/VGA/PRIME integration, and the `nvif` client library.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, `bind_lock_validate()` must validate backing GEM BOs before `bind_link_gpuvas()` links new `DRM_GPUVA_OP_MAP`/`REMAP` mappings. In `nouveau_uvmm_bo_validate()`, call `nouveau_bo_placement_set(nvbo, nvbo->valid_domains, 0)` before `nouveau_bo_validate()`. `nouveau_uvmm_bo_unmap_all()`/`map_all()` must call `drm_gpuva_invalidate(va, true/false)` in lockstep with `nouveau_uvma_unmap()`/`map()`, as `nouveau_uvma_unmap()` and `op_unmap_range()` skip `nouveau_uvmm_vmm_unmap()` when `drm_gpuva_invalidated()` is true. [F159, F161, F162]
- In `bind_validate_op()`, `OP_MAP` must check page alignment of `op->gem.offset`, verify `obj->size <= op->gem.offset` before subtracting `obj->size - op->gem.offset`, and call `nouveau_uvmm_validate_range()`. In `bind_validate_region()`, `OP_MAP_SPARSE` must set `sparse = true`, call `bind_validate_map_sparse()`, and fall through to `OP_MAP` (`bind_validate_map_common()`). Any non-sparse `OP_MAP` intersecting a sparse `nouveau_uvma_region` must be enclosed within `[reg->va.addr, reg->va.addr + reg->va.range)` or return `-ENOSPC`. [F181, F183, F198]
- `uvmm->region_mt` uses inclusive bounds `[addr, addr + range - 1]` in `__nouveau_uvma_region_insert()` and `nouveau_uvma_region_find_first()`, whereas `reg->va` stores `addr` and byte length `range`. Every `nouveau_job` on `cli->sched` (`VM_BIND`) must be a `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job` (`bind_validate_map_sparse()` downcasts via `to_uvmm_bind_job()`). [F182, F229]
- A `NOUVEAU_GEM_DOMAIN_NO_SHARE` (`nvbo->no_share`) BO shares `drm_gpuvm_resv(&uvmm->base)` and must be rejected with `-EPERM` in `nouveau_gem_prime_export()` and `nouveau_gem_object_open()` for any client with a different `drm_gpuvm_resv`. [F168]
- In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()` and `nouveau_gem_pushbuf_reloc_apply()`, reject 0-based indices (`push[i].bo_index`, `r->bo_index`, `r->reloc_bo_index`) when `>= req->nr_buffers`. When a reserved BO is mapped via `ttm_bo_kmap()`, set `nvbo->validate_mapped = true` so `validate_fini_no_ticket()` calls `ttm_bo_kunmap()`. `validate_init()` stores `struct nouveau_vma *` in `b->user_priv` when `oclass >= NVIF_CLASS_VMM_NV50` and `struct nouveau_bo *` otherwise; `nouveau_gem_pushbuf_reloc_apply()` must only run on pre-`NV50` channels. [F167, F177, F231]
- In `struct nouveau_sgdma_be`, `struct ttm_tt ttm` must remain at offset 0 for direct casts in `nouveau_sgdma_destroy()`/`bind()`/`unbind()`. On `NV_DEVICE_INFO_V0_TESLA`+ except chipset `0x50`, `nouveau_ttm_init()` registers `NVIF_MEM_KIND` in `type_host[1]`/`type_ncoh[1]`, and `nouveau_mem_host()` selects `type_ncoh[!!mem->kind]`. [F233, F244]
- In `nv84_fence_create()`, when `ram_size == 0`, allocate `priv->bo` with `NOUVEAU_GEM_DOMAIN_GART | NOUVEAU_GEM_DOMAIN_COHERENT`. `priv->bo` slots are 16 bytes indexed by `nv84_fence_chid(chan)` (not `chan->chid`); `nv84_fence_context_del()` must write `fctx->base.sequence` to the slot for `nv84_fence_read(chan)` in `nv84_fence_context_new()`, and `nv84_fence_suspend()`/`resume()` must save/restore all `drm->chan_total` slots. [F170, F245]
- In `nouveau_svm.c`, `buffer->id` must equal its index in `svm->buffer[]` before scheduling `buffer->work` (`container_of` in `nouveau_svm_fault()`). Fault entries in `buffer->object` are 32 bytes (`buffer->get * 0x20`), wrapping `buffer->get` to `0` at `buffer->entries`. `nouveau_svm_fault_cache()` must clear bit 31 (`0x80000000`) at `offset + 0x1c` before writing `buffer->get` to `buffer->getaddr`, and `nouveau_svm_fault_buffer_init()` must re-read `buffer->get` and `put` from hardware on init and resume. [F171, F185, F234]
- `nvif_chan_dma_wait()` must reserve `chan->func->gpfifo.post_size` words (`nr += post_size`, `push->end = push->bgn + free - post_size`) for `nvif_chan_gpfifo_push_kick()`. In `nvif_chan_gpfifo_ctor()`, `gpfifo.max` is `(gpfifo_size >> 3) - 1` and `push.hw.max` is `push_size >> 2`; on `nvif_chan906f`+, `push_size` is at most 64 KiB and power-of-two `gpfifo_size` at most 1 MiB. [F175, F188]
- `struct nvif_disp` stores `conn_mask`, `outp_mask`, and `head_mask` as `unsigned long` for `for_each_set_bit()`. `nouveau_vga_init()`/`fini()` must skip `!dev_is_pci(dev->dev)` and skip `vga_switcheroo` on `pci_is_thunderbolt_attached(pdev)`. In `nouveau_led_get_brightness()`, check `div > 0` (`0x61c880`) before dividing `duty * LED_FULL / div`. [F212, F236, F246]

## Synchronization
- In `nouveau_job_submit()`, hold `sched->mutex` across `job->ops->submit()`, `list_add(&job->entry, &sched->job.list.head)`, `drm_sched_job_arm()`, `armed_submit()`, and `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`, setting `job->state = NOUVEAU_JOB_SUBMIT_SUCCESS` before `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`. [F123, F124]
- Hold `uvmm->mutex` (`nouveau_uvmm_lock()`) for any `uvmm->base` or `uvmm->region_mt` access (outer to GEM `dma_resv` locks), and hold it continuously in `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()` across op preparation, `drm_exec` validation, and `bind_link_gpuvas()` (or unwind). Hold `obj->resv` for every `drm_gpuva_link()`, `drm_gpuva_unlink()`, `drm_gpuvm_bo_obtain()`, and `drm_gpuvm_bo_put()` in `nouveau_uvmm.c`. [F126, F127, F259]
- In `bind_validate_map_common()` and `bind_validate_map_sparse()`, pin the target's kref before dropping `uvmm->mutex` or `sched->job.list.lock` to wait on `complete`, and `goto again` after relocking. In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_cleanup()` (`OP_UNMAP_SPARSE`), call `nouveau_uvma_region_remove(op->reg)` under `nouveau_uvmm_lock(uvmm)` before `nouveau_uvma_region_complete()`. In `armed_submit()`, call `drm_gpuvm_exec_resv_add_fence()` before `drm_gpuvm_exec_unlock()`. [F129, F130, F131]
- In `nouveau_svm_fault()`, acquire `mmget_not_zero(mm)` before faulting `svmm->notifier.mm` (cancel fault if false). In `nouveau_range_fault()` and `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, drop `mmap_read_lock(mm)` before locking `svmm->mutex`, and hold `svmm->mutex` across `mmu_interval_read_retry()` and `nvif_object_ioctl()`. [F135, F281]
- In `nouveau_gem_prime_import_sg_table()`, hold `dma_resv_lock(robj, NULL)` across `nouveau_bo_alloc()`, `drm_gem_object_init()`, and `nouveau_bo_init()`. In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()`, if `do_reloc` is true and `reloc` is not yet copied, call `validate_fini()` before `u_memcpya(req->relocs, ...)` and jump to `revalidate:`. Hold `drm->client_mutex` around `nvif_mem_ctor_type()` and `nvif_mem_dtor()` in `nouveau_mem.c`. [F136, F137, F138]
- Unwrap `fence->channel` with `unrcu_pointer()` in `nv04_fence_emit()`. In `nv17_fence_sync()`, use `mutex_trylock(&cli->mutex)` (`-EBUSY` on failure) and lock `priv->lock` for `priv->sequence`. In `nvif_chanc36f_gpfifo_kick()`, run `wmb()` and `nvif_rd32(&chan->userd, 0)` after writing `userd` `0x8c` before `doorbell()`. In `nvif_userc361_time()`, loop reading `0x084` (`hi`) and `0x080` (`lo`) until `hi` is stable, casting `(u64)hi << 32`. [F210, F248, F266, F267]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `nouveau_gem_new()` and `nouveau_gem_prime_import_sg_table()`, call `drm_gem_object_init(&nvbo->bo.base)` after `nouveau_bo_alloc()` and before `nouveau_bo_init()`. If `drm_gem_object_init()` fails, call `drm_gem_object_release(&nvbo->bo.base)` and `kfree(nvbo)`. `nouveau_bo_init()` consumes `nvbo` even on failure (`nouveau_bo_del_ttm()`), so do not free or release `nvbo` if `nouveau_bo_init()` fails. Pair `arch_io_reserve_memtype_wc()`/`arch_phys_wc_add()` in `nouveau_ttm_init()` with `arch_phys_wc_del()`/`arch_io_free_memtype_wc()` in `nouveau_ttm_fini()`. [F105, F106, F237, F238]
- In `nouveau_svmm_init()`, `__mmu_notifier_register()` takes ownership of `svmm` (freed in `nouveau_svmm_free_notifier()`; `kfree(svmm)` only if registration fails). In `nouveau_svmm_fini()`, set `svmm->vmm = NULL` under `svmm->mutex` before `mmu_notifier_put(&svmm->notifier)` (never `kfree(svmm)`). In `nouveau_svm_fault_buffer_fini()`, call `nvif_event_block(&buffer->notify)` then `flush_work(&buffer->work)` before freeing fault entries or destroying `notify`/`object`. [F111, F133, F240]
- In `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, call `folio_unlock(folio)` and `folio_put(folio)` both on `mmu_interval_read_retry()` retry and after `nvif_object_ioctl()`, and call `mmu_interval_notifier_remove()` before returning. Pair `nouveau_uvma_gem_get(uvma)` in `op_map_prepare()` with `nouveau_uvma_gem_put(uvma)` before `nouveau_uvma_free(uvma)` in `op_map_prepare_unwind()`, `nouveau_uvmm_sm_cleanup()`, and `nouveau_uvmm_fini()`. [F112, F118]
- Zero `vma->size` in `nvif_vmm_get()` before `nvif_object_mthd()`, and reset `vma->size = 0` in `nvif_vmm_put()` after `NVIF_VMM_V0_PUT`. In `nvif_object_map()`, `nvif_object_map_handle()` returns `< 0` on error, `0` when already a KVA, and `1` when `client->driver->map()` is needed; if `map()` returns `NULL`, call `nvif_object_unmap_handle(object)` before returning `-ENOMEM`. [F120, F122, F143]

## API Contracts
- In `nouveau_job_submit()`, once `job->ops->submit()` succeeds, submission cannot fail and must call `armed_submit()` and `nouveau_job_fence_attach()`; on `submit()` error, call `nouveau_job_fence_attach_cleanup()`. `nouveau_job_run()` must check `IS_ERR(fence)` (as `ERR_PTR(0)`/`NULL` means synchronous completion). Bracket `nouveau_sched_timedout_job()` with `drm_sched_stop()` and `drm_sched_start()`. Pass `0` as `flags` to `drm_syncobj_find_fence()` in `sync_find_fence()`, not `sync->flags`. [F115, F144, F154, F271]
- In `nouveau_uvmm_sm_prepare()` and `nouveau_uvmm_sm_prepare_unwind()`, check `args != NULL` before dereferencing `args` or calling `nouveau_uvmm_vmm_get()`/`put()`. UVMM and legacy GEM are mutually exclusive (`nouveau_cli_disable_uvmm_noinit(cli)` vs `nouveau_cli_uvmm(cli)`). [F148, F169]
- Copy embedded user pointers in DRM ioctls via `u_memcpya()` (checking `IS_ERR()`, never passing `IS_ERR()` to `u_free()`). `validate_list()` returns `< 0` on error or `>= 0` relocation count (`*apply_relocs = true` if `> 0`). Use `PFN_UP(nvbo->bo.base.size)` for `ttm_bo_kmap()` page count. Convert byte addresses/sizes with `>> PAGE_SHIFT` for `res->start` and `PFN_UP(size)` for `nouveau_mem_intersects()`/`compatible()`, while passing `PAGE_SHIFT` (`u8 shift`/`page`) to `nvif_vmm_raw_*()`, `nvif_vmm_get()`, and `nvif_mem_ctor_type()`. [F141, F142, F156, F214, F275, F286]
- `nouveau_svmm_init()` returns `-ENOSYS` when `!cli->drm->svm`. Set `range.dev_private_owner = drm->dev` in `nouveau_range_fault()`. In `nouveau_svmm_invalidate_range_start()` and `nouveau_svm_range_invalidate()`, ignore `MMU_NOTIFY_MIGRATE`/`EXCLUSIVE` owned by `drm->dev`, and check `mmu_notifier_range_blockable()` before `mutex_lock(&svmm->mutex)`. [F151, F243, F277, F278]
- Use `check_add_overflow(sizeof(*args), size, ...)` in `nvif_object_mthd()`/`ctor()`. Pass only trailing `data[]` length as `size` to `nvif_outp_infoframe()`. Pass non-NULL `name` to `nvif_client_ctor()`/`nvif_driver_init()`. In `nvif_mem_ctor()`, check `(mmu->type[i].type & type) == type` and loop while `ret != 0`. `nvif_push` uses `u32` words while `gpfifo.push()` takes bytes (`<< 2`). Return `-EINVAL` in hwmon `*_input` reads when `switch_power_state != DRM_SWITCH_POWER_ON`. [F176, F204, F223, F227, F235, F251]

## Error Handling
- In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, jump to `unwind_continue` (`op = list_prev_op(op)`) on failure inside `list_for_each_op`, or set `op = list_last_op(&bind_job->ops)` and jump to `unwind` after the loop; unwind reverses ops via `list_for_each_op_from_reverse`, frees/clears `op->ops = NULL` and `op->reg = NULL`, and unlocks `uvmm` and `vme`. [F116, F194]
- In `validate_init()`, on `-EDEADLK` from `ttm_bo_reserve()`, use `list_splice_tail_init()` before `validate_fini_no_ticket()`, and finish `res_bo` setup after `ttm_bo_reserve_slowpath()` before `goto retry`. Skip `NV_PRINTK(err, ...)` when `ret == -ERESTARTSYS`. Map `long` `dma_resv_wait_timeout()` `0` to `-EBUSY` and `> 0` to `0`. Initialize `object.client = NULL` and sub-pointers to `NULL` first in `nvif_*_ctor()`. [F140, F150, F200, F288]

## External Contracts
- In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()`, bound `nr_push`/`nr_buffers`/`nr_relocs` by `NOUVEAU_GEM_MAX_*` before `u_memcpya()`, and only copy `bo[i].presumed` back to userspace (never `user_priv`). In `nouveau_svmm_bind()`, align `va_start &= PAGE_MASK` and `va_end = ALIGN(va_end, PAGE_SIZE)` before checking `va_start >= va_end`, and reject non-zero `reserved0`/`1`, `stride`, or invalid `header` bits. Use `IS_REACHABLE(CONFIG_LEDS_CLASS)` in `nouveau_led.h`. [F179, F184, F232, F270]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `op_remap()` and `nouveau_uvmm_sm_cleanup()`, sample `end = addr + range` before advancing `addr` past `r->prev` (`end - addr`). Skip `va == &uvmm->base.kernel_alloc_node` in `drm_gpuvm_for_each_va*` loops over `&uvmm->base`. [F160, F230]
- In `nouveau_svm_fault()`, dereference `*(struct nouveau_svm_fault **)a` in `nouveau_svm_fault_cmp()`; sort `buffer->fault` before resolving `svmm` (`!svmm || buffer->fault[fi]->inst != inst`); reset `fn = fi + 1` and `args->p.addr = start`, `page = PAGE_SHIFT`, `size = PAGE_SIZE` each iteration. In `nouveau_hmm_convert_pfn()`, use `1UL << args->p.page`, and sample `addr = args->p.addr` before masking `args->p.addr &= ~(args->p.size - 1)` to rewind `page -= (addr - args->p.addr) >> PAGE_SHIFT`. Use `time_after(jiffies, timeout)` in fault retry loops. [F172, F173, F202, F203, F208, F215, F228]
- In `nvif/chan.c`, check `chan->func->gpfifo.post` for NULL (`nvif_chan506f`); add `chan->gpfifo.max + 1` when `int chan->gpfifo.free < 0` in `nvif_chan_gpfifo_wait()`; poll `read_get(chan) != 0` before wrapping `cur = 0` in `nvif_chan_dma_wait()`; cast `(u64)(tlgethi & 0xff) << 32` in `nvif_chan506f_read_get()`; and bound unchanged timestamp reads at `wait->reads++ == 16` in `nvif_timer_wait_test()`. [F145, F205, F206, F209, F216]
