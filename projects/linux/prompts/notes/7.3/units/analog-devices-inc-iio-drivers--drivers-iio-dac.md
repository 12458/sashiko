# ANALOG DEVICES INC IIO DRIVERS — drivers/iio/dac

Linux IIO DAC drivers for Analog Devices and Linear Technology chips (AD3552R/AD3542R, AD5064, AD5360, AD5380, AD5421, AD5446, AD5449, AD5504, AD5592R/AD5593R, AD5624R, AD5686/AD5696, AD5755, AD5758, AD5761, AD5764, AD5766, AD5770R, AD5791, AD7303, AD8801), supporting voltage/current channels, reference scaling, powerdown modes, DC-to-DC/slew-rate control, and buffered streaming via IIO backends, SPI offload DMA, and triggered buffers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`, `st->config_d` must track `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_INTERFACE_CONFIG_D`; while `AD3552R_MASK_SPI_CONFIG_DDR` is set in `st->config_d`, use `st->data->bus_reg_write` instead of `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits` or `ad3552r_hs_reg_read` (no DDR reads). In `ad3552r_hs_setup()`, after `ad3552r_hs_reset()`, call `iio_backend_ddr_disable()` and write `AD3552R_MASK_RESET_STATUS` to `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_ERR_STATUS`. [F200, F270]
- In `ad3552r.h` and `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable`, multi-byte secondary registers (`>= AD3552R_SECONDARY_REGION_START`) stream in descending address order, so 2-channel streaming (`loop_len = 4`) must pass `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_CH_DAC_16B(1)` (`0x2C`), not channel 0 (`0x2A`), to `iio_backend_data_transfer_addr`. `ad3552r_ch_ranges` and `ad3542r_ch_ranges` indices must match both `model_data->ranges_table` in `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset` and register codes in `ad3552r_hs_set_output_range`. [F199, F215]
- In `ad5592r-base.c` and `ad5592r.c`, `st->gpio_out`, `st->gpio_in`, and `st->gpio_val` are shared between `CH_MODE_GPIO` and `CH_MODE_UNUSED` pins, and `ad5592r_gpio_read` must OR `st->gpio_in` with `AD5592R_GPIO_READBACK_EN` when writing `AD5592R_REG_GPIO_IN_EN`. In `ad5592r_write_raw` (`IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE`), read `AD5592R_REG_CTRL` into `st->cached_gp_ctrl`, update only `AD5592R_REG_CTRL_DAC_RANGE` or `AD5592R_REG_CTRL_ADC_RANGE`, and write it back under `st->lock`. [F205, F206]
- In `ad5592r_alloc_channels`, check child `'reg'` against `ARRAY_SIZE(st->channel_modes)` before indexing `st->channel_modes` or `st->channel_offstate`, and allocate `channels` with at least `1 + 2 * num_channels` entries (`CH_MODE_DAC_AND_ADC` uses two entries per pin plus `IIO_TEMP`). [F217]
- In `ad5758.c`, `ad5758_spi_reg_read` and `ad5758_spi_reg_write` use 4-byte (`__be32`) frames: command/addr in `[31:24]`, 16-bit payload in `[23:8]`, `[7:0]` reserved (`(be32_to_cpu(st->d32[2]) >> 8) & 0xFFFF` on readback). `ad5758_init` must call `ad5758_crc_disable` again after `ad5758_reset`, and `ad5758_set_dc_dc_conv_mode` must enable `AD5758_ADC_CONFIG_PPC_BUF_EN(1)` before setting `AD5758_DCDC_MODE_PPC_CURRENT` in `AD5758_DCDC_CONFIG1`. [F213, F224]
- In `ad5758.c`, `ad5758_dc_dc_ilim` and `ad5758_sr_step` must be sorted ascending for `bsearch` and `ad5758_find_closest_match`; in `ad5758_parse_dt()`, `tmp`, `ad5758_dc_dc_ilim` elements, `index`, and `cmpfunc` must all be `int` for `bsearch()` and `index - ad5758_dc_dc_ilim`. [F228, F260]
- In `ad5766.c`, `ad5766_trigger_handler()` pops into `u16 data[ARRAY_SIZE(ad5766_channels)]` and passes `*indio_dev->active_scan_mask` as `u16` to `__ad5766_spi_write()` (requiring `num_channels <= 16`, `storagebits == 16`), using `ch` from `for_each_set_bit()` in `AD5766_CMD_WR_IN_REG(ch)` and `data[i++]`. Split 32-bit `st->dither_source` and `st->dither_scale` across two 16-bit writes via `FIELD_GET` with `AD5766_LOWER_WORD_SPI_MASK` (ch 0..7) and `AD5766_UPPER_WORD_SPI_MASK` (ch 8..15). [F227, F246, F265]
- In `ad5766_default_setup()` and `ad5761_spi_set_range()`, reset the chip (`ad5766_reset()` or `_ad5761_spi_write(st, AD5761_ADDR_SW_FULL_RESET, 0)`) before writing the range to `AD5766_CMD_SPAN_REG` or `AD5761_ADDR_CTRL_WRITE_REG`. [F274]
- In `ad5755.c`, initialize both `st->channels[i].type` (`ad5755_init_channels`) and `st->ctrl[i] & 7` (`ad5755_setup_pdata`) from `pdata->dac[i].mode` for `ad5755_get_min_max`. In `ad5755_set_channel_pwr_down()`, enabling a channel under `st->lock` must set `AD5755_DAC_INT_EN | AD5755_DAC_DC_DC_EN` via `ad5755_update_dac_ctrl()`, `udelay(200)`, and then set `AD5755_DAC_OUT_EN`. [F207, F278]
- In `ad5360.c`, `ad5380.c`, and `ad5755.c`, copy `chip_info->channel_template` into a per-instance `iio_chan_spec` array before mutating it. In `ad5360`, `ad5360_get_channel_vref_index` must clamp `channel / st->chip_info->channels_per_group` to `st->chip_info->num_vrefs - 1`, and `ad5360_write_raw` / `ad5360_read_raw` must shift negated 14-bit `AD5360_REG_SF_OFS` offsets by `(chan->scan_type.realbits - 14)`. [F208, F225, F226]
- In `ad5064`, `ad5446`, and `ad5686`, `pwr_down_mode` stores 1-based hardware codes (`1..3`, initialized to `1` at probe) while `*_get/set_powerdown_mode` use 0-based enum indices (`mode - 1`). Check `!st->use_internal_vref` in `ad5064` (where `st->vref_reg[0].consumer` can be `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)`) and `st->chip_info->int_vref == 0` in `ad5764` before accessing `st->vref_reg`. [F192, F193, F195]
- In `ad5770r.c`, update `st->ch_pwr_down[chan->channel]` in lockstep with `AD5770R_CFG_SHUTDOWN_B` (`AD5770R_CHANNEL_CONFIG`), `AD5770R_CH_SET` (`AD5770R_CH_ENABLE`), and—on ch 0 when `out_range_mode > AD5770R_CH0_0_300`—`AD5770R_CFG_CH0_SINK_EN`; keep `ad5770r_filter_freqs[]` and `ad5770r_filter_reg_vals[]` 1-to-1 and bounds-checked (`i < ARRAY_SIZE(...)`). [F210, F247]
- In `ad7303.c`, OR `st->config` into every `ad7303_write` and pass `st->dac_cache[chan->channel]` in `ad7303_write_dac_powerdown` under `st->lock`. In `ad5791.c`, `st->ctrl` caches `AD5791_ADDR_CTRL`, `st->pwr_down` tracks `AD5791_CTRL_OPGND` / `AD5791_CTRL_DACTRI`, and `ad5791_buffer_preenable` must return `-EINVAL` when `st->pwr_down` is true. In `ad5421_fault_handler`, call `ad5421_update_ctrl(indio_dev, 0, 0)` on fault IRQ and only emit events for `(old_fault ^ fault) & fault`. [F203, F209, F214]

## Synchronization
- Functions in `ad5592r-base.c` and `ad3552r-hs.c` using `guard(mutex)` or `scoped_guard(mutex, ...)` on `st->lock` or `st->gpio_lock` must never call `mutex_unlock()` explicitly in that scope. [F159]
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`, `ad3552r_hs_write_raw()` must bracket bus accesses with `iio_device_claim_direct()` / `iio_device_release_direct()` (buffered mode uses DSPI/QSPI DDR). Initialize `st->lock` via `devm_mutex_init()` before `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init()` and hold it in `ad3552r_hs_show_data_source` / `ad3552r_hs_write_data_source`. [F172, F173]
- In `ad5592r-base.c`, `gpio_chip` callbacks must hold `st->gpio_lock` across `st->gpio_out`, `st->gpio_in`, `st->gpio_val`, and GPIO register ops; IIO callbacks (`ad5592r_read_raw`, `ad5592r_write_raw`) must hold `st->lock` across `st->ops` bus calls and `st->cached_dac` / `st->cached_gp_ctrl` accesses. [F176, F177]
- Post-probe callers must hold `st->lock` across cache/buffer updates and bus transfers when calling: `ad5064_write()` and `ad5064_sync_powerdown_mode()` (`ad5064.c`); `ad5758_spi_reg_read()`, `ad5758_spi_reg_write()`, and `ad5758_spi_write_mask()` (`ad5758.c`); and `ad5360_write_unlocked`, `ad5421_write_unlocked`, `ad5755_write_unlocked`, `ad5755_write_ctrl_unlocked`, and `ad5755_update_dac_ctrl` (`ad5360.c`, `ad5421.c`, `ad5755.c`). [F178, F184, F290]
- Register `ad5421_fault_handler` only as `thread_fn` in `devm_request_threaded_irq` with `IRQF_ONESHOT` (locks `st->lock`, does SPI I/O, calls `msleep(1000)`). In `ad5766_trigger_handler`, hold `st->lock` around `__ad5766_spi_write` and jump to `done` on every exit path so `iio_trigger_notify_done(indio_dev->trig)` always runs. [F167, F287, F288]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `ad3552r_hs_probe()`, call `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init(indio_dev)` after `devm_iio_device_register()`. In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_predisable()`, switch the bus to `AD3552R_IO_MODE_SPI` and disable DDR on both target (`st->config_d` write) and backend (`iio_backend_ddr_disable()`) before calling `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits()`. [F162, F297]
- In `ad5592r_probe()`, run `ad5592r_set_channel_modes()` before `ad5592r_gpio_init()`. In `ad5592r_alloc_channels()`, call `fwnode_handle_put(child)` before any early exit from `device_for_each_child_node(st->dev, child)`. [F174, F234]
- With non-devres `iio_device_register()` (`ad5360`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r`, `ad5764`), remove/error paths must call `iio_device_unregister()` before disabling regulators, freeing `indio_dev->channels`, `ad5592r_reset_channel_modes()`, or `ad5592r_gpio_cleanup()`. With `devm_iio_device_register()` (`ad5064`, `ad7303`), register `devm_add_action_or_reset()` for regulator disable before `devm_iio_device_register()`. [F165, F279, F280]
- In dual-bus modules (`ad5064`, `ad5380`, `ad5446`), register SPI before I2C in `init` (and unregister SPI if I2C fails), and unregister I2C before SPI in `exit`. In `ad5755_parse_fw()`, call `devm_kfree(dev, pdata)` before returning `NULL` after `devm_kzalloc()`. In `ad5791_offload_setup()`, keep `offload_msg` and `offload_xfer` in `struct ad5791_state`, not on the stack. [F170, F171, F263]

## API Contracts
- Read helpers returning data or negative errno (`ad5360_read`, `ad5421_read`, `ad5504_spi_read`, `ad5686_spi_read`, `ad5686_i2c_read`, `ad5755_read`, `ad5758_spi_reg_read`) require `ret < 0` checks (before `!(ret & mask)` in `ad5758_wait_for_task_complete()`). I2C write callbacks (`ad5064_i2c_write`, `ad5622_write`, `ad5686_i2c_write`) must normalize positive `i2c_master_send` return values to `0` (short writes to `-EIO`). [F188, F189, F242]
- In `ad3552r-common.c` and `ad3552r-hs.c`: `ad3552r_get_output_range` returns `-ENOENT` when `"adi,output-range-microvolt"` is absent, and callers must only fall back to `ad3552r_get_custom_gain` on `-ENOENT`; `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset` requires `ch_data->range_override = 1` with `p`, `n`, `rfb`, and `gain_offset` for custom gain (or `range_override = 0` with valid `ch_data->range`); `ad3552r_hs_read_raw` must return `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_MICRO` for scale/offset. [F190, F201, F294]
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`: callers of `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits()` must pre-shift `val` to `mask`; `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable()` must configure backend transfer addr/format/stream only after DAC register writes and bus mode transitions, and on unwind reset `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_TRANSFER_REGISTER` to `AD3552R_SPI` before `bus_set_io_mode()`; `ad3552r_hs_reg_access` must check `reg > st->model_data->max_reg_addr` and pass `xfer_size = 1` to `ad3552r_hs_reg_read`. In `.debugfs_reg_access` callbacks (`ad3552r_hs`, `ad5758`, `ad5770r`), branch on `if (readval)`. [F196, F216, F232, F302]
- In `ad5592r-base.c`, `ad5592r.c`, and `ad5593r.c`: `ad5592r_read_raw` (`IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE`) divides by `1000000000LL` and must return `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_NANO`; `ad5592r_gpio_init` must set `can_sleep = true` and `ad5592r_gpio_request` must reject offsets not in `st->gpio_map` with `-ENODEV`; `ad5592r_read_adc()` must issue two `ad5592r_spi_wnop_r16()` calls after `AD5592R_REG_ADC_SEQ`; `ad5593r_read_word()` must use `i2c_smbus_write_byte()` then `i2c_master_recv()` and `get_unaligned_be16()`. [F271, F283, F292, F301]
- In dual-bus drivers (`ad5064`, `ad5446`, `ad5592r`/`ad5593r`, `ad5686`/`ad5696`), shared code must not downcast `st->dev` to `spi_device` or `i2c_client`. Bus drvdata in `ad5360`, `ad5421`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r-base`, `ad5755`, `ad5758`, `ad5764`, and `ad5770r` stores `struct iio_dev *` (use `iio_priv()`), while `ad5360` `device_attribute` callbacks must use `dev_to_iio_dev(dev)`. Check `st->slew_time != 0` before calling `ad5758_slew_rate_config()`. [F253, F261, F262]

## Error Handling
- Store return values of `ad5360_write_unlocked` and `ad5421_write_unlocked` in signed `int`, not `unsigned int` (e.g. in `ad5360_update_ctrl`, `ad5421_update_ctrl`). Reset `st->reg` / `st->vref_reg` to `NULL` in `ad5592r_probe` and `ad7303_probe` when `devm_regulator_get_optional` returns `-ENODEV`. Do not overwrite `ret` in unwind labels of `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable` and `ad5592r_probe`. [F187, F191, F194]

## External Contracts
- In `ad5592r-base.c`, `ad5592r_read_raw` must verify bits `[14:12]` of `st->ops->read_adc` equal `(chan->channel & 0x7)` before masking with `GENMASK(11, 0)`, and `ad5592r_write_raw_get_fmt` must return `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_NANO` for `IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE`. In `ad5624r_probe`, fall back to `"vcc"` when `"vref"` returns `-ENODEV`. [F218, F219, F229]

## Known Pitfalls
- Where `chan->address` differs from `chan->channel` (`ad5064`, `ad5360`, `ad5504`, `ad5686`, `ad5770r`), pass `chan->address` to hardware helpers and index state arrays/bitmasks with `chan->channel`. In `ad3552r_get_custom_gain()`, read custom gain properties from `gain_child` (`"custom-output-range-config"`), not `child`. [F197, F233]
- In `ad5504.c`, `st->pwr_down_mask` is active-high (`1 << chan->channel` = powered up), and every `AD5504_ADDR_CTRL` write requires a follow-up `AD5504_ADDR_NOOP` write. In `ad5766.c`, `st->dither_enable` is active-low (`0` = enabled), and `ad5766_get_dither_source()` must subtract `1` from the shifted 1-hot 2-bit `st->dither_source` field. In `ad5764_read_raw()`, sign-extend `AD5764_REG_OFFSET` (`sign_extend32(*val, 7)`) and `AD5764_REG_FINE_GAIN` (`sign_extend32(*val, 5)`). [F241, F245, F255]
- Avoid 32-bit overflow and raw 64-bit `/` in scale/offset/slew math: cast `rem` to `s64` before `* 1000000` and use `DIV_ROUND_CLOSEST()` / `div_s64()` in `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset()`; use `s64` (`common`, `gn`, `gp`, `offset`, `tmp`) and `div_s64()` in `ad3552r_get_custom_range()`; widen `vref_mV` to `s64` and use `div_s64_rem(..., 1000000000LL)` in `ad5592r_init_scales()` and `ad5592r_read_raw()` (`IIO_TEMP`); cast `st->vref_neg_mv` to `u64` before `<< chan->scan_type.realbits` and use `do_div()` in `ad5791_read_raw()`; keep `AD5758_FULL_SCALE_MICRO` in `u64` and divide with two sequential `do_div()` steps in `ad5758_slew_rate_config()`. [F248, F249, F250, F251, F252]
