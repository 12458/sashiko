# AMD POWERPLAY AND SWSMU — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/pm/swsmu (part 2)

This component provides common SMU communication helpers (`smu_cmn.c`), internal macro dispatchers (`smu_internal.h`), and the SMU v14.0 hardware backend (`smu_v14_0.c`, `smu_v14_0_0_ppt.c`, `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`) for AMD GPUs and APUs. It manages firmware loading, C2PMSG mailbox command/response transactions, shared DRAM table transfers, DPM clock/power/thermal policy configuration, OverDrive settings, I2C adapter transfers, and thermal/MP1 interrupt handling.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- On APU SMU v14.0.0, `smu_v14_0_0_set_ppt_funcs()` and `smu_v14_0_0_init_smc_tables()` leave `smu->clock_map`, `smu->pwr_src_map`, `smu->workload_map`, and `smu->smu_dpm.dpm_context` NULL; `smu_v14_0_0_ppt_funcs` must not wire in `smu_v14_0.c` helpers that dereference `dpm_context` (`smu_v14_0_set_performance_level`) or require `CMN2ASIC_MAPPING_CLK` (`smu_v14_0_set_soft_freq_limited_range`, `smu_v14_0_get_dpm_ultimate_freq`). [F33]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_ppt.c`, `smu->smu_table.clocks_table` and `tables[SMU_TABLE_DPMCLOCKS]` hold `DpmClocks_t_v14_0_1` when `amdgpu_ip_version(adev, MP1_HWIP, 0) == IP_VERSION(14, 0, 1)` and `DpmClocks_t` otherwise; allocations must use `max(sizeof(DpmClocks_t), sizeof(DpmClocks_t_v14_0_1))` and readers must branch on the IP version before casting. [F66]
- In `DpmClocks_t` and `DpmClocks_t_v14_0_1` (`smu_v14_0_0_ppt.c`), `MemPstateTable` is ordered from highest frequency at index `0` to lowest at `NumMemPstatesEnabled - 1`; `SMU_MCLK`/`SMU_UCLK` queries must use level `0` for max, `NumMemPstatesEnabled - 1` for min, and `count - i - 1` when printing ascending levels in `smu_v14_0_0_print_clk_levels`. [F67]
- In `smu_v14_0_set_irq_state()`, `smu_v14_0_check_fw_status()`, and `smu_v14_0_load_microcode()`, APU and dGPU variants use different MP1 register offsets (`smnMP1_FIRMWARE_FLAGS_14_0_0` and `regMP1_SMN_IH_SW_INT*_mp1_14_0_0` vs `smnMP1_FIRMWARE_FLAGS` and `regMP1_SMN_IH_SW_INT*`) and must branch on `smu->is_apu`. [F113]
- In `smu_v14_0_irq_process()`, handling `SMU_IH_INTERRUPT_ID_TO_DRIVER` from `SOC15_IH_CLIENTID_MP1` must acknowledge the interrupt by setting `MP1_SMN_IH_SW_INT_CTRL__INT_ACK` to `1` in `regMP1_SMN_IH_SW_INT_CTRL`, and updating `DIG_THERM_INTH` in `regTHM_THERMAL_INT_CTRL` must clear `THM_THERMAL_INT_CTRL__THERM_TRIGGER_MASK_MASK`. [F115]
- In `smu_v14_0_enable_thermal_alert()` and `smu_v14_0_disable_thermal_alert()`, skip `amdgpu_irq_get()` and `amdgpu_irq_put()` when `smu->irq_source.num_types == 0` (e.g., SR-IOV VFs where `smu_v14_0_register_irq_handler()` returns early), and call `smu_v14_0_allow_ih_interrupt()` (`SMU_MSG_AllowIHHostInterrupt`) only after `amdgpu_irq_get()` succeeds. [F116]
- In `smu_v14_0_baco_set_armd3_sequence` and `smu_v14_0_baco_set_state`, update `smu->smu_baco.state` to `SMU_BACO_STATE_ENTER` or `SMU_BACO_STATE_EXIT` only after `SMU_MSG_ArmD3`, `SMU_MSG_EnterBaco`, or `SMU_MSG_ExitBaco` succeeds, and zero VBIOS scratch registers `adev->bios_scratch_reg_offset + 6` and `+ 7` on `SMU_MSG_ExitBaco`. In `smu_v14_0_2_baco_enter()`/`exit()`, when `adev->in_runpm && smu_cmn_is_audio_func_enabled(adev)` is true, use `smu_v14_0_baco_set_armd3_sequence()` instead of `smu_v14_0_baco_enter()`/`exit()`. [F49, F117]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_system_features_control()`, send `SMU_MSG_PrepareMp1ForUnload` only when disabling system features (`!en`) and not entering S0i3 (`!adev->in_s0ix`). [F119]
- In `smu_v14_0_set_vcn_enable()` and `smu_v14_0_set_jpeg_enable()`, skip harvested instances (`adev->vcn.harvest_config & (1 << inst)` or `adev->jpeg.harvest_config & (1 << i)`) before sending power-up/down SMC messages, and on APUs send `SMU_MSG_PowerUpJpeg1`/`SMU_MSG_PowerDownJpeg1` only on `IP_VERSION(14, 0, 1)`. [F120]
- In `smu_v14_0_init_microcode` and `smu_v14_0_init_pptable_microcode`, when `adev->firmware.load_type == AMDGPU_FW_LOAD_PSP`, populating `adev->firmware.ucode[AMDGPU_UCODE_ID_SMC]` or `adev->firmware.ucode[AMDGPU_UCODE_ID_PPTABLE]` must also add `ALIGN(..., PAGE_SIZE)` of the payload size to `adev->firmware.fw_size`. [F45]
- `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table` caches `SMU_TABLE_SMU_METRICS` in `smu_table->metrics_table` timestamped by `smu_table->metrics_time`; `metrics_time` must be reset to `0` when `metrics_table` is allocated or freed (`smu_v14_0_0_init_smc_tables`, `smu_v14_0_2_tables_init`, `smu_v14_0_fini_smc_tables`) and updated only after `smu_cmn_update_table` succeeds. [F44]
- `smu_table->gpu_metrics_table` must be allocated in `smu_v14_0_0_init_smc_tables` and `smu_v14_0_2_tables_init` (`smu_table->gpu_metrics_table_size`) with at least the size of the `struct gpu_metrics_v<frev>_<crev>` passed to `smu_cmn_init_soft_gpu_metrics` in `get_gpu_metrics`. [F97]
- In `smu_v14_0_common_set_performance_level` and `smu_v14_0_od_edit_dpm_table`, `smu->gfx_actual_hard_min_freq` and `smu->gfx_actual_soft_max_freq` track active or staged SCLK limits and must be reset to `0` when `smu_v14_0_common_set_fine_grain_gfx_freq_parameters` reinitializes default bounds. [F47]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_set_default_od_settings`, when `smu->adev->in_suspend` is true and `smu->user_dpm_profile.user_od` is set, `user_od_table` must be backed up to `user_od_table_bak`, refreshed from `boot_od_table`, and have all user-editable OverDrive fields restored from `user_od_table_bak`. [F53]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_od_edit_dpm_table` and `smu_v14_0_2_restore_user_od_settings`, `OverDriveTable.FeatureCtrlMask` must be saved and restored across `PP_OD_RESTORE_DEFAULT_TABLE` `memcpy` from `boot_overdrive_table` (which then falls through to `PP_OD_COMMIT_DPM_TABLE`, unlike `smu_v14_0_od_edit_dpm_table` which breaks), skipped via `offset_of_voltageoffset` when updating `user_overdrive_table`, and zeroed after `smu_v14_0_2_upload_overdrive_table`. [F54, F77]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_od_edit_dpm_table` and `smu_v14_0_2_od_restore_table_single`, editing fan curve points (`PP_OD_EDIT_FAN_CURVE`) must set `od_table->OverDriveTable.FanMode = FAN_MODE_MANUAL_LINEAR` and `BIT(PP_OD_FEATURE_FAN_CURVE_BIT)` in `FeatureCtrlMask`, while acoustic/target/min-PWM edits and fan restores must set `FanMode = FAN_MODE_AUTO` and update `FeatureCtrlMask`. [F55]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`, user-visible OverDrive fan curve points are restricted to indices `0` through `NUM_OD_FAN_MAX_POINTS - 2` (5 points, `i < NUM_OD_FAN_MAX_POINTS - 1`) in `PP_OD_EDIT_FAN_CURVE` and `smu_v14_0_2_print_clk_levels`, even though `FanLinearTempPoints` and `FanLinearPwmPoints` in `OverDriveTable_t` have `NUM_OD_FAN_MAX_POINTS` (6) elements. [F70]
- In SMU v14.0.2, `smu_14_0_pcie_table` stores 0-based `pcie_gen[i]` (`0..4`) and encoded `pcie_lane[i]` (`1..7`), whereas `SmuMetrics_t` reports 1-based `PcieRate` (`1..5`) and decoded `PcieWidth` (`1..32`); use `DECODE_GEN_SPEED` and `DECODE_LANE_WIDTH` when comparing them. [F64]
- `smu->custom_profile_params` in `smu_v14_0_2_set_power_profile_mode` is a flat `2 * 9` `long` buffer (`SMU_14_0_2_CUSTOM_PARAMS_SIZE`) indexed by `custom_params[0] * SMU_14_0_2_CUSTOM_PARAMS_COUNT`, where slot `idx` is an enable flag (`0` or `1`) and slots `idx + 1 .. idx + 8` hold the 8 clock activity monitor coefficients; validate `custom_params_max_idx == SMU_14_0_2_CUSTOM_PARAMS_COUNT` and `custom_params[0] < SMU_14_0_2_CUSTOM_PARAMS_CLOCK_COUNT`. [F73]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_xfer`, the final byte of the last message or any `I2C_M_STOP` message must clear `CMDCONFIG_RESTART_MASK` when setting `CMDCONFIG_STOP_MASK` on `cmd->CmdConfig` (overriding any direction-change restart bit set earlier in that iteration), and the response-collection loop must advance `c += msg[i].len` before `continue` on non-`I2C_M_RD` messages so `c` stays aligned with `SwI2cCmds[c]`. [F75, F76]

## Synchronization

- `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_xfer()` must acquire `adev->pm.mutex` around `smu_cmn_update_table(smu, SMU_TABLE_I2C_COMMANDS, 0, req, true)`, and `adev->pm.mutex` always nests outside `smu->message_lock`. [F19]
- `smu_v14_0_irq_process()` runs in interrupt context and must not call sleeping SMU mailbox functions (`smu_cmn_send_smc_msg_with_param()`, `smu_cmn_update_table()`, `read_sensor`); it must defer `THM_11_0__SRCID__THM_DIG_THERM_L2H` to `smu->swctf_delayed_work` via `schedule_delayed_work()` and handle `SMU_IH_INTERRUPT_ID_TO_DRIVER` via direct MMIO. [F20]

## Object Lifetimes

- `smu_cmn_is_audio_func_enabled` acquires a PCI device reference via `pci_get_domain_bus_and_slot` and must release it with `pci_dev_put(p)` before returning whenever `p` is non-NULL. [F2]
- `smu_v14_0_setup_pptable` and `smu_v14_0_2_setup_pptable` set `smu->smu_table.power_play_table` to a borrowed pointer (into VBIOS data tables, `adev->pm.fw->data`, or `smu_table->combo_pptable`), and `smu_v14_0_2_check_powerplay_table` sets `smu->od_settings` to `&powerplay_table->overdrive_table` inside `power_play_table`; neither `power_play_table` nor `smu->od_settings` may be `kfree`d during table or context teardown (`smu_v14_0_fini_smc_tables`). [F4, F5]
- `smu_v14_0_init_microcode` must call `amdgpu_ucode_release(&adev->pm.fw)` on error; when `adev->firmware.load_type == AMDGPU_FW_LOAD_PSP`, `adev->firmware.ucode[AMDGPU_UCODE_ID_SMC].fw` borrows `adev->pm.fw` until `smu_v14_0_fini_microcode` releases `adev->pm.fw` (do not release `ucode->fw` separately). [F14]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_init()`, initialize `smu_i2c->adev`, `smu_i2c->port`, `smu_i2c->mutex`, `control->algo`, `control->quirks`, and `i2c_set_adapdata(control, smu_i2c)` before `devm_i2c_add_adapter(adev->dev, control)` publishes the adapter; because registration uses `devm_i2c_add_adapter`, `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_fini` must only null `adev->pm.ras_eeprom_i2c_bus` and `adev->pm.fru_eeprom_i2c_bus` without calling `i2c_del_adapter`. [F10, F21]
- `smu_v14_0_2_init_smc_tables()` must run `smu_v14_0_2_tables_init()` before `smu_v14_0_init_smc_tables()`, because `smu_v14_0_init_smc_tables()` reads `tables[SMU_TABLE_PPTABLE].size`, `tables[SMU_TABLE_OVERDRIVE].size`, and `tables[SMU_TABLE_COMBO_PPTABLE].size` to allocate `driver_pptable`, OverDrive tables, and `combo_pptable`. [F34]

## API Contracts

- `smu_v14_0_0_get_gpu_metrics()` and `smu_v14_0_2_get_gpu_metrics()` return `ssize_t`: they must call `smu_cmn_init_soft_gpu_metrics()` on the typed `gpu_metrics_v*` buffer before assigning fields (populating `metrics_table_header` and presetting unpopulated fields to `0xFF`), return a negative `errno` without modifying `*table` if `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table()` fails, and on success set `*table = smu_table->gpu_metrics_table` and return the positive struct byte size. [F27, F28]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`, `SMU_TABLE_SMU_METRICS` and `SMU_TABLE_ACTIVITY_MONITOR_COEFF` are sized to `SmuMetricsExternal_t` and `DpmActivityMonitorCoeffIntExternal_t`; callers of `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table()` or `smu_cmn_update_table()` for those tables must pass a pointer to the `*External_t` wrapper rather than the inner `SmuMetrics_t` or `DpmActivityMonitorCoeffInt_t`. [F32]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`, `smu_v14_0_2_is_od_feature_supported()` takes a PMFW `PP_OD_FEATURE_*_BIT` bit index to test `FeatureCtrlMask`, whereas `smu_v14_0_2_get_od_setting_limits()` takes a file-local `PP_OD_FEATURE_*` setting selector (`0..11`), despite both parameters being named `int od_feature_bit`. [F36]
- `smu->smu_table.boot_values` clocks (`gfxclk`, `uclk`, `socclk`, `dcefclk`, `vclk`, `dclk`, `fclk`) and `read_sensor` outputs for `AMDGPU_PP_SENSOR_GFX_{MCLK,SCLK}` are in 10 kHz units, whereas `smu_14_0_dpm_table`, `get_dpm_ultimate_freq`, and `SmuMetrics_t` clocks are in MHz, requiring `/ 100` and `* 100` conversions. [F38]
- In `smu->thermal_range` (`struct smu_temperature_range`), `min`, `max`, `*_crit_max`, and `*_emergency_max` are in millidegrees Celsius (`* SMU_TEMPERATURE_UNITS_PER_CENTIGRADES`), whereas `software_shutdown_temp` and `software_shutdown_temp_offset` are in whole degrees Celsius and programmed directly into `DIG_THERM_INTH`. [F39]
- `smu_cmn_feature_is_supported()`, `smu_cmn_feature_is_enabled()`, `smu_cmn_feature_set_enabled()`, and `smu_cmn_disable_all_features_with_exception()` take an `enum smu_feature_mask` bit index, whereas `smu_cmn_feature_update_enable_state()` and `smu_cmn_set_pp_feature_mask()` take a 64-bit shifted bitmask (`1ULL << feature_id`). [F40]
- `smu_cmn_to_asic_specific_index` returns `-ENOTSUPP` for unmapped `CMN2ASIC_MAPPING_WORKLOAD` entries (unlike `-EINVAL` for other mapping types), and `smu_v14_0_2_get_power_profile_mode` relies on `-ENOTSUPP` to `continue` past unsupported profiles while aborting on other negative errors. [F78]
- `smu_v14_0_notify_memory_pool_location`, `smu_v14_0_set_driver_table_location`, and `smu_v14_0_set_tool_table_location` must pass the GPU MC address (`mc_address`), not `cpu_addr`, split via `upper_32_bits` and `lower_32_bits` to the SMU. [F128]
- `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_init` must register `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_quirks` (capping lengths to `MAX_SW_I2C_COMMANDS` and enforcing `I2C_AQ_COMB_SAME_ADDR`), and `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_xfer` must shift the 7-bit `msg[0].addr` left by 1 for `SwI2cRequest_t.SlaveAddress` and return `num_msgs` on success. [F144]

## External Contracts

- `smu_v14_0_get_dpm_freq_by_index` must mask the `SMU_MSG_GetDpmFreqByIndex` response with `0x7fffffff` because PMFW uses bit 31 (`0x80000000`) to report fine-grained DPM status (when `level == 0xff`). [F69]

## Known Pitfalls

- `pcie_gen_to_speed` indexes the 6-element `link_speed` array with `gen - 1` (for 1-based PCIe Gen 1..6) without upper-bound validation; callers such as `smu_v14_0_2_get_gpu_metrics` must bound-check firmware-reported `metrics->PcieRate` before calling it. [F63]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_set_watermarks_table`, `WM_DCFCLK` (`reader_wm_sets`) maps `MinClock`/`MaxClock` to `min_drain_clk_mhz`/`max_drain_clk_mhz` and `MinMclk`/`MaxMclk` to `min_fill_clk_mhz`/`max_fill_clk_mhz`, whereas `WM_SOCCLK` (`writer_wm_sets`) swaps them (`MinClock`/`MaxClock` from `fill_clk` and `MinMclk`/`MaxMclk` from `drain_clk`). [F82]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_get_smu_metrics_data`, `METRICS_SS_APU_SHARE` must check `metrics->StapmOpnLimit > 0` and `METRICS_SS_DGPU_SHARE` must check `metrics->StapmCurrentLimit > metrics->StapmOpnLimit` before dividing by the STAPM limit or difference. [F87]
- In `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table`, check cache expiration with `!smu_table->metrics_time || time_after(jiffies, smu_table->metrics_time + msecs_to_jiffies(1))` rather than direct relational comparison on `jiffies`. [F91]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_force_clk_levels` and `smu_v14_0_2_force_clk_levels`, guard against `mask == 0` before computing `ffs(mask) - 1` and `fls(mask) - 1` for `soft_min_level` and `soft_max_level`. [F92]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_od_edit_dpm_table`, adding `offset_of_voltageoffset` (`offsetof(OverDriveTable_t, VoltageOffsetPerZoneBoundary)`) to a typed `OverDriveTableExternal_t *` pointer such as `od_table` requires casting the pointer to `(u8 *)` or `(void *)` first. [F98]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`, I2C callbacks receiving `struct amdgpu_smu_i2c_bus *smu_i2c` from `i2c_get_adapdata` (such as `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_xfer`) must obtain the device pointer via `smu_i2c->adev`, not `to_amdgpu_device(smu_i2c)`. [F103]
