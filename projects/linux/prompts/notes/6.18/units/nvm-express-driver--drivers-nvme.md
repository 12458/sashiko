# NVM EXPRESS DRIVER — drivers/nvme

`drivers/nvme` implements the NVMe host subsystem, fabrics library, ANA multipath, passthrough (`ioctl`/`io_uring`), and DH-HMAC-CHAP/TLS PSK management for `nvme_subsystem`, `nvme_ctrl`, `nvme_ns_head`, and `nvme_ns`.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `gendisk->private_data` is `nvme_ns_head *` when `nvme_disk_is_ns_head(disk)` (`disk->fops == &nvme_ns_head_ops`) and `nvme_ns *` otherwise. `req->q->queuedata` is `nvme_ns *` on I/O queues and `NULL` on admin/fabrics/connect queues (`nvme_init_request`, `nvme_req_qid`, `nvme_map_user_request`, `nvme_submit_user_cmd`, `nvme_log_error`). [F41, F42]
- `nvme_request` PDU fields (`status`, `retries`, `flags`) are not zeroed on allocation; paths completing un-prepped requests (`!(rq->rq_flags & RQF_DONTPREP)`) like `nvme_fail_nonready_command` must call `nvme_clear_nvme_request`. All completion side effects (`nvme_end_req_zoned`, error logging, bio tracepoints, `nvme_mpath_end_request`) must live in `__nvme_end_req` so both `nvme_end_req` and `nvme_complete_batch_req` execute them. [F47, F53]
- `nvme_change_ctrl_state` updates `ctrl->state` via `WRITE_ONCE` under `spin_lock_irqsave(&ctrl->lock)` (read via `nvme_ctrl_state`) and only allows: `LIVE` from `CONNECTING` (stopping `failfast_work`, clearing `NVME_CTRL_FAILFAST_EXPIRED`, kicking requeues); `RESETTING` from `NEW`/`LIVE`; `CONNECTING` from `NEW`/`RESETTING` (starting `failfast_work` from `RESETTING`); `DELETING` from `LIVE`/`RESETTING`/`CONNECTING`; `DELETING_NOIO` from `DELETING`/`DEAD`; `DEAD` from `DELETING`. `nvme_path_is_disabled` treats `LIVE` and `DELETING` as enabled, and `nvme_check_ready` returns `queue_live` for Fabrics in `DELETING`. [F32, F51, F94, F95]
- In `nvme_decide_disposition`, `blk_noretry_request(req)`, `NVME_STATUS_DNR`, and `retries >= nvme_max_retries` must return `COMPLETE` before checking `NVME_SC_AUTH_REQUIRED`, `REQ_NVME_MPATH` failover, or `RETRY`. `nvme_failover_req` must reset stolen bios to `ns->head->disk->part0`, clear `REQ_POLLED` (`bi_cookie = BLK_QC_T_NONE`) and `REQ_NOWAIT`, and zero `nvme_req(req)->status` before `nvme_end_req`. [F55, F89]
- In `nvme_update_ns_info`, format limits (`logical/physical_block_size`, `io_min/opt`, `max_write_streams`, `write_stream_granularity`) must be copied directly from `ns->disk->queue->limits` to `head->disk->queue`, and `nvme_mpath_revalidate_paths` must clear `NVME_NS_READY` on capacity-mismatched siblings, clear `head->current_path[]`, and schedule `requeue_work`. `nvme_mpath_add_sysfs_link` must check `GD_ADDED` on both `head->disk->state` and `ns->disk->state` before setting `NVME_NS_SYSFS_ATTR_LINK`, clearing it on error. With `CONFIG_NVME_MULTIPATH`, `nvme_alloc_ns_head` sizes `head->current_path[]` for `num_possible_nodes()`. [F54, F56, F71, F120]
- ANA callers (`nvme_scan_work`, `nvme_handle_aen_notice`, `nvme_failover_req`, `nvme_mpath_update/stop/add_disk`) must verify `ctrl->ana_log_buf != NULL`. In `nvme_mpath_init_identify`, if `ana_log_size > ctrl->ana_log_size`, call `nvme_mpath_stop` and `nvme_mpath_uninit` before reallocating `ana_log_buf`, reset `ctrl->nr_active = 0`, and do not call `nvme_mpath_set_live` from `nvme_update_ns_ana_state` while `ctrl->state != NVME_CTRL_LIVE` (`nvme_start_ctrl` calls `nvme_mpath_update` once live). [F60, F61, F143]
- `nvme_parse_ana_log` must verify `sizeof(*desc)` and `flex_array_size(desc, nsids, nr_nsids)` fit in `ctrl->ana_log_size` at `offset`, `desc->grpid` in `1..ctrl->anagrpmax`, and `desc->state` in `1..NVME_ANA_CHANGE`; `nvme_read_ana_log` tracks `nr_change_groups` (`NVME_ANA_CHANGE`) under `ctrl->ana_lock`, arming `anatt_timer` if `> 0` or calling `timer_delete_sync` if `0`. `nvme_id_cns_ok` bounds Identify `cns <= 1` if `vs < NVME_VS(1, 1, 0)` or `NVME_QUIRK_IDENTIFY_CNS`, and `<= 3` if `vs < NVME_VS(1, 2, 0)`; `nvme_identify_ns_descs` skips `NVME_ID_CNS_NS_DESC_LIST` if `(vs < NVME_VS(1, 3, 0) && !nvme_multi_css(ctrl))` or `NVME_QUIRK_NO_NS_DESC_LIST`, checks `cur->nidl == NVME_NIDT_*_LEN` in `nvme_process_ns_desc`, and breaks on `cur->nidl == 0` or `len < 0` before adding `sizeof(*cur)`. [F62, F74, F76, F102, F145]
- I/O `qid` is 1-based (`0` is admin): `nvme_req_qid` returns `queue_num + 1`, and `__nvme_submit_sync_cmd` passes `qid - 1` to `blk_mq_alloc_request_hctx`. 1-based selectors (`crd` in `nvme_retry_req`, `bi_write_stream` in `nvme_setup_rw`, `cur_fw_slot` in `nvme_get_fw_slot_info`) treat `0` as inactive and subtract 1 before indexing `ctrl->crdt[]` (`(status & NVME_STATUS_CRD) >> 11 <= 3`), `ns->head->plids[]`, and `log->frs[]`. [F78, F79, F110]
- `nvme_cid` packs `genctr & 0xf` in bits 12..15 and 12-bit `rq->tag` in bits 0..11; `nvme_find_rq` and `nvme_cid_to_rq` strip upper bits via `nvme_tag_from_cid`, and `nvme_find_rq` masks `nvme_req(rq)->genctr` with `nvme_genctr_mask` before comparing with `nvme_genctr_from_cid(command_id)`. [F80, F115]
- `nvme_command_effects` clears `NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_CSE_MASK` for I/O commands (`ns != NULL`) and `NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_CSER_MASK` admin commands; `nvme_init_known_nvm_effects` clears it on `nvme_admin_security_recv`. Passthrough submissions (`nvme_submit_io`, `nvme_user_cmd[64]`, `nvme_uring_cmd_io`) reject non-zero userspace `flags` with `-EINVAL`, and `nvme_init_request` clears `NVME_CMD_SGL_ALL`. [F73, F90]
- Modifying `NVME_FEAT_KATO` via passthrough (or reconnecting a discovery ctrl with `kato == 0` in `nvme_start_ctrl`) must call `nvme_stop_keep_alive`, update `ctrl->kato` (`DIV_ROUND_UP(le32_to_cpu(cdw11), 1000)`), and call `nvme_start_keep_alive`. In `nvme_queue_keep_alive_work`, guard `ka_next_check_tm - now` with `time_after(now, ka_next_check_tm)`. [F64, F112, F118]
- Discovery controllers (`NVME_DISC_SUBSYS_NAME`) never share an `nvme_subsystem` in `__nvme_find_get_subsystem`. `nvmf_host_add` enforces a 1-to-1 bijection between `host->nqn` and `host->id` under `nvmf_hosts_mutex`. `nvme_queue_scan` and `nvme_scan_work` require `nvme_ctrl_state(ctrl) == NVME_CTRL_LIVE && ctrl->tagset`. `NVME_AER_ERROR_PERSIST_INT_ERR` in `nvme_complete_async_event` calls `nvme_handle_aer_persistent_error` and returns without queueing `async_event_work`. [F66, F67, F96, F134]
- `nvme_set_ctrl_limits` and `nvme_mpath_alloc_disk` set `dma_alignment = 3` (plus `virt_boundary_mask = NVME_CTRL_PAGE_SIZE - 1` on `ctrl`). `nvme_setup_discard` always allocates/maps `sizeof(*range) * NVME_DSM_MAX_RANGES` bytes (`PAGE_SIZE`). `nvme_auth_extract_key` requires decoded DH-HMAC-CHAP keys of 36, 52, or 68 bytes (subtracting 4 for CRC32 read via `get_unaligned_le32`), and `nvme_auth_get_seqnum` skips 0 on wrap-around. [F77, F116, F122, F176]

## Synchronization
- Drain hierarchy: `nvme_do_delete_ctrl` (`nvme_delete_wq`) flushes `ctrl->reset_work` (`nvme_reset_wq`), which flushes `nvme_wq` via `nvme_stop_ctrl` and `nvme_remove_namespaces`. Leave `NVME_CTRL_LIVE` before flushing `async_event_work` in `nvme_stop_ctrl`. `nvme_remove_namespaces` must call `nvme_mpath_clear_ctrl_paths` and `nvme_unquiesce_io_queues` before `flush_work(&ctrl->scan_work)`. [F18, F20, F21]
- For `NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_CSE_MASK`, `nvme_passthru_start` locks `ctrl->scan_lock` before `ctrl->subsys->lock` and freezes multipath queues before controller queues; any caller receiving `effects != 0` must call `nvme_passthru_end` to unfreeze and unlock in reverse order. [F9, F28]
- `nvme_subsystems_lock` precedes `subsys->lock`, protects `nvme_subsystems` and `subsys->ctrls`, and `__nvme_find_get_subsystem` uses `kref_get_unless_zero(&subsys->ref)`. `nvme_subsys_iopolicy_update` calls `nvme_mpath_clear_ctrl_paths` across `subsys->ctrls` under `nvme_subsystems_lock`. [F35, F59, F164]
- `subsys->lock` protects `subsys->nsheads`, `head->list`, and `head->delayed_removal_secs` (updating `NVME_NSHEAD_QUEUE_IF_NO_PATH` in lockstep + `synchronize_srcu(&head->srcu)`). Lockless readers of `head->list` and `head->current_path[]` hold `srcu_read_lock(&head->srcu)`. `nvme_mpath_remove_disk` and `nvme_remove_head_work` must recheck `list_empty(&head->list)` under `subsys->lock` before unlinking `head->entry` and calling `nvme_remove_head`. [F25, F26, F58]
- `ctrl->namespaces` updates hold `ctrl->namespaces_lock` (aborting in `nvme_ns_add_to_ctrl_list` if `NVME_CTRL_FROZEN`) and use RCU list primitives; readers under `srcu_read_lock(&ctrl->srcu)` use `list_for_each_entry_srcu` and `nvme_get_ns`. `nvme_remove_[invalid_]namespaces` detaches under `namespaces_lock`, calls `synchronize_srcu(&ctrl->srcu)`, and runs `nvme_ns_remove` unlocked with `list_for_each_entry_safe`. `nvme_ns_remove` calls `synchronize_srcu(&ns->head->srcu)` after clearing `NVME_NS_READY`, after `nvme_mpath_clear_current_path`, and after `list_del_rcu(&ns->siblings)` under `subsys->lock`. [F22, F98, F171, F173]
- `ctrl->ana_log_buf` access requires `ctrl->ana_lock`, and `nvme_mpath_stop` stops `anatt_timer` and `ana_work` before `nvme_mpath_uninit` frees `ana_log_buf`. All `head->requeue_lock` acquisitions must disable IRQs (`spin_lock_irq[save]`). `nvme_mpath_alloc_disk` sets `GD_SUPPRESS_PART_SCAN` on `head->disk->state` so partition scan runs in `head->partition_scan_work`. `nvme_scan_ns_list` resets `scan_info.next_nsid = 0` per page and calls `async_synchronize_full_domain(&domain)` before reusing/freeing `ns_list`. [F29, F31, F101, F160]
- `nvme_uring_cmd_end_io` defers to `io_uring_cmd_do_in_task_lazy(ioucmd, nvme_uring_task_cb)`, where `nvme_uring_task_cb` unmaps `pdu->bio` (saved from `req->bio` before submission). `nvme_setup_discard` uses `GFP_ATOMIC | __GFP_NOWARN` with fallback to `ctrl->discard_page` via `test_and_set_bit_lock(0, &ctrl->discard_page_busy)` (freed by `nvme_cleanup_cmd`). Keep-alive and Fabrics register/connect commands use `BLK_MQ_REQ_RESERVED` / `NVME_SUBMIT_RESERVED`, and `NVME_QUIRK_SHARED_TAGS` sets `reserved_tags = NVME_AQ_DEPTH`. `nvmf_create_ctrl` holds `nvmf_transports_rwsem` across `nvmf_lookup_transport` and `try_module_get(ops->module)` on static `nvmf_transport_ops`. [F11, F34, F147, F156, F158, F174]

## Object Lifetimes
- `nvme_init_ctrl` takes ref 1 on `ctrl->ctrl_device` and `nvme_add_ctrl` takes ref 2; failure after `nvme_add_ctrl` requires both `nvme_uninit_ctrl` and `nvme_put_ctrl`. `nvme_do_delete_ctrl` runs in exact order: `flush_work(&ctrl->reset_work)`, `nvme_stop_ctrl`, `nvme_remove_namespaces`, `ctrl->ops->delete_ctrl`, `nvme_uninit_ctrl`. After `device_initialize(&subsys->dev)` in `nvme_init_subsystem`, free via `put_device` (keeping `subsys->instance = -1` until registered). `nvme_remove_admin_tag_set` calls `nvme_stop_keep_alive` before destroying `admin_q` / `admin_tagset`. Failed `nvme_alloc_admin/io_tag_set` must destroy allocated queues (`blk_mq_destroy_queue` + `blk_put_queue`), free tagsets, and NULL out `admin_q`/`fabrics_q`/`connect_q`. [F5, F13, F19, F131, F139]
- Dropping the last `nvme_ns` in `nvme_alloc_ns` rollback or `nvme_remove_head` / `nvme_mpath_remove_disk` must call `nvme_put_ns_head` whenever `head->disk != NULL` (not gated on `NVME_NSHEAD_DISK_LIVE`). Scheduling `head->remove_work` requires `try_module_get(THIS_MODULE)`, released by `module_put` in `nvme_remove_head_work`. `nvme_mpath_put_disk` must schedule/flush `requeue_work` and flush `partition_scan_work` before `put_disk(head->disk)`. [F1, F15, F16]
- `nvme_mpath_start_request` sets `NVME_MPATH_CNT_ACTIVE` and `NVME_MPATH_IO_STATS` in `nvme_req(rq)->flags`, which `nvme_mpath_end_request` tests to decrement `nr_active` and end IO stats. `nvme_ns_head_submit_bio` calls `bio_split_to_limits` before remapping `bio->bi_bdev` to `ns->disk->part0`. `nvme_ns_head_ctrl_ioctl` calls `nvme_get_ctrl(ns->ctrl)` and `srcu_read_unlock(&head->srcu)` before `nvme_ctrl_ioctl`. [F10, F14, F187]
- `nvmf_parse_options` must `kfree`/`key_put` prior string/key fields in `nvmf_ctrl_options` before overwriting, and reset `opts->host`, `opts->keyring`, or `opts->tls_key` to `NULL` on `IS_ERR`. Failed `nvme_identify_ctrl/ns` must `kfree(*id)` and set `*id = NULL`. Balance `dev_pm_qos_expose/hide_latency_tolerance` with `ctrl->apst_enabled` and hide in `nvme_uninit_ctrl`. [F7, F17, F45, F154]

## API Contracts
- Transports must put `struct nvme_request` at offset 0 of their PDU, call `nvme_cleanup_cmd` if `->queue_rq` fails after `nvme_setup_cmd`, propagate `BLK_STS_OK` from `nvme_host_path_error`, and set `BLK_FEAT_SKIP_TAGSET_QUIESCE` on `ctrl->connect_q`. [F8, F126, F192]
- `__nvme_submit_sync_cmd` only sets `*result` when `ret >= 0`. `nvme_map_user_request` takes `NVME_IOCTL_*` bitmasks (not `bool`). `nvme_start_freeze` / `nvme_unfreeze` use `blk_freeze_queue_start_non_owner` / `blk_mq_unfreeze_queue_non_owner`. `nvme_ctrl_from_file` verifies `file->f_op == &nvme_dev_fops`. `nvmf_create_ctrl` checks `(opts->mask & required_opts) != required_opts` and clears `~NVMF_REQUIRED_OPTS` before transport option checks. Allocate `nvme_dhchap_key` with `nvme_auth_key_struct_size(len)`. [F46, F48, F92, F121, F128, F185]

## Error Handling
- Sync helpers (`nvme_execute_rq`, `[__]nvme_submit_sync_cmd`, `nvme_set/get_features`, `nvme_get_log`) return `< 0` for errnos and `> 0` for NVMe status: guard `NVME_STATUS_DNR` with `ret > 0`, convert `ret > 0` via `blk_status_to_errno(nvme_error_status(ret))` before `ERR_PTR(ret)`, and treat `status < 0 || status == NVME_SC_HOST_PATH_ERROR` as fatal in `nvme_set_queue_count`. [F36, F37, F38]
- In `nvme_update_ns_info_block`, fallible checks must precede `queue_limits_start_update` / `blk_mq_freeze_queue` or cancel/unfreeze on error. Check `xa_is_err` on `xa_store(&ctrl->cels, ...)` in `nvme_get/init_effects_log` and free `cel` on error. In `nvme_uring_cmd_end_io`, fall back to `blk_status_to_errno(err)` when `nvme_req(req)->status == 0`, and never `WARN_ON_ONCE` on invalid user flags in `IORING_URING_CMD_FIXED` imports. [F2, F3, F40, F179]
- Do not map `NVME_SC_CMD_SIZE_LIM_EXCEEDED` (`0x183`) to `BLK_STS_NOTSUPP` in `nvme_error_status`. Set `RQF_QUIET` in `nvme_init_request` unless `passthru_err_log_enabled` is set, and check `!(req->rq_flags & RQF_QUIET)` in `__nvme_end_req`. Default zero `ctrl->maxcmd` to `ctrl->sqsize + 1` in `nvme_check_ctrl_fabric_info`. Abort `nvme_wait_ready` on `csts == ~0`, `fatal_signal_pending`, or `time_after`. [F97, F144, F152, F194]

## External Contracts
- `nvme_cmd_allowed` requires `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` if `NVME_IOCTL_PARTITION`, vendor/fabrics opcode, `!ns` (except `nvme_admin_identify` CNS `NS`/`CS_NS`/`NS_CS_INDEP`/`CS_CTRL`/`CTRL`), `!NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_CSUPP` or unknown effects, or write/`NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_LBCC` without `open_for_write`. Support `NVME_IOCTL_SUBMIT_IO32` on `COMPAT_FOR_U64_ALIGNMENT`, convert user pointers with `nvme_to_user_ptr`, and call `force_successful_syscall_return` for `NVME_IOCTL_ID` in `nvme_ns_ioctl`. Require `IO_URING_F_SQE128 | IO_URING_F_CQE32` in `nvme_uring_cmd_checks` and complete with `io_uring_cmd_done32`. [F91, F175, F177, F182]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `nvme_configure_apst`, return if `!ctrl->apsta || ctrl->npss > 31`, skip `state == ctrl->npss` on `NVME_QUIRK_NO_DEEPEST_PS`, and use signed `int state` for `state >= 0`. Clamp `info->runs` to `U32_MAX` for `write_stream_granularity`; use `check_shl_overflow` saturating to `UINT_MAX` in `nvme_mps_to_sectors`; bound `ctrl->dmrsl` by `nvme_sect_to_lba(ns->head, UINT_MAX)` in `nvme_config_discard`; reject `reconnect_delay <= 0` before `DIV_ROUND_UP` in `nvmf_parse_options`. [F82, F107, F108, F109, F114, F153]
- Use `sizeof(*head->plids)` in `nvme_query_fdp_info`; advance `void *log` by `le16_to_cpu(desc->dsze)` in `nvme_query_fdp_granularity`; use `(void *)cur + sizeof(*cur)` in `nvme_process_ns_desc`; never read `desc->nsids[]` on the stack `nvme_ana_group_desc` in `nvme_mpath_add_disk` / `nvme_update_ns_ana_state`; allocate `kzalloc(hmac_len + 1, GFP_KERNEL)` in `nvme_auth_generate_digest`. Convert `cdw10`..`cdw15` and `result` with `le32/64_to_cpu`, shift CQE status via `le16_to_cpu(status) >> 1` in `nvme_try_complete_req`, and store matched `nvme_ns` in a separate pointer (not the loop cursor) in `nvme_find_get_ns` and `nvme_round_robin_path`. [F85, F99, F105, F119, F123, F124, F125, F130]
