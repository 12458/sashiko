# COMMON INTERNET FILE SYSTEM CLIENT (CIFS and SMB3) — fs/smb/client (part 3)

Implements the Linux VFS and netfs integration layer for the SMB client, including file, `address_space`, inode, and dentry operations, `fs_context` mount parsing, symlinks, ioctls, DFS automounts, and fscache.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Under `cinode->open_file_lock`, `cifs_new_fileinfo()` must add `FMODE_READ` `cifsFileInfo` entries at the head of `cinode->openFileList` (`list_add`) and write-only entries at the tail (`list_add_tail`), as `find_readable_file()` stops at the first non-`FMODE_READ` entry. [F205]
- Oplocks & caching: open, reopen, or oplock downgrade (`cifs_new_fileinfo()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, `cifs_oplock_break()`) must reset read-only oplocks to `0` if `cifs_has_mand_locks(cinode)` is true. Uncached writes in `cifs_file_write_iter()` (`O_DIRECT`) or `cifs_strict_writev()` (`!CIFS_CACHE_WRITE(cinode)`) when `CIFS_CACHE_READ(cinode)` is true must call `cifs_zap_mapping(inode)` and set `cinode->oplock = 0`. [F253, F254]
- When `_cifsFileInfo_put()` empties `cifsi->openFileList`, it must call `cifs_set_oplock_level(cifsi, 0)` and, under `CIFS_MOUNT_STRICT_IO`, set `CIFS_INO_INVALID_MAPPING` on `cifsi->flags`. Opening with `O_DIRECT` on `CIFS_MOUNT_STRICT_IO` mounts in `cifs_atomic_open()` / `cifs_open()` must override `file->f_op` with `&cifs_file_direct_nobrl_ops` (`CIFS_MOUNT_NO_BRL`) or `&cifs_file_direct_ops` without mutating `inode->i_fop`. [F252, F255]
- When `cifs_do_create()` finds `is_valid_cached_dir(parent_cfid)` for `direntry->d_parent`, it must copy `parent_cfid->fid.lease_key` to `fid->parent_lease_key`, set `SMB2_LEASE_FLAG_PARENT_LEASE_KEY_SET_LE`, and set `parent_cfid->dirents.is_valid = false` and `is_failed = true`. `cifs_mark_open_files_invalid()` must set `invalidHandle = true` and `oplock_break_cancelled = true` on all `tcon->openFileList` entries and call `invalidate_all_cached_dirs(tcon)`. [F240, F259]
- In `cifs_get_disposition()` and `cifs_do_create()`, test `(O_CREAT | O_EXCL)` and `(O_CREAT | O_TRUNC)` with full-mask equality before single-bit `O_CREAT` or `O_TRUNC`. [F278]
- Inode metadata: in `cifs_fattr_to_inode()`, call `cifs_revalidate_cache(inode, fattr)` before updating mtime and `cifs_i->netfs.remote_i_size`. Compute `inode->i_blocks` as `(512 - 1 + bytes) >> 9` in `cifs_fattr_to_inode()` and `cifs_set_file_size()`. `cifs_get_inode_info()` and `smb311_posix_get_inode_info()` must only short-circuit on `is_inode_cache_good(*inode)` when `@data` is `NULL`. Use `time_after()` / `time_in_range()` for jiffies checks in `cifs_d_revalidate()` and `cifs_inode_needs_reval()`. [F242, F247, F294, F297]
- `cifs_prepare_read()` / `cifs_prepare_write()` must only call `cifs_negotiate_rsize()` / `cifs_negotiate_wsize()` when `rsize` / `wsize` is `0`. `struct netfs_io_request rreq` must remain at offset 0 of `struct cifs_io_request` (unioned with `cifs_io_subrequest`). `struct cifs_fscache_inode_key` must remain `__packed` and match `cifs_find_inode()` (`uniqueid`, `createtime`, `type`). [F241, F301, F305]
- `smb3_fs_context`: `smb3_fs_context_parse_param()` and `cifs_parse_cache_flavor()` must update coupled fields in lockstep (`remap`/`sfu_remap`, `direct_io`/`strict_io`/`cache_ro`/`cache_rw`, `multichannel`/`max_channels`) and bound `Opt_acregmax`, `Opt_acdirmax`, `Opt_actimeo`, `Opt_closetimeo`, and `Opt_echo_interval` before `* HZ`. `target_rfc1001_name` pads all 16 bytes with `0x20`; `source_rfc1001_name` keeps byte 15 as `0x00`. In `cifs_do_automount()`, clear `source`, `leaf_fullpath`, `UNC`, `prepath`, and `dfs_root_ses` before `smb3_fs_context_dup()`, and call `fs_context_set_ids()` and `is_dfs_mount()`. [F245, F260, F265, F311]
- In `parse_dfs_referrals()`, add `DfsPathOffset` and `NetworkAddressOffset` to `(char *)ref` (bounded by `data_end`), while advancing entries with typed `ref++`. [F303]

## Synchronization
- Call `_cifsFileInfo_put()` and `cifsFileInfo_put()` without `tcon->open_file_lock`, `cinode->open_file_lock`, or `cifs_file->file_info_lock`, and with `wait_oplock_handler = false` from `cifs_oplock_break()` or `cifs_close_deferred_file()`. In `cifs_close_*deferred_file*()` and `cifs_reopen_persistent_handles()`, collect targets onto a local list under `open_file_lock` (pinning via `cifsFileInfo_get()` in reopen/writable-file paths) and use `list_for_each_entry_safe()` outside the lock. [F189, F222, F283]
- Call `cifs_queue_oplock_break(cfile)` with `tcon->open_file_lock` held and `cfile->file_info_lock` NOT held, after setting `CIFS_INODE_PENDING_OPLOCK_BREAK` and initializing `oplock_epoch`, `oplock_level`, and `oplock_break_cancelled`. [F215]
- `cinode->lock_sem` protects `cinode->llist`, `cifs_fid_locks->locks`, and `cinode->can_cache_brlcks`. Use `cifs_down_write(&cinode->lock_sem)` for write locks and `down_read_nested(..., SINGLE_DEPTH_NESTING)` in `cifs_relock_file()`. In `cifs_writev()` / `cifs_strict_readv()`, `netfs_start_io_*` (`inode->i_rwsem`) must wrap `cinode->lock_sem`. `cifs_push_posix_locks()` must allocate `lock_to_push` (`GFP_KERNEL`) and call `CIFSSMBPosixLock` outside `flctx->flc_lock` while holding `lock_sem`. [F209, F318, F319, F325]
- In `cifs_reopen_file()`, serialize `cfile->invalidHandle` checks, reopen, and clearing under `cfile->fh_mutex`, releasing `fh_mutex` before `filemap_write_and_wait()` or `cifs_relock_file()`. [F217]
- Hold `ses->session_mutex` and call `smb3_sync_session_ctx_passwords()` before duplicating/reconfiguring `cifs_sb->ctx` in `cifs_do_automount()` or `smb3_reconfigure()`, unlocking `ses->session_mutex` on all exit paths. [F186, F243]
- In `cifs_revalidate_mapping()`, serialize `CIFS_INO_INVALID_MAPPING` and `filemap_invalidate_inode()` under the `CIFS_INO_LOCK` bit-lock (`clear_bit_unlock()`, `smp_mb__after_atomic()`, `wake_up_bit()`); when clearing `CIFS_INO_INVALID_MAPPING` (without `CIFS_MOUNT_RW_CACHE`), set `netfs.zero_point = netfs.remote_i_size` and restore the bit if invalidation fails. [F216, F248]
- In `cifs_iget()` and `cifs_fattr_to_inode()`, complete `cifs_set_netfs_context()`, `cifs_set_ops()`, and `cifs_fscache_get_inode_cookie()` before `unlock_new_inode(inode)` on `I_NEW`, and update `i_size` / `i_blocks` under `inode->i_lock` only if `is_size_safe_to_change()` is true. [F219, F220]
- Dentry/superblock locks: `cifs_d_revalidate()` must return `-ECHILD` immediately on `LOOKUP_RCU` and set `DCACHE_NEED_AUTOMOUNT` under `direntry->d_lock` for `IS_AUTOMOUNT(inode)`. In `__cifs_unlink()` (SMB2+), check `d_count(dentry) > 1` under `dentry->d_lock` after `cifs_close_deferred_file_under_dentry()`. Never take `sb->s_vfs_rename_mutex` in `cifs_lookup()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, or `__cifs_unlink()`. Drop `tcon->tc_lock` before `__cifs_get_super()` in `cifs_get_dfs_tcon_super()`. Call `cifs_release_automount_timer()` only when `list_empty(&cifs_automount_list)`. Use `GFP_NOFS` in `cifs_buf_get()` / `cifs_small_buf_get()`. [F213, F223, F257, F313, F314, F324, F328]

## Object Lifetimes
- Deferred closes: `cifs_add_deferred_close()` requires `cinode->deferred_lock` and consumes `@dclose`. Cancelling `cfile->deferred` requires `cifs_del_deferred_close(cfile)` under `cinode->deferred_lock`. In `cifs_close()`, `queue_delayed_work()` transfers the `cifsFileInfo` ref without `_cifsFileInfo_put()`, whereas `mod_delayed_work()` must call `_cifsFileInfo_put()` (after `cifsFileInfo_get(cfile)` if `mod_delayed_work()` returned `false`). [F191, F192, F256]
- Opens: every successful `cifs_open()` (including `use_cache`) and `cifs_atomic_open()` must call `fscache_use_cookie()`, paired with `cifs_fscache_unuse_inode_cookie()` in `cifs_close()`. In `cifs_do_create()` / `cifs_nt_open()`, post-open failures must close the server handle and `iput(newinode)`. Unlink every stack `cifs_pending_open` via `cifs_new_fileinfo()` or `cifs_del_pending_open()` before returning. [F193, F198, F307]
- Superblock & session refs: `cifs_oplock_break()` must bracket execution with `cifs_sb_active(sb)` and `cifs_sb_deactive(sb)` (after `cifs_done_oplock_break()`). Release `cifs_get_dfs_tcon_super()` with `cifs_put_tcp_super(sb)`. In `cifs_dump_full_key()`, when `out.session_id != 0`, check `ses_status != SES_EXITING` and call `cifs_smb_ses_inc_refcount(ses)` under `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` and `ses_it->ses_lock` (`found = true`), calling `cifs_put_smb_ses(ses)` on exit iff `found` is true. [F199, F202, F207, F221]
- Subrequests & writers: in `cifs_prepare_read()` / `cifs_prepare_write()`, call `get_xid()` only if `!have_xid`, and return unconsumed `credits.value` via `add_credits_and_wake_if()` on failure or in `cifs_free_subrequest()`. Pair `cifs_get_writer()` with `cifs_put_writer()`, waking `CIFS_INODE_PENDING_WRITERS` if rollback hits `writers == 0`. [F194, F200]
- Pointers: null source pointers when moving `symlink_target` / `cf_symlink_target` into `fattr` or `cifs_i`. In `cifs_unlock_range()`, free `tmp_llist` via `cifs_free_llist()` on success or restore via `cifs_move_llist()` on `cifs_lockv()` failure under `cinode->lock_sem`. Pass initialized `*sdesc` to `cifs_alloc_hash()` and free via `cifs_free_hash()`. In `cifs_ioctl_query_info()`, guard `kfree(utf16_path)` with `utf16_path != &root_path`. [F195, F201, F203, F308]

## API Contracts
- `cifs_sanitize_prepath()` modifies `prepath` without freeing it and returns a `kstrdup()` buffer (`NULL` or `ERR_PTR`). Reset `prepath` or `ctx->source` (`smb3_fs_context_fullpath()`) to `NULL` on `IS_ERR()`. [F187, F225]
- `cifs_convert_address()` returns `1` on success, `0` on failure. Pass `struct sockaddr_storage` (`>= sizeof(struct sockaddr_in6)`) and bound non-NUL `name`/`src` by `namelen`/`len` (`memchr`, `%.*s`) in `cifs_convert_address()`, `dns_resolve_name()`, `dns_resolve_unc()`, and `resolve_name()`. [F227, F309, F310]
- `cifs_get_writable_file()` returns `-EBADF` if no handle is open (convert to `0` in `cifs_file_flush()`). Check `uid_eq(open_file->uid, current_fsuid())` under `CIFS_MOUNT_MULTIUSER` when `fsuid_only` is requested. Pass `can_flush = false` to `cifs_reopen_file()` from writeback/write-prep paths. [F235, F237, F340]
- Netfs & SMB2: set `NETFS_SREQ_CLEAR_TAIL` in `cifs_issue_read()` only when `origin` is not `NETFS_UNBUFFERED_READ` or `NETFS_DIO_READ`. Call `netfs_prepare_write_failed()` on `cifs_prepare_write()` error, and return credits + call `cifs_write_subrequest_terminated()` on `cifs_issue_write()` error. Use `cifs_addr_ops_smallbuf` when `max_read < PAGE_SIZE + MAX_CIFS_HDR_SIZE`. In `SMB2_write()`, payload starts at `iov[1]` and `n_vec` excludes `iov[0]`. Pass the `alloc_dentry_path()` buffer to `free_dentry_path()`. [F239, F279, F298, F335, F338]

## Error Handling
- In `cifs_ioctl()`, check `if (pSMBFile == NULL) break;` before dereferencing `pSMBFile`. Initialize `*target_nodes = NULL` before `parse_dfs_referrals()`. In `reparse_info_to_fattr()`, reset `rc = 0` when `parse_reparse_point()` returns `-EOPNOTSUPP` for server-handled non-junction reparse points. [F231, F233, F281]

## External Contracts
- `cifs_d_revalidate()` must translate `-ENOENT` and `-ESTALE` to `0`. `cifs_setattr()` returns `0` on `ATTR_OPEN`, and `cifs_setattr_unix()` / `cifs_setattr_nounix()` clear `ATTR_CTIME | ATTR_MTIME` after `ATTR_SIZE`. [F234, F343]
- Keep `ctx->symlinkroot == NULL` unless `symlinkroot=` is set (starting with `'/'`, `< PATH_MAX`). M-F symlinks (`parse_mf_symlink()`) must be 1067 bytes (`link_len <= 1024` at offset 43, space-padded). `wire_mode_to_posix()` must check `wire_type < ARRAY_SIZE(posix_filetypes)` via `wire_filetype_to_posix()` and use `S_IFDIR` if `wire_type == 0 && is_dir`. [F264, F266, F269]
- `cifs_ioctl_copychunk()` requires `dst_file` `FMODE_WRITE`, `mnt_want_write_file()` / `mnt_drop_write_file()`, and non-dir `src_file` with `cifs_ioctl`. `CIFS_DUMP_KEY` / `CIFS_DUMP_FULL_KEY` require `capable(CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`, `smb3_encryption_required(tcon)`, and `out.in_size >= sizeof(out)` + key lengths. [F341, F342]

## Known Pitfalls
- Mask off `O_CREAT | O_EXCL | O_TRUNC` when matching deferred closes in `cifs_open()` (with `O_SYNC | O_DIRECT`) and before `cifs_posix_open()` in `cifs_reopen_file()`. On `-EACCES` with `rdwr_for_fscache == 1` in `cifs_nt_open()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, and `cifs_do_create()`, strip `GENERIC_READ`, set `rdwr_for_fscache = 2`, and call `cifs_invalidate_cache(inode, FSCACHE_INVAL_DIO_WRITE)` on success. [F276, F277, F287]
- Retries: on reopen failure in `cifs_get_writable_file()`, move `inv_file` to tail of `openFileList`, reset `inv_file = NULL` and `any_available = false`, and increment `refind`. On `CIFS_FATTR_INO_COLLISION` in `cifs_iget()`, clear it, and if `inode_has_hashed_dentries(inode)`, autodisable `serverino`, `iput(inode)`, and set `cf_uniqueid = iunique(...)`. Gate `__cifs_unlink()` `-EACCES` retry on `dosattr == 0`, set `dosattr` non-zero, and restore `origattr` on failure. Reset `cur = buf` and `num = 0` per pass and on full batch in `cifs_push_mandatory_locks()` / `cifs_unlock_range()`. [F285, F286, F288, F289]
- Bounds & misc: verify `total_read` covers `smb_hdr` and `2 * WordCount` before `smbCalcSize()` in `checkSMB()`, and check `data_offset <= len - sizeof(struct file_notify_information)` in `is_valid_oplock_break()`. In `cifs_sfu_type()`, verify even `cf_eof > 8`, even `symlink_len_utf16`, and `UniStrnlen(..., symlink_len_utf16 / 2)` before `cifs_strndup_from_utf16(..., symlink_len_utf16)`. Check `s >= (char *)page + prefix_len` in `__build_path_from_dentry_optional_prefix()` / `automount_fullpath()`. Match `cfile->dentry == dentry` in `cifs_close_deferred_file_under_dentry()`. Set `skip_parsing = true` on empty `Opt_pass`/`Opt_user`/`Opt_pass2` in `smb3_fs_context_parse_param()`. Null pointers on `new_ctx` before `DUP_CTX_STR()` in `smb3_fs_context_dup()`. [F263, F270, F271, F275, F280, F282, F291, F304]
