# TTY LAYER AND SERIAL DRIVERS — drivers/tty (part 3)

Core TTY VFS/buffer/baud/audit layer (`tty_io.c`, `tty_buffer.c`, `tty_baudrate.c`, `tty_audit.c`, `tty.h`), Magic SysRq (`sysrq.c`), SyncLink GT (`synclink_gt.c`), and UART drivers (`tegra-tcu.c`, `tegra-utc.c`, `timbuart.c`, `xilinx_uartps.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `tty_buffer_alloc()` allocates `struct_size(p, data, 2 * size)`, sets `p->size = size`, and adds `size` to `port->buf.mem_used` (even when popping `buf->free`); `tty_buffer_free()` subtracts `b->size` before pushing to `buf->free`. When `flags == false`, all `2 * size` bytes hold chars (no flag array): check `head->flags` before `flag_buf_ptr()` in `lookahead_bufs()`/`receive_buf()`, and allocate a new buffer in `__tty_buffer_request_room()` when `!b->flags && flags`. [F397, F417, F484]
- In `tty_buffer_flush()` and `flush_to_ldisc()`, free/advance `buf->head` only when `smp_load_acquire(&buf->head->next)` is non-NULL; on the tail buffer, `tty_buffer_flush()` must set both `read` and `lookahead` to `commit`. [F418, F462]
- Under `tty_lock`, `tty->count` must equal `tty->tty_files` entries + 1 if `PTY_TYPE_SLAVE` with open master (`tty->link->count`) + 1 if `tty_port_kopened(tty->port)`; keep `tty_add_file()`/`tty_del_file()` in lockstep with `tty->count`. [F420]
- In `__tty_hangup()`, if any file has `write_iter == redirected_tty_write` (`cons_filp != NULL`), do not set `hung_up_tty_fops` on `cons_filp` and call `tty->ops->close(tty, cons_filp)` `closecount` times instead of `tty->ops->hangup(tty)`. [F421]
- When `tty_init_termios()` restores `tty->driver->termios[idx]`, overwrite `tty->termios.c_line` with `driver->init_termios.c_line` and recompute `c_ispeed`/`c_ospeed` via `tty_termios_input_baud_rate()`/`tty_termios_baud_rate()`. [F406]
- In `tegra-tcu.c`, a 32-bit mailbox word packs <= 3 bytes in bits `[23:0]` (`TCU_MBOX_BYTE`) and count in `[25:24]` (`TCU_MBOX_NUM_BYTES`), cast directly to/from `void *msg` (never `&value` or `*(u32 *)msg`); `tegra_tcu_write()` must flush via `tegra_tcu_write_one()` whenever `written == 3` (including after `'\r'` in newline expansion). [F412, F446]
- In `tegra-utc.c`, update `tup->tx_irqmask`/`tup->rx_irqmask` in lockstep with `TEGRA_UTC_INTR_MASK`/`TEGRA_UTC_INTR_SET`, as `tegra_utc_isr()` masks status and clears interrupts with those shadow fields. [F426]
- In `xilinx_uartps.c`: set `port->mapsize = resource_size(res)` in `cdns_uart_probe()` and pass `port->mapsize` to `request_mem_region()`/`ioremap()`/`release_mem_region()`; check `(status & (CDNS_UART_SR_TXEMPTY | CDNS_UART_SR_TACTIVE)) == CDNS_UART_SR_TXEMPTY` in `cdns_uart_tx_empty()`; gate `CDNS_UART_RXBS` and `CDNS_UART_IXR_BRK` on `cdns_uart->quirks & CDNS_UART_RXBS_SUPPORT`. [F455, F497, F501]
- In `xilinx_uartps.c`, call `hrtimer_update_function()` before `hrtimer_start(&cdns_uart->tx_timer, ...)` and keep `rs485_tx_started` synced with RTS in `cdns_rs485_tx_setup()`/`cdns_rs485_rx_setup()`. In `cdns_calc_after_tx_delay()`, cast `delay_rts_after_send` to `u64` before `* NSEC_PER_MSEC` and use `DIV_ROUND_UP(cdns_uart->port->frame_time, 7)`. [F429, F476, F477]
- In `synclink_gt.c`: `calc_regaddr()` maps `< 0x40` adapter-wide, `0x40..0x7f` `+ port_num * 16`, and `>= 0x80` `+ port_num * 32`; `slgt_desc` fields (`count`, `status`, `pbuf`, `next`) are `__le16`/`__le32` with physical addresses spaced by `sizeof(struct slgt_desc)` (not 12); `tx_load()` checks `DIV_ROUND_UP(size, DMABUFSIZE) > free_tbuf_count(info)`; `alloc_tmp_rbuf()` allocates `max_frame_size + 5` and `rx_get_frame()` rejects `framesize > max_frame_size + crc_size`; `rx_async()` advances by 2 bytes/char; guard divisions by `data_rate`/`clock_speed`/`rate` for non-zero. [F449, F451, F452, F480, F481, F492]

## Synchronization
- In `__tty_buffer_request_room()`, `smp_store_release(&b->commit, b->used)` must precede `smp_store_release(&b->next, n)`, and consumers (`flush_to_ldisc()`, `lookahead_bufs()`, `tty_buffer_flush()`) must `smp_load_acquire(&head->next)` before `head->commit`. Flip-buffer producers run in hardirq under `port->lock`, so `tty_buffer_alloc()` must use `GFP_ATOMIC | __GFP_NOWARN` without sleeping locks. [F372, F514, F550]
- `tty_buffer_lock_exclusive()` and `tty_buffer_flush()` must increment `port->buf.priority` before locking `port->buf.lock`, and `tty_buffer_unlock_exclusive()` must requeue `port->buf.work` if `head->commit != head->read`. [F374]
- For PTY pairs, `tty->legacy_mutex` locks master-then-slave (`TTY_LOCK_NORMAL` before `tty_lock_slave()`/`TTY_LOCK_SLAVE`), whereas nested `port->buf.lock` locks slave-then-master (`tty_buffer_set_lock_subclass()`). [F519]
- Hold `tty->atomic_write_lock` (`tty_write_lock()`) across `write_buf`/`write_cnt` resize and `ld->ops->write()` in `iterate_tty_write()`. Hold `tty->termios_rwsem` around `tty_encode_baud_rate()` and `tty_get_baud_rate()`. [F380, F381]
- `do_SAK()` and `tty_hangup()` may run in atomic context and must schedule `SAK_work`/`hangup_work`, whereas `tty_vhangup()`/`tty_vhangup_session()` call `__tty_hangup()` synchronously. `tty_kref_put()` must defer `release_one_tty()` via `queue_release_one_tty()` (reusing `hangup_work` after `tty_flush_works()`). [F522, F523, F541]
- In `show_cons_active()`, lock `console_list_lock()` before `console_lock()` and hold it until after `console_unlock()`. [F525]
- When multithreaded, serialize all `tty_audit_buf` (`dev`, `icanon`, `valid`, `data`, `tty_audit_buf_push()`) accesses under `buf->mutex`; in `tty_audit_add_data()`, clamp copies to `TTY_AUDIT_BUF_SIZE - buf->valid` and call `tty_audit_buf_push(buf)` when `buf->valid == TTY_AUDIT_BUF_SIZE`. [F383, F440]
- `__handle_sysrq()` runs handlers inside `rcu_read_lock()` (often hardirq): handlers must not sleep, `__sysrq_swap_key_ops()` must call `synchronize_rcu()` after updating `sysrq_key_table` under `sysrq_key_table_lock`, and `sysrq_handle_crash()` must call `rcu_read_unlock()` before `panic()`. If `!trigger_all_cpu_backtrace()`, `sysrq_handle_showallcpus()` brackets local dump with `get_cpu()`/`put_cpu()` and defers `smp_call_function(showacpu, ...)` to `sysrq_showallcpus` work (using `raw_spin_lock_irqsave(&show_lock, flags)`). [F384, F516, F518]
- UART RX ISRs must not call `uart_handle_sysrq_char()` under `port->lock` (inverts with `console_lock`); use `uart_prepare_sysrq_char()` + `uart_unlock_and_check_sysrq()` (`cdns_uart_handle_rx`) or drop `port->lock` (`tegra_utc_rx_chars`). In `tegra-utc.c`, `tegra_utc_console_write_atomic()`/`_thread()` must bracket TX register access with `nbcon_enter_unsafe()`/`nbcon_exit_unsafe()`. [F386, F528]
- In `timbuart.c`, `timbuart_handleinterrupt()` saves `TIMBUART_IER` to `uart->last_ier` and masks it before scheduling `uart->tasklet`, and `timbuart_remove()` calls `tasklet_kill(&uart->tasklet)` before `uart_remove_one_port()`. [F530]
- In `xilinx_uartps.c`: call `hrtimer_cancel(&cdns_uart->tx_timer)` before `uart_port_lock_irqsave()` in `cdns_uart_shutdown()`; in `cdns_uart_console_write()`, use `uart_port_trylock_irqsave()` when `oops_in_progress` and unlock only if `locked`; in `cdns_uart_clk_notifier_cb()`, `POST_RATE_CHANGE` sets `locked = 1` under lock and falls through to `ABORT_RATE_CHANGE` (which locks only if `!locked`). [F459, F531, F532]
- In `synclink_gt.c`: shared GPIO regs (`< 0x40`) and `gpio_wait_q` in `set_gpio()`/`wait_gpio()` must lock `info->port_array[0]->lock`; `free_tbuf_count()` subtracts 1 when `TDCSR` `BIT0` is set, and `tx_load()` writes `tbufs[tbuf_start].count` last. [F431, F450]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `tty_init_dev()`, after `tty_driver_install_tty()`, abort with `-EINVAL` (`err_release_lock`) if `!tty->port`, and unwind via `release_tty(tty, idx)` (never `free_tty_struct()`), guarding `tty->port` and `tty->link` for `NULL` in `release_tty()`. `tty_release_struct()` and `tty_kclose()` must call `tty_ldisc_release(tty)` and `tty_flush_works(tty)` before `release_tty()`. [F354, F358, F398]
- In `__tty_hangup()`, `tty_kref_put(tty)` for each ref returned by `tty_signal_session_leader()`, and under `tty->ctrl.lock` set `tty->ctrl.session = NULL` and `tty->ctrl.pgrp = NULL` after `put_pid()`. [F359]
- In `tty_register_device_attr()`, free/NULL `driver->termios[index]` if `!TTY_DRIVER_DYNAMIC_ALLOC`; on error, `device_register()` failure calls `put_device(dev)` while `tty_cdev_add()` failure calls `device_del(dev)` + `put_device(dev)`, and `tty_cdev_add()` calls `kobject_put(&driver->cdevs[index]->kobj)` if `cdev_add()` fails. [F360, F405]
- Call `tty_buffer_free_all()` only after `port->buf.work` is canceled and producers/consumers stopped; both `tty_buffer_free()` and `tty_buffer_free_all()` must check `b->size > 0` to skip `&port->buf.sentinel`. [F375, F487]
- `tty_audit_exit()` sets `tty_audit_buf = ERR_PTR(-ESRCH)` and calls `tty_audit_buf_push(buf)` before `tty_audit_buf_free(buf)`; `tty_audit_buf_get()` uses `cmpxchg()` against `NULL`. In `sysrq_disconnect()`, `input_close_device()`, `cancel_work_sync(&sysrq->reinject_work)`, and `timer_shutdown_sync(&sysrq->keyreset_timer)` must precede `input_unregister_handle()` and `kfree(sysrq)`. [F363, F365]
- In `tegra-tcu.c`, request `"rx"` mbox after `uart_add_one_port()` in probe and `mbox_free_channel(tcu->rx)` before `uart_remove_one_port()` in remove. In `xilinx_uartps.c`, clear `console_port = NULL` (and `cdns_uart_console.index = -1` in probe if `!console_is_registered()`) on non-console/remove, and call `uart_unregister_driver()` only when `!instances` (probe error) or `!--instances` (remove). [F368, F387, F503]
- In `synclink_gt.c`: `block_til_ready()` must decrement `blocked_open`, restore `port->count` (unless `tty_hung_up_p(filp)`), and re-lock `tty_lock` on exit; `hdlcdev_open()` sets `info->netcount = 1` under `info->netlock` when `!port.count && !netcount` and must reset `netcount = 0` (and `shutdown(info)` if started) on error; `wait_gpio()` must call `remove_cond_wait()` under `port_array[0]->lock` on all exit paths. [F370, F371, F493]

## API Contracts
- `tty_init_dev()` and `tty_kopen_exclusive()` return `tty` locked; `tty_kopen_shared()` returns `tty` unlocked with kref++. `tty_driver_lookup_tty()` returns `ERR_PTR`, `NULL`, or `tty` with kref++ (check `IS_ERR` first). `tty_open_current_tty()` returns `NULL` only if `device != MKDEV(TTYAUX_MAJOR, 0)` (propagate `IS_ERR` in `tty_open()`). [F355, F391, F392]
- `__tty_alloc_driver()` rejects `lines == 0` or (`lines > 1` && `TTY_DRIVER_UNNUMBERED_NODE`) with `ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)`. `tty_buffer_set_limit()` requires `limit >= MIN_TTYB_SIZE` (256) before allocations. `*chars` from `tty_prepare_flip_string()` cannot be used for DMA. [F410, F413, F515]
- `tty_termios_encode_baud_rate(termios, ibaud, obaud)` takes `ibaud` first, sets `ibaud = 0` if `obaud == 0`, and leaves `CBAUD << IBSHIFT` as `B0` if `ibaud == obaud` without explicit `IBAUD`. `tty_tiocmset()` masks `set`/`clear` with `TIOCM_DTR | TIOCM_RTS | TIOCM_OUT1 | TIOCM_OUT2 | TIOCM_LOOP`. In `tty_ioctl()`, `TCFLSH` must `break` after `tty_buffer_flush()`. [F407, F409, F457]
- `this_tty()` must check `file->f_op->read_iter == tty_read` before `file_tty(file)` and return `fd + 1` (`__do_SAK()` logs `i - 1`). Bounded `tty_table` sysctls must use `proc_dointvec_minmax`. [F395, F488, F535]
- In `timbuart_set_termios()`, guard `if (old)` before `tty_termios_copy_hw()` and clamp `bindex < 0` from `get_bindex()`. `cdns_uart_calc_baud_divs()` requires `baud != 0` and returns `0` on failure (`cdns_uart_clk_notifier_cb()` returns `NOTIFY_BAD` on `0`). [F396, F399, F447, F478]

## Error Handling
- Use `tty_free_file()` only before `tty_add_file()`; after `tty_add_file()` in `tty_open()`, call `tty_release()` and reset `filp->f_op = &tty_fops` if `tty_hung_up_p(filp)` before `retry_open`. In `iterate_tty_read()`, if `copy_to_iter()` short-copies while `cookie != NULL`, loop with `count = 0, retval = -EFAULT` without overwriting `retval`; zero read data via `memset(p, 0, n)` in `receive_buf()` and `memzero_explicit(kernel_buf, ...)` in `iterate_tty_read()`. [F401, F402, F403]
- In `send_break()`, ignore `-EOPNOTSUPP` (`retval = 0`) from `break_ctl(tty, -1)` when `!TTY_DRIVER_HARDWARE_BREAK`. Unhandled job-control/driver ioctls return `-ENOIOCTLCMD`, translated to `-ENOTTY` at the end of `tty_ioctl()`. Zero output structs in `tty_get_icount()`/`tty_tiocgserial()`/`compat_tty_tiocgserial()`, stop `serial_struct32` `memcpy` at `offsetof(..., iomem_base)`, and set `v.iomap_base = 0` in `compat_tty_tiocsserial()`. In `xilinx_uartps.c`, disable `pclk` if enabling `uartclk` fails. [F393, F394, F404, F442, F505]

## External Contracts
- In `tty_ioctl()`: `TCSBRK` sends 250ms break only if `arg == 0`, while `TCSBRKP` sends `arg ? arg * 100 : 250`; skip `tty_wait_until_sent()` for `TIOCCBRK`; use `real_tty` for `TIOCGWINSZ`/`TIOCSWINSZ`/`TIOCGDEV`, and reject `TIOCCONS` if `real_tty != tty`. `tiocsti()` requires `(tty_legacy_tiocsti || capable(CAP_SYS_ADMIN))` (`-EIO`), `(current->signal->tty == tty || capable(CAP_SYS_ADMIN))` (`-EPERM`), `tty_audit_tiocsti()`, and `tty_buffer_lock_exclusive()`. [F408, F458, F472, F542]
- `tty_update_time()` updates timestamps only on 8s boundaries (`(sec ^ time.tv_sec) & ~7`). Convert pointer ioctl args with `compat_ptr(arg)` in `tty_compat_ioctl()`/`slgt_compat_ioctl()`. `tty_audit_add_data()` skips `PTY_TYPE_MASTER` and `icanon && !L_ECHO(tty)` (unless `AUDIT_TTY_LOG_PASSWD`), pushing on `dev`/`icanon` change; `tty_audit_log()` uses `from_kuid(&init_user_ns, ...)` and `audit_log_untrustedstring()` for `comm`. [F538, F545, F546, F547]
- `write_sysrq_trigger()` processes only `buf[0]` (returning `count`) unless `buf[0] == '_'`. In `synclink_gt.c`: `get_stats(NULL)` zeroes `info->icount`; `rx_enable()` requires upper-16-bit `rbuf_fill_level` to be a multiple of 4 and `<= DMABUFSIZE`; `get_params32()`/`set_params32()` convert fields individually and handle `MGSL_MODE_BASE_CLOCK`. [F400, F411, F445, F453]

## Known Pitfalls
- Read `port->buf.tail` only after `__tty_buffer_request_room()`; in `tty_buffer_space_avail()`, clamp signed `mem_limit - atomic_read(&mem_used)` with `max(space, 0)`. Check `cbaud == BOTHER` before `cbaud & CBAUDEX` (`0x1000`), and in `tty_termios_encode_baud_rate()` sample `ibinput`/`BOTHER` before clearing `CBAUD` bits and match `obaud` before `ibaud`. [F415, F454, F467, F482, F486]
- Call `iov_iter_revert(from, size - ret)` on partial write in `iterate_tty_write()`, and use `file` (not `iocb->ki_filp`) in `file_tty_write()`. In `sysrq.c`, set `sysrq->reinjecting = true` around `input_inject_event()`, clear `KEY_SYSRQ` when `sysrq->active`, ignore autorepeat (`value == 2`), and validate reset keys `<= KEY_MAX` as `unsigned short *`. [F425, F460, F469, F473, F494]
- In `xilinx_uartps.c`: `CDNS_UART_IER`/`IDR` are write-only (read `IMR`), never enable `CDNS_UART_IXR_PARITY`, check `!(CR & CDNS_UART_CR_RX_DIS)` before `cdns_uart_handle_rx()`, reset `isrstatus = 0` per FIFO char in `cdns_uart_handle_rx()`, and preserve `mode_reg & 1` (`CDNS_UART_MR_CLKSEL`) in `cdns_uart_set_termios()`. In `synclink_gt.c:isr_gpio()`, advance `prev = w` only when `w` is not unlinked. [F428, F463, F466, F498, F499, F500]
