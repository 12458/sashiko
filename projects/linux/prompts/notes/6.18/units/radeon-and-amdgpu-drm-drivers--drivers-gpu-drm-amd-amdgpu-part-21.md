# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu (part 21)

Hardware backends for PSP (`psp_v3_1`, `psp_v13_0`, `psp_v13_0_4`, `psp_v14_0`) and SDMA (`sdma_v2_4`, `sdma_v3_0`, `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, `sdma_v5_0`), covering PSP mailbox protocols and SDMA ring emission, VM PTEs, buffer copies, soft-resets, and RAS/ACA reporting.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `sdma_v4_0` and `sdma_v4_4_2`, finalize `adev->sdma.has_page_queue` in `*_early_init` before `*_set_ring_funcs`, `*_set_buffer_funcs`, and `*_set_vm_pte_funcs`, and guard every start, stop, resume, ring-test, queue-stop, and teardown access to `adev->sdma.instance[i].page` on `has_page_queue`. [F1826, F1842]
- In `sdma_v4_4_2`, map logical instance indices (`0 .. num_instances - 1`) via `GET_INST(SDMA0, instance)` before indexing `adev->reg_offset[SDMA0_HWIP]`, computing `aid_id`, querying/resetting RAS registers, or passing a bitmask to `amdgpu_dpm_reset_sdma`. Also ensure `adev->sdma.num_inst_per_aid` is non-zero before `sdma_v4_4_2_set_ring_funcs` or `sdma_v4_4_2_ring_emit_hdp_flush`, and shift `ref_and_mask_sdma0` by `ring->me % num_inst_per_aid`. [F1845, F1887]
- Before resetting SDMA v4.4.2, `sdma_v4_4_2_stop_queue` must snapshot `gfx_guilty`/`page_guilty` and `cached_rptr` so `sdma_v4_4_2_gfx_resume` and `sdma_v4_4_2_page_resume` (`restore = true`) advance guilty queues to `ring->wptr` while resuming innocent queues at `cached_rptr`. [F1844]
- Register aperture rules: in `sdma_v4_0_get_reg_offset`, instances 0..1 use segment 0 (`reg_offset[SDMA0..1_HWIP][0][0]`) and 2..7 use segment 1 (`reg_offset[SDMA2..7_HWIP][0][1]`); in `sdma_v5_0_get_reg_offset`, offsets in `[SDMA0_HYP_DEC_REG_START, SDMA0_HYP_DEC_REG_END]` (`0x5880..0x5893`) use `reg_offset[GC_HWIP][0][1]` with stride `SDMA1_HYP_DEC_REG_OFFSET` (`0x20`), whereas other registers use `reg_offset[GC_HWIP][0][0]` with stride `SDMA1_REG_OFFSET` (`0x600`); `sdma_v4_4_get_reg_offset` requires an `SDMA0` offset (`regSDMA0_*`) and `instance` in `0..4` (returning `0` for `> 4`). [F1839, F1864, F1865]
- Multiply DWord writeback slot indices (`ring->rptr_offs`, `ring->wptr_offs`, or `index` from `amdgpu_device_wb_get`) by 4 before adding to byte-addressed `adev->wb.gpu_addr` in `sdma_v4_4_2_*_resume` and `sdma_v*_ring_test_*` (unlike `adev->wb.wb[index]`). [F1892]
- In `psp_v13_0_memory_training` and `psp_v14_0_memory_training`, keep the `ops` bitmask expansion a non-exclusive chain (`SEND_SHORT_MSG` -> `RESTORE` -> `SAVE` -> `SEND_LONG_MSG` -> clear `SEND_SHORT_MSG` and set `SAVE`), then execute in order: `SEND_LONG_MSG`, `SAVE`, `RESTORE`, `SEND_SHORT_MSG`. [F1866]
- Set `UTC_L1_ENABLE = 1` in `mmSDMA0_CNTL` / `regSDMA_CNTL` during `sdma_v4_0_start`, `sdma_v4_4_2_inst_start`, and bare-metal `sdma_v5_0_gfx_resume_instance`. In `sdma_v2_4_ring_emit_vm_flush` and `sdma_v3_0_ring_emit_vm_flush`, follow `amdgpu_gmc_emit_flush_gpu_tlb` with a 6-dword `SDMA_OP_POLL_REGMEM` on `mmVM_INVALIDATE_REQUEST << 2`. [F1921, F1922]
- When encoding `amdgpu_sdma_phase_quantum` into `PHASE*_QUANTUM` in `sdma_v3_0`, `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` `*_ctx_switch_enable`, halve `value` with ceiling rounding (`(value + 1) >> 1`) per `unit` increment and clamp both fields to their mask limits. [F1886]

## Synchronization
- In `sdma_v4_0_*_resume`, `sdma_v4_4_2_*_resume`, and `sdma_v5_0_gfx_resume_instance`, set `MINOR_PTR_UPDATE = 1` before programming ring WPTR/doorbell and clear it to `0` afterward, prior to setting `RB_ENABLE = 1`. When `ring->use_doorbell` is true in `sdma_v3_0`, `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` `*_ring_set_wptr`, store `ring->wptr << 2` to the writeback slot via `WRITE_ONCE` or `atomic64_set` before ringing `WDOORBELL32`/`WDOORBELL64`. [F1817, F1818]
- In `sdma_v2_4_gfx_resume` and `sdma_v3_0_gfx_resume`, reset `vi_srbm_select(adev, 0, 0, 0, 0)` before `mutex_unlock(&adev->srbm_mutex)`. [F1800]
- In `sdma_v4_4_2`, call `sdma_v4_4_2_update_reset_mask(adev)` in `late_init` (after SMU init and `adev->gfx.mec_fw_version` setup). In `sdma_v4_4_2_inst_enable(enable=false)` on bare metal during reset (`!amdgpu_sriov_vf(adev) && amdgpu_in_reset(adev)`), stop the GFX/RLC/page queues and return before setting `SDMA_F32_CNTL.HALT=1`. [F1813, F1814]

## Object Lifetimes
- `psp_v3_1`, `psp_v13_0`, `psp_v13_0_4`, and `psp_v14_0` `*_ring_destroy` must call `psp_v*_ring_stop` before `amdgpu_bo_free_kernel(&adev->firmware.rbuf, &ring->ring_mem_mc_addr, (void **)&ring->ring_mem)`, and still free the BO and clear `ring->ring_mem` if `ring_stop` fails. [F1793]
- In `psp_v13_0_memory_training` and `psp_v14_0_memory_training` (`PSP_MEM_TRAIN_SEND_LONG_MSG`), `vfree(buf)` must run on every exit path after `vmalloc`, and `drm_dev_exit(idx)` must pair with every successful `drm_dev_enter`. [F1794]
- The `duplicate` flag in `amdgpu_sdma_destroy_inst_ctx` in `sdma_v4_0_sw_fini` and `sdma_v4_4_2_sw_fini` must match `sdma_v4_0_init_microcode` (`IP_VERSION(4, 2, 2)`, `(4, 4, 0)`) and `sdma_v4_4_2_init_microcode` (`IP_VERSION(4, 4, 2)`, `(4, 4, 4)`, `(4, 4, 5)`). [F1796]
- Across `sdma_v2_4`, `sdma_v3_0`, `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0`, `*_ring_test_ring` must call `amdgpu_device_wb_free` on all paths after `amdgpu_device_wb_get`, and `*_ring_test_ib` must release `amdgpu_ib_free` and `dma_fence_put` before `amdgpu_device_wb_free` on all paths. [F1798, F1799]
- In `sdma_v4_0_hw_fini`, `sdma_v4_4_2_hw_fini`, and `sdma_v4_4_2_xcp_suspend`, when `!amdgpu_sriov_vf(adev)` and `amdgpu_ras_is_supported(adev, AMDGPU_RAS_BLOCK__SDMA)`, call `amdgpu_irq_put(adev, &adev->sdma.ecc_irq, AMDGPU_SDMA_IRQ_INSTANCE0 + i)` for each target instance before halting. [F1802]
- Pair `amdgpu_amdkfd_suspend(adev, true)` with `amdgpu_amdkfd_resume(adev, true)` unconditionally after `amdgpu_sdma_reset_engine` in `sdma_v4_4_2_reset_queue` and `sdma_v5_0_reset_queue`. In `sdma_v5_0_ring_preempt_ib`, restore `WREG32(sdma_gfx_preempt, 0)` and `amdgpu_ring_set_preempt_cond_exec(ring, true)` even on poll timeout. [F1805, F1806]
- Pair `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, false)` with `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, true)` in `sdma_v5_0_dump_ip_state`, and release non-NULL `task_info` from `amdgpu_vm_get_task_info_pasid` via `amdgpu_vm_put_task_info` in `sdma_v4_0_print_iv_entry` and `sdma_v4_4_2_print_iv_entry`. [F1801, F1804]

## API Contracts
- `sdma_v5_0_reset_queue` passes `caller_handles_kernel_queues = true` to `amdgpu_sdma_reset_engine` (bracketing with `amdgpu_ring_reset_helper_begin`/`end`), whereas `sdma_v4_4_2_reset_queue` passes `false`. [F1807]
- `sdma_v4_0` and `sdma_v4_4` `*_query_ras_error_count_by_instance` must initialize `sec_count = 0` before `get_ras_error_count`, add `sec_count` into the supported counter, and zero the unsupported counter (`ue_count` on v4.0, `ce_count` on v4.4). `sdma_v4_4_2_aca_bank_is_valid` must verify both `(ACA_REG__IPID__INSTANCEIDLO(bank->regs[ACA_REG_IDX_IPID]) & GENMASK(31, 1)) == mmSMNAID_AID0_MCA_SMU` and `aca_bank_check_error_codes` against `sdma_v4_4_2_err_codes` (`33..36`). [F1832, F1934]
- Under SR-IOV, `ring_set_wptr` in `psp_v3_1`, `psp_v13_0`, `psp_v13_0_4`, and `psp_v14_0` must write the DWord wptr to `C2PMSG_102` and `GFX_CTRL_CMD_ID_CONSUME_CMD` to `C2PMSG_101` (and set `psp->km_ring.ring_wptr = value` in `psp_v3_1`), whereas bare-metal writes `C2PMSG_67`. In `psp_v13_0_update_spirom` and `psp_v14_0_update_spirom`, set `psp->vbflash_done = true` before `C2PMSG_CMD_SPI_UPDATE_FLASH_IMAGE`. [F1924, F1925]
- `emit_copy_buffer`, `emit_fill_buffer`, and `copy_pte` in `sdma_v2_4`..`sdma_v5_0` must not exceed `copy_num_dw` (7), `fill_num_dw` (5, or 7 in `sdma_v2_4`), and `copy_pte_num_dw` (7). In `sdma_v5_0_ring_emit_fence`, emit `SDMA_OP_TRAP` only when `flags & AMDGPU_FENCE_FLAG_INT` is set. [F1929, F1931]

## Error Handling
- On `amdgpu_ucode_request` failure in `sdma_v2_4_init_microcode` and `sdma_v3_0_init_microcode`, iterate over all `adev->sdma.num_instances` and call `amdgpu_ucode_release(&adev->sdma.instance[i].fw)`. [F1795]
- In `sdma_v2_4`..`sdma_v5_0` `*_ring_test_ib`, translate the `long` return `r` of `dma_fence_wait_timeout` from `0` to `-ETIMEDOUT` and `> 0` to `0` (or `-EINVAL` on wb mismatch) before returning `int`. [F1822]
- Store `sdma_v4_0_irq_id_to_seq` in a signed `int` and check `instance < 0` before indexing `adev->sdma.instance[instance]`. Check `if (!adev->sdma.ip_dump) return;` in `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` `dump_ip_state`/`print_ip_state`, and null-check `sdma = amdgpu_sdma_get_instance_from_ring(ring)` before reading `sdma->burst_nop` in `ring_insert_nop`/`ring_pad_ib`. [F1824, F1827, F1828]
- In `psp_v13_0` and `psp_v14_0` `*_read_usbc_pd_fw`, callers must check the return status before reading `*fw_ver`. In `psp_v13_0_get_ras_capability`, check `amdgpu_sriov_vf(adev)` (return `false` on VF) and `con = amdgpu_ras_get_context(adev)` non-null before reading `regMP0_SMN_C2PMSG_127` and `con->poison_supported`. [F1825, F1829]
- In `psp_v13_0`, `psp_v13_0_4`, and `psp_v14_0`, `bootloader_load_component` and `bootloader_load_sos` must `memset(psp->fw_pri_buf, 0, PSP_1_MEG)` before `memcpy`. In `sdma_v4_0_process_ras_data_cb`, return `AMDGPU_RAS_SUCCESS` without calling `amdgpu_sdma_process_ras_data_cb` when `amdgpu_ras_is_supported(adev, AMDGPU_RAS_BLOCK__GFX)` is true. [F1831, F1935]

## External Contracts
- PSP mailbox: 64-bit LFB/MC addresses written to `C2PMSG_36` (`fw_pri_mc_addr`, `c2p_train_data_offset`) in `psp_v3_1`..`psp_v14_0` must be 1 MB-aligned and shifted `>> 20`; in `psp_v13_0`/`psp_v14_0` `*_exec_spi_cmd` and `*_load_usbc_pd_fw`, after polling bit 31 (`0x80000000`), check `(reg_status & 0xFFFF) != 0` for errors; under SR-IOV, `psp_v*_ring_create` must call `ring_stop` before programming `C2PMSG_101`–`C2PMSG_103`. [F1833, F1869, F1911]
- In `psp_v13_0` and `psp_v14_0` `*_memory_training`, `PSP_MEM_TRAIN_SEND_LONG_MSG` must back up `BIST_MEM_TRAINING_ENCROACHED_SIZE` bytes from `adev->mman.aper_base_kaddr` before `PSP_BL__DRAM_LONG_TRAIN` and restore them via `memcpy_toio` and `amdgpu_device_flush_hdp` while setting `PSP_MEM_TRAIN_SAVE`. [F1850]
- SDMA packets: `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` `SDMA_OP_COPY`, `CONST_FILL`, `WRITE`, and `PTEPDE` encode 0-based counts (`count - 1`), whereas `sdma_v2_4` and `sdma_v3_0` use 1-based counts; all five encode `SDMA_PKT_NOP_HEADER_COUNT(count - 1)` when `sdma->burst_nop` is true and still emit `count` dwords; `ring_emit_ib` pads with `(2 - lower_32_bits(ring->wptr)) & 7` NOPs and masks `ib->gpu_addr` with `0xffffffe0`; `sdma_v4_0`/`v4_4_2`/`v5_0` `*_ring_emit_fence` require `!(addr & 0x3)`; `sdma_v4_0`/`v4_4_2` `*_wait_reg_mem` shift register offsets `<< 2` (`mem_space == 0`) but pass memory addresses unshifted (`mem_space == 1`). [F1835, F1836, F1837, F1840, F1858]
- SDMA rings & ucode: software ring pointers are in dwords while `RB_RPTR`/`RB_WPTR`, wb slots, and doorbells use byte offsets (`<< 2` / `>> 2`); `*_RB_BASE` takes 256-byte-aligned addresses (`>> 8`) and `RB_SIZE` takes `order_base_2(ring_size / 4)`; in `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` `*_load_microcode`, offset `fw->data` (`const u8 *`) by `ucode_array_offset_bytes` before casting to `const __le32 *` and loop `ucode_size_bytes / 4` times. [F1861, F1884, F1888]
- In `sdma_v4_0_hw_init`/`hw_fini` on APUs (`AMD_IS_APU`), un-powergate `AMD_IP_BLOCK_TYPE_SDMA` before register access in `hw_init` and re-powergate after halting in `hw_fini`. On SDMA v4.0/v4.2, `sdma_v4_0_reset_ras_error_count` reads `mmSDMA0_EDC_COUNTER` to clear, whereas `sdma_v4_4_reset_ras_error_count` writes `0` to `regSDMA0_EDC_COUNTER` and `regSDMA0_EDC_COUNTER2`. In `sdma_v4_0_process_trap_irq`, `page` traps use `ring_id == 1` on `IP_VERSION(4, 2, 0)` and `ring_id == 3` otherwise. [F1873, F1908, F1910]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `sdma_v4_4_2_process_trap_irq`, `sdma_v4_4_2_irq_id_to_seq` returns only the intra-AID offset; step `i` by `adev->sdma.num_inst_per_aid` until `adev->sdma.instance[i].aid_id == node_id_to_phys_map[entry->node_id]`. In `sdma_v4_4_query_ras_error_count_by_instance`, pass the unadjusted `regSDMA0_EDC_COUNTER`/`COUNTER2` constant to `sdma_v4_4_get_ras_error_count`, not the local `reg_offset` variable. [F1846, F1874]
- In `psp_v13_0_is_reload_needed`, read the microcode TOS version from `psp->sos.fw_version` before calling `psp_v13_0_init_sos_version(psp)`, which overwrites `psp->sos.fw_version` in place. Also add explicit `case` branches for new hardware variants in `psp_v13_0`, `psp_v13_0_4`, `psp_v14_0`, `sdma_v2_4`, and `sdma_v3_0` `*_init_microcode` (`default: BUG()`). [F1851, F1867]
- In `sdma_v4_0`, `sdma_v4_4_2`, and `sdma_v5_0` (`.support_64bit_ptrs = true`), cast `ring->rptr_cpu_addr`, `ring->wptr_cpu_addr`, and `&adev->wb.wb[...]` (`u32 *`) to `u64 *` or `atomic64_t *`, and shift 64-bit dword pointers left by 2 before extracting `upper_32_bits(wptr << 2)` (never `upper_32_bits(wptr) << 2`). In `sdma_v4_0` and `sdma_v4_4_2` `*_print_iv_entry`, cast `entry->src_data[0]` and `entry->src_data[1]` to `u64` before shifting `<< 12` and `<< 44`. [F1879, F1881, F1891]
- In `sdma_v4_0_suspend` and `sdma_v4_0_resume`, when `adev->in_s0ix` is true, `suspend` must only call `sdma_v4_0_gfx_enable(adev, false)` (not `hw_fini`) and `resume` must only unhalt and re-enable GFX RB/IB (not `hw_init`). [F1909]
