# NETWORKING DRIVERS — drivers (part 2)

This component contains the Linux PTP (IEEE 1588) core framework and clock drivers (`drivers/ptp/*`: `ptp_clock`, `ptp_chardev`, `ptp_sysfs`, `ptp_vclock`, Renesas ClockMatrix/FemtoClock3/82P33, Broadcom DTE, ZHAW InES, KVM PTP, Intel PCH), and the IBM s390 CTC/MPC network driver (`drivers/s390/net/ctcm_*`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct timestamp_event_queue` is a 128-slot (`PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS`) ring with max capacity `PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS - 1` (`queue_free(q)`); `head`/`tail` wrap modulo `PTP_MAX_TIMESTAMPS` via `WRITE_ONCE`, and `enqueue_external_timestamp()` advances `head` when `!queue_free(queue)`. Because slots are reused, `enqueue_external_timestamp()` must overwrite `dst->flags = PTP_EXTTS_EVENT_VALID` (not `|=`) before setting `PTP_EXT_OFFSET` and reading `src->timestamp` (`PTP_CLOCK_EXTTS`) vs `src->offset` (`PTP_CLOCK_EXTOFF`). [F905, F940]
- The initial `timestamp_event_queue` in `ptp_clock_register()` must stay at the head of `ptp->tsevqs` until `ptp_clock_release()` for `extts_fifo_show()`, while per-fd queues in `ptp_open()` use `list_add_tail()` and `pccontext->private_clkdata`. In `ptp_read()`, `cnt` must be a multiple of `sizeof(struct ptp_extts_event)` (`-EINVAL` otherwise) and capped to `EXTTS_BUFSIZE` before `kmalloc(EXTTS_BUFSIZE, GFP_KERNEL)` and dequeuing `min((size_t)queue_cnt(queue), cnt / sizeof(*event))` items. [F920, F941]
- Bounds checks: `ptp_mask_en_single()` requires `channel < PTP_MAX_CHANNELS` (2048) before `set_bit()`, and `ptp_clock_event()` requires `0 <= event->index < PTP_MAX_CHANNELS` before `test_bit()`. `ptp_sys_offset()` and `ptp_sys_offset_extended()` require `n_samples <= PTP_MAX_SAMPLES`. In `ptp_clock_adjtime()` (`ADJ_SETOFFSET`), `ts.tv_nsec` (scaled by 1000 if `!ADJ_NANO`) must be cast to `unsigned long` and checked `< NSEC_PER_SEC` before `timespec64_add()`. [F937, F942, F969]
- `ptp_pin_getfunc()` and `ptp_pin_setfunc()` must check `pd.index < ops->n_pins` and clamp with `array_index_nospec()` before indexing `ops->pin_config`, and `ptp_set_pinfunc()` must bound `chan` against `n_ext_ts`, `n_per_out`, or `0` (`PTP_PF_PHYSYNC`). `ptp_populate_pin_groups()` must allocate `1 + n_pins` entries via `kcalloc()` for `ptp->pin_attr` so `pin_attr_group.attrs` is NULL-terminated, keeping `ptp->pin_attr_groups[1]` NULL. [F938, F944]
- Every child device under a physical `ptp->dev` must be a `ptp_vclock` (`unregister_vclock()` downcasts `ptp->info` via `info_to_vclock()`). In `ptp_vclock_adjfine()`, `timecounter_read(&vclock->tc)` must run under `vclock->lock` before updating `vclock->cc.mult`. [F921, F986]
- In `ptp_dte.c`, `ptp_dte_settime()` must reset `ts_ovf_last` and `ts_wrap_cnt` to 0 under `ptp_dte->lock`, and `dte_write_nco_delta()` / `dte_read_nco_with_ovf()` must update them in lockstep. `ptp_dte_suspend()` must read all `DTE_NUM_REGS_TO_RESTORE` registers into `reg_val[]` before writing 0 to `DTE_NCO_INC_REG`. [F922, F997]
- In `ptp_fc3.c`, `idtfc3->ns` and `idtfc3->last_counter` must be updated together under `idtfc3->lock` in `idtfc3_timecounter_read()` (modulo `sub_sync_count`) and `idtfc3_timecounter_update()` (only after `idtfc3_write_subcounter()` succeeds). `ns2counters()` must handle `nsec < 0` via `sync = -div_u64_rem(-nsec - 1, idtfc3->ns_per_sync, &rem) - 1` and `*sub_ns = idtfc3->ns_per_sync - rem - 1`. [F924, F971]
- While software phase pull-in (`channel->phase_pull_in` in `ptp_clockmatrix.c` or `channel->ddco` in `ptp_idt82p33.c`) is active, `current_freq_scaled_ppm` / `current_freq` holds the baseline frequency to be restored when stopping pull-in, `adjtime` must return `-EBUSY`, and `adjfine` returns `0`. In `ptp_idt82p33.c`, `_idt82p33_gettime()` and `_idt82p33_settime()` must set `channel->discard_next_extts = true`, and `_idt82p33_gettime()` must suspend and re-arm `extts_mask` around the read. [F926, F927]
- `idtcm_load_firmware()`, `idtfc3_load_firmware()`, and `idt82p33_load_firmware()` iterate 4-byte records using signed `s32 len` (`for (len = fw->size; len > 0; len -= sizeof(*rec))`), rejecting non-zero `rec->reserved` with `-EINVAL`. After `check_and_set_masks()`, `idtcm_load_firmware()` and `contains_full_configuration()` must skip `regaddr < GPIO_USER_CONTROL || regaddr >= scratch` and `loaddr` in `0x7c..0x7f` / `0xfc..0xff`; `idt82p33_load_firmware()` must skip `loaddr > 0x7b`. [F945, F946]
- `ptp_clockmatrix.c` ToD helpers (`char_array_to_timespec()`, `timespec_to_char_array()`) use 11 bytes (`buf[0]` sub-ns, `buf[1..4]` `tv_nsec`, `buf[5..10]` `tv_sec`), whereas `ptp_idt82p33.c` uses 10 bytes (`buf[0..3]` `tv_nsec`, `buf[4..9]` `tv_sec`). Any `ptp_clockmatrix.c` access to V520-relocated registers (`SCRATCH`, `SM_RESET`, `DPLL_MODE`, `TOD_CFG`, `DPLL_2/4/6`, `OUTPUT_0..11`, `TOD_*`) must use `IDTCM_FW_REG(idtcm->fw_ver, V520, REG)` after `idtcm_set_version_info()`. [F947, F1002]
- In `ptp_ines.c`, `ines_ptp_probe_channel()` must check `index <= INES_N_PORTS - 1` before `ines_find_port(node, index)`. Before reading a 12-word frame from `ts_tx` or `ts_rx`, `DATA_READ_POS` in `ts_stat_tx`/`ts_stat_rx` must be `0`, and `ines_rxts64()`/`ines_txts64()` must truncate each `ines_read32()` read to `u16` before shifting the `u64` result left by 16. In `ptp_pch.c`, 64-bit timestamp registers use 32-ns ticks (`<<`/`>>` `TICKS_NS_SHIFT` = 5), and `pch_reset()` must follow `pch_block_reset()` with `pch_set_system_time_count()`. [F948, F949, F978, F979, F1000]
- In `ctcm`, `ctcm_ch_alloc_buffer()` saves the initial skb data pointer in `ch->trans_skb_data` and binds `ch->ccw[1]`'s CDA to it; every reuse of `ch->trans_skb` after header/data manipulation must reset `data = ch->trans_skb_data`, `skb_reset_tail_pointer()`, and `len = 0`. In `add_channel()`, `ch->ccw` requires `ccw_num = 17` when `IS_MPC(priv)` and `8` otherwise (`GFP_KERNEL | GFP_DMA`). In `enum ctc_ch_events` / `ctc_ch_states`, non-MPC entries precede `CTC_NR_EVENTS`/`CTC_NR_STATES`, `CTC_EVENT_SEND_XID`/`CH_XID0_PENDING` equal those sentinels, and `CTC_MPC_NR_*` are last. [F930, F953, F959]
- In `chx_rx()` and `ctcm_unpack_skb()`, CTC blocks require `8 <= len <= ch->max_bufsize` and `block_len <= len <= check_len` before subtracting 2, and each sub-packet requires `header->length > LL_HEADER_LENGTH`, `len > LL_HEADER_LENGTH`, and `header->length - LL_HEADER_LENGTH <= skb_tailroom(pskb)`. Non-MPC CTC requires `LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2` (8) bytes framing overhead in `ctcm_transmit_skb()`, `ctcm_tx()`, and `ctcm_change_mtu()`, while MPC requires `TH_HEADER_LENGTH + PDU_HEADER_LENGTH` headroom in `ctcmpc_tx()`. [F951, F952]

## Synchronization
- `ptp_clock_event()` acquires `ptp->tsevqs_lock` and nests `queue->lock` from hardirq context; all process-context acquisitions of either lock must disable local IRQs (`spin_lock_irqsave`), and `tsevqs_lock` must never be acquired while holding `queue->lock`. [F1008]
- Callers of `ptp_set_pinfunc()` and `ptp_find_pin()` must hold `ptp->pincfg_mux`. Because `ptp_chardev.c` and `ptp_sysfs.c` invoke `info->enable` and `info->verify` under `ptp->pincfg_mux`, those callbacks must call `ptp_find_pin()` (not `ptp_find_pin_unlocked()`). `ptp_set_pinfunc()` must return 0 immediately on self-assignment (`pin1 && i == pin`) without calling `ptp_disable_pinfunc()`, while disabling and clearing any other pin (`pin1`) that held `(func, chan)`. [F875, F1012]
- `ptp_clock_unregister()` must never be called while holding a driver mutex acquired by `info->enable` or `info->do_aux_work` (`idtcm->lock`, `idt82p33->lock`, `idtfc3->lock`). `ptp_vclock_in_use()` must return `false` without acquiring `ptp->n_vclocks_mux` when `ptp->is_virtual_clock` is true, and virtual clocks must use `PTP_LOCK_VIRTUAL` on `clock.rwsem`. `ptp_kvm_get_time_fn()` and `ptp_kvm_gettime()` must hold `kvm_ptp_lock` across `kvm_arch_ptp_get_crosststamp()` (with `preempt_disable_notrace()`) and `kvm_arch_ptp_get_clock()`. [F890, F1011, F1014]
- In `ctcm_transmit_skb()`, `ch->collect_lock` must be held across `fsm_getstate(ch->fsm) != CTC_STATE_TXIDLE` and enqueueing onto `ch->collect_queue`, pairing with `chx_txdone()` checking `ch->collect_len > 0` under `ch->collect_lock` before setting `CTC_STATE_TXIDLE`. Because `ctcm_irq_handler()` holds `get_ccwdev_lock(cdev)`, FSM actions calling `ccw_device_start()`/`ccw_device_halt()` (`ctcm_chx_setmode`, `ctcm_chx_haltio`, `ctcm_chx_restart`, `ctcm_chx_txretry`, `ctcmpc_chx_rx`) must acquire `get_ccwdev_lock(ch->cdev)` only in non-IRQ contexts. [F898, F899]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `ptp_clock_register()`, once `device_initialize(&ptp->dev)` sets `ptp->dev.release = ptp_clock_release`, any later error must clean up via `put_device(&ptp->dev)` rather than jumping to pre-device labels or freeing `ptp` directly. `ptp_clock_unregister()` must call `get_device(&ptp->dev)`, set `ptp->defunct = 1` and wake `tsev_wq`, call `posix_clock_unregister()`, `ptp_disable_all_events()`, and stop `aux_work`/`kworker` before the final `put_device(&ptp->dev)`. [F871, F893]
- Under `ptp->n_vclocks_mux`, `n_vclocks_store()` must unregister excess vclocks via `device_for_each_child_reverse()` and reset `ptp->vclock_index` slots to `-1`; `max_vclocks_store()` must check `0 < max <= PTP_MAX_VCLOCKS_LIMIT` and `max >= ptp->n_vclocks` before reallocating `vclock_index`. `ptp_clock_index_by_of_node()` and `ptp_clock_index_by_dev()` match `dev->parent` on `ptp_class` and must call `put_device(dev)`. [F876, F939, F995]
- Probe/remove ordering: `pch_probe()` (`ptp_pch.c`) must call `ptp_clock_register()` before `request_irq()`, and `free_irq()` before `ptp_clock_unregister()`. `ines_ptp_ctrl_probe()` (`ptp_ines.c`) must finish `ines_clock_init()` before `register_mii_tstamp_controller()` and adding `clock->list` under `ines_clocks_lock`; `ines_ptp_ctrl_remove()` must unregister and `list_del(&clock->list)` under `ines_clocks_lock` before `ines_clock_cleanup()`. In `ptp_kvm_x86.c` (`CC_ATTR_GUEST_MEM_ENCRYPT`), once `set_memory_decrypted()` succeeds on `clock_pair`, `kvm_arch_ptp_exit()` must call `set_memory_encrypted()` before `free_page()` and null `clock_pair`. [F878, F879, F896]
- CTCM SKBs on a write channel's `collect_queue` or `io_queue` have `skb->users` incremented via `refcount_inc()`; dequeuing or purging them (`chx_txdone()`, `ctcmpc_chx_txdone()`, `ctcm_purge_skb_queue()`) must call `refcount_dec(&skb->users)` before freeing (never use `skb_queue_purge()`). In `ctcm`, `ch->ccw`, `ch->trans_skb`, `sweep_skb`, and TX bounce buffers (`>= 2 GiB`) require `GFP_DMA`. Freeing `ch->trans_skb` requires `clear_normalized_cda(&ch->ccw[1])` and `ch->trans_skb = NULL`; `ctcm_chx_start()` must clear `cda` and `CCW_FLAG_IDA` on `ch->ccw[4]` after copying `ch->ccw[0..2]` to `ch->ccw[3..5]`. Reload any local `ch->trans_skb` pointer after `ctcm_checkalloc_buffer(ch)`. [F882, F884, F990, F1016]
- `struct ctcm_priv` is allocated in `ctcm_probe_device()` (`get_device(&cgdev->dev)`) and freed only in `ctcm_remove_device()` (`put_device(&cgdev->dev)`); `ctcm_free_netdevice()` and `ctcm_shutdown_device()` must never `kfree(priv)` and must null `priv->mpcg`, `priv->fsm`, `priv->xid`, and `priv->channel[]`. In `ctcm_dbug.c`, `ctcm_register_dbf_views()` failure must call `ctcm_unregister_dbf_views()`, which unregisters handles and sets `ctcm_dbf[x].id = NULL`. [F885, F886]

## API Contracts
- `ptp_clock_register()` mutates `ptp_clock_info` callbacks in place and `ptp_set_pinfunc()` mutates `info->pin_config[]`, so drivers must pass per-instance copies. Setting `adjphase` requires `getmaxphase`; setting `n_pins > 0` requires `pin_config` and `verify`. `ptp_schedule_worker()` / `ptp_cancel_worker_sync()` require non-NULL `info->do_aux_work` at registration, and `do_aux_work` returns a `long` delay `>= 0` to reschedule (`0` loops immediately) or `< 0` (`-1`) to stop. [F903, F919, F1021, F1023]
- `ptp_find_pin()`, `ptp_find_pin_unlocked()`, `ptp_clock_index_by_of_node()`, `ptp_clock_index_by_dev()`, and `ptp_pin_name2index()` return `-1` when not found (store in signed `int`, test `< 0`); `pch_set_station_address()` returns `0` or positive `PCH_INVALIDPARAM` (`1`, test `!= 0`). In `ptp_clock_adjtime()`, gate the `ptp_clock_freerun(ptp)` `-EBUSY` check on `tx->modes & (ADJ_SETOFFSET | ADJ_FREQUENCY | ADJ_OFFSET)` so `tx->modes == 0` queries succeed. In `idtcm_enable()`, `idtfc3_enable()`, and `idt82p33_enable()`, `PTP_CLK_REQ_PEROUT` must handle `!on` before checking that `rq->perout.period` is 1s. [F904, F911, F955, F957]
- `ines_txtstamp()` always consumes `skb` (and `ines_txtstamp_work()` must clear `port->tx_skb = NULL` before completion/free), whereas `ines_rxtstamp()` consumes `skb` via `netif_rx()` only when returning `true`. In `ctcm`, `fsm_event()`/`fsm_addtimer()` on `ch->fsm` must pass `struct channel *` as `arg`, and on `priv->fsm` must pass `struct net_device *`. [F877, F991]

## Error Handling
- In `ptp_clock_adjtime()`, update `ptp->dialed_frequency = tx->freq` only when `ops->adjfine()` returns `0`. Store `idtfc3_read_subcounter()` and `get_output_base_addr()` returns in signed `int` and check `< 0` / `> 0` before casting to `u32` / `u16`. `kvm_arch_ptp_init()` must return `-EOPNOTSUPP` when hypervisor PTP is unavailable. [F909, F910, F915, F1029]
- `ctcm_irq_handler()` must check `ctcm_check_irb_error(cdev, irb)` before dereferencing `irb` (which may be `ERR_PTR`). If `ctcm_transmit_skb()` fails after `skb_push(LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2)` and `refcount_inc(&skb->users)`, it must `skb_pull(skb, LL_HEADER_LENGTH + 2)`, undo the refcount/queueing, and call `ctcm_clear_busy(ch->netdev)`. [F912, F914]

## External Contracts
- In `ptp_ioctl()`, `in_compat_syscall()` converts `arg` with `compat_ptr(arg)` for all commands except scalar `PTP_ENABLE_PPS` and `PTP_ENABLE_PPS2`. V2 ioctls (`PTP_*_REQUEST2`, `PTP_PIN_*FUNC2`) must reject unknown flags and non-zero `rsv` fields with `-EINVAL`, whereas V1 masks flags and zeroes `rsv`. Device-mutating ioctls (`PTP_EXTTS_REQUEST*`, `PTP_PEROUT_REQUEST*`, `PTP_ENABLE_PPS*`, `PTP_PIN_SETFUNC*`) require `FMODE_WRITE` (`-EACCES`), and enabling PPS additionally requires `capable(CAP_SYS_TIME)` (`-EPERM`). [F943, F1026, F1028]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `ptp_open()`, compute `queue->dfs_bitmap.n_elements` with `DIV_ROUND_UP(PTP_MAX_CHANNELS, BITS_PER_BYTE * sizeof(u32))`. In `idtcm_load_firmware()` and `idtfc3_load_firmware()`, `check_and_set_masks()` / `idtfc3_set_hw_param()` must return `-EINVAL` only for invalid metadata and `-EFAULT` for ordinary addresses, advancing `rec++` and resetting `err = 0` before `continue`. [F961, F981]
- In `idtcm_extts_enable()` and `idt82p33_extts_enable()`, `ptp_find_pin()` and `event_channel[index]` use the requesting `channel`, whereas trigger arming and refn storage must target `&idtcm->channel[index]` / `&idt82p33->channel[index]`. In `_idtcm_set_dpll_hw_tod()`, write `HW_DPLL_TOD_OVR__0` after clearing `0x08` in `HW_DPLL_TOD_CTRL_1` when `wr_trig == HW_TOD_WR_TRIG_SEL_MSB`, and before clearing `0x08` otherwise. `idt82p33_set_tod_trigger()` must read `dpll_tod_trigger` and preserve the opposite 4-bit nibble (`READ_TRIGGER_MASK`/`WRITE_TRIGGER_MASK`). [F928, F929, F964]
- In `idtcm_get_dco_delay()`, `mbuf` must be `u8 mbuf[8] = {0}` for `get_unaligned_le64(mbuf)`, denominator `n` clamped to `1` if `0`, and `fodFreq >= 500000000` checked before dividing `18 * (u64)NSEC_PER_SEC`. In `ptp_fc3.c`, `tdc_meas2offset()` must `sign_extend64()` `FINE_MEAS_MASK` (bit 12) and `COARSE_MEAS_MASK` (bit 26) and use `div64_s64()`; `idtfc3_get_tdc_apll_freq()` must mask `tdc_ref_div` with `TDC_REF_DIV_CONFIG_MASK` before `1 << tdc_ref_div` and cast `tdc_fb_div_int` to `u64`. [F972, F974, F975, F987]
- In `ptp_dte.c`, cast `sum3`/`sum2` to `s64` before shifting in `dte_read_nco()`; mask `sum3` with `DTE_NCO_SUM3_MASK` and shift left by `DTE_NCO_SUM3_WR_SHIFT` (8) when writing `DTE_NCO_OVERFLOW_REG`; cast `abs(ppb)` to `u64` and use `div64_u64()` in `DTE_PPB_ADJ(ppb)`. In `ctcm`, test composite status bits (`DEV_STAT_BUSY`+`DEV_STAT_ATTENTION`, `SNS0_EQUIPMENT_CHECK`+`SNS0_BUS_OUT_CHECK`) before single bits in `ctcm_irq_handler()`/`ccw_unit_check()`, and parenthesize `CTCM_ID_SIZE` (`20+3`) in arithmetic. [F958, F966, F968, F992, F996]
