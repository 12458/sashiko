# NVM EXPRESS TARGET DRIVER — drivers/nvme/target (part 1)

Core framework, command engines, configfs/debugfs interfaces, and `nvme-loop` and `nvmet-pci-epf` transports for the Linux NVMe Target (`nvmet`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Before Fabrics Connect installs a queue, `req->sq->ctrl` is `NULL` and `req->sq->size` is `0`: `nvmet_req_init()`, `nvmet_req_transfer_len()`, and `nvmet_set_error()` must check `req->sq->ctrl`, and `nvmet_update_sq_head()` must guard `(old_sqhd + 1) % req->sq->size` with `if (req->sq->size)`. [F50, F127]
- In `nvmet_parse_admin_cmd()` and `nvmet_admin_cmd_data_len()`, `nvme_is_fabrics(cmd)` and `nvmet_is_disc_subsys()` precede `nvmet_check_ctrl_status()` and `nvmet_is_passthru_req()`. In `nvmet_parse_io_cmd()`, `nvme_is_fabrics(cmd)` precedes `nvmet_check_auth_status()`, and `nvmet_is_passthru_req()` precedes `nvmet_req_find_ns()`. [F101, F102]
- For file-backed or `ns->buffered_io` namespaces, `ns->bdev` is `NULL`, so shared helpers (`nvmet_ns_dev()`, `nvmet_ns_revalidate()`) must check `ns->bdev`. `nvmet_file_ns_enable()` caps `ns->blksize_shift` at 12, and `io-cmd-file.c` I/O, discard, and write-zeroes handlers must reject offsets plus lengths exceeding `req->ns->size` with `-ENOSPC`. [F48, F87, F123]
- Classify every non-reservation I/O opcode in `nvmet_is_req_write_cmd_group()` or `nvmet_is_req_read_cmd_group()`. In `nvmet_pr_preempt()`, sample `original_rtype = holder->rtype` and call `nvmet_pr_set_new_holder()` before `nvmet_pr_unreg_all_others*()`. In `nvmet_execute_pr_report()`, increment `num_ctrls` for all registrants in `pr->registrant_list`, but check `(void *)ctrl_eds + sizeof(*ctrl_eds) > (void *)data + num_bytes` before writing `ctrl_eds`. Wrap 64-bit reservation log counters from `0` to `1` and handle `next_count <= cur_count`. [F72, F80, F96, F129, F136]
- `nvmet_passthru_ctrl_enable()` and `nvmet_ns_enable()` enforce mutual exclusion between `subsys->passthru_ctrl` and `subsys->nr_namespaces` under `subsys->lock`; `__nvmet_passthru_ctrl_disable()` erases `passthru_subsystems`, puts module/ctrl refs, clears `subsys->passthru_ctrl`, and resets `subsys->ver = NVMET_DEFAULT_VS`. `nvmet_passthru_override_id_ctrl()` reads identity/fabrics fields from `ctrl` and transfer limits from `pctrl`, clamping `id->mdts` to `pctrl->max_segments`, `pctrl->max_hw_sectors`, and `BIO_MAX_VECS`. [F18, F74, F118, F161]
- `nvmet_enable_port()` rejects `trtype == NVMF_TRTYPE_MAX` before indexing `nvmet_transports[]`. `ctrl->sqs` and `ctrl->cqs` have `subsys->max_qid + 1` entries, so `nvmet_check_sqid()`, `nvmet_check_cqid()`, and `nvmet_execute_io_connect()` must reject `qid > max_qid`. ANA group IDs are in `[1, NVMET_MAX_ANAGRPS]`; `nvmet_ana_group_enabled` and `port->ana_state` have `NVMET_MAX_ANAGRPS + 1` entries and callers must validate `grpid <= NVMET_MAX_ANAGRPS` with `array_index_nospec()`. [F81, F82, F83]
- On `ctrl->changed_ns_list` overflow, `nvmet_add_to_changed_ns_log()` sets `changed_ns_list[0] = cpu_to_le32(0xffffffff)` and `nr_changed_ns = U32_MAX`, which `nvmet_execute_get_log_changed_ns()` checks to set `len = sizeof(__le32)`. In `nvmet_execute_get_log_page_ana()`, if `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()` fails in the first loop, the second loop continues from the current `grpid`. In `nvmet_execute_identify_endgrp_list()`, entries start at `i = 1` with count `cpu_to_le16(i - 1)` in `list[0]`. [F89, F91, F111]
- Once `nvmet_execute_identify_ctrl()` sets `subsys->subsys_discovered = true` under `subsys->lock`, configfs stores for `version`, `serial`, `model`, `ieee_oui`, and `firmware` must reject writes under `subsys->lock`. Updating `port->disc_addr.treq` in `nvmet_addr_treq_store()` / `nvmet_addr_tsas_store()` must preserve non-secure-channel bits via `nvmet_port_disc_addr_treq_mask(port)`. [F75, F76]
- In `loop.c`, `ctrl->queues[0]` is admin and `1..nr_io_queues` are I/O: `nvme_loop_tagset()` uses `tags[queue_idx - 1]` for I/O, while `nvme_loop_init_hctx()` / `nvme_loop_init_request()` use `queues[hctx_idx + 1]`. `async_event_iod` must be the last field of `struct nvme_loop_ctrl` and never use `first_sgl[]`; tag-set `cmd_size` must include `NVME_INLINE_SG_CNT * sizeof(struct scatterlist)`. [F100, F131, F132]
- In `pci-epf.c`, `nvmet_pci_epf_queue` unions `nvme_sq` and `nvme_cq`. On link-down or failed enable, `nvmet_pci_epf_clear_ctrl_config()` zeroes `cc` and `csts` in `ctrl`, `tctrl`, and BAR. On shutdown in `nvmet_pci_epf_disable_ctrl()`, clear `NVME_CC_ENABLE` in `ctrl->cc` and `NVME_REG_CC` alongside `NVME_CSTS_SHST_CMPLT`. `nvmet_pci_epf_poll_cc_work()` only calls `nvmet_update_cc()` and writes `NVME_REG_CSTS` when `NVME_REG_CC != ctrl->cc` and `nvmet_pci_epf_enable_ctrl()` succeeds, while still rescheduling `poll_cc` on failure. In `nvmet_pci_epf_iod_parse_prp_list()`, when `xfer_len > ctrl->mps` and `i == nr_prps - 1`, `prps[i]` is a chained pointer (`nr_prps = 0; continue`). In `nvmet_pci_epf_get_sgl_segment()`, copy a trailing segment descriptor to `*desc` with `nr_descs--`, else set `desc->length = 0`. [F63, F64, F65, F93, F115, F147]

## Synchronization
- Lock order is `nvmet_config_sem` -> `subsys->lock` -> `ctrl->lock` (and `ns->pr.pr_sem` -> `subsys->lock` -> `ctrl->pr_log_mgr.lock` / `ctrl->lock`). Drop `ctrl->lock` around `nvmet_req_complete()` in `nvmet_async_events_process()` and `nvmet_async_events_failall()`. [F35, F36]
- `nvmet_config_sem` protects `nvmet_transports`, `nvmet_genctr`, `nvmet_disc_subsys->subsysnqn`, `port->subsystems`, `port->referrals`, `subsys->hosts`, and `subsys->allow_any_host`; mutations hold `down_write` and call `nvmet_port_disc_changed()` or `nvmet_subsys_disc_changed()`. In `nvmet_enable_port()`, drop `nvmet_config_sem` before `request_module()`, then re-acquire and re-read `nvmet_transports[]`. `nvmet_ana_sem` protects `nvmet_ana_group_enabled[]`, `nvmet_ana_chgcnt`, `port->ana_state[]`, and `ns->anagrpid`; updates hold `down_write`, adjust `nvmet_ana_group_enabled[]`, increment `nvmet_ana_chgcnt`, and send an ANA AEN; `nvmet_execute_get_log_page_ana()` holds `down_read`. [F29, F31, F38, F69, F70]
- In `nvmet_ns_enable()` and `nvmet_ns_disable()`, update `ns->enabled`, `NVMET_NS_ENABLED`, P2P maps, `nvmet_pr_init_ns()`, and `ns->ref` under `subsys->lock`. `nvmet_ns_disable()` drops `subsys->lock` before `percpu_ref_kill(&ns->ref)`, `synchronize_rcu()`, `wait_for_completion(&ns->disable_done)`, and `percpu_ref_exit(&ns->ref)` prior to `nvmet_pr_exit_ns()` and `nvmet_ns_dev_disable()`. Configfs callers hold `down_write(&nvmet_config_sem)`. [F20, F22, F68, F171]
- `nvmet_req_init()` (under `rcu_read_lock()` in `nvme_loop_queue_rq()`) and `nvmet_req_complete()` / `__nvmet_req_complete()` / `queue_response` (in IRQ/softirq or RCU context) must never sleep. `ctrl->error_lock` and `cq->lock` in `pci-epf.c` require `spin_lock_irqsave()`. [F162, F163, F167, F168]
- In `pr.c`, `pr->holder` and `pr->registrant_list` are read under `rcu_read_lock()`; mutations under `ns->pr.pr_sem` must use RCU primitives and `kfree_rcu()`. For `NVME_PR_ACQUIRE_ACT_PREEMPT_AND_ABORT`, `nvmet_execute_pr_acquire()` keeps `pr_sem` held across queuing `abort_work`, and `nvmet_pr_do_abort()` releases it after each `nvmet_pr_per_ctrl_ref` (`PERCPU_REF_ALLOW_REINIT`) drains, calls `reinit_completion()` on `confirm_done`/`free_done`, and calls `percpu_ref_resurrect()`. [F33, F73, F173]
- Use `READ_ONCE()`/`try_cmpxchg()` for `req->sq->sqhd`, `cmpxchg()` for `sq->ctrl`/`cq->ctrl` in `nvmet_install_queue()`, `READ_ONCE()`/`WRITE_ONCE()` for `ctrl->aen_enabled`, and `test_and_set_bit()`/`clear_bit()` (when `RAE == 0`) for `ctrl->aen_masked`. Schedule `ka_work`, `async_event_work`, `fatal_err_work`, and passthrough commands with `use_workqueue` or non-trivial effects on `nvmet_wq`. [F39, F40, F41, F166, F191]
- In `pci-epf.c`, test `NVMET_PCI_EPF_Q_LIVE` on entry to `create_cq`/`create_sq` and `set_bit()` after full init. In `nvmet_pci_epf_queue_response()`, successful `DMA_TO_DEVICE` commands only call `complete(&iod->done)` so `nvmet_pci_epf_exec_iod_work()` transfers data in process context. Hold `mmio_lock` across chunked `nvmet_pci_epf_mem_map()` loops in `nvmet_pci_epf_mmio_transfer()`, hold `dma_rx_lock`/`dma_tx_lock` across `nvmet_pci_epf_dma_transfer()`, and check `time_is_before_jiffies()` + `cond_resched()` before `if (nr_sqs) continue` in `nvmet_pci_epf_poll_sqs_work()`. [F19, F117, F152, F153, F164]

## Object Lifetimes
- `nvmet_req_complete()` must save `sq = req->sq` before `__nvmet_req_complete(req, status)` and pass `sq` to `percpu_ref_put(&sq->ref)`. In `nvmet_sq_destroy()`, call `nvmet_async_events_failall(ctrl)` for `sqid == 0` before killing `sq->ref`, and re-read `ctrl = sq->ctrl` after `wait_for_completion(&sq->free_done)` before clearing `ctrl->sqs[sq->qid]` and `sq->ctrl` and calling `nvmet_ctrl_put(ctrl)`. [F3, F5, F23]
- In `nvmet_alloc_ctrl()`, failures after adding `ctrl` to `subsys->ctrls` must call `nvmet_ctrl_put(ctrl)`. In `nvmet_ctrl_free()`, stop `ka_work`, flush `async_event_work`, and cancel `fatal_err_work` before freeing events, queues, or `ctrl`. In `nvmet_set_feat_kato()`, call `nvmet_stop_keep_alive_timer()` before overwriting `ctrl->kato`. [F11, F24, F71]
- `inline_bio` must be first in `req->b`, `req->p`, and `req->z`; `nvmet_req_bio_put()` calls `bio_uninit()` for `&req->b.inline_bio` and `bio_put()` otherwise. `nvmet_req_free_sgls()` branches on `req->p2p_dev` (`pci_p2pmem_free_sgl()` vs `sgl_free()`). [F7, F14, F141]
- `nvmet_bvec_cache` is module-wide while `ns->bvec_pool` is per-namespace. When `req->f.mpool_alloc` is true, `req->f.bvec` has `NVMET_MAX_MPOOL_BVEC` (16) entries: `nvmet_file_execute_io()` chunks synchronous batches at 16 (resetting `bv_cnt` and `len`), and `req->f.bvec != req->inline_bvec` is freed via `mempool_free()` if `mpool_alloc` else `kfree()`. `nvmet_file_ns_disable()` flushes `buffered_io_wq` when `ns->buffered_io` is set before destroying `bvec_pool` and closing `ns->file`. [F13, F25, F78, F113, F135]
- `nvmet_init_debugfs()` precedes `nvmet_init_discovery()` and `nvmet_exit_discovery()` precedes `nvmet_exit_debugfs()`. `nvmet_port_release()` flushes `nvmet_wq`, and `nvme_loop_remove_port()` / `nvme_loop_cleanup_module()` flush `nvme_delete_wq`. Free temporary buffers passed to `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()`, free `dhchap_secret`/`dhchap_ctrl_secret` in `nvmet_host_release()`, and update `subsys` strings (`subsysnqn`, `model_number`, `firmware_rev`) by allocating, swapping under lock, and `kfree()`ing old pointers. Return early in `nvmet_ana_group_release()` if `grp == &grp->port->ana_default_group`. Use `list_for_each_entry_safe()` and copy `reg->hostid` before `nvmet_pr_unregister_one()`. Open backend files and passthrough controllers during configfs enable in task context. [F6, F16, F17, F26, F109, F133, F140, F159, F196]
- In `pci-epf.c`, `epc_init` only calls `nvmet_pci_epf_start_ctrl()` when `!epc_features->linkup_notifier` after header/BAR/IRQ setup; `unbind` calls `nvmet_pci_epf_destroy_ctrl()` before BAR teardown. `nvmet_pci_epf_disable_ctrl()` cancels `poll_sqs`, then deletes I/O SQs, I/O CQs, admin SQ 0, and CQ 0. `nvmet_pci_epf_delete_cq()` cancels `cq->work`, drains `cq`, and clears `NVMET_PCI_EPF_Q_IRQ_ENABLED` via `test_and_clear_bit()` before unmapping and removing the IRQ vector. `nvmet_pci_epf_free_iod()` only frees `data_segs` if `!= &iod->data_seg` and `data_sgt` if `nents > 1`. [F49, F66, F149, F150, F155, F156]

## API Contracts
- When `nvmet_req_init()`, `nvmet_check_transfer_len()`, or `nvmet_check_data_len_lte()` returns `false`, the request is already completed; callers must return without calling `nvmet_req_complete()` or `nvmet_req_uninit()`, whereas failures after `nvmet_req_init()` succeeds must call one of them. [F4, F42]
- Set `req->transfer_len` (including `metadata_len`) before `nvmet_req_alloc_sgls()`. Never pass `ZERO_PAGE(0)` to `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()` (use `nvmet_zero_sgl()`). In `nvmet_bdev_execute_rw()`, set `REQ_NOMERGE` for P2PDMA pages, and when `req->metadata_len > 0`, call `nvmet_bdev_alloc_bip()` on each bio after `bio_add_page()` (shifting sector by `bi->interval_exp - SECTOR_SHIFT` for `bip_set_seed()`, adjusting `miter->consumed -= miter->length - len` before `sg_miter_stop()`, and calling `bio_io_error(bio)` on failure). [F60, F114, F176, F177, F184, F188]
- Shift internal `status` by 1 when writing/reading `cqe->status` or error slots. Convert wire KATO (ms) to `ctrl->kato` (s) via `DIV_ROUND_UP(kato, 1000)` and back via `* 1000`. Convert 0-based wire queue sizes to 1-based `sq.size`/`cq.size` (`size + 1`). Pass `NVME_AEN_BIT_*` to `nvmet_aen_bit_disabled()`/`nvmet_clear_aen_bit()` and `NVME_AEN_CFG_*` to `nvmet_set_feat_async_event()`. [F57, F59, F84, F186]
- `nvmet_parse_pr_cmd()` returns `0` on PR opcodes (which `nvmet_parse_io_cmd()` returns early on) and `1` otherwise. `nvmet_bdev_ns_enable()` returns `-ENOTBLK` for `ns->buffered_io` or regular files so `nvmet_ns_enable()` falls back to `nvmet_file_ns_enable()`. `nvmet_alloc_ctrl()` copies `NVMF_NQN_SIZE` bytes from `args->subsysnqn`/`hostnqn` by value. `host_traddr` ops must return `<= traddr_len`. [F46, F103, F142, F144, F146]
- Transports must implement `add_port`, `remove_port`, `queue_response`, and `delete_ctrl`; PCI transports also require `create_sq`, `delete_sq`, `create_cq`, `delete_cq`, `set_feature`, and `get_feature` (with typed `nvmet_feat_*` structs). In `pci-epf.c`, verify `pci_map.pci_size >= cq->pci_size`, pass `cq->vector + 1` to `pci_epc_raise_irq()` for MSI/MSI-X (falling back to INTx only if `intx_capable`), and return `-EBUSY` in configfs stores when `ctrl.tctrl` is set. [F61, F137, F151, F154, F157, F181, F182]

## Error Handling
- Initialize `req->cqe`, `req->ns`, `req->pc_ref`, `req->error_loc`, and `req->error_slba` before any `goto fail` in `nvmet_req_init()`. Reset `req->ns = NULL` if `!ns->enabled` in `nvmet_req_find_ns()`. Always populate `cqe->sq_head`, `sq_id`, `command_id`, and `status` in `nvmet_pci_epf_cq_work()`. Reset `ns->bdev_file`, `ns->file`, or `port->keyring` to `NULL` on `IS_ERR()`. In `nvmet_set_feat_write_protect()`, restore `ns->readonly = false` without `nvmet_ns_changed()` if flush fails. Capture `status` from `nvmet_passthru_override_id_*()`, and convert positive NVMe status codes to `-EIO` in `nvme_loop_create_ctrl()`. Map `-EOPNOTSUPP`/`BLK_STS_NOTSUPP` to `NVME_SC_INVALID_OPCODE | NVME_STATUS_DNR` in status helpers, but ignore `-EOPNOTSUPP` in discard loops. [F44, F47, F51, F52, F53, F54, F79, F107, F108]

## External Contracts
- In `nvmet_execute_identify()`, dispatch `csi`-independent CNS values directly and handle both `NVME_CSI_NVM` and `NVME_CSI_ZNS` for `CS_NS`/`CS_CTRL`; return a zeroed `nvme_id_ns` (`status = 0`) when `nvmet_req_find_ns()` fails in `nvmet_execute_identify_ns()`; and reject `min_nsid >= 0xFFFFFFFE` in `nvmet_execute_identify_nslist()`. Zero-initialize response structs/SGLs, space-pad `sn`/`mn`/`fr` and `subsys->serial` with `memcpy_and_pad()`, and set `NVME_CMD_EFFECTS_LBCC` on data-modifying I/O commands. Only check `iosqes`/`iocqes` in `nvmet_start_ctrl()` when `!nvmet_is_disc_subsys(ctrl->subsys)`. `nvmet_pci_epf_configure_bar()` requires 64-bit `BAR_0`, and PRP parsing requires 4-byte aligned `prp1` offset and `0` offset on `prp2`/PRP-list entries. [F55, F56, F90, F92, F98, F106, F148, F187, F194]

## Known Pitfalls
- NUL-terminate `d->subsysnqn` and `d->hostnqn` at `NVMF_NQN_FIELD_LEN - 1` after `nvmet_copy_from_sgl()`, and bound `subsys->serial` to `NVMET_SN_MAX_SIZE`. Convert wire endianness (`le*_to_cpu`/`cpu_to_le*`), use `put_unaligned_le*()` for `id->ieee`, 128-bit counters, and `data->regctl`, cast `range.nlb` to `loff_t` before shifting by `blksize_shift` in `nvmet_file_execute_discard()`, and clamp block-limit ratios with `to0based()`. Reset `ctrl = NULL` after the loop in `nvmet_ctrl_find_get()`. Use referral `r` and `r->disc_addr.traddr` for referrals in `nvmet_execute_disc_get_log_page()`. Check `target->ci_type` in configfs `allow_link` callbacks and match parent depth when downcasting `config_item` pointers (`to_nvmet_port(item->ci_parent->ci_parent)` for referral `parent`). [F85, F94, F95, F110, F119, F120, F121, F126, F138, F139, F145]
