# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu (part 22)

This unit implements the System DMA IP block drivers for SDMA v5.2 (RDNA2 / GC 10.3), SDMA v6.0 (RDNA3 / GC 11.0), and SDMA v7.0 (RDNA4 / GC 12.0), the SDMA v6.0 packet header definitions (`sdma_v6_0_0_pkt_open.h`), and the Southern Islands (`si.c`, GCN 1.0 Tahiti/Pitcairn/Verde/Oland/Hainan) SoC family common IP block and ASIC-level initialization.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `sdma_v5_2_gfx_resume_instance`, `sdma_v6_0_gfx_resume_instance`, and `sdma_v7_0_gfx_resume_instance`, programming the ring write pointer (or calling `set_wptr` under SR-IOV) to a potentially smaller value must be bracketed by setting `mmSDMA0_GFX_MINOR_PTR_UPDATE` / `regSDMA0_QUEUE0_MINOR_PTR_UPDATE` to `1` beforehand and clearing it to `0` afterward. [F1973]
- When `sdma_v5_2_gfx_resume_instance`, `sdma_v6_0_gfx_resume_instance`, or `sdma_v7_0_gfx_resume_instance` is called during per-queue recovery (`sdma_v5_2_restore_queue`, `sdma_v6_0_reset_queue`, `sdma_v7_0_reset_queue`), `restore` must be `true`, `ring->wptr` must be preserved, and both hardware `RB_RPTR` and `RB_WPTR` must be initialized to `ring->wptr << 2` rather than zeroed. [F1974]
- In `sdma_v5_2_stop_queue` and `sdma_v5_2_soft_reset_engine`, per-engine reset must isolate the target instance by passing `1 << ring->me` to `sdma_v5_2_gfx_stop` and toggling only `GRBM_SOFT_RESET__SOFT_RESET_SDMA0 << instance_id` via read-modify-write on `mmGRBM_SOFT_RESET`. [F1976]
- `sdma_v5_2_get_reg_offset`, `sdma_v6_0_get_reg_offset`, and `sdma_v7_0_get_reg_offset` map `internal_offset` in `[SDMA0_HYP_DEC_REG_START, SDMA0_HYP_DEC_REG_END]` to GC segment 1 with stride `SDMA1_HYP_DEC_REG_OFFSET * instance`, and other offsets to GC segment 0 (or segment 2 for `instance >= 2` on v5.2). Broken by: computing instance offsets by adding `SDMA1_REG_OFFSET * instance` directly. [F1992]
- In `sdma_v5_2.c`, `sdma_v6_0.c`, and `sdma_v7_0.c`, `copy_num_dw`, `fill_num_dw`, and `copy_pte_num_dw` in `sdma_v*_buffer_funcs` and `sdma_v*_vm_pte_funcs` must match the exact DWord counts written by `sdma_v*_emit_copy_buffer`, `sdma_v*_emit_fill_buffer`, and `sdma_v*_vm_copy_pte` (7, 5, 7 on v5.2/v6.0; 8, 5, 8 on v7.0). [F1978]
- In `sdma_v5_2_ring_funcs`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_funcs`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_funcs`, `.emit_frame_size` and `.emit_ib_size` must remain `>=` the maximum dwords emitted per submission frame and per IB across all ring emission callbacks called by `amdgpu_ib_schedule`. [F1996]
- In `sdma_v5_2_set_mqd_funcs`, `sdma_v6_0_set_mqd_funcs`, and `sdma_v7_0_set_mqd_funcs`, `adev->mqds[AMDGPU_HW_IP_DMA].mqd_size` must equal `sizeof` the struct type (`v10_sdma_mqd`, `v11_sdma_mqd`, or `v12_sdma_mqd`) that the paired `sdma_v*_mqd_init` callback casts `void *mqd` to. [F2015]
- In `sdma_v5_2_update_medium_grain_clock_gating` and `sdma_v5_2_update_medium_grain_light_sleep`, when an SDMA instance's `fw_version` does not support MGCG or light sleep, `AMD_CG_SUPPORT_SDMA_MGCG` or `AMD_CG_SUPPORT_SDMA_LS` must be cleared from `adev->cg_flags` in lockstep with disabling the feature in `mmSDMA0_CLK_CTRL` or `mmSDMA0_POWER_CNTL`. [F1981]
- In `si_gpu_pci_config_reset`, clocks must be set to bypass mode (`si_set_clk_bypass_mode`), SPLL powered down (`si_spll_powerdown`), and PCI bus mastering cleared (`pci_clear_master`) before calling `amdgpu_device_pci_config_reset`, and `pci_set_master` must only be restored once `RREG32(mmCONFIG_MEMSIZE) != 0xffffffff`. [F2035]
- In `si_uvd_send_upll_ctlreq` and `si_vce_send_vcepll_ctlreq`, the polling loop must test `((reg & mask) == mask)` for `mask = UPLL_CTLACK_MASK | UPLL_CTLACK2_MASK` so both acknowledge bits are asserted, and must deassert `UPLL_CTLREQ_MASK` after the loop on both success and `-ETIMEDOUT` paths. [F2006]
- In `si_calc_upll_dividers`, `vco_freq` must be widened to `uint64_t` before multiplying by `fb_factor` (`(uint64_t)vco_freq * fb_factor`) and divided by `ref_freq` using `do_div(fb_div, ref_freq)`. [F2013]
- In `si.c`, `CHIP_HAINAN` has no UVD, VCE, or DCE hardware blocks and `CHIP_OLAND` has no VCE encode support: `si_set_ip_blocks` must not register `uvd_v3_1_ip_block` or DCE on `CHIP_HAINAN`, and `si_query_video_codecs` must return empty codec tables for Oland/Hainan encode and Hainan decode. [F2037]
- In `si.c`, every golden-register and clockgating/powergating table passed to `amdgpu_device_program_register_sequence` in `si_init_golden_registers` (`tahiti_*`, `pitcairn_*`, `verde_*`, `oland_*`, `hainan_*`) must consist of `(reg, and_mask, or_mask)` `u32` triples whose `ARRAY_SIZE` is an exact multiple of 3. [F1997]

## Synchronization

- When `ring->use_doorbell` is enabled, `sdma_v5_2_ring_set_wptr`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_set_wptr`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_set_wptr` must publish `ring->wptr << 2` to `ring->wptr_cpu_addr` via `atomic64_set` before ringing `WDOORBELL64`, paired with `READ_ONCE` in `sdma_v*_ring_get_wptr`. [F1952]
- In `sdma_v6_0_process_fence_irq` and `sdma_v7_0_process_fence_irq`, `xa_lock_irqsave(&adev->userq_xa, flags)` must be held continuously across `xa_load(&adev->userq_xa, doorbell_offset)` and `amdgpu_userq_fence_driver_process(fence_drv)`. Broken by: unlocking `userq_xa` right after `xa_load` without pinning a reference on `fence_drv`. [F1955]
- In `sdma_v5_2_reset_queue`, `sdma_v6_0_reset_queue`, and `sdma_v7_0_reset_queue`, `amdgpu_ring_reset_helper_begin(ring, timedout_fence)` must be called before resetting and resuming the queue (`amdgpu_sdma_reset_engine` or `amdgpu_mes_reset_legacy_queue`), and `amdgpu_ring_reset_helper_end` must be called last. [F1956]
- In `sdma_v5_2_sw_init`, `mutex_init(&adev->sdma.instance[i].engine_reset_mutex)` and `adev->sdma.instance[i].funcs = &sdma_v5_2_sdma_funcs` must be initialized for every SDMA instance before `amdgpu_sdma_reset_engine` can be invoked by `sdma_v5_2_reset_queue`. [F1957]
- In `sdma_v5_2_ring_preempt_ib`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_preempt_ib`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_preempt_ib`, calling `amdgpu_ring_set_preempt_cond_exec(ring, false)` and emitting/committing the trailing fence must precede asserting `mmSDMA0_GFX_PREEMPT` / `regSDMA0_QUEUE0_PREEMPT`, and both the preempt register and `cond_exec` flag must be restored after polling. [F1960]
- In `sdma_v5_2_dump_ip_state`, `sdma_v6_0_dump_ip_state`, and `sdma_v7_0_dump_ip_state`, reading per-instance SDMA registers into `adev->sdma.ip_dump` must be bracketed by `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, false)` and `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, true)`. [F1959]
- In `si.c`, indirect register accessors must hold their IRQ-saving spinlocks (`spin_lock_irqsave`/`spin_unlock_irqrestore`) across the entire index write and data read/write sequence: `adev->pcie_idx_lock` for `si_pcie_*` (`AMDGPU_PCIE_INDEX`/`DATA`), `si_pciep_*` (`PCIE_PORT_INDEX`/`DATA`), `si_pif_phy0_*`, and `si_pif_phy1_*` (`EVERGREEN_PIF_PHY*_INDEX`/`DATA`); `adev->smc_idx_lock` for `si_smc_*` (`mmSMC_IND_INDEX_0`/`DATA_0`); and `adev->uvd_ctx_idx_lock` for `si_uvd_ctx_*` (`mmUVD_CTX_INDEX`/`DATA`). [F1945]
- When `si_get_register_value` reads a GRBM-indexed register from hardware (`indexed == true` and not cached in `adev->gfx.config`), it must hold `adev->grbm_idx_mutex` and restore `amdgpu_gfx_select_se_sh(adev, 0xffffffff, 0xffffffff, 0xffffffff, 0)` before unlocking. [F1946]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `sdma_v5_2_early_init`, `sdma_v6_0_early_init`, and `sdma_v7_0_early_init`, `amdgpu_sdma_init_microcode(adev, 0, true)` must be called before `sdma_v*_set_ring_funcs(adev)` because `amdgpu_sdma_init_microcode` with `duplicate == true` overwrites `adev->sdma.instance[1..num_instances-1]` via `memcpy` from `instance[0]`. [F1938]
- In `sdma_v5_2_sw_fini`, `sdma_v6_0_sw_fini`, and `sdma_v7_0_sw_fini`, `amdgpu_ring_fini(&adev->sdma.instance[i].ring)` must be called for all instances before `amdgpu_sdma_destroy_inst_ctx(adev, true)`, and `duplicate` must be `true`. [F1939]
- In `sdma_v6_0_hw_init`/`sdma_v6_0_hw_fini` and `sdma_v7_0_hw_init`/`sdma_v7_0_hw_fini` (on bare metal), `sdma_v*_set_userq_trap_interrupts(adev, true)` in `hw_init` must be paired with `sdma_v*_set_userq_trap_interrupts(adev, false)` in `hw_fini` to balance `amdgpu_irq_get` and `amdgpu_irq_put` on `adev->sdma.trap_irq`. [F1941]
- `sdma_v5_2_ring_begin_use` and `sdma_v5_2_ring_end_use` must keep `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, false)` and `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, true)` balanced while an SDMA v5.2 ring is in use, because `sdma_v5_2_ring_set_wptr` directly writes `mmSDMA0_GFX_RB_WPTR` and `mmSDMA0_GFX_RB_WPTR_HI` on IP 5.2.1 assuming GFXOFF is disallowed. [F1942]
- `si_read_disabled_bios` must save and unconditionally restore `mmBUS_CNTL`, `R600_ROM_CNTL`, and (when `adev->mode_info.num_crtc` is non-zero) `mmD1VGA_CONTROL`, `mmD2VGA_CONTROL`, and `mmVGA_RENDER_CONTROL` around `amdgpu_read_bios(adev)`. [F1947]

## API Contracts

- `sdma_v7_0_ring_emit_wreg` must shift the caller-supplied dword register index left by 2 (`reg << 2`) when emitting `SDMA_OP_SRBM_WRITE`, whereas `sdma_v5_2_ring_emit_wreg` and `sdma_v6_0_ring_emit_wreg` pass the dword register index `reg` unshifted and `*_ring_emit_reg_wait` shifts `reg << 2` across all three versions. Broken by: passing an unshifted dword offset to `SDMA_OP_SRBM_WRITE` in `sdma_v7_0.c` or passing a pre-shifted byte offset to `amdgpu_ring_emit_wreg` / `amdgpu_ring_emit_reg_wait`. [F1962]
- `sdma_v5_2_ring_init_cond_exec`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_init_cond_exec`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_init_cond_exec` must capture and return `ring->wptr & ring->buf_mask` immediately before writing the trailing `0` placeholder dword for `EXEC_COUNT`. [F1989]
- `si_flush_hdp` and `si_invalidate_hdp` must check `if (!ring || !ring->funcs->emit_wreg)` and fall back to direct MMIO (`WREG32`/`RREG32`) when `ring` is `NULL` or lacks `emit_wreg`, rather than unconditionally calling `amdgpu_ring_emit_wreg`. [F1969]
- In `si_read_register` and `si_get_register_value`, only `si_allowed_read_registers` offsets are allowed, `grbm_indexed` entries must map `se_num == 0xffffffff` and `sh_num == 0xffffffff` to `0` before indexing `adev->gfx.config.rb_config`, and `mmGB_TILE_MODE0..mmGB_TILE_MODE31` index `tile_mode_array[32]` via `reg_offset - mmGB_TILE_MODE0`. [F1998]
- In `si_uvd_calc_upll_post_div` and `si_calc_upll_dividers`, any post divider above `pd_even` must be rounded up to an even integer, `vco_freq / post_div` must not exceed `target_freq`, and `si_calc_upll_dividers` must return `-EINVAL` if no divider combination within `fb_mask` and `pd_max` improves on `optimal_score = ~0`. [F1970]
- `si_read_bios_from_rom` rounds `length_bytes` up to a 4-byte multiple (`length_dw = ALIGN(length_bytes, 4) / 4`) and writes `length_dw` 32-bit words to `(u32 *)bios`, so callers must pass a 4-byte-aligned `bios` buffer allocated to at least `ALIGN(length_bytes, 4)` bytes. [F1999]

## Error Handling

- In `sdma_v7_0_load_microcode`, each per-instance `adev->sdma.instance[i].sdma_fw_obj` created with `amdgpu_bo_create_reserved` must be unmapped (`amdgpu_bo_kunmap`) and unreserved (`amdgpu_bo_unreserve`) after copying firmware data, and `sdma_v7_0_start` must call `sdma_v12_0_free_ucode_buffer(adev)` if `sdma_v7_0_load_microcode` fails. [F1940]
- `sdma_v5_2_stop_queue` and `sdma_v5_2_restore_queue` must pair `amdgpu_gfx_rlc_enter_safe_mode(adev, 0)` with `amdgpu_gfx_rlc_exit_safe_mode(adev, 0)` on all paths (including the freeze timeout path in `sdma_v5_2_stop_queue`), and `sdma_v5_2_reset_queue` must call `amdgpu_amdkfd_resume(adev, true)` even when `amdgpu_sdma_reset_engine` fails. [F1943]
- In `sdma_v5_2_stop_queue`, when the `adev->usec_timeout` poll for `SDMA0_FREEZE__FROZEN` expires (`j == adev->usec_timeout`), the function must still proceed to halt F32 (`SDMA0_F32_CNTL.HALT = 1`) if `(stat1_reg & 0x3FF) == 0x3FF` in `mmSDMA0_STATUS1_REG`, and only fail with `-ETIMEDOUT` via `err0` when `(stat1_reg & 0x3FF) != 0x3FF`. [F2004]
- `sdma_v6_0_process_trap_irq` and `sdma_v7_0_process_trap_irq` extract `queue = entry->ring_id & 0xf` and `instances = (entry->ring_id & 0xf0) >> 4`, and must reject `instances > 1` with `-EINVAL` before indexing `adev->sdma.instance[instances].ring`. [F1993]

## External Contracts

- In SDMA v5.2, v6.0, and v7.0, `ring->wptr` and the return values of `sdma_v*_ring_get_rptr` and `sdma_v*_ring_get_wptr` are in DWords, whereas `rptr_cpu_addr`, `wptr_cpu_addr`, `WDOORBELL64`, and the `RB_RPTR`/`RB_WPTR` registers store byte offsets (`wptr << 2`, converted back via `>> 2`). [F1990]
- In `sdma_v*_gfx_resume_instance` and `sdma_v*_mqd_init` across `sdma_v5_2.c`, `sdma_v6_0.c`, and `sdma_v7_0.c`, `RB_CNTL.RB_SIZE` must be programmed as `order_base_2(size_in_bytes / 4)` (log2 of DWords) and `RB_BASE`/`RB_BASE_HI` (`sdmax_rlcx_rb_base`) must be programmed with the 256-byte-aligned GPU base address shifted right by 8 bits (`gpu_addr >> 8`). [F1991]
- In `sdma_v5_2_ring_emit_ib`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_emit_ib`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_emit_ib`, `(2 - lower_32_bits(ring->wptr)) & 7` NOP dwords must be emitted before the 6-dword `SDMA_OP_INDIRECT` packet so the packet ends on an 8-dword ring boundary, and `ib->gpu_addr` must be masked to 32-byte alignment (`& 0xffffffe0`). [F1964]
- In `sdma_v5_2_ring_emit_fence`, `sdma_v6_0_ring_emit_fence`, and `sdma_v7_0_ring_emit_fence`, `addr` must be 4-byte aligned (`BUG_ON(addr & 0x3)`), and a 64-bit fence (`AMDGPU_FENCE_FLAG_64BIT`) must be emitted as two separate 4-dword `SDMA_OP_FENCE` packets writing `lower_32_bits(seq)` to `addr` and `upper_32_bits(seq)` to `addr + 4`. [F1988]
- `sdma_v5_2_process_trap_irq` must mask `entry->src_data[0]` with `AMDGPU_FENCE_MES_QUEUE_ID_MASK` before `idr_find(&adev->mes.queue_id_idr, ...)`, and `sdma_v6_0_process_fence_irq` and `sdma_v7_0_process_fence_irq` must shift `entry->src_data[0]` right by `SDMA0_QUEUE0_DOORBELL_OFFSET__OFFSET__SHIFT` before `xa_load(&adev->userq_xa, ...)`. [F1994]
- `sdma_v6_0_load_microcode` parses `struct sdma_firmware_header_v2_0` to load two sections: `le32_to_cpu(hdr->ctx_jt_offset + hdr->ctx_jt_size) / 4` dwords from `ucode_array_offset_bytes` to `UCODE_ADDR = 0`, and `le32_to_cpu(hdr->ctl_jt_offset + hdr->ctl_jt_size) / 4` dwords from `ctl_ucode_offset` to `UCODE_ADDR = 0x8000`. [F1995]
- In `si.c`, indirect register index writes must mask `reg` to the hardware index width: `reg & 0xff` in `si_pciep_rreg`/`si_pciep_wreg` (`PCIE_PORT_INDEX`), `reg & 0x1ff` in `si_uvd_ctx_rreg`/`si_uvd_ctx_wreg` (`mmUVD_CTX_INDEX`), and `reg & 0xffff` in `si_pif_phy0_rreg`/`wreg` and `si_pif_phy1_rreg`/`wreg` (`EVERGREEN_PIF_PHY*_INDEX`). [F2001]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `si_get_pcie_usage` (`si.c`), the upper counter overflow fields `cnt0_of` and `cnt1_of` extracted from `ixPCIE_PERF_CNTL_TXCLK` must be stored in `uint64_t` variables before shifting left by 32 (`cnt0_of << 32`, `cnt1_of << 32`) and ORing with the 32-bit lower counter registers. [F2012]
