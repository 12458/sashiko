# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu (part 4)

Implements GMC and GTT TTM management (`amdgpu_gmc.c`, `amdgpu_gtt_mgr.c`), TTM/GEM BO lifecycle (`amdgpu_object.c`), HMM notifiers (`amdgpu_hmm.c`), VMID/PASID allocators (`amdgpu_ids.c`), IB and scheduler jobs (`amdgpu_ib.c`, `amdgpu_job.c`), IH ring/IRQ dispatch (`amdgpu_ih.c`, `amdgpu_irq.c`), MES (`amdgpu_mes.c`), KMS ioctls (`amdgpu_kms.c`), and MCA/JPEG/ISP/I2C/HDP/NBIO helpers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- TTM callbacks (`amdgpu_bo_move_notify`, `amdgpu_bo_release_notify`) must check `amdgpu_bo_is_amdgpu_bo(bo)` before `ttm_to_amdgpu_bo(bo)`. `to_amdgpu_bo_user()` is valid only on `amdgpu_bo_create_user()` BOs (`BUG_ON(bo->tbo.type == ttm_bo_type_kernel)` in tiling/metadata helpers). `amdgpu_bo_{pin,unpin}()` only call `dma_buf_{pin,unpin}()` and update `adev->{vram,visible,gart}_pin_size` on 0/1 transitions of `pin_count`. [F556, F594, F595]
- In `amdgpu_bo_placement_from_domain()`, put `VRAM` before `GTT` so `placements[0]` is VRAM for `TTM_PL_FLAG_DESIRED`, and set `TTM_PL_FLAG_FALLBACK` on GTT only when `abo->tbo.resource != NULL`, `!(adev->flags & AMD_IS_APU)`, and `domain & abo->preferred_domains & AMDGPU_GEM_DOMAIN_VRAM`. Conversely, `amdgpu_bo_validate_size()` tests `GTT` before `VRAM` so `(VRAM | GTT)` BOs validate against `TTM_PL_TT`. [F652, F653, F654]
- `amdgpu_vmid_{mgr_init,reset_all}()` start at `j = 1` (excluding `ids[0]`), and on GCHUB (or MMHUB when `GC_HWIP < 10.0.0`) cap `num_ids` at `first_kfd_vmid`. In `amdgpu_vmid_grab()`, compute `job->gds_switch_needed` via `amdgpu_vmid_gds_switch_needed(id, job)` before overwriting `id->{gds,gws,oa}_{base,size}`. [F616, F617]
- In `amdgpu_job_run()`, if `job->generation != amdgpu_vm_generation(adev, job->vm)` or `job->job_run_counter && job->gang_submit`, mark `finished` `-ECANCELED` and skip `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, while incrementing `job_run_counter` and calling `amdgpu_job_free_resources(job)`. Per-queue resets in `amdgpu_job_timedout()` must increment `adev->gpu_reset_counter`. `struct amdgpu_job` embeds `hw_fence` as `struct amdgpu_fence` for `amdgpu_fence_save_wptr()`. In `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, init `status` with `AMDGPU_HAVE_CTX_SWITCH` and clear it after each `amdgpu_ring_emit_ib()`. [F618, F619, F658, F680]
- In `struct amdgpu_ih_ring`, `rptr`, `wptr`, `ring_size`, and `ptr_mask` are byte offsets into `uint32_t *ring` (use `>> 2` and `le32_to_cpu`/`cpu_to_le32`). When `use_bus_addr` is true in `amdgpu_ih_ring_{init,fini}()`, `dma_{alloc,free}_coherent()` size is `ring_size + 8`. In `amdgpu_ih_process()`, if `ih->overflow` is set, skip `amdgpu_ih_set_rptr()` and `restart_ih`. In `amdgpu_irq_{add_id,dispatch}()`, check `client_id < AMDGPU_IRQ_CLIENTID_MAX` and `src_id < AMDGPU_MAX_IRQ_SRC_ID`. [F639, F641, F642, F659]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_allocate_vm_inv_eng()`, `vm_inv_engs[i]` reserves engine 5 (`enable_mes`) and 3 (`enable_umsch_mm`), and SDMA shared rings copy `vm_inv_eng` from `amdgpu_sdma_get_shared_ring()`. In `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb{,_pasid}()`, direct MMIO and KIQ paths must emit extra `flush_type = 2` and `0` (`flush_tlb_needs_extra_type_{2,0}`) flushes in lockstep, sizing KIQ `ndw` to match. Size PDB0 in `amdgpu_gmc_{pdb0_alloc,init_pdb0}()` for `PAGE_ALIGN((npdes + 1) * 8)`, and stop `gmc->fault_ring` walks in `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults{,_remove}()` when next `fault->timestamp >= tmp`. [F626, F627, F646, F660]
- In `amdgpu_mes_init()`, when `!adev->gfx.disable_kq`, clear kernel queue VMID/HQD bits from `vmid_mask_gfxhub` and `{gfx,compute}_hqd_mask`. In `amdgpu_mes_detect_and_reset_hung_queues()`, zero-clear `hung_queue_db_array_cpu_addr` first and skip `AMDGPU_MES_INVALID_DB_OFFSET`. Convert firmware headers and `ucode_ptr[24]` with `le32_to_cpu()` in `amdgpu_mes_init_microcode()` and `isp_load_fw_by_psp()`. Fill trailing `dev_inst[ip_block][l .. HWIP_MAX_INSTANCE - 1]` with `-1` in `amdgpu_populate_ip_map()`. In `amdgpu_jpeg_dump_ip_state()`, read `reg_list[0]` first and read `j >= 1` only when `(ip_dump[inst_off] & 0x1) != 1`. [F623, F624, F649, F650, F664]

## Synchronization

- Hold `bo->tbo.base.resv` in `amdgpu_bo_{gpu_offset,pin,unpin,kmap,get_tiling_flags}()` (except `gpu_offset` when `pin_count > 0` or `ttm_bo_type_kernel`). In `amdgpu_bo_create()`, unlock `bo->tbo.base.resv` on exit and `fail_unreserve` only when `!bp->resv`. Protect all `amdgpu_gtt_mgr` `mgr->mm` ops with `spin_lock(&mgr->lock)` without sleeping, and run `mmu_interval_set_seq()` and `dma_resv_wait_timeout(..., DMA_RESV_USAGE_BOOKKEEP, ...)` under `adev->notifier_lock` in `amdgpu_hmm_invalidate_gfx()`. [F554, F575, F581, F587]
- In `amdgpu_ih_ring_write()`, write IV dwords before checking `wptr != READ_ONCE(ih->rptr)`, and run `wmb()` before `WRITE_ONCE(*ih->wptr_cpu, cpu_to_le32(wptr))`. IH readers must run `rmb()` after `amdgpu_ih_get_wptr()` before reading `ih->ring`. `amdgpu_irq_{get,put}()` call `amdgpu_irq_update()` only on 0/1 transitions of `enabled_types[type]`, and `amdgpu_irq_update()` re-checks `amdgpu_irq_enabled()` under `adev->irq.lock`. [F570, F576, F577]
- In `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, call `amdgpu_fence_save_wptr(*f)` after all commands, shadows, switch buffers, and wave-limit packets right before `amdgpu_ring_ib_end()` and `amdgpu_ring_commit()`. [F583]
- Direct MMIO/KIQ TLB flushes in `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb{,_pasid}()` and `AMDGPU_INFO_READ_MMR_REG` must use `down_read_trylock(&adev->reset_domain->sem)`. In `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb()`'s SDMA workaround, unlock `gtt_window_lock` after `amdgpu_job_submit()` before `dma_fence_wait()`. In `amdgpu_gmc_flush_gpu_tlb_pasid()` and `amdgpu_gmc_fw_reg_write_reg_wait()`, hold `kiq->ring_lock` across alloc, emission, `amdgpu_fence_emit_polling()`, and `amdgpu_ring_{commit,undo}()`, unlocking before `amdgpu_fence_wait_polling()` and `msleep()`. [F585, F586, F715]
- `amdgpu_mes_{lock,unlock}()` pair `memalloc_noreclaim_{save,restore}()` inside `mes->mutex_hidden` and must not nest BO reservation locks. `amdgpu_jpeg_ring_begin_use()` increments `total_submission_cnt` before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&adev->jpeg.idle_work)`, and `end_use()` decrements it before scheduling `idle_work`. In `amdgpu_i2c_{pre,post}_xfer()`, balance `i2c->mutex` and follow `rec->mask_{clk,data}_reg` writes in `post_xfer` with dummy `RREG32()`s. [F568, F569, F700]
- Protect `vm->fault_info` reads in `amdgpu_info_ioctl()` with `xa_lock_irqsave(&adev->vm_manager.pasids, flags)`. Under `mgpu_info.mutex`, `amdgpu_{register,unregister}_gpu_instance()` keep `num_gpu` and `num_{apu,dgpu}` in lockstep, compacting `gpu_ins[num_gpu - 1]` into slot `i` on removal. [F589, F629]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `amdgpu_bo_create()`, TTM owns `bo` once `ttm_bo_init_reserved()` is called; return immediately if it fails and use `amdgpu_bo_unref(&bo)` on later failures. In `amdgpu_gtt_mgr_new()`, any error after `ttm_resource_init()` must call `ttm_resource_fini(man, &node->base)` before `kfree(node)`. [F553, F571]
- In `amdgpu_hmm_register()`, set `bo->notifier.mm = NULL` if `mmu_interval_notifier_insert()` fails so `amdgpu_hmm_unregister()` skips removal. In `amdgpu_hmm_range_get_pages()`, restore `hmm_range->start = start` and `hmm_range->hmm_pfns = pfns` after the walk loop so `amdgpu_hmm_range_get_pages_done()` `kvfree()`s the base pointer. [F564, F565]
- In `amdgpu_vmid_grab_idle()`, `dma_fence_array_create()` consumes `fences` and the `i` fence refs on success; on failure, put all `i` fences and `kfree(fences)`. In `amdgpu_vmid_grab()`, when `job->vm_needs_flush` is true, `dma_fence_put(id->last_flush)` and set `id->last_flush = NULL` under `id_mgr->lock`. [F566, F567]
- `amdgpu_job_free{,_cb}()` call `dma_fence_put(&job->hw_fence.base)` when `job->hw_fence.base.ops != NULL` and `kfree(job)` only when NULL. `amdgpu_job_set_gang_leader()` must not ref `leader->base.s_fence->scheduled` when `job == leader`, and `amdgpu_job_free()` puts `job->gang_submit` only when `!= &job->base.s_fence->scheduled`. `amdgpu_job_prepare_job()` must not clear `job->vm` after `amdgpu_vmid_grab()`. In `amdgpu_job_submit()`, get `&job->base.s_fence->finished` and call `amdgpu_job_free_resources(job)` after `drm_sched_job_arm()` and before `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`. [F559, F560, F584, F596]
- In `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, any error after `amdgpu_ring_alloc()` must call `amdgpu_ring_undo(ring)` (and `amdgpu_vmid_reset()` if `amdgpu_fence_emit()` fails with `job && job->vmid`). Bracket hardware/MMIO access in `amdgpu_job_timedout()` and `amdgpu_gmc_init_pdb0()` with `drm_dev_enter()`/`drm_dev_exit()`, and in `amdgpu_job_timedout()` init `ti = NULL` and call `amdgpu_vm_put_task_info(ti)` alongside `drm_dev_exit(idx)` after `exit:`. [F561, F562, F705]
- In `amdgpu_driver_open_kms()`, pair `pm_runtime_get_sync()` with `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` on all paths (skipping `mark_last_busy()` if `< 0`). In `amdgpu_driver_postclose_kms()`, capture `fpriv->vm.pasid` and `pd = amdgpu_bo_ref(fpriv->vm.root.bo)` before `amdgpu_vm_fini()`, then call `amdgpu_pasid_free_delayed(pd->tbo.base.resv, pasid)` and `amdgpu_bo_unref(&pd)`. In `amdgpu_driver_unload_kms()`, return after `amdgpu_unregister_gpu_instance()` if `adev->rmmio == NULL`. In `amdgpu_irq_fini_hw()`, call `free_irq()` and `pci_free_irq_vectors()` before `amdgpu_ih_ring_fini()`. [F563, F694, F701, F706]
- Paths reading MCA entries via `amdgpu_mca_smu_get_mca_set()` must merge undispatched entries via `amdgpu_mca_add_mca_set_to_cache()` and still call `amdgpu_mca_bank_set_release(&mca_set)`. Hold `mca_cache->lock` for `mca_caches[type].mca_set` accesses, and use `list_for_each_entry_safe()` in `amdgpu_mca_{dispatch_mca_set,bank_set_release}()`. [F588, F625, F662, F691]

## API Contracts

- `amdgpu_bo_create()` requires `bp->bo_ptr_size >= sizeof(struct amdgpu_bo)` (`>= sizeof(struct amdgpu_bo_vm)` + `entries[]` for `create_vm()`); `bp->xcp_id_plus1` (`0` = any) becomes `bo->xcp_id = bp->xcp_id_plus1 - 1` when `mem_partitions` exists. For `GDS`/`GWS`/`OA`, `amdgpu_bo_create()` shifts `bp->size` left by `PAGE_SHIFT`, so `amdgpu_job_set_resources()` shifts `amdgpu_bo_{gpu_offset,size}()` right by `PAGE_SHIFT`. `amdgpu_bo_placement_from_domain()` sets `places[c].lpfn = range.lpfn + 1`. `amdgpu_bo_{fb_aper_addr,gpu_offset_no_check}()` call `amdgpu_gmc_sign_extend()`, while `amdgpu_{map,unmap}_static_csa()` callers mask `amdgpu_csa_vaddr(adev)` with `& AMDGPU_GMC_HOLE_MASK`. [F605, F606, F637, F670, F678, F682]
- `amdgpu_bo_fault_reserve_notify()` returns `vm_fault_t` (`0`, `VM_FAULT_NOPAGE`, or `VM_FAULT_SIGBUS`), never a negative errno. `amdgpu_bo_get_metadata()` rejects `buffer && buffer_size < ubo->metadata_size` with `-EINVAL`. `amdgpu_bo_{init,fini}()` reserve/free WC memtype/MTRR only when `!xgmi.connected_to_cpu && !is_app_apu`, and `amdgpu_bo_support_uswc()` rejects `CONFIG_X86_32`, `CONFIG_X86 && !CONFIG_X86_PAT`, or `!drm_arch_can_wc_memory()`. [F607, F638, F717]
- Callers allocating a new BO via `amdgpu_bo_create_{reserved,kernel}()` or `isp_kernel_buffer_alloc()` must init `*bo_ptr = NULL`. `amdgpu_bo_create_reserved()` unrefs `*bo_ptr` and returns `0` when `size == 0`, sets `free = true` only when `*bo_ptr == NULL`, and on `error_free` unrefs `bo_ptr` only if `free`. `isp_{user,kernel}_buffer_alloc()` check `is_valid_isp_device()`, and `isp_user_buffer_free()` takes `void *` while `isp_kernel_buffer_free()` takes `void **`. [F599, F685, F704]
- `amdgpu_job_alloc()` leaves `(*job)->num_ibs = 0`, so callers must set `job->num_ibs <= num_ibs`. When `entity == NULL`, `drm_sched_job_init()` is skipped, so `amdgpu_job_{alloc_with_ib,free}()` check `entity`/`job->base.entity` before `drm_sched_job_cleanup()`. `amdgpu_job_submit_direct()` frees `job` only on success; callers must free `job` on error. In `amdgpu_ib_schedule()`, guard nullable `job`, `job->base.s_fence`, and `job->vm`, and require `job->vmid != 0` if `job->vm != NULL`. [F558, F597, F598, F679]
- `amdgpu_vmid_grab{,_idle,_reserved}()` returns `0` both when assigned (`job->vmid != 0`) and when busy (`job->vmid == 0`, `*fence` set). `amdgpu_pasid_alloc()` requires `bits >= 1` and returns a signed `int` (`>= 1` or negative errno); `amdgpu_driver_open_kms()` clamps `< 0` to `pasid = 0` and only calls `amdgpu_pasid_free(pasid)` when `pasid != 0`. `amdgpu_hmm_invalidate_{gfx,hsa}()` check `mmu_notifier_range_blockable(range)` first and return `false` immediately when non-blockable. [F591, F593, F601]
- In `amdgpu_mes_reset_legacy_queue()`, set `queue_input.is_kq = true` (and `legacy_gfx = true` for GFX). `amdgpu_mes_set_shader_debugger()` rejects `flags.process_ctx_flush` with `-EINVAL` (use `amdgpu_mes_flush_shader_debugger()`) and requires `tcp_watch_cntl` of `>= 4` `uint32_t`s; both zero-init `op_input = {0}`. `amdgpu_mes_detect_and_reset_hung_queues()` requires `hung_db_array` of `>= adev->mes.hung_queue_db_array_size` `u32`s. In `amdgpu_mca_smu_get_mca_entry()`, validate `idx` against `mca_funcs->max_{ue,ce}_count`. [F590, F604, F651, F689]

## Error Handling

- `amdgpu_job_prepare_job()` must never return `ERR_PTR` (it calls `dma_fence_set_error(&job->base.s_fence->finished, r)` and returns `NULL`), whereas `amdgpu_job_run()` skips `amdgpu_ib_schedule()` when `finished->error < 0` and returns `ERR_PTR(r)` on failure. In `amdgpu_irq_dispatch()`, zero-init `entry.timestamp{,_src}` before `amdgpu_ih_decode_iv()`. In `amdgpu_driver_load_kms()`, only `amdgpu_device_init()` sets `r` (`amdgpu_acpi_init()` uses non-fatal `acpi_status`). In `amdgpu_driver_open_kms()`, set `file_priv->driver_priv = fpriv` last; `amdgpu_userq_mgr_init()` failure is non-fatal. [F592, F603, F608, F609]

## External Contracts

- In `amdgpu_info_ioctl()`, cap every `copy_to_user()` to `min(size, ...)`, bound `dev_info->cu_bitmap` `memcpy()` by `sizeof(dev_info->cu_bitmap)`, reject `bios_offset >= bios_size` in `AMDGPU_INFO_VBIOS_IMAGE`, and in `AMDGPU_INFO_READ_MMR_REG` validate `se_num`, `sh_num`, and `count <= 128` before `kmalloc_array()` while pairing `down_read_trylock(&adev->reset_domain->sem)` and `amdgpu_gfx_off_ctrl(adev, false)` with `true` and `up_read()` on all paths. [F630, F631, F632, F690, F702]
- In `amdgpu_hw_ip_info()`, reject `ip_instance >= AMDGPU_HW_IP_INSTANCE_MAX_COUNT`, validate `query_hw_ip.type` in `switch` before indexing `amdgpu_ctx_num_entities[]`, and index each IP case's own ring array. In `amdgpu_firmware_info()` and `AMDGPU_INFO_FW_VERSION`, require `ip_instance == 0` and bounds-check `query_fw->index`. In `amdgpu_jpeg_dec_parse_cs()`, require `CP_PACKETJ_GET_RES(ib->ptr[i]) == 0` and `amdgpu_jpeg_reg_valid(reg)`. `amdgpu_get_vblank_counter_kms()` loops reading counter and scanoutpos until counter is unchanged, incrementing `count` when `vpos >= 0`. [F633, F634, F647, F655, F734]

## Known Pitfalls

- When `amdgpu_bo_create_kernel_at()` replaces `(*bo_ptr)->tbo.resource`, call `amdgpu_bo_kunmap()` before `ttm_resource_free()` and `amdgpu_bo_kmap()` after `ttm_bo_mem_space()`. `amdgpu_vmid_grab_{idle,used}()` use `*idle`/`*id` as the list cursor, so reset `*idle = NULL`/`*id = NULL` on loop exhaustion. [F612, F656]
- Compare 48-bit IH timestamps with `amdgpu_ih_ts_after{,_or_equal}(t1, t2)` (shifts left by 16; true when `t2` is after `t1`), and in `amdgpu_gmc_filter_faults()` compute `stamp = max(timestamp, AMDGPU_GMC_FAULT_TIMEOUT + 1) - AMDGPU_GMC_FAULT_TIMEOUT`. Cast 32-bit MB limits and PFNs to `u64` before left-shifting in `amdgpu_gmc_{vram_location,init_sw_mem_ranges}()`. `amdgpu_gmc_get_memory_partition()` `supp_modes` is 0-based (`BIT(mode - 1)` when `mode != 0`), whereas `supported_nps_modes` and `AMDGPU_ALL_NPS_MASK` are 1-based (`BIT(mode)`). [F645, F665, F666, F669, F672]
- In `amdgpu_gmc_fw_reg_write_reg_wait()`, jump to `failed_kiq` without `msleep()` if `in_interrupt()`. In `amdgpu_hdp_generic_flush()`, follow direct MMIO `WREG32()` to `KFD_MMIO_REMAP_HDP_MEM_FLUSH_CNTL` with `nbio.funcs->get_memsize(adev)`. In `amdgpu_jpeg_psp_update_sram()`, cast `dpg_sram_{curr,cpu}_addr` to `uintptr_t` for byte subtraction and reset `dpg_sram_curr_addr` before each sequence; in `amdgpu_jpeg_dec_ring_test_{ring,ib}()`, skip `external.jpeg_pitch` MMIO when `amdgpu_sriov_vf(adev)` is true and follow the initial `WREG32()` in `test_ring` with `RREG32()`. [F688, F698, F699, F707]
