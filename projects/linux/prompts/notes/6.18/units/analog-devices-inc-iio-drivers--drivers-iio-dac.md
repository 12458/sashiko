# ANALOG DEVICES INC IIO DRIVERS — drivers/iio/dac

This directory contains Linux Industrial I/O (IIO) DAC drivers for Analog Devices and Linear Technology chips (AD3552R/AD3542R, AD5064, AD5360, AD5380, AD5421, AD5446, AD5449, AD5504, AD5592R/AD5593R, AD5624R, AD5686/AD5696, AD5755, AD5758, AD5761, AD5764, AD5766, AD5770R, AD5791, AD7303, AD8801).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`, `st->config_d` must match `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_INTERFACE_CONFIG_D`; while `AD3552R_MASK_SPI_CONFIG_DDR` is set, use `st->data->bus_reg_write` instead of `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits` or `ad3552r_hs_reg_read` (no DDR reads). After `ad3552r_hs_reset()`, `ad3552r_hs_setup()` must call `iio_backend_ddr_disable()` and write `AD3552R_MASK_RESET_STATUS` to `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_ERR_STATUS`. [F200, F270]
- In `ad3552r.h` and `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable`, secondary registers (`>= AD3552R_SECONDARY_REGION_START`) stream in descending address order, so streaming both channels (`loop_len = 4`) must start at `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_CH_DAC_16B(1)` (`0x2C`), not channel 0 (`0x2A`). Indices of `ad3552r_ch_ranges` / `ad3542r_ch_ranges` must match both `model_data->ranges_table` in `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset` and register codes in `ad3552r_hs_set_output_range`. [F199, F215]
- When packing a 24-bit BE frame into the low 24 bits of `union { __be32 d32; u8 d8[4]; }` via `cpu_to_be32` / `be32_to_cpu` (`ad5686-spi.c`, `ad5696-i2c.c`, `ad5360.c`, `ad5421.c`, `ad5755.c`, `ad5761.c`, `ad5764.c`, `ad5791.c`), 3-byte transfers must use `&data[i].d8[1]`, not `&data[i].d8[0]`. In `ad5758_spi_reg_read` / `ad5758_spi_reg_write`, 4-byte transfers put command in `[31:24]` and 16-bit payload in `[23:8]`, so readback must shift `be32_to_cpu(st->d32[2]) >> 8` before `& 0xFFFF`. [F223, F224]
- In `ad5064`, `ad5446`, and `ad5686`, `pwr_down_mode` stores 1-based codes (`1..3`, `0` = normal) while IIO enum callbacks (`*_get_powerdown_mode`) use 0-based indices (`mode - 1`), so probe must init `pwr_down_mode` to `1` for all channels. In `ad5686.c`, 2-bit fields in `st->pwr_down_mask` and `st->pwr_down_mode` must be indexed by `chan->channel * 2` (never `chan->address`), and on 16-channel `AD5686_REGMAP` chips, channels `> 0x7` must use powerdown `address = 0x8` and shift `val >> 16`. [F195, F220]
- In `ad5446.c`, `st->cached_val` stores `val << chan->scan_type.shift`; `ad5446_write_raw` must not write hardware while `st->pwr_down` is true, and `ad5446_write_dac_powerdown` must write `st->cached_val` when exiting powerdown. In `ad7303.c`, `ad7303_write` must OR `st->config` into every write, and `ad7303_write_dac_powerdown` must pass `st->dac_cache[chan->channel]` under `st->lock` when changing `AD7303_CFG_POWER_DOWN`. [F202, F203]
- In `ad5592r-base.c` and `ad5592r.c`, `st->gpio_out`, `st->gpio_in`, and `st->gpio_val` are shared between `CH_MODE_GPIO` and `CH_MODE_UNUSED` pins, and `ad5592r_gpio_read` must OR `st->gpio_in` with `AD5592R_GPIO_READBACK_EN` when writing `AD5592R_REG_GPIO_IN_EN`. In `ad5592r_write_raw` (`IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE`), `AD5592R_REG_CTRL` must be read into `st->cached_gp_ctrl`, updated only for `AD5592R_REG_CTRL_DAC_RANGE` or `AD5592R_REG_CTRL_ADC_RANGE`, and written back under `st->lock` (`ad5592r_read_raw` derives scale/temp offset from it). [F205, F206]
- In `ad5592r_alloc_channels`, check child `reg` against `ARRAY_SIZE(st->channel_modes)` before indexing `st->channel_modes` or `st->channel_offstate`, and allocate `channels` with at least `1 + 2 * num_channels` entries (`CH_MODE_DAC_AND_ADC` creates 2 entries plus `IIO_TEMP`). [F217]
- In `ad5064.c`, `st->vref_reg[0].consumer` can be `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)`, so check `!st->use_internal_vref` before accessing `st->vref_reg` in `ad5064_get_vref` or `ad5064_probe`. In `ad5764.c`, `st->vref_reg` is only populated when `st->chip_info->int_vref == 0`, which `ad5764_get_channel_vref`, `ad5764_probe`, and `ad5764_remove` must check first. [F192, F193]
- In `ad5360.c`, `ad5380.c`, and `ad5755.c`, never mutate shared static `chip_info->channel_template` in place; copy it to a per-instance array first. In `ad5755.c`, `st->channels[i].type` (`ad5755_init_channels`) and low 3 mode bits of `st->ctrl[i]` (`ad5755_setup_pdata`) must both come from `pdata->dac[i].mode` because `ad5755_get_min_max` indexes `ad5755_min_max_table` with `st->ctrl[chan->channel] & 7`. [F207, F208]
- In `ad5360_get_channel_vref_index`, clamp `channel / st->chip_info->channels_per_group` to `st->chip_info->num_vrefs - 1` (32/40-channel parts share 2 or 3 VREFs/OFS regs across 4 or 5 groups). In `ad5360_write_raw` / `ad5360_read_raw`, `AD5360_REG_SF_OFS` is always 14-bit, so shift negated `IIO_CHAN_INFO_OFFSET` by `(chan->scan_type.realbits - 14)`. [F225, F226]
- Hardware sequencing: in `ad5755_set_channel_pwr_down()`, enabling a channel under `st->lock` must set `AD5755_DAC_INT_EN | AD5755_DAC_DC_DC_EN` via `ad5755_update_dac_ctrl()`, `udelay(200)`, then set `AD5755_DAC_OUT_EN`. In `ad5758_init`, call `ad5758_crc_disable` again after `ad5758_reset` (reset re-enables CRC), and in `ad5758_set_dc_dc_conv_mode`, set `AD5758_ADC_CONFIG_PPC_BUF_EN(1)` before setting `AD5758_DCDC_MODE_PPC_CURRENT` in `AD5758_DCDC_CONFIG1`. In `ad5766_default_setup()` and `ad5761_spi_set_range()`, reset the chip before writing `AD5766_CMD_SPAN_REG` or `AD5761_ADDR_CTRL_WRITE_REG`. [F213, F274, F278]
- In `ad5758.c`, keep `ad5758_dc_dc_ilim` and `ad5758_sr_step` in ascending order for `bsearch()` and `ad5758_find_closest_match()`, and in `ad5758_parse_dt()`, `tmp`, `ad5758_dc_dc_ilim`, `index`, and `cmpfunc` must all use `int` so `bsearch()` stride and `index - ad5758_dc_dc_ilim` match. [F228, F260]
- In `ad5766.c`, 32-bit `st->dither_source` and `st->dither_scale` must split across two 16-bit SPI writes using `FIELD_GET(AD5766_LOWER_WORD_SPI_MASK, ...)` (ch 0..7) and `FIELD_GET(AD5766_UPPER_WORD_SPI_MASK, ...)` (ch 8..15). In `ad5766_trigger_handler()`, bit `ch` from `for_each_set_bit()` is the DAC address in `AD5766_CMD_WR_IN_REG(ch)` while `i++` indexes `u16 data[ARRAY_SIZE(ad5766_channels)]` from `iio_pop_from_buffer()`, requiring all `ad5766_chip_infos` to have `num_channels <= 16` and `storagebits == 16`. [F227, F246, F265]
- In `ad5770r.c`, update `st->ch_pwr_down[chan->channel]` in lockstep with `AD5770R_CFG_SHUTDOWN_B` (`AD5770R_CHANNEL_CONFIG`), `AD5770R_CH_SET` (`AD5770R_CH_ENABLE`), and on ch 0 when `out_range_mode > AD5770R_CH0_0_300`, `AD5770R_CFG_CH0_SINK_EN` (`BIT(7)`). Keep `ad5770r_filter_freqs[]` and `ad5770r_filter_reg_vals[]` 1-to-1 and check `i < ARRAY_SIZE(...)` in `ad5770r_get_filter_freq()` / `ad5770r_set_filter_freq()`. [F210, F247]
- In `ad5421_fault_handler`, rewrite `st->ctrl` via `ad5421_update_ctrl(indio_dev, 0, 0)` on fault IRQ and only push IIO events for `(old_fault ^ fault) & fault`. In `ad5791.c`, `st->ctrl` caches `AD5791_ADDR_CTRL` while `st->pwr_down` tracks `AD5791_CTRL_OPGND` / `AD5791_CTRL_DACTRI`, and `ad5791_buffer_preenable` must return `-EINVAL` when `st->pwr_down` is true. [F209, F214]

## Synchronization
- Functions in `ad5592r-base.c` and `ad3552r-hs.c` using `guard(mutex)` or `scoped_guard(mutex, ...)` on `st->lock` or `st->gpio_lock` must never call `mutex_unlock()` explicitly. [F159]
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`, `ad3552r_hs_write_raw()` must wrap register accesses with `iio_device_claim_direct()` / `iio_device_release_direct()` (streaming uses DSPI/QSPI DDR). Init `st->lock` via `devm_mutex_init()` before `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init()`, and hold `st->lock` in `ad3552r_hs_show_data_source` and `ad3552r_hs_write_data_source`. [F172, F173]
- In `ad5592r-base.c`, `gpio_chip` callbacks (`ad5592r_gpio_get`, `ad5592r_gpio_set`, `ad5592r_gpio_direction_input`, `ad5592r_gpio_direction_output`) must hold `st->gpio_lock` across `st->gpio_out`, `st->gpio_in`, `st->gpio_val` and GPIO reg ops; `ad5592r_read_raw` and `ad5592r_write_raw` must hold `st->lock` across `st->ops` calls and `st->cached_dac` / `st->cached_gp_ctrl` accesses. [F176, F177]
- Runtime callbacks must hold `st->lock` across state updates and bus transfers: in `ad5064.c` across `st->pwr_down_mode`, `st->pwr_down`, `st->dac_cache`, `ad5064_write()`, and `ad5064_sync_powerdown_mode()`; in `ad5446.c` across `st->pwr_down`, `st->cached_val`, and `st->chip_info->write()`; in `ad5758.c` across `ad5758_spi_reg_read()`, `ad5758_spi_reg_write()`, and `ad5758_spi_write_mask()`; and in `ad5360.c`, `ad5421.c`, and `ad5755.c` across `ad5360_write_unlocked`, `ad5421_write_unlocked`, `ad5755_write_unlocked`, `ad5755_write_ctrl_unlocked`, and `ad5755_update_dac_ctrl`. [F178, F182, F184, F290]
- `ad5421_fault_handler` must only be registered as `thread_fn` in `devm_request_threaded_irq` with `IRQF_ONESHOT` (acquires `st->lock`, does SPI I/O, calls `msleep(1000)`). `ad5766_trigger_handler` runs in threaded poll context, must hold `st->lock` around `__ad5766_spi_write`, and must jump to `done` on all paths so `iio_trigger_notify_done(indio_dev->trig)` is always called. [F167, F287, F288]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `ad3552r_hs_probe()`, call `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init(indio_dev)` after `devm_iio_device_register()` (`iio_get_debugfs_dentry` is `NULL` before). In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_predisable()`, switch host bus to `AD3552R_IO_MODE_SPI` and disable DDR on both target (direct write of `st->config_d`) and backend (`iio_backend_ddr_disable()`) before calling `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits()`. [F162, F297]
- In `ad5592r_probe()`, run `ad5592r_set_channel_modes()` before `ad5592r_gpio_init()` (populates `st->gpio_map`, `st->gpio_in`, `st->gpio_out`, `st->gpio_val`). In `ad5592r_alloc_channels()`, never `break` or `return` from `device_for_each_child_node(st->dev, child)` without `fwnode_handle_put(child)`. [F174, F234]
- In drivers using non-devres `iio_device_register()` (`ad5360`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r`, `ad5764`), remove and probe unwind must call `iio_device_unregister()` before disabling regulators, freeing `indio_dev->channels`, `ad5592r_gpio_cleanup()`, or `ad5592r_reset_channel_modes()`. With `devm_iio_device_register()` (`ad5064_probe()`, `ad7303_probe()`), register `devm_add_action_or_reset()` for `ad5064_bulk_reg_disable()` / `ad7303_reg_disable()` before `devm_iio_device_register()`. [F165, F279, F280]
- In dual-bus modules (`ad5064`, `ad5380`, `ad5446`), `module_init` registers SPI before I2C (and must unregister SPI if I2C fails), while `module_exit` unregisters I2C before SPI. In `ad5755_parse_fw()`, call `devm_kfree(dev, pdata)` before returning `NULL` after `devm_kzalloc()`. In `ad5791_offload_setup()`, `spi_message` and `spi_transfer` passed to `spi_message_init_with_transfers()` / `devm_spi_optimize_message()` must live in `struct ad5791_state`, not on the stack. [F170, F171, F263]

## API Contracts
- Read helpers multiplexing data and errno (`ad5360_read`, `ad5421_read`, `ad5504_spi_read`, `ad5686_spi_read`, `ad5686_i2c_read`, `ad5755_read`, `ad5758_spi_reg_read`) require `ret < 0` checks, not `if (ret)` (and in `ad5758_wait_for_task_complete()`, check `ret < 0` before `!(ret & mask)`). I2C write callbacks (`ad5064_i2c_write`, `ad5622_write`, `ad5686_i2c_write`) must normalize positive byte counts from `i2c_master_send` to `0` (`-EIO` on short write). [F188, F189, F242]
- In `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits(st, reg, mask, val, xfer_size)`, `val` is ORed directly into `(rval & ~mask)`, so callers must pre-shift `val` to `mask`. In `ad3552r_hs_reg_access`, bounds-check `reg` against `st->model_data->max_reg_addr` and pass `xfer_size = 1` to `ad3552r_hs_reg_read`. In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable()`, configure backend address/format/stream only after all DAC writes and I/O mode transitions, and on error reset `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_TRANSFER_REGISTER` to `AD3552R_SPI` before `bus_set_io_mode()`. [F196, F216, F232]
- `ad3552r_get_output_range` returns `-ENOENT` when `"adi,output-range-microvolt"` is absent; `ad3552r_hs_setup` and `ad3552r_configure_device` must only fall back to `ad3552r_get_custom_gain` on `-ENOENT`. Callers of `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset` must set `ch_data->range_override = 1` with `p`, `n`, `rfb`, `gain_offset` for custom gain or set `ch_data->range` with `range_override = 0`; `ad3552r_hs_read_raw` must return `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_MICRO` for scale/offset. [F190, F201, F294]
- In `ad5592r_read_adc()`, write `BIT(chan)` to `AD5592R_REG_ADC_SEQ` followed by two `ad5592r_spi_wnop_r16()` transfers. In `ad5593r_read_word()`, use `i2c_smbus_write_byte()` then `i2c_master_recv()` and `get_unaligned_be16()`, never `i2c_smbus_read_word_swapped()`. [F271, F283]
- In `ad5592r_gpio_init()`, set `st->gpiochip.can_sleep = true` and reject offsets not in `st->gpio_map` with `-ENODEV` in `ad5592r_gpio_request`. In `.debugfs_reg_access` (`ad3552r_hs_reg_access`, `ad5758_reg_access`, `ad5770r_reg_access`), branch on `if (readval)`. Callers of `ad5758_slew_rate_config()` must check `st->slew_time != 0` before calling it (`do_div(sr_step, st->slew_time)`). [F253, F301, F302]
- In dual-bus drivers (`ad5064`, `ad5446`, `ad5592r`/`ad5593r`, `ad5686`/`ad5696`), shared core code must never downcast `st->dev` to `spi_device` or `i2c_client`. In `ad5360`, `ad5421`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r-base`, `ad5755`, `ad5758`, `ad5764`, and `ad5770r`, bus drvdata stores `struct iio_dev *` (use `iio_priv()`), while IIO `device_attribute` callbacks in `ad5360` must use `dev_to_iio_dev(dev)`. [F261, F262]

## Error Handling
- Store return values of `ad5360_write_unlocked` and `ad5421_write_unlocked` (e.g., in `ad5360_update_ctrl`, `ad5421_update_ctrl`) in signed `int`, not `unsigned int`. In `ad5592r_probe` and `ad7303_probe`, reset `st->reg` or `st->vref_reg` to `NULL` when `devm_regulator_get_optional` returns `-ENODEV`. In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable` and `ad5592r_probe`, error unwind labels must not overwrite `ret`. [F187, F191, F194]

## External Contracts
- In `ad5592r_read_raw`, verify raw ADC bits `[14:12]` equal `(chan->channel & 0x7)` before masking with `GENMASK(11, 0)`. Both `ad5592r_read_raw` and `ad5592r_write_raw_get_fmt` must return `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_NANO` for `IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE` to match `st->scale_avail`. [F218, F219, F292]
- In `ad5624r_probe`, fall back to `"vcc"` when `"vref"` regulator returns `-ENODEV`. In `ad3552r_hs_write_data_source`, pass `sizeof(buf) - 1` to `simple_write_to_buffer`, NUL-terminate, and return `count`. [F229, F298]

## Known Pitfalls
- Where `chan->address != chan->channel` (`ad5064`, `ad5360`, `ad5504`, `ad5686`, `ad5770r`), pass `chan->address` to hardware helpers and `chan->channel` to index state arrays (`st->dac_cache`, `st->pwr_down`, `st->ch_pwr_down`). [F197]
- 64-bit math rules: in `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset()`, cast `rem` to `s64` before `* 1000000` and use `DIV_ROUND_CLOSEST()` / `div_s64()`; in `ad3552r_get_custom_range()`, keep `common`, `gn`, `gp`, `offset`, `tmp` as `s64` and use `div_s64()`; in `ad5592r_init_scales()` and `ad5592r_read_raw()` (`IIO_TEMP`), widen `vref_mV` to `s64` before multiplying and use `div_s64_rem(..., 1000000000LL)`; in `ad5791_read_raw()` (`IIO_CHAN_INFO_OFFSET`), cast `st->vref_neg_mv` to `u64` before `<< realbits` and use `do_div()`; in `ad5758_slew_rate_config()`, keep `AD5758_FULL_SCALE_MICRO` in `u64` (`sr_step`, `calc_slew_time`) and divide in two sequential `do_div()` steps. [F248, F249, F250, F251, F252]
- Polarity/sign rules: in `ad5504.c`, `1 << chan->channel` in `st->pwr_down_mask` means powered UP, and every `AD5504_ADDR_CTRL` write requires a following `AD5504_ADDR_NOOP` write; in `ad5766.c`, `st->dither_enable` is active-low (`0` = enabled) and `st->dither_source` stores 1-hot 2-bit fields requiring `- 1` after shifting in `ad5766_get_dither_source()`; in `ad5764_read_raw()`, apply `sign_extend32(*val, 7)` for `AD5764_REG_OFFSET` and `sign_extend32(*val, 5)` for `AD5764_REG_FINE_GAIN`. In `ad3552r_get_custom_gain()`, read custom gain properties from `gain_child` (`"custom-output-range-config"`), not `child`. [F233, F241, F245, F255]
