# FILESYSTEMS (VFS and infrastructure) — fs (part 4)

Implements mount namespaces, mount trees, and shared-subtree propagation (`fs/namespace.c`, `fs/pnode.c`, `fs/pnode.h`, `fs/proc_namespace.c`), file opening/closing/truncation (`fs/open.c`), anonymous and FIFO pipes (`fs/pipe.c`), POSIX ACLs (`fs/posix_acl.c`), and `nsfs`/`pidfs` (`fs/nsfs.c`, `fs/pidfs.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `mnt->mnt_ns` can be `NULL`, `MNT_NS_INTERNAL` (`ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)`), or a `struct mnt_namespace *`; always check `is_mounted()` (`!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(mnt->mnt_ns)`) rather than `if (mnt->mnt_ns)`. [F238]
- In `attach_recursive_mnt()`, for each `child` in `tree_list`, look up any existing mount `q` at `child->mnt_mountpoint` with `__lookup_mnt()`, call `commit_tree(child)`, and run `lock_mnt_tree(child)` before `mnt_change_mountpoint()` reparents `q` onto `topmost_overmount(child)` so `q` does not get `MNT_LOCKED`. Cross-`user_namespace` propagation/copy (`attach_recursive_mnt()`, `copy_mnt_ns()`) must call `lock_mnt_tree()`. [F252, F285, F349]
- `make_visible()` and `__umount_mnt()` must update `parent->overmount` when `mnt_mountpoint == parent->mnt.mnt_root`. Moving an attached mount (`attach_recursive_mnt()`, `pivot_root()`) requires `list_del_init(&mnt->mnt_expire)`, and `pivot_root()` must transfer `MNT_LOCKED` from `root_mnt` to `new_mnt`. [F251, F259]
- In `propagate_umount()`, call `reparent(m->overmount)` only when `!will_be_unmounted(over)`. In pass 1 (`trim_one()`), `trim_ancestors()` only clears `T_UMOUNT_CANDIDATE` without unlinking from `candidates`; pass 2 (`handle_locked()`) unlinks ancestor chains and drains `candidates` with `while (!list_empty(&candidates))`. `propagate_mnt()`, `bulk_make_private()`, and `propagate_umount()` must clear `T_MARKED` and `T_UMOUNT_CANDIDATE` in `mnt->mnt_t_flags` before returning. [F250, F257, F287]
- When adding a peer to an `IS_MNT_SLAVE` member, `clone_mnt()`/`do_set_group()` must insert `mnt_slave` via `hlist_add_behind()` (not `hlist_add_head()`). `propagation_next()`/`next_group()` skip `mnt_slave_list` only on `IS_MNT_NEW(m)`; anonymous namespaces (`is_anon_ns(m->mnt_ns)`) are skipped only as secondary targets in `need_secondary()`. [F253, F288]
- `count_mounts()` stages counts in `ns->pending_mounts` (subtracting `ns->nr_mounts` and `ns->pending_mounts` from `max = READ_ONCE(sysctl_mount_max)` step by step to avoid wraparound), which `commit_tree()` folds into `ns->nr_mounts` and zeroes, or `attach_recursive_mnt()` rollback zeroes. During unmount, `move_from_ns()` keeps `mnt->mnt_ns` non-NULL across `propagate_umount()`; only `umount_tree()`'s final loop decrements `ns->nr_mounts` and sets `p->mnt_ns = NULL`. [F254, F256, F310]
- `skip_mnt_tree(p)` returns the last descendant of `p`: `for (...; p = next_mnt(p, root))` loops (`copy_tree()`) must do `p = skip_mnt_tree(p); continue;`, whereas `while` loops (`copy_mnt_ns()`) must call `next_mnt(skip_mnt_tree(p), root)`. In `copy_mnt_ns()` pass 2, if `q` is non-NULL, advance `p` with `while (p->mnt.mnt_root != q->mnt.mnt_root) p = next_mnt(skip_mnt_tree(p), old)`. [F290, F291]
- In `do_move_mount()` and `clone_private_mount()`, test `check_mnt()`: require `anon_ns_root()` only when `!check_mnt()`, and `mnt_has_parent()`/`!IS_MNT_LOCKED()` only when moving a mount in the caller's namespace. [F292]
- `pipe->ring_size` must be a power of two `<= (pipe_index_t)-1u` (`-EINVAL` in `pipe_resize_ring()`), and `round_pipe_size()` must reject `size > (1U << 31)` before `roundup_pow_of_two(size)` and clamp `< PAGE_SIZE` to `PAGE_SIZE`. Compute occupancy/emptiness/fullness via `(pipe_index_t)(head - tail)` (`pipe_occupancy()`, `pipe_empty()`, `pipe_full()`). [F278, F304, F305, F306]
- `posix_acl_valid()` enforces strict tag order (`ACL_USER_OBJ` .. `ACL_OTHER`), relied on by `posix_acl_permission()`. Cached ACLs in `inode->i_acl`/`i_default_acl` store `kuid_t`/`kgid_t` in `s_user_ns` (never `mnt_idmap` IDs) and are shared, so `__posix_acl_create()`, `__posix_acl_chmod()`, and `posix_acl_create()` must `posix_acl_clone()` before mutating in place. [F266, F301, F360]
- In `pidfs_add_pid()`, `pidfs_ino_nr` starts at 2 and advances by 2 if `pidfs_ino(pidfs_ino_nr)` (`lower_32_bits`) wraps to 0 on 32-bit; `pidfs_ino_cmp()`, `pidfs_ino_find()`, `pidfs_encode_fh()`, and `pidfs_fh_to_dentry()` must use the full `u64` `pid->ino`, not `unsigned long` or `inode->i_ino`. [F276, F308]

## Synchronization

- Unmounting (`umount_tree()`, `umount_mnt()`) or setting `emptied_ns` requires `namespace_lock()`/`namespace_unlock()` (`guard(namespace_excl)`), never raw `down_write()`/`up_write(&namespace_sem)`. Callers holding `namespace_sem` (`do_statmount()`, `do_listmount()`) must defer `path_put()` until after `namespace_sem` is dropped. [F193, F325]
- Lock nesting: `inode_lock(dentry->d_inode)` -> `namespace_lock()` -> `mount_lock`. In `do_lock_mount()`, evaluate `where_to_mount()` and pin `m->mnt`/`dentry` under `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)` before taking `inode_lock` and `namespace_lock`, then revalidate under `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)`. `__unlock_mount()` must call `inode_unlock()` and `unpin_mountpoint()` (under `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)`) before `namespace_unlock()`. [F213, F214]
- Walking `mnt->mnt_mounts` or `mnt->mnt_parent` requires `mount_lock` (`read_seqlock_excl`/`write_seqlock`) or `namespace_sem` while pinned. Propagation fields (`mnt_share`, `mnt_slave_list`, `mnt_slave`, `mnt_master`, `mnt_group_id`, `mnt_t_flags`) require exclusive `namespace_lock()` taken before `lock_mount_hash()`. [F216, F218]
- `__lookup_mnt()` callers must hold `mount_lock` (`read_seqlock_excl`/`write_seqlock`) or `rcu_read_lock()` with `read_seqbegin(&mount_lock)` and retry/fallback to `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)` on `read_seqretry()`. `path_overmounted()` requires `namespace_sem` and must never be called under `lock_mount_hash()`. [F211, F327]
- `mnt_get_write_access()`/`mnt_put_write_access()` disable preemption around `mnt_writers` and `WRITE_HOLD`. In `mnt_get_write_access()`, after `mnt_inc_writers()`, execute `smp_mb()` before spinning on `__test_write_hold(READ_ONCE(mnt->mnt_pprev_for_sb))` (on `CONFIG_PREEMPT_RT`, re-enable preemption and cycle `read_seqlock_excl(&mount_lock)`), and `smp_rmb()` after the loop before `mnt_is_readonly()`. Under `mount_lock`, `mnt_hold_writers()` needs `smp_mb()` after `set_write_hold(mnt)` before `mnt_get_writers(mnt)`, and `mnt_unhold_writers()` needs `smp_wmb()` before `clear_write_hold(mnt)`. [F208, F209, F328]
- After `lock_mount_hash()`, `mntput_no_expire()` and synchronous `do_umount()` must execute `smp_mb()` before `mnt_get_count()` or `propagate_mount_busy()` (pairing with `__legitimize_mnt()`). In `do_idmap_mount()`, modifying `mnt->mnt_idmap` requires `is_anon_ns(mnt->mnt_ns)`, `mnt_hold_writers()`, `lock_mount_hash()`, `OPEN_TREE_CLONE` if `MOUNT_KATTR_IDMAP_REPLACE`, and `smp_store_release()`. [F210, F217]
- Outside `pipe->mutex`, read `pipe->head`/`tail` via `READ_ONCE(pipe->head_tail)`. In `pipe_poll()`, `WRITE_ONCE(pipe->poll_usage, true)` and `poll_wait()` must precede `READ_ONCE(pipe->head_tail)`. For `watch_queue`, `anon_pipe_read()` uses `smp_load_acquire(&pipe->head)`, and `pipe_update_tail()` (if `pipe_has_watch_queue(pipe)`) and `pipe_resize_ring()` (linearizing slots to 0 and setting `tail = 0, head = n`) must hold `spin_lock_irq(&pipe->rd_wait.lock)`. [F220, F221, F222, F263]
- `pipe_double_lock()` locks distinct pipes in ascending address order (`pipe1 < pipe2`); `anon_pipe_write()` rejects `watch_queue` pipes before locking `pipe->mutex`. After `wait_event_interruptible_exclusive()`, `anon_pipe_read()`/`anon_pipe_write()` must set `wake_next_reader`/`wake_next_writer` (clearing under `pipe->mutex` only if empty/full) and `anon_pipe_write()` must refresh `was_empty = pipe_is_empty(pipe)` under `pipe->mutex`. [F223, F299]
- `inode->i_lock` (outside `pipe->mutex`) protects `inode->i_pipe` and `pipe->files`: in `fifo_open()`, call `alloc_pipe_info()` outside `i_lock` and recheck `inode->i_pipe` after relocking; in `put_pipe_info()`, call `free_pipe_info()` only after dropping `i_lock` when `--pipe->files == 0`. [F224, F338]
- In `__get_acl()`, install `uncached_acl_sentinel(current)` via `cmpxchg(p, ACL_NOT_CACHED, sentinel)` before `get_acl`, revert to `ACL_NOT_CACHED` on error, and `posix_acl_dup(acl)` before `try_cmpxchg(p, &sentinel, acl)` (`posix_acl_release(acl)` on failure). `get_cached_acl()` uses `refcount_inc_not_zero(&acl->a_refcount)` under `rcu_read_lock()`, and `get_cached_acl_rcu()` passes `LOOKUP_RCU` when `ACL_DONT_CACHE`. [F207, F225, F341]
- `pidfs_register_pid()`/`pidfs_exit()` synchronize `pid->attr` under `spin_lock_irq(&pid->wait_pidfd.lock)`: `pidfs_exit()` sets `PIDFS_PID_DEAD` or populates `attr->__pei` and `smp_store_release(&attr->exit_info, &attr->__pei)`, while `pidfs_free_pid()` checks `!IS_ERR(attr)`. `pidfs_xattr_set()` requires `inode_is_locked(inode)`, `simple_xattrs_init()`, and `smp_store_release(&pid->attr->xattrs, xattrs)`. `pidfs_ino_tree` updates require `write_seqcount_begin`/`end(&pidmap_lock_seq)`, and `pidfs_ino_get_pid()` uses `rcu_read_lock()`, `rb_find_rcu()`, and `read_seqcount_begin`/`retry(&pidmap_lock_seq, seq)`. [F202, F226, F228, F340]

## Object Lifetimes

- `open_namespace()` always consumes `struct ns_common *ns` via `path_from_stashed()`. `mount_subtree(m, name)` always consumes `m`, returning the dentry with `s->s_active` incremented and `s->s_umount` write-locked. [F188, F194]
- `lookup_mnt_ns()`/`grab_requested_mnt_ns()` increment `mnt_ns->passive`; release with `mnt_ns_release()` (never `put_mnt_ns()`) and hold `namespace_sem` while accessing `ns->mounts`. `mnt_ns_tree_remove()` defers releasing the initial passive ref via `call_rcu(&ns->ns.ns_rcu, mnt_ns_release_rcu)`. [F199, F333]
- Clearing `mnt->mnt_ns = NULL` (`mnt_make_shortterm()`, `umount_tree()`) requires an RCU grace period before final `mntput()`. In `mntput_no_expire()`, non-`MNT_INTERNAL` `cleanup_mnt()` is deferred to process context (`task_work_add(..., TWA_RESUME)` or `delayed_mntput_work`), and `cleanup_mnt()` frees `struct mount` via `call_rcu(&mnt->mnt_rcu, delayed_free_vfsmnt)`. [F215, F334]
- In `statmount()`'s `retry:` loop, every iteration must call `kvfree(ks->seq.buf)` and `path_put(&ks->root)`. Any local `struct pinned_mountpoint` passed to `lookup_mountpoint()`, `get_mountpoint()`, or `do_lock_mount()` must be released with `unpin_mountpoint()`. [F200, F324]
- `vfs_truncate()` takes `mnt_want_write(path->mnt)` then `get_write_access(inode)`, calls `break_lease()`, and drops `put_write_access(inode)` before `mnt_drop_write(path->mnt)`. `finish_open()` does not consume `dentry`; `finish_no_open()` consumes `dentry` when `!IS_ERR(dentry)`. In `pidfd_info()`/`pidfd_ioctl()`, `get_pid_task()` uses `__free(put_task)` and `get_task_cred(task)` requires `put_cred(c)`. [F191, F203, F355]
- `alloc_pipe_info()`, `pipe_set_size()`, and `free_pipe_info()` charge/uncharge `pipe->user->pipe_bufs` (never `current_user()`) via `account_pipe_buffers()`, tracking `pipe->nr_accounted` (unless `pipe_has_watch_queue(pipe)`), reverting on error, and uncharging before `free_uid(pipe->user)`. Any path dropping `pipe->mutex` must re-read `pipe->head`, `pipe->tail`, and `pipe_buf()` after relocking. In `create_pipe_files()` (`pipe->files = 2`), failure before `alloc_file_pseudo()` needs `free_pipe_info()` + `iput(inode)`; `alloc_file_clone()` failure needs `put_pipe_info()` + `fput(f)`. [F204, F205, F321, F358]

## API Contracts

- `mnt_want_write[_file]()` take `sb_start_write()` and pair with `mnt_drop_write[_file]()`; `mnt_get_write_access[_file]()` do not and pair with `mnt_put_write_access[_file]()`. Never mix `_file` and non-`_file` variants. Pair `collect_paths()` with `drop_collected_paths(paths, prealloc)`. Never `fd_install()` a `kernel_file_open()` file. [F197, F201, F347, F356]
- `wants_mount_setattr()` returns `< 0`, `0` (no-op), or `1` (call `do_mount_setattr()`/`finish_mount_kattr()` only when `> 0`). `__legitimize_mnt()` returns `0`, `1` (no ref), or `-1` (`legitimize_mnt()` must drop `rcu_read_lock()` and `mntput()`). `do_lock_mount()`/`lock_mount_exact()` set `res->parent = ERR_PTR(err)` on failure; check `IS_ERR(mp.parent)`. [F229, F239, F240]
- `do_change_type()`/`do_set_group()` require `may_change_propagation()` under `namespace_lock()`. `clone_private_mount()` requires `check_mnt(old_mnt)` or a loop-free `anon_ns_root()`, `ns_capable(old_mnt->mnt_ns->user_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`, and no locked children. `can_idmap_mount()` requires `is_anon_ns(mnt->mnt_ns)`, `FS_ALLOW_IDMAP` without `SB_I_NOIDMAP`, `mnt_userns` neither `&init_user_ns` nor `s_user_ns`, and `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` in both. [F343, F344, F350]
- Verify fops/dops before downcasting: `get_pipe_info()` checks `pipefifo_fops`/`pipeanon_fops` (and `!pipe_has_watch_queue()` if `for_splice`); `pidfd_pid()` checks `pidfs_file_operations`; `nsfs` checks `proc_ns_file(file)` or `ns_dentry_operations` and reads `struct ns_common *` from `d_inode(dentry)->i_private` (never `dentry->d_fsdata`) plus `ns->ns_type`/`ns->ops`. [F246, F249, F262]
- `__posix_acl_create()`/`__posix_acl_chmod()` always consume `*acl`; `set_cached_acl()`/`vfs_set_acl()` do not. `posix_acl_create()` must zero `*acl` and `*default_acl` before any early return, and `posix_acl_create()`/`posix_acl_from_xattr()` must use `GFP_NOFS`. `posix_acl_equiv_mode()`/`posix_acl_create_masq()` return `< 0`, `0` (mode-equivalent), or `1` (extended ACL); `->set_acl()` for `ACL_TYPE_ACCESS` must call `posix_acl_update_mode()` to strip `S_ISGID`. [F206, F233, F235, F342, F361]

## Error Handling

- `mnt_hold_writers(m)` leaves `WRITE_HOLD` set on `-EBUSY`, so `mount_setattr_prepare()` must advance `m = next_mnt(m, mnt)` before breaking so rollback calls `mnt_unhold_writers(m)`. In `anon_pipe_read()`/`anon_pipe_write()`, once `ret > 0`, loop errors must only overwrite `ret` when `!ret`. [F241, F245]

## External Contracts

- `close()` maps `-ERESTART*` from `filp_flush()` to `-EINTR` after `file_close_fd()`. `build_open_flags()` (`openat2`) validates `u64 how->flags` against `VALID_OPEN_FLAGS` before narrowing to `int` and rejects invalid flag/mode combinations with `-EINVAL`. `vfs_fallocate()` rejects `offset < 0 || len <= 0` and checks `check_add_overflow(offset, len, &sum)` against `s_maxbytes`. [F243, F280, F313, F314]
- In `statmount`/`listmount`, `s->sm.mask` flags must be in `STATMOUNT_SUPPORTED` (and `STATMOUNT_STRING_REQ` for strings), `security_sb_show_options()` is called unconditionally, non-zero mount IDs `<= MNT_UNIQUE_ID_OFFSET` (`1ULL << 31`) are rejected, and foreign/unreachable mounts require `ns_capable_noaudit(ns->user_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`. [F272, F297, F309, F351]
- `posix_acl_to_xattr()`/`vfs_posix_acl_to_xattr()` set `ext_entry->e_id = cpu_to_le32(ACL_UNDEFINED_ID)` for non-`ACL_USER`/`ACL_GROUP` tags. `nsfs_fh_to_dentry()` checks `memchr_inv()` on trailing bytes and `ns_capable(owning_ns, CAP_SYS_ADMIN)`. `pidfd_info()` checks `pid_in_current_pidns()`, munges creds, and allows `kinfo.ppid == 0`; `pidfd_ioctl()` checks `ptrace_may_access(..., PTRACE_MODE_READ_FSCREDS)`. [F236, F300, F353, F354]

## Known Pitfalls

- Initialize `newattrs` inside `retry_deleg:` in `chown_common()`, but call `vfs_set_acl_idmapped_mnt()` once before `retry_deleg:` in `vfs_set_acl()`. In `do_ftruncate()`, check `IS_APPEND(file_inode(file))`, not `IS_APPEND(inode)`. [F244, F267, F302]
- In `statmount_string()`, reserve byte 0 as NUL and check `check_add_overflow(sizeof(*sm), seq->count, &kbufsize)` and `kbufsize >= s->bufsize`; `retry_statmount()` checks `check_mul_overflow(*seq_size, 2, seq_size)` and `MAX_RW_COUNT`; `statmount_*` option helpers check `seq_has_overflowed(seq)` before in-place edits and `++count == INT_MAX` (`-EOVERFLOW`). [F260, F271, F311, F312]
- In `propagate_mnt()`, check `if (!need_secondary(n, dest_mp)) continue;` before testing `type & CL_SLAVE`. In `mark_mounts_for_expiry()`/`shrink_submounts()`, drain `graveyard` with `while (!list_empty(&graveyard))` calling `umount_tree()`, never `list_for_each_entry_safe()`. In `move_mount()`, consume `uflags`/`lflags` for `to_path` before resetting and recomputing for `from_path`. [F289, F293, F298]
