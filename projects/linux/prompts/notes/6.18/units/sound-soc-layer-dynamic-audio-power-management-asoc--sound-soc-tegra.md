# SOUND - SOC LAYER / DYNAMIC AUDIO POWER MANAGEMENT (ASoC) — sound/soc/tegra

`sound/soc/tegra` implements NVIDIA Tegra30 and Tegra210/186/234/264 Audio Hub (AHUB) crossbar and audio module drivers (I2S, DMIC, AMX, MIXER, MVC, SFC, and OPE with PEQ/MBDRC), exposing DAPM routing muxes, coefficient RAM programming, and DAI interfaces.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Sequential RAM helpers (`tegra210_peq_read_ram`, `tegra210_peq_write_ram`, `tegra210_mbdrc_write_ram`, `tegra210_amx_write_map_ram`, `tegra210_sfc_write_ram`) write `*_ADDR_INIT_EN | *_SEQ_ACCESS_EN` to `*_RAM_CTRL` once and loop over fixed `*_RAM_DATA`, which must be `.volatile_reg` in `regmap_config`; readable FIFO and RAM data registers (`CHANNEL_TXFIFO`, `CHANNEL_RXFIFO`, `TEGRA210_MIXER_*_RAM_DATA`, `TEGRA210_PEQ_CFG_RAM_*DATA`, `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_RAM_DATA`, `TEGRA210_SFC_CFG_RAM_DATA`) must also be `.precious_reg`. [F330, F361]
- In `tegra210_ahub.c`, `tegra_ahub_put_value_enum()` must iterate over all `ahub->soc_data->reg_count` parts, setting `update[i].val` to `reg_val` for `i == reg_idx` and `0` for all other parts via `snd_soc_dapm_mux_update_power()`, and `reg_count` must not exceed `TEGRA_XBAR_UPDATE_MAX_REG`. `MUX_VALUE(npart, nbit)` is 1-based (`1 + nbit + npart * 32`, `0` = `"None"`) to match `ffs(reg_val)` in `tegra_ahub_get_value_enum`, and each `tegra*_ahub_mux_values` array must match the length and order of its `tegra*_ahub_mux_texts` array. [F310, F312, F313, F314]
- In `tegra30_ahub.h` and `tegra30_ahub.c`, `enum tegra30_ahub_rxcif` values must equal the 0-based register index (`0..TEGRA30_AHUB_AUDIO_RX_COUNT - 1`) of `TEGRA30_AHUB_AUDIO_RX`, and `enum tegra30_ahub_txcif` values must equal the 0-based bit shift (`1 << txcif`) written by `tegra30_ahub_set_rx_cif_source`. [F323]
- In `tegra210_i2s.c` and `tegra210_amx.c`, accesses to registers that shifted on Tegra264 (`TEGRA210_I2S_TX_*`, `TEGRA210_I2S_CTRL`, `TEGRA210_I2S_TIMING`, `TEGRA210_I2S_SLOT_CTRL`, `TEGRA210_AMX_CFG_RAM_*`) must add `soc_data->tx_offset`, `soc_data->i2s_ctrl_offset`, or `soc_data->reg_offset`, and call `tegra264_set_cif()` when `soc_data->max_ch == 32`. [F362]
- `tegra210_amx_startup()` and `tegra210_i2s_init()` must poll `TEGRA210_AMX_STATUS` or `TEGRA210_I2S_RX_STATUS`/`TEGRA210_I2S_TX_STATUS` via `regmap_read_poll_timeout()` to confirm the module is disabled before asserting `TEGRA210_AMX_SOFT_RESET` or `TEGRA210_I2S_RX_SOFT_RESET`/`TEGRA210_I2S_TX_SOFT_RESET`. [F356]
- In `tegra210_amx_runtime_resume()`, after `regcache_sync(amx->regmap)`, `tegra210_amx_write_map_ram(amx)` must be called to restore volatile `TEGRA210_AMX_CFG_RAM_DATA` (`amx->map`) and `TEGRA210_AMX_OUT_BYTE_EN0` (`amx->byte_mask`). Every active `TEGRA210_AMX_BYTE_MAP_CTRL(reg)` control must satisfy `reg < amx->soc_data->ram_depth * 4` and `reg / 32 < amx->soc_data->byte_mask_size`, and `tegra210_amx_dais` must keep the 4 `IN_DAI`s at `0..3` and `OUT_DAI` at `TEGRA_AMX_OUT_DAI_ID` (`4`) for `tegra210_amx_in_hw_params` (`AMX_CH_REG`) and probe. [F297, F315, F317]
- In `tegra210_i2s`, `client_sample_format` must be a signed `int` initialized to `-EINVAL` in `tegra210_i2s_probe()` and checked with `>= 0` in `tegra210_i2s_hw_params()` (`0` is `SNDRV_PCM_FORMAT_S8`); `sync_input` is optional (do not fail probe on `IS_ERR(i2s->clk_sync_input)`, and guard `clk_set_rate` in `tegra210_i2s_set_clock_rate()`). `tegra210_i2s_sw_reset()` must save and restore `cif_reg`, `stream_reg`, and `TEGRA210_I2S_CTRL` (`+ i2s_ctrl_offset`), and `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_RIGHT_J` `data_offset` (`num_bclk - sample_size`) must be deferred from `tegra210_i2s_set_fmt()` to `tegra210_i2s_set_timing_params()`. [F286, F306, F307, F354]
- In `tegra210_sfc.c`, `tegra210_sfc_rates[]` and both dimensions of `coef_addr_table[][]` must have `TEGRA210_SFC_NUM_RATES` (`13`) entries matching hardware `TEGRA210_SFC_RX_FREQ`/`TEGRA210_SFC_TX_FREQ` indices (using `UNSUPP_CONV` for unsupported rates like 64000 Hz), with each valid entry pointing to `>= TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_DEPTH` (`64`) `s32` values. `tegra210_sfc_startup()` clears `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_EN` in `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM`, and `tegra210_sfc_write_coeff_ram()` must only re-enable it when `sfc->srate_in != sfc->srate_out`. [F298, F299, F321]
- In `tegra210_mvc.c`, `tegra210_mvc_put_curve_type()` must verify `TEGRA210_MVC_EN` is clear in `TEGRA210_MVC_ENABLE` before changing `mvc->curve_type` and calling `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings()`. `tegra210_mvc_update_vol()` and `tegra210_mvc_update_mute()` must poll `tegra210_mvc_volume_switch_timeout()` before mutating volume/mute registers and set `TEGRA210_MVC_VOLUME_SWITCH_TRIGGER` in `TEGRA210_MVC_SWITCH` afterward; when `per_ch_enable` is false, `tegra210_mvc_update_vol()` must clear `TEGRA210_MVC_PER_CHAN_CTRL_EN` and copy `mvc->volume[0]` across `mvc->volume[1..TEGRA210_MVC_MAX_CHAN_COUNT-1]`. [F300, F301, F302]
- Because `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL` and `TEGRA210_MVC_SWITCH` are volatile, `tegra210_mvc_runtime_suspend()` must save `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL` into `mvc->ctrl_value` before `regcache_cache_only()`, and `tegra210_mvc_runtime_resume()` must rewrite `mvc->ctrl_value` and trigger `TEGRA210_MVC_VOLUME_SWITCH_TRIGGER` after `regcache_sync()`. [F357]
- In `tegra210_mixer.c`, `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain()` must poll bit 31 of `TEGRA210_MIXER_GAIN_CFG_RAM_CTRL` before each write (`tegra210_mixer_write_ram`), write all `NUM_GAIN_POLY_COEFFS` coefficients, `mixer->gain_value[id]`, all `NUM_DURATION_PARMS` words, and write `VAL_CFG_DONE_TRIGGER` to `reg + REG_CFG_DONE_TRIGGER`. `tegra210_mixer_dais` must place all `TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_MAX` (`10`) `IN_DAI`s at `0..9` before `OUT_DAI`s, and `tegra210_mixer_wr_reg`/`rd_reg`/`volatile_reg` must check `reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_LIMIT` before `reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_TX_LIMIT` (adding `TEGRA210_MIXER_TX1_ENABLE` for TX). [F309, F318, F327]
- In `tegra210_ope_runtime_suspend()` and `tegra210_ope_runtime_resume()`, `ope->regmap`, `ope->peq_regmap`, and `ope->mbdrc_regmap` must transition cache-only/dirty/synced together, calling `tegra210_peq_save()` before enabling cache-only on suspend and `tegra210_peq_restore()` after `regcache_sync()` on resume. [F305]
- In `struct tegra_soc_bytes`, `struct soc_bytes soc` must be the first field at offset 0 for `tegra210_peq_param_info` and `tegra210_mbdrc_param_info`. `tegra210_peq_ram_get`/`put` controls require `params->soc.num_regs <= TEGRA210_PEQ_GAIN_PARAM_SIZE_PER_CH` (`62`, `biquad_coeff_buffer`), `tegra210_mbdrc_threshold_get`/`put` controls require `soc.num_regs` to be a multiple of 4 (`TEGRA210_MBDRC_THRESHOLD_NUM`), and `tegra210_mbdrc_wr_reg`/`rd_reg`/`volatile_reg`/`precious_reg` must reduce `reg >= TEGRA210_MBDRC_IIR_CFG` modulo `TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_PARAM_STRIDE * TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_COUNT` before `switch (reg)`. [F319, F320, F328, F338]
- All AHUB and child module `dev_pm_ops` (`tegra_ahub_pm_ops`, `tegra210_amx_pm_ops`, `tegra210_dmic_pm_ops`, `tegra210_i2s_pm_ops`, `tegra210_mixer_pm_ops`, `tegra210_mvc_pm_ops`, `tegra210_ope_pm_ops`, `tegra210_sfc_pm_ops`, `tegra30_ahub_pm_ops`, `tegra30_i2s_pm_ops`) must use normal `SYSTEM_SLEEP_PM_OPS` rather than `LATE_SYSTEM_SLEEP_PM_OPS`. [F344]

## Synchronization

- In `tegra30_ahub_probe`, global `ahub` singleton fields (`soc_data`, `dev`, `clocks`, `resets`, `apbif_addr`, `regmap_apbif`, `regmap_ahub`) must be initialized and `pm_runtime_enable` called before `of_platform_populate`, and `ahub` must be reset to `NULL` on probe failure or in `tegra30_ahub_remove`. [F277]
- In `tegra210_ope_probe`, `dev_set_drvdata(dev, ope)` must precede `tegra210_peq_regmap_init` and `tegra210_mbdrc_regmap_init`, and all three regmaps (`ope->regmap`, `ope->peq_regmap`, `ope->mbdrc_regmap`) in `tegra210_ope_probe` as well as `amx->map` and `amx->byte_mask` in `tegra210_amx_platform_probe` must be initialized before `devm_snd_soc_register_component` and `pm_runtime_enable`. [F279, F281]
- `tegra210_i2s_sw_reset`, `tegra210_i2s_init`, `tegra210_mixer_write_ram`, `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain`, `tegra210_mvc_volume_switch_timeout`, and `tegra210_sfc_soft_reset` sleep via `regmap_read_poll_timeout` and must only be called from process context, never from atomic PCM `.trigger` callbacks. [F364]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `tegra30_i2s_platform_probe` and `tegra30_i2s_platform_remove`, resources must be torn down in strict reverse order: `tegra_pcm_platform_unregister`, `snd_soc_unregister_component`, unrouting/freeing `capture_fifo_cif`, unrouting `playback_i2s_cif` and freeing `playback_fifo_cif`, and `pm_runtime_disable`. [F271]
- Device-tree node references from `of_get_child_by_name` or `of_graph_get_endpoint_by_regs` in `tegra210_parse_client_convert`, `tegra210_peq_regmap_init`, and `tegra210_mbdrc_regmap_init` must be released with `of_node_put` on all return paths. [F275]
- `tegra30_ahub_runtime_resume()` must assert `ahub->resets`, enable `ahub->clocks`, wait `usleep_range(10, 100)`, and deassert `ahub->resets` before exiting cache-only mode and syncing `regmap_apbif` and `regmap_ahub`, calling `clk_bulk_disable_unprepare(ahub->nclocks, ahub->clocks)` if deassert or sync fails. [F352]

## API Contracts

- Callers of `tegra_set_cif()` and `tegra264_set_cif()` must zero-initialize `struct tegra_cif_conf` and pass 1-based channel counts (`>= 1`) in `audio_ch` and `client_ch`; callers of `tegra30_ahub_set_cif` and `tegra124_ahub_set_cif` must likewise pass 1-based `conf->audio_channels` and `conf->client_channels`, as all four subtract 1 without masking. [F288, F371]
- `tegra30_ahub_set_rx_cif_source()` and `tegra30_ahub_unset_rx_cif_source()` take the destination `enum tegra30_ahub_rxcif` (`i2s->playback_i2s_cif` or `i2s->capture_fifo_cif`) as their first argument, whereas `tegra30_ahub_enable/disable/free_rx_fifo` (and `tx_fifo` counterparts) require an APBIF FIFO CIF in `[0, TEGRA30_AHUB_CHANNEL_CTRL_COUNT)` (`playback_fifo_cif` or `capture_fifo_cif`). `tegra30_ahub_allocate_rx_fifo`/`tx_fifo` must compute `*fiforeg` from physical `ahub->apbif_addr` (`res0->start`), not virtual `regs_apbif`. [F294, F322, F363]
- In CPU DAI `.set_fmt` callbacks `tegra210_i2s_set_fmt` and `tegra30_i2s_set_fmt`, `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_BP_FP` must set the I2S master enable bit (`I2S_CTRL_MASTER_EN` / `TEGRA30_I2S_CTRL_MASTER_ENABLE`) and `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_BC_FC` must clear it. [F365]
- In `tegra210_amx_get_byte_map` and `tegra210_amx_put_byte_map`, control value `256` denotes a disabled slot: `put` must clear `1 << (reg % 32)` in `amx->byte_mask[reg / 32]` and write `value % 256`, while `get` must return `256` when that bit is clear. [F370]
- `TEGRA_SOC_BYTES_EXT` callbacks paired with `tegra210_peq_param_info()` (`SNDRV_CTL_ELEM_TYPE_INTEGER`) must access `ucontrol->value.integer.value[]`, while those paired with `tegra210_mbdrc_param_info()` (`SNDRV_CTL_ELEM_TYPE_BYTES`) must access `ucontrol->value.bytes.data`. In `tegra210_ope_component_probe`, `snd_soc_component_init_regmap` binds the component to `ope->regmap`, so PEQ and MBDRC callbacks must explicitly pass `ope->peq_regmap` and `ope->mbdrc_regmap`. [F284, F329]

## Error Handling

- In `tegra210_mvc_update_vol`, `tegra210_mvc_update_mute`, and `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain`, every exit path after `pm_runtime_get_sync(cmpnt->dev)`—including timeout errors and `mvc->volume[chan] == old_volume`—must jump to the `pm_runtime_put(cmpnt->dev)` label (`end` or `rpm_put`). [F276]
- In `coef_addr_table`, off-diagonal unsupported sample-rate conversions must be `UNSUPP_CONV` (`(void *)(-EOPNOTSUPP)`) rather than `BYPASS_CONV` (`NULL`), because `tegra210_sfc_write_coeff_ram()` checks `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(coeff_ram)` and returns `PTR_ERR_OR_ZERO(coeff_ram)`. [F287]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `tegra210_mvc_platform_probe`, `pm_runtime_enable(dev)` must precede `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings(mvc, &pdev->dev)` so `pm_runtime_get_sync(dev)` exits regmap cache-only mode before writing volatile `TEGRA210_MVC_SWITCH`. In `tegra210_amx_platform_probe()`, `tegra_ahub_probe()`, and `tegra210_i2s_probe()`, once `device_get_match_data()` is stored in the driver struct's `soc_data` member, subsequent probe calls must dereference that struct member rather than a local `soc_data` pointer. [F282, F295]
- In `tegra210_mbdrc_hw_params`, multi-bit mode fields with a zero-valued variant such as `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_MBDRC_MODE_BYPASS` must be masked with `TEGRA210_MBDRC_CFG_MBDRC_MODE_MASK` and compared using `==`, not tested with `&`. [F325]
- Avoid 32-bit overflow and sign-extension bugs in gain/volume math: `tegra210_dmic_hw_params()` must compute `DEFAULT_GAIN_Q23 * dmic->boost_gain` in `u64` (`gain_q23`) and divide via `div_u64()`; `tegra210_mvc_conv_vol()` and `tegra210_mvc_get_vol()` must clamp `CURVE_POLY` `val` to `10000` and shift by 16 after `/ 100` (write) or before `* 100` (read) while keeping `CURVE_LINEAR` `val`/`mvc->volume[]` signed `s32` across `>> 8`; `tegra210_mbdrc_vol_get()` must hold `TEGRA210_MBDRC_MASTER_VOL` in signed `int` before `>> mc->shift` (`23`); and `tegra210_peq_ram_get()` must cast `biquad_coeff_buffer` words to `s32` before assigning to `ucontrol->value.integer.value[i]` (`long`). [F331, F333, F334, F335]
