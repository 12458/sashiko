# MELLANOX MLX5 core VPI driver — drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx5/core (part 3)

Implements the mlx5 E-Switch (SR-IOV, Subfunctions, and Switchdev offloads) in `eswitch.c` and `esw/`, covering vports, ingress/egress ACLs (`esw/acl/`), legacy L2/VEPA (`esw/legacy.c`), indirect tables (`esw/indir_table.c`), bridge and multicast offloads (`esw/bridge*.c`), QoS (`esw/qos.c`), IPsec (`esw/ipsec*.c`), and devlink ports (`esw/devlink_port.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `esw->fdb_table` is a union of `legacy` and `offloads` keyed by `esw->mode`, valid only when `mlx5_esw_is_fdb_created(esw)` is true. `esw_create_legacy_fdb_table` splits `legacy.fdb` (`table_size = fdb->max_fte`) into `0 .. table_size - 3` (`addr_grp`), `table_size - 2` (`allmulti_grp`), and `table_size - 1` (`promisc_grp`). [F1304, F1343]
- `struct mlx5_vport_key` must stay `__packed` without padding and `flow_attr_to_vport_key` must initialize every field, as `mlx5_esw_vporttbl_get`/`put` pass un-memset stack keys to `jhash` and `memcmp`. [F1312]
- `vport->uc_list` and `vport->mc_list` hold `struct vport_addr` whereas `esw->mc_table` holds `struct esw_mc_addr`; `l2addr_hash_find` and `container_of` must use the matching type. `eswitch_vport_event` uses `&esw->nb` (`mlx5_nb_cof(..., struct mlx5_eswitch, nb)`), whereas `mlx5_esw_funcs_changed_handler` uses `&esw->esw_funcs.nb`. [F1374, F1377]
- Skip manager vports (`mlx5_esw_is_manager_vport`) in `esw_legacy_vport_acl_setup`/`cleanup`, `mlx5_esw_vport_caps_get`, `mlx5_esw_vport_vhca_id_map`/`unmap`, and admin-state changes in `esw_vport_setup`/`cleanup`. Call `modify_esw_vport_cvlan` in `esw_vport_setup` and `__mlx5_eswitch_set_vport_vlan` only when `esw->mode == MLX5_ESWITCH_OFFLOADS || !esw_vst_mode_is_steering(esw)`. [F1389, F1391]
- `acl_ingress_ofld_setup` and `esw_acl_egress_ofld_setup` must increment `num_ftes` and `table_size` under the exact conditions used by `esw_acl_ingress_ofld_groups_create` and `esw_acl_egress_ofld_groups_create`. `esw_acl_ingress_ofld_rules_create` must call `esw_acl_ingress_mod_metadata_create` before `esw_acl_ingress_prio_tag_create`, destroy `allow_rule` before deallocating `modify_metadata`, and `mlx5_esw_acl_ingress_vport_metadata_update` must restore `vport->metadata = vport->default_metadata` on failure. [F1329, F1342]
- In `struct mlx5_esw_sched_node`, `node->vport` is `NULL` on node-level TSARs (`SCHED_NODE_TYPE_VPORTS_TSAR`, `SCHED_NODE_TYPE_VPORTS_TC_TSAR`). Keep `sched_node_type_str` and `esw_qos_sched_elem_warn` in sync with `enum sched_node_type`. For `SCHED_NODE_TYPE_RATE_LIMITER` (unlinked from `parent->children`), `esw_qos_nodes_set_parent` updates `child->vport->qos.sched_node->parent` via `VPORT_TC` grandchildren and `mlx5_esw_qos_set_vport_min_rate` propagates `min_rate` to `vport->qos.sched_nodes[i]`. [F1305, F1333, F1335]
- On `min_rate` or parent changes, call `esw_qos_normalize_min_rate` on affected parents (both old and new in `mlx5_esw_qos_node_update_parent`), skipping `bw_share` on `SCHED_NODE_TYPE_VPORTS_TC_TSAR`. `esw_qos_calc_bw_share` returns `0` if `!divider`, else clamps `DIV_ROUND_UP(value, divider)` to `[MLX5_MIN_BW_SHARE, fw_max]`. [F1332, F1363]
- `vport->qos.sched_nodes` has `esw_qos_num_tcs(dev)` entries: validate `tc_bw` with `esw_qos_validate_unsupported_tc_bw`, never index `>= esw_qos_num_tcs(dev)`, and check `esw_qos_tc_bw_disabled(tc_bw)` before `esw_qos_set_tc_arbiter_bw_shares` to avoid a zero divider. Account for the extra `SCHED_NODE_TYPE_VPORTS_TC_TSAR` level in `SCHED_NODE_TYPE_TC_ARBITER_TSAR` when checking `log_esw_max_sched_depth` in `mlx5_esw_qos_node_validate_set_parent`, `esw_qos_node_enable_tc_arbitration`, and `esw_qos_vport_tc_enable`. [F1344, F1345, F1364]
- Link/unlink `mlx5_esw_bridge_fdb_entry` in `bridge->fdb_ht`, `bridge->fdb_list`, and `vlan->fdb_list` in lockstep with flow handles and `ingress_counter` in `mlx5_esw_bridge_fdb_entry_init`/`cleanup`. Keep `entry->num_ports` synced with `entry->ports` in `mlx5_esw_bridge_mdb_port_insert`/`remove`, and in `mlx5_esw_bridge_port_mdb_entry_detach` either destroy `entry` when `num_ports == 0` or refresh `egress_handle`. [F1321, F1322]
- `mlx5_esw_bridge_vlan_filtering_set` and `mlx5_esw_bridge_vlan_proto_set` must flush both FDB and MDB tables (`mlx5_esw_bridge_fdb_flush`, `mlx5_esw_bridge_mdb_flush`), and `vlan_proto_set` must call `mlx5_esw_bridge_vlans_recreate`. Key bridge ports/FDB/MDB by both `vport_num` and `esw_owner_vhca_id`, and in `mlx5_esw_bridge_update` use `time_after` and `time_is_before_jiffies`. [F1323, F1325, F1366]
- Keep `[IDX_FROM, IDX_TO]` flow group ranges in `bridge_priv.h` contiguous and summing to `MLX5_ESW_BRIDGE_{INGRESS,EGRESS,MCAST}_TABLE_SIZE`. In `mlx5_esw_ipsec_rx_setup_modify_header`, allocate `rx_mapped_id` within `XA_LIMIT(1, ESW_IPSEC_RX_MAPPED_ID_MASK)` (0 is unmapped sentinel; fits at `ESW_ZONE_ID_BITS` of `METADATA_REG_C_1`). Keep `#ifdef CONFIG_MLX5_ESWITCH` declarations and `#else` stubs in `eswitch.h` and `esw/acl/ofld.h` in sync. [F1336, F1341, F1349]

## Synchronization

- Call `mlx5_esw_release` iff `mlx5_esw_hold` returned `true`. `mlx5_esw_try_lock` and `mlx5_esw_lock` unlock `esw->mode_lock` on negative error; call `mlx5_esw_unlock` only when `try_lock >= 0` or `lock == 0`. Hold `devl_lock` across `mlx5_eswitch_enable_locked`, `mlx5_eswitch_enable`, `mlx5_eswitch_disable_sriov`, and `mlx5_eswitch_disable`. [F1259, F1260, F1288]
- In `mlx5_esw_vport_alloc`, initialize `dev`, `vport`, `index`, `vhca_id`, and `INIT_WORK(&vport->vport_change_handler, ...)` before `xa_insert(&esw->vports, ...)`. `eswitch_vport_event` (atomic EQ context) must defer work to `esw->work_queue`; never hold `esw->state_lock` across `mlx5_eswitch_event_handler_unregister` or `flush_workqueue(esw->work_queue)`. [F1278, F1280, F1392]
- Lock order: `rtnl_lock` -> `esw->state_lock` -> `esw_qos_lock(esw)`. `mlx5_esw_qos_vport_disable` requires `state_lock` before `esw_qos_lock`. `mlx5_esw_qos_lag_link_speed_get_locked` requires `ASSERT_RTNL()`, and `esw_qos_devlink_rate_to_mbps` must call `mlx5_esw_qos_max_link_speed_get(..., true)` before `esw_qos_lock`. In `mlx5_eswitch_unload_vport`, run `mlx5_esw_offloads_unload_rep` (which detaches QoS parent via `mlx5_esw_qos_vport_update_parent(vport, NULL, NULL)`) before `mlx5_esw_vport_disable`. [F1283, F1284, F1286]
- Hold `rtnl_lock` in `mlx5_esw_acl_ingress_vport_metadata_update`. Hold `esw->fdb_table.offloads.vports.lock` across `esw_vport_tbl_lookup` and `mlx5_esw_vporttbl_get`/`put`, and `esw->fdb_table.offloads.indir->lock` across `mlx5_esw_indir_table_get`/`put` and entry/rule lookups and refupdates. [F1289, F1290, F1295]
- `rtnl_lock` protects all `br_offloads` and `bridge` lists/hashtables; `mlx5_esw_bridge_debugfs_start` must acquire `rtnl_lock()` on all returns so `mlx5_esw_bridge_debugfs_stop` can unconditionally call `rtnl_unlock()`. Async FDB work in `mlx5_esw_bridge_fdb_create` and `mlx5_esw_bridge_port_vlan_lookup` must re-verify port/VLAN existence, and `mlx5_esw_bridge_update` must skip entries with `MLX5_ESW_BRIDGE_FLAG_DELETED` or `ADDED_BY_USER`. [F1276, F1291, F1292, F1293]
- Hold `mlx5_devcom_for_each_peer_begin` across both peer lookup and `peer_esw` flow creation in `mlx5_esw_bridge_ingress_flow_peer_create` and `mlx5_esw_bridge_mcast_filter_flow_peer_create`. In `mlx5_esw_ipsec_restore_dest_uplink`, check `atomic_read(&rep->rep_data[REP_ETH].state) == REP_LOADED` and skip `IS_ERR(flow)` when walking `rpriv->tc_ht`. Use `xa_alloc_bh`/`xa_erase_bh` on `ipsec->ipsec_obj_id_map`. [F1294, F1297, F1401]

## Object Lifetimes

- Free non-error returns from `mlx5_esw_query_functions` with `kvfree()` (it frees internally on error). A `vport_addr` with `mc_promisc == true` holds no `esw_mc->refcnt` reference unless promoted by incrementing `refcnt` and clearing `mc_promisc`. [F1258, F1261]
- `mlx5_esw_vport_disable` must set `vport->enabled = false` before `esw_vport_change_handle_locked`, and `esw_vport_cleanup` must not clear `vport->info`. `mlx5_eswitch_disable_locked` must call `mlx5_esw_mode_change_notify` and `mlx5_eswitch_event_handler_unregister` before tearing down FDB/ACL/QoS, and call `devl_rate_nodes_destroy` when disabling offloads. [F1262, F1263, F1408]
- Load `MLX5_VPORT_PF` before `host_pf_enable_hca` in `mlx5_eswitch_enable_pf_vf_vports` and call `host_pf_disable_hca` before unloading it. In `mlx5_esw_adj_vport_create`/`destroy`, add ACLs and representor (`mlx5_esw_offloads_rep_add`) before `MLX5_ADJ_VPORT_CONNECT`, and disconnect before removing them. `mlx5_esw_offloads_pf_vf_devlink_port_cleanup` frees `vport->dl_port`, whereas `mlx5_esw_offloads_sf_devlink_port_cleanup` only clears `vport->dl_port = NULL`. [F1270, F1380, F1383]
- In `esw_acl_ingress_src_port_drop_create`, call `esw_acl_ingress_ofld_cleanup` on error only if `created == true`. Reset `vport->ingress.acl`, `allow_rule`, `modify_metadata_rule`, and `fwd_rule` to `NULL` on `IS_ERR` because ACL destroy helpers only check for non-NULL. [F1264, F1265]
- `__esw_qos_free_node` calls `list_del(&node->entry)`, so nodes allocated with `parent == NULL` must stay `list_del_init`'d or on `domain->nodes`. In `esw_qos_node_enable`/`disable_tc_arbitration`, reparent `curr_node->children` back to `node` and call `__esw_qos_free_node(curr_node)` on all paths. Call `esw_qos_put` on error after `esw_qos_get` succeeds, never if `esw_qos_get` fails. [F1266, F1267, F1268]
- In `mlx5_esw_indir_table_rule_get`, always call `mlx5e_mod_hdr_dealloc(&mod_acts)`, and hold `mlx5_chains_get_table(chains, 0, 1, 0)` until `rule->refcnt == 0` in `mlx5_esw_indir_table_rule_put` (which must set `e->recirc_rule = NULL`). `mlx5_esw_indir_table_put` destroys `e` only when `e->fwd_ref == 0 && !e->recirc_rule`. [F1271, F1272]
- Call `mlx5_esw_bridge_ingress_table_cleanup` when `br_offloads->bridges` is empty on `mlx5_esw_bridge_lookup` creation failure or in `mlx5_esw_bridge_put`. Clear `MLX5_ESW_BRIDGE_MCAST_FLAG` before calling `mlx5_esw_brige_mcast_global_disable`. In `mlx5_esw_bridge_port_mdb_attach`, call `mlx5_esw_bridge_port_mdb_entry_cleanup` on insert failure iff `!entry->num_ports`. In `esw_ipsec_vf_offload_set_bytype`, enable generic before specific, and disable generic after specific only when both crypto and packet offloads are false. [F1273, F1274, F1275, F1277]

## API Contracts

- `mlx5_devlink_eswitch_get` returns `ERR_PTR`, whereas `mlx5_devlink_eswitch_nocheck_get` returns `NULL` or valid pointer. Check return values before reading outputs of `mlx5_esw_qos_get_vport_rate` (returns `false` if `!sched_node`), `mlx5_esw_vport_vhca_id` (returns `false` on error), and `mlx5_esw_sf_max_hpf_functions` (`*sf_base_id` unset when `*max_sfs == 0`). `mlx5_esw_bridge_ingress_mld_fg_create` returns `NULL` if `MLX5_FLEX_PROTO_ICMPV6` is unsupported or `ERR_PTR` on error. `__esw_fdb_set_vport_rule` converts `IS_ERR` to `NULL`. [F1299, F1300, F1301, F1302, F1303, F1314]
- Pass `MLX5_ESWITCH_IGNORE_NUM_VFS` (`< 0`) to `mlx5_eswitch_enable_locked` and `mlx5_eswitch_update_num_of_vfs` to preserve VF counts. `set_action_in` `data` takes unshifted bit-0-aligned values, whereas `fte_match_param` takes values shifted by `ESW_SOURCE_PORT_METADATA_OFFSET` or `ESW_ZONE_ID_BITS`. In `mlx5_eswitch_set_vport_state`, translate `MLX5_VPORT_UPLINK` to `opmod = MLX5_VPORT_STATE_OP_MOD_UPLINK`, `other_vport = 0`, `vport = 0`. Translate 1-based host VF `vport_num` to `vport_num - 1` (`adj_info.function_id` for adjacent, `vport_num - base_vport` for EC VFs). [F1316, F1318, F1319, F1320]
- Call `esw_update_vport_mc_promisc` between `esw_update_vport_addr_list` and `esw_apply_vport_addr_list` for `MLX5_NVPRT_LIST_TYPE_MC`. Pass `U16_MAX` (checking `vport->adjacent`) to `mlx5_esw_for_each_vf_vport` for adjacent VFs, and guard `mlx5_esw_for_each_ec_vf_vport` with `mlx5_core_ec_sriov_enabled`. Size `QUERY_DELEGATED_VHCA` `outlen` with `+ delegated_vhca_max * MLX5_ST_SZ_BYTES(delegated_function_vhca_rid_info)`. [F1327, F1338, F1339, F1368]
- Convert devlink rate Bps to Mbps via `esw_qos_devlink_rate_to_mbps`. Rate leaf `priv` is `struct mlx5_vport *`; rate node `priv` is `struct mlx5_esw_sched_node *`. Embed `devlink_port` in `struct mlx5_devlink_port` via `mlx5_devlink_port_init`. Keep `TRACE_INCLUDE_*` and `define_trace.h` outside header guards in `*_tracepoint.h`. [F1346, F1371, F1373, F1417]

## Error Handling

- In `mlx5_esw_bridge_egress_table_init`, reset failed `miss_fg`, `miss_pkt_reformat`, or `miss_handle` to `NULL` and free earlier miss resources before `skip_miss_flow`. In `esw_qos_tc_arbiter_scheduling_setup` and `esw_qos_node_enable_tc_arbitration`, destroy the new element and restore `node->ix` (and `node->type`) on failure. [F1308, F1310]

## External Contracts

- `__mlx5_eswitch_set_vport_vlan` rejects `vlan > 4095 || qos > 7` with `-EINVAL`; `mlx5_eswitch_set_vport_vlan` must return `0` when `esw->mode != MLX5_ESWITCH_LEGACY && vlan == 0`. Emit `SWITCHDEV_FDB_{ADD,DEL}_TO_BRIDGE` only when neither `ADDED_BY_USER` nor `PEER` is set, and convert `ageing_time` via `clock_t_to_jiffies`. [F1348, F1410, F1412]

## Known Pitfalls

- `memset` reused `flow_group_in` buffers between `mlx5_create_flow_group` calls, and set both `*_47_16` and `*_15_0` when masking MACs with `MLX5_SET_TO_ONES`. Query and `memcpy` `hca_cap_union` before `MLX5_CMD_OP_SET_HCA_CAP`. Set `other_vport` when `vport_num != 0 || mlx5_core_is_ecpf(dev)`. Use `list_for_each_entry_safe` when reparenting QoS children, never use `sizeof(tc_bw)` on `u32 *tc_bw` parameters, and check `v == SEQ_START_TOKEN` in `mlx5_esw_bridge_debugfs_next`/`show`. [F1313, F1331, F1347, F1352, F1370, F1375, F1388]
