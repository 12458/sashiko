# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd (part 2)

This unit covers the Vega20 Interrupt Handler (`vega20_ih.c`), Volcanic Islands SoC common driver (`vi.c`), Video Processing Engine v6.1 backend (`vpe_v6_1.c`, `vpe_6_1_fw_if.h`), the `amdxcp` platform DRM device allocator (`amdgpu_xcp_drv.c`), and shared headers for ACPI, UEFI CPER error records (`amd_cper.h`), PCIe helpers, sysfs register state dumps (`amdgpu_reg_state.h`), IP blocks (`amd_shared.h`), and Aldebaran/Arcturus register offsets.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `vpe_v6_1_load_microcode()`, when `adev->firmware.load_type == AMDGPU_FW_LOAD_PSP`, only master instance 0's `regVPEC_F32_CNTL` offset and value are placed in `adev->vpe.cmdbuf_cpu_addr[0..1]` (PSP updates slave instances). Direct load (`!= AMDGPU_FW_LOAD_PSP`) must iterate across all `vpe->num_instances` and convert `vpe_firmware_header_v1_0` ucode offsets/sizes and each dword written to `regVPEC_UCODE_DATA` from little-endian via `le32_to_cpu`/`le32_to_cpup`. [F2087, F2095]
- In `vega20_ih.c`, `vega20_ih_rb_cntl` sets `IH_RB_CNTL.MC_SPACE` to `1` (GPA/bus address) when `ih->use_bus_addr` is true and `4` (GPU VM/GART) when false; on non-VF devices (OSSSYS 4.2.1 direct load, 4.4.0, 4.4.2, and 4.4.5), `vega20_ih_irq_init` must also set `IH_CHICKEN.MC_SPACE_GPA_ENABLE = 1` in `mmIH_CHICKEN` / `mmIH_CHICKEN_ALDEBARAN` when `adev->irq.ih.use_bus_addr` is true. [F2143]
- When clearing an IH ring overflow via `IH_RB_CNTL__WPTR_OVERFLOW_CLEAR` in `vega20_ih_toggle_ring_interrupts()` or `vega20_ih_get_wptr()`, the driver must transition `WPTR_OVERFLOW_CLEAR` from 0 to 1 and immediately clear it back to 0, while advancing `ih->rptr` to `(wptr + 32) & ih->ptr_mask` in `vega20_ih_get_wptr()`. [F2085]
- In `vega20_ih_irq_rearm`, the loop polling `ih_regs->ih_rb_rptr` and re-writing `WDOORBELL32(ih->doorbell_index, ih->rptr)` while `(v < ih->ring_size) && (v != ih->rptr)` must remain bounded to `MAX_REARM_RETRY` (10) iterations and break once `ih_rb_rptr` matches `ih->rptr` or is out of range. [F2107]
- `vi_get_register_value` must only be called with a `reg_offset` matched against `vi_allowed_read_registers[]` in `vi_read_register`; when `indexed` is true, it must map the broadcast sentinel `0xffffffff` for `se_num` or `sh_num` to index `0` before indexing `adev->gfx.config.rb_config[se_idx][sh_idx]`, and `mmGB_TILE_MODE*`/`mmGB_MACROTILE_MODE*` offsets must stay within `tile_mode_array` (32) and `macrotile_mode_array` (16). [F2097]
- In `vi.c`, when `adev->flags & AMD_IS_APU` is set, `vi_common_early_init` binds `adev->smc_rreg`/`smc_wreg` to `cz_smc_rreg`/`cz_smc_wreg`, and `vi_read_bios_from_rom`, `vi_asic_reset`, `vi_enable_doorbell_aperture`, `vi_get_pcie_usage`, and `vi_need_reset_on_init` must return early. [F2134]
- In `vi_asic_pci_config_reset`, PCI bus mastering must be cleared with `pci_clear_master(adev->pdev)` before `amdgpu_device_pci_config_reset(adev)` and only re-enabled with `pci_set_master(adev->pdev)` once `RREG32(mmCONFIG_MEMSIZE) != 0xffffffff` within `adev->usec_timeout`. [F2133]
- In `vi_get_pcie_usage()`, after starting and resetting the counters via `ixPCIE_PERF_COUNT_CNTL = 0x5`, the driver must stop the counters and latch shadow registers by writing `0x2` to `ixPCIE_PERF_COUNT_CNTL` before reading `COUNTER0_UPPER`/`COUNTER1_UPPER` and `ixPCIE_PERF_COUNT0/1_TXCLK`. [F2091]
- In `amd_shared.h`, `AMD_CG_SUPPORT_*` bitmask constants are stored in `u64 cg_flags` and extend beyond bit 31 (`AMD_CG_SUPPORT_REPEATER_FGCG` at bit 32, `AMD_CG_SUPPORT_GFX_PERF_CLK` at bit 33); every definition must shift an explicit 64-bit unsigned literal (`1ULL << n` or `BIT_ULL(n)`). [F2114]

## Synchronization
- `vega20_ih_get_wptr` and `vega20_ih_irq_rearm` execute in hardirq context during `amdgpu_ih_process` and must use `RREG32_NO_KIQ`/`WREG32_NO_KIQ` rather than KIQ-routed `RREG32`/`WREG32` for IH registers (`ih_rb_wptr`, `ih_rb_cntl`, `ih_rb_rptr`). Furthermore, `vega20_ih_get_wptr` may only read `*ih->wptr_cpu` on `&adev->irq.ih` (ring0) and `&adev->irq.ih_soft`; on `ih1`, `ih2`, or when `RB_OVERFLOW` is set on ring0, it must read `ih_regs->ih_rb_wptr` via `RREG32_NO_KIQ`. [F2070, F2137]
- `vega20_ih_self_irq` runs in hardirq context when ring 0 dispatches a self-interrupt and must defer `adev->irq.ih1` and `adev->irq.ih2` processing to workqueue context via `schedule_work(&adev->irq.ih1_work)` and `schedule_work(&adev->irq.ih2_work)`. Broken by: calling `amdgpu_ih_process` on `ih1`/`ih2` or doing blocking page-fault/RAS work directly in `vega20_ih_self_irq`. [F2138]
- `vpe_v6_1_set_trap_irq_state` (`vpe_v6_1_trap_irq_funcs.set`) runs under `spin_lock_irqsave(&adev->irq.lock, irqflags)` and `vpe_v6_1_process_trap_irq` (`vpe_v6_1_trap_irq_funcs.process`) runs in hardirq context; neither may sleep, acquire mutexes, or perform `GFP_KERNEL` allocations. [F2142]
- Indirect index/data register accessors in `vi.c` (`vi_pcie_*`, `vi_smc_*`, `cz_smc_*`, `vi_uvd_ctx_*`, `vi_didt_*`, `vi_gc_cac_*`) must hold their respective `adev->*_idx_lock` spinlock via `spin_lock_irqsave` across writing the index register and reading/writing the data register. Similarly, `vi_read_bios_from_rom` must hold `adev->smc_idx_lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave` continuously across programming `ixROM_INDEX`/`ixROM_DATA` into `mmSMC_IND_INDEX_11` and the entire `mmSMC_IND_DATA_11` DWord read loop. [F2065, F2066]
- In `vi_get_register_value`, when reading a non-cached GRBM-indexed register with `se_num != 0xffffffff` or `sh_num != 0xffffffff`, `adev->grbm_idx_mutex` must be held while selecting the target SE/SH via `amdgpu_gfx_select_se_sh`, reading `RREG32(reg_offset)`, and restoring `amdgpu_gfx_select_se_sh(adev, 0xffffffff, 0xffffffff, 0xffffffff, 0)` before unlocking. [F2063]
- `vi_srbm_select` writes `mmSRBM_GFX_CNTL` without internal locking; callers must hold `adev->srbm_mutex` across selecting an `me`/`pipe`/`queue`/`vmid` instance with `vi_srbm_select`, accessing instanced registers, and resetting `vi_srbm_select(adev, 0, 0, 0, 0)`. [F2068]
- In `vi.c`, direct MMIO HDP cache operations in `vi_flush_hdp` and `vi_invalidate_hdp` (`RREG32(mmHDP_MEM_COHERENCY_FLUSH_CNTL)` / `RREG32(mmHDP_DEBUG0)`) and PCIe indirect writes in `vi_pcie_rreg` and `vi_pcie_wreg` (`RREG32_NO_KIQ(mmPCIE_INDEX)` / `RREG32_NO_KIQ(mmPCIE_DATA)`) must read back the written register to flush posted PCIe writes. [F2145]
- In `vpe_v6_1_ring_start`, resetting `regVPEC_QUEUE0_RB_WPTR` and `regVPEC_QUEUE0_RB_WPTR_HI` to a smaller value requires writing `1` to `regVPEC_QUEUE0_MINOR_PTR_UPDATE` before programming the WPTR registers and `0` afterward, before setting `RB_ENABLE` in `regVPEC_QUEUE0_RB_CNTL`. [F2071]
- `vpe_v_6_1_ring_stop` must synchronously poll `regVPEC_QUEUE_RESET_REQ` (or `regVPEC_QUEUE_RESET_REQ_6_1_1` on VPE 6.1.1) via `SOC15_WAIT_ON_RREG` until `VPEC_QUEUE_RESET_REQ__QUEUE0_RESET_MASK` clears on every VPE instance and set `vpe->ring.sched.ready = false`. [F2072]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `amdgpu_xcp_drm_dev_alloc`, resources are acquired in the order `platform_device_register_simple`, `devres_open_group`, and `devm_drm_dev_alloc`; error paths must roll them back in reverse (`devres_release_group(&pdev->dev, NULL)` before `platform_device_unregister(pdev)`), and `pxcp_dev` must not be stored in `xcp_dev[i]` or increment `pdev_num` until `IS_ERR(pxcp_dev)` succeeds. On release, `free_xcp_dev` must clear `xcp_dev[index] = NULL` and decrement `pdev_num` while `xcp_mutex` is held by `amdgpu_xcp_drm_dev_free` or `amdgpu_xcp_drv_release` so slots can be reused without double-freeing. [F2058, F2060, F2074]
- In `vega20_ih_sw_init`, `vega20_ih_init_register_offset(adev)` must be called after `amdgpu_ih_ring_init` has initialized `adev->irq.ih`, `adev->irq.ih1`, and (when supported) `adev->irq.ih2`, because `vega20_ih_init_register_offset` only populates `ih_regs` for rings whose `ring_size` is non-zero. [F2061]
- `vi_read_disabled_bios` must save `mmBUS_CNTL`, `ixROM_CNTL`, and (when `adev->mode_info.num_crtc` is non-zero) `mmD1VGA_CONTROL`, `mmD2VGA_CONTROL`, and `mmVGA_RENDER_CONTROL` before enabling the ROM, and restore all of them after `amdgpu_read_bios(adev)` returns. Broken by: returning early after modifying these registers without restoring saved values. [F2062]

## API Contracts
- In `vega20_ih.c`, `adev->irq.ih2` is not initialized on OSSSYS v4.4.2 and v4.4.5, leaving `ih2.ring_size == 0` and `ih2.ih_regs` unpopulated; any code operating on `adev->irq.ih`, `ih1`, or `ih2` (such as `vega20_ih_enable_ring` or `vega20_ih_toggle_ring_interrupts`) must check `ih->ring_size != 0` before accessing `ih->ih_regs` or programming the ring. [F2075]
- `vpe_v6_1_set_regs` and `vpe_v6_1_irq_init` must obtain `struct amdgpu_device *` via `container_of(vpe, struct amdgpu_device, vpe)` rather than `vpe->ring.adev`, because both callbacks run before `vpe_ring_init` populates `vpe->ring.adev`. [F2078]
- `vi_flush_hdp` and `vi_invalidate_hdp` must check `!ring || !ring->funcs->emit_wreg` before calling `amdgpu_ring_emit_wreg`, falling back to direct MMIO `WREG32`/`RREG32` when either is `NULL`. [F2079]
- Callers of `vi_read_bios_from_rom` must pass a `bios` buffer allocated to a multiple of 4 bytes (at least `ALIGN(length_bytes, 4)`), because `vi_read_bios_from_rom` rounds up to `length_dw = ALIGN(length_bytes, 4) / 4` and writes `length_dw` 32-bit words to `(u32 *)bios`. [F2096]

## Error Handling
- In `amdgpu_xcp_drm_dev_alloc`, `platform_device_register_simple` and `devm_drm_dev_alloc` return `ERR_PTR` values checked with `IS_ERR`/`PTR_ERR`, whereas `devres_open_group` returns `NULL` on failure and must be translated to `-ENOMEM` before jumping to `out_unregister`. [F2073]

## External Contracts
- All UEFI CPER header and section structures in `amd_cper.h` (`struct cper_hdr`, `struct cper_sec_desc`, `struct cper_sec_nonstd_err*`, `struct cper_sec_crashdump*`) must remain inside `#pragma pack(push, 1)` ... `#pragma pack(pop)` so section offsets and record lengths match the byte-packed UEFI CPER binary layout. [F2100]
- Each `struct amdgpu_reg_state_*_v1_0` payload in `amdgpu_reg_state.h` must set `common_header.state_type` to its matching `enum amdgpu_reg_state` constant (`AMDGPU_REG_STATE_TYPE_XGMI`, `WAFL`, `PCIE`, or `USR`) and preserve the explicit `pad` fields in `amdgpu_reg_state_header`, `amdgpu_reg_inst_header`, and `amdgpu_smn_reg_data`. [F2103]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `vpe_v6_1.c`, VPE `IP_VERSION(6, 1, 1)` uses shifted register offsets `regVPEC_CNTL_6_1_1`, `regVPEC_QUEUE_RESET_REQ_6_1_1`, and `regVPEC_PUB_DUMMY2_6_1_1` instead of `regVPEC_CNTL`, `regVPEC_QUEUE_RESET_REQ`, and `regVPEC_PUB_DUMMY2`; accesses to those registers must branch on `amdgpu_ip_version(adev, VPE_HWIP, 0) == IP_VERSION(6, 1, 1)`. [F2088]
- In `vega20_ih_enable_ring` and `vpe_v6_1_ring_start`, ring base registers (`ih_rb_base`/`ih_rb_base_hi` and `regVPEC_QUEUE0_RB_BASE`/`_HI`) take `gpu_addr >> 8` and `gpu_addr >> 40`, whereas writeback pointer registers (`ih_rb_wptr_addr_lo`/`hi` and `regVPEC_QUEUE0_RB_RPTR_ADDR_LO`/`_HI`) take `lower_32_bits` and `upper_32_bits`. [F2115]
- In `vega20_ih_sw_init`, `vega20_ih_irq_init`, and `vega20_doorbell_index_init`, QWORD doorbell assignments from `enum AMDGPU_VEGA20_DOORBELL_ASSIGNMENT` (`adev->doorbell_index.ih`, `AMDGPU_VEGA20_DOORBELL_MAX_ASSIGNMENT`) must be shifted left by 1 (`<< 1`) when computing `doorbell_index`, `retry_cam_doorbell_index`, and `max_assignment`. [F2117]
- In `vi_get_pcie_usage`, upper counter overflow fields read from `ixPCIE_PERF_CNTL_TXCLK` via `REG_GET_FIELD` must be stored in `uint64_t` (`cnt0_of`, `cnt1_of`) before shifting left by 32 (`<< 32`) to combine with `ixPCIE_PERF_COUNT0/1_TXCLK`. In `vi_get_pcie_replay_count`, 32-bit reads `RREG32_PCIE(ixPCIE_RX_NUM_NAK)` and `RREG32_PCIE(ixPCIE_RX_NUM_NAK_GENERATED)` must be widened to `uint64_t` (`nak_r`, `nak_g`) before addition so their sum does not wrap at 32 bits. [F2112, F2113]
- In `struct cper_hdr` and `struct cper_sec_desc` (`amd_cper.h`), `signature[4]` (`"CPER"`), `platform_id[16]`, `creator_id[16]`, `record_id[8]`, `fru_id[16]`, and `fru_text[20]` are fixed-width byte arrays in a `#pragma pack(1)` layout without space for NUL terminators. Broken by: writing a 5-byte NUL-terminated `"CPER"` into `hdr->signature` (overwriting `hdr->revision`) or passing these fields to unbounded string functions. [F2101]
