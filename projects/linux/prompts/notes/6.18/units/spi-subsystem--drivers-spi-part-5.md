# SPI SUBSYSTEM — drivers/spi (part 5)

SPI/QSPI/OSPI controller drivers for STM32, Tegra114/20, TI QSPI, Allwinner sun4i/sun6i, Spreadtrum SPI/ADI, Socionext F-OSPI, Topcliff PCH, UniPhier, ST SSC4, Xilinx, NetLogic XLP, Analog Devices XCOMM, Infineon TLE62x0, and `spi-test.h`.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `stm32_ospi/qspi_setup()`, cache `CR`/`DCR` in `cr_reg`/`dcr_reg` for resume; in `stm32_qspi_setup()`, group `mode = spi->mode & (SPI_TX_OCTAL | SPI_RX_OCTAL)` with `gpiod_count(qspi->dev, "cs") == -ENOENT` (`CR_DFM` if `mode`), and clamp `buswidth >= 4` to `CCR_BUSWIDTH_4` in `stm32_qspi_get_mode()`; in `dirmap_read()`, use `CR_FMODE_MM`/`CCR_FMODE_MM` only if `op.addr.val + op.data.nbytes + 1 < mm_size && op.addr.buswidth`, and in `stm32_ospi/qspi_send()` MM reads must `goto abort` after `xfer`/`tx` even if `err == 0` to stop prefetching and skip `wait_cmd`. [F229, F247, F258, F262, F308]
- In `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, `f_ospi_adjust_op_size()` clamps `op->data.nbytes` to `OSPI_DAT_SIZE_MAX` (2048); when `f_ospi_config_indir_protocol()` writes `0` (without `OSPI_DAT_SIZE_EN`) to `OSPI_DAT_SIZE_INDIR`, `f_ospi_indir_read()`/`write()` must call `f_ospi_indir_stop_xfer()` and `f_ospi_indir_read()` must reset `OSPI_SWRST_INDIR_READ_FIFO`; bracket `OSPI_CLK_CTL`, `OSPI_PROT_CTL_INDIR`, and `OSPI_ACC_MODE` writes in `f_ospi_init()`/`f_ospi_indir_prepare_op()` with `f_ospi_prepare_config()` (polling `(val & (stat0 | stat1)) == stat1` on `OSPI_STAT`) and `f_ospi_unprepare_config()`. [F238, F251, F259, F311]
- In `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, per-CS updates in `setup()`/`set_hw_cs_timing()` must preserve sibling CS bits in `def_command1_reg`, `spi_cs_timing1/2`, and `def_command_reg`; in `spi-tegra114.c`, guard `SPI_INTR_MASK` with `has_intr_mask_reg` and set `tspi->last_used_cs = host->num_chipselect + 1` whenever `SPI_COMMAND2` is reset in `resume()` or on timeout in `transfer_one_message()`; test `SPI_FIFO_EMPTY`/`SLINK_FIFO_EMPTY` with `(status & MASK) != MASK`, do a dummy register read before `clk_disable_unprepare()` in `tegra_spi/slink/sflash_runtime_suspend()`, and compare `jiffies` deadlines with `time_after()` in `tegra_spi_flush_fifos()`, `qspi_is_busy()`, and `ti_qspi_poll_wc()`. [F227, F228, F260, F287, F303, F312]
- In `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, unpacked (`!tspi->is_packed`) words use 4-byte `u32` slots in `tx/rx_dma_buf` (`curr_dma_words <= max_buf_size / 4`, DMA len `curr_dma_words * 4`), while unpacked helpers in `spi-tegra114.c` clamp byte counts to `t->len - tspi->cur_pos` and update `tspi->cur_pos` from `cur_tx/rx_pos` only after RX readout; packed (`tspi->is_packed`) FIFO/DMA setup uses `DIV_ROUND_UP(..., 4)`. [F252, F268, F282, F290]
- In `spi-uniphier.c`, calling `uniphier_spi_set_mode()` in `setup_transfer()` must reset `priv->is_save_param = false`, and `fill_tx_fifo()` must compute `fifo_threshold` with `DIV_ROUND_UP(priv->rx_bytes, bpw)` and clamp to `SSI_FIFO_DEPTH` before `set_fifo_threshold()` and subtracting `DIV_ROUND_UP(priv->rx_bytes - priv->tx_bytes, bpw)`. [F224, F283]
- In `spi-sprd.c`, `set_speed()`/`init_hw()` update `ss->hw_speed_hz` and `ss->word_delay` with `SPRD_SPI_CLKD`/`CTL5` (clamping `t->word_delay.value` to `[SPRD_SPI_MIN_DELAY_CYCLE, SPRD_SPI_MAX_DELAY_CYCLE]` before `DIV_ROUND_UP(word_delay - 10, 4)`), set `ss->write_bufs = sprd_spi_write_only_receive` for `SPRD_SPI_RX_MODE`, and in `dma_txrx_bufs()` round `ss->dma.rx_len` down to a multiple of `fragmens_len` when `t->len > fragmens_len`; in `spi-sprd-adi.c`, `sprd_adi_read()`/`write()` validate `reg` via `sprd_adi_check_addr()` (`< slave_addr_size`), and `hw_init()` skips `chn_id < 2` before `REG_ADI_CHN_ADDR(chn_id)` and bounds `chn_id < ADI_HW_CHNS` (50). [F231, F232, F244, F245, F255, F284]
- In `sun4i/sun6i_spi_transfer_one()`, set `SUN4I_CTL_DHB`/`SUN6I_TFR_CTL_DHB` iff `!sspi->rx_buf`; in `sun4i`, set `SUN4I_CTL_CS_ACTIVE_LOW` in `sun4i_spi_set_cs()` when `!(spi->mode & SPI_CS_HIGH)`, reject TX `tfr->len >= SUN4I_MAX_XFER_SIZE` (RX `> SUN4I_MAX_XFER_SIZE`), and pre-fill `<= SUN4I_FIFO_DEPTH - 1` bytes; in `sun6i`, write `SUN6I_CLK_CTL_REG` only if `sspi->cfg->has_clk_ctl` (else set `sspi->mclk` rate and `SUN6I_TFR_CTL_SDM`/`SDC`). [F256, F304, F306, F310]
- In `spi-xilinx.c`, `probe()` must detect endianness via `XSPI_CR_LOOP` at `XSPI_CR_OFFSET` before writing `XIPIF_V123B_RESET_MASK` in `find_buffer_size()`/`xspi_init_hw()`, and `setup_transfer()`/`chipselect()` must only toggle `BIT(spi_get_chipselect(spi, 0))` against `xspi->cs_inactive` in `XSPI_SSR_OFFSET`; in `spi-xlp.c`, `transfer_one()` sets `xspi->cs = spi_get_chipselect(spi, 0)` first, `txrx_bufs()` splits chunks `<= XLP_SPI_XFER_SIZE` (28) with `cmd_cont = 1` on intermediate chunks, and `fill_txfifo()`/`read_rxfifo()` pack/unpack `nbytes = min(len, 4)` from `nbytes - 1` down to `0`. [F236, F237, F254, F298]
- In `spi-ti-qspi.c`, `ti_qspi_setup_clk()` updates `qspi->ctx_reg.clkctrl` with `QSPI_SPI_CLOCK_CNTRL_REG`, and `qspi_read_msg()` (`wlen == 1`, `count < QSPI_WLEN_MAX_BYTES`) caps `rxlen` at `min(count, 4)`; in `spi-st-ssc4.c`, `spi_st_transfer_one()` saves/restores `SSC_CTL` (`ctl`) around 16-bpw switches and wires `spi_st_pm` via `pm_ptr(&spi_st_pm)`; in `spi-test.h`, `iterate_len` initializers end with `-1` within `SPI_TEST_MAX_ITERATE`. [F226, F235, F239, F279, F316]

## Synchronization
- In `spi-stm32-ospi.c` and `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, call `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` before locking `ospi/qspi->lock` and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` after unlocking; hold `lock` across setting `fmode` (`status_timeout`, `PSMKR`, `PSMAR`), `send()`, and CS GPIO changes in `transfer_one_message` (where loop errors must `goto end_of_transfer`); in `wait_cmd()`/`wait_poll_status()`, call `reinit_completion()` before enabling `CR_TCIE | CR_TEIE` or `CR_SMIE`, and clear those bits in `CR` in the IRQ handler before `complete()`. [F173, F185, F186, F187, F320]
- In `spi-ti-qspi.c`, hold `qspi->list_lock` across `mmap_enabled`/`current_cs`, clock/mmap setup, and transfers in `exec_mem_op()` and `start_transfer_one()`; in `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, hold `ospi->mlock` across `f_ospi_indir_read()`/`write()` from `prepare_op()` through completion and FIFO reset; in `spi-tle62x0.c`, hold `st->lock` in `status_show()`, `gpio_show()`, and `gpio_store()` across `gpio_state`, `tx/rx_buff`, and `tle62x0_read()`/`write()`; in `spi-sprd-adi.c`, `sprd_adi_read()`/`write()` hold `sadi->hwlock` via `hwspin_lock_timeout_irqsave()`/`unlock_irqrestore()` when non-NULL. [F190, F192, F194, F319]
- In `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, `handle_dma_based_xfer()` must await `tx/rx_dma_complete` and call `dmaengine_terminate_all()` before `spin_lock_irqsave(&tspi->lock, flags)`, and `tegra_slink_probe()` must call `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` and pulse reset before `request_threaded_irq()`, and `spi_register_controller()` before `pm_runtime_put()`; in `spi-uniphier.c`, set `SSI_DMA_RX/TX_BUSY` in `priv->dma_busy` before submitting DMA descriptors and use `atomic_fetch_andnot()` in `rxcb()`/`txcb()` to finalize only when the other bit is clear; in `sprd_spi_handle_irq()`, `SPRD_SPI_MASK_TX_END` completes `xfer_completion` only when `!(ss->trans_mode & SPRD_SPI_RX_MODE)`. [F196, F197, F264, F318]
- In `sun4i/sun6i_spi_handler()`, check `TC` before FIFO threshold bits and clear threshold bits in `INT_STA_REG` only after draining/refilling the FIFO; in `sun6i_spi_transfer_one()`, RX DMA (`rx_len > 0`) must wait on `sspi->dma_rx_done` after `sspi->done` and call `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` on `dma_rx`/`dma_tx` on timeout; in `xilinx_spi_txrx_bufs()` with `use_irq`, keep `XSPI_CR_TRANS_INHIBIT` set while enabling `XIPIF_V123B_GINTR_ENABLE` and refilling TX FIFO, clearing only around `wait_for_completion(&xspi->done)`; in `spi_st_irq()`, clear `SSC_IEN` and flush via `readl(spi_st->base + SSC_IEN)` before `complete()`. [F204, F205, F272, F327]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `stm32_ospi/qspi_remove()`, call `spi_unregister_controller()` while runtime-resumed before disabling `CR`, releasing DMA, and `mutex_destroy()`; in `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, get `ospi->rstc` via `devm_reset_control_array_get_exclusive_released()` and bracket with `reset_control_acquire()` in `probe`/`resume` and `reset_control_release()` in `remove`/`suspend`; in `stm32_ospi/qspi_tx_dma()`, unmap `sgt` via `spi_controller_dma_unmap_mem_op_data()` on every exit after `map_mem_op_data` (calling `dmaengine_terminate_all()` first on timeout), and in `ti_qspi_dma_xfer()` call `dmaengine_terminate_sync(chan)` on timeout before unmapping. [F172, F189, F191, F300]
- In `tle62x0_probe()` (`err_gpios` for `gpio_attrs[0..ptr-1]`) and `tle62x0_remove()`, remove all created sysfs files (`gpio_attrs` and `dev_attr_status_show`) before `kfree(st)`; in `tegra_slink_remove()`, call `spi_unregister_controller(host)` before `free_irq()`, `pm_runtime_force_suspend()`, and `tegra_slink_deinit_dma_param()`; in `spi-tegra114.c`, `tegra_spi_setup()` must call `tegra_spi_cleanup(spi)` on error after `tegra_spi_parse_cdata_dt(spi)`, and `tegra_spi_cleanup()` must check `if (spi->dev.of_node)` before `kfree(cdata)`. [F174, F179, F195, F198, F220]
- In `pch_spi_request_dma()`/`release_dma()`, balance `pci_get_slot()` with `pci_dev_put(dma_dev)` and on RX channel failure release/null `dma->chan_tx` and set `data->use_dma = 0`; in `pch_spi_pd_remove()`, set `data->status = STATUS_EXITING` and call `pch_spi_free_resources()` (`flush_work(&data->work)`) before disabling IRQs, `free_irq()`, and unmapping `io_remap_addr`; in `sprd_spi_dma_request()`/`runtime_resume()`, release `SPRD_SPI_RX` on TX failure and disable `ss->clk` on DMA failure; in `sun6i_spi_runtime_resume()`/`suspend()`, manage `hclk` -> `mclk` -> `rstc` in strict LIFO order. [F175, F181, F184, F201]

## API Contracts
- In `stm32_ospi/qspi_dirmap_create()`, return `-EOPNOTSUPP` for `SPI_MEM_DATA_OUT` or zero `mm_base`/`mm_size`, and return `ret ?: len` from `dirmap_read`; in `stm32_ospi_xfer()`/`stm32_qspi_tx()`, fall back to polling when `tx_dma` fails; in `f_ospi_supports_op()`, finish with `return spi_mem_default_supports_op(mem, op)`; in `spi-ti-qspi.c`, `ti_qspi_adjust_op_size()` clamps `op->data.nbytes` to `mmap_size - op->addr.val` below `mmap_size` (and `QSPI_FRAME - (1 + op->addr.nbytes + op->dummy.nbytes)` above), `ti_qspi_exec_mem_op()` returns `-EOPNOTSUPP` unless `SPI_MEM_DATA_IN` with 1..4 addr bytes and `from + nbytes <= mmap_size`, and `rx_chan` uses `spi_controller_dma_map_mem_op_data()` (and `unmap`) only if `virt_addr_valid(op->data.buf.in)`, else `ti_qspi_dma_bounce_buffer()`. [F249, F328, F329, F331, F332, F333]
- In `tegra_spi_set_hw_cs_timing()`, check `delay->value != 0` before requiring `delay->unit == SPI_DELAY_UNIT_SCK`; in `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, callers of `calculate_curr_xfer_param()` must ensure `t->len > tspi->cur_pos` (skipping `xfer->len == 0`), and bracket CPU accesses to `tx/rx_dma_buf` with `dma_sync_single_for_cpu()`/`for_device()`; in `sprd_adi_transfer_one()`, reads pass offset `< slave_addr_size` in `*(u32 *)t->rx_buf` and writes pass `u32 {reg, val}` in `t->tx_buf`; in `spi-sprd.c`, `write_bufs`/`read_bufs` take `len` in words, advance `tx/rx_buf` by `len * bytes_per_word`, and return bytes; in `xilinx_spi_probe()`, setting `bitbang.txrx_bufs` requires setting `bitbang.setup_transfer = xilinx_spi_setup_transfer`. [F208, F221, F281, F291, F325, F335]

## Error Handling
- In `stm32_ospi_probe()`, failures after `stm32_ospi_get_resources()` and `pm_runtime_enable()` must jump to `err_pm_enable`/`err_pm_resume`; in `stm32_ospi_get_resources()`, `stm32_qspi_probe()`, and `ti_qspi_probe()`, check `devm_ioremap_resource()`/`devm_platform_get_and_ioremap_resource()` with `IS_ERR()`; in `stm32_ospi/qspi_transfer_one_message()`, init `ret = 0` and set `ret = -EINVAL` before `goto end_of_transfer` if a `dummy_data` transfer is last in `msg->transfers`. [F170, F206, F210, F334]
- In `pch_spi_probe()` and `tle62x0_probe()`, keep loop counter `i`/`ptr` as signed `int` for `while (--i >= 0)` / `while (--ptr >= 0)` unwind, and in `pch_spi_probe()` call `platform_device_put(pd_dev)` before `goto err_platform_device` if `platform_device_add_data()`/`add()` fails; in `uniphier_spi_setup_transfer()`, reset `priv->error = 0`; in `spi_xcomm_transfer_one()`, reset `status = 0` after adding positive return values from `spi_xcomm_sync_config()`/`txrx_bufs()` to `msg->actual_length`. [F176, F215, F217, F223]
- In `tegra_spi/slink_start_dma_based_transfer()`, if RX DMA start fails when `tspi->cur_direction & DATA_DIR_TX`, call `dmaengine_terminate_all(tspi->tx_dma_chan)`; in `sun6i_spi_prepare_dma()` and `uniphier_spi_transfer_one_dma()`, init `rxdesc = NULL` and terminate `host->dma_rx` if TX `dmaengine_prep_slave_sg()` fails when `rxdesc != NULL`; in `uniphier_spi_probe()`, guard `dma_release_channel()` with `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`; in `tegra_spi/sflash/slink_probe()`, check `platform_get_irq() < 0` in a signed `int`; in `xilinx_spi_probe()`, `-ENXIO` from `platform_get_irq()` is non-fatal (keep `xspi->irq` signed and check `>= 0` in `txrx_bufs()`); in `spi_st_probe()`, check `if (!irq)` on `irq_of_parse_and_map()` and set `ret = -EINVAL`; in `sprd_adi_probe()`, gate `ret == 0` from `of_hwspin_lock_get_id()` with `IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_HWSPINLOCK)` and set `ret = -ENXIO` on `!sadi->hwlock`. [F178, F183, F209, F211, F212, F213, F218]

## External Contracts
- In `spi-tle62x0.c`, sysfs `dev_attr_gpio1`..`dev_attr_gpio16` use 1-based names while `to_gpio_num()` returns `0..15` to shift `st->gpio_state`; `tx_buff[4]`, `rx_buff[4]`, and `gpio_attrs[16]` require `st->nr_gpio <= 16`, and `tle62x0_status_show()` overwrites `sprintf`'s NUL with `'\n'` before returning `bp - buf`. [F242, F297]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `stm32_ospi/qspi_send()` and `f_ospi_get_dummy_cycle()`, check `op->dummy.nbytes` (and `buswidth`) for non-zero before dividing by `op->dummy.buswidth`, and in `stm32_ospi/qspi_send()` check `op->data.nbytes` and `op->addr.nbytes` for non-zero before writing `nbytes - 1` to `DLR` or `CCR_ADSIZE_MASK`; in `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, index `spi->chip_select[]` and `spi->cs_gpiod[]` by `ffs(spi->cs_index_mask) - 1` (`< STM32_OSPI_MAX_NORCHIP`); in `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, `spi-ti-qspi.c`, and `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, `platform_set_drvdata()` stores the driver private struct rather than `struct spi_controller *`. [F214, F246, F248, F289]
- In `spi-topcliff-pch.c`, `pch_spi_process_messages()` must check `if (!data->cur_trans->len) goto out;` before `(data->cur_trans->len - 1) / PCH_BUF_SIZE + 1`, save/restore `cur_trans->len` and `cur_trans->rx_buf` around DMA chunks, and rely on `pch_spi_nomore_transfer()` setting `data->cur_trans = NULL` at list end; in `pch_spi_set_tx()`, allocate `u16 *` buffers `pkt_tx/rx_buff` with `cur_trans->len * sizeof(*data->pkt_tx_buff)` bytes; in `spi-tegra114.c`, `bits_per_word == 32` must set `tspi->is_packed = true` because unpacked RX evaluates `((u32)1 << t->bits_per_word) - 1`; in `spi_st_setup()`, compute `baud = spi_st_clk / (2 * sscbrg)` after checking `sscbrg >= 0x07 && sscbrg <= BIT(16)` and before wrapping `sscbrg == BIT(16)` to `0x0`; in `xlp_spi_txrx_bufs()`, advance `tx/rx_buf` by `sz` only when non-NULL. [F234, F266, F276, F278, F280, F292, F295]

## Other
- In `spi-xcomm.c`, `spi_xcomm_setup_transfer()` rejects `t->len > 62` (`u8 buf[63]` with command at `buf[0]`) and `spi_xcomm_sync_config()` uses `put_unaligned_be16()` at `&buf[1]` and `&buf[3]`; in `spi-uniphier.c`, `uniphier_spi_send()`/`recv()` clamp word bytes to `min(bytes_per_word(priv->bits_per_word), priv->tx/rx_bytes)` and use `get/put_unaligned_le16/32()`; in `spi-ti-qspi.c`, 128-bit (`QSPI_WLEN_MAX_BYTES`) bursts with `wlen == 1` in `qspi_write/read_msg()` must use `cpu_to_be32()`/`be32_to_cpu()` and access `QSPI_SPI_DATA_REG_3` down to `QSPI_SPI_DATA_REG`. [F241, F250, F253, F294]
