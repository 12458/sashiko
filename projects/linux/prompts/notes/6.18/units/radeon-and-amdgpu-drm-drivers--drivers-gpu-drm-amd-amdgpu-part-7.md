# RADEON and AMDGPU DRM DRIVERS — drivers/gpu/drm/amd/amdgpu (part 7)

Implements the GPU VM page table manager (`amdgpu_vm.c`), usermode queues/fences (`amdgpu_userq.c`, `amdgpu_userq_fence.c`), SR-IOV virtualization (`amdgpu_virt.c`, `amdgpu_vf_error.c`), multimedia engines (`amdgpu_uvd.c`, `amdgpu_vce.c`, `amdgpu_vcn.c`, `amdgpu_umsch_mm.c`), and Virtual KMS (`amdgpu_vkms.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `amdgpu_vm_bo_{map,replace_map,clear_mappings}` must call `amdgpu_vm_verify_parameters` before computing `saddr + (size - 1) / AMDGPU_GPU_PAGE_SIZE` or modifying `vm->va`. `amdgpu_vm_bo_clear_mappings` must collect overlapping mappings on a temp list while iterating `vm->va` before calling `amdgpu_vm_it_remove`, clamping `tmp->start`/`last` to `[saddr, eaddr]`, moving `tmp` to `vm->freed`, and inserting split `before`/`after` mappings. [F794, F811]
- In `amdgpu_vm_bo_{unmap,del}`, `bo_va->valids` mappings clear `mapping->bo_va` and move to `vm->freed`, while `bo_va->invalids` are freed via `amdgpu_vm_free_mapping`. `amdgpu_vm_bo_del()` (requiring `vm->root.bo` and `bo_va->base.bo` reserved) must clear `ttm_bo_set_bulk_move(&bo->tbo, NULL)` for always-valid BOs, subtract `-1` via `amdgpu_vm_update_stats()`, unlink `bo->vm_bo`, and reset XGMI pstate if `bo_va->is_xgmi`. [F750, F795]
- `amdgpu_vm_update_stats_locked` accounts `drm.shared`, `drm.private`, and `evicted` under `amdgpu_bo_mem_stats_placement(bo)`, and `drm.resident`/`drm.purgeable` under `res->mem_type` only when `res != NULL`. `amdgpu_vm_ready` must check `!vm->immediate.stopped && !vm->delayed.stopped` under their locks alongside `!vm->evicting` and `list_empty(&vm->evicted)`. [F793, F897]
- `amdgpu_vm_generation` casts `atomic_read(&adev->vram_lost_counter)` to `u64` before `<< 32` and adds only `lower_32_bits(vm->generation)` (plus 1 on `drm_sched_entity_error(&vm->delayed)`). When `amdgpu_vm_validate` sees `vm->generation != amdgpu_vm_generation(adev, vm)`, it must update `vm->generation`, call `amdgpu_vm_bo_reset_state_machine(vm)`, and reinit `vm->immediate` and `vm->delayed`. [F798, F843]
- In `amdgpu_vm_validate`, `amdgpu_vm_handle_moved`, and `amdgpu_userq_bo_validate`, every non-error iteration over `vm->{evicted,evicted_user,moved,invalidated}` must move `list_first_entry` to another status list before re-locking `vm->status_lock`. `amdgpu_vm_update_fault_cache` only overwrites `fault_info` when `status != 0`, and `amdgpu_vm_set_task_info` only copies `tgid`/`process_name` from `current->group_leader` when `current->group_leader->mm == current->mm`. [F800, F825, F905]
- In `amdgpu_userq_suspend`/`resume`, S0ix (`adev->in_s0ix`) pairs `amdgpu_userq_preempt_helper` with `amdgpu_userq_restore_helper`, while non-S0ix pairs `amdgpu_userq_unmap_helper` with `amdgpu_userq_map_helper`. While `adev->userq_halt_for_enforce_isolation` is true (under `adev->userq_mutex`), `amdgpu_userq_create` must not map `AMDGPU_HW_IP_GFX` or `AMDGPU_HW_IP_COMPUTE` queues. [F801, F802]
- Userq fence seqnos in `fence_drv->cpu_addr` and WPTR BO values must use `le64_to_cpu`/`cpu_to_le64`, and `amdgpu_userq_fence_{create,driver_process,signaled}` must use `dma_fence_init64` and 64-bit unsigned comparisons (`rptr < fence->seqno`, `rptr >= wptr`). [F816, F848]
- In `amdgpu_vcn_suspend`, skip `amdgpu_vcn_save_vcpu_bo_inst` if `amdgpu_ras_intr_triggered()` or `adev->pcie_reset_ctx.in_link_reset` is true so `saved_bo` stays `NULL`. `amdgpu_vcn_put_profile` must verify all `adev->vcn.num_vcn_inst` instances have `cur_state == AMD_PG_STATE_GATE` under `workload_profile_mutex` before disabling `PP_SMC_POWER_PROFILE_VIDEO`. In `amdgpu_vcn_{dump,print}_ip_state`, `reg_list[0]` is `mmUVD_POWER_STATUS`; read `reg_list[1..]` only if `(ip_dump[inst_off] & UVD_POWER_STATUS__UVD_POWER_STATUS_TILES_OFF) != UVD_POWER_STATUS__UVD_POWER_STATUS_TILES_OFF`. [F792, F806, F839]
- Keep `present_flag_0` at offset 0 of `amdgpu_{fw,vcn4_fw,vcn5_fw}_shared` for `amdgpu_vcn_fwlog_init()`, and `struct amdgpu_ring ring` at offset 0 of `struct amdgpu_umsch_mm` for `umsch_mm_ring_*` downcasts. When `amdgpu_vcnfw_log` is enabled, `fw_shared` is at `bo_size - fw_shared_size - AMDGPU_VCNFW_LOG_SIZE` (log at `fw_shared.cpu_addr + mem_size`); `amdgpu_{vcn,umsch}_fwlog` ring headers are at offset 0 so `rptr`/`wptr` init/wrap to `header_size` and debugfs reads reject `rptr`/`wptr` exceeding log size. [F819, F820, F859, F860]
- When `!adev->uvd.address_64_bit`, `amdgpu_uvd_create_msg_bo_helper()` and `amdgpu_uvd_cs_pass1()` must force BOs into the lower 256MB (`amdgpu_uvd_force_into_uvd_segment()`, `TTM_PL_FLAG_CONTIGUOUS`) and `amdgpu_uvd_cs_pass2()` must reject 256MB crossings; UVD IBs must run a 64B-aligned msg cmd (`cmd == 0`) first to set `ctx->has_msg_cmd` and `ctx->buf_sizes`. In `amdgpu_vce_ring_parse_cs{,_vm}`, `allocated` handles from `amdgpu_vce_validate_handle` require `created`, and exit must jump to `out:` to clear `destroyed` (success) or `allocated` (error) in `adev->vce.handles`. `amdgpu_virt_pre_reset` must call `amdgpu_virt_fini_data_exchange(adev)` before `PP_MP1_STATE_FLR`. [F804, F817, F876, F895]

## Synchronization
- Lock `adev->userq_mutex` before `userq_mgr->userq_mutex`, and hold `uqm->userq_mutex` when iterating `uqm->userq_idr` for `amdgpu_userq_{map,unmap,preempt,restore}_helper`. `amdgpu_userq_restore_worker` must call `flush_delayed_work(&fpriv->evf_mgr.suspend_work)` before locking `uq_mgr->userq_mutex`, and `amdgpu_userq_ensure_ev_fence` must unlock `uq_mgr->userq_mutex` before flushing `uq_mgr->resume_work` on `retry:` and return with it held. [F760, F761, F763, F834]
- Initialize `fence_drv` before storing in `adev->userq_xa` (`amdgpu_userq_fence_driver_alloc`), erase from `adev->userq_xa` before freeing `fence_drv->va` (`amdgpu_userq_fence_driver_destroy`), and use `GFP_ATOMIC` for `fence_drv_array` under `xa_lock(&userq->fence_drv_xa)` (`amdgpu_userq_fence_create`). Hold `vm->root.bo` reservation lock across `amdgpu_vm_bo_lookup_mapping()` and dereferencing `mapping` in `amdgpu_userq_fence_read_wptr()` and `amdgpu_userq_input_va_validate()`, unreserving on all paths. [F741, F756, F878]
- In `amdgpu_vm_handle_moved()`, if `ticket && dma_resv_locking_ctx(resv) == ticket`, leave `resv` locked (`unlock = false`), only unlocking if acquired via `dma_resv_trylock(resv)`. In `amdgpu_vm_lock_done_list`, re-lock `vm->status_lock` after `drm_exec_prepare_obj` before advancing `prev = prev->next`. Always use `amdgpu_vm_eviction_{lock,trylock,unlock}` for `vm->eviction_lock` to bracket `memalloc_noreclaim_{save,restore}`. [F751, F766, F767]
- `adev->vm_manager.pasids` uses `XA_FLAGS_LOCK_IRQ` and requires `xa_lock_irqsave`/`xa_store_irq`/`xa_erase_irq`; in `amdgpu_vm_handle_fault`, after dropping `xa_lock_irqsave` for `amdgpu_bo_reserve(root, true)`, re-lock and verify `xa_load(&adev->vm_manager.pasids, pasid)` still has `vm->root.bo == root`. In `amdgpu_vm_tlb_seq` (under `rcu_read_lock()`) and `amdgpu_vm_fini` (after `dma_fence_wait`), cycle `vm->last_tlb_flush->lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave` to serialize with `amdgpu_vm_tlb_seq_cb`. [F769, F770, F884]
- In `amdgpu_vm_update_range()`, locked updates (`!unlocked`) when `vm->last_unlocked` is unsignaled must attach `vm->last_unlocked` to `vm->root.bo` via `amdgpu_bo_fence()` and replace it with a stub fence (`amdgpu_vm_evictable()` rejects eviction while `vm->last_unlocked` is unsignaled); when `params.needs_flush` is true on a locked compute VM (`!params->unlocked && vm->is_compute_context`), `amdgpu_vm_tlb_flush()` must attach the TLB flush fence to `vm->root.bo->tbo.base.resv` (`DMA_RESV_USAGE_BOOKKEEP`). [F888, F889]
- In `amdgpu_vcn_ring_begin_use`/`end_use`, increment `vcn_inst->total_submission_cnt` (and `dpg_enc_submission_cnt` when `(adev->pg_flags & AMD_PG_SUPPORT_VCN_DPG) && !vcn_inst->using_unified_queue` and `ring->funcs->type == AMDGPU_RING_TYPE_VCN_ENC`) before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&vcn_inst->idle_work)` and ungating under `vcn_pg_lock`, paired with `atomic_dec` before `schedule_delayed_work` on end. `amdgpu_vcn_reset_engine` must hold `vinst->engine_reset_mutex` across `drm_sched_wqueue_stop` (`ring_dec` and all `ring_enc[i]`), `vinst->reset(vinst)`, ring tests, forcing fence completion, and `drm_sched_wqueue_start`. [F775, F776, F803]
- `amdgpu_virt_req_ras_{err_count,cper_dump}()` must take `down_read_trylock(&adev->reset_domain->sem)` outside `virt->ras.ras_telemetry_mutex`, and host calls populating `virt->fw_reserve.ras_telemetry` plus the subsequent read/copy must stay under `ras_telemetry_mutex`. In `amdgpu_vkms.c`, `amdgpu_vkms_vblank_simulate` must call `hrtimer_forward_now(&amdgpu_crtc->vblank_timer, output->period_ns)` before `drm_crtc_handle_vblank(crtc)`, `amdgpu_vkms_get_vblank_timestamp` subtracts `output->period_ns` from `READ_ONCE(amdgpu_crtc->vblank_timer.node.expires)`, `amdgpu_vkms_disable_vblank()` uses `hrtimer_try_to_cancel()`, and `amdgpu_vkms_sw_fini()` uses `hrtimer_cancel()`. [F772, F773, F891, F894]

## Object Lifetimes
- `amdgpu_userq_fence_create()` consumes `userq_fence` on success (freed via `call_rcu` in `amdgpu_userq_fence_release()`), requiring caller `kmem_cache_free(amdgpu_userq_fence_slab, userq_fence)` on error and `rcu_barrier()` in `amdgpu_userq_fence_slab_fini()`. Every `fence_drv` in `waitq->fence_drv_xa` holds a ref moved to `userq_fence->fence_drv_array` or put in `amdgpu_userq_fence_driver_free()`. `amdgpu_userq_get_doorbell_index()` pins/refs `db_obj->obj`, which must be unpinned/unreffed on post-pin error and in `amdgpu_userq_destroy()`. [F738, F739, F740, F742]
- In `amdgpu_vm_update_range()`, `amdgpu_vm_tlb_flush()` consumes `tlb_cb`, so set `tlb_cb = NULL` before `error_free: kfree(tlb_cb)`. `amdgpu_vkms_prepare_fb()` pins `rbo`, binds GART (unpinning at `error_unpin`), unreserves, and refs `rbo`, balanced in `amdgpu_vkms_cleanup_fb()` by reserving, unpinning, unreserving, and unreffing `rbo`. When `!adev->vcn.per_inst_fw`, `amdgpu_vcn_sw_fini()` must only `amdgpu_ucode_release(&adev->vcn.inst[0].fw)` and set `inst[i].fw = NULL`. In `amdgpu_{vcn,uvd}_resume()`, set `saved_bo = NULL` after `kvfree()`. `amdgpu_virt_release_ras_err_handler_data()` must call `amdgpu_virt_ras_release_bp()` on `data->bps_bo[0 .. data->last_reserved - 1]` before freeing arrays and resetting `ras_init_done = false` and `virt_eh_data = NULL`. [F744, F745, F752, F754, F755]

## API Contracts
- `amdgpu_bo_va_mapping->start`/`last` are inclusive GPU PFNs (`addr >> AMDGPU_GPU_PAGE_SHIFT`, size `last - start + 1` pages): `amdgpu_vm_bo_lookup_mapping` (requiring a hole-masked PFN) and `amdgpu_vm_update_range` take GPU page numbers, whereas `amdgpu_vm_bo_{map,replace_map,unmap,clear_mappings}` take byte `saddr`/`offset`/`size`. `amdgpu_vm_bo_add` allows `bo == NULL`, so check `bo != NULL` (or `amdgpu_vm_is_bo_always_valid`) before dereferencing. `amdgpu_vm_get_task_info_{vm,pasid}` can return `NULL` or a kref'd pointer that must be NULL-checked before `amdgpu_vm_print_task_info` and released via `amdgpu_vm_put_task_info()`. [F748, F787, F789, F790, F810]
- In `amdgpu_vm_bo_update()` and `amdgpu_vm_clear_freed()`, unmapping (`clear`/freed) syncs to `vm->root.bo->tbo.base.resv` (`AMDGPU_SYNC_EQ_OWNER`), whereas mapping (`!clear && bo`) syncs to `bo->tbo.base.resv` (`AMDGPU_SYNC_EXPLICIT`). `amdgpu_vm_clear_freed()` leaves `*fence` unchanged when `vm->freed` is empty or on error (putting intermediate `f`), only replacing `*fence` when all iterations succeed and `f != NULL`. [F753, F890]
- `amdgpu_userq_get_doorbell_index` returns `uint64_t` (`0` is valid; errors are `(uint64_t)-EINVAL`). `to_amdgpu_userq_fence` returns `NULL` for non-userq fences. `amdgpu_vcn_dec_{send,sw_send}_msg()` always free `ib_msg`, while `job` is consumed by `amdgpu_job_submit_direct()` on success and freed via `amdgpu_job_free(job)` on submit error. In `amdgpu_vcn_unified_ring_ib_header`/`checksum`, `ib_pack_in_dw` includes the 4-dword engine info header plus payload, and checksum runs after filling payload at `*ib_checksum + 2`. `amdgpu_vce_{validate_bo,cs_reloc}` take `lo` before `hi` despite VCE packets storing `hi` at `idx + N` and `lo` at `idx + N + 1`. [F746, F777, F779, F783, F821]

## Error Handling
- In `amdgpu_userq_fence_driver_alloc()`, `amdgpu_seq64_alloc()` failure jumps to `free_fence_drv`, whereas `__xa_store()` failure jumps to `free_seq64`. In `amdgpu_userq_input_va_validate` and `amdgpu_userq_create`, set negative `r` before jumping to `out_err`/`unlock`, and if `pm_runtime_get_sync()` in `amdgpu_userq_create` fails, still call `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()`. In `amdgpu_userq_{signal,wait}_ioctl`, size `u32` handle `memdup_user()` calls with `size_mul(sizeof(u32), count)` and jump past that array's `kfree` label on `IS_ERR`. In `amdgpu_userq_{restore_all,evict_all,suspend,resume}` and enforce-isolation loops, latch errors via `if (r) ret = r;` without breaking early. [F737, F778, F780, F833, F849, F873]

## External Contracts
- `amdgpu_userq_input_args_validate` must enforce `ip_type < AMDGPU_HW_IP_NUM`, reject non-zero creation fields on `AMDGPU_USERQ_OP_FREE`, and reject unknown `flags` on `AMDGPU_USERQ_OP_CREATE`; `amdgpu_userq_create` must check `amdgpu_userq_priority_permit(filp, priority)`. In `amdgpu_userq_wait_ioctl`, when `wait_info->num_fences == 0`, only return the count without dereferencing `out_fences`; when non-zero, check `num_fences >= wait_info->num_fences` before every `fences[num_fences++]` write. [F812, F813, F815, F904]
- Zero-init buffers before `amd_sriov_msg_checksum` and pass the current checksum field. Gate non-forced SR-IOV RAS telemetry queries by `__ratelimit`, and in readers (`amdgpu_virt_cache_host_error_counts`, `amdgpu_virt_write_cpers_to_ring`, `amdgpu_virt_cache_chk_criti_hit`) verify `used_size <= (AMD_SRIOV_RAS_TELEMETRY_SIZE_KB << 10)`, `kmemdup` `host_telemetry->body`, and validate `amd_sriov_msg_checksum`. In `amdgpu_virt_write_cpers_to_ring`, if `cper_dump->wptr < adev->virt.ras.cper_rptr`, reset `cper_rptr = cper_dump->wptr` and return; otherwise sync `cper_rptr = cper_dump->wptr` after copying and advance `entry` via `(char *)entry + entry->record_length`. [F785, F809, F822, F856, F907]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `amdgpu_vm_bo_invalidate`, `if (evicted && amdgpu_vm_is_bo_always_valid(vm, bo))` must precede `if (bo_base->moved) continue;`. In `amdgpu_vm_flush`, when `amdgpu_vmid_had_gpu_reset(adev, id)` is true, set `gds_switch_needed`, `vm_flush_needed`, `pasid_mapping_needed`, and `spm_update_needed` to `true` before `&=` capability masks and sync `id->current_gpu_reset_count` to `atomic_read(&adev->gpu_reset_counter)` under `id_mgr->lock`. Do not call `amdgpu_vm_it_remove`/`rb_erase` inside `rbtree_postorder_for_each_entry_safe` in `amdgpu_vm_fini`, and check `&mapping->list == &bo_va->{valids,invalids}` after `list_for_each_entry` in `amdgpu_vm_bo_unmap`. In `amdgpu_vm_bo_update`, use reassigned `bo` for XGMI VRAM BOs, and in `amdgpu_vm_adjust_size`, cast `vm_size` to `uint64_t` before `<< 18` and use `DIV_ROUND_UP(fls64(tmp) - 1, 9)`. [F807, F826, F827, F829, F830, F831, F844]
- In `WREG32_SOC{15,24}_DPG_MODE` (`indirect == true`) and `WREG32_SOC15_UMSCH` (`AMDGPU_FW_LOAD_PSP`), reset `dpg_sram_curr_addr`/`cmd_buf_curr_ptr` before recording and flush via PSP (`WREG32_SOC15_UMSCH` shifts `reg_offset << 2` for PSP cmd buf vs unshifted for `WREG32()`). Check `amdgpu_virt_get_rlcg_reg_access_flag` return value before `amdgpu_virt_rlcg_reg_rw()`, which must hold `spin_lock_irqsave(&adev->virt.rlcg_reg_lock, flags)` and scale dword offsets by 4 for `adev->rmmio` but keep `offset` unscaled in `writel(offset | flag, scratch_reg1)`. [F771, F786, F852, F855, F870]
- Guard VCPU BO `memcpy_{from,to}io` in VCN/UVD/VCE suspend/resume with `drm_dev_enter`/`exit`, and keep `indirect_sram = false` on Vangogh BIOS `"F7A0113"`/`"F7A0114"`. In `amdgpu_vce_cs_reloc`, reset `index == 0xffffffff` to `0`, use `((uint64_t)size) * ((uint64_t)index)`, and subtract it back after `amdgpu_cs_find_mapping`; in `amdgpu_virt_update_sriov_video_codec`, check `max_width > 0` before dividing for both `encode` and `decode`. [F846, F847, F867, F871]
