# INTEL ETHERNET DRIVERS — drivers/net/ethernet/intel (part 7)

This component comprises the Intel 82576/I350 Virtual Function driver (`igbvf`) and the hardware abstraction, diagnostics, register dump, ethtool, LED, and MAC/NVM/I225 layers of the Intel I225/I226 2.5GbE driver (`igc`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `struct igbvf_buffer`, `mapped_as_page` selects `skb_frag_dma_map()` vs `dma_map_single()` on Tx, while `adapter->rx_ps_hdr_size` selects `dma_unmap_single()` length and `page_dma` usage on Rx. In `igbvf_change_mtu()`, `igbvf_down()` must precede mutating `rx_buffer_len`, and `igbvf_setup_srrctl()` must update `rx_ps_hdr_size` before `igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers()`. [F545, F563]
- In `igbvf_clean_rx_irq()`, split-header length `hlen` must be clamped to `adapter->rx_ps_hdr_size` before `skb_put(skb, hlen)`. When `E1000_RXD_STAT_EOP` is clear, `next_buffer`'s unused `skb` and `dma` must move back to `buffer_info` while the partial `skb` is placed in `next_buffer->skb` with `next_buffer->dma = 0` before `next_desc`. [F583, F591]
- On timeout, `e1000_poll_for_msg()` and `e1000_poll_for_ack()` set `hw->mbx.timeout = 0`, failing subsequent `e1000_write_posted_mbx()` calls and making `e1000_check_for_link_vf()` request a reset until `e1000_reset_hw_vf()` restores `E1000_VF_MBX_INIT_TIMEOUT`. [F559]
- In `igbvf`, `igbvf_vlan_rx_add_vid()` and `igbvf_vlan_rx_kill_vid()` must only modify `adapter->active_vlans` after `hw->mac.ops.set_vfta()` succeeds, and `igbvf_configure()` must replay all set bits via `igbvf_restore_vlan()`. [F560]
- `igbvf_update_stats()` must not read VF counters when `adapter->link_speed == 0`, `test_bit(__IGBVF_RESETTING, &adapter->state)`, or `pci_channel_offline(pdev)`. Because 32-bit VF counters do not clear on read, `igbvf_initialize_last_counter_stats()` sets `last_<stat>` and `base_<stat>` at probe, `UPDATE_VF_COUNTER` accumulates wraps, and `igbvf_get_ethtool_stats()` subtracts `base_stat_offset`. [F561, F643]
- Fields in `igbvf_gstrings_stats[]`, `igc_gstrings_stats[]`, and `igc_gstrings_net_stats[]` must be 32-bit or 64-bit (with matching current/base widths in `igbvf`). `IGC_STATS_LEN`, `igc_ethtool_get_strings()`, and `igc_ethtool_get_stats()` must stay in lockstep, emitting `IGC_TX_QUEUE_STATS_LEN` (3, summing `restart_queue` + `restart_queue2`) per Tx queue and `IGC_RX_QUEUE_STATS_LEN` (5) per Rx queue. [F572, F615]
- In `igc_rx_fifo_flush_base()`, when `IGC_MANC_RCV_TCO_EN` is set, save and restore `IGC_RXDCTL(0..3)`, `IGC_RLPML`, `IGC_RCTL`, and `IGC_RFCTL` around the flush, and read `IGC_ROC`, `IGC_RNBC`, and `IGC_MPC` afterward to clear flush error counts. [F568]
- In `igc_mac.c`, `igc_config_fc_after_link_up()` must only mutate `hw->fc.current_mode` (never `requested_mode`) so `igc_setup_link()` can restore `current_mode = requested_mode` on reconnect. When decoding `IGC_STATUS` speed in `igc_get_speed_and_duplex_copper()` and `igc_ethtool_get_link_ksettings()`, check `IGC_STATUS_SPEED_2500` before or inside the `IGC_STATUS_SPEED_1000` branch (which is set for both 1G and 2.5G). [F571, F595]
- In `igc_led_hw_control_is_supported()`, the combined TX+RX check (`(flags & BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_TX)) && (flags & BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_RX)) && !(flags & ~IGC_ACTIVITY_MODES)`) must precede `flags & IGC_ACTIVITY_MODES` and `flags & (flags - 1)`. [F596]
- `TXD_USE_COUNT(S)` in `igc.h` must use `DIV_ROUND_UP(S, IGC_MAX_DATA_PER_TXD)`. In `igbvf_configure_tx()`, clear `E1000_DCA_TXCTRL_TX_WB_RO_EN` in `DCA_TXCTRL(0)` to prevent out-of-order Tx write-backs. [F603, F634]
- `reg_test[]` in `igc_diag.c` and `igc_reg_info_tbl[]` in `igc_dump.c` must end with a zeroed sentinel (`{ 0, 0, 0, 0 }` and `{}`). `IGC_PTM_SHORT_CYC_DEFAULT` in `igc_defines.h` must be at least 4 us. [F630, F631]

## Synchronization
- In `igc_ethtool.c`, hold `adapter->nfc_rule_lock` across `igc_get_nfc_rule()`, `igc_ethtool_check_nfc_rule()`, `igc_add_nfc_rule()`, `igc_del_nfc_rule()`, and any use of the pointer returned by `igc_get_nfc_rule()`. In `igc_ethtool_add_nfc_rule()`, `rule` ownership transfers to `nfc_rule_list` only when `igc_add_nfc_rule()` returns 0; on failure, `kfree(rule)` after unlocking `nfc_rule_lock`. [F507, F508]
- In `igc_i225.c`, `igc_get_hw_semaphore_i225()` must call `igc_put_hw_semaphore()` if `IGC_SWSM_SWESMBI` times out, and `igc_acquire_swfw_sync_i225()` / `igc_release_swfw_sync_i225()` must call `igc_put_hw_semaphore(hw)` before sleeping or returning. [F516]
- While holding `hw->nvm.ops.acquire(hw)`, `igc_validate_nvm_checksum_i225()` and `igc_update_nvm_checksum_i225()` must use raw `igc_read_nvm_eerd()` and `igc_write_nvm_srwr()` (not `hw->nvm.ops.read`/`write`, which re-acquire the non-recursive semaphore) and release on all exits. `igc_read_nvm_srrd_i225()` and `igc_write_nvm_srwr_i225()` must cycle `acquire`/`release` in bursts of at most `IGC_EERD_EEWR_MAX_COUNT` words. [F517, F535]
- In `igc_led_setup()` and `igc_setup_ldev()`, initialize `adapter->led_mutex`, `ldev->netdev`, and `ldev->index` before `led_classdev_register()`. In `igc_led_set()` and `igc_led_get()`, serialize `IGC_LEDCTL` with `adapter->led_mutex` inside `pm_runtime_get_sync()` / `pm_runtime_put()`. [F523, F530]
- In `igc_ethtool_get_stats()`, hold `adapter->stats64_lock` across `igc_update_stats()` and copying `adapter->stats`/`stats64`, and read `ring->tx_stats` / `rx_stats` inside `u64_stats_fetch_begin()` / `u64_stats_fetch_retry()` loops on `tx_syncp`, `tx_syncp2`, and `rx_syncp`. [F529]
- In `igbvf_tx_queue_adv()`, execute `wmb()` after populating `tx_desc->read.*` and before setting `buffer_info[first].next_to_watch = tx_desc` and writing `tx_ring->tail`, paired with `smp_rmb()` in `igbvf_clean_tx_irq()` after reading non-NULL `eop_desc`. In `igbvf_maybe_stop_tx()`, execute `smp_mb()` after `netif_stop_queue()` before rechecking `igbvf_desc_unused()`, paired with `smp_mb()` in `igbvf_clean_tx_irq()` between updating `next_to_clean` and checking `netif_queue_stopped()`. [F536, F538]
- All `igbvf` PF-VF mailbox callers (`e1000_write_mbx_vf`, `e1000_read_mbx_vf`, `hw->mac.ops` callbacks) must hold `spin_lock_bh(&hw->mbx_lock)`. `igbvf_set_multi`, `igbvf_set_uni`, and `igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers` run in atomic/softirq context (`GFP_ATOMIC`, `udelay` only), and `igbvf_put_txbuf` runs in hardirq via `igbvf_intr_msix_tx` so it must use `dev_kfree_skb_any`. [F644, F645, F646]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `igbvf_down()` and `igbvf_remove()`, set `__IGBVF_DOWN` before `timer_delete_sync(&adapter->watchdog_timer)` and `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->watchdog_task)` so `watchdog_timer` is not re-armed. In `igbvf_remove()`, `timer_delete_sync(&adapter->watchdog_timer)`, `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->reset_task)`, `cancel_work_sync(&adapter->watchdog_task)`, and `unregister_netdev()` must complete before `netif_napi_del(&adapter->rx_ring->napi)`, which must precede `kfree(adapter->rx_ring)`. [F510, F525]
- In `igbvf_open()`, `igbvf_setup_tx_resources()`, `igbvf_setup_rx_resources()`, and `igbvf_configure()` must complete before `igbvf_request_irq()`, freeing Rx then Tx resources and calling `igbvf_reset()` if IRQ request fails. [F511]
- In `igbvf_set_ringparam()` and `igc_ethtool_set_ringparam()`, do not free `adapter->tx_ring` or `rx_ring` structs when live; allocate into `temp_ring` first, freeing old resources and copying `temp_ring` only on success, and in `igc` update `adapter->tx_ring_count` and `rx_ring_count` in lockstep with `ring->count`. [F512, F557]
- Every path unmapping or freeing a `struct igbvf_buffer` (`igbvf_alloc_rx_buffers`, `igbvf_clean_rx_irq`, `igbvf_clean_rx_ring`, `igbvf_put_txbuf`) must zero `skb`, `page`, `dma`, and `page_dma`. After `skb_fill_page_desc()` in `igbvf_clean_rx_irq()`, either set `buffer_info->page = NULL` (if `rx_buffer_len > PAGE_SIZE / 2` or `page_count(page) != 1`) or call `get_page(buffer_info->page)`. [F514, F520]
- On DMA error in `igbvf_tx_map_adv()`, clear the failed `buffer_info` and unmap prior fragments via `igbvf_put_txbuf()` without freeing `skb` (wrapping index `i` from 0 to `tx_ring->count` before decrementing), and in `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` free `skb`, clear `buffer_info[first].time_stamp`, and rewind `next_to_use` to `first`. [F515, F585]
- In `igc_led_setup()`, if `igc_setup_ldev()` fails at `i`, unregister LEDs `i - 1` down to 0 and free `leds`. Only call `igc_led_free()` when `adapter->leds_available` is true, as it unconditionally dereferences all `IGC_NUM_LEDS` entries. [F518, F544]
- In `igc_ethtool_set_mm()`, only call `static_branch_inc`/`dec(&igc_fpe_enabled)` when `fpe->mmsv.pmac_enabled != cmd->pmac_enabled`, checked before `ethtool_mmsv_set_mm()`. In `igc_ethtool_diag_test()`, clear `__IGC_TESTING` before calling `igc_open(netdev)`. [F519, F521]
- Call `hw->phy.ops.reset(hw)` before `igc_get_phy_id(hw)` in `igc_init_phy_params_base()`, and call `igc_clear_hw_cntrs_base(hw)` after `igc_setup_link(hw)` in `igc_init_hw_base()`. [F624, F625]

## API Contracts
- In `igbvf`, read `V2PMAILBOX(0)` only via `e1000_read_v2p_mailbox()` and `e1000_check_for_bit_vf()` to latch R2C bits into `v2p_mailbox`. `e1000_check_for_bit_vf()`, `check_for_msg_vf()`, `check_for_ack_vf()`, and `check_for_rst_vf()` return 0 (`E1000_SUCCESS`) when the bit is set and `-E1000_ERR_MBX` when clear; callers must negate the return value. `e1000_check_for_link_vf()` returns 0 even when link is down and non-zero only to trigger reset; check `!hw->mac.get_link_status` or `E1000_STATUS_LU` for link state. [F539, F540, F558]
- `hw->mbx.ops.read`, `write`, `read_posted`, and `write_posted` take `size` in 32-bit `u32` words (max `E1000_VFMAILBOX_SIZE = 16`). `e1000_update_mc_addr_list_vf()` and `igc_update_mc_addr_list()` take a packed `ETH_ALEN`-byte array and address count, and `e1000_update_mc_addr_list_vf()` must cap `hash_list` (`&msgbuf[1]`) at 30 `u16` hashes. [F553, F610, F611]
- On flashless I225/I226 (`!igc_get_flash_presence_i225(hw)`), `hw->nvm.ops.write`, `validate`, and `update` are NULL; check before calling. `hw->nvm.ops.read()` and `write()` take 16-bit word offsets/counts (requiring byte callers to shift by 1, swap endianness, and RMW odd bytes), and `igc_write_nvm_srwr()` must reject `offset >= nvm->word_size`, `words > (nvm->word_size - offset)`, or `words == 0`. [F543, F550, F575]
- Pass unshifted 16-bit software masks to `igc_acquire_swfw_sync_i225()` and `igc_release_swfw_sync_i225()` (which shift `mask << 16` internally). In `igc_power_down_phy_copper_base()`, only call `igc_power_down_phy_copper(hw)` when both `igc_enable_mng_pass_thru(hw)` and `igc_check_reset_block(hw)` are false. [F554, F642]
- In `igbvf_set_ringparam()` and `igc_ethtool_set_ringparam()`, clamp descriptor counts to `[MIN, MAX]` and align to 8 (`REQ_TX_DESCRIPTOR_MULTIPLE` / `REQ_RX_DESCRIPTOR_MULTIPLE`). [F573]
- In `igbvf_poll()`, when `work_done < budget`, only call `igbvf_set_itr()` and write `EIMS` if `napi_complete_done(napi, work_done)` returns true and `__IGBVF_DOWN` is clear. In `igbvf_probe()`, add `NETIF_F_HW_VLAN_CTAG_FILTER`/`RX`/`TX` to `netdev->features` only after initializing `netdev->vlan_features`. [F660, F666]
- In `igbvf_tso()`, `skb_cow_head(skb, 0)` must succeed before modifying headers, and a negative return requires `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` to `dev_kfree_skb_any(skb)` and return `NETDEV_TX_OK`. `igbvf_features_check()` must clear checksum/TSO if `skb_network_offset(skb) > 127` or network header length `> 511`, and clear `NETIF_F_TSO` on encapsulated SKBs without `NETIF_F_TSO_MANGLEID`. [F661, F662]
- In `igbvf_tx_csum()`, verify `skb_csum_is_sctp(skb)` before setting `E1000_ADVTXD_TUCMD_L4T_SCTP` and fall back to `skb_checksum_help(skb)` for unknown offsets; on `no_csum` fallback it still emits a context descriptor if `IGBVF_TX_FLAGS_VLAN` is set, so `igbvf_xmit_frame_ring_adv()` must recheck `skb->ip_summed == CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` before setting `IGBVF_TX_FLAGS_CSUM`. In `igbvf_rx_checksum_adv()`, set `CHECKSUM_UNNECESSARY` only when `IXSM`, `RX_CSUM_DISABLED`, `TCPE`, and `IPE` are clear and `TCPCS` or `UDPCS` is set. [F593, F663, F664]

## Error Handling
- `igbvf_set_interrupt_capability()` sets `adapter->msix_entries = NULL` on failure without returning an error, so all MSI-X paths (`igbvf_request_irq`, `igbvf_free_irq`, `igbvf_irq_disable`, `igbvf_up`) must guard `adapter->msix_entries` for NULL. [F542]
- Zero-initialize (`kzalloc`) `struct igc_nfc_rule` before `igc_ethtool_init_nfc_rule()` and `igc_ethtool_check_nfc_rule()` (which uses `memcmp()`), and zero `u32 msgbuf[3]` with `memset()` in `e1000_rar_set_vf()` and `e1000_set_uc_addr_vf()` before copying the 6-byte MAC. [F547, F548]
- In `igc_init_nvm_params_base()`, check `rd32(IGC_EECD)` for `!(~eecd)` and return `-ENXIO` immediately if PCIe access failed, and cap shift exponent `size` at 15 before assigning `BIT(size)` to `u16 nvm->word_size`. [F604, F626]

## External Contracts
- In `igc_ethtool_get_nfc_rules()`, check `cnt == cmd->rule_cnt` before writing `rule_locs[cnt]` and return `-EMSGSIZE` if full. In `igc_ethtool_add_nfc_rule()`, reject `fsp->ring_cookie >= adapter->num_rx_queues` or `fsp->location >= IGC_MAX_RXNFC_RULES` with `-EINVAL`, and under `nfc_rule_lock` delete any existing rule at `fsp->location` via `igc_del_nfc_rule()` before `igc_add_nfc_rule()`. In `igc_ethtool_init_nfc_rule()`, set `IGC_FILTER_FLAG_DEFAULT_QUEUE` only when `match_flags == 0`, `flow_type == ETHER_FLOW` (no `FLOW_EXT`), and MAC/EtherType masks are zero. [F577, F578, F671]
- `igc_ethtool_set_rxfh()` must verify all `IGC_RETA_SIZE` (128) `rxfh->indir[]` entries are `< adapter->rss_queues`. In `igc_ethtool_get_regs()` and `igbvf_get_regs()`, `memset()` the buffer over `get_regs_len`, and in `igc` read `IGC_EICS`/`IGC_ICS` (not clear-on-read `EICR`/`ICR`) while keeping duplicate `IGC_RAL`/`IGC_RAH` at `regs_buff[172..203]`. [F579, F669]
- Reject `igc_ethtool_set_channels()` with `-EINVAL` when `adapter->strict_priority_enable` is set. In `igc_ethtool_set_coalesce()`, when `IGC_FLAG_QUEUE_PAIRS` is set, reject `tx_coalesce_usecs` changes with `-EINVAL` and mirror `rx_itr_setting` into `tx_itr_setting`; clear `IGC_FLAG_DMAC` when `rx_coalesce_usecs == 0`; and update `itr_val` and `set_itr = 1` on all `q_vector`s. [F569, F674]

## Known Pitfalls
- Program `TDLEN(0)` and `RDLEN(0)` in `igbvf_configure_tx()`/`rx()` with `ring->count * sizeof(descriptor)`, not page-aligned `ring->size`. After clearing `E1000_TXDCTL_QUEUE_ENABLE` or `E1000_RXDCTL_QUEUE_ENABLE` in `igbvf_configure_tx()`, `igbvf_configure_rx()`, or `igbvf_down()`, call `e1e_flush()` and `msleep(10)` before touching rings. [F602, F633]
- In `igbvf_receive_skb()`, when `IGBVF_FLAG_RX_LB_VLAN_BSWAP`, `E1000_RXD_STAT_VP`, and `E1000_RXDEXT_STATERR_LB` are set (`e1000_vfadapt_i350`), decode VLAN with `be16_to_cpu()` instead of `le16_to_cpu()`, and always mask with `E1000_RXD_SPC_VLAN_MASK` (0x0FFF) before indexing `adapter->active_vlans`. [F617, F629]
- In `igc_rar_set()`, follow both `wr32(IGC_RAL(index), rar_low)` and `wr32(IGC_RAH(index), rar_high)` with `wrfl()` to prevent bridge burst write-combining. [F632]
