# NVM EXPRESS DRIVER — (top level)

NVMe host transport drivers for PCIe (`pci.c`), TCP/TLS (`tcp.c`), and RDMA (`rdma.c`), plus sysfs (`sysfs.c`), Persistent Reservations (`pr.c`), ZNS (`zns.c`), tracepoints (`trace.c`), and headers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `nvme_dev`, `nvme_tcp_ctrl`, and `nvme_rdma_ctrl`, `queues[0]` is the admin queue and `queues[1..n]` are I/O queues, whereas I/O `blk_mq_tag_set` indices (`hctx_idx`, `tags[]`, `mq_map[]`) are 0-based (offset by 1). [F274]
- Async Event Requests (`nvme_is_aen_req` on QID 0 or `nvme_tcp_async_req`) have no `struct request`; check them before calling `nvme_find_rq` or `blk_mq_rq_from_pdu`. In `rdma.c`, `ctrl->async_event_sqe` must use `cqe.done = nvme_rdma_async_done`, never `nvme_rdma_send_done`. [F240, F322]
- Reused blk-mq PDUs must be reset per I/O: `nvme_prep_rq` zeros `iod->flags`, `nr_descriptors`, `total_len`, and `meta_total_len`; `nvme_tcp_setup_cmd_pdu` resets `req->status = cpu_to_le16(NVME_SC_SUCCESS)` and send counters. [F243]
- When `nvme_pci_setup_data_prp` fills a PRP page (`i == NVME_CTRL_PAGE_SIZE >> 3`) with data left, move `old_prp_list[i - 1]` to `prp_list[0]` of the new page, store `cpu_to_le64(prp_list_dma)` in `old_prp_list[i - 1]`, and set `i = 1`. When `iod->nr_descriptors > 1`, `nvme_free_descriptors` must read `next_dma_addr` from `prp_list[last_prp]` before `dma_pool_free`. [F199, F300]
- In `nvme_pci_setup_meta_sgls`, when `entries > 1`, `sg_list[0]` holds the segment descriptor and data descriptors are at `sg_list[1..i]`, so `max_integrity_segments` is `NVME_MAX_META_SEGS` (`(NVME_SMALL_POOL_SIZE / sizeof(struct nvme_sgl_desc)) - 1`) and `nvme_unmap_metadata` passes `&sge[1]` to `nvme_free_sgls`. When metadata SGLs are supported, `NVME_REQ_USERCMD` forces SGL mode (`nvme_pci_use_sgls`, `nvme_pci_metadata_use_sgls`) even when `entries == 1`. [F279, F280]
- If `nvme_admin_dbbuf` fails in `nvme_dbbuf_set`, call `nvme_dbbuf_free` on online I/O queues (`1..dev->online_queues`) alongside `nvme_dbbuf_dma_free` to NULL per-queue `dbbuf_*` pointers. When `nvme_dbbuf_dma_alloc` reuses `dev->dbbuf_dbs`, it must zero `dbbuf_dbs` and `dbbuf_eis` (`nvme_dbbuf_size(dev)` bytes each). [F250, F251]
- `nvme_pci_alloc_dev` allocates `nvme_dev` with `struct_size(dev, descriptor_pools, nr_node_ids)`; `nvme_setup_descriptor_pools` uses 512-byte alignment for `.small` when `NVME_QUIRK_DMAPOOL_ALIGN_512` is set. `nvme_setup_io_queues` samples `write_queues` and `poll_queues` into `dev->nr_write_queues`/`nr_poll_queues` once per reset for `nvme_setup_irqs` and `nvme_calc_irq_sets`. [F255, F315, F339]
- In `nvme_map_cmb`, check `offset > bar_size` before `bar_size - offset`. In `nvme_alloc_host_mem`, check `dma_merge_boundary != 0` before testing `(PAGE_SIZE & dma_merge_boundary) == 0`. [F286, F290]
- `nvme_tcp_set_queue_io_cpu` picks the online CPU with `mq_map[cpu] == qid - 1` and lowest `nvme_tcp_cpu_queues[cpu]`, incrementing it and setting `NVME_TCP_Q_IO_CPU_SET`; `nvme_tcp_stop_queue_nowait` decrements `nvme_tcp_cpu_queues[queue->io_cpu]` when `test_and_clear_bit(NVME_TCP_Q_IO_CPU_SET, &queue->flags)` succeeds. [F247, F248]
- `nvme_tcp_init_recv_ctx` sets `pdu_remaining = sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_rsp_pdu) + hdgst`, `pdu_offset = 0`, `data_remaining = -1`, `ddgst_remaining = 0`; `nvme_tcp_recv_state` must check `pdu_remaining` and `ddgst_remaining` before `NVME_TCP_RECV_DATA`. `nvme_tcp_recv_pdu` must verify `hdr->hlen == sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_rsp_pdu)` before digest checks, and call `nvme_tcp_init_recv_ctx` before `nvme_tcp_rsp`/`nvme_tcp_r2t` but not in `nvme_tcp_c2h_data` (where `nvme_tcp_recv_data`/`nvme_tcp_recv_ddgst` resets it after payload). [F258, F266, F292, F296]
- In `nvme_tcp_recv_data`, bound copies by `queue->data_remaining` and `iov_iter_count(&req->iter)`; when `iov_iter_count` hits 0, check `req->curr_bio = req->curr_bio->bi_next` is non-NULL (else `-EIO`) before `nvme_tcp_init_iter`. `nvme_tcp_req_data_pdu` places `nvme_tcp_data_pdu` at `req->pdu + sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_cmd_pdu) - sizeof(struct nvme_tcp_data_pdu)`. [F270, F281]
- `nvme_rdma_alloc_tag_set`, `nvme_rdma_init_request`, and `nvme_rdma_dma_map_req` must keep PDU layout in lockstep: `data_sgl` SGs at `req + 1`, `metadata_sgl` after `NVME_RDMA_DATA_SGL_SIZE`, and metadata SGs at `req->metadata_sgl + 1`. Check `wc->byte_len >= sizeof(struct nvme_completion)` in `nvme_rdma_recv_done` and `rej_data_len >= sizeof(u16)` in `nvme_rdma_conn_rejected`. [F262, F273]
- In `nvme_pr_read_keys` and `nvme_pr_read_reservation`, bound loops by the allocated count, read `((struct nvme_reservation_status *)rse)->regctl_ds[i]` when `!eds`, and `kfree(rse)` before `goto get_num_regs` if `num_regs != get_unaligned_le16(&rse->regctl)`. [F284, F303]

## Synchronization

- Non-IRQ callers of `nvme_poll_cq` (`nvme_poll`, `nvme_poll_irqdisable`, `nvme_reap_pending_cqes`) must hold `nvmeq->cq_poll_lock`, bracketed with `disable_irq`/`enable_irq` in `nvme_poll_irqdisable`. `nvme_reap_pending_cqes` runs only after PCI disable and IRQ free, before `nvme_cancel_tagset`. After `nvme_cqe_pending` matches phase, `dma_rmb()` must precede reading CQE fields in `nvme_handle_cqe`. [F211, F212, F330]
- Serialize `nvmeq->sq_cmds`, `sq_tail`, `last_sq_tail`, `nvme_sq_copy_cmd`, and `nvme_write_sq_db` under `nvmeq->sq_lock`. Per-I/O allocations in `nvme_prep_rq` must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. In `nvme_dbbuf_update_and_check_event`, put `wmb()` before writing `*dbbuf_db`, `mb()` before reading `*dbbuf_ei`, and cast `(new_idx - event_idx - 1)` and `(new_idx - old)` to `u16` in `nvme_dbbuf_need_event`. [F213, F214, F313, F352]
- Complete `nvme_init_queue` before `queue_request_irq`, and set `NVMEQ_ENABLED` only after `queue_request_irq` succeeds. In `nvme_pci_update_nr_queues`, `mutex_trylock(&dev->shutdown_lock)` and verify `dev->online_queues > 0` before `blk_mq_update_nr_hw_queues` and `nvme_free_queues`. Drop `shutdown_lock` across sync admin commands in `nvme_setup_io_queues`/`nvme_create_queue` and re-acquire via `nvme_setup_io_queues_trylock`. [F217, F218, F327]
- `nvme_pci_subsystem_reset` must hold `shutdown_lock`, check `bar_mapped_size != 0` before writing `NVME_REG_NSSR`, and flush with `readl(dev->bar + NVME_REG_CSTS)` after setting `NVME_CTRL_LIVE`. Transition `dev->ctrl` to `NVME_CTRL_DELETING` before `nvme_dev_disable` on `nvme_reset_work` failure or PCI disconnect in `nvme_timeout`. Always unquiesce admin/I/O queues after canceling requests and before removing tagsets on shutdown/teardown. [F347, F358, F380]
- In `tcp.c`, `req_list` uses `llist_add`, while draining to `send_list`, updating `queue->request`, and `nvme_tcp_try_send` require `send_mutex`. `nvme_tcp_fetch_request` calls `list_del_init` and `init_llist_node`, and `nvme_tcp_process_req_list` uses `list_add` only when `send_list` is empty to reverse LIFO to FIFO. In `nvme_tcp_try_send_data`, snapshot `data_len`, `h2cdata_left`, and `data_sent` before `sock_sendmsg` and call `nvme_tcp_advance_req` only if `req_data_sent + ret < req_data_len`. [F221, F299, F305]
- Protect `sk_user_data` and `sk_*` callbacks with `write_lock_bh`/`read_lock_bh` on `sk_callback_lock`; in `__nvme_tcp_stop_queue`, call `kernel_sock_shutdown` and `nvme_tcp_restore_sock_ops` before `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)`. `nvme_rdma_stop_queue` must hold `queue_lock` across `test_and_clear_bit(NVME_RDMA_Q_LIVE)` and `__nvme_rdma_stop_queue`. `nvme_rdma_find_get_device` must hold `device_list_mutex` and use `nvme_rdma_dev_get` (`kref_get_unless_zero`). Hold `srcu_read_lock(&head->srcu)` across `nvme_find_path(head)` and sync command submission. [F226, F227, F228, F333, F355]

## Object Lifetimes

- Add `ctrl->list` in `nvme_rdma_create_ctrl`/`nvme_tcp_create_ctrl` only after `setup_ctrl` succeeds, and call `nvmf_free_options` in `free_ctrl` only if `!list_empty(&ctrl->list)`. Allocate `iod->dma_vecs` only when `!dma_use_iova(&iod->dma_state) && dma_need_unmap(dma_dev)`. Restore `dev->ctrl.abort_limit` via `atomic_inc` if `atomic_dec_return < 0`, `blk_mq_alloc_request` fails, or in `abort_endio` (with `blk_mq_free_request`). [F195, F197, F208]
- `nvme_free_host_mem` passes `dev->host_mem_descs_size` to `dma_free_coherent`, while `nvme_free_host_mem_multi` iterates `dev->nr_host_mem_descs`. `nvme_tcp_free_admin_queue` must cancel `async_event_work`, call `nvme_tcp_free_async_req`, and NULL `async_req.pdu` before `nvme_tcp_free_queue(ctrl, 0)`; `nvme_rdma_destroy_admin_queue` must NULL `async_event_sqe.data`. [F202, F314]
- In `nvme_tcp_alloc_queue`, `sock_alloc_file` consumes `queue->sock` on error (do not release it); after success, release via `fput(queue->sock->file)` and NULL `queue->sock`. Follow `nvme_tcp_stop_queue_nowait` (which clears `tls_enabled = false`) with `nvme_tcp_wait_queue`. Call `nvme_tcp_key_revoke_needed`/`nvme_auth_revoke_tls_key` before `nvme_tcp_teardown_admin_queue` or `nvme_tcp_teardown_ctrl` (which clears `ctrl->tls_pskid = 0`). [F205, F206, F223, F260]
- When `nvme_rdma_process_nvme_rsp` posts `nvme_rdma_inv_rkey`, return without calling `nvme_rdma_end_request(req)` so `nvme_rdma_inv_rkey_done` drops the second ref. In `nvme_ctrl_dhchap_*_secret_store`, free unchanged secrets without replacing the key, and free old keys via `nvme_auth_free_key` only after swapping under `ctrl->dhchap_auth_mutex`. [F204, F209]

## API Contracts

- `nvme_try_complete_req` takes wire `__le16` status (`cpu_to_le16(status << 1)` in `nvme_tcp_end_request`) and stores a 15-bit status in `nvme_req(req)->status`, so `nvme_handle_cqe` must pass `nvme_req(req)->status != NVME_SC_SUCCESS` to `blk_mq_add_to_batch`. Look up wire `command_id`s via `nvme_find_rq`, using `nvme_cid_to_rq` only in `nvme_tcp_recv_data`/`ddgst` after `nvme_tcp_handle_c2h_data`. [F231, F244, F271]
- Call `nvme_pci_avg_seg_size` only when `blk_rq_nr_phys_segments(req) > 0`, and return `BLK_STS_AGAIN` from `nvme_pci_setup_data_simple` when `is_pci_p2pdma_page(bv.bv_page)`. On reconnect (`!new`) in RDMA/TCP `configure_io_queues`, start only `min(tagset->nr_hw_queues + 1, ctrl->queue_count)` queues before `blk_mq_update_nr_hw_queues`. In `nvme_tcp_timeout`/`nvme_rdma_timeout`, if state `!= NVME_CTRL_LIVE`, stop the queue, call `nvmf_complete_timed_out_request`, and return `BLK_EH_DONE`. [F310, F334, F367, F382]
- Configure TCP sockets with `sk_allocation = GFP_ATOMIC`, `sk_use_task_frag = false`, `sk_set_memalloc`, and `nvme_tcp_reclassify_socket`; run `nvme_tcp_try_send` under `memalloc_noreclaim_save`, clear `MSG_SPLICE_PAGES` when `!sendpages_ok`, and return `false` from `nvme_tcp_queue_more` when `nvme_tcp_queue_tls(queue)`. Sync `req->sqe.dma` for CPU before `nvme_setup_cmd`/`nvme_rdma_map_data` and for device before `nvme_rdma_post_send`. Destroy RDMA QPs via `ib_destroy_qp(queue->qp)` after MR pools, and free CQs via `ib_free_cq` (poll) or `ib_cq_pool_put`. [F264, F338, F375, F376]
- Convert between 512B block sectors and ZNS LBAs via `nvme_lba_to_sect`/`nvme_sect_to_lba`, validate `is_power_of_2(zi->zone_size)` in `nvme_query_zone_info`, and `break` if `!nz` in `nvme_ns_report_zones`. In `trace.c`, save `trace_seq_buffer_ptr(p)` and append `trace_seq_putc(p, 0)` before returning. [F245, F285, F304, F326]

## Error Handling

- On failure after initial admin queue setup in `nvme_tcp_setup_ctrl`/`nvme_rdma_setup_ctrl` (including the 2nd `nvme_tcp_configure_admin_queue` for concat), jump to `destroy_admin` and pass `new` to `teardown_admin_queue`. Call `nvme_unquiesce_io_queues` after `nvme_dev_disable(dev, false)` only if `nvme_try_sched_reset` returns non-zero. Populate `dptr`/`sg_list[0]` before calling `nvme_unmap_data`/`nvme_unmap_metadata` on `iter.status` failure. [F200, F230, F242]
- Convert positive NVMe status codes (`ret > 0`) to `-EIO` in `nvme_tcp_create_ctrl`, `nvme_rdma_create_ctrl`, and `nvme_ns_report_zones`, but normalize `ret >= 0` to `0` in `nvme_create_io_queues`. In `nvme_rdma_map_sg_fr`, treat `nr < count` from `ib_map_mr_sg` as failure, put `req->mr` back into `rdma_mrs`, and set `req->mr = NULL`. Gate `nvme_identify_ns` with `__ratelimit(&head->rs_nuse)` in `ns*_update_nuse`, and call `device_remove_file_self` before `nvme_delete_ctrl_sync` in `nvme_sysfs_delete`. [F233, F234, F236, F238, F387]
- In `nvme_tcp_init_connection`, use `MSG_WAITALL`, check `ret < 0` before `ret < sizeof(*icresp)` (`-ECONNRESET`), validate `plen == sizeof(*icresp)` and `maxh2cdata` alignment/`>= NVME_TCP_MIN_MAXH2CDATA`, and check `tls_get_record_type == TLS_RECORD_TYPE_DATA` when TLS is enabled. Negate `status` into `queue->tls_err` in `nvme_tcp_tls_done` and call `tls_handshake_cancel` on timeout (`0` -> `-ETIMEDOUT`) or signal (`<= 0`) in `nvme_tcp_start_tls`. [F232, F237, F268, F377]

## External Contracts

- Create I/O CQs before SQs and delete all I/O SQs before CQs; in `__nvme_delete_io_queues`, do not reset `nr_queues` or `sent` at `retry:`. Set `dev->subsystem` and write `NVME_CSTS_NSSRO` only when `NVME_REG_VS >= NVME_VS(1, 1, 0)` and `NVME_CAP_NSSRC(cap)`. Return `0` after `nvme_tcp_start_queue(ctrl, 0)` in `nvme_tcp_configure_admin_queue` when `ctrl->opts->concat && !ctrl->tls_pskid`. [F302, F329, F332, F340]
- Dispatch `nvme_tcp_c2h_term` in `nvme_tcp_recv_pdu` before digest checks, bound `plen` and `fes`, and use `NVME_TCP_FES_DATA_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` for `0x05`. In `nvme_rdma_route_resolved`, `hrqsize` is 1-based, `hsqsize` is 0-based, and `cntlid` is set only when `qid != 0`. In `pr.c`, pass `< 0` errnos through, retry `nvme_pr_resv_report` with `cdw11 = 0` on `NVME_SC_HOST_ID_INCONSIST`, and mask with `NVME_SCT_SC_MASK` in `nvme_status_to_pr_err` (without mapping `NVME_SC_CMD_SIZE_LIM_EXCEEDED` as unsupported). In `sysfs.c`, fall back to `ids->nguid` when `uuid_is_null(&ids->uuid)` and include `dummy_attr` + `nvme_disk_is_ns_head` check for `nvme_ns_mpath_attr_group`. [F246, F267, F288, F293, F294, F372, F385]

## Known Pitfalls

- Use `put_unaligned_le24`/`le32` and `get_unaligned_le16` for `nvme_keyed_sgl_desc` (`length`, `key`) and `nvme_reservation_status*` (`regctl`). `nvme_pci_setup_meta_sgls` overwrites `cmd.common.flags = NVME_CMD_SGL_METASEG`, so check data SGLs via `nvme_pci_cmd_use_sgl` and meta SGLs via `nvme_pci_cmd_use_meta_sgl`. Keep `nvme_tcp_try_send`'s sequential `if` chain and `if (!nvme_tcp_has_inline_data(req)) goto out;` after `NVME_TCP_SEND_CMD_PDU`. [F283, F291, F297]
- Use `1ULL << (12 + 4 * szu)` in `nvme_cmb_size_unit`, `u64` casts in `nvme_setup_host_mem`, `div_u64`/`do_div` in `nvme_cmb_qdepth`/`nvme_alloc_host_mem_multi`, `DIV_ROUND_UP` in `nvme_pci_setup_data_prp`, and `max_fr_pages + 1` in `nvme_rdma_create_queue_ib`. Use `SQ_SIZE(nvmeq)` and `sq_tail << sqes` (copying `sizeof(*cmd)`) for `sq_cmds`; index `u32 *` doorbells by `db_stride` directly (`8 * db_stride` for byte sizes). Hold `shutdown_lock` with `adminq` IRQ disabled across `nvme_remap_bar` and update `adminq->q_db = dev->dbs`. [F307, F308, F311, F312, F316, F317, F348, F369]
- Call `nvme_update_attrs` only after `nvme_pci_enable`, `nvme_init_ctrl_finish`, and `nvme_setup_host_mem`; sample `was_suspend` before `nvme_dev_disable`/`nvme_pci_enable`. Gate `acpi_storage_d3` with `!noacpi && !(quirks & NVME_QUIRK_FORCE_NO_SIMPLE_SUSPEND)` after `check_vendor_combination_bug`, and clear `PCI_IRQ_MSI` when `NVME_QUIRK_BROKEN_MSI`. In `nvme_suspend`, freeze/sync queues, disable HMB, and `pci_save_state` before `nvme_set_power_state` (clearing saved state if `ret > 0`), and always `nvme_unfreeze`. [F331, F342, F343, F350, F351]
