# Hyper-V/Azure CORE AND DRIVERS — drivers (part 2)

High-performance paravirtualized networking (`hv_netvsc`) and virtual PCIe root bus controller (`pci-hyperv`) drivers for Hyper-V guests. The `hv_netvsc` driver implements the Network Virtual Service Consumer (NVSP) and RNDIS protocols, multi-queue VRSS sub-channels, XDP/BPF support, and transparent SR-IOV VF bonding/failover. The `pci-hyperv` driver exposes pass-through PCIe/SR-IOV devices as synthetic root PCI buses, managing MMIO config space access, BAR resource probing, and MSI/MSI-X IRQ domains mapped via hypervisor interrupt remapping.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `netvsc_send_pkt()` must explicitly initialize `packet->dma_range = NULL` before checking `packet->page_buf_cnt`, because `netvsc_xmit()` places `struct hv_netvsc_packet` in `skb->cb` without zeroing it and `netvsc_send()` sets `packet->page_buf_cnt = 0` when copying packets to the send buffer. [F242]
- In `struct nvsc_rsc`, the `vlan`, `csum_info`, and `hash_info` fields retain stale values across packets and must only be read when their corresponding bit (`NVSC_RSC_VLAN`, `NVSC_RSC_CSUM_INFO`, or `NVSC_RSC_HASH_INFO`) is set in `nvchan->rsc.ppi_flags` by `rsc_add_data()`. [F244]
- In `netvsc_switch_datapath()`, `net_device_ctx->data_path_is_vf` must be cleared to `false` before sending `NVSP_MSG4_TYPE_SWITCH_DATA_PATH` when switching away from the VF (`!vf`), and set to `true` only after `vmbus_sendpacket()` and `wait_for_completion(&nv_dev->channel_init_wait)` succeed when switching to the VF. [F251]
- When `netvsc_vf_changed()` switches the datapath via `netvsc_switch_datapath()`, it must update `ndev`'s TSO maximum size in lockstep via `netif_set_tso_max_size()`—setting it to `vf_netdev->tso_max_size` when switching to the VF and restoring `netvsc_dev->netvsc_gso_max_size` when switching back to the synthetic path. [F252]
- When `netvsc_register_vf()` bonds `vf_netdev` to `ndev`, it must raise `ndev->needed_headroom` to at least `vf_netdev->needed_headroom`, sync `vf_netdev->wanted_features = ndev->features`, and propagate `netvsc_xdp_get(netvsc_dev)` via `netvsc_vf_setxdp()`, while `netvsc_unregister_vf()` must reset `ndev->needed_headroom` to `RNDIS_AND_PPI_SIZE`. [F253]
- When `rndis_set_subchannel()` succeeds, it must update `nvdev->num_chn`, `ndev_ctx->tx_table`, `netif_set_real_num_tx_queues()`, and `netif_set_real_num_rx_queues()` in lockstep; on failure, callers (`netvsc_subchan_work`, `netvsc_attach`) must reset `nvdev->max_chn = 1` and `nvdev->num_chn = 1`. [F257]
- `rndis_netdev_set_hwcaps()` must reset `net->hw_features &= ~NETVSC_SUPPORTED_HW_FEATURES` and `net_device_ctx->tx_checksum_mask = 0` before recomputing both from `hwcaps`, mask `net->features` with `~NETVSC_SUPPORTED_HW_FEATURES | net->hw_features`, and update `nvdev->netvsc_gso_max_size` and `netif_set_tso_max_size()` in lockstep. [F258]
- `hv_send_resources_allocated()` and `hv_send_resources_released()` must maintain `hbus->wslot_res_allocated` as the highest allocated child slot (down to `-1` when all are released), and `hv_pci_enter_d0()` must set `hbus->wslot_res_allocated = 255` before calling `hv_pci_bus_exit(hdev, true)` on retry. [F259]

## Synchronization

- In `netvsc_probe()`, `rtnl_lock()` must be acquired before `rndis_filter_device_add()` and held continuously through scheduling `nvdev->subchan_work`, calling `register_netdevice()`, and adding `net_device_ctx->list` to `netvsc_dev_list` (with `rndis_filter_device_remove()` called before `rtnl_unlock()` on failure). [F216]
- `netvsc_vf_setxdp()` must use lockless `netif_xdp_propagate()` rather than `dev_xdp_propagate()`, and any caller invoking `netvsc_register_vf()` outside the `NETDEV_REGISTER` notifier (such as `netvsc_probe()`) must bracket the call with `netdev_lock_ops(vf_netdev)` and `netdev_unlock_ops(vf_netdev)`. [F217]
- Before a matching VF `net_device` is exposed via `NETDEV_REGISTER`, `netvsc_prepare_bonding()` must set `IFF_SLAVE` and `IFF_NO_ADDRCONF` during `NETDEV_POST_INIT` (or prior to `netvsc_register_vf()` in `netvsc_probe()`), and `get_netvsc_byslot()` must match `ndev->perm_addr` against both `vf_netdev->perm_addr` and `vf_netdev->dev_addr`. [F218]
- In `netvsc_device_add()` and `netvsc_sc_open()`, `nvchan->channel` must be assigned and `nvchan->napi` must be enabled (`napi_enable()` and `netif_queue_set_napi()`) before `vmbus_open()` is called, and `rcu_assign_pointer(net_device_ctx->nvdev, net_device)` must occur only after `netvsc_connect_vsp()` completes. [F219]
- Workqueue handlers that acquire `rtnl_lock` (`netvsc_subchan_work()`, `netvsc_vf_setup()`, `netvsc_vfns_work()`, and `netvsc_link_change()`) must use `rtnl_trylock()` and reschedule on contention, and must re-validate `nvdev`, `vf_netdev`, or `ndev` after acquiring the lock. [F220]
- In `rndis_filter_halt_device()`, setting `nvdev->destroy = true` must be ordered by `wmb()` before `wait_event(nvdev->wait_drain, netvsc_device_idle(nvdev))` waits for all channels' `queue_sends` and `mrc` rings to drain, and `rndis_filter_close_device()` must call `cancel_work_sync(&dev->mcast_work)` before clearing the packet filter. [F221]
- `netvsc_device_remove()` must clear `net_device_ctx->nvdev` with `RCU_INIT_POINTER()`, disable and delete all channel NAPI instances (`napi_disable()` and `netif_napi_del()`), and call `vmbus_close(device->channel)` before deferring `netvsc_device` deallocation via `free_netvsc_device_rcu()`. [F222]
- `netvsc_tx_enable()` and `netvsc_tx_disable()` must issue `virt_wmb()` after updating `nvscdev->tx_disable` and before calling `netif_tx_wake_all_queues()` or `netif_tx_disable()` so that concurrent TX completion and send paths observe the updated `tx_disable` flag before checking queue stop state. [F223]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `free_netvsc_device()`, `nvdev->recv_buf` and `nvdev->send_buf` must only be passed to `vfree()` when `!nvdev->recv_buf_gpadl_handle.decrypted` and `!nvdev->send_buf_gpadl_handle.decrypted` respectively. [F198]
- In `netvsc_device_remove()`, `netvsc_revoke_recv_buf()` and `netvsc_revoke_send_buf()` must run before `vmbus_close()`, whereas `netvsc_teardown_recv_gpadl()` and `netvsc_teardown_send_gpadl()` must run before `vmbus_close()` when `vmbus_proto_version < VERSION_WIN10` and after `vmbus_close()` when `>= VERSION_WIN10`. [F199]
- In `netvsc_device_remove()`, `napi_disable()` and `netif_queue_set_napi(..., NULL)` must only be called for channel indices `i < ndev->real_num_rx_queues`, whereas `netif_napi_del()` must be called for all `i < net_device->num_chn`. [F200]
- DMA bounce mappings allocated by `netvsc_dma_map()` into `packet->dma_range` are unmapped and freed by `netvsc_send_pkt()` if `vmbus_sendpacket_mpb_desc()` fails, by `netvsc_send_tx_complete()` for data SKBs, and by `rndis_filter_receive_response()` for RNDIS control requests (`skb == NULL`). [F202]
- `netvsc_xdp_set()` adds `nvdev->num_chn - 1` references to a non-NULL `prog` (consuming the caller's reference for channel 0) and drops `nvdev->num_chn` references from `old_prog`, so callers reusing `dev_info->bprog` (such as `netvsc_attach()`) must call `bpf_prog_inc(prog)` before `netvsc_xdp_set()` and `bpf_prog_put(prog)` if it fails. [F204]
- `new_pcichild_device()` must initialize `hpdev->desc` and acquire two initial references (`refcount_set(&hpdev->refs, 1)` plus `get_pcichild(hpdev)`) before inserting `hpdev` into `hbus->children` under `hbus->device_list_lock`, and `get_pcichild_wslot()` must increment `hpdev->refs` before dropping `hbus->device_list_lock`. [F232]
- Because `new_pcichild_device()` takes two references on `hpdev`, removing a child from `hbus->children` in `pci_devices_present_work()`, `hv_pci_bus_exit()`, or `hv_eject_device_work()` requires two `put_pcichild(hpdev)` calls (plus a third in `hv_eject_device_work()` for `hv_pci_eject_device()`). [F350]
- In `hv_pci_probe()` and `hv_pci_remove()`, only `hbus->bridge` is devres-managed (`devm_pci_alloc_host_bridge()`); `hbus->irq_domain`, `hbus->fwnode`, `hbus->cfg_addr`, MMIO windows, `hbus->wq`, the domain bit (`hv_put_dom_num()`), and `hbus` must be manually freed on probe error and remove. [F351]

## API Contracts

- `netvsc_send()` consumes previously batched `msdp->skb` packets (via `dev_consume_skb_any()` when coalescing into `send_buf` or `dev_kfree_skb_any(msd_skb)` on `netvsc_send_pkt()` failure), but leaves the current `skb` unfreed when returning a non-zero error to `netvsc_xmit()`. [F201]
- `hv_pci_start_relations_work()` takes ownership of `struct hv_dr_state *dr` (adding it to `hbus->dr_list` to be freed by `pci_devices_present_work()`) only when it returns `0`; on non-zero return, the caller (`hv_pci_devices_present()` or `hv_pci_devices_present2()`) must `kfree(dr)`. [F211]
- In `netvsc_drv_init()`, `netvsc_ring_bytes` must be computed from the `ring_size` module parameter by multiplying by `4096` (4 KB hypervisor pages) and aligning with `VMBUS_RING_SIZE()`, never by multiplying by `PAGE_SIZE`. [F234]
- Any path that tears down and recreates `netvsc_device` via `netvsc_detach()` and `netvsc_attach()` must snapshot `nvdev` configuration (`num_chn`, send/recv sections, `rss_key`, and `bprog`) via `netvsc_devinfo_get(nvdev)` before `netvsc_detach()` and release it with `netvsc_devinfo_put()`. [F256]
- `hv_pci_allocate_bridge_windows()` must set `IORESOURCE_WINDOW` and clear `IORESOURCE_BUSY` on `low_mmio_res`/`high_mmio_res` before `pci_add_resource()`, `hv_pci_free_bridge_windows()` must restore `IORESOURCE_BUSY` before `vmbus_free_mmio()`, and `hv_allocate_config_window()` must set `IORESOURCE_BUSY` on `hbus->mem_config`. [F346]
- In `netvsc_dma_map()`, each `struct hv_page_buffer` entry in `pb` must be individually mapped with `dma_map_single()` rather than `dma_map_sg()` because middle entries can have non-zero `offset` and sub-page `len` and are not physically contiguous. [F354]
- `netvsc_probe()` must advertise `NETDEV_XDP_ACT_BASIC | NETDEV_XDP_ACT_REDIRECT | NETDEV_XDP_ACT_NDO_XMIT` in `net->xdp_features` to match the XDP actions handled in `netvsc_run_xdp()` and the `.ndo_xdp_xmit` callback in `device_ops`. [F372]

## Error Handling

- On ARM64, if `hv_pci_vec_irq_gic_domain_alloc()` fails at index `i` inside `hv_pci_vec_irq_domain_alloc()`, rollback via `hv_pci_vec_irq_free(domain, virq, nr_irqs, i)` must release the full `nr_irqs` bitmap region in `chip_data->spi_map` while only resetting and freeing the `i` parent-domain IRQs already allocated. [F215]
- `hv_pci_restore_msi_msg()` must verify that `pdev->msi_enabled` or `pdev->msix_enabled` is set before acquiring `msi_descs_lock` and iterating descriptors with `msi_for_each_desc()`. [F236]
- In `pci-hyperv.c`, `irq_data->chip_data` (`struct tran_int_desc *`) is only populated after `hv_compose_msi_msg()` succeeds, so `hv_irq_retarget_interrupt()` and `hv_msi_free()` must check `data->chip_data` for NULL before dereferencing it, and `hv_compose_msi_msg()` must zero `*msg` on every error path. [F237]
- `rndis_filter_receive()`, `rndis_filter_receive_data()`, and `netvsc_recv_callback()` return NVSP wire status codes `NVSP_STAT_SUCCESS` (1) or `NVSP_STAT_FAIL` (2)—never `0` or negative errno—because `netvsc_receive()` tests `ret != NVSP_STAT_SUCCESS` and forwards the status to the host in `enq_receive_complete()`. [F238]
- `netvsc_device_add()` and `rndis_filter_device_add()` return `ERR_PTR(-errno)` on failure rather than `NULL`, whereas `net_device_ctx->nvdev` is a nullable RCU pointer that is `NULL` when detached and must only be assigned a valid `struct netvsc_device *` inside `netvsc_device_add()`. [F239]
- In `hv_pci_assign_slots()`, if `pci_create_slot()` returns an `ERR_PTR`, `hpdev->pci_slot` must be reset to `NULL` so that teardown paths (`hv_pci_remove_slots()`, `hv_eject_device_work()`, `pci_devices_present_work()`, and `hv_pci_bus_exit()`) that test `if (hpdev->pci_slot)` do not pass an error pointer to `pci_destroy_slot()`. [F240]
- In `netvsc_send_tx_complete()`, `request_addr_callback()` returns `VMBUS_RQST_ERROR` on invalid transaction IDs while `0` (`skb == NULL`) is a valid RNDIS control packet completion that must still decrement `nvchan->queue_sends`. [F241]

## External Contracts

- In `struct ndis_offload_params`, a byte value of `0` (`NDIS_OFFLOAD_PARAMETERS_NO_CHANGE`) means no change, so callers of `rndis_filter_set_offload_params()` must zero-initialize the struct to leave other offloads unchanged and use explicit `*_DISABLED` constants (not `0`) to disable an offload. [F245]
- In `hv_write_config_block()`, `write_blk->byte_count` must be set to `len` (`1..HV_CONFIG_BLOCK_SIZE_MAX`), while the VMBus packet size `pkt_size` passed to `vmbus_sendpacket()` must include `sizeof(pkt.reserved)` (4 zeroed trailing bytes) beyond `offsetof(struct pci_write_block, bytes) + len`. [F248]
- `prepopulate_bars()` must clear `PCI_COMMAND_MEMORY` in `PCI_COMMAND` on every child device in `hbus->children` before writing BAR registers via `_hv_pcifront_write_config()`, and must leave `PCI_COMMAND_MEMORY` cleared after populating the BARs. [F267]
- `rndis_filter_set_rss_param()` must send `NDIS_RSS_PARAM_FLAG_DISABLE_RSS` via `rndis_set_rss_param_msg()` before sending the enable message (`flag = 0`), and `rndis_set_rss_param_msg()` must only update `rdev->rss_key` when `set_complete->status == RNDIS_STATUS_SUCCESS` and RSS is not being disabled. [F268]
- In `netvsc_linkstatus_callback()`, `indicate->status_buf_offset` and `indicate->status_buflen` for `RNDIS_STATUS_LINK_SPEED_CHANGE` must be validated against `data_buflen - RNDIS_HEADER_SIZE` rather than `resp->msg_len - RNDIS_HEADER_SIZE`. [F278]
- In `netvsc_send_table()`, when `nvscdev->nvsp_version <= NVSP_PROTOCOL_VERSION_6` and `msglen` is at least `sizeof(struct nvsp_message_header) + sizeof(union nvsp_6_message_uber) + count * sizeof(u32)`, `offset` must be overridden to `sizeof(struct nvsp_message_header) + sizeof(union nvsp_6_message_uber)` before bounds checking. [F279]
- When `nvdev->nvsp_version <= NVSP_PROTOCOL_VERSION_4`, `rndis_filter_set_offload_params()` must truncate the `OID_TCP_OFFLOAD_PARAMETERS` payload length and `offload_params->header.size` to `VERSION_4_OFFLOAD_SIZE` (22 bytes) and zero `udp_ip_v4_csum` and `udp_ip_v6_csum`. [F289]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `netvsc_probe()`, `ret` is overwritten inside the post-registration `for_each_netdev()` loop by `check_dev_is_matching_vf(vf_netdev)`, so the success path after `register_netdevice()` must explicitly `return 0` rather than `return ret`. [F250]
- In `netvsc_xmit()`, `skb_linearize()` and `skb_cow_head(skb, RNDIS_AND_PPI_SIZE)` must complete before computing `rndis_msg = (struct rndis_message *)skb->head`, and any `ETH_P_8021Q` `__skb_vlan_pop()` and length updates must complete before `init_page_array()` records `skb->data` and fragment PFNs in `pb`. [F261]
- Because incoming `data` in `rndis_filter_receive()` resides in the host-shared GPADL buffer `net_device->recv_buf`, `rndis_filter_receive()`, `rndis_filter_receive_data()`, and `rndis_get_ppi()` must copy the RNDIS header, `rndis_packet`, and PPIs into `nvchan->recv_buf` before validating and reading their fields. [F269]
- `struct rndis_device::link_state` uses inverted boolean polarity where `false` (0) means link up and `true` (1) means link down, so callers (`netvsc_open`, `netvsc_attach`, `netvsc_link_change`) must test `!rdev->link_state` for carrier-present state. [F291]
- Before passing `u64 bar_val` to `get_bar_size()`, `survey_child_resources()` and `prepopulate_bars()` must cast the upper 32-bit `probed_bar` word to `u64` before `<< 32` for 64-bit BARs (`PCI_BASE_ADDRESS_MEM_TYPE_64`), and must OR `0xffffffff00000000ULL` into `bar_val` for 32-bit BARs. [F309]
- In `prepopulate_bars()` and `hv_pci_allocate_bridge_windows()`, `hbus->low_mmio_space` and `hbus->high_mmio_space` must be checked non-zero before calling `__builtin_clzll()`, and the power-of-two shift `1ULL << (63 - __builtin_clzll(...))` must use a 64-bit unsigned `1ULL` left operand. [F310]
- In `netvsc_init_buf()`, the product of `net_device->recv_section_size` and `net_device->recv_section_cnt` (both `u32` values from the host `nvsp_1_message_send_receive_buffer_complete` message) must be widened to `u64` before comparing against `buf_size`. [F311]
