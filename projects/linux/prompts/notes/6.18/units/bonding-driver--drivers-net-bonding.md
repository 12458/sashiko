# BONDING DRIVER — drivers/net/bonding

The Linux Ethernet Channel Bonding driver aggregates multiple network interfaces (slaves) into a logical master virtual `net_device` (`bond%d`) across seven modes (balance-rr, active-backup, balance-xor, broadcast, 802.3ad LACP, balance-tlb, and balance-alb), managing slave enslavement/release, MII and ARP/NS link monitoring, hardware offloads (VLAN, IPsec, TLS, XDP), and configuration via rtnetlink, sysfs, ioctl, procfs, and debugfs.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `SLAVE_AD_INFO(slave)` is allocated only in `BOND_MODE_8023AD`, and `BOND_ALB_INFO(bond).tx_hashtbl` is allocated only while the bond is open (`bond_open()` to `bond_close()`); guard `SLAVE_AD_INFO` with a mode check and check `tx_hashtbl != NULL` in paths reachable while down (e.g. `__tlb_clear_slave()`). [F56, F58]
- `bond->params.arp_targets[]` and `ns_targets[]` are dense zero-terminated arrays; mutations to `arp_targets[]` must update each slave's `slave->target_last_arp_rx[]` at the same index in lockstep, and `bond_option_arp_ip_target_rem()` must compact `target_last_arp_rx` before `arp_targets` because the slave loop tests `targets[i+1]`. [F77, F98]
- In active-backup mode with `arp_validate`, solicited-node multicast MACs for `ns_targets` are programmed only on backup slaves (`!bond_is_active_slave(slave)`); call `bond_slave_ns_maddrs_add()` before `bond_select_active_slave()` in `bond_enslave()`, call `bond_slave_ns_maddrs_del()` after `bond_change_active_slave(bond, NULL)` in `__bond_release_one()`, and sync filters across role swaps and option changes. [F8, F78]
- In `bond_arp_rcv()` and `bond_na_rcv()`, when a backup slave validates a probe because `curr_active_slave` received traffic after `last_link_up`, pass `sip`/`tip` or `saddr`/`daddr` in reversed order to `bond_validate_arp()`/`bond_validate_na()`. `bond_na_rcv()` and `bond_validate_na()` must accept both `NDISC_NEIGHBOUR_SOLICITATION` and `NDISC_NEIGHBOUR_ADVERTISEMENT` and allow `daddr` matching `&in6addr_linklocal_allnodes` or `bond_has_this_ip6()`. [F114, F115]
- Because `0` is a valid bucket in 256-entry `tx_hashtbl` and `rx_hashtbl`, all index links must use `TLB_NULL_INDEX`/`RLB_NULL_INDEX` (`0xffffffff`), and callers of `tlb_choose_channel()` must mask `bond_xmit_hash()` with `& 0xFF`. When deleting entries in `__tlb_clear_slave()`, `rlb_purge_src_ip()`, or `rlb_clear_vlan()`, save `next`/`src_next`/`used_next` before `tlb_init_table_entry()` or `rlb_delete_table_entry()`. [F99, F101, F123]
- In `bond_alb.c`, `rx_hashtbl[i].src_first` heads entries whose `ip_src` hashes to `i`, independent of the client whose `ip_dst` hashes to `i`; `rlb_delete_table_entry()` must only call `rlb_init_table_entry_dst()` and `rlb_src_unlink()` without resetting `src_first`. `rlb_choose_channel()` hashes `arp->ip_dst` and matches `(ip_src, ip_dst)` on outbound ARPs, while `rlb_update_entry_from_arp()` hashes `arp->ip_src` and matches `(ip_dst, ip_src)` on inbound replies. [F100, F130]
- In `bond_alb_deinit_slave()`, reset `bond_info->rx_slave = NULL` before `rlb_clear_slave()`. In `rlb_req_update_slave_clients()` and `rlb_rebalance()`, set `bond_info->rx_ntt = 1` only after the `rx_hashtbl_used_head` loop finishes. In `rlb_arp_xmit()`, verify `bond_slave_has_mac_rcu(bond, arp->mac_src)` and reject if `ip_dev_find()` is `netif_is_any_bridge_master(dev)` before rewriting `arp->mac_src`. In `BOND_MODE_TLB`, save `slave->dev->dev_addr` before `dev_set_mac_address()` and restore via `dev_addr_set()`. [F87, F94, F125, F195]
- In 802.3ad mode, propagate `lacp_active`, `lacp_fast`, `ad_actor_sys_prio`, `ad_actor_system`, and `port_priority` changes via `bond_3ad_update_*()` under `spin_lock_bh(&bond->mode_lock)`. Gate periodic LACPDU TX in `ad_periodic_machine()` on `LACP_STATE_LACP_ACTIVITY` in `actor_oper_port_state` or `partner_oper.port_state` (not `params.lacp_active`), and do not set `LACP_STATE_LACP_ACTIVITY` on `partner_oper.port_state` in `AD_RX_EXPIRED`. [F80, F81]
- In `bond_3ad.c`, `__update_selected()` and `__record_pdu()` compare/copy `lacpdu->actor_*` against `port->partner_oper`, whereas `__choose_matched()` and `__update_ntt()` compare `lacpdu->partner_*` against `port->actor_*`. In `ad_agg_selection_test()`, `BOND_AD_PRIO` falls through to `BOND_AD_COUNT`, which falls through to `BOND_AD_STABLE`/`BOND_AD_BANDWIDTH`. Keep `sizeof(struct lacpdu) == sizeof(struct bond_marker)` (110 bytes) for `bond_3ad_lacpdu_recv()`. [F103, F120, F129]
- In `bond_miimon_inspect()`, initialize `ignore_updelay = false`, set it from `!rcu_dereference(bond->curr_active_slave)` in active-backup and `usable_slaves && !usable_slaves->count` in other modes, and clear it once the first `BOND_LINK_BACK` slave becomes `BOND_LINK_UP`; `BOND_LINK_UP` falls through to `BOND_LINK_FAIL` and `BOND_LINK_DOWN` to `BOND_LINK_BACK`. In `bond_miimon_commit()` and `bond_ab_arp_commit()`, defer `bond_select_active_slave()` via `do_failover = true` until `bond_for_each_slave` finishes. [F67, F117, F118, F119]
- Enabling `miimon` and `arp_interval` are mutually exclusive: enabling one must zero the other, cancel its delayed work if `IFF_UP`, and update `recv_probe` and `arp_validate`. In wrap-around slave loops (`bond_ab_arp_probe()` and `__rlb_next_rx_slave()`), set `found = true` on `curr_arp_slave`/`rx_slave` only at the end of the loop body after recording `before`. [F83, F126]
- Stacked bonds can have both `IFF_MASTER` and `IFF_SLAVE`: `bond_netdev_event()` must test them with separate `if` statements, `bond_enslave()` must reject `!netif_is_bond_master(slave_dev)` when `IFF_MASTER` is set, and slave release must clear `IFF_BONDING` only if `!netif_is_bond_master(slave_dev)`. In `bond_enslave()`, guard randomizing a duplicate MAC under `BOND_FOM_FOLLOW` with `bond_has_slaves(bond)`. In `bond_neigh_setup()`, check `if (parms->dev == dev)` before setting `parms->neigh_setup = bond_neigh_init`. [F116, F131, F149, F150]
- Keep slave selection and hashing in sync between SKB and XDP TX (`bond_xmit_roundrobin_slave_get` vs `bond_xdp_xmit_roundrobin_slave_get`, `bond_xmit_hash` vs `bond_xmit_hash_xdp`), including routing IPv4 IGMP to `curr_active_slave` in round-robin; only call `reciprocal_value()` when `packets_per_slave > 0` and `reciprocal_divide()` when `packets_per_slave > 1`. [F96, F137]
- `bond->send_peer_notif` must be `u32`, `peer_notif_delay` capped at `300000` ms, and `peer_notif_delay` clamped with `max(1, ...)` before modulo or multiplication. In `bond_fold_stats()`, when `((nv | ov) >> 32) == 0`, compute `delta = (s64)(s32)((u32)nv - (u32)ov)` and add only when `delta > 0`. MAC buffers passed to `bond_slave_has_mac[_rcu]`, `MAC_ADDRESS_EQUAL`, or `ether_addr_equal_64bits` need 2 bytes of trailing padding (`ETH_ALEN + 2`). Use `time_in_range()`/`time_after()`/`time_before()` for ARP/NS jiffies checks. [F132, F133, F134, F138, F147]

## Synchronization
- `bond_update_slave_arr()` requires RTNL held (pinning `bond->slave_cnt` for `struct_size` allocations) and `bond->mode_lock` not held; callers holding `mode_lock` must defer via `bond_slave_arr_work_rearm()`. If 802.3ad has no active aggregator, `bond_reset_slave_arr()` must clear `usable_slaves` and `all_slaves` to `NULL`. Any early exit before `bond_set_slave_arr()` must jump to `out:` to `kfree_rcu` local arrays and run `bond_skip_slave()`. [F21, F30, F76, F140]
- Hold `bond->mode_lock` for all 802.3ad `port`/`aggregator` mutations after `bond_3ad_bind_slave()` and all `tx_hashtbl`/`rx_hashtbl` accesses. `bond_alb.c` helpers calling `dev_set_mac_address()` or `dev_set_promiscuity()` must run under RTNL without `mode_lock` or `rcu_read_lock()`. In `bond_alb_handle_active_change()`, update `curr_active_slave` and call `tlb_clear_slave()` on `swap_slave` and `new_slave` before swapping/setting MACs. [F37, F38, F39, F40]
- In `bond_ipsec_add_sa_all()` and `bond_ipsec_del_sa_all()`, hold both `bond->ipsec_lock` and `spin_lock_bh(&ipsec->xs->lock)` while checking `xs->km.state != XFRM_STATE_DEAD`, updating `xs->xso.real_dev`, and calling `xdo_dev_state_delete`. `bond_ipsec_del_sa()` runs in atomic context under `xs->lock` and must not take `ipsec_lock` or mutate `ipsec_list` (deferred to `bond_ipsec_free_sa()`). [F26, F27]
- Use `rtnl_dereference()`/`bond_slave_get_rtnl()` in RTNL-only paths and `rcu_dereference_rtnl()` in dual RCU/RTNL helpers, never dereferencing a slave pointer after `rcu_read_unlock()` unless pinned with `netdev_hold()`/`dev_hold()` (as in `bond_ipsec_add_sa` and `bond_ethtool_get_ts_info`). Cache `READ_ONCE(bond->recv_probe)` in `bond_handle_frame()` and `rcu_dereference(tls_get_ctx(skb->sk)->netdev)` in `bond_tls_device_xmit()`. [F13, F46, F166, F167, F168]
- In `bond_get_stats()`, use `spin_lock_nested(&bond->stats_lock, bond_get_lowest_level_rcu(bond_dev))` inside `rcu_read_lock()`. Hold `netif_addr_lock_bh(bond->dev)` around `dev_uc_sync*()`/`dev_mc_sync*()` in RTNL context. When `__bond_release_one()` has `unregister == true`, restore MTU via `__netif_set_mtu()` under `netdev_lock_ops(slave_dev)`. Use `rtnl_trylock()` + `restart_syscall()` in `bond_opt_tryset_rtnl()`, and bracket `bond_compute_features()` on `NETDEV_FEAT_CHANGE` with `bond->notifier_ctx = true`/`false`. [F24, F41, F47, F179, F181]

## Object Lifetimes
- `bond_create_debugfs()` must run before `register_pernet_subsys()`/`bond_netlink_init()`, and `bond_destroy_debugfs()` after `bond_netlink_fini()`/`unregister_pernet_subsys()`. In `bond_net_ops`, remove sysfs in `pre_exit`, unregister bonds in `exit_rtnl`, and remove `/proc/net/bonding` in `exit_batch`; clear `bond->proc_entry = NULL` in `bond_remove_proc_entry()`. In `bond_newlink()`, run `register_netdevice()` and `bond_work_init_all()` before `bond_changelink()`, unwinding with `bond_work_cancel_all()` and `unregister_netdevice()` on error. In `bond_alb_initialize()`, call `tlb_deinitialize()` if `rlb_initialize()` fails. [F1, F2, F3, F10, F25]
- In `bond_enslave()`, set `bond_dev->dev_addr` before `bond_3ad_initialize()`, call `bond_3ad_initialize()` and `bond_3ad_bind_slave()` before `netdev_rx_handler_register()` and `bond_master_upper_dev_link()`, and call `bond_alb_init_slave()` after copying `perm_hwaddr` and before `bond_master_upper_dev_link()`. After `bond_kobj_init()`, always release via `kobject_put(&slave->kobj)`, where `slave_kobj_release()` calls `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&slave->notify_work)` before `kfree()`. [F36, F43, F72, F157, F160]
- In `__bond_release_one()` and `bond_enslave()` error unwind, `netdev_rx_handler_unregister()`, `bond_upper_dev_unlink()`, `bond_update_slave_arr()`, and clearing RCU slave pointers must precede `synchronize_rcu()`, which must precede `slave_disable_netpoll()`, `dev_close()`, and `kobject_put()`. In `__bond_release_one()` for TLB/ALB, call `bond_alb_deinit_slave()` after `bond_upper_dev_unlink()` and `bond_change_active_slave(bond, NULL)`, and before `bond_select_active_slave()`. [F7, F177]
- Balance every `block_netpoll_tx()` with `unblock_netpoll_tx()`. On per-slave loop failure in `bond_change_mtu`, `bond_set_mac_address`, `alb_set_mac_address`, `bond_vlan_rx_add_vid`, or `bond_xdp_set`, roll back preceding slaves up to `slave` without clobbering the error code. Pair `dev_xdp_propagate()` with `bpf_prog_inc(prog)` and balance `bpf_prog_put(old_prog)` and `bpf_master_redirect_enabled_key`. Only adjust `bond_bcast_neigh_enabled` while `IFF_UP` in `BOND_MODE_8023AD` (never in `bond_option_mode_set()`). [F17, F18, F19, F20, F176]
- In `bond_handle_frame()`, call `consume_skb(skb)` when `recv_probe` returns `RX_HANDLER_CONSUMED`. In `bond_xmit_broadcast()`, pass original `skb` only on `bond_is_last_slave()`, clone for others, and `dev_kfree_skb_any(skb)` if `!skb_used`. Pair `ip_dev_find()` with `dev_put()`, and `ip_route_output()`/`ip6_route_output()` with `ip_rt_put()`/`dst_release()`. [F14, F16, F22, F23]

## API Contracts
- `bond_verify_device_path()` requires `rcu_read_lock()`, allocates with `GFP_ATOMIC` terminated by `BOND_VLAN_PROTO_NONE`, and returns array, `NULL`, or `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)`; callers must check `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(tags)` and `kfree(tags)`, and `bond_handle_vlan()` must iterate until `BOND_VLAN_PROTO_NONE` (incrementing `tags++` on `!tags->vlan_id` `continue`). `bond_handle_vlan()` returns `false` only after `skb` was freed. [F15, F52, F106, F127, F180]
- In IPsec callbacks, check `!netif_is_bond_master(real_dev)` and non-NULL `xfrmdev_ops` callback, set `xs->xso.real_dev` only after `xdo_dev_state_add` succeeds, and clear it to `NULL` before delete/free. Always update `curr_active_slave` via `bond_change_active_slave()`. Verify `netif_is_bond_slave(dev)` and master match before `bond_slave_get_rtnl()` on arbitrary devices, and check `bond_slave_get_rtnl()`/`_rcu()` for `NULL`. Check both `slaves` and `READ_ONCE(slaves->count)` before `hash % count`. [F12, F59, F73, F107, F148, F162]
- `updelay`, `downdelay`, and `peer_notif_delay` are stored in `miimon` ticks (verify `miimon != 0` before `/` or `%`). `bond_update_speed_duplex()` returns positive `1` on failure. Convert `ns_targets` via `addrconf_addr_solict_mult()` before `ndisc_mc_map()`/`ndisc_send_skb()`. [F51, F68, F69, F135]
- Reject mode changes in `bond_option_mode_set()` if `xdp_prog` is set and `!bond_xdp_check()`. `bond_xdp_set_features()` must clear features if `!bond_xdp_check()` or `!bond_has_slaves()`, and mask out `NETDEV_XDP_ACT_XSK_ZEROCOPY`. `bond_xdp_xmit()` sends 1 frame at a time until error, returning negative errno only if `nxmit == 0`. [F84, F161, F190]
- In `bond_compute_features()`, pass `vlan_features` and `mpls_features` through `netdev_base_features()`, aggregate `gso_partial_features` with `NETIF_F_GSO_PARTIAL`, keep `NETIF_F_GSO_ENCAP_ALL` in `BOND_VLAN_FEATURES`, and advertise CTAG/STAG VLAN features in `bond_setup()`. Wrap slave `dev->type` changes in `NETDEV_PRE/POST_TYPE_CHANGE`, UC/MC flush, and `dev_close()`/`bond_ether_setup()`. Save/restore `qdisc_skb_cb(skb)->slave_dev_queue_mapping` in `bond_select_queue()`/`bond_dev_queue_xmit()`. [F92, F163, F184, F185, F186, F187, F194, F200]

## Error Handling
- In `bond_xdp_get_xmit_slave()`, do not use `WARN_ON_ONCE()` and rate-limit `netdev_err()` with `net_ratelimit()` for unsupported modes/policies. [F189]

## External Contracts
- In `bond_changelink()`, check `nla_len(attr)` before `nla_get_be32()`/`nla_get_in6_addr()` on nested ARP/NS targets, and update `bond_get_size()`, `bond_get_slave_size()`, or `bond_3ad_stats_size()` for any new netlink attributes. Gate mutating ioctls with `ns_capable(net->user_ns, CAP_NET_ADMIN)` and 802.3ad actor/partner info in netlink/procfs with `capable(CAP_NET_ADMIN)`. [F111, F112, F197, F198]

## Known Pitfalls
- Re-anchor `data` and re-derive header pointers after any `pskb_may_pull()`, `pskb_network_may_pull()`, or `bond_pull_data()`. In `bond_handle_frame()`, write `skb_share_check()` back to `*pskb` and call `skb_cow_head()` before mutating `eth_hdr(skb)->h_dest`. On TX, pass `mhoff = 0` to `__bond_xmit_hash()`, call `skb_reset_mac_header()` before `eth_hdr()`, and use `skb_mac_header_len(skb)` with `skb_header_pointer()` (vs offset `0` on RX). In `bond_arp_rcv()`, check `ar_hln == bond->dev->addr_len` and `ar_pln == 4` and `memcpy()` `sip`/`tip`. [F85, F86, F108, F109, F144, F191]
- In `bond_opts[]`: keep `unsuppmodes` as `unsigned long` of `BIT(BOND_MODE_*)`; end `bond_opt_value` tables with `{ NULL, -1, 0 }` and names `<= 32` chars; pass `&slave_dev` to `bond_opt_slave_initval()`; pass writable strings to `bond_opt_initstr()`; check `strlen() >= 1` before `+ 1` in `BOND_OPTFLAG_RAWVAL` setters and size `+ifname` buffers `IFNAMSIZ + 1` (`"%16s"`); and pass raw memory bytes to `BOND_OPT_AD_ACTOR_SYSTEM` `newval.value`. [F70, F71, F97, F105, F142, F152, F153, F154]
- Zero-initialize `fl6` in `bond_ns_send_all()` (leaving `flowi6_oif` unset) and `parms` in `bond_neigh_init()`. Cache `old_flags = bond_dev->flags` at `__bond_release_one()` entry for cleanup after notifiers. After `bond_for_each_slave(bond, slave_iter, iter)`, clear candidate pointers (e.g. `new_aggregator` in `bond_3ad_unbind_slave()`) if `!slave_iter`. [F65, F66, F93, F122]
