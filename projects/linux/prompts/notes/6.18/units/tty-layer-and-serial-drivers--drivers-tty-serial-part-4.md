# TTY LAYER AND SERIAL DRIVERS — drivers/tty/serial (part 4)

This component contains the `serial-base` bus layer (`serial_base_bus.c`, `serial_base.h`) managing `serial_ctrl_device` and `serial_port_device` instances, along with hardware-specific UART drivers (OMAP, Actions Semi Owl, Intel EG20T PCH, Microchip PIC32, PowerMac Zilog ESCC, Intel PXA, Renesas RSCI, StrongARM SA1100, Broadcom SB1250 DUART, NXP SC16IS7xx I2C/SPI bridge, and NXP SCCNXP).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- On `serial_base_bus_type`, `serial_ctrl_device` and `serial_port_device` are distinguished by `dev->type` (`&serial_ctrl_type` vs `&serial_port_type`); check `dev->type` before `to_serial_base_ctrl_device()` or `to_serial_base_port_device()`. `to_pmz(p)` and `serial_pxa_pops` cast `struct uart_port *` directly, so `port` must stay at offset 0 of `uart_pmac_port` and `uart_pxa_port`. [F114, F115, F121]
- In `sc16is7xx_probe()`, `dev_set_drvdata(dev, s)` stores `sc16is7xx_port *s` with `s->p[i].port.dev = dev` (`dev_get_drvdata(port->dev)` is `sc16is7xx_port *`, not `sc16is7xx_one *`), and `s->p[i].port.membase` must be `(void __iomem *)~0` so `uart_configure_port()` does not abort on channel 0. In `sc16is7xx_i2c_probe()`/`sc16is7xx_spi_probe()`, copy `sc16is7xx_regcfg` per channel with `sc16is7xx_regmap_name(i)` and `sc16is7xx_regmap_port_mask(i)` (plus `SC16IS7XX_SPI_READ_BIT` for SPI). [F41, F58, F117]
- In `sc16is7xx.c`, `sc16is7xx_handle_rx()` uses per-channel `one->buf` while `sc16is7xx_handle_tx()` reads `tport->xmit_fifo` via `kfifo_out_linear_ptr()`; after TX, call `sc16is7xx_ier_set(port, SC16IS7XX_IER_THRI_BIT)` only if `!kfifo_is_empty(&tport->xmit_fifo)`, else `sc16is7xx_stop_tx(port)`. In `sc16is7xx_set_baud()`, when `div >= BIT(16)` sets the `/4` prescaler (`SC16IS7XX_MCR_CLKSEL_BIT`), return `DIV_ROUND_CLOSEST((clk / prescaler) / 16, div)`. [F54, F55, F56]
- In `sc16is7xx_port_irq()`, if `iir == SC16IS7XX_IIR_RTOI_SRC` and `SC16IS7XX_RXLVL_REG == 0`, force `rxlen = 1` to read `SC16IS7XX_RHR_REG` (errata 18.1.4); in `sc16is7xx_handle_rx()`, while `read_lsr` is true (`SC16IS7XX_IIR_RLSE_SRC` with `SC16IS7XX_LSR_FIFOE_BIT`), read 1 byte per pass via `sc16is7xx_port_read()` before `sc16is7xx_fifo_read()`. `sc16is7xx_setup_irda_ports()` must bound `"irda-mode-ports"` count to `SC16IS7XX_MAX_PORTS` and each entry to `< s->devtype->nr_uart`. [F79, F93, F94]
- In `omap-serial.c`, `serial_omap_restore_context()` restores `dll`, `dlh`, `ier`, `fcr`, `mcr`, `scr`, `efr`, `lcr`, `mdr1`, and `wer` from `uart_omap_port`, so persistent writes must update those shadows. In `serial_omap_stop_tx()` with `SER_RS485_ENABLED`, if `OMAP_UART_SCR_TX_EMPTY` is not set in `up->scr`, set it in `up->scr` and `UART_OMAP_SCR` with `UART_IER_THRI` enabled, clearing it, toggling `up->rts_gpiod`, and disabling `UART_IER_THRI` on the next THR IRQ. [F60, F63]
- `serial_omap_rlsi()` must read `UART_RX` if `lsr & UART_LSR_DR` before returning on `uart_handle_break()`; in `serial_omap_mdr1_errataset()`, wait with bounded `timeout` while `(serial_in(up, UART_LSR) & (UART_LSR_THRE | UART_LSR_DR)) != UART_LSR_THRE`; in `calculate_baud_abs_diff()`, clamp `n = port->uartclk / (mode * baud)` to `1` if `0`. [F96, F97, F106]
- In `sb1250-duart.c`, `sbd_console_setup()`, `sbd_init()`, and `sbd_exit()` must check `sport->duart != NULL`; shared `sport->memctrl` registers must index the channel as `(uport->line) % 2`; `sport->tx_stopped` must track `M_DUART_TX_DIS` (`1`) and `M_DUART_TX_EN` (`0`) writes to `R_DUART_CMD`; in `sbd_set_termios()`, bound `baud <= 5000000` before `V_DUART_BAUD_RATE(baud)` and clamp `brg` to `M_DUART_CLK_COUNTER` (`0xfff`). [F51, F67, F83, F110]
- In `sa1100.c`, `sa1100_set_termios()` must preserve `UTSR0_TO_SM(UTSR0_TFS)` in `read_status_mask`, and `sa1100_int()` must mask `UART_GET_UTSR0(sport)` reads with `SM_TO_UTSR0(sport->port.read_status_mask) | ~UTSR0_TFS`. In `owl-uart.c`, clear `OWL_UART_CTL_TRFS_TX` before polling `OWL_UART_STAT_RFEM` in `owl_uart_receive_chars()`, and set it before polling `OWL_UART_STAT_TRFL_MASK` in `owl_uart_port_write()` (restoring `old_ctl`). [F69, F72, F104]
- In `pch_uart.c`, `pch_uart_hal_set_fifo()` must reject `trigger & ~PCH_UART_FCR_RFTL` before indexing `trigger_level_*` and cache `priv->fcr` and `priv->trigger_level`; `pch_uart_hal_fifo_reset()` must OR `PCH_UART_FCR_FIFOE | flag` with `priv->fcr` and restore `priv->fcr`; in `pch_uart_hal_read()`, re-read `lsr` in the `for` step while incrementing `i` only on `buf[i++] = rbr`. [F71, F100, F113]
- In `rsci_receive_chars()`, decrement `count` and `i` before `continue` on `uart_handle_sysrq_char()`, and if `copied == 0` on exit, still read `RDR` and `CSR` and call `rsci_clear_DRxC(port)`. In `pmac_zilog.c`, `ZS_IS_CONS(uap)` ports must not call `__pmz_startup()` in `pmz_startup()` or clear `RxENABLE`/`TxENABLE` or power off in `pmz_shutdown()`; in `pmz_transmit_chars()`, paths not calling `write_zsdata()` (except `ZS_IS_CONS(uap)` when `!(status & Tx_BUF_EMP)`) must jump to `ack_tx_int` (`RES_Tx_P`). In `pxa.c`, `receive_chars()` must `goto ignore_char` on break/SysRq so `*status` updates and `UART_IER_RTOIE` is re-enabled. [F98, F99, F102, F103, F143]

## Synchronization

- In `sc16is7xx.c`, `one->efr_lock` nests outside `port->lock`: never acquire `efr_lock` or do sleeping `regmap` I/O under `port->lock`. Accesses to `SC16IS7XX_EFR_REG`/`XON1`/`XOFF1` or `SC16IS7XX_DLL_REG`/`SC16IS7XX_DLH_REG` must hold `one->efr_lock`, switch `SC16IS7XX_LCR_REG` to `CONF_MODE_B`/`CONF_MODE_A`, enable `regcache_cache_bypass()`, and restore both before unlocking. [F17, F127]
- In `sc16is7xx.c`, `sc16is7xx_ier_clear()`, `sc16is7xx_ier_set()`, and `sc16is7xx_enable_ms()` require `port->lock`, and `sc16is7xx_reg_proc()` must snapshot and zero `one->config` under `uart_port_lock_irqsave()` before unlocked `regmap` writes; in `sc16is7xx_probe()`, initialize `s->kworker`, `efr_lock`, and `tx_work`/`reg_work`/`ms_work` before `uart_add_one_port()` and `devm_request_threaded_irq()`. [F18, F20]
- In `pmac_zilog.c`, `pmz_interrupt()` must lock Channel A (`uap_a = pmz_get_port_A(uap)`) `uap_a->port.lock` to read `R3` and service Channel A, unlock before pushing flip buffers, then lock `uap_b->port.lock` (`uap_a->mate`). `pmz_load_zsregs()` requires `uap->port.lock` with IRQs off and TX idle; runtime `uap->curregs[]` updates must call `pmz_maybe_update_regs(uap)`. In `pmz_shutdown()`, call `pmz_interrupt_control(uap, 0)` under `uap->port.lock`, unlock before `free_irq()`, and clear `PMACZILOG_FLAG_IS_OPEN` under `uap->port.lock` before `pmz_set_scc_power(uap, 0)`. [F21, F23, F24]
- In `sccnxp.c`, `s->lock` (not `port->lock`) protects `s->imr`, `s->opened[]`, and multi-port register accesses; in `sccnxp_remove()`, call `devm_free_irq()` or `timer_delete_sync(&s->timer)` before removing ports, unregistering the driver, or disabling `s->regulator`. In `sa1100.c`, `sa1100_mctrl_check()` requires `sport->port.lock`, while `sa1100_shutdown()` and `sa1100_set_termios()` must call `timer_delete_sync(&sport->timer)` unlocked. [F25, F26, F27]
- In `omap-serial.c`, `wait_for_xmitr()` must save `UART_LSR_BI` in `up->lsr_break_flag` and `MSR_SAVE_FLAGS` in `up->msr_saved_flags`, and `serial_omap_console_write()` must call `check_modem_status(up)` if `up->msr_saved_flags` is non-zero. `pch_console_write()`, `serial_omap_console_write()`, `owl_uart_port_write()`, and `serial_pxa_console_write()` must use `uart_port_trylock_irqsave()` when `oops_in_progress` is non-zero and only unlock if `locked`. [F28, F160]
- In `pic32_uart.c`, `pic32_uart_irqtxen()` must gate `enable_irq()`/`disable_irq_nosync(sport->irq_tx)` on `sport->enable_tx_irq` under `port->lock`; in `pic32_uart_startup()`, set `IRQ_NOAUTOEN` on `irq_fault`, `irq_rx`, and `irq_tx` before `request_irq()`, and call `enable_irq(sport->irq_rx)` only after `pic32_uart_en_and_unmask(port)`. [F30, F31]
- In `pch_uart.c`, when `priv->use_dma` is set, disable RX IRQs before `dma_handle_rx()` and re-enable them in `pch_dma_rx_complete()` (or when `dma_handle_rx()` returns `0`), and gate TX DMA on `priv->tx_dma_use` until `pch_dma_tx_complete()` clears it and re-enables `PCH_UART_HAL_TX_INT`; `dma_handle_tx()` runs under `priv->port.lock` in hardirq context and must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F33, F52, F154]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `serial_base_bus.c`, `serial_base_device_init()` acquires `dev->of_node` via `device_set_of_node_from_dev()`, so release callbacks must call `of_node_put(dev->of_node)` and error paths after `serial_base_device_init()` must call `put_device()`, not `kfree()`. Free `port->port_id` via `ida_free(&ctrl_dev->port_ida, port->port_id)` on `serial_base_port_add()` errors and in `serial_base_port_device_remove()` (which must run while `port_dev->port` and `port_dev->dev.parent` are valid and before `serial_base_ctrl_device_remove()`). [F1, F2, F125, F163]
- In `sc16is7xx_remove()` and `sc16is7xx_shutdown()`, cancel `one->ms_work` and `s->poll_work` via `kthread_cancel_delayed_work_sync()` and flush `s->kworker` via `kthread_flush_worker()` before stopping `s->kworker_task` or disabling `s->clk`. In `sb1250-duart.c`, `duart->mapctrl` is refcounted by `duart->map_guard`, and `sbd_release_port()`/`sbd_map_port()` error paths must NULL `uport->membase` and `sport->memctrl` after `iounmap()`. [F4, F5, F6]
- In `pch_request_dma()`, every path after `pci_get_slot()` must call `pci_dev_put(dma_dev)` and release/NULL `priv->chan_tx` if RX channel allocation fails; `pch_free_dma()` must zero `chan_tx`, `chan_rx`, `rx_buf_virt`, and `rx_buf_dma`. [F7, F8]
- In `serial_omap_startup()`, free `up->port.irq` if `dev_pm_set_dedicated_wake_irq()` fails, and call `dev_pm_clear_wake_irq(up->dev)` in `serial_omap_shutdown()`; `serial_omap_probe()` (`err_add_port`) and `serial_omap_remove()` must unwind `pm_runtime_put_sync()`, `pm_runtime_disable()`, `cpu_latency_qos_remove_request(&up->pm_qos_request)`, and `device_init_wakeup(..., false)`. [F9, F10]
- Under `CONFIG_PPC_PMAC`, `pmz_detach()` must only call `macio_release_resources(uap->dev)` if `PMACZILOG_FLAG_RSRC_REQUESTED` is set; in `owl_uart_probe()` and `owl_uart_remove()`, reset `owl_uart_ports[pdev->id] = NULL` on error and removal; in `pic32_uart_probe()`, call `clk_disable_unprepare(sport->clk)` if `uart_console_registered(port)` after `uart_add_one_port()`. [F12, F13, F14]

## API Contracts

- `serial_base_ctrl_device_remove()` and `serial_base_port_device_remove()` must accept `NULL`; in `serial_base_port_add()`, `port->port_id == 0` allocates dynamically (`min = 0, max = -1`) while non-zero requests that exact ID; `serial_base_match_and_update_preferred_console()` must map `-ENOENT` to `0`; `serial_base_bus_type` drivers must prefix `drv->name` with `"ctrl"` or `"port"`. [F35, F74, F105, F162]
- `sc16is7xx_probe()` must check `IS_ERR(regmaps[i])` across `devtype->nr_uart` before dereferencing `regmaps[0]`, with `devtype->nr_uart <= SC16IS7XX_MAX_PORTS`, `nr_gpio <= BITS_PER_LONG`, and `devtype->name` (`char[10]`) `<= 9` chars; `SC16IS7XX_TCR_RX_HALT(words)` and `SC16IS7XX_TCR_RX_RESUME(words)` take char counts (`0..60`, step 4) with halt `>` resume; callers of `sc16is7xx_set_baud()` must clamp `baud` to `[port->uartclk / 16 / 4 / 0xffff, port->uartclk / 16]`. [F39, F44, F107, F116, F118, F123]
- `sccnxp_update_best_err(a, b, &besterr)` returns `0` on a better match (`abs(a - b) < *besterr`, initialized to `INT_MAX`); in `sa1100.c`, `read_status_mask`/`ignore_status_mask` pack `UTSR0` in `[7:0]` (`UTSR0_TO_SM`) and `UTSR1` in `[15:8]` (`UTSR1_TO_SM`); `serial_pxa_probe_dt()` returns `1` when `!np`, `0` on DT alias match, and `< 0` on error, falling back to `dev->id` only when `ret > 0`. [F46, F49, F50]
- `serial_omap_runtime_suspend()` must return `-EBUSY` when `up->is_suspending && !console_suspend_enabled && uart_console(&up->port)`, and `serial_omap_runtime_resume()` must call `serial_omap_restore_context(up)` if `serial_omap_get_context_loss_count(up)` is `< 0` or changed; in `pch_uart.c`, pass `num`/`priv->orig_nent` to `dma_sync_sg_for_device()` and `dma_unmap_sg()`, and `nent`/`priv->nent` to `dmaengine_prep_slave_sg()`; in `pmac_zilog.c`, Z8530 `R0` `CTS` is active-low (`!(status & CTS)`). [F137, F153, F169]

## Error Handling

- In `sc16is7xx_probe()`, `s->p[i].port.line` starts at `SC16IS7XX_MAX_DEVS` and `port_registered[i]` is set only after `uart_add_one_port()` succeeds, so `out_ports` only frees IDA lines `< SC16IS7XX_MAX_DEVS` and removes registered ports; in `sc16is7xx.c`, out-of-range `RXLVL`/`TXLVL` or unexpected `IIR` values must be ratelimit-logged and clamped to `SC16IS7XX_FIFO_SIZE` (`64`), never `WARN_ON()`. [F3, F78, F170]
- In `sa1100_serial_add_one_port()`, reset `sport->gpios = NULL` on non-`-EPROBE_DEFER` errors from `mctrl_gpio_init_noauto()`; in `sccnxp_probe()` and `sccnxp_remove()`, `s->regulator` may hold an `ERR_PTR`, so guard `regulator_disable(s->regulator)` with `!IS_ERR(s->regulator)` on all error/remove paths. [F15, F38, F45]

## External Contracts

- `serial_base_device_init()` and `serial_base_match_and_update_preferred_console()` must format port names identically as `"%s:%d.%d"` with `(dev_name(port->dev), ctrl_id, port_id)`. Keep `sc16is7xx_rs485_supported.delay_rts_after_send = 1` so `sc16is7xx_config_rs485()` returns `-EINVAL` when `SER_RS485_ENABLED` and `delay_rts_after_send` are set; in `owl_uart_set_termios()`, `pch_uart_set_termios()`, `pic32_uart_set_termios()`, and `sccnxp_set_termios()`, call `tty_termios_encode_baud_rate()` only if `tty_termios_baud_rate(termios)` is non-zero. [F75, F167, F168]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `sc16is7xx.c`, set `s->polling = (irq <= 0)`; `sc16is7xx_gpio_direction_output()` must write `SC16IS7XX_IODIR_REG` before `SC16IS7XX_IOSTATE_REG`; `SC16IS7XX_RHR_REG`/`SC16IS7XX_THR_REG` FIFO transfers must use `regmap_noinc_read()`/`regmap_noinc_write()`, and `sc16is7xx_fifo_write()` must return early if `!to_send`. [F59, F91, F129]
- In `pmac_zilog.c`, `R7P` (`16`) is a `uap->curregs[]` index and must never be passed to `read_zsreg()`/`write_zsreg()` (set `EN85C30` in `R15`, access `R7`, then clear `EN85C30`); in `pmz_convert_to_zs()`, `230400`, `115200`, and IrDA `baud >= 115200` must bypass `BPS_TO_BRG(baud, ZS_CLOCK / 16)`. In `sb1250-duart.c`, all MMIO must use `read_sbdchn()`, `read_sbdshr()`, `write_sbdchn()`, and `write_sbdshr()` with 64-bit `__raw_readq()`/`__raw_writeq()` (`unsigned int` value in `write_sbdchn()`) so `CONFIG_SB1_PASS_2_WORKAROUNDS` runs `__war_sbd1956()`. [F48, F66, F82, F111]
- In `sccnxp.c`, `MCTRL_OBIT(cfg, sig)` (`1..8`) and `MCTRL_IBIT(cfg, sig)` (`9..15`) require checking `cfg & MCTRL_MASK(sig)` before shifting; `sccnxp_[port_]read()`/`write()` take unshifted indices, shift by `<< port->regshift`, delay by `ndelay(s->chip->trwd)`, and gate `OPCR`/`SOP`/`ROP`/`IPCR` on `SCCNXP_HAVE_IO` and `MR0` on `SCCNXP_HAVE_MR0`. [F84, F112, F119, F146]
- In `omap-serial.c`, `serial_in()`/`serial_out()` apply `<< up->port.regshift` with 16-bit `readw()`/`writew()`, whereas `omap_serial_fill_features_erratas()` (after `pm_runtime_get_sync()`) must use 32-bit `readl(up->port.membase + (UART_OMAP_MVER << up->port.regshift))`; use `serial_omap_mdr1_errataset()` when `UART_ERRATA_i202_MDR1_ACCESS` is set; in `serial_omap_set_termios()`, write `UART_FCR` only while `UART_DLL`/`UART_DLM` are `0` in `CONF_MODE_A`, and `UART_MCR_TCRTLR` only while `UART_EFR_ECB` is set. [F62, F88, F120, F134]
- In `rsci.c`, set `CCR0_TE` after or with `CCR0_TIE` in `rsci_start_tx()`, and dummy-read `CSR` before `rsci_clear_DRxC(port)` in `rsci_receive_chars()`; in `pic32_uart.c`, call `pic32_wait_deplete_txbuf(sport)` before clearing `PIC32_UART_STA_UTXEN` or `PIC32_UART_MODE_ON`; in `pxa.c`, clear `UART_IER_RTOIE` before reading `UART_RX` and re-enable it after, keeping `UART_IER_UUE` set; in `pch_uart.c`, set `UART_MCR_AFE` only when `priv->fifo_size == 256`; in `owl-uart.c`, `OWL_UART_STAT_RIP` and `OWL_UART_STAT_TIP` are write-1-to-clear. [F138, F140, F147, F148, F149]
