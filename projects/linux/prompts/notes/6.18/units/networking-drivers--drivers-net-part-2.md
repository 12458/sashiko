# NETWORKING DRIVERS — drivers/net (part 2)

Virtual/tunnel netdevs, bus-backed drivers, AX.25 drivers, and MDIO helpers: `geneve`, `ipvlan`/`ipvtap`, `macvlan`/`macvtap`, `ifb`, `loopback`, `fjes`, `mhi_net`, `mdio`, `bpqether`, `hdlcdrv`, and `mkiss`.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `geneve`, `info` must be last in `geneve_config` and `cfg` last in `geneve_dev` (flexible array `options[]`). `genevehdr.opt_len` is 4-byte words vs `info.options_len` in bytes: multiply by 4 on RX (`geneve_rx`, `geneve_udp_encap_recv`, `geneve_hlen`) and divide by 4 on TX (`geneve_build_header`). `sock4`/`sock6` can be `NULL` when `IFF_UP`; NULL-check before `->sock`. In `geneve_nl2info()`, process `IFLA_GENEVE_REMOTE6` before `LABEL`/`UDP_ZERO_CSUM6_*`. In `geneve_find_dev()`, `collect_md` cannot share `tp_dst` in a netns, and the loop must store matches in `t` without breaking early on `tp_dst` or returning the cursor. [F548, F564, F606, F607, F624, F625]
- `ipvtap_dev`/`macvtap_dev` must keep `vlan` at offset 0, and `ipvtap_device_event()`/`macvtap_device_event()` must check `dev->rtnl_link_ops == &ipvtap_link_ops` / `&macvtap_link_ops` (not `netif_is_ipvlan()`/`netif_is_macvlan()`) before downcasting `netdev_priv(dev)`. On `NETDEV_UNREGISTER`, `macvlan_device_event()`/`ipvlan_device_event()` must use `list_for_each_entry_safe` and call `dev->rtnl_link_ops->dellink`. [F604, F634, F635]
- `macvlan_eth_hash()`, `macvlan_hash_lookup*()`, and `macvlan_addr_busy()` require 8-byte (6 MAC + 2 pad), 2-byte aligned buffers; `macvlan_eth_hash()` shifts `get_unaligned((u64 *)addr)` by 16 bits (right BE, left LE). Shared `port->vlan_source_hash` ops (`macvlan_hash_lookup_source`, `macvlan_flush_sources` with `hash_for_each_safe`, `macvlan_fill_info_macaddr`) must filter `entry->vlan == vlan` and maintain `vlan->macaddr_count`. Call `update_port_bc_queue_len(port)` when `port->vlans` or `bc_queue_len_req` changes. [F565, F566, F588, F605, F618]
- `MACVLAN_MODE_PASSTHRU` allows at most one `macvlan_dev` and cannot be toggled in `macvlan_changelink()`. In `macvlan_changelink()`, validate `MODE` before `FLAGS`, commit `vlan->mode` after `FLAGS` succeeds and before `MACADDR_MODE`. Passthru `macvlan_set_mac_address()` must call `macvlan_set_addr_change(vlan->port)` before `dev_set_mac_address(vlan->lowerdev, ...)`. [F568, F570, F603]
- Check `addr->atype` before reading `ipvl_addr.ipu` (`ip4addr`/`ip6addr`). Hash `ipvl_addr` in `port->hlhead` iff `netif_running(ipvlan->dev)`, and unlink from both `hlhead` and `ipvlan->addrs` under `addrs_lock`. In `ipvlan_set_port_mode()`, keep old `port->mode` until the `port->ipvlans` loop and `ipvlan_l3s_register()` succeed, without modifying `ipvlan` before `goto fail`. Migrate L3S hooks via `ipvlan_migrate_l3s_hook()` on `NETDEV_REGISTER`, and allocate `dev_id` in `[0x1, 0xFFFD]` (wrapping `dev_id_start` at `0xFFFE`). [F551, F573, F591, F601, F673]
- In `ifb_ri_tasklet()`, clear `skb->redirected`, set `tc_skip_classify = 1`, call `nf_skip_egress(skb, true)`, and resolve `skb->dev` from `skb_iif` before setting `skb_iif = txp->dev->ifindex`; if `dev_get_by_index_rcu()` is `NULL`, jump to `resched` if `txp->tq` is non-empty or `break` to drain `txp->rq`. In `mhi_net_newlink()`, init `mhi_netdev` and `dev_set_drvdata()` before `mhi_prepare_for_transfer()`, and set `rx_queue_sz` before `register_netdev()`. [F582, F608, F639]
- In `fjes`, register `fjes_driver` before `fjes_acpi_driver` (reverse on exit), verify `is_extended_socket_device()` and `acpi_check_extended_socket_status()` for `PNP0C02`, enable NAPI/IRQ before unmasking `REG_ICTL_MASK_ALL` in `fjes_open()`, and cancel `interrupt_watch_task` + mask `REG_ICTL_MASK_ALL` before `free_irq()`. `fjes_hw_setup_epbuf()` must preserve `vlan_id[]` across `memset` and set `head = 0`, `tail = 1`, `FJES_RX_MTU_CHANGING_DONE` (TX indexes `tail - 1`, RX indexes `head`). Skip `epidx == hw->my_epid` in all `0..max_epid - 1` loops (including `FJES_EP_STATS_LEN` ethtool stats), keep `fjes_support_mtu[]` 0-terminated, keep `share_buffer.buffer[]` within `FJES_DEV_COMMAND_SHARE_BUFFER_REQ_LEN`, and in `fjes_poll()` preserve `cur_epid` and call `fjes_rxframe_release()` even if `napi_alloc_skb()` fails. [F576, F592, F593, F594, F611, F627, F629, F641, F642, F643]
- In `mkiss`, `ax_changedmtu()` must copy `ax->xleft` bytes from `ax->xhead` to new `xbuff` under `buflock`; `ax_bump()` must decrement `rcount` by 2 and clear `0x80`/`0x20` in `rbuff[0]` after CRC check; `kiss_unesc()` on `END` must run `!test_and_clear_bit(AXF_ERROR, &ax->flags)` before `(ax->rcount > 2)`; `ax_encaps()` early returns must call `netif_start_queue(dev)`. In `hdlcdrv`, check `2 <= pkt_len < HDLCDRV_MAXFLEN` before copying to `hdlctx.buffer[HDLCDRV_MAXFLEN + 2]`, drain 16 bits when `hdlctx.numbits >= 16` before `tx_state`, and drain 8 bits while `hdlcrx.numbits >= 8`. [F580, F581, F599, F614, F615, F620, F621]

## Synchronization
- Call `netdev_lockdep_set_classes()` before `register_netdevice()` in `bpq_setup`, `ipvlan_init`, `macvlan_set_lockdep_class` (plus `addr_list_lock`), `geneve_init`, and `loopback_dev_init`. In `geneve_changelink()`, wrap `cfg` updates in `geneve_quiesce()`/`geneve_unquiesce()`, and call `dst_cache_reset()` + `geneve_link_config()` on remote address change. [F520, F563]
- `ipvlan_l3s_register()` sets `l3mdev_ops` before `IFF_L3MDEV_RX_HANDLER`, and `unregister` clears the flag without clearing `l3mdev_ops`. `ipvlan_add_addr()` requires `spin_lock_bh(&ipvlan->addrs_lock)` and `GFP_ATOMIC`; removals require `addrs_lock` and `kfree_rcu()`. `ipvlan_process_multicast()` requires `spin_lock_bh(&port->backlog.lock)` and `local_bh_disable()`/`enable()` around RX/stats. [F519, F654, F655]
- `macvlan_hash_change_addr()` must call `macvlan_hash_del(vlan, true)` before `eth_hw_addr_set()`. `ifb_ri_tasklet()` must hold `__netif_tx_trylock(txq)` when splicing `txp->rq` and when checking `skb_peek(&txp->rq)` before clearing `tasklet_pending`. [F653, F657]
- In `fjes`, hold `hw->hw_info.lock` around `fjes_hw_request_info()`, `fjes_hw_register/unregister_buff_addr()`, and `fjes_hw_start/stop_debug()`; schedule `force_close_task` instead of calling `rtnl_lock()`/`dev_close()` from work tasks; hold `hw->rx_status_lock` (`spin_lock_irqsave` outside poll) for `fjes_hw_setup_epbuf()`, `rx_status` updates, and `FJES_RX_MTU_CHANGING_DONE` in `fjes_change_mtu()`; and disable local IRQs around `fjes_intr()` in `fjes_irq_watch_task()`. [F537, F540, F578, F661]
- `bpq_device_event()` must check `dev_is_ethdev()` (rejecting `netdev_need_ops_lock()`), `bpq_devices` updates require `rtnl_lock` and `list_add_rcu`/`list_del_rcu` (after `register_netdevice()` in `bpq_new_device()`), and `bpq_get_ax25_dev()` requires `rtnl_lock` or `rcu_read_lock()`. In `hdlcdrv`, use `spin_lock_irqsave(&hb->lock, flags)`, `in_hdlc_rx`/`tx` bitlocks, and `dev_kfree_skb_irq()`. [F535, F649, F650, F663]

## Object Lifetimes
- Destroy `geneve_dev` via `geneve_dellink()` (iterating only `gn->geneve_list` in `geneve_exit_rtnl_net()`). In `geneve_stop()`/`geneve_sock_release()`, unlink `hlist4`/`6`, NULL `sock4`/`6`, and `synchronize_net()` before `__geneve_sock_release()`. When `skb_tunnel_check_pmtu() > 0` in `geneve_xmit_skb()`/`geneve6_xmit_skb()`, call `skb_tunnel_info_unclone(skb)` and return `-EMSGSIZE` after `__netif_rx(skb)`. [F499, F528, F666, F669]
- Release `dev_put(src->dev)` on `macvlan` `bc_queue` SKBs in `macvlan_process_broadcast()`/`macvlan_port_destroy()`. On `ipvlan` `port->backlog`, save `dev = skb->dev` before overwriting `skb->dev` in `ipvlan_process_multicast()` to call `dev_put(dev)`, call `netdev_rx_handler_unregister()` before `cancel_work_sync(&port->wq)` and `dev_put(skb->dev)` in `ipvlan_port_destroy()`, and call `skb_dst_drop(skb)` before `skb_dst_set()` in `ipvlan_l3_rcv()`. [F506, F507, F510, F522]
- Call `netdev_rx_handler_unregister(dev)` before `tap_del_queues()` in `ipvtap_dellink()`/`macvtap_dellink()`. Call `tasklet_kill(&txp->ifb_tasklet)` before purging queues and freeing `tx_private` in `ifb_dev_free()`. In `mhi_net`, always free/consume `mhi_res->buf_addr` in UL/DL callbacks, and complete `unregister_netdev()` + `mhi_unprepare_from_transfer()` before `kfree_skb(skbagg_head)` and `free_netdev()` in `mhi_net_dellink()`. [F514, F526, F531, F679]
- In `fjes_close()`, disable NAPI/IRQ and cancel all work items before `fjes_hw_wait_epstop()` and `fjes_free_resources()`; NULL pointers when freeing in `fjes_hw_setup()`/`cleanup()`; `kfree(buffer.pointer)` after `acpi_evaluate_object()` in `is_extended_socket_device()`. In `mkiss_close()`, clear `tty->disc_data` under `disc_data_lock`, wait on `ax->dead`, and `unregister_netdev()` before freeing buffers/dev. Set `s->skb = NULL` when freeing in `hdlcdrv_transmitter()`/`hdlcdrv_close()`. [F511, F513, F518, F539, F644]

## API Contracts
- `geneve_build_skb()` calls `dst_release(dst)` on error (leaving `skb` for `geneve_xmit()`); `geneve_rx()` only drops ECN errors when `err > 1`. Check `netif_is_ipvlan_port()`/`netif_is_macvlan_port()` on `phy_dev`/`lowerdev` before `ipvlan_port_get_*()`/`macvlan_port_get_*()`, and call `netdev_rx_handler_register()` before `ipvlan_link_new()`/`macvlan_common_newlink()` in `ipvtap_newlink()`/`macvtap_newlink()`. [F501, F549, F636, F670]
- `ipvlan_nl_validate()` rejects `(flags & (PRIVATE | VEPA)) == (PRIVATE | VEPA)`; `ipvlan_nl_changelink()` and stacked `ipvlan_link_new()` require `ns_capable(dev_net(phy_dev)->user_ns, CAP_NET_ADMIN)`; outbound L3 in `ipvlan` must call `ipvlan_skb_crossing_ns()`, strip L2, and zero `IPCB`/`IP6CB`. `macvlan_hwtstamp_set()` rejects `!net_eq(dev_net(dev), &init_net)`; `macvlan_open()` must zero `accel_priv` and call `dev_uc_add()` if `ndo_dfwd_add_station()` fails. `blackhole_netdev_ops` needs `blackhole_neigh_construct`, and `loopback_xmit()` needs `skb_clear_tstamp()`, `skb_orphan()`, and `skb_dst_force()`. [F602, F665, F671, F672, F674, F676, F678]
- `mdio45_links_ok()` takes `1 << devad`, returns bool, clears `1 << devad`, reads `MDIO_STAT1` (and `STAT2`) once to clear latches, then checks `(reg & (FAULT | LSTATUS)) == LSTATUS`; check `mdio->mmds & MDIO_DEVS_AN` for `MDIO_MMD_AN` and zero-init `cmd` before `mdio45_ethtool_ksettings_get_npage()`. In `fjes`, `fjes_hw_issue_request_command()` returns `FJES_CMD_STATUS_NORMAL == 1` (zero buffers before, check `length`/`code` after) and `vzalloc()` buffers require `vmalloc_to_page()`. AX.25 `ndo_start_xmit` must pass `ETH_P_IP` to `ax25_ip_xmit()`; `hdlcdrv_register()` needs `hdlcdrv_state` at offset 0, and `hdlcdrv_siocdevprivate()` checks `!in_compat_syscall()`, capabilities, and `!netif_running(dev)`. [F557, F560, F612, F632, F647, F648, F662, F680, F682, F683]

## Error Handling
- Clean up `geneve_configure()` failure with `free_netdev(dev)` and later failures in `geneve_dev_create_fb()` with `geneve_dellink()`. In `macvlan_common_newlink()`, only destroy port on error if `create && macvlan_port_get_rtnl(lowerdev)`. Skip `NETDEV_UNREGISTER` cleanup in `ipvtap`/`macvtap_device_event()` when `tap.minor == 0`. Link `ipvlan->pnode` last in `ipvlan_link_new()`, and call `dst_release(dst)` on `dst->error` in `ipvlan_route_v6_outbound()`. Handle `-EOVERFLOW` as a fragment in `mhi_net_dl_callback()` and NULL `skbagg_head` on `status == 0`. In `fjes`, call `fjes_free_irq()` on `fjes_request_irq()` failure and check `count_max == 0` / `max_epid == 0`. Check `bitrate > 0` and `calibrate <= INT_MAX / bitrate` in `hdlcdrv_siocdevprivate()`. [F502, F505, F509, F517, F542, F552, F558, F619, F622]

## External Contracts & Known Pitfalls
- Convert `ifla_geneve_port_range` with `ntohs()`; reject `IFLA_MACVLAN_MACADDR_COUNT` and `BC_QUEUE_LEN_USED` from userspace; encode BPQ length as `le16(len + 5)` after pulling KISS byte; in `mkiss` `ax_encaps()`, skip CRC when `(*p & 0x0f) != 0` and consume `0x85` locally. [F587, F589, F598, F681]
- Use `skb_eth_hdr(skb)` or `skb_reset_mac_header(skb)` in `geneve_xmit_skb()`, `ipvlan_xmit_mode_l2()`, and `macvlan_queue_xmit()`; return `RX_HANDLER_PASS` for `PACKET_LOOPBACK` in `macvlan_handle_frame()`. Refresh pointers after pull/defrag/unshare (`gh` in `geneve_gro_receive()`, `oiph = skb->head + nh` in `geneve_rx()`, `*pskb = skb` and `eth` in `macvlan_handle_frame()`/`ipvlan_rcv_frame()`, and pull `+ sizeof(struct in6_addr)` and refresh `ip6h`/`icmph` for `NDISC_NEIGHBOUR_SOLICITATION` in `ipvlan_get_L3_hdr()`). Cast `vni[0..2]` to `u64` on LE in `vni_to_tunnel_id()`, NUL-terminate `utf16s_to_utf8s(..., sizeof(str_buf) - 1)` in `is_extended_socket_device()`, and pass `len + 2` and `swab16(crc16(...))` to `kiss_esc_crc()`. [F562, F590, F596, F617, F628, F638, F667, F668, F677]
