# NFS, SUNRPC, AND LOCKD CLIENTS — fs/nfs (part 6)

Implements NFS buffered read, write, and commit pagecache engines (`read.c`, `write.c`), the pNFS client core, deviceid cache, and DS helpers (`pnfs.c`, `pnfs_dev.c`, `pnfs_nfs.c`), superblock/mount operations (`super.c`), async silly-delete/rename (`unlink.c`), NFSv2 procedures (`proc.c`), symlink reading (`symlink.c`), and sysfs/sysctl interfaces (`sysfs.c`, `sysctl.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `pnfs_layoutreturn_free_lsegs()`, after freeing matching segments and updating `lo->plh_stateid` via `pnfs_set_layout_stateid()`, call `pnfs_reset_return_info(lo)` to re-establish `plh_return_iomode`, `plh_return_seq`, and `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN_REQUESTED` for segments left on `lo->plh_return_segs`. `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()` sets `NFS_LAYOUT_INVALID_STID` when removing the last segment with `plh_outstanding == 0`, unless `NFS_LSEG_LAYOUTRETURN` is on `lseg->pls_flags` or `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN_REQUESTED` / `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN` is on `lo->plh_flags`. [F414, F417]
- `lo->plh_stateid.seqid` is `__be32`, while `plh_barrier`, `plh_return_seq`, and `pls_seq` are host-endian `u32` compared via `pnfs_seqid_is_newer()` (`(s32)(s1 - s2) > 0`), requiring `be32_to_cpu()`/`cpu_to_be32()` at every boundary; in `pnfs_set_layout_stateid()`, when `!update_barrier` and `atomic_read(&lo->plh_outstanding) == 1`, call `pnfs_barrier_update(lo, be32_to_cpu(lo->plh_stateid.seqid))`. [F442, F471]
- `plh_lwb` and `nfs_commit_data::lwb` store 1-based exclusive end offsets (`req_offset(req) + req->wb_bytes`), which `pnfs_layoutcommit_inode()` converts to 0-based inclusive `end_pos - 1` (or `U64_MAX` if `0`) in `data->args.lastbytewritten`; it must also `kzalloc()` `struct nfs4_layoutcommit_data` so `data->res.seq_res.sr_slot` is `NULL`. [F435, F479]
- In `struct nfs4_layoutget`, `lgp->args.layout.pglen` (aliased by `lgp->res.layoutp->pglen`) must stay `max_pages * PAGE_SIZE` because `pnfs_layoutget_free()` frees `pglen / PAGE_SIZE` pages via `nfs4_free_pages()`. `pnfs_detach_layout_hdr()` must reset `nfsi->write_io = 0` and `nfsi->read_io = 0` when setting `nfsi->layout = NULL`. [F415, F475]
- `set_pnfs_layoutdriver()` increments `cl_mds_count` only after `ld_type->set_layoutdriver` succeeds; `unset_pnfs_layoutdriver()` decrements `cl_mds_count` and calls `nfs4_deviceid_purge_client()` on zero before clearing `nfss->pnfs_curr_ld`. [F421]
- In `nfs_pageio_reset_read_mds()` and `nfs_pageio_reset_write_mds()`, call `pgio->pg_ops->pg_cleanup(pgio)` before setting `pgio->pg_ops = &nfs_pgio_rw_ops` (or `pgio->pg_lseg` leaks), call `nfs_pageio_stop_mirroring(pgio)` on writes, and reset `pg_mirrors[0].pg_bsize` to `rsize`/`wsize`. [F425]
- In `pnfs_generic_commit_pagelist()`, `ds_commit_index` is `-1` for MDS commits and `0 .. nbuckets - 1` for DS commits. `pnfs_alloc_commit_array()` must allocate with `struct_size(p, buckets, n)` and set `p->nbuckets = n` before indexing `p->buckets` (`__counted_by(nbuckets)`) or decrementing `n`. [F439, F440]
- `nfs_destroy_unlinked_subrequests()` must advance `destroy_list` (stopping at `subreq->wb_this_page == old_head`) before resetting `wb_this_page = subreq` and `wb_head = subreq` under `nfs_page_set_headlock(subreq)`. In `nfs_update_folio()`, `nfs_can_extend_write()` rounds `offset`/`end` only to `PAGE_SIZE` boundaries (capped at `pagelen`), not `folio_size(folio)`. [F458, F503]

## Synchronization

- `nfs_inode_add_request()` locks `req` (`nfs_lock_request`) first and holds `mapping->i_private_lock` while setting `PG_MAPPED`, `folio_set_private(folio)`, and `folio->private = req`. Detaching `folio->private` in `nfs_inode_remove_request()` and checking `head == folio->private` in `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()` both require `nfs_page_group_lock(head)`, after which `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()` must call `nfs_cancel_remove_inode(head, inode)` to clear `PG_REMOVE` and restore `PG_INODE_REF`. [F360, F378, F393]
- `nfs_page_group_lock_subreq()` requires `head` locked and `nfs_page_group_lock(head)` held; it drops `nfs_page_group_unlock(head)` before `nfs_wait_on_request(subreq)` and re-acquires it after. On error, it calls `nfs_unroll_locks(head, subreq)` and `nfs_release_request(subreq)`, returning with `head`'s page group lock dropped. [F361, F486]
- `nfs_request_add_commit_list_locked()`, `nfs_request_remove_commit_list()`, and `pnfs_generic_clear_request_commit()` require `NFS_I(cinfo->inode)->commit_mutex` and `PG_BUSY` on `req`. Under `commit_mutex`, `PG_CLEAN`, commit-list membership, and `cinfo->mds->ncommit` update in lockstep (`nfs_clear_request_commit()` also calls `nfs_folio_clear_commit()`), as do `cinfo->ds->nwritten` / `ncommitting`, `PG_COMMIT_TO_DS`, and bucket lists. [F383, F424, F430]
- `pnfs_add_commit_array()`, `pnfs_generic_ds_cinfo_release_lseg()`, and `pnfs_generic_ds_cinfo_destroy()` require `inode->i_lock`, and `pnfs_free_commit_array()` uses `kfree_rcu(p, rcu)`. RCU walks over `fl_cinfo->commits` (`pnfs_generic_scan_commit_lists()`, `pnfs_generic_recover_commit_reqs()`, `pnfs_alloc_ds_commits_list()`) must pin `array` via `pnfs_get_commit_array()`, drop `rcu_read_unlock()` before bucket work, and re-acquire `rcu_read_lock()` before `pnfs_put_commit_array()`. [F492, F493]
- `pnfs_free_lseg()` and `pnfs_free_lseg_list()` must run outside `inode->i_lock` because `ld->free_lseg` acquires `inode->i_lock`. Clearing wait bits in `nfs_layoutget_end()`, `pnfs_clear_layoutreturn_waitbit()`, `pnfs_clear_first_layoutget()`, and `pnfs_clear_layoutcommitting()` requires `smp_mb__after_atomic()` before `wake_up_bit()`. `pnfs_wait_on_layoutreturn()` and `pnfs_clear_layoutreturn_waitbit()` require `inode->i_lock`. [F379, F489, F491]
- Before sending `LAYOUTGET`, link `lo` onto `server->layouts` under `clp->cl_lock` via `_add_to_server_list()`. `pnfs_prepare_layoutreturn()` only defers for `atomic_read(&lo->plh_outstanding) != 0` when `lo->plh_return_seq == 0`. `pnfs_layout_bulk_destroy_byserver_locked()` requires `clp->cl_lock` and `rcu_read_lock()`, dropping both before `iput(inode)` or `nfs_sb_deactive(server->super)`, then reacquiring them and returning `-EAGAIN`. `pnfs_destroy_layout_final()` waits under `inode->i_lock` via `wait_var_event_spinlock` until `nfsi->layout != lo`. [F386, F387, F388, F450]
- In `_nfs4_pnfs_v3_ds_connect()` and `_nfs4_pnfs_v4_ds_connect()`, `ds->ds_clp` (and `nfs4_init_ds_session()` for v4) must be ordered with `smp_wmb()` before `WRITE_ONCE(ds->ds_clp, clp)` and `nfs4_clear_ds_conn_bit(ds)`. [F390]
- `__nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` uses `atomic_inc_not_zero(&d->ref)` under `rcu_read_lock()`. `nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` rechecks `__nfs4_find_get_deviceid()` under `nfs4_deviceid_lock` before incrementing `new->ref` a second time and calling `hlist_add_head_rcu` (or `free_deviceid_node(new)`). `nfs4_delete_deviceid()` and `_deviceid_purge_client()` unhash under `nfs4_deviceid_lock` and drop the lock before `nfs4_put_deviceid_node()`. [F367, F391, F494]
- `__nfs_list_for_each_server()` takes `nfs_sb_active(server->super)` under `rcu_read_lock()`, drops `rcu_read_unlock()` before `nfs_sb_deactive()`, `fn`, and `cond_resched()`, and keeps `last` active until advancing. `shutdown_nfs_client()` must not shut down `clp` until all servers in `clp->cl_superblocks` have `NFS_MOUNT_SHUTDOWN` set under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F380, F495]
- In `nfs_get_link()`, when `dentry == NULL` (RCU pathwalk), do not sleep: use `nfs_revalidate_mapping_rcu(inode)` and `filemap_get_folio()`, returning `ERR_PTR(-ECHILD)` if absent/not uptodate. `nfs_netns_client::identifier` uses `rcu_dereference` under `rcu_read_lock()` in `show` and `xchg()` + `synchronize_rcu()` before `kfree(old)` in `store`. `nfs_ctx_key_to_expire()` drops `rcu_read_unlock()` before `lookup_cred` and `put_rpccred(old)`, updating `ctx->ll_cred` via `xchg()`. [F480, F496, F497]

## Object Lifetimes

- `nfs_read_add_folio()` consumes the caller's folio lock on all paths (callers must never unlock `folio`). In `nfs_return_empty_folio()`, `nfs_readpage_release()`, and `nfs_read_add_folio()`, call `folio_unlock(folio)` only if `nfs_netfs_folio_unlock(folio)` returns non-zero. [F358, F403]
- `nfs_init_commit()` steals `@lseg` into `data->lseg`, takes `get_nfs_open_context(ctx)`, and calls `nfs_commit_begin(cinfo->mds)`. `pnfs_bucket_get_committing()` returns a referenced `lseg` (stealing `bucket->lseg` only when `committing` and `written` are both empty, else `pnfs_get_lseg()`), which callers pass to `nfs_init_commit()` or `pnfs_put_lseg()`. [F363, F364]
- `pnfs_generic_layout_insert_lseg()` takes a `pnfs_get_layout_hdr(lo)` ref dropped in `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()`, so `pnfs_put_lseg()` must hold an extra `pnfs_get_layout_hdr(lo)` across `pnfs_layout_remove_lseg()` and `spin_unlock(&inode->i_lock)`, and skip `pnfs_free_lseg()` if `pnfs_cache_lseg_for_layoutreturn()` is true. `pnfs_layout_set_fail_bit()` takes a `plh_refcount` ref dropped via `pnfs_layout_clear_fail_bit()`, and `pnfs_set_layoutcommit()` takes a `pls_refcount` ref on setting `NFS_LSEG_LAYOUTCOMMIT` that must be dropped when clearing it. [F368, F371, F416]
- `pnfs_prepare_layoutreturn()` sets `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN_LOCK` / `NFS_LAYOUT_RETURN` and takes `lo->plh_refcount` and `*cred = get_cred(lo->plh_lc_cred)`, consumed by `pnfs_send_layoutreturn()` or, in `pnfs_roc()`, by `put_cred(lc_cred)` and `pnfs_roc_release()`. In `pnfs_read_resend_pnfs()`, when setting `NFS_IOHDR_REDO` succeeds, put and clear `hdr->lseg = NULL` before `nfs_pageio_resend()`. [F369, F370]
- `nfs_sb_active()` multiplexes one `sb->s_active` ref across `server->active`; pair a `true` return with `nfs_sb_deactive(sb)`, never `deactivate_super(sb)`. `nfs_netns_sysfs_destroy()` deletes/puts `clp->kobject` before `clp->nfs_net_kobj` (which `kfree()`s `struct nfs_netns_client`). `nfs_sysfs_sb_release()` is a no-op and `nfs_sysfs_remove_server()` only calls `kobject_del(&server->kobj)` before `nfs_free_server()` calls `kobject_put(&server->kobj)`. [F372, F376, F377]
- `nfs_sillyrename()` pre-allocates `nfs_unlinkdata` via `nfs_async_unlink()` before `nfs_async_rename()` (cancelling via `nfs_cancel_async_unlink()` on error). Storing `nfs_unlinkdata` in `dentry->d_fsdata` with `DCACHE_NFSFS_RENAMED` under `d_lock` must `kfree()` any displaced `d_fsdata` outside `d_lock`, and completion/cancellation clears `d_fsdata = NULL` and `DCACHE_NFSFS_RENAMED` under `d_lock`. In `nfs_call_unlink()`, if `d_in_lookup(alias)` is true, `rmdir_sem`, `alias`, `data`, and `nfs_sb_active()` transfer to `nfs_async_unlink_release()`. [F374, F375, F483]

## API Contracts

- `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()` and `nfs_try_to_update_request()` return a locked `struct nfs_page *`, `NULL`, or `ERR_PTR(-errno)`. `nfs_wb_folio_cancel()` calls `folio_cancel_dirty(folio)` after detaching a head request. `nfs_migrate_folio()` returns `-EBUSY` if `folio_test_private(src)` or `folio_test_private_2(src)` remains set after flushing (`MIGRATE_SYNC` only) or waiting (`!MIGRATE_ASYNC`). [F402, F504, F506]
- `nfs_commitdata_alloc()` and `nfs_writehdr_alloc()` fall back to `mempool_alloc(..., GFP_NOWAIT)` + `memset(0)` and free via `mempool_free()`. `pnfs_add_commit_array()` does not consume `@new` if an entry exists (`if (array != new) pnfs_free_commit_array(new)`). `nfs4_pnfs_ds_add()` only splices `@dsaddrs` when creating a new DS, so callers must always drain and free remaining `@dsaddrs`. `pnfs_commit_list()` returns `PNFS_NOT_ATTEMPTED` (`1`), `PNFS_ATTEMPTED` (`0`), or `-ENOMEM`; `__nfs_commit_inode()` clamps non-negative returns from `nfs_generic_commit_list()` to `0`. [F365, F366, F404, F481]
- `pnfs_register_layoutdriver()` requires non-zero `id` and non-NULL `alloc_lseg`/`free_lseg` (`ld_prefs[]` ends with `0`). `pnfs_layout_process()` validates `res->range` via `pnfs_sanity_check_layout_range()` before `alloc_lseg()` and converts `NULL` from `alloc_lseg()` to `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)`. `pnfs_alloc_init_layoutget_args()` sets `lgp->res.status = -NFS4ERR_DELAY` and rounds `max_layoutget_response` up via `(max_reply_sz + PAGE_SIZE - 1) >> PAGE_SHIFT`. In `pnfs_roc_done()`, breaking out of `switch (*ret)` must clear `*argpp = NULL` and `*respp = NULL`. `nfs4_decode_mp_ds_addr()` splits the last two `.ABC.DEF` octets before `rpc_pton()` and passes `((tmp[0] << 8) | tmp[1])` through `htons()`. [F397, F398, F433, F438, F443, F462, F469, F498]
- `nfs_request_mount()` passes `ctx->timeo`/`retrans` only when `(request.protocol == XPRT_TRANSPORT_UDP) == !(ctx->flags & NFS_MOUNT_TCP)`. Mount/DS `timeo` is in deciseconds (`HZ / 10`) and `ctx->ac*` is in seconds (`HZ`). For NFSv2: writes force `NFS_FILE_SYNC` and commit ops `BUG()`; `create`/`mknod`/`mkdir`/`symlink` init `fh->size = 0` and `nfs_fattr_init(fattr)` when unpopulated; `nfs_proc_symlink()` rejects `len > NFS2_MAXPATHLEN`; and `nfs_lock_check_bounds()` rejects `__s32` truncation. [F406, F407, F409, F447, F499]

## Error Handling

- In `nfs_lock_and_join_requests()`, if `nfs_wait_on_request(head)` fails before `nfs_lock_request(head)` succeeds, call `nfs_release_request(head)`; errors after locking `head` call `nfs_unlock_and_release_request(head)`. [F359]
- In `nfs_readpage_retry()` and `nfs_writeback_result()`, short I/O with `resp->count == 0` is a fatal `-EIO` (do not restart). Non-zero short reads and non-`NFS_UNSTABLE` short writes advance `hdr->mds_offset`, `argp->offset`, and `argp->pgbase` by `resp->count` and reduce `argp->count`, while an `NFS_UNSTABLE` short write resends the full range with `argp->stable = NFS_FILE_SYNC` after zeroing `resp->count` and `resp->verf->committed`. [F426, F427, F460]
- Failed write/commit RPCs record errors on both `mapping` and `i_sb->s_wb_err` via `nfs_mapping_set_error()` and call `nfs_set_pageerror()`. `nfs_writepages()` breaks its retry loop on `err == -EAGAIN` when `NFS_MOUNT_SOFTERR` is set. [F461, F511]
- On `ERR_PTR` from `pnfs_update_layout()`, `pg_init` callbacks set `pgio->pg_error = PTR_ERR(pgio->pg_lseg)` and `pgio->pg_lseg = NULL`. In `pnfs_layoutcommit_inode()`, `-ENOSPC` from `ld->prepare_layoutcommit()` re-sets `NFS_INO_LAYOUTCOMMIT` and `mark_as_dirty = true` but still sends `nfs4_proc_layoutcommit()`. In `nfs_get_tree_common()`, call `nfs_free_server(server)` if `sget_fc()` fails or reuses a superblock (`s->s_fs_info != server`), else `deactivate_locked_super(s)`. [F373, F395, F399]

## External Contracts

- DS cache entries (`nn->nfs4_data_server_cache`) and superblocks (`nfs_compare_super_address()`, `nfs_compare_super()`) are scoped per netns, `cl_cred->user_ns`, and `security_sb_mnt_opts_compat()`; `pnfs_roc()` requires `cred_fscmp(cred, lc_cred) == 0`. Async RPCs (`nfs_do_call_unlink()`, `nfs_async_rename()`, `nfs_commit_list()`) pass `RPC_TASK_CRED_NOREF` and hold `data->cred`/`context` until `rpc_release`. Server sysfs attributes under `server->kobj` use `sysfs_create_file_ns()` with `nfs_netns_server_namespace(&server->kobj)`, and `nfs_netns_identifier_store()` clamps to `CONTAINER_ID_MAXLEN` (64), strips `'\n'`, and uses `kmemdup_nul()`. [F445, F501, F502, F509, F510]

## Known Pitfalls

- `pnfs_mark_matching_lsegs_return()` and `pnfs_mark_layout_stateid_return()` return negative errnos (`-EBUSY`, `-ENOENT`), contrary to kernel-doc. When `!task->tk_ops`, short I/O in `nfs_readpage_retry()` / `nfs_writeback_result()` sets `hdr->pnfs_error = -EAGAIN` instead of `rpc_restart_call_prepare(task)`, and `pnfs_ld_read_done()` / `pnfs_ld_write_done()` must re-test `if (hdr->pnfs_error)` after `rpc_call_done()`. [F396, F401]
- In `pnfs_update_layout()`, jumping to `lookup_again` after `first = true` requires `pnfs_clear_first_layoutget(lo)` (and resetting `first = false`), and `PAGE_ALIGN(arg.length)` must be skipped when `arg.length == NFS4_MAX_UINT64`. [F451, F464]
- Guard against integer underflow/overflow: check `req_start >= seg_end` before `seg_end - req_start` in `pnfs_generic_pg_test()`; check `bytes > hdr->good_bytes` before `hdr->good_bytes - bytes` on EOF in `nfs_read_completion()`; check `i_size > 0` before `i_size - 1` in `nfs_grow_file()`; clamp `fattr->size` with `nfs_size_to_loff_t()` in `nfs_writeback_check_extend()`; cast 32-bit `nfs2_fsstat` block counts to `u64` before multiplying in `nfs_proc_statfs()`; and keep page index `i` signed in `nfs4_get_device_info()` for `while (--i >= 0)`. [F436, F437, F441, F448, F465, F467]
