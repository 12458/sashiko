# MELLANOX ETHERNET DRIVER (mlx5e) — drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx5/core (part 1)

This component implements the core netdevice lifecycle, control plane, configuration, and flow-steering management for the Mellanox ConnectX-4+ Ethernet driver (`mlx5e`), registering `mlx5e_driver` and managing channels (RQs, SQs, CQs, ICOSQs, XDP/XSK), shared hardware objects (PD, TD, MKey, TIS, TIR), flow tables (VLAN, L2, TTC, aRFS, ethtool RXNFC), and DIM.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Guard `priv->rx_res` calls with `if (priv->rx_res)` in `mlx5e_num_channels_changed`, `mlx5e_activate_priv_channels`, and `mlx5e_deactivate_priv_channels`. `mlx5e_num_channels_changed` must update `real_num_rx_queues`, `mlx5e_update_tc_and_tx_queues`, and `mlx5e_rx_res_rss_update_num_channels` in lockstep, rolling back `tc_to_txq` and `real_num_rx_queues` on error. [F238, F257]
- `mlx5e_attach_netdev` must verify `mlx5e_get_max_sq_wqebbs(mdev) >= MLX5E_MAX_TX_WQEBBS` (`-EIO` otherwise), and when clamping `params.num_channels` to `max_nch`, clear `IFF_RXFH_CONFIGURED` and reset `TC_MQPRIO_MODE_CHANNEL` (`mlx5e_params_mqprio_reset`). Sync `fs->state_destroy` via `mlx5e_fs_set_state_destroy` in attach/detach, and only call `mlx5e_del_vlan_rules` after setting `MLX5E_STATE_DESTROYING` and flushing `priv->wq`. [F259, F266, F343]
- Null-check `fs->vlan` in paths reachable without `MLX5E_PROFILE_FEATURE_FS_VLAN` (`mlx5e_disable_cvlan_filter`, `mlx5e_fs_vlan_rx_add_vid`, `mlx5e_fs_vlan_rx_kill_vid`, `mlx5e_fix_features`). Update `active_cvlans` before `mlx5e_add_vlan_rule` (clearing on error) and `mlx5e_fs_del_vlan_rule`. Clear `NETIF_F_HW_VLAN_CTAG_RX` when `active_svlans` is non-empty, and call `netdev_update_features` under `netdev_lock` on S-VID add/kill. [F237, F265, F380]
- `priv->stats_nch`, `max_opened_tc`, `tx_ptp_opened`, and `rx_ptp_opened` are monotonic high-water marks; all `channel_stats[i]` readers and cleanup must bound on `priv->stats_nch` (not `max_nch` or `num_channels`). Indexing `priv->channels.c[queue]` requires holding `priv->state_lock` and checking `queue < priv->channels.num`. Size `num_tx_queues`, `txq2sq`, `txq2sq_stats`, and `tx_rates` via `mlx5e_get_max_num_txqs()`, and subtract `ptp_txqs`/`qos_txqs` in `mlx5e_calc_max_nch()`. [F261, F286, F289, F291]
- `mlx5e_set_tx_maxrate` shifts `rate` (Mbit/s) by `<< 10` to Kbit/s before `mlx5_rl_is_in_range`, `mlx5e_set_sq_maxrate`, or storing `tx_rates[index]`; `mlx5e_open_txqsq` reapplies `tx_rates[sq->txq_ix]`, and `mlx5e_close_txqsq` removes `sq->rate_limit`. When creating/replacing `priv->mqprio_rl`, update `params->mqprio.channel.hw_id` (`mlx5e_mqprio_rl_update_params`), freeing old instances only after `mlx5e_safe_switch_params` succeeds. [F250, F268, F269]
- `mlx5e_dcbnl_build_netdev` requires `vport_group_manager` and `qos` caps, and DSCP handlers require `MLX5_DSCP_SUPPORTED(mdev)`. `mlx5e_set_trust_state` must recalculate `params.tx_min_inline_mode` and reopen channels if changed. In `mlx5e_dcbnl_ieee_getets`, zero `ets->tc_tx_bw[i]` for `MLX5E_LOWEST_PRIO_GROUP` (`is_zero_bw_ets_tc`) after querying all TCs and before setting `tc_tsa[i]`; in `mlx5e_build_tc_tx_bw`, guard division with `if (num_ets_zero_bw)`. [F275, F301, F314, F344]
- In `en_fs_ethtool.c`, strip `FLOW_EXT | FLOW_MAC_EXT | FLOW_RSS` via `flow_type_mask()`, and reject `num_tuples <= 0` from `validate_flow()` before `get_flow_table()`. In `mlx5e_apply_netdev_addr()`, iterate with `mlx5e_for_each_hash_node()` and reset `hn->action = MLX5E_ACTION_NONE` on `MLX5E_ACTION_ADD`; in `mlx5e_fill_addr_array()`, skip `ether_addr_equal(ndev->dev_addr, hn->ai.addr)`. Create wildcard trap flow groups last at the highest `start_flow_index`, and in `mlx5e_set_*ttc_params()`, skip `mlx5_ttc_is_decrypted_esp_tt(tt)` and route `MLX5_TT_ANY` to direct TIR 0. [F235, F277, F292, F296, F297, F298, F299]
- Allocate `wq_sz << log_num_frags` entries in `mlx5e_init_wqe_alloc_info`; in `mlx5e_alloc_rq()`, terminate cyclic RQ segment `f = num_frags` (`lkey = params->terminate_lkey_be`) when `num_frags < (1 << log_num_frags)`. Bound `klm_pas_mtt` writes in `mlx5e_create_umr_mkey` by `mlx5e_mpwrq_umr_entry_size(umr_mode) * npages`, and guard `hw_objs->num_bfregs == 0` in `mlx5e_channel_pick_doorbell()`. [F282, F288, F324, F325]
- In `mlx5e_set_features()`, init `oper_features = features` and handle `NETIF_F_RXFCS` before `LRO`/`GRO_HW` when `features & (NETIF_F_GRO_HW | NETIF_F_LRO)` is non-zero, and after otherwise. Do not advertise `NETIF_F_LRO` if `tunnel_lro_vxlan`/`gre` is set, gate `NETIF_F_GRO_HW` on `mlx5e_hw_gro_supported`, and require `NETIF_F_GRO_HW` for `ETHTOOL_TCP_DATA_SPLIT_ENABLED`. `GRO_HW`/`LRO` are mutually exclusive with XDP, AF_XDP, and cyclic RQ (`mlx5e_set_xdp_feature` clears `xdp_features` when `packet_merge.type != MLX5E_PACKET_MERGE_NONE`). Keep `MLX5E_RQ_STATE_NO_CSUM_COMPLETE` set while `xdp_prog` is attached, and set `netns_immutable` and clear unsupported offloads on uplink representors (`mlx5e_fix_uplink_rep_features`). [F308, F341, F342, F374, F375, F378, F381]

## Synchronization
- Lock order is `rtnl_lock()` -> `netdev_lock(netdev)` -> `mutex_lock(&priv->state_lock)`: unlock `state_lock` before `netdev_update_features`, and acquire `netdev_lock` outside `state_lock` in `dcbnl_rtnl_ops` before `mlx5e_safe_switch_params`. Hold `rtnl_lock()` across `mlx5e_psp_register` and `mlx5e_set_xdp_feature` in `mlx5e_nic_init` when `reg_state == NETREG_REGISTERED`. [F226, F227, F367]
- Call `mlx5e_deactivate_priv_channels` (`netif_tx_disable` + `mlx5e_deactivate_channels`) before reconfiguring TX queues/TCs or closing channels. Deactivating/closing an RQ, TXQSQ, ICOSQ, or XDPSQ or disabling DIM must clear the `ENABLED`/`DIM` bit and call `synchronize_net()` before stopping queues, canceling DIM work, or freeing resources (and assign `rq->dim`/`sq->dim` before setting `DIM`). [F216, F218, F220]
- Recovery works (`mlx5e_tx_timeout_work`, `mlx5e_rq_timeout_work`, `mlx5e_tx_err_cqe_work`) must use a `netdev_trylock` loop aborting when `MLX5E_STATE_CHANNELS_ACTIVE` or `MLX5E_SQ_STATE_ENABLED` is cleared; clear that bit before `cancel_work_sync`, and guard `if (current_work() != &priv->tx_timeout_work)` in `mlx5e_cancel_tx_timeout_work`. [F219, F359]
- `mlx5e_set_rx_mode` and `async_event` run in atomic context and must queue `set_rx_mode_work` or `update_carrier_work` on `priv->wq`. `mlx5e_sync_netdev_addr` holds `netif_addr_lock_bh`, so `mlx5e_add_l2_to_hash` must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and defer hardware updates to `mlx5e_execute_l2_action` after unlocking. Hold `spin_lock_bh(&c->async_icosq_lock)` in `mlx5e_trigger_napi_icosq`, wrap `napi_schedule` with `local_bh_disable()`/`enable()` in `mlx5e_trigger_napi_sched`, and hold `td.list_lock` in `mlx5e_refresh_tirs`. [F228, F352, F353, F354, F355]
- In `arfs_may_expire_flow()`, `arfs_del_rules()`, and `_mlx5e_cleanup_tables`, clear `MLX5E_ARFS_STATE_ENABLED` and unlink rules onto a local `del_list` via `mlx5e_for_each_arfs_rule()` under `arfs->arfs_lock`, then cancel `arfs_work`, delete `rule->rule`, and free entries via `hlist_for_each_entry_safe()` after unlocking and before `destroy_workqueue(arfs->wq)` and `arfs_destroy_table`. [F198, F294]

## Object Lifetimes
- Resolve Socket Direct PFs via `mlx5_sd_get_adev` and pair `mlx5_sd_init`/`cleanup`. Run `profile->init` and `_mlx5e_resume` before `register_netdev`, and `unregister_netdev` before `_mlx5e_suspend` and `profile->cleanup`. `_mlx5e_suspend` must call `mlx5e_detach_netdev` before `mlx5e_destroy_mdev_resources`, and `_mlx5e_resume` must create `mdev` resources across all SD devices before `mlx5e_attach_netdev`. [F332, F346, F348]
- Open CQs before bound SQs/RQs; on close, `cancel_work_sync` all per-queue works (`dim->work`, `recover_work`, `rx_timeout_work`) and destroy `rq_xdpsq`, `rq`, `sqs`, `icosq`, and `async_icosq` before closing their CQs or freeing memory. In `mlx5e_queue_start`, deactivate and `mlx5e_close_channel(old)` before installing `new->c` and activating. [F205, F209, F221]
- When `mlx5e_xdp_set` swaps programs with `reset == false` while open, increment `prog` for every active `c->rq` and `c->xskrq` and put each `old_prog` via `mlx5e_rq_replace_xdp_prog`. Pre-set skip-release bits on all WQE slots on RQ init; in `mlx5e_free_rx_descs`, call `mlx5e_free_rx_missing_descs()` before `mlx5_wq_ll_pop()` on striding RQs, and capture `missing = mlx5_wq_cyc_missing(wq)` and `head` before `mlx5_wq_cyc_pop()` on cyclic RQs. [F203, F270, F305]
- In `mlx5e_ethtool_flow_replace`, copy `eth_rule->flow_spec = *fs` before `set_flow_attrs`, call `put_flow_table(eth_ft)` if `get_ethtool_rule` fails, and once `eth_rule->eth_ft = eth_ft` is set, clean up via `del_ethtool_rule` (which decrements `rss`, `tot_num_rules`, and `eth_ft->num_rules`, destroying and NULLing `eth_ft->ft` at 0); iterate `ethtool->rules` with `list_for_each_entry_safe()`. [F194, F267, F293, F340]
- In `mlx5e_create_flow_steering`, `arfs_add_default_rule` must use `mlx5e_rx_res_get_tirn_rss(rx_res, tt)` (as `mlx5e_arfs_create_tables` precedes `mlx5e_create_ttc_table`), clearing `NETIF_F_NTUPLE` if aRFS creation fails; call `mlx5e_fs_set_arfs(fs, NULL)` when freeing `arfs` and null-check `mlx5e_fs_get_arfs(fs)` in `mlx5e_arfs_disable`. [F197, F199, F334]
- In `mlx5e_set_sq_maxrate`, remove old `sq->rate_limit` and reset to `0` before `mlx5_rl_add_rate`, removing the new rate if `mlx5e_modify_sq` fails. Free `res->bfregs` only up to `res->num_bfregs`. In `mlx5e_dcbnl_ieee_setapp`, revert trust to `MLX5_QPTS_TRUST_PCP` on `fw_err` if `dscp_app_cnt == 0`, and only increment `dscp_app_cnt` when `is_new`. [F204, F214, F215]

## API Contracts
- `mlx5e_fs_init` and `mlx5e_create_netdev` return `NULL` on error; `mlx5e_tc_table_alloc`, `mlx5e_rx_res_create`, and `mlx5e_create_devlink` return `ERR_PTR`. `mlx5e_mqprio_rl_create` returns `NULL` when no TC has `max_rate > 0`, a pointer when configured, or `ERR_PTR` on error (check `IS_ERR(rl)` for error and `if (rl)` for cleanup). [F233, F234]
- Pass `vf + 1` in SR-IOV VF callbacks, `(esw, vport, max_tx_rate, min_tx_rate)` to `mlx5_eswitch_set_vport_rate`, and `lag_tx_port_affinity = i + 1` / `prio = tc << 1` in `mlx5e_create_tises`. Convert `dim_cq_moder.cq_period_mode` via `mlx5e_cq_period_mode()` for `mlx5e_modify_cq_moderation` (requires `cq_moderation` + `cq_period_mode_modify` caps), and pair `mlx5e_rq *` / `mlx5e_txqsq *` with matching DIM work fns in `mlx5e_dim_enable`. [F248, F249, F251, F253, F327, F345]
- Reject `mlx5e_ethtool_set_channels` when HTB or `TC_MQPRIO_MODE_CHANNEL` is active or configured RXFH RQT size changes, and bracket `mlx5e_safe_switch_params` with `mlx5e_arfs_disable`/`enable`. `MLX5E_PFLAG_TX_PORT_TS` and `TC_MQPRIO_MODE_CHANNEL` are mutually exclusive and blocked under HTB offload. [F258, F260]
- For `fte_match_set_lyr_2_4`, pass `ntohs()` for 16-bit fields to `MLX5_SET`/`MLX5E_FTE_SET` and copy IP/MAC addresses in network order to `MLX5_ADDR_OF`. In `en_dcbnl.c`, loop TCs with `i <= mlx5_max_tc(mdev)`, use `mlx5_max_tc(mdev) + 1` for cap counts, and return `0`/`1` (never negative errnos) from `setdcbx`, `getcap`, and `setall`. [F278, F283, F387]
- Size `create_rq`/`sq`/`cq` mailboxes with `MLX5_ST_SZ_BYTES(create_*_in) + sizeof(u64) * wq_ctrl.buf.npages`, wrap 1-arg `preactivate` fns with `MLX5E_DEFINE_PREACTIVATE_WRAPPER_CTX`, and use `mlx5e_wqc_has_room_for` + `mlx5_wq_cyc_ctr2ix` in `mlx5e_deactivate_txqsq`. Set `rq->buff.map_dir = DMA_BIDIRECTIONAL` when `xdp_prog` is set, and create/free a separate readable `rq->hd_page_pool` for SHAMPO when `netif_rxq_has_unreadable_mp` is true (`rq->hd_page_pool != rq->page_pool`). [F320, F326, F329, F365, F377]
- Run `mlx5e_build_txq_maps` before `netif_tx_start_all_queues` in `mlx5e_activate_priv_channels`, and sum only inactive queues in `mlx5e_get_base_stats`. Validate tunnels via `mlx5e_tunnel_features_check` in `mlx5e_features_check`, allow `TC_SETUP_BLOCK`/`FLOW_BLOCK_UNBIND` when `!netif_device_present` in `mlx5e_setup_tc`, handle `ETHTOOL_GRXRINGS` directly in `mlx5e_get_rxnfc`, and in `mlx5e_rx_flow_steer` (BH context), reject `skb->encapsulation` or non-IPv4/IPv6 with `-EPROTONOSUPPORT`, use `spin_lock_bh` + `GFP_ATOMIC`, and mod `filter_id` by `RPS_NO_FILTER`. [F369, F371, F379, F382, F386, F388]

## Error Handling
- Reset struct fields to `NULL` after extracting `PTR_ERR` from failed `ERR_PTR` constructors (`mlx5_add_flow_rules`, `mlx5_create_flow_table`/`group`, `mlx5_crypto_dek_init`, `page_pool_create`, `mlx5e_rx_res_create`). In `mlx5e_safe_switch_params` / `mlx5e_switch_priv_channels`, call `mlx5e_selq_prepare_params` before `mlx5e_open_channels`, and on failure restore/reactivate `old_chs`, close `new_chs`, and call `mlx5e_selq_cancel`; use a separate variable for rollback errors so `err` is preserved. [F241, F243, F256]
- Revert both `priv->fs->vlan_strip_disable` and `params.vlan_strip_disable` if `mlx5e_modify_channels_vsd` fails. In `mlx5e_create_tises()` rollback, start inner loop at `for (tc--; tc >= 0; tc--)` and reset `tc = MLX5_MAX_NUM_TC` each outer pass. Clamp `old_ntc = netdev->num_tc ? : 1` before dividing `old_num_txqs / old_ntc` in `mlx5e_update_tc_and_tx_queues`. [F264, F300, F316]

## Known Pitfalls
- Snapshot `profile`, `netdev`, `mdev`, and `ppriv` before `mlx5e_priv_cleanup` (which `memset`s `*priv`), and guard `if (priv->mdev)` / `if (priv->profile)` in cleanup. Access `mlx5e_mpw_info` via `mlx5e_get_mpw_info(rq, i)` (not `info[i]`), only touch `wqe->hdr` in `mlx5e_build_umr_wqe` (`rq->mpwqe.umr_wqe` is header-only), and size `union mlx5e_alloc_units` (`sizeof == 0`) via its flex array element size. [F273, F321, F322, F323]
- Bound loops in `mlx5e_vport_context_update_vlans()` and `mlx5e_fill_addr_array()` by `i >= size`, check `app->selector == IEEE_8021QAZ_APP_SEL_DSCP` and `app->protocol < MLX5E_MAX_DSCP` before indexing `dscp2prio[]`, gate `hn` dereference after search in `mlx5e_add_l2_to_hash()` on `found`, and only dereference `trap_event_ctx` inside `case MLX5_DRIVER_EVENT_TYPE_TRAP:` in `blocking_event`. [F280, F284, F295, F328]
- Use `mul_u32_u32(i, rq->mpwqe.mtts_per_wqe)` before `<< page_shift` in `mlx5e_alloc_rq`, `u64 nentries` in `mlx5e_create_umr_ksm_mkey`, `div_u64()` and a `: 1` clamp in `mlx5e_dcbnl_ieee_setmaxrate`, and `DIV_ROUND_UP` for `ds_cnt` in `mlx5e_build_umr_wqe`. Skip XSK channels in `mlx5e_wait_channels_min_rx_wqes`, use `time_before(jiffies, exp_time)`, and queue `rq->rx_timeout_work` on timeout. [F312, F313, F317, F318, F319, F347]
