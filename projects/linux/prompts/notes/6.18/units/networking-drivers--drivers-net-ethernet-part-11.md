# NETWORKING DRIVERS — drivers/net/ethernet (part 11)

This unit covers five Ethernet drivers: SMSC EPIC/100 PCI (`epic100.c`), SMC 9194 ISA/platform (`smc9194.c`), SMC 91c92 PCMCIA (`smc91c92_cs.c`), SpacemiT K1 SoC EMAC (`k1_emac.c`), and Sun Cassini PCI Gigabit (`cassini.c`, `cassini.h`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `epic100.c`, `cur_tx`, `dirty_tx`, `cur_rx`, and `dirty_rx` are monotonically increasing unsigned counters; index via `% TX_RING_SIZE`/`% RX_RING_SIZE` and compute occupancy via `cur - dirty`. Set `dirty_rx = (unsigned int)(i - RX_RING_SIZE)`, break from `epic_rx()` refill before setting `DescOwn` or advancing `dirty_rx` if `netdev_alloc_skb()` fails, and in `epic_restart()` program `PRxCDAR`/`PTxCDAR` with DMA addresses of `cur_rx % RX_RING_SIZE` and `dirty_tx % RX_RING_SIZE`. When `!netif_running(dev)`, `ethtool_begin()`/`ethtool_complete()` track `ethtool_ops_nesting` to wake (`GENCTL = 0x0200`, `NVCTL |= 0x4800`) on `0 -> 1` and sleep (`GENCTL = 0x0008`, `NVCTL & ~0x483c`) on `1 -> 0`. [F382, F383, F385, F413]
- In `smc9194.c` and `smc91c92_cs.c`, increment `packets_waiting` on TX queue, decrement it in `smc_tx_err()`/`smc_tx()` on failed packets, and add it to `tx_packets` then zero it on `IM_TX_EMPTY_INT`. On `IM_ALLOC_INT`, `smc_interrupt()` must clear `IM_ALLOC_INT` from `mask`, call `smc_hardware_send_packet(dev)`, set `IM_TX_EMPTY_INT | IM_TX_INT` in `mask`, and wake the queue. Once `!(inw(ioaddr + FIFO_PORTS) & FP_RXEMPTY)` in `smc_rcv()`/`smc_rx()`, every exit path (including `netdev_alloc_skb()` failure and `RS_ERRORS`) must write `MC_RELEASE` to `MMU_CMD` in Bank 2. [F379, F415, F416]
- In `smc91c92_cs.c`, on `IM_RX_OVRN_INT` while `smc->duplex` is set, `smc_interrupt()` sets `smc->rx_ovrn = 1` instead of resetting in IRQ; `smc_start_xmit()` and `media_check()` must write `MC_RESET` to `MMU_CMD` in Bank 2 and clear `rx_ovrn = 0`. Whenever `TCR` in Bank 0 is re-enabled (`smc_reset()`, `smc_tx_err()`, `smc_eph_irq()`), OR `smc->duplex` (`TCR_FDUPLX` or `0`) into `TCR`. [F380, F381]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `emac_rx_frame_good()` must reject `len <= ETH_FCS_LEN || len > priv->dma_buf_sz` before `emac_rx_clean_desc()` subtracts `ETH_FCS_LEN` for `skb_put()`. Every consumed descriptor in `emac_rx_clean_desc()` (valid or error) must set `rx_buf->skb = NULL` and advance `rx_ring->tail` so `emac_alloc_rx_desc_buffers()` refills from `rx_ring->head` while `!rx_buf->skb`. `emac_set_mac_addr()` must program both `MAC_ADDRESS1_HIGH` and `MAC_FC_SOURCE_ADDRESS_HIGH`. In `union emac_hw_tx_stats` and `union emac_hw_rx_stats`, every field of `stats` must be a `u64` ordered by hardware counter index. [F368, F390, F406, F425]
- In `cassini.c`, `cas_xmit_tx_ringN()` appending a target-abort descriptor via `tx_tiny_map()` must set `tx_tiny_use[ring][entry].used = 1` and increment `tx_tiny_use[ring][tentry].nbufs` on the SKB's initial slot `tentry`. `cas_tx_ringN()` must subtract `skb_shinfo(skb)->nr_frags + tx_tiny_use[ring][entry].nbufs + 1` from `count` (breaking if `< 0`) before freeing `skbs[entry]`, and advance `entry` past slots with `tx_tiny_use[ring][entry].used` set without unmapping them. [F374, F407]
- In `cassini.c`, post RX descriptors to `REG_RX_KICK` (`REG_PLUS_RX_KICK1`) only on multiples of 4, and advance RX completion indices in `cas_rx_ringN()` by `(1 + CAS_VAL(RX_COMP1_SKIP, words[0])) & RX_COMP_ENTRY`. In `cas_rx_process_pkt()`, clamp `hlen = min(cp->page_size - off, dlen)` and abort if `hlen < 0`. `cp->net_stats` and `cp->stat_lock` are sized `N_TX_RINGS + 1`; `cas_get_stats()` folds MAC error registers and `net_stats[0..N_TX_RINGS-1]` into `net_stats[N_TX_RINGS]`, then calls `cas_clear_mac_err(cp)` and zeroes `net_stats[i]` under `stat_lock`. Keep `ethtool_cassini_statnames` and `data[i++]` in `cas_get_ethtool_stats()` in lockstep. [F372, F377, F398, F401, F430]
- In `cassini.c`, `cas_set_link_modes()` must update `MAC_RX_CFG_STRIP_FCS`, `cp->crc_size` (0 full-duplex, 4 half-duplex), and `cp->min_frame_size` in lockstep. `REG_MAC_ADDRN(0..44)` uses 0..2 for station MAC, `i * 3 + 0..2` (`1 <= i <= 15`) for multicast in `cas_process_mc_list()`, and 42..44 for pause control. `cas_saturn_firmware_init()` must check `fw->size >= 2` before reading `fw->data[0..1]` and subtracting 2. In `cas_txmac_interrupt()`, return early for `MAC_TX_DEFER_TIMER` only when `!(txmac_stat & ~MAC_TX_DEFER_TIMER)`. [F371, F399, F402, F411]

## Synchronization
- In `epic100.c`, hold `ep->napi_lock` across `napi_schedule_prep()` and `epic_napi_irq_off()` in `epic_interrupt()`, and across clearing `EpicNapiEvent` in `INTSTAT` and `epic_napi_irq_on()` after `napi_complete_done()` in `epic_poll()`. Populate `bufaddr`, `buflength`, and `tx_skbuff`/`rx_skbuff` before setting `DescOwn`. [F331, F332]
- In `smc9194.c`, `smc_hardware_send_packet()` runs in both process and hardirq contexts, so free `lp->saved_skb` with `dev_kfree_skb_any()`. In `smc91c92_cs.c`, `media_check()` must restore `saved_bank` and drop `smc->lock` before calling `smc_interrupt()`, then re-read `saved_bank` from `BANK_SELECT` after relocking. [F335, F469]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `emac_alloc_rx_desc_buffers()` writes `rx_desc`, calls `dma_wmb()`, and sets `RX_DESC_0_OWN` via `WRITE_ONCE()`, paired with `READ_ONCE(rx_desc->desc0)` and `dma_rmb()` in `emac_rx_clean_desc()`. `emac_tx_mem_map()` withholds `TX_DESC_0_OWN` on `old_head` until all fragments are written, then calls `dma_wmb()` and `WRITE_ONCE()` before `emac_dma_start_transmit()`. `emac_tx_clean_desc()` must hold `netif_tx_lock(ndev)` while advancing `tx_ring->tail`, freeing TX buffers, and checking `emac_tx_avail(priv)` to wake the queue. [F339, F458, F460]
- In `k1_emac.c`, all accesses to `tx_stats`, `rx_stats`, `tx_stats_off`, `rx_stats_off`, and `emac_stats_update()` must hold `priv->stats_lock` (`spin_lock_bh()` in process context because `emac_stats_timer()` runs in softirq), and `emac_read_stat_cnt()` must use `readl_poll_timeout_atomic()`. [F340, F456]
- In `cassini.c`, lock order is `cp->pm_mutex` outermost (held across `cas_open()`, `cas_close()`, `cas_ioctl()`, `cas_suspend()`, `cas_resume()`, and `cas_shutdown()` to serialize `hw_running` and `opened`), then `cp->lock` (`spin_lock_irqsave()`), then `cp->tx_lock[0..N_TX_RINGS-1]` in ascending order via `spin_lock_nested(&cp->tx_lock[i], i)` (`cas_lock_tx()`, `cas_lock_all_save()`). Call `napi_disable(&cp->napi)` in `cas_close()` before `pm_mutex` and `cas_lock_all_save()`. In `cas_get_stats()`, acquire `stat_lock[N_TX_RINGS]` with `spin_lock_irqsave()` before `stat_lock[0..N_TX_RINGS-1]` or `cas_clear_mac_err(cp)`. [F343, F461, F462, F463]
- In `cassini.c`, scheduling `cp->reset_task` increments `reset_task_pending` and `reset_task_pending_{all,spare,mtu}`, and `cas_reset_task()` must decrement `reset_task_pending` on every exit. `cas_shutdown()` must set `hw_running = 0`, call `timer_delete_sync(&cp->link_timer)`, and wait for `atomic_read(&cp->reset_task_pending) == 0` before `cas_lock_all_save()`. `cas_rxc_init()` zeroes words 1..3 and sets `RX_COMP4_ZERO` in word 4; `cas_rx_ringN()` must check both `CAS_VAL(RX_COMP1_TYPE, words[0]) != 0` and `(words[3] & RX_COMP4_ZERO) == 0`. [F328, F348, F350]
- In `cassini.c`, protect `rx_inuse_list` with `rx_inuse_lock` and `rx_spare_list` (`rx_spares_needed`) with `rx_spare_lock`. When reposting a page with `page_count(page->buffer) > 1` in `cas_page_spare()` or `cas_post_rxds_ringN()`, queue it on `rx_inuse_list` under `rx_inuse_lock` and replace it via `cas_page_dequeue()`. `cas_page_dequeue()` must drop `rx_spare_lock` before `cas_spare_recover()` and recheck `list_empty(&cp->rx_spare_list)` after relocking; `cas_spare_recover()` must `list_splice_init()` `rx_inuse_list` under `rx_inuse_lock`, iterate with `list_for_each_safe()`, and splice entries still having `page_count > 1` back under `rx_inuse_lock`. [F326, F346, F409]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `epic_rx()`, when passing `ep->rx_skbuff[entry]` directly to `netif_receive_skb()`, unmap `ep->rx_ring[entry].bufaddr` and set `ep->rx_skbuff[entry] = NULL`. In `smc9194.c` and `smc91c92_cs.c`, once `skb` is stored in `saved_skb`, every path that frees `skb` (including `numPages > 7`, which must free `skb`, clear `saved_skb = NULL`, and return `NETDEV_TX_OK`, or `smc_hardware_send_packet()`) must clear `saved_skb = NULL`. In `smc91c92_cs.c`, `smc_open()` increments `link->open` only after `pcmcia_dev_present(link)` and `check_sig(link) >= 0`, paired with decrement in `smc_close()`. [F316, F318, F320, F487]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `emac_tx_mem_map()` and `emac_tx_map_frag()` pack up to two fragments per TX descriptor via `frag_idx % 2` (even allocates at `head` for `buf[0]`, odd reuses for `buf[1]`), set `dma_addr` and `map_as_page` only after `!dma_mapping_error(dev, addr)`, and set `tx_buf->skb = skb` only on the final fragment (`f == frag_num`) so `emac_free_tx_buf()` unmaps non-zero `dma_addr` slots with the matching API and frees `skb` once. [F321, F355, F391]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `emac_up()` calls `pm_runtime_get_sync()` and `emac_phy_connect()`, and any later failure in `emac_up()` or teardown in `emac_down()` must call `phy_disconnect()` and `pm_runtime_put_sync()`. Before `pm_runtime_put_sync()`, `emac_down()` must stop `free_irq()`, `napi_disable()`, `timer_delete_sync(&priv->txtimer)`, `cancel_work_sync(&priv->tx_timeout_task)`, and `timer_delete_sync(&priv->stats_timer)`, and under `stats_lock` call `emac_stats_update()` and copy `tx_stats`/`rx_stats` into `tx_stats_off`/`rx_stats_off`. [F323, F341, F366]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `emac_mdio_init()` and `emac_phy_connect()` must `of_node_put()` device_nodes on all paths; `of_phy_register_fixed_link()` in `emac_probe()` requires `devm_add_action_or_reset(dev, emac_phy_deregister_fixed_link, dev->of_node)`. `emac_suspend()` always disables `priv->bus_clk`, so `emac_resume()` must re-enable `bus_clk` before checking `netif_running(ndev)` and disable it if `emac_open()` fails. In `cassini.c`, restore `PCI_CACHE_LINE_SIZE` to `orig_cacheline_size` on `cas_init_one()` failure and in `cas_remove_one()`. [F324, F325, F330, F436]

## API Contracts
- In `epic_start_xmit()`, `smc_wait_to_send_packet()`, and `cas_start_xmit()`, return `NETDEV_TX_OK` when `skb_padto()` fails. In `smc9194.c` and `smc91c92_cs.c`, `smc_interrupt()` must save/restore `BANK_SELECT`, Bank 2 `POINTER`, and interrupt mask, while `smc_tx()`/`smc_tx_err()` save/restore `PNR_ARR`. In `smc91c92_cs.c`, `mdio_read()`, `mdio_write()`, and `mii_*` callers must select Bank 3 (`SMC_SELECT_BANK(3)`) and restore the bank under `smc->lock`. [F319, F446, F486]
- In `smc91c92_cs.c`, `osi_config()` adds `0x10` to `dev->base_addr` for Ositech multifunction cards (`MANFID_OSITECH`, `cardid != PRODID_OSITECH_SEVEN`), so `OSITECH_AUI_PWR`/`OSITECH_RESET_ISR` accesses relative to `dev->base_addr` must subtract `0x10`. CIS MAC parsers must validate lengths and NUL-termination before `cvt_ascii_address()`/`eth_hw_addr_set()` (`mhz_setup()`: `len >= 13`, `buf[12] = '\0'`; `pcmcia_get_versmac()`: `ns > 3`; `pcmcia_osi_mac()`: `TupleDataLen >= 8`). [F360, F389]
- In `k1_emac.c`, `platform_set_drvdata()` stores `struct emac_priv *` (not `struct net_device *`). `emac_config_dt()` bounds `tx_delay`/`rx_delay` by `EMAC_MAX_DELAY_PS` and converts them in-place via `delay_ps_to_unit()`, so `emac_update_delay_line()` writes them directly. `priv->regmap_apmu` accesses must add `priv->regmap_apmu_offset` to `APMU_EMAC_CTRL_REG`/`APMU_EMAC_DLINE_REG` and use `regmap_update_bits()`/`regmap_set_bits()`. `emac_start_xmit()` requires `emac_tx_avail(priv) >= nfrags + 1` before mapping and stops the queue if `<= MAX_SKB_FRAGS + 2`. `emac_get_stat_tx_drops()` must aggregate `dev_dstats_tx_dropped()` with `u64_stats_fetch_begin`/`retry`. [F352, F369, F419, F427, F488, F491]
- In `cassini.c`, `CAS_TABORT(cp)` returns `2` or `1` (never `0`): check `CAS_TABORT(cp) == 1` when testing if disabled and multiply `MAX_SKB_FRAGS + 1` by `CAS_TABORT(cp)` in `TX_BUFFS_AVAIL`. `cas_reset(cp, blkflag)` and `cas_init_hw(cp, restart_link)` have opposite boolean senses (`blkflag` is `!(pending_all > 0)`). In `cas_set_multicast()`, clear `MAC_RX_CFG_EN` and poll until clear, then clear `MAC_RX_CFG_PROMISC_EN | MAC_RX_CFG_HASH_FILTER_EN` and poll until `MAC_RX_CFG_HASH_FILTER_EN` clears before `cas_setup_multicast()`. [F362, F364, F376]
- In `cassini.c`, `ethtool_register_table` uses negative offsets for MII (`cas_phy_read(cp, -offsets)`) and non-negative for MMIO, and `cas_read_regs()` takes `u32` entry count (`regs->len / sizeof(u32)`). `cas_hp_inst_t` tables must have non-NULL `note` on active entries and end with `{ NULL }`. In `cas_ioctl()`, `SIOCGMIIPHY` sets `data->phy_id = cp->phy_addr` and falls through to `SIOCGMIIREG`, bracketing `cas_phy_read()`/`cas_phy_write()` with `cas_mif_poll(cp, 0)`/`cas_mif_poll(cp, 1)`. `cas_rx_process_pkt()` must copy at least `RX_COPY_MIN` (64) bytes (or `hlen`) into `skb->data` before attaching fragments, and when `cp->crc_size` is non-zero fold trailing FCS out of `RX_COMP4_TCP_CSUM` via `csum_partial()` and set `CHECKSUM_COMPLETE` only for `ETH_P_IP`. [F365, F412, F431, F432, F494, F495]

## Error Handling
- In `epic100.c`, `mdio_read()` returns `0xffff` on timeout (not negative). In `smc9194.c`, `smc_init()` returns `ERR_PTR(...)` and never `NULL` (check `IS_ERR()`). In `smc91c92_cs.c`, `check_sig()` returns `s & 0xff` or `-ENODEV` (test `< 0` in signed type); `smc->base` is only ioremapped by `mhz_mfc_config()`, so check `smc->base`, `link->resource[2]->end`, or card IDs before use/unmap; `media_check()` must verify `(smc->cfg & CFG_MII_SELECT) && smc->mii_if.phy_id >= 0` before MII reads. [F356, F357, F358, F359, F361]

## External Contracts & Known Pitfalls
- In `epic100.c`, `LAN0`/`MC0` use 4-byte strides (`+ i*4`); descriptors use host byte order (`GENCTL` byteswaps under `#ifdef __BIG_ENDIAN`); after waking/resetting via `GENCTL`, write `0x0008` to `TEST1` 16 times before MII access; in `epic_rx_err()`, check `status == EpicRemoved` (`0xffffffff`) before bitmask checks. [F384, F414, F435, F443]
- In `smc9194.c` and `smc91c92_cs.c`, SMC91xxx framing adds 6 bytes: TX writes `length + 6` (`((length & 0xfffe) + 6) / 256 - 1` pages), RX subtracts 5 if `RS_ODDFRAME` and 6 otherwise. `smc_rx()` (`smc91c92_cs.c`) must call `netdev_alloc_skb(dev, packet_length + 2)` before subtracting the 5/6-byte overhead, and `smc_reset()` waking from `CTL_POWERDOWN` must write `SMC_SELECT_BANK(0)` before any read. `smc9194.c` `interfaces[]` uses `dev->if_port - 1` (1..2); `smc91c92_cs.c` `if_names[]` uses `dev->if_port` (0..2). [F387, F424, F433, F447]
- In `k1_emac.c`, descriptor `total_cnt - 1` must set `RX_DESC_1_END_RING`/`TX_DESC_1_END_RING`; `emac_set_mac_addr_reg()` writes three 32-bit registers with two little-endian bytes each; `emac_set_rx_mode()` packs the 64-bit hash into `u32 mc_filter[4]` at 16 bits per word (`hash / 16`, `hash % 16`). `emac_update_counter()` must hold the upper 32 bits in a `u64` before `<< 32` and increment on `old_low > new_low`; `emac_ethtool_stats.offset` stores `offsetof(...) / sizeof(u64)`; `MREGBIT_SOFTWARE_RESET` in `DMA_CONFIGURATION` is not self-clearing (write, sleep 9–10 ms, write `0x0`, sleep 9–10 ms). [F392, F395, F417, F426, F428, F442]
- In `cassini.c`, `cas_init_rx_dma()`/`cas_init_tx_dma()` must add the ring offset to `u64 desc_dma = cp->block_dvma` before `>> 32`, and `cas_init_tx_dma()` must not write 0 to `REG_TX_KICKN(i)`. `cas_init_one()` must call `pci_save_state(pdev)` before `cas_hard_reset()` and must not set `NETIF_F_HW_CSUM | NETIF_F_SG` when `CAS_FLAG_NO_HW_CSUM` is set. `cas_pcs_link_check()` must read `REG_PCS_MII_STATUS` twice if link is 0 and require both `PCS_MII_STATUS_AUTONEG_COMP` and `PCS_MII_STATUS_REMOTE_FAULT` for remote fault; `cas_phy_init()` must clear `REG_PCS_CFG` before writing `REG_PCS_MII_ADVERT` and re-enable `PCS_CFG_EN` after; `cas_get_vpd_info()` must bracket `REG_EXPANSION_ROM_RUN_START` reads with `BIM_LOCAL_DEV_PROM | BIM_LOCAL_DEV_PAD` and `0` in `REG_BIM_LOCAL_DEV_EN`. [F410, F420, F448, F449, F451, F452, F453]
