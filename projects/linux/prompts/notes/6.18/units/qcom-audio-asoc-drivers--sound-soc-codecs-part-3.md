# QCOM AUDIO (ASoC) DRIVERS — sound/soc/codecs (part 3)

Qualcomm ASoC codec and smart speaker amplifier drivers covering the WCD9380/WCD9385 and WCD9390/WCD9395 master codecs and SoundWire slaves (with USB Type-C analog audio switch support), and the WSA881x, WSA883x, and WSA884x SoundWire Class-D smart speaker amplifiers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `wcd939x-sdw.c`, `wcd->regmap` is allocated only on TX (`wcd->is_tx`); `wcd9390_remove` and runtime PM callbacks must check `if (wcd->regmap)`. `wcd9390_probe` starts it in `regcache_cache_only(true)` until enumeration syncs it, and `wcd939x_sdw_runtime_suspend` sets `regcache_cache_only(true)` and `regcache_mark_dirty()` so `wcd939x_sdw_runtime_resume` syncs state to hardware. [F272, F295]
- Updating `comp1_enable`/`comp2_enable` in `wcd938x_set_compander`/`wcd939x_set_compander` or `wcd->port_enable[portidx]` in `wcd938x_set_swr_port`/`wcd939x_set_swr_port` must call `wcd938x_connect_port`/`wcd939x_connect_port` to sync `wcd->port_config[port_num - 1].ch_mask`. `wsa881x_hw_params`, `wsa883x_hw_params`, and `wcd939x_sdw_hw_params` must reset `active_ports = 0` (and `wcd->sconfig.ch_count = 1` in `wcd939x_sdw_hw_params`) before compacting `port_config` for `sdw_stream_add_slave`. [F284, F285, F300]
- In `wcd938x_sdw_priv` and `wcd939x_sdw_priv`, `port_config` is sized to `WCD938X_MAX_SWR_PORTS` / `WCD939X_MAX_SWR_PORTS` (the larger RX count) as the struct is shared by RX and TX. For an `N`-element `sdw_dpn_prop` array in `wcd9390_probe`, `wsa881x_probe`, or `wsa883x_probe`, `sink_ports` / `source_ports` must be `GENMASK(N - 1, 0)`, not `GENMASK(N, 0)`. [F302, F328]
- In `wcd938x.c` and `wcd939x.c`, PDM watchdog IRQs (`hphr_pdm_wd_int`, `hphl_pdm_wd_int`, `aux_pdm_wd_int` / `ear_pdm_wd_int`) are disabled via `disable_irq_nosync` after `request_threaded_irq` in probe; PA DAPM widgets must pair `enable_irq` in `POST_PMU` with `disable_irq_nosync` in `PRE_PMD`. In `wcd938x_codec_ear_dac_event` and `wcd938x_codec_enable_ear_pa`, `wcd938x->ear_rx_path` is latched from `WCD938X_DIGITAL_CDC_EAR_PATH_CTL` in `PRE_PMU` and must be reused (not re-read) in `POST_PMU`, `PRE_PMD`, and `POST_PMD`. [F248, F313]
- In `wcd938x.c`, `wcd938x->flyback_cur_det_disable` refcounts active EAR/AUX paths: `wcd938x_codec_ear_dac_event` and `wcd938x_codec_aux_dac_event` clear `WCD938X_EN_CUR_DET_MASK` in `WCD938X_FLYBACK_EN` when incrementing from 0 in `PRE_PMU`, and `wcd938x_codec_enable_ear_pa` / `wcd938x_codec_enable_aux_pa` restore it when decrementing to 0 in `POST_PMD`. [F287]
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x` `codec_hphl_dac_event` and `codec_hphr_dac_event`, `POST_PMU` skips the 5ms delay when the opposite channel's compander is enabled in software (`comp1_enable`/`comp2_enable`) but not yet set in `COMP_CTL_0`. DAPM widget shift values on `ADC1`..`ADC4` (0..3) must match `WCD_ADC1`..`WCD_ADC4` bit indices in `status_mask`. [F312, F334]
- In `wcd938x_micbias_control` and `wcd939x_micbias_control`, 1-based `micb_num` (`MIC_BIAS_1`..`4`) must be validated by `switch (micb_num)` before indexing `micb_ref` and `pullup_ref` with `micb_num - 1`. On `MICB_DISABLE`, if `micb_ref[micb_index]` reaches 0 while `pullup_ref[micb_index] > 0`, transition to pull-up mode (not disabled) and skip `PRE`/`POST_MICBIAS_2_OFF` events. [F306, F314]
- In `wcd938x_wcd_mbhc_calc_impedance` and `wcd939x_wcd_mbhc_calc_impedance`, save `ANA_MBHC_BTN5..7`, `MBHC_CTL_CLK`, `MBHC_NEW_ZDET_ANA_CTL`, and `ANA_MBHC_ELECT` `FSM_EN` before ramps, and restore them plus `ANA_MBHC_MECH` at `zdet_complete` on all exits. On WCD938x, if left `z1L` is `WCD938X_ZDET_FLOATING_IMPEDANCE` or `> WCD938X_ZDET_VAL_100K`, reset `zdet_param_ptr` and `d1` to `&zdet_param[1]` and `d1_a[1]` before the right ramp. [F290, F316]
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x` `mbhc_get_result_params`, bound both ramp-completion polling and `ramp_down` discharge loops by `WCD938X_ZDET_NUM_MEASUREMENTS` / `WCD939X_ZDET_NUM_MEASUREMENTS`. In `wcd_mbhc_qfuse_cal`, EFUSE byte `q1` is sign-magnitude: bit 7 (`0x80`) indicates subtraction and must be masked off (`q1 & 0x7F`) before multiplying by the step (25 on WCD938x, 10 on WCD939x). [F310, F317]
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x` `mbhc_moisture_config` and `mbhc_get_moisture_status`, force moisture detection to `R_OFF` in `MBHC_NEW_CTL_2` whenever `mbhc_cfg.moist_rref == R_OFF`, `!mbhc_cfg.hphl_swh`, or (on WCD939x) `typec_analog_mux` is true. [F291]
- In `wcd938x.c`, `wcd938x_swap_gnd_mic` and `wcd938x_remove` must call `mux_control_deselect(wcd938x->us_euro_mux)` only when `mux_setup_done` is true; if `mux_control_try_select` fails in `wcd938x_swap_gnd_mic`, set `mux_setup_done = false` without toggling `mux_state`. In `wcd939x.c`, `wcd939x_swap_gnd_mic()` and `wcd939x_bind()` cleanup must check `wcd939x->typec_analog_mux` (and `wcd939x->typec_switch` in `wcd939x_swap_gnd_mic()`) before using/releasing `typec_switch`. [F274, F293]
- In `wcd938x_soc_codec_probe` and `wcd939x_soc_codec_probe`, variant-specific ALSA controls (`wcd9380`/`wcd9385_snd_controls`, `wcd9390`/`wcd9395_snd_controls`) must be registered via `snd_soc_add_component_controls()` only after reading the chip ID from `DIGITAL_EFUSE_REG_0`. [F343]
- In `wsa881x.c`, `wsa881x_set_port` must call `wsa881x_boost_ctrl` when `port_enable[WSA881X_PORT_BOOST]` changes, while `wsa881x_spkr_pa_event` gates visense TXFE/ADC in `POST_PMU`/`POST_PMD` on `port_prepared[WSA881X_PORT_VISENSE]`. `wsa881x_probe` must set `pdev->prop.clk_stop_mode1 = true`. [F297, F352]
- In `wsa883x_init`, `WSA8830`/`WSA8832` variants (`WSA883X_OTP_REG_0 & WSA883X_ID_MASK`) must override `wsa883x->comp_offset` from `COMP_OFFSET2` to `COMP_OFFSET3` and update `WSA883X_DRE_OFFSET_MASK` in `WSA883X_DRE_CTL_0`. In `wsa883x_get_reset`, `powerdown` GPIO (`wsa883x->sd_n`) is requested only when `sd_reset` is `NULL`; pass logical high (`1`) in `wsa883x_get_reset`/`wsa883x_reset_assert` to assert shutdown and `0` in `wsa883x_reset_deassert`. [F345, F349]

## Synchronization
- In `wcd938x_bind()` and `wcd939x_bind()`, `wcd938x_irq_init()`/`wcd939x_irq_init()` must initialize `virq` and `irq_chip` and assign `virq` to `sdw_priv[AIF1_PB]->slave_irq` and `sdw_priv[AIF1_CAP]->slave_irq` before `snd_soc_register_component()`. [F255]
- In `wcd938x_mbhc_micb_adjust_voltage()` and `wcd939x_mbhc_micb_adjust_voltage()`, `micb_lock` must be held continuously across reading micbias enable/VOUT, switching enabled micbias to pull-up/pull-down, updating VOUT, re-enabling micbias, and sleeping 2ms. [F263]
- Under `wsa883x->sp_lock`, `wsa883x_spkr_event` sets `pa_on = true` in `POST_PMU` and `false` in `PRE_PMD`. `wsa883x_get_temp` must hold `sp_lock`: if `pa_on`, return cached `wsa883x->temperature * 1000` without touching `WSA883X_PA_FSM_BYP` or `WSA883X_TADC_VALUE_CTL`; if `!pa_on`, balance `pm_runtime_resume_and_get(wsa883x->dev)` with `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend(wsa883x->dev)` and clear `WSA883X_PA_FSM_BYP` to `0x0` before any exit. [F251, F298]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `wcd938x_bind` and `wcd939x_bind`, after `component_bind_all`, `device_link_add()` with `DL_FLAG_STATELESS | DL_FLAG_PM_RUNTIME` must link `rxdev` to `txdev` and `dev` to `txdev`/`rxdev` so `txdev` never suspends while `rxdev` or `dev` is active; unwind links, `of_sdw_find_device_by_node` refs (`put_device`), and `component_unbind_all` in reverse order on error and in `.unbind`. [F241, F337]
- In `wcd938x_soc_codec_probe` and `wcd939x_soc_codec_probe`, hardware register accesses (efuse read, `io_init`, `DIGITAL_INTR_LEVEL_0`) must follow `tx_sdw_dev->initialization_complete` inside `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()`/`pm_runtime_put()`. Unwind `wcd_clsh_ctrl_alloc`, the three watchdog IRQs, and `wcd938x_mbhc_init`/`wcd939x_mbhc_init` in reverse order on probe failure and in `soc_codec_remove`. [F242, F336]
- In `wcd9390_probe`, `wcd->regmap` must use non-devres `regmap_init_sdw` and be freed with `regmap_exit` after `component_del` in `wcd9390_remove` (avoiding `devres_release_group` in `component_unbind` if aggregate bind fails). [F245]
- When `wcd939x->typec_analog_mux` is enabled, `wcd939x_add_typec` registers the codec's mux/switch in `wcd939x_probe`, whereas `fwnode_typec_switch_get()` for remote `wcd939x->typec_switch` must be deferred to `wcd939x_bind`. In `wcd938x_remove` and `wcd939x_remove`, after `component_master_del()`, call `pm_runtime_disable()`, `pm_runtime_set_suspended()`, and `pm_runtime_dont_use_autosuspend()`. [F339, F353]

## API Contracts
- `snd_soc_component_write_field()` takes an unshifted value and shifts it by the mask's lowest set bit (`wcd939x_set_swr_clk_rate()`), whereas `snd_soc_component_update_bits()` and `regmap_update_bits()` require a pre-shifted value (`wcd938x_set_swr_clk_rate()`, `wsa881x_put_pa_gain()`). [F281]
- SoundWire port numbers (`ch_info->port_num`, `prepare_ch->num`) are 1-based: index `port_config[]` and `port_prepared[]` with `num - 1`, whereas `sdw_slave.m_port_map[]` is populated starting at `&pdev->m_port_map[1]`. SoundWire `regmap_config` structs (`wcd939x`, `wsa881x`, `wsa883x`) must not set `.can_multi_write = true`. [F279, F363]
- Master platform drvdata is `wcd938x_priv *` / `wcd939x_priv *`, while child SoundWire slave drvdata (`rxdev`, `txdev`) is `wcd938x_sdw_priv *` / `wcd939x_sdw_priv *`. In `wsa883x_probe()`, pass `wsa883x_priv *` as drvdata to `devm_hwmon_device_register_with_info()`. In `wcd939x_add_typec()`, `mux_desc.drvdata` and `sw_desc.drvdata` are `wcd939x_priv *`, while `devm_add_action_or_reset()` unregister callbacks take `typec_mux_dev *` and `typec_switch_dev *`. [F330, F331, F332]
- In `wcd938x_probe()` and `wcd939x_probe()`, initialize `common.dev` and `common.max_bias` (`<= WCD_MAX_MICBIAS`) before `wcd938x_populate_dt_data()` / `wcd939x_populate_dt_data()`. In `wcd938x.h` and `wcd939x.h`, keep `*_sdw_free`, `*_sdw_set_sdw_stream`, and `*_sdw_hw_params` declarations in sync with their `#else` `-EOPNOTSUPP` stubs. [F278, F301]
- In `wcd_irq_chip_map()`, call `irq_set_nested_thread(virq, 1)` on the root `wcd_irq_chip` mapping so `wcd_interrupt_callback()` can dispatch `regmap_irq_thread()` synchronously via `handle_nested_irq()`. [F358]
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x` `mbhc_zdet_ramp()`, `zl` and `zr` are individually optional (`NULL` for single-channel); guard measurements and writes with `NULL` checks. In `mbhc_program_btn_thr()`, reject `num_btn > WCD_MBHC_DEF_BUTTONS` (8) before indexing `btn_high[i]` and `ANA_MBHC_BTN0 + i`. [F275, F307]
- `wcd938x_ear_pa_put_gain` must return `-EINVAL` if `wcd938x->comp1_enable` is true. `wcd939x_rx_hph_mode_put` must validate `item[0]` against `wcd939x->variant`, returning `-EINVAL` for `CLS_H_HIFI`/`CLS_AB_HIFI` on `CHIPID_WCD9390` or out-of-range values. [F292, F311]
- In `wsa881x_put_pa_gain`, bracket `WSA881X_SPKR_DRV_GAIN` updates with `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` (allowing `-EACCES`) and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()`, stepping from `min_gain` (`G_0DB` if `max_gain > G_4P5DB`, else `max_gain + 3`) down to `max_gain` with `usleep_range(1000, 1010)` before and after each write. [F351]
- In `wsa883x_get_temp()`, `wsa883x->temperature` and interpolation math are in degrees Celsius, while `*temp` output for `wsa883x_hwmon_read()` must be scaled by `1000` to millidegrees. [F368]
- In `DECLARE_TLV_DB_SCALE` / `TLV_DB_SCALE_ITEM(min, step, mute)`, `min`/`step` are in 0.01 dB units and `mute` is boolean (`0` if raw 0 does not mute). In `snd_soc_dai_driver` (`wcd938x`, `wcd939x`, `wsa881x`, `wsa883x`), set `.rate_min <= .rate_max` and `.channels_min <= .channels_max`. [F267, F364]

## Error Handling
- `wcd938x_mbhc_init()` and `wcd939x_mbhc_init()` must check `wcd_mbhc_init()` with `IS_ERR()` and return `PTR_ERR()`. Callers of `wcd_get_micb_vout_ctl_val()` and `wcd938x_get_adc_mode()` / `wcd939x_get_adc_mode()` must store the result in a signed `int` and check `< 0` before writing registers. [F265, F268, F269]
- `wcd938x_populate_dt_data()` and `wcd938x_probe()` must propagate exact error codes from resource getters (not `-EINVAL`) to preserve `-EPROBE_DEFER`. Call `devm_mux_control_get()` only when `of_property_present(dev->of_node, "mux-controls")` is true, and return any error from it or `mux_control_try_select()` without falling back to `us_euro_gpio`. [F266, F342]
- In `wsa881x_runtime_resume`, after deasserting `sd_n` (`!wsa881x->sd_n_val`), wait for `slave->initialization_complete` before clearing `regcache_cache_only` and syncing; on timeout, re-assert `sd_n` (`wsa881x->sd_n_val`) and leave regmap cache-only dirty. In `wsa883x_get_temp`, guard against `d1 == d2` before dividing by `d2 - d1` and check `in_range(val, WSA883X_LOW_TEMP_THRESHOLD, range)` before updating `wsa883x->temperature`. [F296, F308]

## External Contracts
- In `wcd938x_snd_controls`, volume controls where increasing raw values decrease dB gain (`HPHL Volume`, `HPHR Volume`) must set `SOC_SINGLE_TLV` `invert = 1` with `DECLARE_TLV_DB_SCALE(line_gain, -3000, 150, 0)`. [F362]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `wcd938x`/`wcd939x` `mbhc_get_result_params`, `denom = (x1 * d1) - (1 << (14 - noff))` must be signed (`int32_t`/`s32`) and checked with `denom > 0` before dividing so floating impedances (`denom <= 0`) take the floating branch. [F323]
- In `wsa881x_put_pa_gain`, `for (val = min_gain; max_gain <= val; val--)` requires `val` to be signed `int` and `min_gain` capped at `G_0DB` to avoid unsigned underflow when `max_gain == 0`. In `wsa881x.c`, `wsa881x->sd_n_val` caches `gpiod_is_active_low(wsa881x->sd_n)`; pass `!wsa881x->sd_n_val` in probe/resume to enable the chip and `wsa881x->sd_n_val` in suspend. [F309, F348]
