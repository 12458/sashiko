# NVM EXPRESS TARGET DRIVER — drivers/nvme/target (part 2)

This unit implements the NVMe/RDMA (`rdma.c`) and NVMe/TCP (`tcp.c`) fabric transport drivers—including IB verbs, SRQs, RDMA RW contexts, kernel sockets, TLS 1.3 PSK handshakes, and CRC32C digests—along with Zoned Namespaces (`zns.c`) command execution and ftrace helpers (`trace.h`, `trace.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `rdma.c`, `cm_id->context` always points to `struct nvmet_rdma_port`, while per-connection `struct nvmet_rdma_queue *` lives only in `cm_id->qp->qp_context` (and `wc->qp->qp_context`). In `nvmet_rdma_cm_handler()`, `RDMA_CM_EVENT_ADDR_CHANGE` must check `if (!queue)` to schedule `port->repair_work` and `break` for listener `cm_id`s before falling through to `nvmet_rdma_queue_disconnect(queue)`. [F288, F313]
- In `nvmet_rdma_read_data_done()` and `nvmet_rdma_write_data_done()`, after refunding `rsp->n_rdma` to `queue->sq_wr_avail` and calling `nvmet_rdma_rw_ctx_destroy(rsp)`, reset `rsp->n_rdma = 0` so `nvmet_rdma_release_rsp(rsp)` credits only 1 WR and does not destroy the RW context twice. In `struct nvmet_rdma_rsp`, `send_cqe`, `read_cqe`, and `write_cqe` are distinct `ib_cqe` fields recovered via `container_of(wc->wr_cqe, ...)`; `nvmet_rdma_recv_done()` must clear `rsp->flags`, `rsp->n_rdma`, and `rsp->invalidate_rkey` to `0`, and `nvmet_rdma_queue_response()` must reset `rsp->send_wr.opcode = IB_WR_SEND` when `rsp->invalidate_rkey == 0`. [F202, F256, F314]
- `nvmet_rdma_cmd.sge[0]` maps `nvme_cmd` while `c->sge[1 .. ndev->inline_page_count]` map `c->inline_sg[]`; `nvmet_rdma_alloc/free_inline_pages()` must start at `&c->sge[1]` and reserve 1 SGE for the capsule. Clamp `nport->inline_data_size` to `NVMET_RDMA_MAX_INLINE_DATA_SIZE` in `nvmet_rdma_add_port()` and to `(max(max_sge_rd, max_recv_sge) - 1) * PAGE_SIZE` in `nvmet_rdma_find_get_device()` (which must also clear `nport->pi_enable` if `kernel_cap_flags` lacks `IBK_INTEGRITY_HANDOVER`). [F274, F311, F324]
- Because `rsp->cmd->inline_sg` is reused, `nvmet_rdma_map_sgl_inline()` must validate `off + len <= rsp->queue->dev->inline_data_size` and `len > 0`, and `nvmet_rdma_use_inline_sg()` must call `sg_unmark_end()` on entries `0 .. sg_count - 2` and `sg_mark_end()` on `sg_count - 1`. In `nvmet_rdma_set_sig_attrs()`, clearing `NVME_RW_PRINFO_PRACT` and adding `req->metadata_len` to `req->transfer_len` must happen in lockstep before `nvmet_req_alloc_sgls()`. [F257, F275]
- Even when `nvmet_rdma_use_srq` is true, `nvmet_rdma_init_srqs()` leaves `ndev->srqs` NULL if `max_srq_wr` or `max_srq` is 0, and otherwise clamps `srq_size` to `max_srq_wr` and `srq_count` to `min(num_comp_vectors, max_srq)`; callers must test `ndev->srqs`, `queue->nsrq`, or `cmd->nsrq` rather than `nvmet_rdma_use_srq`. In `__nvmet_rdma_queue_disconnect()`, `NVMET_RDMA_Q_CONNECTING` must drain `rsp_wait_list` via `nvmet_rdma_put_rsp()` and fall through to `NVMET_RDMA_Q_LIVE` to set `NVMET_RDMA_Q_DISCONNECTING` and `disconnect = true`, whereas `NVMET_RDMA_Q_DISCONNECTING` leaves `disconnect` false. [F243, F289, F325]
- In `tcp.c`, keep `nvme_tcp_pdu_sizes[]`, `nvmet_tcp_pdu_valid()`, and `nvmet_tcp_done_recv_pdu()` in lockstep. `nvmet_tcp_try_recv_pdu()` must verify `nvmet_tcp_pdu_valid(hdr->type)` and `hdr->hlen == nvmet_tcp_pdu_size(hdr->type)` before computing `queue->left = hdr->hlen - queue->offset + hdgst`. `nvmet_tcp_done_recv_pdu()` must check `queue->state == NVMET_TCP_Q_CONNECTING` before the `hdr->type` switch so only `nvme_tcp_icreq` is accepted while connecting and `nvme_tcp_icreq` is rejected with `nvmet_tcp_fatal_error()` otherwise. [F261, F266, F293]
- In `nvmet_tcp_handle_h2c_data_pdu()`, `exp_data_len` (`le32_to_cpu(data->hdr.plen)`) must subtract `sizeof(*data)`, `nvmet_tcp_hdgst_len(queue)`, and `nvmet_tcp_ddgst_len(queue)`, and validate `cmd->pdu_len != 0`, `cmd->pdu_len == exp_data_len`, `cmd->pdu_len <= NVMET_TCP_MAXH2CDATA`, and `le32_to_cpu(data->data_offset) == cmd->rbytes_done` before `nvmet_tcp_build_pdu_iovec()`. [F262, F263]
- `nvmet_tcp_map_data()` must allocate `cmd->iov` for `cmd->req.sg_cnt` elements and `cmd->req.sg` with lowmem pages (`GFP_KERNEL` without `__GFP_HIGHMEM`) because `nvmet_tcp_calc_ddgst()` uses `sg_virt(sg)`. `nvmet_tcp_build_pdu_iovec()` must pass clamped `iov_len` (`min_t(u32, length, sg->length - sg_offset)`) to `bvec_set_page()` and compute `nr_pages` with `DIV_ROUND_UP(length, PAGE_SIZE)`. [F301, F307, F343]
- In `nvmet_tcp_alloc_queue()`, when `NVMET_TCP_Q_TLS_HANDSHAKE` is set, reset `sk->sk_user_data = NULL` and `sk->sk_data_ready = port->data_ready` before `nvmet_tcp_try_peek_pdu()` and `nvmet_tcp_tls_handshake()`. `nvmet_tcp_restore_socket_callbacks()` must keep `if (!queue->state_change) return;`. [F252, F253]
- Keep `cmd->state` and `queue->rcv_state` checks as sequential `if` statements in `nvmet_tcp_try_send_one()` and `nvmet_tcp_try_recv_one()`. In `nvmet_tcp_io_work()`, return immediately without requeuing `io_work` on negative return from `nvmet_tcp_try_recv()`/`try_send()`, and requeue `io_work` when `pending` is true at `ops >= NVMET_TCP_IO_WORK_BUDGET` or `nvmet_tcp_check_queue_deadline()` (which must use `!time_after(jiffies, queue->poll_end)`). [F295, F296, F302]
- `nvmet_bdev_zns_enable()` must reject devices where `get_capacity(bd_disk)` is not a multiple of `bdev_zone_sectors(ns->bdev)` or `validate_conv_zones_cb` finds `BLK_ZONE_TYPE_CONVENTIONAL`. In `nvmet_bdev_zone_zmgmt_recv_work()`, `nvmet_bdev_validate_zone_mgmt_recv()` must precede `blkdev_report_zones()` and its `zrasf` cases must match `nvme_zrasf_to_blk_zcond[]`. Every `zsa_req_op()` result (`!= REQ_OP_LAST`) must be handled in `nvmet_bdev_execute_zmgmt_send_all()` and `zmgmt_send_scan_cb()`, where `d.zbitmap` requires `BITS_TO_LONGS(nr_zones)` `unsigned long`s. [F283, F285, F317, F328]
- In `trace.h`, `parse_nvme_cmd()` must check `opcode == nvme_fabrics_command` before branching on `qid`, and `__assign_req_name()` must use `strscpy_pad(..., DISK_NAME_LEN)` (or `memset` when `!req->ns`). Sparse table lookups (`zsa_strs`, `zrasf_strs`, `nvme_tcp_pdu_sizes[]`) must check `index < ARRAY_SIZE(table)` and `table[index] != 0/NULL`. [F279, F281, F303]

## Synchronization
- `nvmet_rdma_recv_not_live()` must recheck `queue->state` under `spin_lock_irqsave(&queue->state_lock, flags)` before queuing on `rsp_wait_list` or calling `nvmet_rdma_put_rsp(rsp)`, and `nvmet_rdma_queue_established()` must drop `state_lock` before `nvmet_rdma_handle_command()`. Similarly, `queue->rsp_wr_wait_list` requires `queue->rsp_wr_wait_lock`, dropped before `nvmet_rdma_execute_command(rsp)`. `nvmet_rdma_find_get_device()` must use `kref_get_unless_zero(&ndev->ref)` under `device_list_mutex`. [F223, F229, F231, F330]
- Unlinking from `nvmet_rdma_queue_list` requires `nvmet_rdma_queue_mutex`, `list_for_each_entry_safe()`, and `list_del_init(&queue->queue_list)`. In `tcp.c`, callers iterating `nvmet_tcp_queue_list` must only call `kernel_sock_shutdown()` and let `nvmet_tcp_release_queue_work()` unlink `queue->queue_list`. [F286, F287]
- In `tcp.c`, softirq socket callbacks must hold `read_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock)` to read `sk_user_data`, and modifying `sk_user_data` or `sk_*` callbacks requires `write_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock)` (under which `nvmet_tcp_set_queue_sock()` must also verify `sk_state == TCP_ESTABLISHED`). `nvmet_tcp_queue.state_lock` must use `spin_lock_bh()` and nest inside `sk_callback_lock`; `nvmet_tcp_schedule_release_queue()` sets `NVMET_TCP_Q_DISCONNECTING` and calls `kref_put(&queue->kref, nvmet_tcp_release_queue)` under `state_lock`, so `nvmet_tcp_release_queue()` must defer sleeping teardown to `queue->release_work`. [F219, F220, F337, F338, F339]
- `nvmet_prepare_receive_pdu()` must run `WRITE_ONCE(queue->cmd, NULL)` before `smp_store_release(&queue->rcv_state, NVMET_TCP_RECV_PDU)`, pairing with `smp_load_acquire(&queue->rcv_state)` before `READ_ONCE(queue->cmd)` in `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()`. `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()` enqueues on `queue->resp_list` via `llist_add()`, whereas `resp_send_list` and `free_list` are unguarded and only touched from `queue->io_work`. [F341, F342]
- `nvmet_bdev_execute_zone_mgmt_recv/send()` must defer blocking zone I/O to `zbd_wq` (`WQ_MEM_RECLAIM`), `nvmet_bdev_zone_mgmt_emulate_all()` must allocate `d.zbitmap` with `GFP_NOIO`, and `nvmet_execute_identify_ns_zns()` must hold `req->ns->subsys->lock` around `nvmet_ns_changed()`. [F344, F345]

## Object Lifetimes
- Claim `nvmet_rdma_port->cm_id` via `xchg(&port->cm_id, NULL)` between `nvmet_rdma_disable_port()` and `nvmet_rdma_device_removal()` (returning 1 for port `cm_id`s, 0 for queue `cm_id`s). `nvmet_rdma_remove_port()` must call `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&port->repair_work)` before `nvmet_rdma_disable_port(port)`, which must call `rdma_destroy_id(cm_id)` before `nvmet_rdma_destroy_port_queues(port)`. [F200, F226]
- `nvmet_rdma_cm_handler()` must never call `rdma_destroy_id(queue->cm_id)` or `nvmet_rdma_free_queue()` synchronously: defer disconnect/fail to `queue->release_work`, and set `queue->cm_id = NULL` before `nvmet_rdma_free_queue()` on accept failure in `nvmet_rdma_queue_connect()`. In `nvmet_rdma_free_queue()` / `release_queue_work()`, `ib_drain_qp(queue->qp)` must finish before destroying `cm_id`/QP/CQ, freeing `cmds`/`rsps`, and `kref_put(&dev->ref, nvmet_rdma_free_dev)`. `nvmet_rdma_remove_one()` must flush `nvmet_wq` after disconnecting device queues, and `nvmet_rdma_init/exit()` must register `nvmet_rdma_ib_client` outside `nvmet_rdma_ops` and `nvmet_rdma_queue_ida`. [F201, F205, F227, F318, F331]
- `nvmet_rdma_put_rsp()` requires `rsp->queue` to be set before freeing `NVMET_RDMA_DISCRETE_RSP_TAG` responses or clearing `rsp->tag` in `rsp_tags`. `nvmet_rdma_release_rsp()` must only call `nvmet_req_free_sgls(&rsp->req)` when `rsp->req.sg != rsp->cmd->inline_sg`. [F203, F204]
- When `nvmet_req_init()` fails in `nvmet_tcp_done_recv_pdu()` on an inline write, `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()` defers queuing while `rcv_state == NVMET_TCP_RECV_PDU`, and `nvmet_tcp_handle_req_failure()` maps a buffer and sets `NVMET_TCP_F_INIT_FAILED` to drain before queuing. Keep `queue->nr_cmds == 0` until `nvmet_tcp_alloc_cmds()` succeeds so `queue->connect` is used; `nvmet_tcp_put_cmd()` must ignore `queue->connect`, while `nvmet_tcp_free_cmds()` / `free_cmd_data_in_buffers()` clean up both `cmds` and `connect`, and `nvmet_tcp_free_cmd_buffers()` must null `cmd->iov` and `cmd->req.sg`. [F207, F208, F212, F238]
- In `nvmet_tcp_alloc_queue()`, initialize `tls_handshake_tmo_work`, `release_work`, and `io_work` unconditionally before teardown paths, and after `sock_alloc_file()` use `fput(queue->sock->file)` rather than `sock_release()`. In `nvmet_tcp_release_queue_work()`, run `nvmet_tcp_uninit_data_in_cmds()` between the first `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)` and `nvmet_sq_destroy()`, then run a second `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)` before freeing buffers, `sock->file`, and `cmds`. [F209, F210, F217]
- `nvmet_tcp_tls_handshake()` takes a `kref_get(&queue->kref)` before `tls_server_hello_psk()`, dropped on failure, in `nvmet_tcp_tls_handshake_done()`, or in `nvmet_tcp_tls_handshake_timeout()` only if `tls_handshake_cancel()` returns `true`. `nvmet_tcp_remove_port()` must restore `sk_data_ready` and clear `sk_user_data` under `write_lock_bh(&sk_callback_lock)`, then `cancel_work_sync(&port->accept_work)` before destroying queues and `port->sock`. `nvmet_tcp_exit()` must unregister `nvmet_tcp_ops`, flush `nvmet_wq`, shut down `nvmet_tcp_queue_list` sockets, and flush `nvmet_wq` again before destroying `nvmet_tcp_wq` and `nvmet_tcp_queue_ida`. [F211, F326, F327]

## API Contracts & Error Handling
- `nvmet_rdma_rw_ctx_init()` returns a signed WR count (`< 0` on error) added to `rsp->n_rdma`; when `req->metadata_len` is non-zero, use `rdma_rw_ctx_signature_init/destroy_signature()` and pass `&rsp->write_cqe` to `rdma_rw_ctx_wrs()`. Sync `cmd->sge[0]` and `rsp->send_sge` with `ib_dma_sync_single_for_cpu/device()`. Format wildcard `disc_traddr` (`inet_addr_is_any()`) with `%pISc` from the local CM/socket address, and keep `MODULE_ALIAS("nvmet-transport-<type>")` matching `nvmet_fabrics_ops.type`. [F244, F334, F351, F364, F365]
- Convert `blkdev_report_zones()` return in `nvmet_bdev_zone_mgmt_emulate_all()` to `-EIO` (`ret != nr_zones`) or `0` (`ret == nr_zones`). Express `zasl` in `ilog2` of 4 KiB pages (`NVMET_MPSMIN_SHIFT = 12`), store `nvmet_sect_to_lba(req->ns, bio->bi_iter.bi_sector)` in `req->cqe->result.u64` in `nvmet_bdev_zone_append_bio_done()`, and terminate `trace_seq` helpers in `trace.c` with `trace_seq_putc(p, 0)`. [F234, F306, F359, F362]
- Check `queue->nvme_sq.ctrl` before `nvmet_ctrl_fatal_error()` in `nvmet_rdma_error_comp()` and `nvmet_tcp_fatal_error()`. In `nvmet_tcp_handle_h2c_data_pdu()`, abort via `nvmet_tcp_fatal_error()` and return `-EPROTO` instead of `nvmet_req_complete()`. When `nvmet_tcp_map_data()` fails in `nvmet_tcp_done_recv_pdu()` without inline data, call `nvmet_req_complete()`, `nvmet_prepare_receive_pdu()`, and return `-EAGAIN`. In `nvmet_execute_identify_ns_zns()`, run `nvmet_ns_revalidate()` before `bdev_is_zoned()` and jump to `out` (not `done`) on failure. Reset `off`/`sg_offset` to `0` after entry 0 in `nvmet_rdma_use_inline_sg()` and `nvmet_tcp_build_pdu_iovec()`. [F233, F237, F242, F291, F294]

## External Contracts & Known Pitfalls
- In `nvmet_rdma_parse_cm_connect_req()`, `queue->recv_queue_size = le16_to_cpu(req->hsqsize) + 1`, while `send_queue_size = le16_to_cpu(req->hrqsize)`. In `zns.c`, map `0` to `U32_MAX` and decrement non-zero `bdev_max_open/active_zones()` for `mor`/`mar`; check `req->cmd->zms.select_all` before validating `sect` in `nvmet_bdev_zmgmt_send_work()`; clamp `rz_data.nr_zones` to `out_nr_zones` when `req->cmd->zmr.pr` is set; and guard `get_nr_zones_from_buf()` against `bufsize <= sizeof(struct nvme_zone_report)`. [F259, F268, F276, F282, F298]
- In `tcp.c`, check `req->cmd->common.dptr.sgl.length` (not `nvmet_tcp_has_inline_data()`) when `nvmet_req_init()` fails; read `icreq` before `memset(icresp, ...)` (`queue->pdu` alias); sample `recv_digest` at `pdu + hdr->hlen` before `nvmet_tcp_hdgst()`; cast PDU/digest pointers to `void *`/`u8 *` before adding `offset`; and use `cpu_to_le*()`/`le*_to_cpu()` on wire headers. [F239, F241, F251, F267, F310]
- Use `get_unaligned_le24/32()` on `nvme_keyed_sgl_desc` in `nvmet_rdma_map_sgl_keyed()`, and keep unwind loop counters `i` as signed `int` in `nvmet_tcp/rdma_alloc_*` and `nvmet_rdma_init_srqs()`. In `trace.h`/`trace.c`, null-check `req->sq`, `req->sq->ctrl`, and `req->ns`, copy 24 bytes from `&cmd->common.cdws`, and index `u8 *` `cdw10`/`spc` at byte offset `(N - 10) * 4` with `get_unaligned_le*()`. [F277, F280, F304, F305, F347]
