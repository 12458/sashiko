# NETWORKING [IPv4/IPv6] — net/ipv6 (part 5)

Implements IPv6 routing, `rt6_info` dst cache, PMTU/redirect exceptions, and ECMP (`route.c`), SRv6 core, HMAC, lwtunnel, and End.* behaviors (`seg6*.c`), RPL (`rpl*.c`), SIT/6rd tunnels (`sit.c`), netns sysctls (`sysctl_net_ipv6.c`), and TCP-AO (`tcp_ao.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- User-supplied `fib6_config` must pass `fib6_config_validate()` (`fc_dst_len/fc_src_len <= 128`, `fc_type <= RTN_MAX`, no `RTF_PCPU | RTF_CACHE`) before `ip6_route_info_create()`. In `fib6_nh_init()` and `ip6_route_info_create_nh()`, routes over `IFF_LOOPBACK` where `fib6_is_reject()` is true must get `RTF_REJECT | RTF_NONEXTHOP`. [F960, F1048]
- In `rtm_to_fib6_config()`, `RTA_DST`/`RTA_SRC` bit-to-byte conversion must round up via `(len + 7) >> 3`, while `inet6_rtm_getroute()` must check `nla_len() >= sizeof(struct in6_addr)`. `rtm_to_fib6_multipath_config()` must verify nested `RTA_GATEWAY` has `nla_len(nla) >= sizeof(cfg->fc_gateway)`. In `rt6_route_rcv()`, `rinfo->prefix` is 16 bytes only when `rinfo->length == 3`; for 1 or 2, copy via `ipv6_addr_prefix()`. [F961, F962, F963, F995]
- `fib6_select_path()` and `rt6_multipath_rebalance()` must resolve the first ECMP sibling from `fn->leaf` (`rt6_multipath_first_sibling_rcu()` / `rt6_multipath_first_sibling()`); inline-nexthop siblings (`!rt->nh && rt->fib6_nsiblings`) changing `RTNH_F_DEAD` or `RTNH_F_LINKDOWN` in `fib6_ifup()`/`fib6_ifdown()` must call `rt6_multipath_rebalance()`. In `fib6_select_path()`, once the first nexthop with `hash <= nh_upper_bound` is reached, selection must terminate (`goto out`/`break`) even if `rt6_score_route() < 0`. [F945, F979, F980]
- `rt6_multipath_hash()` must right-shift by 1 (`>> 1`) on every return path. In `fib6_select_path()`, store `fl6->mp_hash` in signed `int` before comparing with `atomic_read(&nh->fib_nh_upper_bound)` so `-1` on dead nexthops is not promoted to `UINT_MAX`. In `rt6_upper_bound_set()`, cast `*weight` to `u64` before `<< 31` and divide by `total` via `DIV_ROUND_CLOSEST_ULL()` only when `!rt6_is_dead(rt)`. In `rt6_nh_dump_exceptions()`, once `w->skip == 0`, expired entries skipped via `rt6_check_expired()` must still increment `w->count`. [F984, F989, F990, F991]
- In `seg6_get_srh()`, call `pskb_may_pull()` twice—for `srhoff + sizeof(*srh)` then `srhoff + ((srh->hdrlen + 1) << 3)`—recomputing `srh = (struct ipv6_sr_hdr *)(skb->data + srhoff)` before `seg6_validate_srh()`. In `seg6_icmp_srh()`, restore `skb->network_header` after `skb_reset_network_header(skb)`. `seg6_pop_srh()` requires `skb->data == skb_network_header(skb)`, `srhoff >= sizeof(struct ipv6hdr)`, `skb_ensure_writable(skb, srhoff + srhlen)`, and `skb_network_header_len(skb)` not in `[srhoff + 1, srhoff + srhlen - 1]`. [F955, F966, F972]
- `seg6_genl_sethmac()` must check `slen <= SEG6_HMAC_SECRET_LEN` and `slen <= nla_len(info->attrs[SEG6_ATTR_SECRET])`, and `seg6_hmac_info_add()` must validate `hinfo->alg_id`. `seg6_get_tlv_hmac()` must verify `srh->hdrlen >= (srh->first_segment + 1) * 2 + 5` and `tlv->tlvhdr.len == 38`. `seg6_hmac_compute()` must check `plen = 16 + 1 + 1 + 4 + (hdr->first_segment + 1) * 16 < SEG6_HMAC_RING_SIZE` (256) and zero-pad `output` to `SEG6_HMAC_FIELD_LEN` (32). [F967, F968, F969]
- In `seg6_local.c`, `seg6_action_desc` must keep `(attrs & optattrs) == 0`, and `parse_nla_action()` / `parse_nla_optional_attrs()` must pass the failing index `i` to `__destroy_attrs(..., i, slwt)`. Every attribute in `seg6_action_params` emitted by `seg6_local_fill_encap()` must be sized in `seg6_local_get_encap_size()`. Check `seg6_lwtunnel_counters_enabled(slwt)` before `slwt->pcpu_counters`, and in `input_action_end_dt6()` check `seg6_end_dt6_get_mode(slwt) == DT_LEGACY_MODE` before using `slwt->dt_info`. [F934, F959, F985]
- In `input_action_end()` and `input_action_end_x()`, check `seg6_next_csid_enabled(fops)` before `end_flv8986_core()`. `seg6_chk_next_csid_cfg()` must verify `lcblock_bits` and `lcnode_func_bits` are in `[8, 120]`, multiples of 8, and sum to `<= 128`. Keep `SEG6_LOCAL_PKTINFO_SL_ZERO` and `SL_ONE` consecutive for `seg6_get_srh_pktinfo()`, and in `seg6_local_flv8986_act_lookup()` check `!(flvmask & ~SEG6_LOCAL_FLV8986_SUPP_OPS)` and `flv8986_act_tbl_idx(pf, fm) < FLV8986_ACT_TBL_SIZE`. [F970, F971, F986, F987]
- In `seg6_build_state()` and `rpl_build_state()`, `lwtunnel_state_alloc()` must include `tuninfo_len` / `srh_len` after verifying `nla_len()` covers `(hdrlen + 1) << 3`. `rpl_validate_srh()` must verify `srh_len >= sizeof(*srh)`, `(srh->hdrlen << 3) == srh_len - sizeof(*srh)`, `srh->segments_left > 0`, `(srh->segments_left * sizeof(struct in6_addr)) == srh_len - sizeof(*srh)`, and `!srh->cmpri && !srh->cmpre`. [F973, F1004]
- In `struct sit_net`, `sitn->tunnels[0]` (`tunnels_wc`) has 1 element while `tunnels[1..3]` have `IP6_SIT_HASH_SIZE` (16), so bound `h` to `prio ? IP6_SIT_HASH_SIZE : 1`. `ipip6_tunnel_lookup()` must search `tunnels_r_l`, `tunnels_r`, `tunnels_l`, then `tunnels_wc`. `ipip6_tunnel_update()` must call `ipip6_tunnel_unlink()` + `synchronize_net()` before mutating `saddr`/`daddr`, relink via `ipip6_tunnel_link()`, and call `dst_cache_reset()`. Reject `sitn->fb_tunnel_dev` in `changelink`, `dellink`, `tunnel_del`, and PRL ioctls; in `ipip6_tunnel_update_6rd()`, reject `relay_prefixlen > 32` or `prefixlen + (32 - relay_prefixlen) > 64` and guard `<< (32 - relay_prefixlen)` with `if (ip6rd->relay_prefixlen)`. [F947, F977, F988, F992, F1044]
- Every `ctl_table` entry in `ipv6_table_template` (`sysctl_net_ipv6.c`) must point `.data` into `init_net` (relocated by `(void *)net - (void *)&init_net`); globals belong in `ipv6_rotable`. [F999]

## Synchronization

- Do not hold `rcu_read_lock()` across `ip6_route_info_create_nh()` or `fib6_nh_init()` in `ip6_route_add()`/`multipath_add()` (they sleep under `GFP_KERNEL`). `rt6_probe()` runs under `rcu_read_lock()` and must use `GFP_ATOMIC`, rate-limit via `cmpxchg(&fib6_nh->last_probe, ...)`, and defer `ndisc_send_ns()` via `schedule_work()`. [F1008, F1020]
- In `inet6_rt_notify()`, `rt6_nlmsg_size()` and `rt6_fill_node()` run under RCU without `tb6_lock`, so `-EMSGSIZE` must reallocate a larger skb and retry. `rt6_select()` must hold `spin_lock_bh(&tb6_lock)` and verify `next->fib6_node != NULL` before setting `fn->rr_ptr`. In `rt6_route_rcv()`, hold `tb6_lock` and pair `fib6_clean_expires()` with `fib6_remove_gc_list()` and `fib6_set_expires()` with `fib6_add_gc_list()`. [F909, F915, F946]
- `fib6_nh->rt6i_exception_bucket` encodes `FIB6_EXCEPTION_BUCKET_FLUSHED` (`0x1UL`); mask via `fib6_nh_get_excptn_bucket()` and abort `rt6_insert_exception()` if `fib6_nh_excptn_bucket_flushed(bucket)` is true. Hold `rt6_exception_lock` (BH disabled) when mutating `bucket->chain`, `bucket->depth`, and `fib_rt_cache`; drop `rt6_exception_lock` before taking `tb6_lock` in `rt6_insert_exception()` for `fib6_update_sernum()` and `fib6_add_gc_list()`, followed by `fib6_force_start_gc()`. [F910, F911, F943]
- `rt6_get_pcpu_route()` and `rt6_make_pcpu_route()` require `rcu_read_lock()` and `local_bh_disable()` (`BUG_ON(prev)` on `cmpxchg`). After publishing `pcpu_rt`, `rt6_make_pcpu_route()` must recheck `res->f6i->fib6_destroying` and, if set, release `xchg(&pcpu_rt->from, NULL)`. Evicting `nh->rt6i_pcpu` requires atomic `xchg(..., NULL)` before `dst_dev_put()` and `dst_release()`. `rt6_uncached_list` ops must hold `spin_lock_bh(&ul->lock)`, locking `rt->dst.rt_uncached_list->lock` on delete. [F912, F1013, F1014, F1017]
- Paths reading `dst` device/netns without a dst device ref (`__ip6_rt_update_pmtu()`, `ip6_default_advmss()`, `rt6_do_redirect()`) require `rcu_read_lock()` and `dst_dev_rcu()` / `dst_dev_net_rcu()`. `ip6_sk_update_pmtu()` and `ip6_sk_redirect()` must use `READ_ONCE(sk->sk_mark)` and `sk_uid(sk)`. [F921, F1011]
- Per-CPU `hmac_storage.hmac_ring` and `seg6_bpf_srh_states` require `local_lock_nested_bh()` across their entire use (including `seg6_bpf_has_valid_srh()`). `seg6_genl_family` uses `.parallel_ops = true`: hold `mutex_lock(&sdata->lock)` when mutating `sdata->hmac_infos` or `sdata->tun_src` (using `rcu_assign_pointer()` + `synchronize_net()` before freeing old `tun_src`), and hold `rcu_read_lock()` across `seg6_hmac_info_lookup()` and `seg6_hmac_compute()`. In `sit.c`, free `t->prl` via RCU (`rcu_barrier()` in `sit_cleanup()`) under RTNL, and drop `rcu_read_unlock()` before `copy_to_user()` in `ipip6_tunnel_get_prl()`. [F916, F917, F953, F1022, F1028]

## Object Lifetimes

- `__ip6_ins_rt()` does not consume `rt`, so callers must always call `fib6_info_release(rt)`. Any `fib6_info` looked up under RCU for `rt->from` or post-RCU use must be pinned with `fib6_info_hold_safe()`; callers of `ip6_rt_copy_init()` / `rt6_set_from()` must hold `res->f6i`, and detaching `rt->from` requires `unrcu_pointer(xchg(&rt->from, NULL))` before `fib6_info_release()`. [F892, F894, F914]
- `ip6_pol_route()` returns `rt6_uncached_list_add()` routes with a ref held even under `RT6_LOOKUP_F_DST_NOREF`, so `ip6_route_output_flags()` must check `list_empty(&rt6->dst.rt_uncached)` before `dst_hold_safe()`. Evicting cached `rt6_info` requires `dst_dev_put(&rt->dst)` before `dst_release()`. In `ip6_negative_advice()`, hold `rcu_read_lock()` across `sk_dst_reset(sk)` and `rt6_remove_exception_rt(rt)`. In `ip6_route_dev_notify()`, guard loopback `rt6i_idev` release with `dev->reg_state != NETREG_UNREGISTERED` and `in6_dev_put_clear()`. [F895, F896, F898, F1019]
- In `seg6_local`, tear down `seg6_local_lwtunnel_destroy_state(slwt)` before `destroy_attrs(slwt)`. In `seg6`/`rpl` input/output, save `orig_dst->lwtstate` before `ip6_route_input()`/`skb_dst_drop()`, run `dst_cache_get()`/`set_ip6()` under `local_bh_disable()`, cache `dst` only if `lwtst != dst->lwtstate` (and `!dst->error`), and call `dst_release(dst)` on `do_srh` failure. In `sit.c`, free PRLs and `netdev_put()` in `ipip6_tunnel_uninit()`, and destroy `dst_cache` in `ipip6_dev_free()`. In `ipv6_sysctl_net_exit()`, save `ctl_table_arg` before `unregister_net_sysctl_table()` and `kfree()`. Pair `tcp_sigpool_start(&hp)` with `tcp_sigpool_end(&hp)`. [F901, F902, F904, F906, F907, F918, F1038]

## API Contracts

- `ip6_route_info_create_nh()` frees `rt` on error, so callers must not release `rt`. `ip6_route_info_create()`, `ip6_route_info_create_nh()`, and `fib6_nh_init()` must propagate `gfp_flags`. `ip6_blackhole_route()` always consumes `dst_orig`. `rt6_nlmsg_size()` must account for every attribute in `rt6_fill_node()`. [F891, F899, F1009, F1049]
- `end_dt_vrf_rcv()` and `end_dt_vrf_core()` return `skb`, `NULL` (consumed; return `0`), or `ERR_PTR` (freed; return `PTR_ERR` without `kfree_skb()`); `end_dt_vrf_rcv()` must call `skb_unset_mac_header(skb)` before `l3mdev_l3_rcv()` and drop if `skb->dev != dev` or `skb->skb_iif != dev->ifindex`. `seg6_validate_srh()` requires `len >= sizeof(*srh)` and `reduced = false` when `segments_left <= first_segment`. In `ipv6_rpl_srh_decompress()`/`compress()`, `n` is `total_segments - 1` and requires `cmpri, cmpre <= 15`. [F932, F965, F974, F1039]
- `ipip6_rcv()`/`sit_tunnel_rcv()` return `0` on match (consuming `skb`) and `1` without freeing `skb` on miss. `ipip6_tunnel_locate()` returns existing tunnel when `create == 0`, or only a newly created tunnel when `create != 0`. In `ipip6_tunnel_xmit()`, `skb_realloc_headroom()` must call `skb_set_owner_w(new_skb, skb->sk)` and reload `iph6`. SIT ioctls must check `ns_capable(t->net->user_ns, CAP_NET_ADMIN)` on creator `t->net`. Do not reuse `proc_rt6_multipath_hash_policy`/`fields` on other `ctl_table` entries. [F941, F942, F1000, F1042, F1045]
- `tcp_v6_ao_calc_key()` sets `tmp->outlen` in bits (`* 8`), `tcp_v6_ao_hash_pseudoheader()` takes `daddr` before `saddr`, and `tcp_v6_ao_calc_key_sk()` swaps address/port/ISN pairs when `!send`; use `hp.scratch` (not stack) and `memcpy(tmp->label, "TCP-AO", 6)`. `tcp_v6_ao_lookup()`/`_rsk()` must pass `l3mdev_master_ifindex_by_index()` to `tcp_ao_do_lookup()`. [F940, F1007, F1053]

## Error Handling

- On error in `fib6_nh_init()`, `out:` must call `in6_dev_put(idev)`, `fib_nh_common_release()`, clear `nhc_pcpu_rth_output` and `fib_nh_lws`, and call `netdev_put()`. In `ip6_route_info_create()`, if `ip_fib_metrics_init()` fails, jump to `free: kfree(rt)` rather than `fib6_info_release(rt)`. [F890, F927]
- In `ip6_route_multipath_add()`, clear `NLM_F_EXCL | NLM_F_REPLACE` and set `NLM_F_CREATE` after the first nexthop, and on error delete already-inserted nexthops up to `err_nh` via `ip6_route_del()`. Call `dst_release_immediate(&nrt->dst)` when `rt6_insert_exception()` fails. In `ipip6_newlink()`, call `unregister_netdevice_queue(dev, NULL)` if `ipip6_tunnel_update_6rd()` fails after `ipip6_tunnel_create()`. [F893, F897, F905, F956]

## External Contracts

- In `rt6_fill_node()`, clamp `rtm->rtm_table` to `RT_TABLE_COMPAT` when `table >= 256` and emit `RTA_TABLE`. Use `crypto_memneq()` in `seg6_hmac_validate_skb()` and `NLA_POLICY_EXACT_LEN()` for `SEG6_LOCAL_NH4`/`NH6`. In `ipip6_tunnel_get_prl()`, clamp unprivileged `cmax > 1` to `min(t->prl_count, cmax)` with `__GFP_ACCOUNT | __GFP_NOWARN`, copy to `a + 1`, and write `sizeof(*kp) * c` to `a->datalen`. [F964, F976, F1030, F1032, F1043]

## Known Pitfalls

- Call `rt6_info_init(rt)` after `dst_alloc()` for `rt6_info`, and allocate `fib6_null_entry` via `fib6_info_alloc(GFP_KERNEL, true)` (not `kmemdup()`). `__rt6_find_exception_spinlock()`/`_rcu()` advance `*bucket += hval` in place, so reset via `fib6_nh_get_excptn_bucket()` on retry. Use `time_after()`/`time_before()` for route/exception aging, and keep `ipv6_route_table_template` indices aligned with `ipv6_route_sysctl_init()`. [F930, F952, F981, F996, F998]
- Zero `IP6CB(skb)` and restore `IP6CB(skb)->iif = skb->skb_iif` when encapsulating non-IPv6 inner packets in SRv6. Use `memmove()` for overlapping shifts in `__seg6_do_srh_inline()`, `seg6_pop_srh()`, and `seg6_next_csid_advance_arg()`. `seg6_bpf_srh_state::hdrlen` is `srh->hdrlen << 3` (multiple of 8). In `check_6rd()`, guard `>> relay_prefixlen` with `< 32` and keep `pbi1` signed `int`. In `ipip6_rcv()`, reload `iph = (const struct iphdr *)skb_mac_header(skb)` after `iptunnel_pull_header()`. [F935, F938, F993, F1003, F1041]
